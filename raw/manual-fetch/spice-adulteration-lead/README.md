# Spice adulteration — lead chromate and lead-based pigments

Papers documenting lead chromate and other lead-based pigments as intentional adulterants in spices (turmeric, cinnamon, chili pepper) and as non-agricultural contamination mechanisms in the food chain.

## Purpose

These papers, combined with the existing troeschel2024 (CDC MMWR WanaBana recall) and rylander2025 (Georgian children BLL + spice use), will meet the 3-source threshold to promote the "Spice and cinnamon adulteration as Pb pathway for infants and toddlers" synthesis candidate (`spice-adulteration-lead-infants`).
