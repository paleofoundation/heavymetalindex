# Vendored fonts

These TTFs are embedded in the scholarly PDFs generated by
`tools/postbuild-synthesis-pdf.mjs` (via `tools/pdf/fonts.mjs`). They are the
three families the "Content Page PDF v2" design specifies, vendored as static
TTFs so the PDF build is deterministic and offline — nothing is fetched at build
time — and so the embedded glyphs cover Latin, Latin Extended, the micro sign
and Cyrillic natively (no ASCII-folding of "µg/kg" or "Ćwieląg-Drabek").

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
bundling and redistribution. The full licences are alongside:
`OFL-Inter.txt`, `OFL-IBMPlex.txt`, `OFL-Lora.txt`.

| Family        | Role in the design                                   | Files | Source |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------ |
| Lora          | Display serif — title, section subheadings, "Abstract" | `Lora-Regular.ttf`, `Lora-Bold.ttf`, `Lora-Italic.ttf` | github.com/cyrealtype/Lora-Cyrillic (OFL) |
| Inter         | Body copy, tables, references, front/back matter      | `Inter-Regular.ttf`, `Inter-Italic.ttf`, `Inter-Medium.ttf`, `Inter-SemiBold.ttf` | github.com/rsms/inter v4.1 (OFL) |
| IBM Plex Mono | Masthead, section numbers, labels, table headers      | `IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf`, `IBMPlexMono-Medium.ttf` | github.com/IBM/plex (OFL) |

To change a face, replace the TTF and keep the filename, or update the
`Font.register` calls in `tools/pdf/fonts.mjs`.
