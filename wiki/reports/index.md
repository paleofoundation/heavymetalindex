## Current edition

### [State of Heavy Metals in Food 2026](/reports/state-of-heavy-metals-in-food-2026)

A reproducible cross-corpus report covering 25 promoted synthesis findings and 126 disclosed anchor-source relationships. Includes a complete review-state register and downloadable [JSON](/static/data/state-of-heavy-metals-in-food-2026.json) and [CSV](/static/data/state-of-heavy-metals-in-food-2026.csv) data.

The report is a public preprint and a map of existing evidence. It is not a pooled market survey, a certification threshold document, or a substitute for the linked source records.
