# Lithium

## Overview

Lithium is most relevant to this wiki as a metal used in medical therapy for mania and bipolar disorder. [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] attaches lithium to the wiki through its narrow therapeutic index and toxicities involving neuromuscular, central nervous system, cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, renal, and thyroid effects.

## Frequently asked questions
<!-- audience: consumer, educator -->

### Why does a heavy metals wiki cover lithium at all?

Lithium is most relevant here as a metal used in medical therapy for mania and bipolar disorder. According to [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], it is tied to the toxicology literature through its narrow therapeutic index and its range of toxic effects.

### What does it mean that lithium has a "narrow therapeutic index"?

A narrow therapeutic index means the gap between a helpful dose and a toxic dose is small. [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] identifies this narrow margin as the main reason lithium's toxicity is a concern in the context of its use as a therapy.

### What kinds of health effects are associated with lithium toxicity?

The page reports that lithium toxicities involve neuromuscular, central nervous system, cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, renal, and thyroid effects, per [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]]. The page does not provide dose thresholds or quantitative detail for these effects.
