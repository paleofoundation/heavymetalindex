### Is barium considered a toxic metal?

According to [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], barium is listed among the minor toxic metals for which toxicity has been described. Beyond noting that toxicity has been described, this page does not detail specific barium health effects.

### Does this page have detailed information about barium in food or consumer products?

No. This page is currently a source-map stub and provides source-map context only. The cited toxicology chapter does not include a detailed barium subsection, so specific dietary, exposure, or product information is not yet covered here.

### What source does the information about barium come from?

The barium entry draws on [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], the "Toxic Effects of Metals" chapter (Ch 23) in Casarett & Doull's Essentials of Toxicology, 4th ed. It is referenced here for its listing of barium among minor toxic metals rather than for a dedicated barium analysis.
