# Gold

## Overview

Gold is named by [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] in two chapter-level contexts: metals that can provoke immune reactions and historical/therapeutic use of metal compounds. The Essentials chapter does not provide a dedicated gold subsection, so this node is source-map context only.

### Why is gold mentioned in the toxicology literature at all?

According to [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], gold is named in two chapter-level contexts: as one of the metals that can provoke immune reactions, and in connection with the historical and therapeutic use of metal compounds. The source discusses these only at a chapter level rather than in a dedicated gold section.

### Can gold trigger immune or allergic reactions?

[[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] lists gold among metals that can provoke immune reactions. The page does not provide further detail on the mechanism, dose, or affected populations, because the underlying chapter treats gold only in passing.

### Has gold historically been used in medicine or therapy?

The cited source, [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], references gold in the context of the historical and therapeutic use of metal compounds. The page does not elaborate on specific therapeutic applications beyond noting this context.
