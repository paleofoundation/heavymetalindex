Silver (Ag) is listed among the minor toxic metals for which toxicity has been described, according to [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]]. Beyond noting it as a metal with described toxicity, this page does not yet detail specific silver health effects.
