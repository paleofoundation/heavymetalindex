# Selenium

## Coverage

Selenium is indexed here as an element measured or discussed in the cited literature. Nutritional intake, occurrence and selenium–mercury interactions remain distinct; this page does not introduce a tolerable intake or a contamination limit.

This page indexes source records. A dedicated synthesis remains pending.
