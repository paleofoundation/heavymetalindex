## Frequently asked questions
<!-- audience: consumer, educator -->

### Is cesium a toxic metal?

Cesium is listed among minor toxic metals for which toxicity has been described, per [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]]. Beyond that listing, the page does not yet describe specific cesium toxicity, because the source chapter does not provide a detailed cesium subsection.

### Does this page tell me how much cesium is in food or how I might be exposed?

No. This page is currently a source-map stub and does not cover dietary sources, exposure routes, or contamination levels for cesium. Substantive cesium-specific content awaits a dedicated ingest.

### Why is there so little information on this cesium page?

The page was created as source-map context during the re-ingest of [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], which lists cesium but does not include a detailed cesium subsection. As a result the page is explicitly marked a stub, and fuller expansion is pending a dedicated ingest.

### What source does the cesium information come from?

The single source cited is [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], the "Toxic Effects of Metals" chapter (Ch 23) in Casarett & Doull's Essentials of Toxicology, 4th edition. It is the only reference currently underpinning this page.
