# Thallium

## Overview

Thallium is listed by [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] among minor toxic metals for which toxicity has been described. The Essentials chapter does not provide a detailed thallium subsection, so this node is source-map context only.

## Texas animal-feed survey

The Texas feed survey reports thallium mean 0.27 mg/kg, SD 1.79 and median zero in 124 mineral products (Dai et al. 2016, Table 3). Results below the 1 mg/kg quantitation limit were replaced with zero, so the mean and median can lie below that limit. Five mineral samples were reported above 1 mg/kg. This supplies feed-source occurrence, not a feed safety threshold. [[sources/dai2016-texas-animal-feed-metals]]
