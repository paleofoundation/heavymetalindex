# Magnesium

## Overview

Magnesium is a nutritionally essential metal and enzyme cofactor. [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] attaches magnesium to the wiki as an essential-metal node where deficiency is usually more clinically important than excess, while high exposure can produce neuromuscular, cardiovascular, and central nervous system toxicity.

## Frequently asked questions
<!-- audience: consumer, educator -->

### Is magnesium a toxic heavy metal or a nutrient?

Magnesium is a nutritionally essential metal and an enzyme cofactor, so it is primarily a nutrient rather than a purely toxic heavy metal. According to [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]], deficiency is usually more clinically important than excess, though high exposure can still cause harm.

### Can you get too much magnesium, and what happens if you do?

Yes. While magnesium deficiency is usually the more clinically important concern, [[sources/ufelle2021-metals-chapter|Ufelle & Barchowsky 2021]] notes that high magnesium exposure can produce neuromuscular, cardiovascular, and central nervous system toxicity.
