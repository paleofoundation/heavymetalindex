# Fish-based / marine-protein complete diets (iAs/MeHg/tHg-dirty matrix)

## Direct answer

For the question of whether cat and dog food contains heavy metals, this row is fish-based and marine-protein complete diets, it does not name retail brands, and no source is listed on this row. Kim et al. 2018 measured 17 United States fish-formulation dry dog foods and reported medians per 1,000 kcal of metabolizable energy: total arsenic 0.343 mg/Mcal (range 0.025 to 1.104), cadmium 0.027 mg/Mcal (range 0.014 to 0.215), total mercury 0.0082 mg/Mcal (range 0.0010 to 0.0139), and lead 0.049 mg/Mcal (range 0.018 to 0.325) ([[sources/kim2018-dry-dog-food-heavy-metals]], DOI 10.3389/fvets.2018.00264). Those figures are also on the dry kibble page; the study did not speciate arsenic or mercury, and the group summaries cannot be converted to mg/kg.
