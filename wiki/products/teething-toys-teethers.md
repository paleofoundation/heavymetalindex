# Teething toys/teethers

## Overview

This page is the HMTc Taxonomy v2.2 Category 9 (Infant and Child Contact Products, Ages 0-5), Row 5: **Teething toys/teethers**. It is the routing destination for source-page declarations of `products: [teething-toys-teethers]`. A teether is a mouthable article governed by toy-safety law, not a food; its heavy-metal limits are element **migration** limits from the toy material (EU Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC Annex II Part III, the EN 71-3 test standard, ASTM F963 soluble migration) together with the CPSIA total-lead limit for children's products. The routing boundary in the taxonomy places teethers here rather than in Category 21 (Children's Toys) because the mouthing-specific exposure pathway governs.

## Methodology

This page reports what the cited sources say about heavy-metal migration and content in teething toys and teethers. Speciation is non-substitutable (contact-product arsenic and mercury limits regulate total element, tAs and tHg; inorganic arsenic and methylmercury have no migration counterpart and are recorded not-material for this matrix). Basis is preserved and never conflated: element migration from toy material (mg/kg of material) is a different quantity from total-content limits (mg/kg of product), and each is labelled with its basis. Non-detect handling follows each source's reporting convention. Pooling is avoided across differing bases and methods.

The applicable regulatory jurisdictions for this row are: **CPSIA, CPSC (ASTM F963), EU Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC**.
