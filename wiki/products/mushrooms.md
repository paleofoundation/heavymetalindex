## Cultivated mushrooms in Mataram

Fardani measured zinc, copper, lead, and cadmium by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in cultivated Pleurotus osteratus and Auricularia auricula-judae from Mataram, Indonesia. The spelling osteratus is the paper's. n=3 in each table. Methods started from 2 g wet weight and then dried the sample. The Auricularia zinc and copper text says dry basis. The content column is copied, with the table's decimal commas. The abstract uses periods for some of the same digits. [[sources/fardani2023-edible-mushrooms-elements]]

## Further mushroom measurements

Qin and colleagues measured market mushrooms in Shaanxi. n=244. Auricularia auricula and Tremella fuciformis are dry and the other species are fresh. Table 4 means, mg/kg: lead 0.0624±0.0088 (ND to 1.6637), cadmium 0.3215±0.0322, total arsenic 0.1728±0.0985, total mercury 0.0148±0.0041, chromium 0.1165±0.0114. The text says 2 samples exceeded the lead limit and 12 exceeded the arsenic limit. Table 4 prints 4/244 and 20/244. [[sources/qin2024-shaanxi-edible-mushrooms-metals]]

Pecina and colleagues measured mushrooms on a dry-weight basis. Table 2 is mg/kg. Xerocomellus pruinatus cadmium is 12.1±8.67 (n=8). Xerocomellus chrysenteron lead is 3.23±2.93 (n=21). Methods say 96 samples of eight species. The table n values sum to 94. [[sources/pecina2022-czechia-forest-mushrooms-metals]]

Stihi and Dumitrescu measured wild mushrooms from Dambovita County. The abstract prints nickel 0.033 to 4.507 mg/kg, chromium 0.003 to 0.760 mg/kg, lead maximum 0.886 mg/kg, and cadmium maximum 0.850 mg/kg. [[sources/stihi2025-dambovita-wild-mushrooms-metals]]

Ndimele and colleagues measured wild mushrooms from Ibadan on a dry-weight basis. Lead 40.00±3.56 to 76.00±9.78. Cadmium 67.92±5.89 to 92.45±12.34. [[sources/ndimele2017-ibadan-wild-mushrooms-metals]]

Bucurica and colleagues measured wild edible mushrooms and report species means rather than one overall mean. The abstract says 228 mushrooms and section 3.3 says 128 subsamples. Arsenic and mercury: Data gap. [[sources/bucurica2024-romania-wild-mushrooms-metals]]

Orywal and colleagues measured dried retail wild mushrooms. n=40 in each group. The label is dried retail wild mushrooms. Methods say μg/kg. The abstract and Table 3 are mg/kg. Boletus: mercury 3.039±1.092, cadmium 1.983±1.145, lead 1.156±1.049, arsenic 0.897±0.469 mg/kg. The Xerocomus lead standard deviation 1.810 sits outside the printed minimum 0.228 and maximum 1.167 and is copied as printed. [[sources/orywal2021-heavy-metals-dried-mushrooms]]

Brzezicha-Cirocka and colleagues measured cadmium only, dry biomass. There is no overall mean. Cantharellus 0.58±0.05. Boletus from Tarnobrzeska 5.2±4.7. Leccinum versipelle, spelled Lecinum in the table, 14±14 with median 9.8. Lead and the other target metals: Data gap. [[sources/brzezicha-cirocka2016-edible-wild-mushrooms-poland]]
