## Methodology

This page reports what the cited sources say about heavy-metal concentrations in fabric refresher products. Speciation is non-substitutable (iAs vs tAs, MeHg vs tHg, Cr-VI vs total Cr). Basis is preserved (finished-product as sold unless the source specifies otherwise; see each row for the basis label). Non-detect handling follows each source's reporting convention. Pooling is avoided across LOD/LOQ, period, geography, and analytical-basis differences.
