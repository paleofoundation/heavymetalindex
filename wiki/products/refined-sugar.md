## White sugar from one factory

Bekele measured lead and cadmium in white sugar from the Omo Kuraz factory across five annual batches. The record is a preprint, single factory. Averages are mg/kg. [[sources/bekele2024-omo-kuraz-white-sugar-metals]]

| Study and location | Design | Metal | Finding | Units and basis | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bekele 2024, Omo Kuraz | preprint, single factory; five batches | cadmium | average 0.429±0.031 mg/kg; batch cells include 0.217 and 0.617 mg/kg | mg/kg | 5 batches |
| Bekele 2024, Omo Kuraz | preprint, single factory; five batches | lead | average 0.296±0.013 mg/kg; highest Batch-2023 0.508±0.013 mg/kg; lowest Batch-2019 0.054±0.004 mg/kg | mg/kg | 5 batches |
