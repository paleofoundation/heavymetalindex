# Milk and Dairy

> **HMTc Category 24 master node (Dairy and Milk Products).** Promoted from a provisional scaffold under the v2.1 taxonomy reorganization ([[docs/governance/category-24-step0-lock]], 2026-06-08). This is the category rollup; the eight rows (cow, goat/sheep, and camel milk; cheese; yogurt and cultured dairy; cream; milk powders; condensed and evaporated milks) are routing destinations that carry no candidate values yet. Camel milk (Row 3) is the priority home that previously had nowhere to route. Butter and ghee remain Category 7 Row 3 and cross-link here. Standards are not derived in this pass.
