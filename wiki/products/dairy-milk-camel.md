# Camel milk

## Literature scope

Five measurement papers report camel milk concentrations. The figures below are copied from those source notes in the units the notes print. No population typical or upper-tail value is calculated here.

## Reported measurements

Chen and colleagues measured raw camel milk from Xinjiang, China (n=50) by ICP-MS. Table 4 of the source note prints camel means as lead 18.2 ± 7.1 ng/g, cadmium 0.786 ± 1.191 ng/g, total arsenic 8.06 ± 6.57 ng/g, chromium 13.6 ± 5.3 ng/g, nickel 131 ± 148 ng/g, tin 50.6 ± 29.0 ng/g, and aluminum 0.455 ± 0.287 µg/g. The same table header states that ng/g equals ppb for the trace rows. The note also prints camel calcium 888 µg/g, sodium 428 µg/g, zinc 5.81 µg/g, manganese 0.188 µg/g, and strontium 3.05 µg/g. Mercury, inorganic arsenic, and hexavalent chromium were not measured. The authors cite a Codex raw-milk lead maximum of 20 ng/g and state that the camel lead mean is below that figure. That comparator is the authors' citation, not a limit set on this page. [[sources/chen2020-milk-types-elements-china]]

Konuspayeva and colleagues report two Kazakhstan camel-milk sets. Sample (i), herd-mixed camel milk, Table 1, ppm wet weight: copper 0.07 ± 0.04, iron 1.48 ± 0.53, manganese 0.084 ± 0.03, zinc 5.16 ± 2.17, arsenic <0.1, lead 0.025 ± 0.019. The source note restates that lead mean as 25 ± 19 ppb, with a maximum of 60 ppb, and restates arsenic as <100 ppb. Sample (ii), 63 raw camel milk samples, is printed as lead 250 ± 56 ppb (maximum 532 ppb at Atyrau) and chromium 59.5 ± 45.4 ppb. The abstract and discussion state zinc 5.42 ± 0.2 ppm, while Table 2 regional zinc means are 48.62 to 59.90 ppm. The source note flags that regional zinc column as about ten times the abstract mean and does not harmonize the two. This page repeats both and does not choose one. Cadmium and mercury were not measured. Paired shubat (fermented camel milk) in Table 1 is lead 0.007 ± 0.005 ppm. Turkey 20 ppb, Germany and the Netherlands 50 ppb, and Kazakhstan 100 ppb are the authors' cited milk lead comparators, not limits adopted here. [[sources/konuspayeva2010-camel-milk-kazakhstan]]

Yazdanian and colleagues report ppm (mg/L) for 49 of 115 raw camel milk samples from Chaharmahal Bakhtiari, Iran. Metals were measured only in samples positive for Staphylococcus aureus (n=27) or Escherichia coli (n=22). The source note states that the 66 microbiologically negative samples were not tested. Staphylococcus aureus-positive means: lead 0.0053 ± 0.011 ppm (range 0.0013 to 0.055 ppm; the note also prints 13 to 55 µg/kg), cadmium 0.0031 ± 0.001 ppm (0.0012 to 0.0059 ppm; 1.2 to 5.9 µg/kg), arsenic 0.0020 ± 0.008 ppm (0.001 to 0.0041 ppm; 1.0 to 4.1 µg/kg). Escherichia coli-positive means: lead 0.0039 ± 0.006 ppm (0.001 to 0.034 ppm; 1.0 to 34 µg/kg), cadmium 0.0032 ± 0.002 ppm (0.0015 to 0.0043 ppm; 1.5 to 4.3 µg/kg), arsenic 0.0024 ± 0.001 ppm (0.0012 to 0.0067 ppm; 1.2 to 6.7 µg/kg). The note's summary maxima are lead 55 µg/kg, cadmium 5.9 µg/kg, and arsenic 6.7 µg/kg. Arsenic is total arsenic. No speciation is reported. [[sources/yazdanian2025-camel-milk-heavy-metals-iran]]

Zakaria and colleagues, Table 1, ppm in raw camel milk from South Egypt: Group A (non-industrial) copper 0.02 to 0.09, mean 0.05 ± 0.04, and aluminum ND to 0.04, mean 0.02 ± 0.01. Group B (industrial) copper 0.04 to 4.38, mean 1.43 ± 0.71, and aluminum 2.95 to 72.49, mean 27.80 ± 1.48. The authors' cited WHO maximum residue levels are copper 0.1 ppm (0 of 15 Group A samples over that figure, 9 of 15 Group B) and aluminum 0.05 ppm (0 of 15 Group A, 15 of 15 Group B). Those residue levels are the authors' comparators. Fermentation changed the industrial means to copper 1.45 ± 0.68 ppm and aluminum 28.08 ± 1.64 ppm, which the source describes as non-significant. Boiling in aluminum pans raised aluminum from 27.80 ± 1.48 ppm to 48.08 ± 1.64 ppm. [[sources/zakaria2024-camel-milk-copper-aluminum]]

Parsaei and colleagues measured 150 camel milk samples among 1100 raw milks from Isfahan Province, Iran. Camel milk had the lowest species means in the note: cadmium 1.22 ± 0.11 ppb, lead 3.14 ± 0.26 ppb, and mercury 2.36 ± 0.21 ppb. The source note files mercury as total mercury. It is not reported as methylmercury. Overall means that mix bovine, ovine, caprine, buffalo, and camel milk are not camel milk concentrations and are not repeated here. The note also records detection limits in mg/L beside sample minima in ppb and does not resolve that unit conflict. [[sources/parsaei2019-milk-cadmium-lead-mercury]]
