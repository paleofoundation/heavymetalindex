## Reported measurements

Mohammed and colleagues report one rubber duck, as total elemental content in mg/kg of the whole item after dry ashing, not a migration extract. Lead 9.0 ± 0.1 mg/kg, nickel 5.9 ± 0.3 mg/kg, chromium 12.4 ± 0.3 mg/kg, cadmium 4.1 ± 0.6 mg/kg, copper 0.2 ± 0.1 mg/kg, and manganese 1.0 ± 0.8 mg/kg.

The abstract says one sample exceeded the regulatory limit for cadmium. The body does not state whether that comparator is the EN 71-3 Category I total-content limit of 1.9 mg/kg or the Category III migration limit of 17 mg/kg. This page repeats both comparators and does not choose one.

Chromium is total chromium. The authors compare chromium with a chromium(III) figure of 37.5 mg/kg and nickel with a figure of 75 mg/kg. Those are the authors' comparators. The note does not identify hexavalent chromium.

The same note prints two descriptions of lead against a regulatory limit. The abstract says two samples exceed the regulatory limit for lead. The body says all eighteen samples exceed the EN 71-3:2013 Category I total-content limit of 2 mg/kg. This page repeats both and does not choose one. Study-wide cadmium is printed as below detection to 4.1 mg/kg in the table and as 0.002 to 4.14 mg/kg in the abstract. Study-wide copper is printed as below detection to 2.2 mg/kg in the table and as 0.03 to 2.15 mg/kg in the abstract. Study-wide manganese is printed as below detection to 4.2 mg/kg in the table and as 0.03 to 4.22 mg/kg in the abstract. This page repeats each pair and does not choose one. Those study-wide ranges are not rubber-duck-only values. Pacifiers, a teething ring, a sippy cup, a rattle, a bottle nipple, a plush bear, jewellery, and other toys in the same table are not squeeze toys and are not repeated here. ## Squeeze toys are mouthed articles

Soft squeeze toys are oral-contact articles. Lead cohorts, rat cadmium, and hexavalent chromium gavage did not test the polymer.

### What remains uncertain

No cited study dosed a squeeze toy. A content test stays a chemistry result. The chromium arms used hexavalent salts.

This page reports what the cited sources say about heavy-metal concentrations in squeeze toys. Speciation is non-substitutable (iAs vs tAs, MeHg vs tHg, Cr-VI vs total Cr).
