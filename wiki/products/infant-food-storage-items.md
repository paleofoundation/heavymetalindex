# Infant food-storage items (milk storage bags/containers)

## Overview

This page is the HMTc Taxonomy v2.2 Category 9 (Infant and Child Contact Products, Ages 0-5), Row 6: **Infant food-storage items (milk storage bags/containers)**. It is the routing destination for source-page declarations of `products: [infant-food-storage-items]`. A milk-storage bag or container is a food-contact article; its heavy-metal limits are **specific migration** limits into the stored food (EU Regulation 10/2011 Annex II for plastics, US FDA 21 CFR food-contact provisions) together with the CPSIA total-lead limit for children's products. The measured quantity is element migration *into food*, not element content of the article.

## Literature scope

The Heavy Metal Index contact-product corpus reports element migration and occurrence in food-contact materials; sources routed to this row are surfaced below as they are added. Until sources land, the literature-evidence sections are deliberately empty rather than guessed. HMTc certification thresholds for products in this row are developed under the certification program at [heavymetalcertified.com](https://heavymetalcertified.com), not on this public page; this page reports literature evidence and the applicable regulatory caps only.

## Methodology

This page reports what the cited sources say about heavy-metal migration and content in infant food-storage items. Speciation is non-substitutable (contact-product arsenic and mercury limits regulate total element, tAs and tHg; inorganic arsenic and methylmercury have no migration counterpart and are recorded not-material for this matrix). Basis is preserved and never conflated: specific migration into food (mg/kg food) is a different quantity from total-content limits (mg/kg of article), and each is labelled with its basis. Non-detect handling follows each source's reporting convention.

The applicable regulatory jurisdictions for this row are: **FDA (food-contact substances), EU Regulation 10/2011, CPSIA**.
