# Research Chemicals, Peptides & SARMs (Unregulated)

> **HMTc Category 26 master node (Peptides, SARMs, and Research Chemicals).** Promoted from the 2026-06-08 provisional scaffold under the v2.1 taxonomy reorganization ([[docs/governance/category-26-step0-lock]]). This category covers unregulated ingestible and injectable compounds sold outside the supplement framework; it is legally distinct from Category 16. The four rows (injectable peptide hormones, oral SARMs, injectable AAS, raw AAS/peptide powders) are routing destinations with no candidate values yet. This class introduces the **parenteral pathway**: an injected compound bypasses gut absorption, so derivation must apply the documented methodology variants (no regulatory anchor, so the min() cap term drops; a potentially tighter health-based term) before any value is published. Collagen and protein-peptide supplements route to Category 16; topical peptide serums to Category 13.
