## Methodology

This page reports what the cited sources say about heavy-metal concentrations in bread and baked goods. Speciation is non-substitutable (iAs vs tAs, MeHg vs tHg, Cr-VI vs total Cr). Basis is preserved (finished-product as sold unless the source specifies otherwise; see each row for the basis label). Non-detect handling follows each source's reporting convention. Pooling is avoided across LOD/LOQ, period, geography, and analytical-basis differences. Initial HMT&C limits are compiled separately at heavymetalcertified.com under the government-limit-only method; this public page reports descriptive literature evidence and does not select a certification value or status.
