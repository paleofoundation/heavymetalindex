# Lipstick — RETIRED (no children's personal care placement)

> This page has been retired. Lipstick was scaffolded into the children's personal care taxonomy on 2026-05-14 when two source pages declared lipstick in their product frontmatter, but the underlying papers ([[sources/li2021-lead-lip-cosmetics-china-ecommerce]] and [[sources/attard2022-heavy-metals-cosmetics-review]]) report adult lip cosmetics. The children's personal care taxonomy covers ages 0-5 only.
>
> For the children's-lip-balm portion of the Li 2021 dataset (the small subset that is age-relevant), see [[products/childrens-lip-balm-mineral-bearing]] for tinted/SPF children's lip balm and [[products/childrens-lip-balm-plain]] for plain children's lip balm.
>
> Adult lipstick will be filed under the adult leave-on cosmetics taxonomy when that category is built. Until then, this page is a redirect target for the children's personal care scope cleanup.

## Source attribution

The Li 2021 and Attard 2022 product-frontmatter declarations were valid at the time of the scaffolder's run (a single-row lipstick page did not exist) but did not carry children-versus-adult disambiguation. The 2026-04-28 program scope lock retroactively settles the ambiguity: adult lipstick belongs in the future adult leave-on cosmetics taxonomy.
