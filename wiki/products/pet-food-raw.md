# Raw pet foods (frozen/chilled raw diets)

## Direct answer

For the question of whether cat and dog food contains heavy metals, this row is frozen and chilled raw diets, it does not name retail brands, and no source is listed on this row. Pain et al. 2023 measured 90 packages of commercial raw pheasant dogfood in the United Kingdom: the shot-free weighted mean lead was 220.99 ppm dry weight (95% confidence interval 78.84 to 421.14; the source overview counts 81 shot-free samples), and the full-sample mean including shot-contaminated packages was 1,159.92 ppm dry weight (standard error 379.52) ([[sources/pain2023-lead-pheasant-dogfood-uk]], DOI 10.1007/s13280-023-01856-x). That study is filed on the dry kibble row, and its dried-stick and tinned figures are not raw diets.
