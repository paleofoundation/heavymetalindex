# Methylmercury and the gut microbiome

## Coverage

The indexed study distinguishes human observations from gnotobiotic-mouse experiments in methylmercury demethylation and elimination. Environmental methylation and demethylation are separate pathways.

This page indexes source records. A dedicated synthesis remains pending.
