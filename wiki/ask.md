<div class="hmi-ask-aside-title">How answers are constructed</div>
<p>Retrieval is restricted to public Index pages and the structured evidence layer. The assistant may quote a value only if that value exists in an extraction record, and every answer lists the records it used.</p>
<p>It does not browse the open web, does not infer values by interpolation, and does not answer brand-level questions. When the corpus carries no evidence on a question, the answer says so rather than filling the gap.</p>
