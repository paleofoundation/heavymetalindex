## Source Pages Created

### Group 1 (FM_12747974 – FM_12847127)

**wei2025-cd-as-rice-yunnan-karst-thresholds** (`FM_12750342`)
ICP-MS, n=125 paired field samples, Yunnan karst region, China. Cd mean 381 ppb and tAs mean 1,120 ppb at MAC-exceeding sites. Primary purpose is deriving region-specific soil safety thresholds; food concentration data is secondary but well-reported. *Frontiers in Plant Science* 2025, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd, tAs. Matrices: rice-grain. Jurisdictions: CN.

**ibrahim2025-metals-flavored-dairy-egypt** (`FM_12847127`)
15-element ICP-MS/MS survey of 180 flavored dairy products (UHT milk, pasteurized milk, milk powder, yogurt, drinking yogurt, ice cream) from Giza, Egypt. Pb, Al, tAs, Ni, Cr, Sb reported; Cd and Hg below LOD. Pb exceeded EU limit (20 ppb) in multiple products; carcinogenic risk above acceptable threshold for toddlers consuming strawberry products twice daily. *Biological Trace Element Research* 2025. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Pb, Al, tAs, Ni, Cr, Sb. Matrices: dairy. Jurisdictions: EG.

### Group 2 (FM_12847395 – FM_12932597)

**xie2026-selenium-enriched-rice-china** (`FM_12894730`)
HDXRF, n=54 commercial selenium-enriched rice samples from 14 Chinese provinces. tAs mean 110 ppb, Cd mean 80 ppb — both below Chinese GB limits. Co-occurrence of Se with toxic elements characterized. *Scientific Reports* 2026, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tAs, Cd. Matrices: rice-grain. Jurisdictions: CN.

**balzani2026-fish-metals-dalyan-lake** (`FM_12904875`)
ICP-MS, n=27 fish (3 species, 9 per species), Dalyan Lake, Türkiye. Cr, Ni, tAs, Pb measured; Cr biomagnifies with trophic level, As and Ni show trophic dilution, Pb very low across all species. All concentrations below regulatory limits. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cr, Ni, tAs, Pb. Matrices: freshwater-fish. Jurisdictions: TR.

**albishi2026-cassava-flour-biofortification** (`FM_12909235`)
Nigerian cassava flour control samples (pre-biofortification): Pb 62–69 ppb, Cd 58–63 ppb, tAs 10–11 ppb, Cr 65–68 ppb. Baseline food-safety data for cassava flour in Nigeria. *Frontiers in Nutrition* 2026, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Pb, Cd, tAs, Cr. Matrices: cassava-flour. Jurisdictions: NG.

**zhang2026-trace-metals-five-crops-chongqing** (`FM_12913949`)
ICP-MS, n=71 samples, 5 crops (rice, maize, soybean, sweet potato, sorghum) near Mn mining area, Chongqing, China. Rice grain tAs 294 ppb, Cr 1,980 ppb — both exceed China GB limits. Mining-area contamination gradient visible across all five crops. *Scientific Reports* 2026, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tAs, Pb, Cd, Cr, Ni. Matrices: rice-grain, maize, soybean, sweet-potato, sorghum. Jurisdictions: CN.

**qin2026-rice-cadmium-liver-cancer-guangxi** (`FM_12923468`)
Cd in 3,754 rice grain samples from 44 Guangxi counties; median 70 ppb, mean 120 ppb. Spatial coupling between Cd-contaminated rice production areas and elevated liver cancer incidence documented at county level. ICP-MS, large scale. *Environmental Geochemistry and Health* 2026, CC BY-NC-ND. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd. Matrices: rice-grain. Jurisdictions: CN.

### Group 3 (FM_12935679 – FM_13070825)

**marcelino2026-liver-trace-metals-netherlands-herbivores** (`FM_12971517`)
20-year ICP-MS monitoring of As, Cd, Cr, Pb, Ni and 8 additional elements in livers of 346 Heck cattle, Konik horses, and red deer at Oostvaardersplassen nature reserve, Netherlands (2003–2023). Establishes species-specific reference intervals; documents temporal Pb and Fe declines. Included as organ-meat occurrence baseline for EU game/livestock liver. *Frontiers in Veterinary Science* 2026, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: As, Cd, Cr, Pb, Ni. Matrices: livestock-liver, game-liver. Jurisdictions: NL.

### Group 4 (FM_13071475 – FM_11065944)

**alinezhad2024-heavy-metals-milk-systematic-review** (`FM_10843077`)
PRISMA systematic review, n=981 milk samples across 48 studies. Pb, Cd, Al, As, Hg in pasteurized and sterilized cow's milk worldwide. Cd, Zn, Pb, Cu most commonly exceeded limits across included studies. Largest milk-metals systematic review in corpus to date. *PLoS ONE* 2024, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Pb, Cd, Al, tAs, tHg. Matrices: cow-milk. Jurisdictions: multiple.

**bravo2024-cadmium-cacao-arauca** (`FM_10954870`)
Cd in cacao seeds (avg 780 ppb) and artisanal chocolate (avg 1,100 ppb — all 6 samples exceeding EU limit of 800 ppb) from 180 farms in Arauca, Colombia. FAAS. Confirms Arauca as high-Cd Colombian cacao origin zone. *Environmental Monitoring and Assessment* 2024. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd. Matrices: cacao-bean, chocolate. Jurisdictions: CO.

**ibrahim2024-heavy-metals-egyptian-milk-cheese** (`FM_10970126`)
Pb, Cd, Hg, As in 100 raw milk and 100 Egyptian cheese samples (Karish/Domiati/Ras). 46% of milk exceeded EU Pb limit. AAS. *Open Veterinary Journal* 2024, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Pb, Cd, tHg, tAs. Matrices: cow-milk, cheese. Jurisdictions: EG.

**kamouh2024-heavy-metals-chicken-meat-egypt** (`FM_11018418`)
Pb, Cd, Cu in 100 chicken samples (breast/thigh/gizzard/liver) from Egypt. Liver > gizzard > thigh > breast tissue gradient confirmed. *Open Veterinary Journal* 2024, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Pb, Cd. Matrices: chicken-muscle, chicken-liver. Jurisdictions: EG.

**hussein2024-toxic-metals-canned-fish-egypt** (`FM_11018424`)
Cd, Pb, tAs, tHg, Al, Sn in 100 canned fish (herring/mackerel/salmon/sardine/tuna) from Mansoura, Egypt. AAS/ICP. Sardine and tuna had highest EU exceedance rates; mean Hg EDI exceeded TDI for tuna. *Open Veterinary Journal* 2024, CC BY. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd, Pb, tAs, tHg, Al, Sn. Matrices: canned-fish. Jurisdictions: EG.

**kim2024-metals-kale-urban-rural-maryland** (`FM_11020932`)
As, Ba, Cd, Cr, Pb, Ni, U, V in Darkibor kale from urban vs. rural farms, Baltimore/Maryland. Urban kale had higher non-essential metals but all Pb/Cd below public health guidelines. Useful urban/rural variance data for leafy greens. *PLoS ONE* 2024, CC0. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tAs, Cd, Cr, Pb, Ni, U. Matrices: kale. Jurisdictions: US.

**li2024-selenium-cadmium-rice-black-shale-china** (`FM_11033549`)
Cd (and Se) in rice from Chinese black shale exposure areas (Enshi/Zhuxi, Hubei). Enshi local rice Cd up to 2,488 ppb — approximately 6× China GB limit and 12× Codex ML. Most extreme rice Cd values in corpus to date. *GeoHealth* 2024. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd. Matrices: rice-grain. Jurisdictions: CN.
