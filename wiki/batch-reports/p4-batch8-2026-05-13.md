Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd. Matrices: rice-grain. Jurisdictions: CN.

Most comprehensive multi-matrix TDS synthesis in corpus. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tAs, iAs, Cd, Pb. Matrices: rice-grain, cocoa-bean, shellfish, tea, coffee.
