## Notable findings

**Ayurvedic supplement contamination — extreme outliers.** ciocan2021-ayurvedic-lead-hg-poisoning documents two Romanian poisoning cases from Ayurvedic supplements: Pb 23,043 mg/kg and Hg 145,711 mg/kg — concentrations five orders of magnitude above food thresholds. Advances the supplements Pb seeding thread; these are not food matrices but illustrate the upper tail of herbal supplement contamination.

**Mushroom mercury — second paper.** orywal2021-heavy-metals-dried-mushrooms reports mean tHg of 3.039 mg/kg DW in Boletus edulis from Polish markets, with a maximum of 4.884 mg/kg DW. Joins the mushroom-Hg seeding thread alongside the earlier mushroom paper. B. edulis is a bio-accumulator; the mechanism is distinct from fish MeHg.

**Infant rice cereal thickener — post-FDA-reassurance data.** du2025-infant-thickener-urinary-arsenic documents urinary arsenic in children consuming rice-starch-based infant thickeners; adds a post-2022 data point to the infant-rice iAs thread and directly touches the FDA CTZ reassessment timeline.

**Climate × marine mercury mechanistic linkage.** seelen2023-methylmercury-dom-thiol provides mechanistic support for DOM-thiol-mediated MeHg bioavailability under changing dissolved organic matter regimes — relevant background for the climate-fish-MeHg seeding thread.

**Arsenolipids in seafood.** liu2021-arsenolipids-seafood characterizes arsenolipid speciation in marine organisms, distinguishing fat-soluble organic arsenic from tAs; relevant to the iAs/tAs speciation discussion on seafood matrices and the wiki's strict iAs ≠ tAs convention.

**Hemp/cannabis heavy metals.** zafeiraki2021-hemp-cannabis-metals-greece documents Pb, Cd, Ni, Cu, Zn in hemp Cannabis sativa from Greek cultivars; touches the emerging hemp-ingredient risk profile.

**Wheat × cadmium × fungicide interaction.** motta-romero2021-wheat-cadmium-fungicide reports that certain fungicide applications alter Cd uptake in wheat, adding a supply-chain lever relevant to the wheat/grain ingredient pages.
