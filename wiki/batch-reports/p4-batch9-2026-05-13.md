## Group 1 (FM_11999714 – FM_12375182) — 8 pages, 42 FP

**wu2025-climate-meghan-freshwater-fish-china** (`FM_12002180`)
PNAS GeoHealth, 13,000+ freshwater fish samples across 131 Chinese reservoirs and lakes, tHg/MeHg; projects +60% MeHg increase 2031–2060 under climate warming. Anchor source for [[synthesis/subsistence-fishing-mehg-disparity]]. Evidence tier: A. Metals: MeHg, tHg. Matrices: freshwater-fish.

**yan2025-rice-heavy-metals-henan-china** (`FM_12043637`)
ICP-MS, n=180 rice samples across 18 Henan districts. Multi-metal profile: iAs mean 52 ppb, Cd mean 30 ppb, Cr mean 240 ppb, Pb mean 12 ppb, tHg mean 3 ppb. All below China GB 2762 limits. Geographic breakdown adds to rice iAs and Cd profiles. Evidence tier: A. Metals: iAs, Cd, Cr, Pb, tHg. Matrices: rice.

**zhou2025-microbial-methylmercury-rice-china** (`FM_12130583`)
Metagenomic + culture study identifying Syntrophaceae and Methanomicrobiales as the dominant microbial methylators of Hg in flooded paddy soil. Mechanistically links temperature rise (climate) to increased MeHg production. Connects [[synthesis/climate-metals-tradeoffs]] and [[microbiome/rice-paddy-mercury]]. Evidence tier: A. Metals: MeHg, tHg. Matrices: rice, paddy-soil.

**dietz2025-arctic-mercury-isotopes-greenland** (`FM_12162874`)
Hg isotope analysis in Greenland freshwater and marine biota; documents methylation pathways in polar ecosystems. Biomonitoring context for Arctic subsistence consumption. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tHg, MeHg. Matrices: freshwater-fish, marine-fish, marine-mammal.

**shumba2025-tilapia-mercury-zambia-mining** (`FM_12178998`)
n=96 tilapia from artisanal gold-mining-impacted Zambian waterways; tHg 0.12–1.94 µg/g ww. Mining-impact signature. Contributes to freshwater-fish mining cluster (see Synthesis threads). Evidence tier: A. Metals: tHg. Matrices: freshwater-fish.

**cardini2025-cadmium-speciation-plant-foods-sec-icp** (`FM_12276129`)
SEC-ICP-MS Cd speciation in rice, legumes, root vegetables, leafy vegetables: distinguishes bioavailable (low-MW) vs protein-bound (high-MW) fractions. Cd speciation data is rare; most occurrence surveys report total Cd. Implications for bioavailability adjustments in contamination profiles. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd. Matrices: rice, legumes, root-vegetable, leafy-vegetable.

**uthayarajan2025-cdku-food-water-sri-lanka-review** (`FM_12289754`)
Systematic review of heavy metals in food and water among CKDu (Chronic Kidney Disease of unknown aetiology) patients in Sri Lanka. Multi-metal, multi-matrix: rice, tea, vegetables, freshwater fish, drinking water. Cd, Pb, iAs, Ni, Al, Cr implicated. CKDu context is the primary health-link. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd, Pb, iAs, tAs, Al, Cr, Ni, Sn, tHg. Matrices: rice, freshwater-fish, tea, vegetables, legumes, bread, drinking-water.

**laoye2024-fish-fruits-veg-heavy-metals-nigeria-review** (`FM_12375182`)
Review of 21 Nigerian studies on Pb, Cd, Hg, As, Al in freshwater fish, fruits, and vegetables. Meta-summary of sub-Saharan West Africa food safety monitoring. Evidence tier: B. Metals: Pb, Cd, tHg, iAs, tAs, Al. Matrices: freshwater-fish, vegetables, fruit.

## Group 3 (3638–3707 range screened) — 3 pages, 47 FP

**alsulaiti2023-qatar-fish-mehg** (`FM_10140118`)
Direct mercury analyzer + LC-ICP-MS MeHg speciation, n=120 commonly consumed fish and seafood from Qatar markets. MeHg accounts for 78–94% of tHg. Adds Gulf region data; several large pelagic species exceed 0.5 µg/g. Evidence tier: A. Metals: tHg, MeHg. Matrices: fish, seafood.

**kamaly2023-egypt-poultry-metals** (`FM_10238321`)
ICP-OES, n=60 chicken samples (muscle + liver) from Egyptian markets. Al, Cd, Pb, Ni, Cr detected; Pb and Cd in liver exceed FAO/WHO guidance values in some samples. Egypt food safety monitoring cluster. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Al, Cd, Pb, Ni, Cr. Matrices: chicken-meat, poultry-liver.

**suomi2023-finland-dietary-metals-cumulative** (`FM_10244267`)
Cumulative dietary risk assessment (Cd, Pb, iAs, MeHg, Ni, Al) in Finnish adults (FinDiet 2012, n=1,295). Using relative potency factors, hazard index (HI) exceeds 1 for the average adult in all age groups for neurotoxicity AND kidney endpoints. Women aged 25–45 have the highest cumulative exposure. Corroborates [[synthesis/combined-exposure-underprotection]] with a third independent national-scale assessment. Evidence tier: A. Metals: Cd, Pb, iAs, MeHg, Ni, Al. Matrices: dietary-intake.

**bakhshalizadeh2023-mullet-ree-trace-caspian** (`FM_10403408`)
