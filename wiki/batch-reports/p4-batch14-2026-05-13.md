## Synthesis threads

### Mining-impacted freshwater fish (promoted candidate — 3 of 4 criteria met)

`oliveira2022-mercury-fish-pregnant-women-tapajos` adds Amazon basin data at the human-exposure level: 48% of 110 pregnant women in Tapajos communities exceeded the US EPA hair Hg reference dose from piscivorous fish consumption, and the fish (tucunaré, pescada) exceeded FDA/EPA 0.5 mg/kg tHg thresholds in at least one season. This strengthens the subsistence-fishing-mehg-disparity synthesis page (already promoted) and the Amazon river system documentation. It is mercury-only. Multi-metal criterion still unmet; ASGM-plus-Pb/Cd paper still needed for promotion.

### Three EFSA food additive opinions — pattern noted but not synthesis-worthy

Batch 14 yielded three EFSA opinions (E 422 glycerol, E 475 polyglycerol esters, E 476 PGPR) all recommending tightening EU heavy metal specification limits for food additives. These are low-exposure-contribution items individually and are best filed as sources for the EFSA and EU regulations pages rather than a synthesis thread.

### Wade 2022 cacao cadmium — strong ingredient-page update, not a new thread

`wade2022-cacao-cadmium-drivers` (489 site-years, 10 countries, soil Cd + pH as sole predictors) is a definitive A-tier synthesis paper for the cacao Cd contamination profile. It belongs on `ingredients/cacao.md` as a primary contributing source and on the mining-impacted thread as context, but does not seed a new synthesis page — the cacao Cd contamination story is documented by existing sources and this paper refines the mechanism.
