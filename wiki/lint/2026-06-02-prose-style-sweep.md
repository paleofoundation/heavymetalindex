Converting claim- and citation-dense lists to prose for marginal register gain risks dropping an anchor. Left as bullets.

- **chocolate.md:111 "minimal cadmium":** mechanistically anchored in the same sentence (white chocolate "contains cocoa butter but not cocoa solids," and the preceding sentences establish that the solids carry the Cd). Defensible. Left.
- **seaweed.md:94 "orders of magnitude above":** the seaweed value is anchored (5-80 mg/kg dw); supplying a terrestrial-plant baseline requires a source number not on the page, which would be fabrication. Left.
- **seaweed.md:123 "negligible processing-driven change":** a real gap, but the fix is a sourcing task (cite or cut), not a style edit. Routed to synthesis rather than fixed here.

Calibration fed back into [[docs/tasks/prose-style-sweep]]: the qualifier check stays scoped to consumer-facing risk magnitude (not evidence-quality hedges), and definition-label lists of discrete named items count as legitimate catalogs even in consumer/mechanism sections.
