## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class.** The wiki has authentic strengths — strict per-claim provenance, an explicit wiki/HMTc firewall, a deterministic standards engine, and a live autonomy loop that keeps the corpus current — but it is missing the formal apparatus that defines a Cochrane review: a pre-registered protocol, a documented search strategy, a PRISMA-style flow record, dual independent screening, and a formal risk-of-bias instrument. Today a hostile plaintiff's expert can ask "show me your inclusion/exclusion criteria, your search dates, your screening disagreements, and your risk-of-bias scoring per study" and the wiki cannot produce those artifacts on demand.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 957 sources against a stated target of ~23,000 is roughly 4% coverage, and 305 entries are in `BLOCKED` pool state with no published reason taxonomy. Ingest leans on OpenAlex; there is no documented PubMed/Embase/Web of Science query strategy, no grey-literature protocol, and no PRISMA flow. Cochrane and EFSA both publish their search strings and dates; this wiki does not. Below FDA Closer to Zero on documentation, well below EFSA (~4) and Cochrane (5).

**Source provenance — 4/5.** This is the strongest dimension. Every wiki claim must trace to a `wiki/sources/<cite-key>.md` page, lint fails on provenance gaps (Part 18), routing is deterministic and audited (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`), and `routing_unresolved` is at 6 — a tractable backlog, not systemic rot. Loses a point because A/B/C tiering is coarser than GRADE, and `evidence_tier` is assigned at ingest without a documented adjudication record.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** The HMTc threshold side is genuinely Cochrane-grade: P97/P45 is locked, the row-pass calibration is published, the math is deterministic code in `tools/evidence/`, and rationale tags are required. The wiki-synthesis side is weaker — synthesis triggers are documented (Part 9), but there is no published protocol for prose synthesis, no declared assumptions for moment-matching expansion at the wiki level, no formal screening criteria, and no risk-of-bias instrument. The two halves are at different maturity levels.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Structured artifacts (routing audit, pool state, `review_events.jsonl`, `contamination_profile` blocks) are fully replicable from `wiki/sources/` and the deterministic generators. Synthesis prose is LLM-authored: a second Claude session running the same trigger on the same evidence will produce semantically equivalent but not byte-identical prose, and there is no recorded prompt/temperature/seed/model-version stamp on synthesis events. An external reviewer can re-derive numerical claims; they cannot bit-exact re-derive narrative synthesis.

**Currency — 4/5.** 684 commits, 786 new source pages and 23 synthesis cells written in 7 days, automated triggers on doubled-`n_studies` and 2× value disagreements, and an active autonomy loop. The synthesis confidence distribution (16 null→low, 5 null→medium, 2 null→high, zero upgrades from existing confidence) shows the system is breadth-first and synthesis lag is the real failure mode — but currency itself is a strength. Comparable to a Cochrane living review.

**Independence — 3/5.** Structurally well-designed: separate domains (`heavymetalindex.com` vs `heavymetaltested.com`), explicit firewall in CLAUDE.md Part 2, direction-of-drift check on substantive edits, brand-firewall in Part 12, standards arithmetic excluded from `wiki/` by governance rule. The structural design is unusually thoughtful. The operational reality is still that the certifier operates the wiki, with no external editorial board, no independent oversight, and no public conflict-of-interest disclosures on synthesis pages. Cochrane reviews have declared author conflicts at the review level; this wiki does not yet.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness, weakest at 2/5)

**Vector 1 — Pre-register and version a search protocol.** Add `tools/autonomy/emit-search-protocol.mjs` that writes `docs/methodology/search-protocol-v<n>.md` containing: databases queried (OpenAlex IDs, PubMed eUtils, Embase, Web of Science when licensed), exact query strings per metal × matrix, date ranges, language inclusion, document-type filters, deduplication rules, and inclusion/exclusion criteria with reason codes. Version-controlled; later ingest runs read the active version as their policy file and stamp the version into source frontmatter as `discovered_under_protocol: v<n>`. Without this artifact, "we reviewed the complete literature" is an assertion, not a claim a regulatory-affairs auditor can verify.

**Vector 2 — Emit a PRISMA flow record per ingest batch.** Extend the ingest pipeline to append to `data/evidence/prisma/flow-<YYYY-MM>.jsonl`, one record per candidate paper, with fields: `query_id`, `retrieved_at`, `dedup_outcome`, `title_abstract_decision` (include/exclude/defer with reason code), `full_text_decision` (include/exclude with reason code), `final_disposition` (`ingested` / `BLOCKED` with the specific pool-admission failure / `out_of_scope`). The morning report renders this as a PRISMA 2020 flow diagram. The 305 current `BLOCKED` entries get backfilled with structured reasons. This converts "BLOCKED: 305" from an unexplained number into auditable disposition records.

**Vector 3 — Add a corpus-coverage audit against external authoritative bibliographies.** Build `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-coverage.mjs` that ingests the reference lists of high-authority bibliographies (FDA Closer to Zero technical documents, EFSA Pb and Cd Scientific Opinions, WHO/JECFA heavy metals monographs, IARC monographs touching Pb/Cd/iAs/Cr-VI, HBBF reports, Consumer Reports methodology pages) and computes set-difference against `wiki/sources/`. Emit `data/evidence/corpus-coverage-gaps-<date>.csv` ranked by citation count across the authoritative set. Feed top-N gaps into the manual-fetch tracker. This converts corpus completeness from "we ingest what OpenAlex returns" into "we can demonstrate we have read everything the recognized authorities read," which is the specific claim a plaintiff's expert will probe.
