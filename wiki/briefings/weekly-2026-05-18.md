## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki already exceeds Cochrane on provenance (100% of 3,840 claims traceable to cite-keyed sources, machine-checked), on replicability of the *synthesis math* (P97/P45 in deterministic code, sensitivity-banded across three parametric assumptions, versioned in 720 commits), and on currency (daily ingest, 787 source pages in the last 7 days vs. Cochrane's 5-year refresh cycle). It is not yet Cochrane-class because the *search and screening* layer — pre-registered protocol, PRISMA flow, double-screening, structured per-study risk-of-bias — exists only implicitly, and that is the precise surface a plaintiff's expert is trained to attack.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 3/5.** 957 sources, 86% A-tier (peer-reviewed/regulatory), broad geographic spread, P1–P5 tiered priority. But there is no documented database list (PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, CINAHL, gray-lit), no logged search strings, no PRISMA identification → screening → eligibility → inclusion flow, and no double-reviewer screening. Sources arrive via OpenAlex pulls plus manual fetch — a defensible *acquisition pipeline*, not a defensible *search protocol*. **Cochrane baseline: 4–5. EFSA: 4. WHO: 4. This wiki sits between FDA Closer to Zero (3) and EFSA (4).**

- **Source provenance — 5/5.** `claim-provenance-summary.json` reports 3,840/3,840 claims supported, lint blocks unsupported numerical claims, every source page carries an explicit cite-key, and routing fan-out is reproducible from frontmatter. Better than Cochrane's narrative provenance because it is machine-enforced rather than reader-trusted.

- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` and `docs/standards-methodology.md` lock the math (P97 clean / P45 dirty, native-basis, regulatory cap, four rationale tags including `precautionary` and `feasibility-driven`). Pool admission rules cover seven reporting shapes (sample-level → single-value-no-N). Sensitivity bands are defined (±25% stable / 25–50% provisional / >50% needs review). The gap to 5/5: no formal risk-of-bias instrument, and `confidence: low/medium/high` is not yet tied to a documented derivation rule (study count? heterogeneity? RoB-weighted? unclear).

- **Replicability — 4/5.** Synthesis is deterministic code: `build-hmtc-aggregate-distributions.mjs` plus the routing audit plus `review_events.jsonl` (1,181 events) let an external reviewer re-derive every `contamination_profile` cell from the source corpus on any historical commit. The weakness is upstream: a reviewer *cannot* re-derive *which sources were included in the corpus* without a documented search protocol, only *what was done with them once included*. Cochrane requires both.

- **Currency — 5/5.** Daily autonomy loop, weekly briefing, resynthesis triggers when `n_studies` doubles or contradictions emerge, routing audit on every source-page write. This is structurally faster than Cochrane (5-year updates), EFSA opinion cycles (multi-year), or FDA Closer to Zero milestones (years). The risk is the inverse problem — drift from over-update — which the synthesis-skip-no-downgrade events in `review_events.jsonl` show is being managed.

- **Independence — 4/5.** Part 2 firewall is documented, the two-direction drift check is named in CLAUDE.md, wiki and HMT&C live on architecturally separate domains (heavymetalindex.com vs. heavymetaltested.com), and Part 12 bars brand mentions in the wiki. The structural gap to 5/5: same operator (Paleo Foundation) on both sides, and the firewall has not been externally audited. Cochrane reviews carry per-author COI statements; this wiki carries a system-level COI declaration but no third-party verification. Defensible *as documented*, more defensible *once audited*.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

**a. Pre-register search protocols as first-class artifacts.** Create `data/evidence/search-protocols/<category>-<analyte>-<period>.md` with: databases queried (PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, gray-lit registries), exact query strings, date ranges, language restrictions, snowballing depth, hand-search journals, registry IDs (PROSPERO-equivalent UUIDs). Extend `tools/evidence/build-raw-ingest-inventory.ts` and the OpenAlex puller to record `ingest_protocol:` in each source's frontmatter and reject any source whose acquisition path is not protocol-bound. Then "the corpus" becomes the deterministic output of the protocol set, and an external reviewer can re-execute it.

**b. Emit a PRISMA flow diagram from the ingest pipeline.** Add `tools/evidence/build-prisma-summary.mjs`. Wire `ingest-manual-fetch-batch.mjs`, `ingest-marker-batch.mjs`, and the OpenAlex pulls to append per-batch records to `data/evidence/prisma-flow.jsonl` with the canonical counts: identified, deduplicated, title/abstract screened, excluded with reason code (off-topic, no primary data, language, retracted, paywall-unrecoverable), full-text screened, included. Render the cumulative flow as `wiki/methodology/prisma-flow.md` and bind it to `npm run prebuild` so the diagram is current at every deploy. This makes "we reviewed the complete literature" an auditable claim with numerator and denominator.

**c. Extend the audit subagent to emit a structured risk-of-bias block per primary source.** Add a `risk_of_bias:` map to source frontmatter capturing six items: analytical method documented, LOD/LOQ reported in finished-sample units, CRM-validated recovery, blanks/replicates reported, sample N adequate for the row, declared conflicts. Have `tools/autonomy/extract-source-values.mjs` populate it during ingest; have the Codex audit pass verify it. Then make pool admission weight a function of RoB score (downweight `weak`/`range_only_weak` further when methodology flags are present) and define `confidence: low/medium/high` in `docs/standards-methodology.md` as a GRADE-equivalent rule: study count × RoB-weighted heterogeneity × directness. The output: every `contamination_profile` cell carries a defended confidence rating tied to a documented instrument, not a synthesis-pass judgment call.

Executing all three closes the gap to Cochrane-class on the dimension hostile readers are most equipped to attack, without touching the dimensions where the wiki already exceeds the benchmark.
