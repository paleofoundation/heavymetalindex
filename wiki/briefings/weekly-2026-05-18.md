## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The provenance discipline (per-claim cite-keys, routing-audit enforcement, deterministic pooling math in code) and the architectural independence from HMTc already exceed what most regulatory science bodies achieve, including FDA Closer to Zero and arguably EFSA. But the synthesis throughput is currently 50-100x behind ingest velocity (8 unique pages touched against 787 new source pages this week), and the wiki lacks the formal pre-registered protocols, dual-reviewer adjudication, and per-study risk-of-bias scoring that define Cochrane's procedural floor — so the corpus is becoming comprehensive faster than the synthesis is becoming defensible.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 3/5.** 958 sources with active OpenAlex pull and a manual-fetch backlog is substantial, but there is no PRISMA-style search-strategy registry per (ingredient, metal) cell: no documented database list, query strings, date ranges, dedup logic, or exclusion-with-counts. `pool_state` shows 305 BLOCKED vs 48 READY (86% of cells cannot yet produce a standard), which is the corpus-completeness signal showing through to the synthesis layer. Cochrane: 5 (PRISMA-mandated). EFSA: 4. This wiki has the scale but not the auditable search-strategy artifact.

**Source provenance — 5/5.** Cite-key system, frontmatter-driven routing, `routing_unresolved.csv` at zero, lint failure on un-provenanced numerical claims, and 730 commits of reconstructible history. This is genuinely Cochrane-class and arguably tighter than Cochrane's typical citation discipline because routing is enforced by pre-commit hook rather than by reviewer attention.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** Pooling math is locked in code (`basis-conversion.mjs`, P97/P45 with documented row-pass calibration rationale in `hmtc-governing-principles.md`), and the rationale-tag vocabulary (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) forces deviations to be named. The gap to 5: per-(ingredient, metal) synthesis cells are not pre-registered before evidence accumulates, so inclusion criteria are inferred post-hoc rather than declared in advance. WHO monographs: 4-5. This sits right at WHO.

**Replicability — 4/5.** Git history plus deterministic routing audit means an external reviewer can reconstruct any wiki state on any date and re-derive the routing from `wiki/sources/`. But Codex adjudication during ingest involves LLM judgment that is not bit-deterministic; an external re-run would produce slightly different `evidence_tier` assignments and synthesis prose. The structured fields (numerical pools, routing, frontmatter) are fully replicable; the prose synthesis is not.

**Currency — 2/5.** This is the load-bearing weakness. 787 new source pages this week, 23 synthesis cells written, zero promotions from low→medium or medium→high confidence. Most freshly ingested literature has not yet influenced any wiki claim. The `confidence_distribution` showing 16 null→low and 0 upward promotions means the synthesis pass is operating only in initial-population mode and not running the incremental-update or full-resynthesis triggers from Part 9. A plaintiff's expert asking "your wiki cites 958 sources; how many of them have been read together?" would currently get an uncomfortable answer. Cochrane: 4-5 (living systematic reviews update on a defined cadence). This wiki: 2.

**Independence — 5/5.** Architectural separation (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), the Part 2 firewall with bidirectional drift checks, the silent-threshold-deviation lint, no brand mentions, EU database-rights positioning, and standards arithmetic explicitly excluded from `wiki/` and placed in `data/workbench/`. The independence design exceeds Cochrane's typical posture (Cochrane reviews are produced by clinicians who often have specialty-society ties); it is closer to the architectural separation between IARC monographs and WHO policy.

**Composite: 3, 5, 4, 4, 2, 5 — average 3.83.** Cochrane baseline ≈ 4.5. The wiki is one dimension (currency) away from Cochrane parity and one dimension (corpus completeness documentation) away from clear Cochrane-class status.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: Currency)

**Vector A — Wire the synthesis trigger into the autonomy loop on routing-audit deltas.** Build `tools/autonomy/dequeue-synthesis-triggers.mjs`. After each ingest batch, diff the routing audit against the prior audit; for every (ingredient, metal) cell whose contributing-source count crossed a Part 9 threshold (2 for initial; 4/8/16/32 for full resynthesis; >2x value disagreement for contradiction), append a synthesis task to `data/evidence/synthesis_queue.jsonl`. The autonomy loop runs N synthesis tasks per cycle from this queue before starting the next ingest batch. This binds synthesis throughput to ingest throughput by construction rather than leaving it as a separate workflow that depends on Karen requesting it.

**Vector B — Diagnose the 305 BLOCKED pools and route the blockers back into ingest.** Build `tools/evidence/diagnose-blocked-pools.mjs`. For each BLOCKED (row, metal) pool, classify the blocker into a fixed vocabulary: `insufficient_n` (need more sources), `basis_conversion_missing` (sources exist but in non-native basis without conversion factor), `analyte_speciation_gap` (have tAs but row needs iAs), `censoring_unhandled` (LOD/LOQ flagging incomplete), `jurisdiction_mismatch` (sources from non-applicable jurisdictions). Emit `data/evidence/pool_blocked_diagnoses.csv` with one row per (row, metal, blocker, suggested_action). The autonomy loop reads this file and routes: `insufficient_n` → manual-fetch backlog with a generated PubMed/OpenAlex query string; `basis_conversion_missing` → basis-conversion task; `analyte_speciation_gap` → speciation-extraction subagent. Currently the 305 BLOCKED count is an opaque metric; this turns it into a worklist.

**Vector C — Add a staleness-weighted resynthesis prioritizer to the morning report.** Extend `tools/autonomy/morning-report.mjs` to compute, for every cell where `n_studies >= 2`, a staleness score = `(n_sources_added_since_last_reviewed) × (days_since_last_reviewed)`. Surface the top-50 in `data/evidence/autonomy/resynthesis-priority-<date>.md` and have the autonomy loop dequeue from this list in addition to the new-trigger queue from Vector A. This forces the loop to attack the back-pressure backlog (the cells that have silently fallen out of currency since the last visit) rather than only handling cells that crossed a fresh threshold. Without this, Vector A would keep currency healthy for newly ingested papers but leave the existing populated cells to drift indefinitely.

All three are executable by the existing autonomy infrastructure (autonomy loop, evidence-tooling pattern, morning-report extension) and would raise the currency dimension from 2 to a defensible 4 within a normal ingest cadence.
