## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The architectural commitments — provenance-preserving source pages, deterministic pooling math, explicit wiki/HMTc firewall, routing audit as system layer rather than session task — are stronger than what most Cochrane reviews bother to formalize, and on independence the wiki is actually ahead. But 305 of 367 (ingredient × metal) cells are `BLOCKED` for synthesis, no per-cell inclusion/exclusion protocol is pre-registered, and the structured extractor demonstrably cannot parse a meaningful tail of admitted sources (`extraction-unparseable.csv` was modified today). A hostile expert would land the corpus-completeness punch before they got to the methodology punch.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane baseline 4-5; EFSA ~4. 948 sources is non-trivial, but 83% of cells (305/367 pool states) lack the source mass to enter synthesis. Ingest is opportunistic — what's in `raw/Manual Fetch Kimi/`, what OpenAlex returns on a 7-day batch — not driven by a per-cell search-string protocol that would identify every plausibly-relevant primary study for, say, (rice cereal × inorganic arsenic). No PRISMA flow diagram exists per cell. This is the dimension a class-action expert attacks first: "you didn't search for studies you'd have had to exclude."

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Strong. Per-claim provenance is a design invariant (Part 4, Part 14); the lint pass treats unprovenanced numerical claims as failures; the routing audit makes the source-to-page map machine-auditable and `routing_unresolved.csv` sits at 2. This is at or above Cochrane baseline. Half-point deduction because audit evidence that ingredient/product page *body* tables cite per-row-per-claim (rather than per-page) is not fully visible from the dashboard.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** Standards-setting math is exemplary: P97/P45 locked, 90/30 row-pass intent published, basis conventions in code, regulatory-cap arithmetic deterministic, rationale tags (`literature-baseline`/`regulatory-alignment`/`precautionary`/`feasibility-driven`) enforced. That side beats FDA Closer to Zero (~3) and matches WHO monographs (~4-5). But the *synthesis* side — which papers enter a cell's pool, which get excluded, why — is governed only by pool-admission rules in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` and ad-hoc triage. There is no PROSPERO-equivalent pre-registration per cell. Methodology is transparent in retrospect, not pre-committed.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Math is reproducible: pooling code is deterministic, native-basis conversions logged per source, routing audit regenerable from `wiki/sources/`. An external reviewer can re-run the pooling. They cannot, today, re-derive *which* papers should have been in the pool — that requires the search-string protocol that doesn't exist yet. The unparseable-extraction problem compounds this: papers that should pool-admit via moment-matching are silently rejected because the extractor doesn't capture the moments. Pool admission is the gate, and the gate has a non-deterministic failure mode.

**Currency — 3/5.** Infrastructure is good: `last_reviewed` and `last_full_resynthesis` are first-class fields; synthesis triggers on n_studies doubling, contradiction, and source ingest; routing audit fires on file watcher and pre-commit. 776 source pages in 7 days demonstrates real velocity. But 23 cells synthesized against 305 BLOCKED cells is a 13:1 backlog ratio at current cadence — currency of the synthesis layer lags ingest by an order of magnitude. Cochrane living-review baseline is ~4; this is on track but not there.

**Independence — 5/5.** Architecturally above Cochrane baseline. The wiki/HMTc separation is enforced at four layers: two domains (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), two repos prospectively (Part 26), per-commit drift check obligation (Part 2), and lint rule for silent threshold deviation (Part 18). Standards math is explicitly excluded from `wiki/`. No brand pages. HMTc-certified brands never mentioned. Cochrane relies on declarative COI; this wiki relies on structural impossibility. The remaining risk is enforcement of the per-commit drift articulation, not the architecture.

### 3. Improvement vectors

Weakest dimension: **corpus completeness**. Three executable build steps:

1. **Per-cell search-string generator with PRISMA-style screening log.** Add `tools/autonomy/generate-cell-search-strategy.mjs` that takes each `BLOCKED` (ingredient × metal) cell from the pool-state audit and emits a deterministic OpenAlex/PubMed/EFSA query (controlled vocabulary terms for the ingredient, the metal/analyte synonyms from the governing analyte list, jurisdictional filters from `data/governance/jurisdictions.json`), runs it, writes the result list to `data/evidence/cell-searches/<ingredient>-<metal>.csv` with hit count, then enqueues unscreened hits into the manual-fetch tracker tagged with the originating cell. Output is the "we searched for what we'd have had to exclude" artifact.

2. **Pre-registered inclusion/exclusion protocol per cell, gating synthesis.** Extend the synthesis trigger conditions in Part 9 with a precondition: a cell cannot move from `pending` to `populated` until `docs/cells/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` exists with inclusion criteria (population, analyte species, matrix specificity, jurisdictional scope, study-design tier requirements), exclusion criteria, search-string used, search date, and reviewer signature. The synthesis pass writes the PRISMA flow numbers (identified / screened / included / excluded with reasons) into the cell's `contamination_profile` sub-block on first population. Lint failure if a populated cell lacks its protocol file.

3. **Extend structured extractor to capture moment-matching inputs from summary-only sources.** The governing principles already admit summary-only sources via log-normal/gamma pseudo-sample expansion, but `extraction-unparseable.csv` reveals the extractor isn't capturing the moments. Add typed extraction targets for: arithmetic mean, geometric mean, median, max, SD, GSD, IQR (Q1/Q3), LB/UB bounds, detection limit, N, censoring fraction — keyed by (matrix, analyte, basis). Route partial captures (e.g., mean+SD but no N) to a `extraction-partial.csv` reviewer queue rather than rejecting them entirely. This collapses the BLOCKED backlog directly: many of those 305 cells have admissible summary sources sitting in `raw/` that the extractor is silently dropping.
