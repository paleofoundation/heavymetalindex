## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — on architecture, not yet on substance.** The wiki has the structural prerequisites Cochrane demands (per-claim provenance, deterministic reproducibility, locked methodology, version-controlled audit trail), but its current substantive output — 51 READY pool cells against 302 BLOCKED, 23 synthesized cells in the last week — is far below the density a Cochrane review of this scope would carry, and the corpus-identification protocol is not yet PRISMA-grade. The bones are right; the muscle is thin.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 963 sources with an OpenAlex autonomy loop is a credible start, but there is no documented PRISMA-style search protocol per (ingredient, metal) cell: no declared query strings, no multi-database search (PubMed/Embase/Europe PMC/CAB Abstracts/AGRIS/CENTRAL/gray literature), no hand-search of conference proceedings, no inclusion/exclusion criteria recorded per topic, no PRISMA flow diagram showing identified/screened/excluded/included counts. 302 BLOCKED pools means most cells cannot yet be synthesized — that may be a corpus gap or an extraction gap, and the system doesn't currently distinguish. Cochrane sits at 5; EFSA at ~4; the wiki today is below EFSA.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim is required to trace to a source page; `routing_unresolved.csv` is at 0; 920 commits give per-edit attribution. This is genuinely stronger than EFSA opinions, which often cite paragraph-level rather than claim-level. Held back from 5 because per-claim citation inside synthesized prose is page-level, not page+section+sentence-level — a plaintiff's expert could still ask "show me the exact sentence in source X that supports this number," and the answer would require human re-reading.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** The Governing Principles document and the locked P97/P45 math with the 90/30 row-pass calibration rationale are unusually explicit — most certification programs hide this. Standards math is in code, deterministic, versioned. Cochrane sits at 5 partly because each review pre-registers a protocol in PROSPERO before data extraction begins; the wiki has methodology lock but no per-topic pre-registered protocol with a timestamped commit-before-extraction. Beats FDA Closer to Zero (~3).

**Replicability — 5/5.** Every transformation is a script in `tools/`, every input is in `raw/` or `data/evidence/`, every output is regenerable from git history. An external reviewer with the repo and Node can re-derive the entire build. This genuinely beats Cochrane, which still leans on RevMan spreadsheets and PDF protocols that are hard to mechanically re-execute.

**Currency — 4/5.** 781 new source pages and 97 OpenAlex pulls in 7 days demonstrates a live corpus, not a snapshot. Held back from 5 because the synthesis layer lags: 23 cells written against 781 ingested means the cell-state of most ingredient/metal pages does not yet reflect the new evidence, and there is no per-cell `evidence_currency` metric exposed so a reader could see "this synthesis reflects 12 of 47 known sources." Cochrane reviews are updated on cycles (often 2-5 years); this could be better than Cochrane on currency once synthesis throughput catches up.

**Independence — 2/5.** This is the dimension a plaintiff's expert will attack first. Architectural separation between wiki and HMTc is documented as policy (CLAUDE.md Part 2) and enforced by lint (silent-threshold-deviation check), but the Paleo Foundation runs both, with the same staff, same funding, and no independent editorial board. Cochrane reviews go through external peer review by people with no financial interest in the conclusion; this wiki does not. The drift-direction check ("name the direction") is a discipline, not an institutional firewall. A hostile reading is: "self-published evidence base operated by the same entity that monetizes the conclusions." The mitigation (Part 2's two-directions-of-drift rule, the deferred external review brief in `docs/external-review-brief.md`) is good policy but is not yet a structural defense.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest dimensions are corpus completeness (2) and independence (2). Three executable steps:

**1. Add a per-cell search-protocol register.** Extend the autonomy loop with `tools/autonomy/build-search-protocol.mjs` that emits one `data/evidence/search-protocols/<ingredient>-<metal>.json` per active cell, recording: declared queries (Boolean strings), databases queried (initially OpenAlex; add PubMed E-utilities and Europe PMC REST in the same pass), date range, language filter, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and PRISMA flow counts (identified, deduplicated, screened, full-text-assessed, included, excluded-with-reason). Render these as `wiki/methodology/search-protocols/<cell>.md`. This converts "we ingested whatever showed up" into "we ran this protocol; here is the flow." Directly addresses corpus completeness from 2 toward 4.

**2. Add a per-source risk-of-bias extractor.** Build `tools/evidence/extract-rob.mjs` that runs a five-domain rubric per source (sampling frame, analytical method validation, censoring/LOD handling, selective reporting, funding/conflict declaration), writes to `data/evidence/risk-of-bias.csv`, surfaces a RoB summary on every source page, and feeds the synthesis confidence rating so a cell's `confidence` reflects the weakest contributing source rather than count alone. The current A/B/C evidence tier is coarser than Cochrane's RoB 2 / ROBINS-I; this closes the gap and gives the synthesis layer a quantitative basis for downgrading confidence. Lifts corpus completeness and methodology transparency simultaneously.

**3. Auto-publish a quarterly independence audit.** Build `tools/autonomy/build-independence-audit.mjs` that joins the routing audit, the staff Standards Workbench, and the published HMTc thresholds; for every certified row, computes the delta between the wiki's literature-baseline percentile and the final HMTc value; flags any deviation that lacks a `precautionary` / `feasibility-driven` / `regulatory-alignment` rationale; writes the result to `wiki/governance/independence-audit-<YYYY-QQ>.md` on a cron. This converts the drift-direction discipline from a per-session habit into a publishable artifact that an external reviewer can inspect without trusting the operator. Independence rating cannot reach 5 without a genuinely external editorial board, but a published, mechanically generated audit moves it from 2 toward 3-4 with no organizational restructuring required.
