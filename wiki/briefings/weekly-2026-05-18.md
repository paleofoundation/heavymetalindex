## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The infrastructure choices that matter most for hostile scrutiny — a documented corpus-genesis search, per-claim source provenance enforced by a routing layer, deterministic pooling math sitting outside the public wiki, and an explicit two-direction firewall against the certification program — are already in place and would survive a deposition. What is not yet at Cochrane parity is corpus saturation (≈3% of the 29,600-file raw corpus is ingested), synthesis throughput (23 cells/week against ~2,450 ingredient×analyte cells needed), and the absence of a PRISMA-style flow diagram with pre-registered inclusion/exclusion screening counts.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 3/5.** A documented 23,262-PDF PMC Open Access + targeted regulatory base is defensible, but inclusion is *bounded to open-access* (paywalled toxicology and analytical-chemistry literature is excluded by design). Cochrane standard is access-agnostic and uses paid databases (Embase, CINAHL, trial registries). The wiki is approximately at EFSA-corpus level for what's included, but with a narrower access tier and only 966/29,600 files actually compiled into source pages.
- **Source provenance — 4.5/5.** Every wiki claim is required to trace to a `wiki/sources/<cite-key>.md` page; the routing audit (`routing_unresolved.csv` = 0) enforces fan-out; lint fails on provenance gaps; the database-rights argument depends on this and it's structurally enforced. This matches Cochrane.
- **Methodology transparency — 3.5/5.** `wiki/methodology/corpus-genesis-2026-04.md` documents 240 PMC queries reproducibly. `docs/standards-methodology.md` + `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` lock the pooling math, P97/P45 calibration, native-basis rule, and rationale tags. What's missing: a PROSPERO-style pre-registered protocol, explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria as a single document, and PRISMA-format flow counts (identified → screened → eligible → included with reasons-for-exclusion). Cochrane would not accept synthesis without the latter; EFSA monographs typically include them.
- **Replicability — 3.5/5.** The standards arithmetic is in code and deterministic. Git has 937 commits with batch reports. But replicating the synthesis state requires running the autonomy toolchain, not just reading documented procedure; the trigger-condition heuristics for synthesis (n_studies doubling, 2x disagreement) are documented but not yet emitting a publishable audit trail per cell. Cochrane gold standard is "an external statistician can re-derive every number in the review from the supplementary data."
- **Currency — 3.5/5.** The 7-day window shows live ingestion (97 OpenAlex pulls, 783 new sources, 937 commits total). However, 82% of pools (302/367) are in BLOCKED state, meaning the corpus is growing faster than it is being made synthesis-ready — currency of the *ingested* layer outpaces currency of the *synthesized* layer. Cochrane updates on a multi-year cycle but maintains 100% synthesis closure; this wiki is at the inverse balance.
- **Independence — 4.5/5.** CLAUDE.md Part 2 is the strongest editorial-independence frame I've seen in a project of this type — the two-directions-of-drift rule, the deliberate `heavymetalindex.com` ≠ `heavymetaltested.com` split, the "wiki never mentions HMTc-certified brands" rule, and the standards-arithmetic-not-in-wiki rule together exceed what FDA Closer to Zero (~3) and HBBF (~2) achieve, and meet Cochrane's editorial separation requirement. Half a point withheld only because the independence is *governed by* a document the certifier wrote about itself; an external editorial board with veto power would close that gap.

**Cochrane baseline:** 4-5 across all six. **Current weighted position:** 3.8 average. The strongest dimensions (provenance, independence) carry the framing argument; the weakest (corpus completeness, methodology transparency) are exactly the dimensions a plaintiff's expert would probe first.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeted at the weakest dimension: methodology transparency)

1. **Build a PRISMA flow generator.** Add `tools/evidence/build-prisma-flow.mjs` that reads the corpus-genesis manifest, the OpenAlex pull logs, the manual-fetch trackers, and `wiki/sources/*.md` frontmatter to emit `data/evidence/prisma-flow-<date>.csv` with the four canonical PRISMA stages (Identification, Screening, Eligibility, Included) and counts with reasons-for-exclusion. Then write `wiki/methodology/prisma-2026.md` rendered from that CSV. Without this, "we have reviewed the complete literature" is asserted but not visually demonstrated. With it, the assertion is reproducible from artifacts.

2. **Author a pre-registered protocol document.** Add `docs/protocol-prisma-2026.md` capturing: PICO-equivalent framing for each metal × food-matrix question, locked inclusion criteria (publication date range, language, study type, matrix scope, analyte scope, LOD floor), locked exclusion criteria (in-vitro-only, animal-model-only without dosimetry, single-N case reports unless regulatory), and the screening-disagreement resolution rule. Commit it with `protocol_locked: <date>` frontmatter and never silently mutate. This is the PROSPERO analog and is the single document a plaintiff's expert will demand first.

3. **Unblock the 302 BLOCKED pools by extending the structured extractor.** Run a one-shot diagnostic that categorizes the BLOCKED reasons (likely: basis ambiguity, statistic-type unclear, censoring-rule unclear, N-not-reported, analyte-species unclear) and extend `tools/evidence/structured-extractor.mjs` (or the Codex adjudication prompt) to capture each of those fields explicitly per source-row. The current 51 READY / 302 BLOCKED ratio is the single biggest obstacle to closing the methodology-transparency and currency gaps simultaneously: every BLOCKED pool is a documented source whose data the wiki cannot yet defend in synthesis. Target a >70% READY ratio before any external review brief is sent.
