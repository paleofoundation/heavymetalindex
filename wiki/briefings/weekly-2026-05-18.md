## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict: **not yet Cochrane-class**

The wiki has corpus scale, per-claim provenance, and version-locked threshold math that already meet or approach Cochrane discipline on the dimensions that matter most for legal defensibility — the routing audit makes provenance gaps a build failure, and the P97/P45 math is deterministic code rather than reviewer judgment. It falls short of Cochrane-class because three artifacts a plaintiff's expert will demand are absent: a pre-registered protocol per (metal, matrix), a PRISMA flow that names what was excluded and why, and an editorially independent reviewer panel that is structurally separate from the certifier's payroll.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 954 sources is substantial, but `pool_state` shows 305 BLOCKED to 48 READY — roughly 5% of candidates currently contribute to pooled distributions. There is no documented search-strategy file naming the databases queried (MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL, FDA TDS, EFSA panel opinions, agency registers), no declared inclusion/exclusion vocabulary, and no PRISMA-style identified→screened→included accounting. This is "comprehensive opportunistic collection," not systematic search. Cochrane baseline: 5. EFSA: 4. FDA Closer to Zero: 2.
- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every numerical wiki claim must trace to a `wiki/sources/<cite-key>.md` page; lint fails the build on orphaned claims; the routing audit deterministically fans sources to target pages. This is genuinely strong and arguably Cochrane-comparable in mechanics. Held below 5 because evidence tiering (A/B/C) is a reviewer judgment field, not a formal RoB 2.0 or ROBINS-I instrument with per-domain scoring.
- **Methodology transparency — 3/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 locks the per-analyte math, names the 90/30 row-pass policy choice explicitly, requires rationale tags on every staged value, and lives in code. But there is no PROSPERO-equivalent pre-registration per synthesis cell, no formal protocol-amendment log distinct from git history, and the clean/dirty subcategory classification is not pre-specified per category before evidence is loaded. Cochrane: 5. WHO monograph: 4-5.
- **Replicability — 3/5.** Pooling math is deterministic Node; routing audit and provisional-scaffold logic are generator-driven. An external reviewer can re-derive a P97 from the listed source pages. But the corpus mutates daily (782 new sources this week alone) and there is no `as-of <date>` snapshot mechanism — re-deriving the synthesis as it stood on a specific publication date is not currently possible.
- **Currency — 4/5.** Autonomy loop is ingesting ~100 papers/day, synthesis triggers fire on routing-audit signals, regulatory-value drift is in the lint pass. This is closer to Cochrane's "living review" model than most static reference works. Held below 5 because currency is bounded by completeness — a fast-updating subset of the literature is still a subset.
- **Independence — 2/5.** The architectural firewall is real: separate domain, gap-stating rule, no brand mentions, wiki-to-HMTc one-way reference only. But the operator identity is the same legal entity (Paleo Foundation), and there is no external reviewer registry, no declared-interests page, no funding-source disclosure on synthesis pages. The defense reduces to "the firewall is documented and audited by Claude," which is structurally weaker than Cochrane's formal independence or EFSA's declared-interests regime. Cochrane: 5. EFSA: 4.

### 3. Improvement vectors

Weakest dimensions are corpus completeness (2) and independence (2). Each vector below is a build step the autonomy loop can execute without Karen intervention.

1. **Pre-register a per-(metal, matrix) protocol and freeze it before synthesis publishes.** Create `data/protocols/<metal>-<matrix>.yaml` containing: database list with exact query strings, inclusion vocabulary (matrix slugs, analyte names, study designs), exclusion reason codes (out-of-scope-matrix, secondary-source, retracted, non-peer-reviewed-non-agency), declared corpus-freeze SHA, and planned subgroup analyses (clean/dirty, geographic, basis). Build `tools/protocols/freeze-protocol.mjs` to hash the protocol + corpus snapshot and refuse synthesis runs against unfrozen cells. Lift the Part 18 lint to fail when a `contamination_profile` block was last_resynthesized without a matching frozen protocol hash. This converts "we ingested what we found" into "we declared our scope before we looked."

2. **Emit a PRISMA flow per (metal, matrix) from extractor output.** Build `tools/evidence/build-prisma-flow.mjs` reading OpenAlex/PubMed query results → screened-count → excluded-with-reason-code → eligible → included → contributing-to-pool. Output a structured JSONL audit at `data/evidence/prisma/<metal>-<matrix>.jsonl` and render a Mermaid flow diagram into the corresponding synthesis page footer. Extend the autonomy loop to re-emit the flow on every corpus delta. This makes the BLOCKED:READY ratio auditable: the 305 BLOCKED candidates currently sit in an opaque queue; PRISMA flow names each exclusion reason and lets a plaintiff's expert see exactly what was filtered.

3. **Productionize the external-reviewer handoff already drafted in `docs/external-review-engagement-plan.md`.** Extend `data/evidence/audit-queue.csv` with `external_reviewer_id`, `coi_declared_at`, and `review_decision` columns. Build `tools/governance/external-review-handoff.mjs` to generate review packets (source PDFs + extracted values + draft synthesis + protocol hash) for every cell crossing from low→medium confidence, and refuse the confidence promotion until a non-Paleo-Foundation reviewer signs the packet. Publish a `wiki/governance/reviewers.md` registry with declared interests per reviewer. This is the single intervention that moves independence from a documented-architectural-firewall (2) to a credentialed-external-panel (4) — the form of independence that survives a hostile deposition.
