## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The methodology infrastructure (published 10-database search protocol, PRISMA-equivalent coverage page, deterministic pooling math, architecturally enforced editorial firewall, per-claim cite-key provenance, public errata channel) is at or above EFSA/WHO baseline and meaningfully ahead of FDA Closer to Zero. The wiki is not yet Cochrane-class because (a) the curatorial board is a one-person scaffold with all external reviewers marked PENDING INVITATION, (b) the pre-GA quality-assurance audit promised by `methodology/quality-assurance-audit-protocol` has not yet been executed, and (c) the corpus has promoted only ~1,140 of 23,260 identified papers, with no published within-scope exhaustiveness claim that a hostile expert could test.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,142 source pages against 23,260 papers identified by the search; 16,141 of those sit in P5 untriaged and 6,571 in P4. More damaging: there is no published per-HMTc-row exhaustiveness ratio. Cochrane reviews trade breadth for *within-scope* completeness near 100%; this wiki currently can claim neither breadth nor a defensible within-scope number. EFSA opinions sit at ~4 because their scope-bounded searches close out. Hostile read: "you've sampled 5% of the literature you yourself identified."

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every numeric claim links to a cite-keyed source page; the routing audit enforces source-to-target mapping; lint blocks provenance gaps; per-claim audit logs are written. Drag: `routing_malformed.csv` is at 603 rows (source pages missing required frontmatter), and 15 unresolved routing entries declare ingredient slugs (sage, whey, human milk, jujube fruit) with no destination page. The audit machinery is Cochrane-class; the residual hygiene backlog isn't.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `methodology/search-strategy.md` documents every database, the dedupe rules, the auto-fetch scoring rubric, and the polite-pool identifier. `methodology/quality-assurance-audit-protocol.md` declares the stratified sampling design before any audit runs (i.e., pre-registered). Coverage page auto-regenerates from on-disk data. Code is on a public GitHub remote. Weakness: top-level `methodology.md` carries `status: stub`, and the QA audit has not yet produced an error-rate publication — pre-registration without execution is half the protocol.

**Replicability — 3/5.** An external reviewer can re-run the search (protocols + code are public), re-derive routing (audit script is deterministic), and recompute pooling math (the standards-workbench code is on disk). They cannot yet verify error rates against a published audit, and they cannot reproduce synthesis decisions where the trigger fired on `low-confidence` two-source initial-population (24 of 30 cells written this week). Cochrane GRADE assessments make every downgrade visible; here the confidence label is present but the *reasoning* for each downgrade isn't logged in machine-readable form.

**Currency — 5/5.** 135 new source pages in 7 days, 140 PDFs auto-pulled from OpenAlex, gap-driven wishlist daemon running nightly. This is materially *faster* than Cochrane refresh cycles (multi-year) and faster than EFSA opinion updates. This is the wiki's strongest axis and a genuine beyond-baseline claim.

**Independence — 3/5.** Architectural firewall is real and enforced (one-way linking, separate domains, no brand mentions in wiki, no HMT&C-certified-brand strings anywhere in `wiki/`, evidence-state separation between `approved_for_public` and `approved_for_internal`). COI posture is documented. But the curators page is a scaffold: Karen is the sole named curator and editor-in-chief, and she also operates HMT&C. Three external-reviewer slots are filled with `[PENDING INVITATION]`. The hostile-expert attack writes itself: "one person, who runs the certification program, is editor-in-chief of the reference that justifies the certification program's thresholds." Architecture answers it; staffing does not yet.

**Vector A — Per-row exhaustiveness audit.** Extend `tools/evidence/build-coverage.mjs` (or a sibling) to compute, for each locked HMTc row, the ratio of P1–P3 papers from the literature search whose abstracts mention any of the row's matrix slugs and have been promoted to `wiki/sources/`.
