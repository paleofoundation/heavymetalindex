## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — closer in some dimensions, structurally behind in others.** The wiki's compiled-evidence architecture, deterministic routing layer, per-claim provenance, and editorial firewall between literature synthesis and HMTc threshold-setting exceed what FDA Closer to Zero or industry self-cert programs offer and are broadly comparable to EFSA scientific opinions; the gap to Cochrane proper is corpus completeness (1,107 sources vs. Cochrane's exhaustive multi-database protocol-driven sweep) and the absence of a published, pre-registered methodology that an external party could replicate without read-access to this repo.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5/5.** 1,107 ingested sources against an estimated 23,000-paper universe (per CLAUDE.md Part 11), with 371 BLOCKED pool cells dwarfing 58 READY. The discover/wishlist autonomy loop is the right mechanism, but it has not yet driven coverage to the floor where a hostile reviewer would fail to surface a counter-citation. Cochrane: 4-5. EFSA: 4. Current state: clearly below both.

**Source provenance — 4.5/5.** Per-claim provenance is architecturally enforced: every numerical claim must link to a source page or it is a lint failure (Part 18), the routing audit ties source → target page deterministically, and `data/evidence/` carries the structured trace. This is at or above EFSA practice and matches Cochrane's "every effect estimate traces to a study" standard. The 0.5-point deduction is for provisional scaffolds and the 15 routing_unresolved entries, which create transient gaps.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** The HMTc threshold math (P97/P45, 90/30 row-pass calibration, native-basis pooling, regulatory-cap binding rule) is locked, versioned, and rationale-tagged — that part is genuinely strong. But the wiki *synthesis* methodology (how a `contamination_profile` cell is derived from N source pages, what triggers full resynthesis, how contradictions are arbitrated) lives in CLAUDE.md Part 9 and is not published as a standalone protocol document an outside reviewer could cite. Cochrane publishes a Handbook; HMI publishes commit history. Closer to Zero: 3. EFSA: 4. WHO: 4-5.

**Replicability — 2.5/5.** An external reviewer with repo access could re-derive most synthesis from source pages plus the routing audit plus `review_events.jsonl` — *if* that JSONL is consistently populated (CLAUDE.md Part 9 flags this as not-yet-true: "until that file is consistently populated, page-level fields are the working source of truth"). Without the event log, full resynthesis history is lost between snapshots. Pooling math is in code (good), but the inputs to a given pool decision are not yet reconstructible from a single artifact.

**Currency — 4/5.** 419 commits, 100 new sources in the last 7 days, daemon-driven gap-fill ingest, daily checkpoints, OpenAlex pull live. Currency is a genuine strength — most reference works update annually or slower; this one updates daily. The 1-point deduction: synthesis lags ingest (only 30 cells written for 100 new sources), and the resynthesis-trigger conditions are formally defined but not yet auditable against a "what is overdue" report.

**Independence — 4.5/5.** The wiki/HMTc firewall is architecturally enforced: standards arithmetic explicitly excluded from `wiki/` (Governing Principles §4, CLAUDE.md Part 2), threshold-setting outputs live in `data/workbench/standards/`, certified brands never named in wiki pages, the drift-direction check is a lint rule. This is structurally *stronger* than EFSA (whose opinions sometimes shade toward EU policy positions) and comparable to Cochrane's editorial firewall against industry sponsorship. The 0.5-point deduction is unavoidable: the same organization operates both layers, which a hostile expert will name regardless of architecture.
