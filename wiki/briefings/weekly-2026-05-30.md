## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class, not yet there.** The structural commitments — per-claim provenance via the routing layer, methodology locked in code and governance documents, batch-commit reconstructibility, the literature/threshold firewall — are Cochrane-tier in design and in several respects exceed what EFSA scientific opinions or WHO monographs provide as audit surface. What blocks the verdict is corpus completeness: 1,117 sources against a heavy-metals food-literature universe in the tens of thousands, 371 BLOCKED pools versus 58 READY, and only 30 synthesis cells written in the most recent week against hundreds of (ingredient × metal) cells pending. The bones are Cochrane; the flesh is not yet there.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Continuous ingest, gap-driven OpenAlex queries, multi-DB discovery via paper-lookup, and PMC/preprint coverage are wired, but the absolute count (1,117) is small relative to the addressable literature, and BLOCKED:371 / READY:58 reveals pool admission is the bottleneck, not pure discovery. Cochrane baseline is 4-5 (exhaustive identification of all primary studies with documented search). EFSA opinions sit at ~4 on the strength of standardized terms-of-reference searches; HMI is currently below EFSA on this axis.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** The routing layer enforces (source → target page) traceability at the row level; lint fails the build on `routing_malformed.csv` or claims without source backlinks; the 15 unresolved routing entries are an addressable backlog, not a structural defect. This matches Cochrane (4-5) and exceeds FDA Closer to Zero (~3, which footnotes selectively).

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** P97/P45 math is locked, versioned, and in `tools/`; governance principles state the 90/30 row-pass intent and reject the obvious adversarial reframing ("why P97 and not P95"); pool admission rules, basis-conversion logic, and rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) are all declared. The remaining gap from 5 is that the discovery-side methodology (which queries fire, against which databases, on what cadence, with what admission criteria for inclusion in pools) is not yet pre-registered or PRISMA-shaped. WHO monographs sit at 4-5 here; HMI matches WHO on synthesis math, trails on search protocol.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Git history with batch commits per 200 papers, routing audit regenerable from `wiki/sources/`, deterministic pool math, and audit logs in `data/evidence/` mean an external reviewer with the repo can re-derive every synthesis cell. What stops a 5 is that the *corpus itself* is not reproducible: an external reviewer cannot re-run the exact discovery queries that produced this set of 1,117 sources because the query log, admission decisions, and exclusion rationale are not yet fully captured in a PRISMA-equivalent artifact. Cochrane requires both replicable synthesis *and* replicable corpus assembly.

**Currency — 4/5.** 110 new source pages and 86 OpenAlex PDFs pulled in 7 days, daemon-driven ingest, gap-driven wishlist rotation. This exceeds Cochrane baseline, where reviews routinely lag the literature by 2-5 years; HMI is structurally a living review. The deduction is that currency of *synthesis* trails currency of ingest — 30 cells synthesized against 110 ingested means synthesis lag is accumulating.

**Independence — 3/5.** The architectural firewall (Part 2 of CLAUDE.md, separate domains, two-directions-of-drift discipline, explicit rationale tagging for HMTc deviations) is well-designed and the most that institutional structure permits. The structural ceiling is that the Paleo Foundation operates both the wiki and HMTc; Cochrane's independence comes from review groups with no commercial product downstream. The architectural mitigation is real but cannot fully substitute for institutional separation. Hostile counsel will press this; the defense is the deviation-tagging audit trail, which holds, but it is a defense, not a non-issue.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting corpus completeness, the weakest addressable dimension)

**Vector 1 — PRISMA-shaped discovery audit log.** Extend `tools/discovery/` and the `discover` skill to emit, per query: (a) the exact query string, (b) databases hit, (c) date executed, (d) N hits returned, (e) N deduplicated against corpus, (f) N admitted to wishlist with score, (g) N excluded with categorical reason (off-topic, non-English without translation pathway, conference abstract only, etc.). Persist to `data/evidence/discovery-prisma-log.csv` with one row per query and a rolled-up `discovery-flow.md` regenerated nightly that renders the canonical PRISMA flow diagram (Records identified → Records screened → Records assessed for eligibility → Records included). This is the single artifact that converts the wiki from "we ingested a lot" to "we ingested everything that met pre-declared inclusion criteria, here is the documented exclusion of everything else" — the exact artifact a plaintiff's expert will demand.

**Vector 2 — Backward and forward citation chasing on A-tier sources.** Add `tools/discovery/cite-chase.mjs` that, for every source page with `evidence_tier: A`, (a) extracts the reference list from the Marker-converted markdown, (b) resolves each reference to a DOI via Crossref/OpenAlex, (c) dedupes against the existing corpus, (d) queues unmatched DOIs to the wishlist with `discovery_method: backward_citation` and the source cite-key as parent. Mirror with forward chasing via OpenAlex `cited_by`. This is standard Cochrane technique (the Cochrane Handbook §4.3) and is the single highest-yield way to close gaps around already-strong evidence clusters without paying the cost of broad-topic searches.

**Vector 3 — Cell-driven gap targeting with admission criteria pre-declared.** Replace the current freq-based wishlist rotation with a cell-driven scheduler: iterate every (ingredient × metal) cell across the 264 ingredients × 10 HMTc analytes (2,640 cells), rank by `n_studies` ascending with regulatory-importance weighting, and for the bottom decile auto-fire targeted multi-DB searches against pre-declared per-cell query templates (`"{ingredient} {metal}" food contamination`, plus matrix-specific variants). Persist per-cell admission criteria to `data/evidence/cell-search-protocols/{ingredient}-{metal}.md` *before* the search fires, so the protocol cannot be retrofitted to the results. This pre-registration is the move that converts cell-level synthesis from "we wrote what we found" to "we declared what we would accept, then synthesized what met the bar" — and that distinction is what Cochrane reviews trade on.
