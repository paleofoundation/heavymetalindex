## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has the structural ingredients Cochrane requires — per-claim provenance, evidence tiering, an Evidence Fitness layer, a deterministic routing audit, a published PRISMA-equivalent at `wiki/coverage.md`, and a documented HMTc/literature firewall that arguably exceeds Cochrane's independence posture. But it currently sits at 4.0% promotion of an identified 23,260-paper universe, the public methodology page is still a stub with key sections marked "forthcoming," there is no pre-registered review protocol, no per-study risk-of-bias instrument, and the 7-day synthesis distribution is overwhelmingly null→low confidence (39 of 39 cells at low/medium/high, zero upgrades). The bones are Cochrane; the meat isn't there yet.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane baseline ~5; EFSA ~4. The literature search is wide (23,260 triaged, P1–P5 banded), but only 921 source pages are promoted and only 661 routing rows are written. Pool state is 55 READY / 16 PROVISIONAL / **367 BLOCKED** — the majority of (row, metal) cells lack admissible pooled distributions. A hostile reviewer can correctly say "you've ingested 4% of the universe you yourself identified."

**Source provenance — 5/5.** Cochrane baseline 5. Every source page carries DOI, `raw_path` to the locally-archived PDF, `evidence_tier`, `sample_n_total`, jurisdiction, matrix list, page-table provenance in body sections, and machine-checked routing. Every wiki claim must link to a source page or it lints as a provenance gap. This dimension is Cochrane-class today and would survive a Daubert challenge on traceability.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** Cochrane baseline ~5; WHO monographs 4–5; FDA Closer to Zero ~3. `wiki/methodology.md` is `status: stub`. Search strategy is described prose-style, not as reproducible database queries with Boolean strings and date stamps. Inclusion/exclusion criteria are "forthcoming." The HMTc Governing Principles and standards-math doc are locked and excellent — but those are downstream of the wiki's evidence layer; the literature methodology itself is under-documented.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Cochrane baseline ~5. Pooling math lives in `tools/evidence/` deterministically; the routing audit is regeneratable from `wiki/sources/` alone; `review_events.jsonl` carries 1,423 synthesis events. But there is no PROSPERO-equivalent pre-registered protocol an external reviewer could use to rerun the corpus build from scratch and confirm the same papers were screened in/out for the same reasons. Replicability of pooling math, yes. Replicability of the *selection process*, no.

**Currency — 4/5.** Cochrane baseline 3 (typical 3–7 year review cycles). The autonomy loop produced 511 new source pages and 990 commits in 7 days; OpenAlex pulls 17 papers fresh. Heavy Metal Index is structurally *more current* than Cochrane today. The risk is durability: currency depends on the loop continuing to run, not on a publication cadence.

**Independence — 5/5.** Cochrane baseline 5. The Part 2 firewall (wiki reports literature; HMTc sets thresholds; the gap is stated, not hidden), the architectural domain split (`heavymetalindex.com` vs `heavymetaltested.com`), and the silent-threshold-deviation lint check together create a stronger independence posture than Cochrane operates under, because Cochrane is not running a downstream commercial certification program. This dimension is the wiki's strongest defensibility argument.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target the weakest dimension: corpus completeness)

**3a. Build a screening-decision emitter for the full 23,260-paper universe.** Today the PRISMA flow on `coverage.md` shows 23,260 → 921 promoted, but the ~22,000 unpromoted papers carry no machine-queryable include/exclude decision — they're just "not yet." Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `screen-triage-universe.mjs` step that, for each P1–P5 paper, emits a JSONL row with `cite_key`, `screening_decision ∈ {include-promote, include-defer, exclude-out-of-scope, exclude-superseded, exclude-language, exclude-non-primary}`, `decision_reason`, and `decided_at`. Surface counts on `coverage.md`. This converts "4% promoted" from a defensibility liability into "of 23,260 identified, X% screened in, Y% formally excluded with documented reason, Z% promoted to source pages" — which is the Cochrane PRISMA shape.

**3b. Unblock the 367 BLOCKED pool cells by adding a structured "admissibility gap" extractor.** Each BLOCKED cell has a reason (basis mismatch, censoring not reported, no sample-level data, wrong matrix, etc.) but those reasons are not currently emitted per-cell in a queryable form. Add `tools/evidence/pool-admissibility-report.mjs` that, for every (row, metal) cell in BLOCKED state, writes `data/evidence/pool_admissibility.csv` with columns `row, metal, blocking_reason_code, contributing_sources_examined, smallest_fix_needed`. The autonomy loop then routes the top blocking reason per cell into either (a) a re-extraction pass against the existing source PDF or (b) a targeted OpenAlex pull for a paper that would supply the missing field. This turns 367 cell-level gaps from invisible to executable.

**3c. Replace the `evidence_tier: A|B|C` field with a per-source risk-of-bias panel.** Cochrane synthesis requires within-tier bias gradients; A-tier currently lumps a 144-sample FDA ICP-MS market survey with a single-region academic convenience sample of n=12. Extend the source-page frontmatter schema with `risk_of_bias:` carrying Newcastle-Ottawa-equivalent sub-scores (`sampling_frame`, `analytical_method_validated`, `loq_reporting`, `censoring_handling`, `conflict_of_interest`, `replication_status`) each 0–2. Backfill via an autonomy pass that reads each existing source page's Methods and Limitations sections. Synthesis can then weight by ROB rather than treating all A-tier as equivalent, and the wiki gains a defensible answer to "why did you pool that paper at full weight" — which is the second question a plaintiff's expert asks after provenance.
