## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class — approaching on methodology, but materially weak on corpus completeness, currency, and independence from the commercial deliverable.** A Cochrane review of "heavy metals in food" would require a pre-registered protocol, a documented systematic search across MEDLINE/EMBASE/CENTRAL with reproducible search strings, dual-reviewer screening with kappa statistics, a PRISMA flow diagram, and risk-of-bias assessment per primary study; this wiki has 1,005 sources opportunistically ingested via a manual-fetch loop, no PRISMA flow, no documented inclusion/exclusion criteria with kappa, no risk-of-bias instrument, and a non-trivial pool state of 361 BLOCKED cells against only 55 READY. The provenance and pooling math are unusually strong for a non-Cochrane reference, but the corpus is still ~5% of what a comprehensive search would surface, and the wiki is operated by the same legal entity that monetizes the certification — a posture Cochrane explicitly forbids.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,005 sources against a literature universe of ~20,000-25,000 candidate primary studies on dietary heavy metals (CLAUDE.md's own "23,000 papers" target). There is no documented systematic search across MEDLINE/EMBASE/Web of Science/CENTRAL, no PRISMA flow, no recorded inclusion/exclusion ratio, and ingest is currently driven by manual-fetch queues plus OpenAlex pulls (17 last week). Cochrane baseline 4-5; EFSA opinions ~4 because their search strings are published. This wiki has not yet published its search strategy. **Weakness:** the corpus is what was reachable, not what is comprehensive, and there is no artifact a reviewer can use to verify either way.

- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim links to a source page (the claim-provenance audit reports 3,840/3,840 supported, 100%). Source pages carry cite-keys, evidence tiers, jurisdiction, and matrix metadata. This is genuinely Cochrane-comparable on the per-claim-trace dimension and exceeds FDA Closer to Zero (~3). **What keeps it off 5:** evidence-tier assignment is currently model-adjudicated without a published rubric matching a standard tool (GRADE, ROBINS-E, OHAT), and no dual-reviewer agreement statistic is computed.

- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** Standards math is locked in code (`tools/evidence/`, `data/workbench/`), the P97/P45 calibration is documented with rationale, pool admission rules are enumerated by source type (sample-level, P-vector, mean+SD+N, LOD-censored, range-only), basis conversions are deterministic, and sensitivity is reported across log-normal/gamma/nonparametric. This compares favorably to WHO monographs (~4-5) and beats EFSA on the deterministic-code-vs-ad-hoc-per-cell axis. **What keeps it off 5:** the methodology is for *threshold-setting*, not for *systematic review*. There is no documented protocol for the wiki's own evidence synthesis (initial-population, incremental, full-resynthesis are described in CLAUDE.md but not in a pre-registered protocol with version control external to the implementing party).

- **Replicability — 3/5.** The pooling math is deterministic code and would re-run identically given the same source set — that part is strong. But replicability of the *upstream* selection (which papers entered the pool) is weak: there is no published query log, no recorded screening decisions, no documented inter-rater agreement. A hostile reviewer asking "show me you would have arrived at the same 1,005 sources if I re-ran your search today" cannot be answered from the current artifacts. The 14 routing_unresolved entries and 26 pending Step-0 locks also indicate live drift in what counts as in-scope.

- **Currency — 3/5.** 1,014 commits and 576 new source pages in seven days demonstrates the autonomy loop is moving. But 361 BLOCKED pool cells means most analyte-row cells cannot yet produce a synthesized value, and synthesis touched only 8 unique pages last week against 255 ingredients × 36 metals = ~9,000 potential cells. The confidence distribution is bottom-loaded: 28 null→low, 0 lateral upgrades. **Weakness:** the wiki is currently in build-up, not steady-state currency; a 2024 paper landing today is not guaranteed to be ingested within any defined SLA.

- **Independence — 2/5.** This is the structural weak point and the one a plaintiff's expert will attack first. The wiki and HMTc are architecturally separated (different domains, firewall in CLAUDE.md Part 2, no brand mentions, lint catches threshold deviation) — all good. But both are operated by the Paleo Foundation. Cochrane's authority rests on its editorial board being institutionally separate from any party with a commercial stake in the conclusions. The Cyprus database-rights frame and the external-review brief (3 reviewers, $8K-15K honoraria, 60-day clock) are mitigations, not solutions. **What makes it weak:** until the external-reviewer brief is executed and findings published, the independence claim is asserted rather than demonstrated.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting independence, the weakest non-corpus dimension; corpus completeness vectors follow)

1. **Build `tools/evidence/search-strategy-execute.mjs` that runs a versioned, pre-registered search across PubMed/Europe PMC/OpenAlex/CrossRef using a checked-in query manifest (`data/evidence/search-strategy/v1.yaml`) and writes a PRISMA-format flow record to `data/evidence/prisma-flow-<date>.json`.** The manifest enumerates search strings per metal × matrix combination, date ranges, language filters, and exclusion reasons. Each autonomy-loop tick re-executes the strategy, diffs the result set against the prior run, and either (a) ingests new hits or (b) records a numbered exclusion reason against each non-ingested record. Output: a publishable `/methodology/search-strategy` page plus a numerical answer to "of N candidate records, K were ingested, M excluded by reason 1.x, L excluded by reason 2.x" — the artifact corpus-completeness 2/5 is missing.

2. **Extend the structured extractor to capture a risk-of-bias scoring vector per source on the OHAT instrument (9 questions: exposure characterization, outcome assessment, confounding, selection, attrition, reporting, conflict of interest, statistical methods, other), writing the scored vector to source-page frontmatter as `risk_of_bias: {ohat_v1: {q1: low, q2: probably_low, ...}}`.** Run dual-adjudication by spawning a second Claude session over the same source with no access to the first session's scores; compute Cohen's kappa weekly and publish to `/methodology/risk-of-bias`. This directly converts the "model-adjudicated without published rubric" weakness in provenance and gives reviewers the kappa statistic Cochrane reports.

3. **Stand up `docs/governance/editorial-firewall.md` codifying the editorial board as institutionally separate from HMTc revenue, then operationalize it as a commit-gate: any wiki edit that touches a page also referenced in the active HMTc workbench requires a co-sign in the commit trailer from an editorial-board member outside the HMTc P&L.** Pair with the external-review brief execution (3 commissioned reviewers, findings published verbatim with finding-by-finding responses within 30 days). This converts independence from claim to demonstrated practice — the bar a plaintiff's expert cannot dismiss as "they audited themselves."
