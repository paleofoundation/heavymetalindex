## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class on methodology and provenance; not yet Cochrane-class on corpus discipline and synthesis depth.** The locked P97/P45 math, native-basis conversion engine, per-source cite-key provenance, and architectural wiki/HMTc firewall already exceed what FDA Closer to Zero and most EFSA opinions document in public — but the synthesis layer is 39 cells deep (mostly null→low confidence) against 1,001 sources and a 351-deep BLOCKED pool, which means a plaintiff's expert today could credibly say "you have an evidence database, not a systematic review."

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,001 source pages and 590 ingested in the last week is volume, not completeness. There is no preregistered search strategy per (ingredient, metal) cell — no documented PubMed/OpenAlex queries, no PRISMA-style screening log, no inclusion/exclusion criteria captured per cell. The pool state (351 BLOCKED vs 55 READY) indicates most sources have not been screened to admission. Cochrane baseline 4-5: preregistered protocol, dual-reviewer screening, PRISMA flow diagram per question. EFSA baseline ~4: documented mandate, dated literature cut-off, named search platforms. This wiki currently sits below FDA Closer to Zero (~3) on this dimension because Closer to Zero at least publishes its data-call scope.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every source has a `cite_key`, every routing decision is in `product_source_routing_audit.csv`, the routing-unresolved/malformed logs catch frontmatter drift, and `review_events.jsonl` (1,347 entries) is a real audit trail. Held back from 5 by the provisional-scaffold mechanism: 24 product pages and ~16 ingredient pages exist without locked categorization, and provisional status is not yet visibly rendered in the public wiki text. A hostile reader landing on a provisional page would not see the disclaimer Cochrane would require.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 publishes both the 90/30 row-pass intent *and* the P97/P45 mechanism with the row-pass calibration derivation. Pool admission, moment-matching for summary-only sources, native-basis conversion, and the regulatory-cap rule are all written down. This exceeds FDA Closer to Zero (~3) and matches WHO monograph practice (~4-5). Held back from 5 because the wiki-side (vs HMTc-side) protocol — *which* sources enter *which* synthesis cell, and how a screened-out source is dispositioned — is not yet documented at the same depth as the standards math.

**Replicability — 3/5.** 1,011 commits, deterministic pooling code, basis conversion in `tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`, and a routing audit that fails the build on drift. An external reviewer can in principle re-derive any cell's percentile from the contributing sources. However: (a) the AI adjudication queue (`ai_adjudication_queue.csv`) introduces non-deterministic LLM judgment that is not currently captured as a versioned prompt+seed+model record, and (b) the wiki prose itself is Claude-generated and not provably re-derivable from the source corpus — there is no test that says "given these N sources, the wiki page text would re-emerge."

**Currency — 4/5.** 590 new source pages in 7 days, routing audit triggered on every commit, synthesis triggers on doubling thresholds. This is faster than any Cochrane review and faster than EFSA's typical re-opinion cadence. Held back from 5 because of the BLOCKED-pool backlog — sources are ingested faster than they are admitted to synthesis, so "current" means "we know it exists" not "the synthesis reflects it."

**Independence — 4/5.** The wiki lives at `heavymetalindex.com`, HMTc at `heavymetaltested.com`; the gap-direction check is in CLAUDE.md Part 2; standards arithmetic is excluded from `wiki/` by governance rule; there is no `wiki/brands/` directory. This is structurally cleaner than Cochrane, where reviewer COI declarations are self-reported. Held back from 5 because the same operator (Paleo Foundation) runs both sides — the firewall is procedural and lint-enforced, not organizational. A hostile reader will note this and the wiki must demonstrate the firewall is intact, not just claim it.

### 3. Improvement vectors (all attack corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

1. **Per-cell preregistered search protocol generator.** Build `tools/evidence/generate-cell-protocol.mjs` that, given an (ingredient, metal) tuple, emits `data/protocols/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` containing: (a) the OpenAlex/PubMed query string, (b) date range and language filters, (c) inclusion criteria (sample type, basis, N threshold), (d) exclusion criteria, (e) screening date. The autonomy loop then runs the query, ingests hits, and writes a PRISMA-style `data/evidence/screening/<cell>.csv` recording every screened paper with admission decision and reason. This is the single largest Cochrane gap and the one a plaintiff's expert will hit first.

2. **BLOCKED-pool disposition extractor.** Extend `tools/evidence/` with a disposition pass that walks `pool_state: BLOCKED` sources and writes a per-source admission decision: `admitted`, `excluded_basis_mismatch`, `excluded_population_mismatch`, `excluded_methodology_below_threshold`, `excluded_duplicate_reporting`. Today 351 sources sit BLOCKED with no published reason; Cochrane requires every excluded study to be named with cause. Surface the disposition table on each product-category page as the "studies considered but not pooled" section.

3. **Search-corpus completeness audit against external indices.** Build `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-coverage.mjs` that, for each (ingredient, metal) cell with `n_studies >= 4`, queries OpenAlex with the cell's protocol query and reports the count of indexed papers vs the count in `wiki/sources/`. Emit `data/evidence/coverage-gaps.csv` ranking cells by the gap between external-index count and ingested count. This turns "we have 1,001 sources" into a defensible "we have X% of the indexable literature on cell Y, with the missing N papers enumerated."
