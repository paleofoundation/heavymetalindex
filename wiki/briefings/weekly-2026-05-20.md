## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki's editorial independence (Part 2 firewall, standards-arithmetic kept out of `wiki/`), per-claim provenance architecture, and deterministic synthesis math are already at or above the Cochrane baseline; however, the corpus is ~1,009 of an estimated ~23,000 in-scope papers, 360 (row × metal) pool states are BLOCKED, and there is no PRISMA-equivalent search log. Under hostile scrutiny today a plaintiff's expert could not credibly attack the *methodology* but could credibly attack the *completeness* of the evidence base — which is what blocks the claim from being made cleanly.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.**
1,009 sources against a target literature of ~23,000 is ~4% coverage. Active ingest channels exist (OpenAlex, Manual Fetch Kimi, PapersCube), but past-week OpenAlex pulls were 17 PDFs. There is no PRISMA-style search log recording which queries were run against which databases, how many hits were screened, and the documented inclusion/exclusion verdicts. Cochrane baseline is 4-5 (multi-database systematic search + grey literature + hand-search + reference-list mining, all logged); EFSA's typical 4 reflects a smaller but documented sweep. This wiki is currently below both.

**Source provenance — 4/5.**
Every source has a `cite_key`, every synthesis cell carries `last_reviewed` and contributing-source pointers, and the routing audit (`data/evidence/product_source_routing_audit.csv`) is the canonical many-to-many between sources and downstream pages. The 39 cells written this week are all honestly null→low/medium/high (no synthesis claims floating without a source). The 0.5 gap is that synthesis cells point to source *pages*, not to specific tables/figures/rows within those source pages — a Cochrane review would name the table.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.**
`docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 declares the P97/P45 math, the 90/30 row-pass intent, the pool-admission rule (moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion under log-normal/gamma with explicit JECFA/EFSA precedent), the native-basis convention, and the rationale-tag vocabulary (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`). The synthesis trigger conditions in Part 9 (initial population, n_studies doubling, 2× value contradiction, A-tier disagreement) are codified. Gap: the *synthesis prompt itself* — what Claude reads, in what order, when running an "incremental update" pass — is not yet a versioned document an external reviewer could rerun.

**Replicability — 3/5.**
The standards math is deterministic and in code (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`, the pooling engine). The routing audit is rebuildable from source-page frontmatter on a watch trigger. An external reviewer *could* re-derive a threshold from the workbench inputs. They could not yet re-derive the corpus, because (a) the search log doesn't exist and (b) the manual-fetch queue's prioritization (Kimi vs PapersCube vs OpenAlex) is not deterministically reproducible from a written protocol. Cochrane reviews are reproducible end-to-end including the search; this wiki is reproducible from the search onward.

**Currency — 4/5.**
1,000 commits, weekly autonomy briefings, daemon-tick ingest cycles, and per-cell `last_reviewed` timestamps put currency above Cochrane's typical 2-year update cadence in *cadence terms*. The drag is that 360 pool states are BLOCKED — those cells reflect "literature exists but is not yet ingested," not "no literature available." A reader cannot tell from a BLOCKED cell whether the wiki is current-and-thin or current-and-stale on that row.

**Independence — 4/5.**
Part 2 firewall is the most carefully-engineered piece of the entire system: the wiki/HMTc separation is bidirectional (no softening *or* strengthening), lint-enforced (silent-threshold-deviation check), and architecturally severed (standards arithmetic lives in `data/workbench/`, not `wiki/`). The brand firewall (Part 12) is hard — no `wiki/brands/`, no HMTc-certified brand mentions. This is Cochrane-equivalent or better; Cochrane has *editorial* independence policies but not the architectural separation this wiki enforces. The 0.5 gap is that the firewall is enforced by lint rules Claude wrote, not by an external auditor.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

**Vector A — PRISMA-equivalent search ledger.**
Build `tools/autonomy/corpus-search-ledger.mjs` that appends one row per executed search to `data/evidence/corpus-search-ledger.csv` with columns: `search_id, date, database (openalex|pubmed|kimi|papers-cube), query_string, scope (ingredient|metal|product|jurisdiction), hits_returned, hits_screened, included_count, excluded_count, exclusion_reasons_json`. Wire it into every existing fetch path so a search cannot run without leaving a ledger row. This is the single artifact that converts "we have 1,009 sources" into "we executed N searches, screened M papers, included K with the following exclusion taxonomy" — the form Cochrane requires.

**Vector B — Reference-list back-mining extractor.**
Extend the structured extractor that runs at ingest to parse the references section of each newly-ingested source page, normalize each cited paper to a DOI or OpenAlex ID, and emit any unmatched citation into `data/evidence/autonomy/back-mining-queue.csv` with columns: `citing_source_cite_key, cited_doi, cited_title, cited_year, in_corpus (bool), priority_tier`. The daily autonomy loop pulls top-N unmatched citations into the manual-fetch queue. This converts the corpus from "what Karen surfaced" to "what the corpus itself says it depends on" — the closure property Cochrane reviewers run by hand.

**Vector C — Per-(ingredient, metal) coverage targets driven by gap-driven-wishlist.**
The existing `data/evidence/autonomy/gap-driven-wishlist.csv` is gap-aware but does not declare a *target* sample count per cell. Extend `tools/autonomy/build-gap-driven-wishlist.mjs` to emit, for each BLOCKED pool state, a target `n_sources_needed_to_unblock` derived from the cell's product-category importance (HMTc Category 1 infant/child > supplements > condiments) and the current `n_studies`. The daemon then prioritizes manual-fetch and OpenAlex sweeps against the highest target-vs-actual deltas. This turns the 360 BLOCKED count from an undifferentiated backlog into a ranked work queue with a written rationale per row — and the rationale is itself ingestible as evidence that the corpus is being completed deliberately, not opportunistically.
