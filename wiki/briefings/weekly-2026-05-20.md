## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The methodology layer (P97/P45, native-basis pooling, sensitivity bands, code-bound determinism, the Part 2 firewall) and per-claim provenance discipline already sit at or above Cochrane's median — they are stronger than FDA Closer to Zero and competitive with EFSA Scientific Opinions on the analytical side. What holds the wiki below Cochrane-class today is the absence of a documented systematic-search protocol, a backlog (366 BLOCKED pool cells vs 55 READY) that means most evidence is sitting unsynthesized, and architectural-rather-than-organizational independence from HMT&C.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5 / 5.** 1,011 source pages is a respectable working corpus, but the snapshot shows only 17 OpenAlex pulls against 462 new source pages this week — the pipeline is manual-fetch dominant, not query-driven. There is no PRISMA flow, no documented (metal × matrix × jurisdiction) search matrix with hit counts, and no preregistered eligibility criteria. Cochrane: 4–5 (MEDLINE/Embase/CENTRAL exhaustive, dual-screened). EFSA Opinions: ~4. **Hostile-scrutiny risk:** "How do you know you have the complete literature?" is the question this dimension fails today.

**Source provenance — 4 / 5.** The 1:1 source-page-to-source convention, routing layer, and lint rule that every numerical claim must trace to a source page is structurally stronger than most reference works. `review_events.jsonl` (1,447 entries) gives a per-cell audit trail. Loses 1 point because some `contamination_profile` blocks (e.g., apple) carry `confidence: medium` with `typical_ppb: [0, 0]` — the trace exists but the value formatting hides whether censoring or LOD-handling was applied at the page surface.

**Methodology transparency — 4 / 5.** P97/P45 is documented with row-pass calibration, the policy choice (90/30) is named separately from the mechanism, native-basis conversion is in code, the sensitivity band thresholds (±25% stable, 25–50% provisional, >50% needs review) are explicit. `docs/standards-methodology.md` reads like a WHO monograph methods annex. Loses 1 point because the moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion ("EFSA/JECFA convention") is asserted rather than cited to a specific JECFA document — a plaintiff's expert would press on that.

**Replicability — 3 / 5.** The math is reproducible — `tools/evidence/` is deterministic and a third party with the source corpus could re-run pooling. What is not reproducible is the inclusion decision: there is no per-cell inclusion log saying "these 14 sources were considered, 9 admitted, 5 dispositioned out for reasons X." An external reviewer cannot tell whether the omitted sources were missed, deemed ineligible, or silently filtered.

**Currency — 2.5 / 5.** Git history (994 commits) and `last_reviewed` per cell give better date provenance than Cochrane (typically a 2–5 year update cycle). But the snapshot is brutal: 366 cells BLOCKED, 55 READY, 17 PROVISIONAL, and only 39 cells synthesized in the trailing week — and of those, 28 went null→low confidence. The evidence base is accumulating roughly 10× faster than synthesis is consuming it. A challenger pointing to a 2024 paper and asking "why doesn't your synthesis reflect this?" wins more often than they should today.

**Independence — 3 / 5.** Part 2's bidirectional drift rule is well-designed and the architectural separation (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com, Cyprus database-rights positioning, no brands named) is genuine. But Cochrane has organizational independence: the review authors are not employed by anyone selling a product that depends on the review. Here, the same Foundation operates the wiki and the certifier. The firewall is structural, not organizational, and a sophisticated adversary will frame it as "the certifier grades its own homework." The defense exists but is rhetorical, not arms-length.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest dimension is corpus completeness (2.5), with currency (2.5) tied. Three executable build steps:

**(a) Stand up a PRISMA-style systematic-search pipeline.** Extend the OpenAlex puller into a scheduled job that runs queries by (metal × matrix × jurisdiction) tuples on a documented cadence, writes `data/evidence/search-protocol/<query-id>.jsonl` with one row per hit (DOI, title, year, decision, decision-reason), and emits a per-(ingredient, metal) PRISMA flow diagram (`identified / screened / eligible / included`) at synthesis time. Mirror to PubMed and Web of Science where API access exists. This directly answers "how do you know the corpus is complete" and lifts both corpus completeness and replicability simultaneously. Estimated lift: corpus 2.5 → 3.5–4.

**(b) Add a per-cell inclusion log emitted by the synthesis pass.** Extend the synthesizer to write `data/evidence/inclusion-decisions/<ingredient>-<metal>.jsonl` with one row per candidate source containing: cite-key, admission rule applied (sample-level / P-vector / mean+SD+N / mean-only / range-only / single-point / interval-censored), basis-conversion applied, weight after the 2× median-N cap, decision (included | dispositioned-out + reason). Surface it as a "Search and inclusion" section on each ingredient page, linking the log. This closes the "external reviewer cannot re-derive the synthesis" gap and converts the methodology transparency advantage into actual replicability. Estimated lift: replicability 3 → 4.

**(c) Drain the BLOCKED pool with an AI adjudicator throughput SLA.** 366 BLOCKED cells is the currency bottleneck — pool admission, not synthesis math, is the constraint. Extend the AI adjudication queue (`data/evidence/ai_adjudication_queue.csv`) to triage BLOCKED cells nightly on the four common failure modes (basis-conversion ambiguity, LOD-censoring handling, analyte-species disambiguation, statistic-type mismatch), promote decisions deterministic enough for code to READY, and surface ambiguous ones in the morning report. Add a hard SLA: any cell BLOCKED >14 days escalates. Pair with a synthesis-throughput target — at current 39 cells/week the wiki will never clear the backlog; the target should be ≥150/week sustained. Estimated lift: currency 2.5 → 3.5.
