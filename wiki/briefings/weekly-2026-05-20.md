## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The architectural commitments (per-claim provenance, deterministic pooling math in code, an explicit two-direction firewall between literature and certification thresholds, ISO-dated commits, machine-enforced lint) are at or above Cochrane's typical structural baseline — and in some dimensions (independence, currency) exceed it. The binding constraint is corpus completeness: 1,000 ingested sources against a known universe of ~23,000 candidate papers (≈4%), with 351 of 421 pools still BLOCKED, means the strategic claim "we have reviewed the complete literature" cannot yet survive hostile scrutiny on its plain reading. The infrastructure is Cochrane-grade; the corpus is not, yet.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane = 5, EFSA = 4, FDA Closer to Zero = 3. With ~4% of the candidate population ingested and no documented denominator (the 23,000 figure lives in CLAUDE.md prose, not in a queryable artifact), there is no defensible way to characterize gap size in litigation. 351 BLOCKED pools means most (ingredient, metal) cells are evidentially under-served; the synthesis layer has only touched 8 unique pages in the last week. A plaintiff expert would attack here first, and the wiki has no quantitative rebuttal ready.
- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Cochrane = 5. Every claim is required to trace to a source page; cite-keys, evidence tiers, per-analyte routing audit, and the lint failure on un-sourced numerical claims are all in place. Below Cochrane because tier assignment is largely automated rather than dual-reviewer adjudicated, and the synthesis log (`review_events.jsonl`) is being populated but is not yet the audited source of truth.
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** Comparable to EFSA scientific opinions; below WHO monographs only on volume of worked examples. `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` derives P97/P45 from a stated 90/30 row-pass intent, `docs/standards-methodology.md` specifies pool admission per data shape, the basis-conversion rule is in code (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`), and rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) are mandatory. This is the strongest documented dimension.
- **Replicability — 3/5.** The pooling math is deterministic and the audit artifacts (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`, `review_events.jsonl`) are checked into git, but an external reviewer cannot today re-derive most ingredient pages because 83% of pools are BLOCKED on admission criteria and the basis-conversion logs are per-source rather than per-cell aggregated. The path exists; the artifacts a reviewer would actually pick up are sparse.
- **Currency — 5/5.** 588 source pages added in 7 days, autonomous ingest loop, lint failure on stale audits, hard `last_full_resynthesis` doubling triggers. Cochrane reviews are notoriously stale (median update lag ≈5 years); this wiki is structurally faster than any reference work named in the prompt.
- **Independence — 5/5.** Part 2 of CLAUDE.md codifies bidirectional drift checks; standards arithmetic is firewalled out of `wiki/` into `data/workbench/`; the `heavymetalindex.com` / `heavymetaltested.com` split is architecturally enforced; no brand pages are permitted. Stronger than EFSA (which sets its own thresholds) and comparable to Cochrane (which has no commercial deliverable). This is the wiki's structural moat.

Weighted average ≈ 3.8. The high-confidence dimensions (independence, currency, methodology) are the durable, hard-to-fake ones; the weak dimension (corpus completeness) is the one a hostile reader actually attacks.

### 3. Improvement vectors (all targeting corpus completeness)

**Vector A — Build a queryable corpus denominator.**
Add `tools/evidence/build-corpus-denominator.mjs` that runs saved OpenAlex + PubMed + EFSA-OpenData + FDA-TDS queries for each (metal × food-domain) pair from a fixed query manifest, dedupes against `wiki/sources/`, and writes `data/evidence/corpus-denominator.jsonl` plus a per-cell coverage % to the morning report. The autonomy loop's queue gets sorted by `(denominator_hits − ingested) DESC`, so the highest-gap cells get worked first. Today the 23,000-paper figure lives only as prose; this turns it into an audited, monotonically-improving metric the wiki can cite under oath.

**Vector B — Coverage-gap auditor wired to the ingest queue.**
Extend the routing audit to emit `data/evidence/coverage-gaps.csv` with one row per (ingredient, metal) cell whose `status` is `pending` or whose `n_studies < 4`, joined against the corpus denominator from Vector A and against `data/evidence/hmtc_aggregate_distributions.csv` for consumer-priority weighting. The autonomous loop's "what should I ingest next" decision reads the top row of that file rather than discovering work opportunistically from `raw/`. This closes the open-loop ingest into a punch list with a finite end state — a precondition for ever honestly claiming completeness.

**Vector C — PRISMA-style exclusion ledger.**
Extend the source-page schema with a required `screening_decision` block on every ingested PDF (`included` / `excluded`, with `excluded_reason` from a fixed vocabulary: `not-food-matrix`, `secondary-citation-of-pooled-source`, `non-quantitative`, `duplicate-cohort`, `out-of-scope-metal`) and add `tools/audit-exclusion-rates.mjs` to flag implausibly low exclusion rates per ingest batch (indicating un-screened bulk ingest) or implausibly high ones (indicating over-restrictive screening). Cochrane reviews are judged on whether null and excluded studies are captured; right now the pipeline only knows what it ingested, not what it ruled out and why. This is the single change that most directly answers a plaintiff expert's "how do you know you didn't cherry-pick?"
