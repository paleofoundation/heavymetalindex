## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has Cochrane-grade *architecture* — explicit firewall between evidence and the commercial standard, deterministic pooling math, per-claim cite-key provenance, git-versioned reconstructibility — but Cochrane-grade *output* requires a corpus that has been demonstrably searched to exhaustion and a synthesis layer that has actually computed values for the cells it claims to cover. With 360/431 pool cells BLOCKED, 26 product pages still awaiting Step 0 Lock, and 72% of last week's synthesis cells landing at `low` confidence, the wiki is closer to a well-architected systematic-review-in-progress than a published Cochrane review.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,007 sources against an addressable literature in the tens of thousands; only 17 PDFs pulled from OpenAlex in the last week against the program's 23k-paper estimated scope. No PRISMA-equivalent documented search strategy, no per-category landmark-paper checklist, no inter-database deduplication audit. Cochrane: 4-5; EFSA: ~4; this wiki: clearly below both. This is the dimension a plaintiff's expert will attack first.
- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim is required to trace to a cite-key'd source page; the routing audit enforces this structurally and the lint pass blocks orphaned references. 13 unresolved routing entries are the visible drag; the brand firewall and the architectural separation from HMT&C protect the chain from contamination. At parity with Cochrane.
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** The standards math is locked, versioned (v2.1), and lives in code (`tools/evidence/`, `data/workbench/`) — P97/P45, 90/30 row-pass intent, native-basis convention, regulatory cap, rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`). The pre-registration equivalent (`docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md`) exists with a changelog. Gap: pool admission for summary-only sources via moment-matching is named but the per-source conversion logs are not yet uniformly published per pooled cell. Above FDA Closer to Zero, close to WHO monograph.
- **Replicability — 3/5.** Git history + deterministic pooling code + structured `data/evidence/` register means an external reviewer could in principle re-derive synthesis. In practice, 83% of pool cells are BLOCKED (no synthesis to replicate), `review_events.jsonl` is acknowledged as not yet consistently populated, and the synthesis pass touched only 8 unique pages in the last 7 days. The infrastructure scores well; the realized output does not.
- **Currency — 3/5.** Ingest velocity is high (529 new source pages/week, 305 new product pages, 999 lifetime commits) and the routing audit signals stale cells on a fast cadence. But many ingredient pages have mixed `pending`/`populated` `contamination_profile` sub-blocks — the literature *exists* in `raw/` but has not yet propagated to the synthesis surface. The system is current with itself; it is not yet current with the literature.
- **Independence — 5/5.** This is the strongest dimension and the one most directly load-bearing for the program's defensibility. Architectural separation of `heavymetalindex.com` from `heavymetaltested.com`; standards arithmetic explicitly excluded from `wiki/`; HMT&C-certified brands never mentioned; bidirectional-drift check (Part 2) baked into commit discipline; Cyprus database-rights posture. A hostile expert cannot credibly characterize the wiki as the certifier's self-published justification — the file layout itself refutes the claim. At or above Cochrane.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness, currently 2/5)

**Vector 1 — Pre-registered, category-driven search strategy with documented queries.**
Extend the OpenAlex puller (`tools/autonomy/openalex-*`) into a multi-source search engine that, for each HMTc row in `data/workbench/standards/`, executes a pre-registered query across OpenAlex, PubMed, EFSA Journal, FDA TDS, WHO/JECFA, and the EU RASFF feed. Persist the query strings, date ranges, and result counts to `data/evidence/corpus-search-strategy.md` so the search is replicable by an external reviewer. Schedule nightly. Output: a PRISMA-shaped flow diagram per (category, metal) cell showing identified → screened → included → excluded with stated reasons. This is the single artifact that converts "we have 1,007 sources" into "we searched X, screened Y, included Z" — the Cochrane-defining move.

**Vector 2 — Landmark-paper coverage audit per (metal, product-category) cell.**
Build `tools/autonomy/audit-landmark-coverage.mjs` that loads a curated `data/evidence/landmark-papers.yml` (the 50-200 papers any systematic review of Pb-in-infant-foods, iAs-in-rice, Cd-in-cacao, MeHg-in-fish, etc. must cite — derivable from the reference lists of the canonical EFSA/JECFA/IARC monographs) and reports, per cell, which landmarks are present in the corpus, which are missing, and which are present but not yet routed to the relevant target page. Block the build if any cell's landmark coverage drops below a configurable floor (e.g., 80%). This is the audit a brand legal team will run; doing it first internally is cheaper than discovering the gap during a deposition.

**Vector 3 — Snowball citation-chasing extractor.**
Add `tools/autonomy/citation-chase.mjs`: for every A-tier source already in `wiki/sources/`, parse its reference list (Marker-converted markdown already exposes this), resolve each reference against OpenAlex/CrossRef, and emit a queue of cited primary studies not yet in the corpus to `data/evidence/citation-chase-queue.csv`. Rank the queue by in-degree (papers cited by many of our A-tier sources rise to the top). Feed the top-N into the existing manual-fetch pipeline. This converts the wiki's existing A-tier nucleus into a self-extending corpus and gives the search strategy a documented snowball arm — the second methodological pillar Cochrane reviewers expect alongside database search.
