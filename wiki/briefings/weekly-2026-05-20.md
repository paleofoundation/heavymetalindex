## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — structurally, not yet operationally.** The wiki has the bones a Cochrane reviewer would recognize (immutable raw corpus, version-controlled provenance, deterministic pooling math, codified evidence tiers, declared firewall against commercial drift), but the operational gap — 361 BLOCKED cells against 39 synthesized this week, no declared search strategy, no PRISMA flow, AI-mediated single-pass extraction without a dual-reviewer equivalent, and a single-entity governance posture — means a hostile expert can land real hits today. The infrastructure is built to clear Cochrane scrutiny; the populated state of it is not there yet.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,004 sources covering all 36 metals × 255 ingredients × 346 products is sparse against any single-contaminant Cochrane or EFSA opinion (typically 500–2,000 sources for one analyte in one matrix). Only 17 OpenAlex pulls this week, no declared database list (PubMed, EMBASE, CENTRAL, agency repositories, gray lit), no language-coverage statement, no pre-registered inclusion criteria. The corpus is growing fast but its boundary is undeclared, which is the Cochrane-versus-blog distinction.

- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Routing audit is canonical (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`), cite-keys enforced, source-page frontmatter discipline is real, brand-firewall is codified. Per-claim provenance is a designed property of the system, not a manual practice. Marked down because 14 `routing_unresolved` and 26 pending Step 0 locks mean ~3% of routing edges are still unbound, and many wiki claims are not yet backed by a written synthesis trace.

- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** The locked P97/P45 math, the row-pass calibration rationale (0.9¹⁰ argument), native-basis convention, moment-matching for summary-only sources with stated WHO/JECFA precedent, regulatory-cap binding rule, and rationale-tag vocabulary together exceed FDA Closer to Zero's published transparency and match EFSA Scientific Opinion baseline. The methodology lives in code (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`, pooling engines) which is *better* than EFSA's PDF-only methodology disclosure. Marked down because the wiki-corpus inclusion protocol (as opposed to the threshold math) is not separately documented as a pre-registered protocol.

- **Replicability — 3/5.** Immutable `raw/`, 1,016 commits, evidence register, routing audit, deterministic basis conversion. An external reviewer with `raw/` access can re-derive thresholds. The hole is AI-mediated synthesis: Cochrane requires dual independent extraction with disagreement adjudication; this wiki uses single-pass extraction followed by an audit queue. There is no documented kappa or inter-rater equivalent. A plaintiff's expert will ask "how do we know Claude didn't hallucinate cell `rice × iAs = 142 ppb`?" and today the answer is "the audit-queue caught it 80% of the time" without a measured 80%.

- **Currency — 4/5.** 590 source pages in 7 days, daemon-driven loop, OpenAlex integration, structured triggers for resynthesis. More current than any Cochrane review (which freezes at submission). Marked down only because the source-ingest velocity is outpacing synthesis: 361 BLOCKED cells means the corpus is becoming current faster than the syntheses derived from it.

- **Independence — 2/5.** The Paleo Foundation operates both the wiki and HMTc. Part 2's firewall is policy and lint, not institutional. Cochrane and EFSA have formal editorial independence from regulated parties; this wiki has self-declared independence enforced by a `silent threshold deviation` lint check. Under hostile cross-examination — "Is the wiki editorially independent from the certifier that pays for it?" — the truthful answer today is "structurally separated, commonly governed," which is not Cochrane-equivalent. This is the dimension most likely to be attacked first and the one autonomy-loop work cannot fix.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest grade-eligible dimension is **Corpus completeness (2)**, with Independence (2) untouchable by code. Three executable build steps for the autonomy loop:

1. **PRISMA-flow generator in the autonomy daemon.** Extend the ingest-batch reporter to emit `data/evidence/prisma-flow-<date>.md` per tick, capturing: records identified per database (OpenAlex, Manual Fetch Kimi, Papers Cube, Manual Resourcing), records deduplicated against existing cite-keys, records screened, exclusions with codes (out-of-scope-metal, out-of-scope-matrix, language, no-quantitative-data, retracted), records included. Tie each exclusion to a stable reason vocabulary, and aggregate into a rolling corpus-completeness manifest. This converts an undeclared corpus into a declared one and is the single highest-leverage step toward Cochrane-class.

2. **Dual-extraction disagreement audit.** Extend the audit-queue to run a fresh-context, independent second-pass extraction on a stratified 10% sample of each ingest batch (5% A-tier, 5% B-tier), comparing extracted `numeric_findings`, basis assignments, censoring flags, and analyte-species calls. Disagreements land in `data/evidence/dual-extraction-disagreements.csv` with a cohen's-kappa-equivalent agreement statistic emitted to the morning briefing. This is the AI-mediated analogue of Cochrane's dual-reviewer requirement and directly defends replicability under hostile review.

3. **BLOCKED-cell triage and prioritization loop.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` to enumerate the 361 BLOCKED cells with a structured reason code (`insufficient_sources_n<2`, `basis_conversion_unresolved`, `censoring_ambiguity`, `unit_disagreement`, `tier_floor_unmet`), emit per-cell remediation tickets to `data/evidence/blocked-cell-queue.csv`, and feed those tickets back into ingest priority — manual-fetch and OpenAlex pulls preferentially target the ingredient × metal cells nearest to clearing P97/P45 calculation. The 1,004 sources / 39 cells-this-week ratio is the dashboard's loudest signal that breadth is outrunning depth; this closes the loop.

Independence is the dimension the autonomy loop cannot reach. The defensibility case rests on raising it through institutional moves — an editorial board with no HMTc revenue exposure, a published conflict-of-interest statement on each synthesis page, and an external methodology review before the first commercial deployment of an HMTc threshold cites a wiki page as its evidence baseline. Worth flagging for Karen as off-loop work.
