## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The infrastructure is Cochrane-shaped — deterministic pipeline, code-driven routing, PRISMA-equivalent coverage page, 100% claim-to-source-page provenance, doctrinal editorial firewall, locked threshold math with rationale tagging — but execution lags the design in three places that a plaintiff expert would target: corpus depth is at 4% of the identified universe without per-paper exclusion records, the literature-search protocol is not pre-registered, and no independent peer review has been completed yet. Cochrane reviews carry the authority of a published, sustained methodology and external editorial board; HMI has built the methodology surface but has not yet executed the external validation that converts the claim from assertion to fact.

### 3. Improvement vectors — targeted at corpus completeness

**Vector A — Per-paper exclusion ledger.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` (likely a new `tools/autonomy/build-exclusion-ledger.mjs`) to emit one row per triaged paper not ingested: `cite_key, title, year, priority_tier, exclusion_reason, decided_by, decided_at`. Reason vocabulary should be fixed (`out-of-scope-matrix`, `superseded-by-newer`, `non-English-no-translation`, `unobtainable-fulltext`, `duplicate-of-X`, `triage-tier-P5-not-yet-reached`). Surface as `wiki/coverage/exclusion-ledger.md` (paginated) and link from `coverage.md`. This converts "we ingested 4%" from a weakness into a PRISMA-compliant flow diagram with documented reasons for the other 96%, which is the form Cochrane and EFSA both use. The autonomy loop can produce ~500 reason-assignments per run by reading the triage manifest and applying deterministic rules; only the ambiguous ones escalate to Karen.

**Vector B — Pre-registered search protocol with per-run logs.** Create `docs/governance/literature-search-protocol.md` capturing the search strings, databases (OpenAlex, PubMed, FDA, EFSA, JECFA, Health Canada), date ranges, language filters, and inclusion/exclusion criteria as a versioned document. Then extend the autonomy daemon to emit `data/evidence/autonomy/search-runs/<ISO-date>.jsonl` for every literature-pull cycle: query, database, hit count, dedupe-against-existing, ingestion decision. Cochrane's reproducible-search requirement is satisfied by exactly this artifact pair (protocol + run log). Cost: one new doc, one new autonomy task, no model-judgment dependency.

**Vector C — Second-extractor independent re-extraction with divergence report.** Extend the structured extractor (`tools/evidence/extract-*` family) to run a second-model re-extraction on a rolling 10% sample of source pages and emit `data/evidence/extraction-divergence.csv` (cite_key, field, page_value, re_extract_value, agree, magnitude_of_disagreement). Anything beyond ±5% on a numeric field opens an audit-queue row. This raises **provenance** (current audit only proves "a link exists," not "the linked number is correct"), **replicability** (an external reviewer can verify the divergence rate without re-running the whole pipeline), and indirectly **independence** (an empirical disagreement rate is itself a hostile-witness-resistant artifact). At current throughput, 10% × 999 sources = ~100 re-extractions/cycle; that fits inside the existing autonomy budget.

Each vector is autonomy-executable, addresses a specific Cochrane-comparison gap that a plaintiff expert would name, and produces a published artifact that converts a defensibility claim into an auditable record.
