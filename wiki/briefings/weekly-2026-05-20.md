## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The methodology layer, provenance architecture, and editorial-independence design are at or above Cochrane baseline — locked percentile math, per-claim source tracing, immutable raw layer, and an architecturally enforced wiki/certification firewall are the kind of choices Cochrane reviewers would credit. The corpus, at 1,013 sources, is two-to-three orders of magnitude smaller than what "complete review of the heavy-metals food literature" would require (EFSA's Pb scientific opinion alone reviews ~1,000+ primary studies), and the synthesis layer (39 cells written this week, 8 unique pages touched, zero confidence-elevation events) is far below the cadence that would saturate even the current corpus — so the defensibility claim does not yet hold up under hostile scrutiny.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,013 sources against a literature universe in the tens of thousands; 366 of 438 (ingredient × metal) cells are in `BLOCKED` pool state because the minimum source density to pool has not been met. A plaintiff's expert with a Web of Science subscription can demonstrate omissions within an hour. Below EFSA (~4) and well below Cochrane (5).
- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every source has a cite-key, a structured frontmatter block, a `wiki/sources/` page, and a routing audit row; 1,463 review events are logged in `review_events.jsonl`. The remaining 0.5 gap is that "every claim traces" is asserted by `tools/audit-claim-provenance.mjs` but the audit has not been published against the live wiki for external verification. At Cochrane baseline.
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** P97/P45 locked, native-basis convention specified, moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion declared with WHO/JECFA + EFSA references, regulatory-cap rule deterministic, rationale-tag vocabulary fixed. The gap: pre-registration. Cochrane protocols are registered *before* synthesis runs; here the methodology has versioned itself three times (1.0 → 2.1) without a public protocol registration, which a hostile reader will frame as "the methodology moved to fit the data." At EFSA level, slightly below Cochrane.
- **Replicability — 3/5.** The pooling engine is in code and deterministic; the routing audit is rebuildable from frontmatter; commit history (982 commits) preserves daily state. But no external party has re-derived a single ingredient × metal cell from `raw/` end-to-end, and the autonomy pipeline contains LLM-extracted fields (analyte species, censoring, basis) whose extraction prompts are not version-pinned to specific commits in the evidence records. A reviewer cannot bit-reproduce a value. Below Cochrane.
- **Currency — 3/5.** 408 new source pages this week and 17 OpenAlex pulls show the pipeline runs, but currency is currently improving from a sparse base, not maintaining steady-state coverage. No page carries a "literature surveyed through YYYY-MM-DD" stamp that an auditor can check against OpenAlex's count for the same query. Below EFSA/Cochrane until corpus completeness stabilizes.
- **Independence — 4/5.** Architectural separation (`heavymetalindex.com` vs `heavymetalcertified.com`), the explicit Part 2 firewall in CLAUDE.md, no `wiki/brands/` directory, the no-HMTc-certified-brand-mentions rule, and the Cyprus EU-database-rights posture are stronger than most regulator-operated reference works (FDA Closer to Zero, where the synthesis and the regulatory action share authors, would score ~2). Lint enforces it (`silent threshold deviation` check). The 0.5 gap is that the firewall has never been adversarially tested by Slot 3 of the external review brief. At Cochrane baseline.

Cochrane typical: 4-5 across all six. This wiki: **3.3 average**, with corpus completeness as the clear binding constraint.

### 3. Improvement vectors (all targeting corpus completeness)

1. **Build `tools/audit-corpus-saturation.mjs` that computes per-cell coverage ratios against OpenAlex.** For each (ingredient × metal) pair, query OpenAlex for the canonical search string (`"<ingredient>" AND ("<metal>" OR "<symbol>")`), record the result count, and write `data/evidence/corpus-saturation.csv` with columns `ingredient_slug, metal, wiki_sources, openalex_total, saturation_pct, top_missing_dois`. Gate the routing-audit's `READY` status on saturation ≥ 60%; currently-`READY` cells with low saturation get demoted to `PROVISIONAL` until the gap is closed. The autonomy loop's next-ingest priority becomes "lowest saturation × highest HMTc importance," which is what `data/evidence/autonomy/manual-fetch-tracker.csv` should be sorted on.

2. **Add EFSA scientific opinions and JECFA monographs as a Tier-A bulk-ingest class with summary-data extraction.** These documents each contain pre-pooled occurrence distributions for dozens of (food × metal) combinations — a single EFSA Pb opinion ingest could unblock 40-80 currently-`BLOCKED` cells via the moment-matching admission path the methodology already specifies. Build `tools/autonomy/ingest-efsa-opinion.mjs` that parses the standard EFSA appendix tables (mean, P95, N, basis) into one synthetic source page per (food × metal) summary row, tagged `summary_only: true` with the parent opinion as `derived_from`.

3. **Pin extraction-prompt commit-SHA into every source page's frontmatter, and republish the autonomy loop's extraction prompts as versioned files under `tools/autonomy/prompts/`.** Each source page's `extraction_provenance` block records `extractor_commit`, `extractor_model`, `extractor_prompt_sha`, `extraction_run_id`. A future re-extraction at a different prompt version writes a new block rather than overwriting. This converts the LLM-extraction step from "trust the model" to "rerun the prompt at the recorded SHA and verify output bit-equivalence" — the missing replicability primitive a Cochrane reviewer would expect, and the specific weakness Slot 3 (plaintiff-side attorney) would attack.
