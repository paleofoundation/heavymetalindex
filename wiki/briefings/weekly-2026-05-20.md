## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class.** The architectural scaffolding — per-claim provenance via the routing audit, the wiki/HMTc epistemic firewall, deterministic pooling math, locked methodology versioning — is Cochrane-grade in *design*. But with 1,000 ingested sources against an estimated 23,000-paper corpus (≈4% coverage), 351 BLOCKED cells against 57 READY in the pool state, and 39 synthesis cells written across only 8 ingredient pages, the wiki has not yet executed the systematic-review move it is structured to deliver. Under hostile scrutiny, "we ingested ~4% of the relevant literature" is a fatal opening line.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** No documented search strategy artifact exists (no PRISMA-style flow, no date-stamped query log, no database-by-database hit counts). Manual-fetch + 17 OpenAlex pulls/week is artisanal sourcing, not systematic identification. Cochrane baseline 5; EFSA ~4; this is below FDA Closer to Zero's ~3 because Closer to Zero at least publishes its inclusion criteria.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Cite-keys, routing audit (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`), `routing_unresolved.csv` discipline, and the rule that "every numerical claim must link to a source" matches Cochrane's GRADE-traceability standard. Loses one point because the synthesis layer (`contamination_profile`) is still propagating — many ingredient pages remain `pending`, so the per-claim chain isn't yet exercised end-to-end at scale.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1, `docs/standards-methodology.md`, and the schema-history file constitute a versioned, public methodology stack with explicit rationale for P97/P45 calibration. Stronger than FDA Closer to Zero (~3), comparable to WHO monographs (4-5). Loses one point: the *synthesis* methodology (how a `contamination_profile` cell is built from N source pages) is less formalized than the *standards* methodology — Part 9 specifies modes and triggers but no published protocol equivalent to a Cochrane review protocol.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Compiled-wiki posture, git-versioned with 999 commits, deterministic generators, and the audit-CSV trail mean an external reviewer with `raw/` access could re-derive. But `raw/` is private, the search-strategy log is missing, and 351 BLOCKED cells indicate most evidence is not yet in a re-derivable state. Cochrane reviews publish their full search syntax and inclusion log; this wiki does not yet.

**Currency — 3/5.** Live ingest cadence (589 sources/week), routing-audit triggers, `last_reviewed` and `last_full_resynthesis` per cell, and resynthesis triggers (doubling, contradiction, A-tier conflict) are designed at Cochrane level. But only 39 cells synthesized, and the resynthesis loop has not yet visibly fired on legacy pages. Mechanism: 5; execution: 2; combined 3.

**Independence — 4/5.** Part 2 firewall, separate domains (`heavymetalindex.com` vs `heavymetaltested.com`), explicit lint check for "silent threshold deviation," and the rule that standards arithmetic lives in `data/workbench/` not `wiki/` is structurally *stronger* than Cochrane (which relies on declared COIs rather than architectural separation). Loses one point until the lint check is build-blocking and an external party has tested the firewall (the external-review-brief.md exists; engagement has not happened).

Composite: ~3.3/5 against a Cochrane baseline of 4.5. The wiki is on a trajectory that, fully executed, would land at 4.5-5.0. Today it does not.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest dimension is **corpus completeness (2/5).** All three vectors target it directly and are executable by the autonomy loop.

**Vector A — Emit a PRISMA-equivalent search-strategy artifact per ingest batch.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` to write `data/evidence/search-strategy/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` containing: (i) date-stamped search strings run against OpenAlex, PubMed, and Embase APIs; (ii) per-database hit counts; (iii) dedup counts against existing `cite_key`s; (iv) included/excluded counts with exclusion reasons keyed to a fixed vocabulary (out-of-scope-matrix, non-English-without-translation, conference-abstract-only, retracted, duplicate). Surface the artifact in `wiki/methodology/search-strategy.md` (public). This converts artisanal sourcing into a defensible systematic identification protocol — the single highest-leverage move against a plaintiff's "you cherry-picked the literature" attack.

**Vector B — Add a corpus-coverage matrix generator.** Build `tools/evidence/build-corpus-coverage.mjs` that for every (HMTc-category × metal × jurisdiction × decade) cell computes: expected literature volume (from an OpenAlex faceted query holding the cell's query constant), ingested volume (from `wiki/sources/` frontmatter), and a coverage ratio. Emit `data/evidence/corpus-coverage.csv` and flag cells <30% as P1 ingest targets in the gap-driven wishlist. This makes the 4% headline number cell-resolved — "we are 8% covered on Pb-in-cereal-grains-EU-2010s, 41% on Cd-in-cacao-Americas-2020s" — and turns corpus completeness from a global uncertainty into an auditable matrix.

**Vector C — Add cross-database de-duplication and discovery against PubMed/Embase.** Currently OpenAlex is the sole automated feeder. Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `tools/autonomy/cross-db-discovery.mjs` step that, for every (category × metal) query already run against OpenAlex, also queries PubMed (E-utilities) and Embase (via institutional access or Scopus fallback). Diff the result sets, write papers present in PubMed/Embase but absent from `raw/` to `data/evidence/autonomy/cross-db-gap.csv`, and auto-enqueue them into `manual-fetch-tracker.csv`. Cochrane's defining methodological move is querying multiple databases with the same syntax; this vector reproduces that move at automation scale.
