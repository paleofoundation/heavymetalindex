## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class.** The infrastructure is Cochrane-shaped in design — locked deterministic standards math, per-claim source provenance, an architectural firewall between evidence layer and commercial deliverable — but execution is still at low single-digit percent of the stated 23,000-paper target (1,003 sources) and synthesis has touched only 8 unique pages this week with 351 cells in `BLOCKED` pool-state against 55 `READY`. The bones would survive hostile scrutiny; the flesh would not, because no plaintiff's expert needs to dispute methodology when "you reviewed ~1,000 of an estimated ~23,000 papers and have not documented your search strategy" is already a fatal answer.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,003 sources against the stated complete-literature target is ~4% coverage, and there is no published search strategy (databases queried, query strings, date ranges, hit counts, exclusion reasons) the way Cochrane mandates via PRISMA. The 590-sources/week velocity is excellent, but velocity is not coverage, and without a search manifest a reviewer cannot tell whether the corpus is 4% of the literature or 4% of a biased subset. Cochrane baseline 4–5; EFSA ~4; this wiki sits below FDA Closer to Zero on this dimension.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim is required to trace to a `wiki/sources/` page; the routing audit (`product_source_routing_audit.csv` plus `routing_unresolved.csv` / `routing_malformed.csv`) mechanically enforces fan-out and catches missing-frontmatter sources. 15 unresolved routing entries against 1,003 sources is a low and visible defect rate. Falls short of 5/5 only because per-claim *page-line* provenance is not yet structured — a hostile reviewer asking "which specific sentence in source X supports the P97 value for Pb in canned tomatoes" cannot get a deterministic answer.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** The Locked Standards Math (P97 clean / P45 dirty, native-basis pooling, regulatory-cap binding, four rationale tags) is published in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1, deterministic, lives in code, and the row-pass calibration rationale is articulated against the five Governing Principles. This matches Cochrane / WHO monograph practice. The missing piece: no externally addressable protocol-of-record (PROSPERO-equivalent registration, version DOI, frozen-on-date publication) that pre-commits the method before evidence is collected.

**Replicability — 3/5.** The pooling math is reproducible from `data/workbench/` and `tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`. But because the search strategy is undocumented and the source corpus is incomplete-by-construction, an external reviewer cannot independently reconstruct inclusion — they could re-run the math on the existing sources but cannot verify the sources are the right sources. Replicability of the arithmetic ≠ replicability of the systematic review.

**Currency — 3/5.** 1,018 commits and 590 new sources in seven days demonstrates a living-systematic-review cadence well above Cochrane's typical 3–5 year update cycle. Counter-weights: synthesis is far behind ingest (39 cells written vs 351 BLOCKED), 24 products are pending Step 0 Lock, and there is no per-page "evidence base current as of <date>" stamp that downstream consumers (HMTc, journal, courses) can cite. Fast in motion, weak in surface.

**Independence — 4/5.** Architecturally the strongest dimension: separate domains (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), Part 2 of CLAUDE.md prohibits both directions of HMTc drift, standards arithmetic explicitly excluded from `wiki/` and confined to `data/workbench/`, no brand pages, no HMTc-certified-brand mentions, and the four rationale tags force the literature-vs-policy gap to be named rather than hidden. Falls short of 5/5 only because independence has not yet been tested adversarially — Cochrane's independence is audited; this wiki's is asserted.

### 3. Improvement vectors

All three target the weakest dimension (corpus completeness), with secondary lifts to replicability and methodology transparency.

**(a) Generate per-cell systematic search manifests.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `build-search-manifest.mjs` that, for each (product-category × metal) cell, emits `data/evidence/search-manifests/<row>-<metal>.md` containing: declared inclusion criteria (population, analyte form, matrix, date range, language), the OpenAlex/PubMed/Web of Science query strings actually executed, raw hit counts per database, deduplicated hit count, ingested count, excluded count with per-exclusion reason codes, and a PRISMA-equivalent flow. This is the single highest-leverage change — it converts a 4%-coverage corpus from "we have not searched comprehensively" to "we have searched comprehensively; here is what is in-scope, what is excluded, and why." The night-loop already pulls from OpenAlex; persisting the query state as a manifest is incremental.

**(b) Build a reverse-citation gap report against anchor papers.** Add `tools/autonomy/build-anchor-citation-graph.mjs` that takes a curated list of anchor papers per cell (e.g., for canned vegetables × Sn: Trandafir 2012, Blunden & Wallace 2003; for infant cereals × iAs: Karagas group, FDA Closer to Zero technical docs) and walks their forward + reverse citations via OpenAlex's `referenced_works` and `cited_by_api_url`. Output `data/evidence/corpus-gap-report.md` listing cited works not yet in `wiki/sources/` ranked by in-citation count. This converts "what's missing" from an unknowable into a finite, prioritized ingest queue and gives a defensible answer to "how do you know your corpus is complete."

**(c) Extend the source-page schema with a `study_quality` block.** Add structured fields the extractor must fill: `sampling_frame` (random/stratified/convenience/targeted), `n_samples`, `geographic_scope`, `analytical_method` (ICP-MS/GFAAS/AAS/HG-AAS/colorimetric/XRF), `lod_loq_declared` (bool), `censoring_handling` (substitution/Tobit/MLE/unstated), `funding_source`, and the existing A/B/C tier paired with GRADE-style downgrade reasons. Surface aggregated quality at the cell level so synthesis can weight or stratify. This raises corpus *quality* assessment (Cochrane's risk-of-bias requirement), replicability (an external reviewer can recompute the pool with different inclusion thresholds), and provenance (the gap between A-tier-cited claims and the underlying study features becomes auditable) in one pass.
