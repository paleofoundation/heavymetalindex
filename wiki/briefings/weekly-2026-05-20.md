## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The architectural commitments — per-claim provenance, deterministic pooling math, an explicit wiki/HMTc firewall, and a locked methodology with a changelog — are Cochrane-equivalent in design. Execution is the gap: with 366 of 438 pool cells BLOCKED (84%), only 8 unique pages touched by synthesis this past week against 1,011 source pages ingested, and no pre-registered search protocol, the wiki cannot today re-derive a defensible synthesis across most of its claimed coverage. Strong bones; incomplete flesh.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane: pre-registered PROSPERO protocols, PRISMA-compliant multi-database searches with hand-search and author-contact fallback. HMI: 1,011 sources is respectable absolute volume, but ingest is opportunistic (`raw/Manual Fetch Kimi/`, OpenAlex pulls of 17 PDFs/week) with no documented search-protocol-per-cell, no PRISMA flow diagram per (ingredient, metal), no record of what was screened out and why. An adversary asking "show me the search strategy for cadmium in cocoa" gets a list of files, not a protocol. Below EFSA's ~4.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Cochrane: every claim traced. HMI: every numerical wiki claim must link to a cite-keyed source page; the lint pass fails the build on provenance gaps; the routing audit forces source→page fan-out into auditable CSVs. This is the system's strongest defensible dimension — it would survive expert deposition. Half-point off because 13 routing-unresolved entries and ongoing routing-discipline warnings (Phase 1 still `--warn-only`) mean the guarantee is not yet hard-enforced.

**Methodology transparency — 3.5/5.** Cochrane/WHO baseline: ~4-5. HMI: the governing-principles document (v2.1) and `docs/standards-methodology` lock the P97/P45 math, basis-conversion rules, pool-admission rules, and rationale-tag vocabulary in code, with a versioned changelog. Strong. The deduction: synthesis-mode rules (initial / incremental / full resynthesis) are documented but the corpus-completeness side — what *should* be in the pool for a given cell — is not pre-registered. Methodology version 2.0 → 2.1 (April → May 2026) was a substantive math change (P10/P20 → P97/P45) that re-derives every prior standard, which is the kind of revision Cochrane would tag and preserve historical outputs for.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Cochrane: external reviewer reproduces synthesis from search to conclusion. HMI: pooling math is deterministic and in code; 995 commits give per-day state reconstruction; routing audit and review_events.jsonl are designed for re-derivation. But 84% of pool cells BLOCKED means the synthesis-from-corpus path is not actually running for most cells — replicability of *intent* is high, replicability of *current state* is mostly "re-derives to BLOCKED." An external reviewer running the pipeline today reproduces the blockage, not the standards.

**Currency — 3/5.** Cochrane: living-review surface in best cases; periodic updates the norm. HMI: 512 new source pages in 7 days is fast ingest velocity, but synthesis wrote only 39 cells across 8 unique pages — a >10× lag of ingest over synthesis. The trigger conditions in Part 9 (n_studies doubling, 2x disagreement, contradiction) exist on paper; the week's `null_to_*` distribution (39/39 cells were first-population, zero upgrades) shows the resynthesis trigger has not yet fired in anger. Regulatory-drift lint depends on regulation page changes being detected, which works for the 55 regulations present but the corpus of relevant regulations is likely larger than 55.

**Independence — 4.5/5.** Cochrane: editorial separation from commercial sponsors. HMI: this is the dimension built deepest. Standards arithmetic explicitly does not live in `wiki/` (CLAUDE.md Part 19, governing-principles Principle 4); the firewall is named ("two directions of drift") and lint-enforced ("silent threshold deviation"); the wiki contains zero HMTc-certified brand mentions by policy; the rationale-tag vocabulary forces every gap to be labeled. Half-point off because the same organization operates both — structural separation is by *convention enforced by lint*, not by independent governance.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest dimension is **corpus completeness** (2/5), with **replicability** (3/5) and **currency** (3/5) tied as second-weakest. The three vectors below target completeness directly and lift the other two as side effects.

**Vector 1 — Per-cell pre-registered search protocol.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `cell-search-protocol.mjs` that emits a `data/evidence/protocols/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` artifact per active (ingredient, metal) cell, containing: databases queried (OpenAlex, PubMed, EFSA opinion archive, FDA TDS, national TDS programs by jurisdiction), search strings, date-range bounds, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screening decisions (with cite-keys for excluded papers and reason codes), and PRISMA counts (identified / screened / included / excluded). Add a lint check that fails the build when a `contamination_profile` cell is `populated` or `in_progress` without a corresponding protocol. This turns the corpus from "what we happen to have read" into "what the protocol said to read, and which of those we read."

**Vector 2 — Structured extractor for the four fields that gate pool admission.** The 366 BLOCKED cells (84% of pool state) are blocked because pool admission per `hmtc-governing-principles.md` requires `n_samples`, `basis` (wet/dry/as-consumed/powder), `analyte_species` (iAs vs tAs, MeHg vs tHg), and `statistic_type` (sample-level vs mean/median/IQR/max/SD) on every source row. Build `tools/autonomy/extract-pool-admission-fields.mjs` as a deterministic extractor that re-reads already-ingested source PDFs (Marker output exists in `raw/`) and emits these four fields into the evidence register, auto-promoting BLOCKED→PROVISIONAL→READY as fields fill. This is the single highest-leverage change available: it directly converts existing corpus into pool-eligible evidence without re-ingesting anything.

**Vector 3 — Risk-of-bias tagging adapted from Cochrane RoB-2.** Add a required `risk_of_bias` frontmatter block to source pages with five fields tuned for environmental exposure literature: sampling-frame representativeness (population vs convenience), analytical-method validation (ICP-MS with CRMs vs unspecified), detection-limit reporting (numeric LOD/LOQ vs absent), conflict-of-interest disclosure (declared vs absent), and reporting completeness (per-sample vs summary-only). Each rated low/some-concerns/high with a one-line rationale. Propagate into synthesis: pooled values weighted down for high-risk sources; ingredient-page `contamination_profile` carries a `bias_distribution` sub-field. Lint flags synthesis pages where >25% of contributing sources are high-risk. This is what gives `confidence: high` the meaning Cochrane would expect, and is the single change most likely to survive a plaintiff's expert challenging "how do you know this study is reliable?"
