## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The methodology layer (locked P97/P45 math, native-basis pooling in deterministic code, explicit rationale tagging) and the editorial-independence architecture (heavymetalindex.com / heavymetaltested.com separation, bidirectional-drift lint, Cyprus database-rights posture) already meet or exceed Cochrane's structural baseline. But the wiki is not Cochrane-class yet because corpus completeness sits at roughly 4–5% of its own 23,000-paper target, 32% of pages carry at least one numerical claim without a paragraph-local citation, and only 57 of 421 pool cells are READY — the synthesis surface a hostile expert would actually attack is still mostly empty.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,001 sources against a stated ~23,000-paper universe. `BLOCKED: 351` vs `READY: 57` says ~85% of pool cells lack the evidence floor to set anything. The gap-driven wishlist identifies 9,554 candidate queries but only executes 600/day, all via OpenAlex OA-PDF — paywalled A-tier evidence (Elsevier, ACS, RSC toxicology journals) is structurally excluded from autonomous fetch. **Below EFSA's ~4; well below Cochrane's 4–5.**
- **Source provenance — 3/5.** Per-source-page provenance is structurally enforced (cite_keys, routing audit, frontmatter required fields). But the 2026-05-20 provenance audit shows 397 unsourced numerical claims across 227/697 files (32% page failure rate), including high-traffic pages: `metals/arsenic.md` (8), `regulations/eu-reg-2022-617-mercury-fish.md` (16), `products/canned-fish.md` (5). The lint mechanism exists and is documented in Part 18 but does not block builds. **Mechanism: 4. Execution: 2. Net: 3.** Cochrane requires every numerical claim in a synthesis to trace to a primary study; today, one in three pages would fail that bar.
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 specifies P97 clean / P45 dirty with row-pass calibration rationale (90/30), pool admission rules, moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion, native-basis conversion, the `min(percentile, regulatory cap)` rule, and the four rationale tags. The math lives in `tools/evidence/` as deterministic code. This is comparable to WHO/JECFA pooled TDS methodology and exceeds FDA Closer to Zero. The missing -1 is that the pre-registration of percentile choice is post-hoc-justified rather than empirically calibrated against held-out lot data — a competent statistician will note that the ≈0.6 correlation assumption powering the P97 uplift is asserted, not measured.
- **Replicability — 3/5.** The compiled-wiki architecture (raw → evidence → wiki → standards workbench) means an external reviewer who has the repo can re-derive every standard. But (a) raw PDFs are not redistributable, so the corpus is not externally reproducible by a third party from scratch; (b) AI-adjudication is the default evidence workflow without a published reviewer-agreement metric (κ, percent agreement on a held-out set); (c) the routing audit, while reproducible, has no published "version-of-record" snapshot tied to each wiki release. **Cochrane's bar is "another team could rerun the search and reach the same inclusion set" — this wiki does not yet expose enough of its search and inclusion provenance to clear that bar.**
- **Currency — 5/5.** 589 new source pages in 7 days, daily extraction reports, gap-driven wishlist with day-seeded rotation, weekly resynthesis triggers, autonomous OpenAlex fetch. Cochrane reviews update roughly every 2 years; this wiki updates daily. **Structurally superior to Cochrane on this dimension** — the question is whether currency without saturation is meaningful.
- **Independence — 4/5.** Part 2 of CLAUDE.md and the two-direction drift rule are stronger than what most pharmacoeconomic Cochrane reviews achieve. The lint for "silent threshold deviation" is a real mechanism. The architectural separation across two domains and a Cyprus entity is genuinely defensible. The -1 is that the wiki and the certifier share an operator; a hostile expert will argue the firewall is procedural, not structural, until at least one published case exists where the wiki's literature finding visibly contradicts an HMT&C threshold without being softened.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

1. **Extend `tools/autonomy/wishlist-fetch.mjs` with a PubMed + EFSA OpenData adapter.** The current pipeline hits OpenAlex with an OA-only filter, which means every paywalled toxicology study in *Food Additives & Contaminants*, *Environmental Pollution*, *J. Agric. Food Chem.* is invisible to autonomous corpus expansion. Add two adapters: (a) PubMed E-utilities (free, full metadata, links to PMC OA full-text where available) and (b) EFSA OpenData scientific-opinion feed. Each adapter writes to `raw/external-fetch/<source>/<date>/` and emits a manifest row. This raises the eligible-source ceiling from "OA-PDF universe" to "indexed-literature universe."

2. **Build `tools/evidence/build-prisma-search-log.mjs`.** Per (HMTc row × analyte), emit a PRISMA-style flow diagram in machine-readable JSON: search strings used, databases queried, dates, hit counts, dedup count, inclusion/exclusion reasons with cite_keys. Persist one log file per cell at `data/evidence/prisma/<row>/<analyte>.json` and render to `wiki/methodology/prisma/<row>-<analyte>.md`. This converts the gap-driven wishlist into an auditable completeness ledger — the artifact a plaintiff's expert needs to attack and the artifact that, when present, makes the attack uneconomic.

3. **Add a saturation metric to the morning report.** For each READY cell, compute the marginal shift in the pooled P97/P45 induced by the most recent k=10 sources; when |shift| < ε for two consecutive deciles of new evidence, mark the cell `saturated: true` and stop wishlist-driven fetch for it (redirect quota to BLOCKED cells). New script: `tools/evidence/build-corpus-saturation-report.mjs`. This converts "1,001 sources" — an attackable claim — into "1,001 sources, of which N cells are demonstrably saturated and M are demonstrably under-evidenced with the following named gaps," which is the answer Cochrane's own reviews give and the answer a brand-legal team will accept as defensible.
