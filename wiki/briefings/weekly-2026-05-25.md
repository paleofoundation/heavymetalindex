## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has Cochrane-grade per-claim provenance, an architecturally enforced editorial firewall, and a continuous-update cadence that exceeds Cochrane's 2-year refresh, but it lacks the documented per-topic search protocols, formal risk-of-bias instruments, and structural author independence that define Cochrane reviews. It is already more defensible than FDA Closer to Zero or any food-industry standards body, comparable to EFSA scientific opinions on transparency, and below WHO monographs and Cochrane on inclusion-protocol rigor.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5/5.** 1,012 sources across the full heavy-metals-in-food space is breadth, not depth; a Cochrane review of a single PICO question typically pools 50–500 studies that were exhaustively retrieved against a pre-registered search string. The 366 BLOCKED pool entries and active gap-driven wishlist are honest signals that retrieval is opportunistic, not exhaustive. No PRISMA flow diagram exists for any (ingredient, metal) cell, so an external reviewer cannot tell whether the absence of a paper is a gap or a deliberate exclusion. Cochrane baseline 4–5; EFSA ~4; this wiki sits closer to FDA Closer to Zero on this axis than to EFSA.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** The routing layer (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`) explicitly maps every claim to source pages, and lint fails on provenance gaps. Per-claim traceability is genuinely Cochrane-grade. The half-point gap is that source pages do not yet carry a risk-of-bias rating per claim — the evidence-tier A/B/C field grades the source but not the specific extraction; Cochrane attaches RoB to each outcome, not just each study.

**Methodology transparency — 3.5/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 is genuinely strong: locked P97/P45 math, versioned changelog, pool admission rule, native-basis convention, regulatory cap behavior, and rationale-tag vocabulary. Synthesis triggers are codified. What is missing is a per-topic protocol: there is no equivalent of a PROSPERO registration declaring "for `ingredients/rice × metals/cadmium` we searched databases X on date Y with query Z, screened N records, applied inclusion criteria C." Methodology-of-the-math is well documented; methodology-of-the-search is implicit. Cochrane 5; WHO ~4.5; this wiki ~3.5.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Standards math is deterministic code (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs` etc.) and 439 commits preserve full state history. An external reviewer with the repo can re-derive every published value. They cannot, however, re-derive the corpus itself — the search history that produced the 1,012 sources is not captured as a structured artifact. Replicability of synthesis-given-corpus is strong; replicability of corpus-given-protocol is absent. Cochrane 4–5.

**Currency — 4/5.** 55 new source pages and 24 synthesis cells per week, autonomous discovery via paper-lookup and BGPT skills, file-watcher routing audit, weekly commit rate sustained at scale. This actively beats Cochrane (median 5.5 years between updates per Cochrane's own data). The half-point gap: 18/24 cells synthesized this week landed at `confidence: low`, and only 6 unique pages were touched, meaning the cadence is high but breadth-per-week is narrow. The 366 BLOCKED pool count means currency is uneven across cells.

**Independence — 3/5.** The architectural firewall is real: separate domains, lint check for silent threshold deviation, explicit Part 2 rule against bidirectional drift, the read-the-direction-before-editing protocol. But Cochrane's independence rests on multi-author teams with public COI declarations and a non-financial editorial board; here the wiki, HMT&C, the standards workbench, and the commercial revenue stream all sit inside one organization governed by one person. The firewall is procedural and software-enforced rather than structural. A hostile expert will note this. The defense is the audit log, the lint enforcement, and the git history — strong, but not the same as institutional independence.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting the weakest dimension: corpus completeness)

**Build a per-cell PRISMA registration generator.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a script that, for any (ingredient, metal) cell entering its first synthesis pass, emits a `wiki/protocols/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` file containing: search date, exact query strings issued to each of the 10 paper-lookup databases, record counts at each PRISMA stage (identified → screened → eligible → included), inclusion/exclusion criteria, and the list of excluded records with reasons. The discover skill already runs the queries; this captures their state as a citable artifact rather than a transient log. Lint rule: a `contamination_profile` cell at `status: populated` without a corresponding protocol page fails the build. This converts opportunistic discovery into reproducible search.

**Add a structured risk-of-bias extractor to the ingest pipeline.** Extend the source-page extractor (the one Codex runs during ingest) to populate a `risk_of_bias` block per source with the dimensions food-toxicology reviewers actually use: analytical method validation (ICP-MS vs colorimetric), LOD/LOQ reporting completeness, sample-collection representativeness (random vs convenience), matrix-matched calibration, certified reference material use, and conflict-of-interest declaration. Today's `evidence_tier: A/B/C` collapses these into a single grade; an A-tier paper with unreported LOD is being treated identically to an A-tier paper with full method validation. Cochrane attaches RoB per outcome, not per study; replicate that with a per-claim RoB field on the routing audit.

**Run a corpus-completeness audit against three external benchmark bibliographies.** Build `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-completeness.mjs` that ingests the reference lists from (a) the most recent EFSA scientific opinion on each of the 10 HMTc analytes, (b) the JECFA monographs for the same metals, and (c) the FDA Closer to Zero technical documents, then reports for each: how many cited primary sources are in this wiki's corpus, how many are not, and prioritizes the missing ones for the daemon's manual-fetch queue. This produces a defensible numerator for the claim "we have reviewed the complete literature" — without it, that claim cannot survive cross-examination by a plaintiff's expert who has read the EFSA opinion and can name a paper this wiki has not ingested.
