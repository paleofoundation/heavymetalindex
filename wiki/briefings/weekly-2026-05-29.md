## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class on methodology and provenance infrastructure; not yet Cochrane-class overall** because corpus coverage sits at roughly 5% of the target population (~1,107 of ~23,000 papers) and synthesis has touched roughly 1% of the (ingredient × metal) matrix. The deterministic pooling math, routing audit, and rationale-tag vocabulary are genuinely Cochrane-grade — ahead of FDA Closer to Zero and competitive with WHO/JECFA — but until corpus saturation and synthesis cadence cross threshold, a plaintiff's expert can credibly argue "you have not yet read the literature you claim to summarize."

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 1,107 sources against an estimated ~23,000-paper target population; 371 BLOCKED pools and 19 PROVISIONAL pools mean the engine has identified evidence shortfalls in most live cells but has not closed them. The autonomy loop is pulling ~14 PDFs/day from OpenAlex only, while the broader multi-database `/discover` skill is Karen-triggered, not daemon-fired. Cochrane: 5 within scope. EFSA: 4 within scope. HMI's scope is broader than either, which makes the gap larger and more visible.

- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every numerical claim is required to trace to a source page with `cite_key`; the routing audit fan-out forces fan-in to be reproducible; `contamination_profile` carries per-metal `n_studies` and contributing-source backlinks. The 15 entries in `routing_unresolved.csv` and the policy that `data/evidence/review_events.jsonl` is "not yet consistently populated" mean a small but real fraction of synthesis values cannot currently be re-derived deterministically from sources. The architecture supports 5; the implementation lags one step behind.

- **Methodology transparency — 5/5.** P97/P45 is locked in code with row-pass calibration rationale published in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md`; basis-conversion is deterministic (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`); rationale tags (`literature-baseline` / `regulatory-alignment` / `precautionary` / `feasibility-driven`) are required at staging and make deviations visible rather than hidden. The version table on the governing principles doc tracks methodology drift over time. This is above FDA Closer to Zero (no published threshold math) and matches Cochrane/WHO on reproducibility of stated method.

- **Replicability — 3/5.** The standards pipeline reruns deterministically and git history reconstructs any page state at any date — both Cochrane-grade. But synthesis values on ingredient pages are written by sessions reading multiple source pages, and CLAUDE.md itself flags that `review_events.jsonl` is incomplete. An external reviewer today can replay routing and pooling but cannot replay synthesis. Cochrane: 5 (PRISMA flow diagrams + protocol pre-registration). Drop from 4 to 3 because the gap is a known live deficit, not a theoretical one.

- **Currency — 3/5.** 100 new source pages and 410 commits in 7 days is ingest velocity well above Cochrane (whose currency is typically poor) or EFSA (5+ year revisit cycles). But synthesis output for the same window is 23 cells across 7 pages, against a 2,640-cell matrix (264 ingredients × 10 metals). Ingest outpaces synthesis ~4-5×; the freshness asymmetry means newer sources can sit in source-page form for weeks before influencing the wiki's published contamination_profile. The trigger system (Part 9) is designed to fix this; right now it is throughput-bound.

- **Independence — 3/5.** Architectural separation (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com, Part 2 firewall, two-direction drift check, rationale tags forcing gaps to be named) is unusually explicit. But the Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS) operates both. Cochrane (independent charity) and EFSA (regulator with no certification revenue) clear 5 structurally. HMI's defense is procedural, which is exactly the surface a hostile expert will press. Hard to lift above 3 without an external editorial board or independent audit panel reviewing synthesis decisions.

Weakest dimension is corpus completeness (2). Three executable builds:

1. **Build `tools/wishlist/blocked-cell-targeter.mjs` that consumes `pool_state` per (row, metal), classifies each of the 371 BLOCKED cells by reason (insufficient sources, basis-conversion gap, missing summary statistics, jurisdiction missing from benchmark pool), and emits one OpenAlex query per reason directly into `gap-driven-wishlist.csv`.** Today the wishlist generator is gap-driven against the wiki's ingredient × metal matrix; it does not yet target the pooling engine's BLOCKED reasons specifically. This routes the autonomy loop's PDF budget at the cells whose unblocking would actually shift a published P97/P45 value.

2. **Promote the `/discover` 10-database fan-out from Karen-triggered to daemon-fired nightly.** Add `tools/discovery/nightly-multidb-sweep.mjs` that pops the top 50 entries from `routing_unresolved.csv` plus the top 50 BLOCKED-cell topics, runs the existing `paper-lookup` adapters against PubMed/PMC/bioRxiv/medRxiv/arXiv/Crossref/Semantic Scholar/CORE/Unpaywall/OpenAlex, dedupes against the corpus, scores HMI-fit, and queues qualifying open-access PDFs into `raw/Manual Fetch Discovery/` for the existing manual-fetch ingester. Single-database sourcing is one of the easiest lines of attack a plaintiff's expert has; closing it is necessary for the Cochrane comparison to hold.

This is the artifact that lets the wiki *claim* a coverage percentage per cell when challenged, rather than relying on raw source count.
