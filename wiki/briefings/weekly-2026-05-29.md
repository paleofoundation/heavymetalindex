## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The mechanical infrastructure — per-claim provenance via the routing audit, deterministic standards math under lock, declared pool admission rules, basis-conversion in code, continuous ingest — is built to a standard that exceeds FDA Closer to Zero and is roughly EFSA-equivalent on methodology transparency. What keeps the wiki from "Cochrane-class" is a thin corpus relative to the universe of relevant literature (1,107 sources against a probable ceiling of 25k–40k for heavy-metals-in-food across PubMed/OpenAlex), the absence of seated external reviewers (0 of 6 slots filled), and a synthesis layer that is still demonstrably early-stage (18 of 23 cells this week moved `null → low`, only 2 reached `high`, and 371 of 448 pool cells are `BLOCKED` for lack of admissible evidence).

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5 / 5.** Cochrane reviews on narrower questions routinely document 80–95% capture against a PRISMA flow diagram. The Index has no comparable per-cell capture metric; the BLOCKED:READY ratio (371:58) reveals that most (ingredient, metal) cells lack the two-source minimum to even *begin* synthesis. The discover skill spans 10 databases and the daemon back-fills gaps, but there is no published search strategy or de-duplicated yield log per (ingredient, metal) question. EFSA scientific opinions sit at ~4 here; this wiki is below EFSA and well below Cochrane until per-cell capture is measured and reported.

**Source provenance — 4 / 5.** The routing audit (`data/evidence/product_source_routing_audit.csv`), the lint rules that fail builds on `routing_unresolved` / `routing_malformed`, and the requirement that every numerical claim trace to a `wiki/sources/` page give the wiki Cochrane-mechanical-grade provenance. The half-point gap: provenance to a *source page* is not provenance to a *verified extracted value*. The extraction record (which sentence/table cell yielded `typical_ppb`?) is not yet a first-class artifact a hostile reviewer can replay.

**Methodology transparency — 4 / 5.** `docs/standards-methodology.md` plus `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 document P97/P45 with the 90/30 row-pass calibration, the N-weighting cap, the basis-conversion convention, the regulatory-cap floor, and the rationale-tag taxonomy. This is better than FDA Closer to Zero (~3) and comparable to a WHO/JECFA monograph. The gap is the *literature search strategy*: there is no pre-registered protocol document specifying inclusion/exclusion criteria, language scope, date windows, or risk-of-bias assessment per source class. Cochrane requires this; the wiki does not yet produce it.

**Replicability — 3 / 5.** Standards math is deterministic and in code (`tools/evidence/`), so the threshold-setting step is replicable. But the *synthesis-input* step — how a given paper became a specific `typical_ppb` value on `ingredients/rice.md` — is not byte-reproducible by an external party. The extracted-value records are not stored as first-class artifacts keyed by source-SHA and extractor-prompt-hash, so a reviewer cannot re-run the pipeline and verify the input row. Cochrane gold-standard sits at 5 (data-extraction forms are public); this wiki is at 3.

**Currency — 4 / 5.** 100 new sources, 20 OpenAlex PDFs, and 404 commits in 7 days, with a continuous gap-fill autonomy loop. Cochrane reviews refresh on multi-year cycles and are often stale within 18 months; EFSA opinions worse. The wiki *beats* Cochrane on currency. Half-point gap: the synthesis pass is throttled (only 23 cells written / 7 days against 371 BLOCKED), so currency of *raw evidence* outpaces currency of *synthesized claims*.

**Independence — 3 / 5.** Structurally strong: separate domains, lint rules that fail builds on "silent threshold deviation," the wiki/HMT&C firewall codified in CLAUDE.md Part 2, no `wiki/brands/` directory. But independence is also a *social* property: Cochrane reviews are independent because external editors and external reviewers say so. The Index has 0 seated external reviewers; the `wiki/curators.md` slots and `data/peer-review/<slug>.jsonl` logs are placeholders. A plaintiff-side expert will note "operated by the certifier with no seated external editorial board" — true today.

### 3. Improvement vectors

These are concrete, executable autonomy-loop steps, ordered by leverage against the weakest dimension (corpus completeness).

**(a) Per-cell corpus-completeness audit, written to disk and lint-enforced.** Extend `tools/discovery/build-corpus-index.mjs` to emit, per (ingredient, metal) cell, a `data/evidence/corpus-completeness/<ingredient>-<metal>.json` record containing: total candidates returned across the 10 databases, de-duplication rate, inclusion rate after HMI-fit scoring, count actually ingested, and a derived `capture_estimate`. Add a lint rule that fails any `contamination_profile` block with `confidence: high` where `capture_estimate < 0.6`. This converts corpus completeness from an asserted property into an auditable one and gives a defensible answer to "have you read everything?" — moving the dimension from 2.5 toward 4.

**(b) First-class extraction-replay records, keyed by source-SHA and extractor-prompt-hash.** Modify the structured extractor to write `data/evidence/extractions/<source-sha256>/<prompt-hash>.jsonl` alongside the source page, with one record per (analyte, statistic, value, basis, N, page-or-table-anchor). Add a `tools/evidence/replay-extraction.mjs` CLI that, given a source PDF and a stored extraction record, re-runs the extractor and verifies bit-equality (or diffs). An external reviewer with `raw/` access can then re-derive any synthesis input independently — Replicability moves from 3 to 4 and Source provenance closes its half-point gap to 4.5.

**(c) Automated Phase-1 reviewer-dispatch packet generator.** Build `tools/peer-review/build-dispatch-packet.mjs` that, given a `wiki/curators.md` slot and a target synthesis page, assembles a single zip: methodology brief (`docs/external-review-brief.md`), the synthesis page snapshot with current `page_sha256`, the contributing source pages and their extraction records, the empty JSONL log path, and a pre-filled honorarium memo per `docs/peer-review-onboarding.md`. Karen sends; the first verdict landing in `data/peer-review/*.jsonl` flips the footer on the synthesis page from "no reviews yet" to a named external verdict. Independence moves from 3 toward 4 the moment one verdict lands; the strategic asymmetry argument shifts from "structurally separate" to "structurally separate *and* externally audited."
