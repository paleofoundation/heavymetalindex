## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — architecturally, not yet evidentially.** The wiki's per-claim provenance discipline, locked pooling methodology, deterministic routing audit, and codified wiki/HMTc firewall are Cochrane-grade in *design*; a plaintiff's expert auditing the *system* would find it harder to attack than they expect. But the underlying corpus is ~1,107 sources against a heavy-metals-in-food literature plausibly two orders of magnitude larger, synthesis is not dual-reviewed by independent humans, and the operating org is also the certifier — three gaps Cochrane closes structurally and HMI currently closes only by argument.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane reviews for a narrow PICO routinely screen 5k–50k records via documented PRISMA searches; HMI's full corpus is 1,107 across the entire heavy-metals-and-food domain. The `wishlist`/`discover` machinery is gap-driven, not exhaustive — no (ingredient, metal) cell has a documented "we searched these databases with these strings on this date and screened N→k records." 371 BLOCKED pool cells against 58 READY says the corpus is thin where it matters most. **Below EFSA (~4); far below Cochrane.**

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim is required to trace to a source page (Part 18 lint: "Provenance gaps"); routing audit enforces source→target wiring; cite-keys and frontmatter are machine-checkable. Weakness: claims can cite a source page that itself summarizes a PDF the LLM read, and there is no automated check that the source page's numbers match the PDF — a hostile auditor pulls 30 random claims and verifies them by hand. **Cochrane-comparable in architecture, one verification hop short.**

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** Pooling math is locked (P97/P45 with row-pass rationale documented in `hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1), conversion factors are in code (`basis-conversion.mjs`), rationale tags are required on every staged standard, and the wiki/HMTc separation is explicit. Weakness: no externally pre-registered protocol — Cochrane reviews register protocols on PROSPERO before data collection; HMI's methodology is self-published and self-revised. **Strong, but not pre-registered.**

**Replicability — 3/5.** Pooling, basis conversion, and routing are deterministic from source pages; the audit log lets a reviewer reconstruct what each page said on any date. But the *synthesis text* — the prose on ingredient and metal pages — is LLM-generated, and there is no test that re-running synthesis on the same inputs produces the same outputs. An external reviewer can re-derive the pooled percentile; they cannot re-derive the narrative. **Quantitative layer Cochrane-class; prose layer is not.**

**Currency — 4/5.** 100 new source pages in 7 days, daemon-driven OpenAlex pulls, gap-driven wishlist rotation, autonomous ingest of manual-fetch folders. The 23 synthesis cells written/week against new evidence is the right loop. Weakness: 371 BLOCKED cells means most cells are *not* current, they're starved. **Better than most static references; weaker than the throughput suggests.**

**Independence — 3/5.** Part 2 of CLAUDE.md is the strongest editorial-firewall language I have seen in a non-academic reference work, with both directions of drift named and a per-commit direction-check required. Separate domains (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), one-way linking, no brand pages, no HMTc-certified-brand mentions. But the org operating the wiki is the org running the certification program; the firewall is procedural, not structural. A plaintiff's expert will say: "Cochrane reviewers have no financial stake in a certification mark; the Paleo Foundation does." **Architecturally defensible, structurally vulnerable to the conflict-of-interest attack.**

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

**Vector A — PRISMA-style search-strategy capture per (ingredient, metal) cell.** Extend `tools/wishlist/` and the `discover` skill to emit, per cell, a structured search record: databases queried, exact query strings, date executed, hit counts before dedup, hit counts after dedup against the corpus, screening decisions with reasons. Write to `data/evidence/search-strategies/<ingredient>-<metal>.json`. Surface in the morning report which cells have a documented search and which were populated opportunistically. This converts "we ingested what showed up" into "we executed this search on this date and screened N records" — the single biggest gap a Cochrane reviewer would name. Autonomy loop can execute: it already runs OpenAlex queries; it just doesn't persist the search metadata in PRISMA form.

**Vector B — Coverage estimator against OpenAlex denominators.** Build `tools/audit-cell-coverage.mjs` that, for each populated cell, issues a broad OpenAlex query for `(ingredient terms) AND (metal terms) AND (food OR exposure OR contamination)` filtered to peer-reviewed work since 2000, counts hits, and compares against the cell's `n_studies`. Emit `data/evidence/cell-coverage-audit.csv` with columns `cell, openalex_population, hmi_n_studies, coverage_pct, top_missing_dois`. Cells under ~20% coverage are flagged as "evidentially thin" in the public render; cells over ~70% are flagged as "approaching exhaustive." This is the single artifact that lets the wiki survive the "you only read the papers that confirmed your threshold" attack — it numerically bounds the gap.

**Vector C — Dual-reviewer agreement check on a stratified 5% sample.** Cochrane requires two independent reviewers per included study; HMI uses one LLM pass. Build `tools/audit-dual-extraction.mjs` that selects a stratified 5% sample of source pages (weighted toward A-tier and high-routed-fanout sources), re-extracts the structured fields (matrix, metal, value, basis, n, statistic-type) in a fresh-context subagent run with no access to the existing source page, and diffs against the recorded extraction. Emit `data/evidence/dual-extraction-agreement.csv` with κ/agreement metrics per field. Run weekly. This is the verification hop missing from source provenance (vector for the second-weakest dimension while also raising corpus-completeness credibility) and produces a publishable agreement statistic — the number a brand legal team will ask for first.
