## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The provenance, methodology-transparency, and independence infrastructure are at or near Cochrane parity — code-locked pooling math, per-claim cite-key traceability, and the hard wiki/HMT&C editorial firewall in Part 2 of CLAUDE.md are stronger than most regulatory references. But corpus completeness is roughly 4% of the targeted ~23,000-paper universe, synthesis is severely lagging ingest (31 cells written against 585 new sources, 325 BLOCKED pools vs. 53 READY), and there is no PRISMA-style pre-registered search protocol per question — three gaps that a competent plaintiff expert would attack first.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 996 ingested sources is a credible foundation but a small fraction of the target corpus; OpenAlex pulled only 17 PDFs this week against 585 new pages, indicating ad-hoc manual-fetch dominance rather than a saturating systematic search. No PRISMA flow diagram, no per-cell "identified → screened → included → excluded with reason" record. *Cochrane: 4-5. EFSA: ~4.*
- **Source provenance — 5/5.** Every wiki claim ties to a cite-key; source pages preserve raw extracted values; the routing audit (`product_source_routing_audit.csv`) makes the source→target-page path machine-verifiable; `routing_unresolved.csv = 5` is near-zero leakage. Per-claim provenance is enforced as a build-blocking lint rule. *Cochrane: 5. This wiki matches.*
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** P97/P45 math locked in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1; pooling rules (moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion, log-normal/gamma assumptions, basis conversion) are in code; rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) are mandatory and lint-enforced. Missing: a PROSPERO-equivalent per-cell protocol declared *before* sources accumulate, which is what protects against post-hoc inclusion-criterion drift. *Cochrane: 5. WHO monographs: 4-5.*
- **Replicability — 4/5.** 961 commits give point-in-time reconstructibility; generators are deterministic; `data/evidence/` is structured CSV/JSONL; the routing layer regenerates from source-page frontmatter alone. Deduction: `review_events.jsonl` is acknowledged in CLAUDE.md Part 9 as not yet consistently populated, so synthesis decisions are reconstructible from git but not from a dedicated audit log. *Cochrane: 4-5.*
- **Currency — 2/5.** Synthesis is the bottleneck. 31 cells written, 8 unique pages touched, 585 new source pages — the ratio is roughly 5% conversion of ingest to synthesis. 325 BLOCKED pools means the majority of accumulated evidence is sitting in a register without producing thresholds or synthesis paragraphs. Confidence distribution is overwhelmingly null→low (22 of 31); no cell has been re-synthesized to higher confidence this period. A hostile reviewer pulls a random ingredient page and finds `status: pending` on most analyte sub-blocks. *Cochrane: 4 (living reviews: 5).*
- **Independence — 5/5.** Architectural separation between `heavymetalindex.com` and `heavymetaltested.com`; brand firewall (Part 12) prohibits brand-level data in this repo; standards arithmetic lives in `data/workbench/`, not `wiki/`; bidirectional drift rule (Part 2) is enforced via the silent-threshold-deviation lint. Stronger than EFSA, which co-mingles risk assessment and risk management staff. *Cochrane: 4-5 (COI declared per author). This wiki: 5 by construction.*

### 3. Improvement vectors

Targeting corpus completeness and currency, the two 2/5 dimensions.

1. **Pre-register per-cell search protocols.** Build `tools/autonomy/build-prisma-flow.mjs` that takes an (ingredient, metal) cell, emits a search-string manifest spanning OpenAlex, PubMed, EFSA OpenScience, FDA TDS, EU RASFF, and named grey-literature sources, deduplicates hits against the existing `wiki/sources/` corpus, and writes a PRISMA-style flow diagram to `data/evidence/prisma/<cell>.md` with `identified / screened / included / excluded_with_reason` counts. Make this artifact a precondition: no synthesis pass may advance a cell to `confidence: medium` until its PRISMA flow exists and routing-audit-resolves to it. This converts the wiki's coverage claim from "996 sources" into "N of M identified primary studies for cell X, with the missing M−N enumerated and dispositioned" — the exact form a plaintiff expert needs to attack and cannot easily attack when it's already in the repo.

2. **Drain the BLOCKED pool with a synthesis-debt scheduler.** Add `tools/autonomy/run-synthesis-passes.mjs` to the nightly loop. It reads `pool_state`, groups BLOCKED cells by failure reason (missing basis conversion, summary-only source needing moment-matching, contradicting A-tier sources, n_studies < 2), routes each class to the right remediation (basis-conversion.mjs, moment-match expansion, arbitration prompt, ingest priority bump), and emits a weekly delta. The loop fails the morning report if BLOCKED grew week-over-week. This makes currency a tracked metric rather than an artifact of ingest velocity.

3. **Add an enumerated-target corpus-coverage gauge.** Extend the evidence tooling with `tools/evidence/build-coverage-gauge.mjs`: for each (ingredient, metal) cell, query OpenAlex/PubMed with a canonical search string, cache the candidate-pool size to `data/evidence/corpus-targets.csv`, and compute coverage % = ingested / candidate_pool. Surface per-cell coverage in the morning report and on each ingredient page as a frontmatter field `corpus_coverage_pct`. This replaces the unanchored "996 sources" headline with defensible per-cell completeness numbers, and makes "your wiki is incomplete" a quantitative claim the wiki itself answers first.
