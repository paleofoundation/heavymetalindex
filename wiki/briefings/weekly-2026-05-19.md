## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The architectural commitments — provenance-preserving source pages, deterministic pooling math kept out of `wiki/`, the wiki/HMTc firewall in Part 2, the external-review brief, the routing layer — are genuinely Cochrane-aspirational and put the project ahead of FDA Closer to Zero on transparency and on par with EFSA on methodology disclosure. The fatal-on-hostile-reading gap is execution depth: 31 synthesized cells against an addressable space of roughly 248 ingredients × 10 HMTc analytes ≈ 2,480 cells (about 1.3% coverage), 302 BLOCKED pool entries with no published reason vocabulary, and 969 sources against a literature whose PubMed surface alone runs into the tens of thousands. A plaintiff's expert today would correctly say: "this is a well-designed corpus and a credible methodology, not yet a systematic review."

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 969 source pages is below the order of magnitude a comprehensive heavy-metals-in-food corpus requires. There is no published denominator — no PRISMA-style flow showing total search hits, screened, included, excluded with reasons — which means the corpus cannot defend itself as *comprehensive*, only as *growing*. EFSA opinions usually score ~4 here because they enumerate their search; this wiki currently cannot. The 788-new-sources-in-7-days velocity is real and matters, but velocity is not coverage.

- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Every source page carries `raw_path`, `doi`, `cite_key`, `evidence_tier`, structured `metals`/`ingredients`/`matrices`/`jurisdictions`. The routing layer mechanically enforces source→target traceability; `audit-claim-provenance.mjs` exists. This is genuinely strong and matches Cochrane practice. It loses a point because per-numerical-claim trace coverage (every ppb value in `contamination_profile` linked to a specific source page table cell) is not yet demonstrated as 100%, and synthesis-page narrative claims are harder to audit than structured frontmatter.

- **Methodology transparency — 3/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` and `docs/standards-methodology.md` lock the math, name the rationale tags, and document the row-pass calibration. That beats FDA Closer to Zero (~3) but trails Cochrane (5) on one specific point: there is no pre-registered protocol per synthesis question. Cochrane's authority comes from PROSPERO-style registration *before* the review runs; this wiki's synthesis triggers are post-hoc (n_studies thresholds), which means a hostile reader cannot verify that the methodology wasn't tuned after seeing the data.

- **Replicability — 3/5.** Pooling math is in code, deterministic, and reproducible — that is the load-bearing claim, and it holds for the standards-setting step. The weakness is upstream: LLM-driven extraction is not deterministic. Running the same PDF through the autonomy loop twice can produce different structured frontmatter, different `sample_n`, different matrix tagging. There is no dual-extraction reconciliation step, which is the canonical Cochrane control for this exact problem.

- **Currency — 4/5.** 967 commits, 97 OpenAlex pulls and 788 new source pages in 7 days, active routing audit, file-watcher rebuild. This is materially fresher than EFSA opinion cadence (~years between updates) or WHO monograph cadence. One point off because currency requires a *retrieval-rate* measurement (are we keeping up with new publications?) that the wiki doesn't yet publish.

- **Independence — 3/5.** The Part 2 architectural firewall is unusually thoughtful and the commitment to publish external-reviewer findings verbatim is genuinely Cochrane-grade. But the wiki and HMTc share an operator (Paleo Foundation), HMTc commercial revenue is the explicit motivator, and the strategic frame names brand-legal defensibility as the use case. A plaintiff's expert will argue that operator-level commercial interest taints editorial choices regardless of internal firewall design. The defense — "the gap is stated honestly, the wiki moves toward the literature, the methodology is locked in code" — is strong but cannot fully overcome the structural objection. Cochrane scores ~5 here because Cochrane sells *nothing* downstream of its reviews.

### 3. Improvement vectors

Targeting the weakest dimension (corpus completeness, 2/5) with executable autonomy-loop steps:

1. **Build a PRISMA-style search-and-screen ledger.** Extend `tools/corpus-discovery/` (creating it if absent) to run a fixed set of registered queries on PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, CAB Abstracts, and OpenAlex on a weekly cron, persisting both the query string and the result-set hash to `data/evidence/search-runs/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<db>-<query-id>.json`. Generate `wiki/methodology/prisma-<ingredient>-<metal>.md` per cell showing identified / screened / included / excluded counts with reason codes. This converts "969 sources, growing" into "of N identified across registered queries, M included, K excluded for reasons L" — the form a hostile reader cannot dismiss.

2. **Add dual-pass extraction with disagreement adjudication.** Extend the autonomy loop so each PDF is extracted by two independent LLM sessions (different model or different prompt seed). Diff structured frontmatter (`metals`, `matrices`, `sample_n`, `jurisdictions`, key numerical values) and write disagreements to `data/evidence/extraction-disagreements.csv`. Pages move from `pending` to `populated` only after disagreement resolution. This addresses both replicability (3→4) and provenance (4→5) and is the single change most likely to survive a "your LLM hallucinated the numbers" attack.

3. **Pre-register synthesis protocols per cell.** Before the synthesis pass runs for an (ingredient, metal) cell, emit `wiki/synthesis-protocols/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` containing the PICO-style question, registered search strings, inclusion criteria, basis-conversion plan, and expected pooling method — committed before the synthesis itself. The synthesis page then references the protocol as its registered plan. Drives methodology transparency from 3 to 4 and removes the "you tuned the method after seeing the data" attack vector entirely. The 302 BLOCKED pool entries should publish their blocking reason in the same registry, so blocked cells are visibly blocked-with-cause rather than silently absent.
