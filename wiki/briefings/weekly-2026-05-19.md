## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class** on the architecture, **not yet Cochrane-class** on execution. The methodology layer (locked standards math, routing audit, per-claim provenance rule, Part 2 firewall) is structurally Cochrane-grade — in some respects sharper, because the wiki/HMTc separation is more rigorously formalized than the typical Cochrane sponsor-firewall. But the corpus is ~4% of its own declared target (998 of ~23,000 papers), search strategy is not pre-registered, and 326 of 391 evidence pools are BLOCKED — the assembled evidence does not yet support a "complete literature" claim under hostile cross-examination.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2 / 5.** 998 source pages against an internal target of 23,000 P1-P5 papers; the past 7 days pulled only 17 PDFs from OpenAlex. The intake pipeline is Manual Fetch Kimi folders, Papers Cube, and ad-hoc resourcing — not a documented PRISMA-style systematic search across PubMed, EFSA, FDA TDS, JECFA, peer-reviewed databases, and grey literature with declared inclusion/exclusion criteria. **EFSA opinions sit at ~4 here because their search protocols are pre-registered and PRISMA-flow-diagrammed; this wiki is below that bar today.** A plaintiff expert asking "what was your search strategy and what did you exclude" cannot be answered from the current artifacts.

**Source provenance — 4 / 5.** Per-claim provenance is enforced architecturally (cite-keys, routing audit, lint failure on provenance gaps, source pages in `wiki/sources/` carrying frontmatter that fans out to wiki claims). Stronger than FDA Closer to Zero, comparable to Cochrane. The half-point gap: routing_unresolved = 6 and routing_malformed cases exist, meaning some sources have frontmatter that does not yet trace cleanly to target pages. Trivially closeable.

**Methodology transparency — 3 / 5.** Standards arithmetic is locked in code, deterministic, and documented (P97/P45 with the 90/30 row-pass calibration rationale). Pool admission rules, moment-matching, native-basis convention, and weighting cap are all specified. **What is missing: a corpus-selection protocol of equivalent rigor.** Cochrane reviews carry a pre-registered protocol on PROSPERO before the review begins, declaring search strings, databases, languages, date ranges, and inclusion/exclusion criteria. The HMI methodology is documented for what happens *after* a paper enters `raw/`, but the front door — what gets pulled in the first place — is governed by triage manifests and folder conventions rather than a registered protocol. WHO monographs hit ~4-5 here; this wiki is mid-3.

**Replicability — 3 / 5.** Strong on the deterministic layer: the routing audit, build-routing-audit.mjs, basis-conversion.mjs, and the locked pooling math are reproducible by an external party with the repo. Weak on the corpus layer: an external reviewer cannot re-derive the corpus from documented search strings because none are published. They also cannot replicate the synthesis upgrade behavior — 31 cells written this week, all of them null→{low,medium,high} with zero low→medium or medium→high upgrades, which means the synthesis workflow is producing first-pass evidence but the resynthesis triggers in Part 9 have not yet exercised on the existing corpus.

**Currency — 4 / 5.** 977 commits, 587 new source pages and 17 OpenAlex pulls in the past 7 days, file-watcher-driven routing rebuild, automated lint on agency-source ingest. Better than EFSA opinions (which are reissued on multi-year cycles) and FDA Closer to Zero (which moves at agency pace). Half-point gap: 22 products are pending Step 0 Lock, meaning governance is trailing ingest and some category pages are routing destinations without locked taxonomy.

**Independence — 4 / 5.** The Part 2 firewall (wiki/HMT&C drift check, direction-of-edit rule, separate domains, no brand mentions in wiki, no HMTc-certified-brand promotion) is more rigorously codified than the conflict-of-interest disclosures in most Cochrane reviews. The structural problem that prevents a 5: both properties are operated by the Paleo Foundation, and a plaintiff expert can argue that institutional independence is asserted but not verified. The lint check for `silent threshold deviation` between certification pages and wiki pages is the strongest available mitigation; making its results publicly auditable would close most of the remaining gap.

### 3. Improvement vectors (weakest dimension: corpus completeness)

**Vector A — Build a pre-registered systematic search runner.** Add `tools/corpus/systematic-search.mjs` that executes documented search strategies against PubMed E-utilities, OpenAlex, EFSA Journal, FDA TDS, JECFA monographs, and EU RASFF, with declared search strings, date ranges, languages, and exclusion criteria checked into `docs/corpus/search-protocol-v1.md`. Output: a PRISMA-flow CSV at `data/evidence/prisma-flow.csv` (identified / screened / eligible / included / excluded with reasons) regenerated on each run. This is the single artifact that closes the "what was your search strategy" attack and lifts corpus completeness from 2 toward 4.

**Vector B — Extend the autonomy loop to drain BLOCKED pools first, not freshest-paper first.** The current daemon ingests what arrives in `raw/`. Add a planner step in `tools/autonomy/` that reads `pool_state` from the morning report, identifies the 326 BLOCKED (row, metal) cells, computes the minimum source set each needs to reach READY (typically 3-5 papers per cell), generates a targeted OpenAlex/PubMed query per blocked cell, and prioritizes those pulls in the daily fetch queue. Outcome: BLOCKED count trends down as a function of compute, not arrival order.

**Vector C — Generate a public PRISMA-flow page per HMTc category at build time.** Add a generator under `tools/evidence/` that, for each product-category page, emits the PRISMA flow specific to that category's contributing sources: total identified by search, screened, included in pool, excluded with stated reason, pool sample-size weight cap applied. Render it as a `## Evidence base` section on the public page. This turns the corpus-completeness claim from an internal assertion into a per-page externally-auditable artifact, which is what raises the dimension from "we say it's complete" to "here is the flow diagram showing why."
