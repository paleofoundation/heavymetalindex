## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** Methodology lock (P97/P45, native-basis pooling, regulatory cap, rationale tags), per-claim provenance discipline, deterministic routing/synthesis code, and the heavymetalindex.com ↔ heavymetaltested.com architectural firewall put this wiki at a register most industry "evidence reviews" never reach — but it is not yet Cochrane-class because there is no published search-and-screen protocol, no PRISMA-style record of records-identified vs. records-included per cell, and the synthesis layer covers only ~14% of pool cells (53 READY / 381 total) so the literature-completeness claim cannot yet be defended end-to-end under hostile expert review.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5/5.** 996 source pages and 17 OpenAlex pulls/week is meaningful throughput, but there is no declared search string, no database-coverage list (OpenAlex + manual fetch is implicit, not documented), no per-cell PRISMA flow, and no record of which papers were *seen and excluded*. Cochrane: ~4.5 (multi-database protocol, registered). EFSA: ~4. A plaintiff's expert asking "what was your search strategy for cadmium in cocoa" currently has no single artifact to point to.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** This is the wiki's strongest dimension. CLAUDE.md Part 18 lint fails the build on provenance gaps; every numerical claim must link to a source page; the routing audit is the deterministic source→target ledger; cite-keys are stable. Drops half a point because `contamination_profile` cell values currently expose the *result* of synthesis but not the per-paper contribution table inline on the rendered page — a reviewer has to re-derive the contributing source list from the routing audit rather than read it under the value.

**Methodology transparency — 3.5/5.** The standards math is locked, public (governing-principles.md), and code-resident (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`, the pooling engine). Rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) are enforced. What is *not* yet transparent: the source→structured-row extraction methodology (how a paper's reported mean/median/IQR becomes pool input), the moment-matching assumptions per source, and the per-paper basis-conversion log. WHO monographs: ~4.5. EFSA opinions: ~4. The methodology *exists* and is reproducible in code; it just is not yet rendered as a reader-facing artifact.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Compiled-wiki architecture means an external reviewer with the same `raw/` corpus and `tools/` could re-derive synthesis deterministically — this is genuinely Cochrane-equivalent in spirit. The blocker is `raw/` itself: copyrighted PDFs cannot be redistributed, so the replicability claim has to rest on cite-keys + the reviewer re-fetching primary sources. Cochrane faces the same constraint and lands at ~4. The 0.5 deduction reflects that the extraction-to-pool path is not yet documented step-by-step in a way an external reviewer could audit *without* reading the JS.

**Currency — 3/5.** Source-page currency is strong (daemon-driven, 585 new sources/week, OpenAlex polling). Synthesis currency is the gap: 315 BLOCKED cells vs 53 READY means only ~14% of (ingredient × metal) cells carry up-to-date synthesis. Of the 31 cells written this week, 22 came in at `low` confidence and only 3 at `high`. The wiki *knows* the literature far better than it *summarizes* the literature. Cochrane: ~4 (reviews flagged stale after 2 years; this wiki has the trigger infrastructure but hasn't yet caught up).

**Independence — 4/5.** Architecturally stronger than Cochrane's typical posture: separate domains, separate repos, Part 2 of CLAUDE.md mandates bidirectional drift-flagging, no HMTc-certified brand may appear in the wiki, no brand-level data at all (Part 12 firewall), staff-only standards math kept out of `wiki/` entirely. Deducted one point because independence has not yet been *demonstrated* under adversarial conditions — no class-action expert has tried to attack the wiki yet, so the design is unproven in practice rather than proven.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness)

**(1) Materialize a PRISMA-style search-and-screen ledger per cell.** Build `tools/autonomy/build-prisma-ledger.mjs` that, for each (metal × matrix × jurisdiction) cell, emits a `data/evidence/prisma/<metal>-<matrix>.json` record containing: OpenAlex query strings used, date ranges searched, databases queried, records-identified count, duplicates-removed count, records-screened count, records-excluded-with-reasons (one row per exclusion), records-included. Render a generated wiki page per cell at `wiki/methodology/prisma/<metal>-<matrix>.md` so the search protocol is the reader-facing artifact, not a tribal-knowledge thing inside the autonomy loop. This is the single artifact a plaintiff's expert will ask for first.

**(2) Add a corpus-coverage daemon tick that compares ingested-source counts against the OpenAlex universe per cell.** Extend the morning report with `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-coverage.mjs` that, for each (metal × matrix), runs the canonical OpenAlex query (locked per cell in step 1), counts the universe of matching works, and reports `ingested / universe` as a coverage ratio. Cells under 70% coverage become P1 ingest targets the next cycle; cells over 90% get a "saturation flag" the synthesis layer can use to upgrade `confidence`. Output: `data/evidence/autonomy/corpus-coverage-<date>.md` + frontmatter `coverage_ratio` and `coverage_asof` on every ingredient and metal page.

**(3) Lock an inclusion/exclusion protocol in `docs/inclusion-criteria.md` and emit `exclusion_reason` on every screened-but-rejected paper.** The wiki has A/B/C evidence tiers but no published *exclusion* protocol — meaning every rejection is currently a per-session judgment call with no audit trail. Add an `exclusion_reason` enum (`out-of-scope-matrix`, `non-quantitative`, `non-food`, `retracted`, `superseded`, `non-peer-reviewed-and-not-agency`, `language-untranslated`, etc.), extend the routing audit to log it for every paper the screener touched but didn't ingest, and write rejected records to `data/evidence/screening_rejected.csv`. This converts "we read everything that mattered" from an assertion into a reproducible ledger — the move that takes corpus completeness from 2.5 to 4.
