## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class — but the infrastructure is approaching it.** The wiki has Cochrane-grade *machinery* (per-claim provenance, locked deterministic methodology, versioned governance, an active resynthesis trigger system, architectural firewall against commercial deliverables), but the *substantive corpus* is still early: 999 sources and 57 READY pools against 351 BLOCKED cells means roughly 14% of (row, metal) cells currently have admissible evidence, and 73% of last-week's synthesis events were null→low confidence. A hostile expert would not yet be able to say "you read the literature"; they could only say "you built the system that would read it."

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane baseline 4–5. The heavy-metals-in-food literature exceeds 50,000 records across PubMed/Web of Science/CABI/AGRIS once you include occurrence surveys, TDS reports, and exposure assessments. 999 source pages with 17 OpenAlex pulls/week and the rest via manual fetch is not a systematic search — it's opportunistic. No pre-registered search strategy, no PRISMA-style record-identification log, no documented database coverage list. EFSA scientific opinions (~4) routinely cite 300–500 primary records for a single metal-food pair; this wiki has fewer than that across the entire corpus.

- **Source provenance — 4/5.** Cochrane baseline 4–5. Genuinely strong: routing audit enforces source-to-target traceability, only 7 unresolved entries against 999 sources (<1%), and Part 2's discipline that every numerical claim must link to a source page is structurally enforced by lint. The half-point gap is the absence of formal inclusion-criteria fingerprints on each source — Cochrane records *why* a record was included or excluded against pre-specified PICO criteria; this wiki records that a source was ingested, not the screening decision.

- **Methodology transparency — 3/5.** Cochrane baseline 4–5; WHO monographs ~4–5; FDA Closer to Zero ~3. The *standards-setting* methodology (P97/P45, native basis, regulatory cap min, moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion, ratchet trigger) is locked, versioned (Governing Principles 2.1), reproducible, and in code. That's the strong half. The weak half is the *evidence-inclusion* methodology: no documented PICO equivalent for what counts as admissible literature, no pre-specified exclusion criteria, no protocol-style pre-registration of what each synthesis pass will read before it reads it. The pooling math is bulletproof; the upstream funnel feeding the pooling math is not.

- **Replicability — 3/5.** Cochrane baseline 4. Deterministic pooling, git-versioned source pages, deterministic routing audit, evidence registers in `data/evidence/` — an external reviewer with read access could re-derive the standards arithmetic from the source corpus. But they could *not* re-derive which sources should have been in the corpus in the first place, because that step is not specified. Replicability of synthesis-from-corpus is good; replicability of corpus-from-literature is absent.

- **Currency — 4/5.** Cochrane baseline 3 (Cochrane reviews update every 2–7 years; many are stale). This wiki publishes 981 commits, runs a daemon loop, has `last_reviewed`/`last_full_resynthesis` fields, and resynthesizes at n_studies doublings. Architecturally superior to Cochrane on currency. The half-point gap: currency of synthesis is moot for the 351 BLOCKED cells where there is no synthesis yet to keep current.

- **Independence — 3/5.** Cochrane baseline 5 (Cochrane is institutionally separate from any commercial deliverable). The wiki has *architectural* separation from HMTc (different domain, Part 2 firewall, drift-direction commit-message check, standards arithmetic banished from `wiki/`), but the Paleo Foundation operates both. That is editorial separation under common ownership, not institutional independence. A plaintiff's expert can and will frame "the certifier operates the reference wiki" as a structural conflict. The firewall holds up to honest scrutiny; it will not survive *adversarial* framing as cleanly as Cochrane's institutional posture.

### 3. Improvement vectors (raising corpus completeness, 2 → 4)

**A. Build a multi-database systematic search runner under `tools/corpus/`.** Implement `tools/corpus/run-systematic-search.mjs` that executes pre-specified Boolean queries against PubMed E-utilities, Europe PMC, CrossRef, AGRIS, and EFSA Journal across the cartesian product of {10 HMTc analytes} × {Category 1–N taxonomy rows} × {occurrence, exposure, TDS, dietary}, writes each query's result set to `data/evidence/search-strategy/<query-hash>.csv` with date-stamped run metadata, and queues novel DOIs/PMIDs into the existing manual-fetch tracker. Replaces opportunistic OpenAlex pulls with reproducible, documented coverage that a hostile reviewer can re-run.

**B. Emit per-cell PRISMA flow records.** Extend `tools/evidence/build-routing-audit.mjs` (or add a sibling) to emit `data/evidence/prisma/<ingredient>-<metal>.json` per (row, metal) cell containing: records identified by source-database, deduplicated, screened-by-title/abstract, excluded with reason (wrong matrix, wrong analyte, brand-level data, retracted, etc.), full-text reviewed, included in pool. Render the flow as the standard PRISMA 2020 diagram on each ingredient page below the contamination profile. Closes the replicability gap and converts the wiki from "we read what we read" to "here is what was identified, here is what was excluded, here is why."

**C. Backfill grey-literature target list.** Add `tools/corpus/grey-literature-targets.yaml` enumerating every authoritative occurrence dataset that should exist in the corpus — EFSA Scientific Opinions (Pb 2010, Cd 2009, iAs 2009, MeHg 2012 and updates), JECFA monographs, FDA Total Diet Study tables 1991–present, UK FSA TDS, ANSES EAT2/EAT-Infant, Health Canada TDS, FSSAI surveys, China CDC TDS, Korea MFDS, Australia NZ FSANZ — then have the autonomy loop gap-fill against the list and emit `data/evidence/grey-literature-gaps.csv` showing which targets are not yet ingested. Turns "we found these grey-literature sources" into "we have systematically inventoried the grey literature and accounted for each entry."
