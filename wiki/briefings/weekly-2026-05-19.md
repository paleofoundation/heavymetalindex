## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has built the spine Cochrane requires — per-claim provenance, locked deterministic synthesis math, doctrinal independence from the commercial deliverable, and update cadence that already exceeds Cochrane's — but it has not yet stood up the artifacts Cochrane reviewers reach for first: a published PRISMA-style flow per synthesis cell, a per-study risk-of-bias framework, and an executed external review. A hostile plaintiff's expert today could not break the provenance or methodology claims, but could ask "where is the protocol, the flow diagram, the RoB table for this cell?" and the wiki would not yet have a clean answer.

### 2. Dimensional grading

- **Corpus completeness — 3/5.** 970 source pages with active OpenAlex pull (97 PDFs in 7 days) is large, but the pool state tells the real story: **306 BLOCKED cells vs. 51 READY** means most (ingredient, metal) cells do not yet have admitted evidence. No PRISMA flow diagram is published per cell; no documented per-cell search-string registry exists. Cochrane: 5 (documented searches per question, PRISMA flow mandatory). EFSA: 4. HMI today: corpus is growing fast but its *boundary* isn't yet published, which is what completeness actually means.
- **Source provenance — 4/5.** This is the strongest dimension. `tools/audit-claim-provenance.mjs` is a build-blocking lint; raw PDFs are immutable; routing audit is deterministic and currently at `routing_unresolved: 0`. Every wiki claim must trace to a source page; every source page is frozen at ingest. Genuinely competitive with Cochrane (5) and ahead of FDA Closer to Zero (3). Loses a point because provenance reaches the **source** but not the **figure/table/sample-set** within the source — paragraph-level provenance, not claim-cell-level.
- **Methodology transparency — 4/5.** The locked P97/P45 math, the row-pass calibration rationale, the rationale-tag taxonomy (`literature-baseline | regulatory-alignment | precautionary | feasibility-driven`), and basis-conversion in code are all transparent and adversarially defensible. Cochrane (5) wins on **GRADE certainty assessment** and **PROSPERO-style pre-registration of protocols**, neither of which HMI publishes. WHO monographs (4-5) match HMI here. The methodology is documented; the per-cell *application* of it is not yet pre-registered.
- **Replicability — 4/5.** In mechanical terms HMI is arguably ahead of Cochrane: synthesis is auto-generated from sources by code in `tools/evidence/`, the routing audit is deterministic, and anyone with the repo can re-run. The blocker is LLM non-determinism in the extraction step — `data/evidence/ai_adjudication_queue.csv` exists because not all extraction is deterministic — and no pinned toolchain spec / dataset release. Cochrane wins on human-readable inclusion logs; HMI wins on re-runnable pipeline.
- **Currency — 5/5.** 788 new source pages and 293 new product pages in 7 days, 973 lifetime commits, lint pass flags stale claims and regulatory drift automatically. Cochrane reviews update every 5-7 years and are routinely cited as out of date; HMI's autonomy loop updates daily. This is the dimension where HMI genuinely **beats** Cochrane, and it should be marketed as such.
- **Independence — 4/5.** The Part 2 firewall is doctrinal, structurally enforced (separate domains heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), and lint-checked (silent threshold deviation is a lint failure). Standards arithmetic is exiled to `data/workbench/`, not `wiki/`. The external-review brief at `docs/external-review-brief.md` is well-designed but **not yet executed** — three reviewer slots planned, zero engaged. Same operator (Paleo Foundation) owns both wiki and certification; only published external review converts the firewall claim from doctrine to fact.

### 3. Improvement vectors (targeting corpus completeness)

The weakest dimension is corpus completeness, specifically the absence of PRISMA-style boundary documentation. Three executable build steps:

1. **Build a per-cell PRISMA manifest generator.** Add `tools/coverage/build-prisma-manifest.mjs` that, for every (ingredient, metal) cell, emits a flow record: `identified` (OpenAlex query hits + manual-fetch candidates), `screened` (passed title/abstract filter), `excluded_with_reason` (drawn from `data/evidence/audit-queue.csv` REVISE/REJECT dispositions, bucketed: off-topic, wrong-population, no-quantitative-data, basis-unconvertible, language), `included` (admitted to pool). Render as `wiki/coverage/<ingredient>-<metal>.md` with a flow diagram and a search-string registry. This converts the 306 BLOCKED cells from "we don't have evidence" to "here is exactly why we don't yet have evidence" — the Cochrane move.

2. **Extend the structured extractor to capture per-source risk-of-bias.** Add a `risk_of_bias:` frontmatter block to source pages with fields: `sampling_frame` (probabilistic / convenience / market-basket / regulatory-surveillance), `analytical_method_validated` (Y/N + method cite), `speciation_reported` (Y/N — required for iAs/MeHg), `LOD_LOQ_reported` (Y/N), `n_per_subgroup`, `replication`, `blinding`. Have the extraction LLM populate from method sections; have a human-spot-check queue. Then weight pool admission on RoB, not just evidence tier. This gives reviewers the per-study table Cochrane RoB 2.0 readers expect.

3. **Stand up cell-level pre-registered protocols.** For each (ingredient, metal) cell that enters synthesis, the autonomy loop emits `wiki/protocols/<cell>.md` *before* synthesis runs, declaring: search string, inclusion criteria, basis conversion plan, planned pooling method (raw-sample / moment-matched), planned percentile, and named exclusion rules. Commit-lock the protocol; the synthesis pass writes `protocol_followed: true` or `deviation: <reason>` to `data/evidence/review_events.jsonl`. This is the PROSPERO analog. It also closes the only methodology-transparency gap: deviations become a logged, auditable artifact instead of an invisible judgment call.

Executing all three would lift corpus completeness to 4-5 and methodology transparency to 5, putting the wiki at Cochrane parity on every dimension except executed external review — which is a separate workstream already specified in `docs/external-review-brief.md` and needs only contracting, not building.
