## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Not yet Cochrane-class — but approaching it on the dimensions that matter most for the litigation defense use case.** Per-claim provenance and the architectural HMT&C/wiki firewall are genuinely Cochrane-grade, but corpus completeness is roughly two orders of magnitude short of where it needs to be (986 sources against a target literature universe of ~23,000+ papers, with 306 pool cells still BLOCKED) and there is no pre-registered search protocol on the record — that is the first dimension a plaintiff's expert or an EFSA reviewer would attack, and it would fall.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 986 source pages is a small fraction of the relevant peer-reviewed and regulatory literature on dietary heavy-metal exposure. 306 BLOCKED pools across product × analyte cells confirms the corpus is not yet dense enough for synthesis on most rows. More damaging: the corpus is being assembled by opportunistic ingest (OpenAlex pulls, Papers Cube, manual fetches), not from a documented database-by-database systematic search with declared strings and dates. Cochrane baseline: 4-5 with PROSPERO-registered search of MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL, plus hand-search and author contact. EFSA opinions sit at ~4; FDA Closer to Zero at ~3. This wiki is below FDA today on this dimension.

**Source provenance — 5/5.** Genuinely Cochrane-class. Routing audit at 0 unresolved. Every numerical claim is required by lint to trace to a source page (`provenance gaps` is a build-blocking failure). Cite-keys, evidence tiers, ISO dates, three-field state system, and the routing layer's automated fan-out mean that if any source is withdrawn or contested, the dependent claims can be identified and re-derived. This is the dimension on which the wiki is already defensible.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** The pooling math is deterministic, in code, and documented (`docs/standards-methodology.md`, `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1). The 90/30 row-pass calibration is named as a policy choice with the P97/P45 mechanism derived from it — the adversarial reframing in the governance doc ("attack 90/30, not P97") is sophisticated. Native-basis discipline, regulatory-cap rule, and rationale tags (literature-baseline / regulatory-alignment / precautionary / feasibility-driven) are all explicit. What is missing: a pre-registered, externally-visible search protocol; declared inclusion/exclusion criteria for the corpus; a PRISMA flow diagram. The synthesis math is transparent; the corpus-building criteria are not. Cochrane baseline: 5. WHO monographs: 4-5. This wiki: 3 — high marks on math, low marks on corpus-assembly criteria.

**Replicability — 3/5.** An external reviewer with this repo at a given commit can re-derive every cell value: source pages are in `wiki/sources/`, the routing audit regenerates from those, and the pooling math is in `tools/evidence/`. That is genuinely replicable forward from the source set. But the corpus itself is not replicable from a documented search — the reviewer cannot independently re-assemble it from declared databases and strings, only verify what is present. That is the gap between "audit-replicable" (currently true) and "search-replicable" (Cochrane standard, currently false). 959 commits and git history are an underutilized asset here.

**Currency — 3/5.** The wiki is in the steep part of an active build (685 new source pages and 31 synthesis cells written in the last seven days). 22 cells went null→low; only 3 reached null→high. Most cells remain pending. On the structural side, the synthesis trigger rules (n_studies doubling, 2×-disagreement, two A-tier contradictions) are well-designed for a living-review posture once stable. Cochrane reviews currently run at 4-5 with the shift to living reviews; this wiki is a 3 today with a credible path to 4 once the BLOCKED count comes down.

**Independence — 4/5.** The architectural firewall in CLAUDE.md Part 2 — naming both drift directions (softening wiki claims toward HMT&C convenience *and* strengthening claims to justify HMT&C thresholds), forcing direction-of-drift declaration on every substantive edit, separating the math layer (`data/workbench/`) from the public literature layer (`wiki/`) — is a more thoughtful design than what most certification programs operate with. The HMTc Governing Principles v2.1 explicitly names "make it legally defensible" as a non-subordinate principle. The remaining 1-point gap: the wiki and the certifier are the same organization, so independence is *architectural* rather than *institutional*. Cochrane has institutional separation from product manufacturers; this wiki does not. The architecture is the answer, but it is the only answer.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness, the weakest dimension)

**Vector 1 — Pre-registered search protocol and PRISMA generator.** Add `data/evidence/search-protocol.md` declaring each database (PubMed/MEDLINE, Web of Science, Scopus, EFSA Knowledge Junction, FDA databases, JECFA monographs, national TDS programs), the search strings, the date ranges, the inclusion/exclusion criteria, and the screening protocol. Build `tools/evidence/build-prisma-flow.mjs` to regenerate a PRISMA-2020-style flow diagram (`identified → screened → excluded with reasons → included`) from the existing OpenAlex pull logs, manual-fetch tracker, and source pages. This is the single artifact a regulatory-affairs reviewer or plaintiff expert will ask for first; not having it is the visible weakness.

**Vector 2 — Gap-driven corpus expansion.** Add `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-coverage.mjs` to the night loop. It reads the (row × analyte) BLOCKED pool list (306 cells today), emits MeSH-bounded PubMed and OpenAlex queries for every blocked cell, ranks queries by HMTc category readiness and ratchet-event proximity, and writes them into the existing manual-fetch tracker with priority tier. The wishlist generator already exists in adjacent form (`data/evidence/autonomy/gap-driven-wishlist.csv`); extend it to emit *executable* queries rather than topic suggestions. Corpus growth moves from opportunistic to systematic in a single tool change.

**Vector 3 — Replay-and-hash audit harness.** Add `tools/evidence/replay-synthesis.mjs` that, given a commit SHA and a (row, analyte) cell, re-runs the synthesis pass from the source set at that commit and emits the cell value plus a SHA-256 hash of the contributing-source state. Persist `data/evidence/synthesis-replay-hashes.jsonl` alongside `review_events.jsonl`. The result: any external auditor — brand legal team, EFSA reviewer, plaintiff expert — can clone the repo at any historical commit and independently verify that every cell value re-computes deterministically from the documented source set. This converts the existing "math is in code" property into the externally-verifiable "audit replay" property Cochrane reviewers expect.
