## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has Cochrane-grade infrastructure for provenance, version control, locked methodology, and the wiki/HMTc firewall, but its corpus is incomplete (970 of ~23,000 potentially-relevant papers, ~4%) and its independence is structural rather than institutional — the same legal entity operates the literature review and the commercial certification that consumes it. The math, file conventions, and audit pipeline could survive hostile scrutiny today; the *scope* and *separation-of-roles* arguments could not.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5 / 5.**
970 sources against a domain of ~23,000 candidate papers is below EFSA Scientific Opinions on Pb/Cd in food (which work from near-complete subdomain corpora) and well below Cochrane's pre-registered exhaustive search. The triage manifest exists but there is no PRISMA flow diagram, no documented database-by-database search strategy with date cutoffs, and no recorded exclusion rationale at corpus level. 302 BLOCKED pool cells confirm most (row × metal) intersections lack sufficient evidence to synthesize.

**Source provenance — 4.5 / 5.**
Every wiki claim must trace to a source page (Part 18 lint blocks the build on provenance gaps). 8 routing_unresolved entries out of 970 sources is ~99.2% clean routing. Cite-key discipline, frontmatter schema enforcement, immutable `raw/`, and append-only `review_events.jsonl` match Cochrane's per-study traceability. The one gap versus Cochrane: no per-study risk-of-bias instrument beyond the A/B/C evidence tier.

**Methodology transparency — 4 / 5.**
CLAUDE.md, docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md, and docs/standards-methodology are public, in-repo, and locked at named versions (Governing Principles v2.1, May 2026). The P97/P45 row-pass calibration is justified explicitly against adversarial reading. This matches WHO monograph practice. The gap: synthesis fires on `n_studies` thresholds without a per-cell pre-registered protocol declaring inclusion criteria *before* the evidence is read — Cochrane's pre-registration baseline.

**Replicability — 3.5 / 5.**
961 commits, deterministic pooling code (`tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs`, pool admission rules), and structured extraction in `data/evidence/` mean an external auditor can re-derive the threshold math bit-for-bit. The weak link: LLM-mediated synthesis steps (initial population, contradiction arbitration) are reproducible in *structure* via `review_events.jsonl`, but not in *output* — the same source set re-synthesized later may produce slightly different narrative claims. Cochrane requires deterministic dual extraction; this wiki has neither dual extraction nor determinism on the synthesis surface.

**Currency — 3 / 5.**
786 new sources in 7 days and 97 OpenAlex pulls/week is aggressive — faster than EFSA, faster than any Cochrane living review. But synthesis throughput lags: only 8 unique pages touched by 31 cell writes against 246 ingredient × 36 metal = ~8,856 possible cells. Ingest is winning, synthesis is not. The wiki is current at the source-page layer and stale at the synthesis layer.

**Independence — 2.5 / 5.**
The architectural separation (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com, no brand mentions, drift-direction logged per edit) is real and defensible *as far as it goes*. But Cochrane reviews are authored by parties with no commercial stake in any clinical product; EFSA reviews are authored by panels independent of regulated entities. The Paleo Foundation owns both the literature layer and the certification that prices the literature layer's findings. A plaintiff's expert can credibly characterize this as a methodological vulnerability regardless of how clean the firewall is in practice. Cyprus database-rights positioning does not address it.

**Composite:** infrastructure dimensions (provenance, transparency) score at Cochrane baseline. Scope and separation dimensions (completeness, independence) score below EFSA and well below Cochrane.

### 3. Improvement vectors — raise corpus completeness from 2.5 to 4+

The autonomy loop can execute all three. Each is a specific tool.

**Vector A — `tools/autonomy/prisma-pull-record.mjs`: PRISMA flow logging per corpus expansion.**
Extend the OpenAlex pull (and any future PubMed/Scopus pull) to emit `data/evidence/prisma/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<query-hash>.json` per query with: query string, database, date range searched, records identified, records screened (and screening criteria), records excluded with reason codes (off-topic, wrong matrix, non-peer-reviewed, duplicate, paywall-unobtainable), records included, records pending. Roll up weekly into `data/evidence/prisma/cumulative.json` to drive a public PRISMA diagram on heavymetalindex.com. This converts "we have 970 sources" from an asserted number into a re-derivable one and answers the "how do we know you didn't cherry-pick?" attack directly.

**Vector B — `tools/autonomy/agency-catalog-sweep.mjs`: systematic gray-literature ingest.**
The non-peer-reviewed authoritative corpus (FDA Total Diet Study quarterly releases, FDA Closer to Zero documents, EFSA Scientific Opinions, JECFA reports, EU RASFF notifications, Health Canada food surveillance, state AG enforcement orders, Australia/NZ FSANZ surveys) is *enumerable* — each agency publishes a catalog. Build a per-agency adapter that scrapes the catalog, diffs against `wiki/sources/`, and queues missing items into `data/evidence/autonomy/gap-driven-wishlist.csv` with `source_class: agency-catalog` and `agency: <slug>`. Run weekly. This is the single biggest scope gap: Cochrane equivalents always include gray literature systematically, and HMTc threshold-setting depends on regulatory ceilings whose source documents must be exhaustively present.

**Vector C — `tools/autonomy/citation-snowball.mjs`: backwards-and-forwards citation chasing on A-tier sources.**
For every source promoted at A-tier, extract its reference list (via OpenAlex `referenced_works` or Crossref) and queue any cite-keys not already in `wiki/sources/` into the wishlist with `discovery_path: snowball-from-<parent-cite-key>` and `tier_prior: A`. Symmetrically, query OpenAlex for works citing each A-tier source and queue those too. Snowballing is how Cochrane reviewers catch papers the database search missed (alternative terminology, older indexing, non-English titles). Right now the corpus grows by query — it should also grow by citation graph.

Together these move corpus completeness toward 4 by making the corpus auditable (A), exhaustively covering the regulatory layer (B), and closing the database-search blind spots Cochrane reviewers explicitly screen for (C). Independence remains structurally limited and is not fixable from inside the repo; the long-term answer there is an editorial board external to the Foundation, which is a governance decision, not a build step.
