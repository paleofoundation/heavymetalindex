## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The wiki has the structural bones — per-claim provenance via the routing layer, deterministic standards math in code, immutable `raw/`, git-versioned reconstructibility, and an architecturally enforced firewall between literature and certification — that most Cochrane reviews achieve through editorial discipline rather than infrastructure. What is missing is the *front matter* of a Cochrane review: a pre-registered search protocol, documented inclusion/exclusion criteria, PRISMA-style flow accounting, and a corpus large enough (922 sources, 286 BLOCKED pool cells) to credibly claim completeness across the heavy-metals-in-food literature.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness: 2/5.** Cochrane baseline 4-5; EFSA ~4. 922 sources is small for a corpus that claims to cover the complete heavy-metals food-and-supply-chain literature. There is no pre-registered search protocol, no documented database list (OpenAlex is the only visible source for the 97 PDFs/week pull), no PRISMA flow diagram per (metal × matrix), and no rejection log — 286 BLOCKED pool cells confirm that most (row, analyte) targets do not yet have enough evidence to compute even a P97. A plaintiff's expert with a Web of Science account would produce relevant papers not in this corpus within an hour.

**Source provenance: 4/5.** Cochrane baseline 5. This is the wiki's strongest dimension. The routing audit forces every wiki claim to trace to a source page with a populated cite-key, evidence tier, and source-type. Lint blocks builds on provenance gaps. The `raw/` directory is append-only. The only reason this is not a 5 is that synthesis-pass numerical values (typical_ppb, p95_ppb) sometimes derive from summary statistics via moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion — defensible per WHO/JECFA practice, but the pseudo-sample step is a layer of inference between the source and the wiki value that a hostile reader can probe.

**Methodology transparency: 4/5.** Cochrane baseline 4-5; WHO ~5; FDA Closer to Zero ~3. The standards math (P97 clean / P45 dirty, 90/30 row-pass calibration, native-basis convention, regulatory cap) is locked in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` and `docs/standards-methodology.md` with version history and rationale. Deterministic, reproducible, in code. The weak spot is synthesis methodology: "read top 5 most-cited prior contributors" for incremental updates is a heuristic without a published acceptance criterion, and the n_studies-doubling resynthesis trigger has no published convergence guarantee.

**Replicability: 3/5.** Cochrane baseline 5. The infrastructure to re-derive the wiki from `raw/` and code exists (immutable sources, deterministic generators, git-tracked routing). But replicability in the Cochrane sense means an independent team could run the same search protocol and arrive at the same corpus. There is no published search protocol to re-run. An external reviewer can verify that *given this corpus*, the wiki follows from it; they cannot verify that *this corpus* is the right corpus.

**Currency: 3/5.** Cochrane baseline 3-4 (most reviews update every 2-5 years). 557 commits and weekly ingest cadence is faster than Cochrane. But only 15 synthesis cells were written this week and 286 pool cells remain BLOCKED, meaning the ingest rate outpaces the synthesis rate and the visible wiki content lags the underlying corpus. The confidence distribution (10 null→low, 1 null→high) confirms that newly-synthesized cells are low-confidence; the wiki is current but thin.

**Independence: 5/5.** Cochrane baseline 3-4 (industry funding is the chronic problem). The Part 2 firewall, the architectural separation of heavymetalindex.com from heavymetaltested.com, the brand-firewall in Part 12, the rationale tags (`literature-baseline` / `regulatory-alignment` / `precautionary` / `feasibility-driven`) that force the literature/HMTc gap to be named not hidden, and the rule that HMTc brands are never mentioned in the wiki — this is stronger independence than most Cochrane reviews achieve, because it is enforced by repo structure and lint rather than by reviewer disclosure.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness)

**Vector 1 — Pre-registered search protocol artifact.** Add `docs/search-protocol.md` (one file per metal × broad matrix pair, e.g., `docs/search-protocols/lead-infant-foods.md`) declaring: databases queried (OpenAlex, PubMed, EFSA Knowledge Junction, FDA TDS, EU RASFF), exact search strings, date range, language restrictions, inclusion criteria (matrix in scope, metal measured, sample size threshold), exclusion criteria (single-case reports, conference abstracts without methods). The autonomy loop already produces `data/evidence/autonomy/ingest-batch-YYYY-MM-DD.md`; extend `tools/autonomy/` to emit a PRISMA-style flow record (`identified` / `screened` / `eligible` / `included` / `excluded with reason`) per batch, keyed to the protocol. This converts the existing ingest pipeline from undocumented to pre-registered without changing the ingest itself.

**Vector 2 — Automated corpus-completeness audit.** Add `tools/autonomy/audit-corpus-completeness.mjs`: for each (ingredient × metal) cell in `data/workbench/standards/`, run a deterministic OpenAlex query (titles + abstracts matching the metal and ingredient slugs from frontmatter), count candidate papers published in the cell's evidence window, and compare to the routing audit's count of contributing sources. Emit `data/evidence/corpus-completeness.csv` with columns `cell_id, openalex_candidates, ingested_sources, coverage_pct, gap_count`. Cells with `coverage_pct < 50%` block synthesis-pass promotion from PROVISIONAL → READY. This is the OpenAlex-augmented version of a Cochrane "did we miss anything" check, runnable nightly.

**Vector 3 — Forward-citation snowball ingest.** Extend the ingest skill so that when a source page is created, the tool fetches the paper's forward citations from OpenAlex (papers that cite *this* paper) and reverse citations (papers *this* paper cites), then checks whether each citing/cited work is already in the corpus. Unmatched citations write rows to `data/evidence/autonomy/snowball-candidates.csv` with the originating cite-key, citation direction, and OpenAlex ID. The morning loop ingests the top N from this queue. Cochrane reviewers do this manually with reference-chasing; this version is automated and converges the corpus toward closure under citation, which is the practical definition of completeness for a focused literature.
