## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class** — on methodology lock, provenance, and currency, the wiki already meets or exceeds Cochrane practice; the pooling math is deterministic, every claim is wikilinked to a source page, and ingest velocity is years ahead of a typical Cochrane review cycle. What keeps it from "Cochrane-class" is corpus completeness (941 sources is ~4% of the ~23,000-paper field implied by the triage manifest) and the absence of a per-source risk-of-bias instrument; the A/B/C tier collapses what Cochrane RoB 2 splits into five domains, and that compression is the obvious attack surface for a plaintiff's expert.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 941 ingested sources against a field that the priority-ordering plan itself sizes at ~23k papers. P5 tiers are largely unattempted, and `pool_state.BLOCKED: 286` says many (row, metal) cells cannot yet synthesize for lack of admissible evidence. Cochrane on a *narrow* question hits ~95% recall of the relevant literature via MEDLINE + EMBASE + CENTRAL + handsearch; EFSA scientific opinions hit ~4 by virtue of mandated multi-database protocols. HMI is wider in scope than either, but the breadth doesn't excuse the depth gap on individual (ingredient, metal) cells.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Every wiki claim links to a `wiki/sources/<cite-key>.md` page with stable cite-keys; `data/evidence/claim-provenance-audit.csv` and the lint pass enforce traceability; the routing audit prevents silent contamination across pages. Cochrane parity. The half-step missing is per-claim provenance granularity inside synthesis pages — currently the page links to contributing sources but doesn't always identify *which source* supports *which numeric value* in the `contamination_profile` block (e.g., rice U at n=4, confidence: medium — the four sources are recoverable via the routing audit but not pinned in the YAML).

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/standards-methodology.md` plus `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` lock P97 clean / P45 dirty with the 90/30 row-pass calibration rationale, native-basis convention, summary-source moment-matching expansion, and the regulatory-cap min. Pool admission is in code. This is at WHO-monograph register and better than FDA Closer to Zero (~3) for documented reasoning. The deduction: methodology is at v2.1 with active changelog — Cochrane convention is to *freeze* a protocol per review and re-issue under a new DOI on update. A hostile reviewer will note that the methodology document changed in the middle of synthesis runs (v2.0 → v2.1 in May 2026) without per-cell re-derivation.

**Replicability — 3/5.** The arithmetic is reproducible: routing audit, pool admission, P97/P45, and basis conversion are deterministic and live in `tools/evidence/`. But synthesis writing is LLM-mediated, and the audit-queue + GPT cross-audit is *adjudication*, not dual-independent extraction with kappa. Cochrane requires two reviewers extract independently, resolve disagreements, and report extraction agreement. HMI's `review_events.jsonl` captures *that* a synthesis happened, not the alternative extractions that would let a third party reconstruct the disagreement space. An external reviewer with the same `raw/` corpus and the same tools could re-derive pool values; they could not re-derive the prose summaries identically.

**Currency — 5/5.** 768 new source pages, 280 new product pages, 621 commits in seven days. The Cochrane Library updates reviews on a 2–7 year cadence; EFSA scientific opinions refresh on agency timelines (years); HMI updates on commit. Currency is the wiki's structurally strongest dimension and the one the autonomy loop most clearly enables. Caveat: velocity ≠ depth — many of the 280 new product pages are provisional scaffolds, not synthesized pages. But the underlying *plumbing* is at a cadence no Cochrane review approaches.

**Independence — 4/5.** Architecturally engineered: separate domains (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), separate repos planned, Part 2 firewall in CLAUDE.md, brand-firewall in Part 12, mandatory direction-of-drift naming in every synthesis commit. This is stronger than most regulatory monographs because the conflict is named and the lint catches silent threshold deviation. The half-point deduction is unavoidable: the same nonprofit operates both, and a plaintiff's expert will press on that affiliation regardless of how clean the architectural separation is. The only thing that would close it is a third-party editorial board or audit attestation on the wiki itself.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness)

**Vector 1 — Coverage-gap audit against external indices.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `corpus-coverage-gap.mjs` step that runs weekly: query OpenAlex (and PubMed for clinical, Web of Science for citation graph) using the analyte × matrix terms already present in `wiki/sources/` frontmatter, intersect with the existing `cite_key` set, and emit `data/evidence/corpus-coverage-gap.csv` ranked by citation count of un-ingested papers. The autonomy loop's existing `Manual Fetch` tracker can then prioritize from that file rather than from Karen's ad-hoc queueing. This directly converts "we have 941 sources" into "we have 941 of N, here are the top-K missing."

**Vector 2 — Per-source risk-of-bias frontmatter.** Extend the source-page schema (and the structured extractor) to capture an `rob:` block with the five Cochrane-style domains adapted for occurrence studies: sampling frame, analytical method validation (LOD/LOQ reported, certified reference material used), result reporting (censoring handled correctly, basis stated), funding/conflict, and selective reporting. Each domain gets `low | some_concerns | high | unclear`. The existing A/B/C tier becomes derived from the rob block via deterministic rule. Adds an extraction column to the structured extractor and a lint pass that flags `rob: unclear` cells on otherwise A-tier sources.

**Vector 3 — Pre-registered per-cell synthesis protocols.** At synthesis trigger time, emit `data/evidence/protocols/<ingredient>__<metal>__<date>.md` capturing: the inclusion query, the candidate source list at trigger time, the methodology version hash from `docs/standards-methodology.md`, the basis-conversion factors applied, and the planned pool admission decisions *before* the synthesis writes. The synthesis page then links to this frozen protocol artifact. Resynthesis at n_studies doubling emits a new protocol. This converts the v2.0 → v2.1 problem into "every cell was synthesized under a named, frozen, citable protocol" — which is the Cochrane convention HMI is currently missing.
