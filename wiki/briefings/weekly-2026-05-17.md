## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — closer than most regulatory references, not yet at parity.** The methodology layer (locked P97/P45 math, deterministic pooling in code, native-basis conversion, rationale-tagged thresholds, immutable `raw/`, append-only `review_events.jsonl`) is genuinely Cochrane-adjacent and in places stronger than EFSA's published opinions — that math is reproducible from a clean checkout. What blocks "Cochrane-class" is the absence of a documented systematic search protocol, the thin synthesis coverage (15 cells/week against 922 sources and 286 BLOCKED pools), and the fact that the corpus is large but not provably *complete*.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2.5 / 5.** This is the weakest dimension and the most exposed to hostile scrutiny. There are 922 source pages, but no PRISMA-style search protocol, no documented inclusion/exclusion criteria, no per-(ingredient × metal) search strings with hit counts, no exclusion log. A plaintiff's expert asking "what did you search for, and what did you reject" cannot be answered from current artifacts. 286 BLOCKED pools against 49 READY confirms the corpus is materially incomplete for the categories the program intends to certify. Cochrane: 5 (PRISMA-mandatory); EFSA: 4; FDA Closer to Zero: 3.

**Source provenance — 4 / 5.** Strong. Every claim is keyed to a `cite_key`, the routing audit forces frontmatter discipline at commit time, `claim-provenance-audit.csv` runs as lint, and `wiki/sources/` is the single chokepoint between `raw/` and any wiki claim. Drops a point because `review_events.jsonl` records cell-level `sources_cited` but not per-sample row contributions, so a single synthesis cell can't yet be decomposed back to the individual rows that fed its percentile. Cochrane: 5; EFSA: 4; WHO: 5.

**Methodology transparency — 4.5 / 5.** The strongest dimension and arguably best-in-class for this domain. `hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 publishes the 90/30 row-pass intent, the P97/P45 mechanism, the row-pass calibration math, the regulatory-cap rule, the four rationale tags, and the ratchet trigger conditions. Math lives in `tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs` and the pooler — not in prose. The gap from 5: `docs/standards-methodology.md` is 49 lines; the pool-admission rules, moment-matching expansion under log-normal/gamma, and weighting caps are stated but not yet worked-example-illustrated for an external auditor. Cochrane: 5; EFSA: 4-5; WHO monographs: 4-5.

**Replicability — 3.5 / 5.** Pooling math is deterministic; routing audit is regenerable; `npm run build` will fail on stale audit. But the upstream path — PDF → extracted sample rows → pool admission — still involves Codex adjudication with judgment calls (`ai_adjudication_queue.csv` exists), and there is no frozen "extraction protocol vN" artifact that two independent reviewers could apply to the same PDF and reach identical sample rows. The pooler is replicable; the extractor's outputs are not yet provably so. Cochrane: 4-5 (double-extraction is standard); EFSA: 4.

**Currency — 3.5 / 5.** Ingest is active (750 new source pages, 97 OpenAlex pulls in 7 days, 537 commits total) and `last_reviewed` is tracked per metal sub-block. But synthesis (15 cells/week) is running roughly 1/50th the velocity of ingest, so most of the corpus is captured-but-not-yet-synthesized. `last_full_resynthesis` triggers (Part 9) are defined but most ingredient pages have never crossed them. Currency for a Cochrane review means the synthesis reflects the latest literature; today the corpus reflects it, the synthesis layer lags. Cochrane: 4 (living-review track is 5); EFSA: 3-4.

**Independence — 4 / 5.** Structural separation is real and unusually well-thought-through: `heavymetalindex.com` vs `heavymetaltested.com` as separate properties, Part 2 firewall explicitly polices both directions of drift, Part 12 forbids brand pages, Part 19 keeps standards arithmetic out of `wiki/`. EU database-rights jurisdiction is deliberate. The point off: same operator runs both, no third-party editorial board, no published conflict-of-interest statement on the wiki itself, and the drift check in Part 2 is enforced by Claude sessions reading prose rules, not by lint that would catch a wiki edit that silently softens a claim to match an HMTc threshold. Cochrane: 5 (independent editorial groups); WHO: 4-5.

### 3. Improvement vectors — all targeting corpus completeness

**(a) Build a PRISMA-style search registry.** Add `tools/corpus/build-search-registry.mjs` that, for each (ingredient × metal) cell, records the OpenAlex / PubMed / EFSA-register / FDA-CFSAN search strings executed, the dates run, the raw hit count, the screened-in count, and the screened-out count. Output: `data/evidence/corpus-search-log.csv` (one row per search execution) and `data/evidence/corpus-search-strategy.md` (the protocol document). Pre-register the protocol by committing it before the next bulk ingest wave. This is what brand legal will demand verbatim — "show me your search strategy" is the first discovery request.

**(b) Implement a coverage-gap auditor with auto-queued pulls.** Add `tools/audit-corpus-coverage.mjs` that walks every (ingredient × metal) cell at BLOCKED, reads the routing audit, and emits a structured backfill plan: required N to reach READY, the deterministic OpenAlex query that would close the gap, expected hit count, and the priority tier from Part 11. Pipe the output into the existing autonomy loop so BLOCKED pools become an actionable queue, not a backlog. This converts the 286 BLOCKED counter from a passive metric into a falsifiable "we searched, here's what existed, here's what we ingested" claim — the exact shape Cochrane requires.

**(c) Build an exclusion log.** Add `tools/corpus/build-exclusion-log.mjs` that scans `raw/`, OpenAlex pull manifests, and the screened-out side of the search registry, and emits `data/evidence/corpus-exclusions.csv`: one row per excluded item with rationale code (off-topic, brand-only-data, non-quantitative, no-extractable-sample-rows, untranslated-foreign-language, retracted, preprint-superseded-by-published, duplicate-of-<cite_key>). Without this, "we have 922 sources" is asymmetric — the wiki can prove what it included but not what it screened out and why. PRISMA mandates the exclusion side; right now hostile counsel can argue selection bias because the exclusion record does not exist.
