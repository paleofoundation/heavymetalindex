## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — structurally, not yet evidentially.** The infrastructure (per-claim provenance, locked pooling methodology, immutable source corpus, routing audit, deterministic synthesis triggers) is built to a standard Cochrane would recognize, but the wiki currently holds ~4% of its stated target corpus (936 of ~23,000 papers) with only 15 synthesis cells written and 286 pools BLOCKED. A hostile reviewer can credibly argue the methodology is sound but the evidentiary base is too thin to back the "complete literature" claim on which strategic defensibility rests.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 936 sources against a 23,000-paper target with no published search-strategy registry, no PRISMA-format identification/screening/inclusion record, and no per-database query log. Pull-rate is high (97 OpenAlex PDFs/week, 765 source pages/week) but there is no artifact that lets a reviewer say "this is what was searched and why nothing was missed." Cochrane baseline: 4-5. EFSA opinions: ~4 with documented search strings. This is the wiki's structurally weakest dimension and the most direct attack surface.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Per-claim provenance is enforced at the lint layer; every numerical claim must link to a source page or the build fails. Cite-keys, evidence tiers (A/B/C), and routing audits make traceability mechanical rather than aspirational. Gap: most pages today are source pages and stubs; the provenance discipline has not yet been stress-tested across many synthesis cells (n=15). Cochrane baseline: 5. This rating climbs as synthesis volume grows.

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** The pooling math is locked, deterministic, in code, and reproducible (P97 clean / P45 dirty, native-basis conversion, regulatory-cap rule, four rationale tags). Synthesis triggers are codified. *But:* there is no per-study risk-of-bias instrument (no ROBINS-E or equivalent), no GRADE certainty rating derived from auditable inputs, and the `confidence: low/medium/high` field is currently an opaque single value rather than a derivation. Cochrane baseline: 5. WHO monographs: 4-5. The pooling layer is at parity; the per-study assessment layer is missing.

**Replicability — 4/5.** Source pages are immutable; pooling math is code; the routing audit regenerates deterministically from frontmatter; git history reconstructs any page on any date. An external reviewer with corpus access can re-derive every synthesis cell. The 1-point deduction: corpus access is intentionally gated (EU database rights), so external replicability is bounded by license — a feature, but it limits the strongest form of the replicability claim.

**Currency — 3/5.** Ingest velocity is exceptional (~110 source pages/day this week). Synthesis is not keeping pace: 15 cells written against 765 new sources means the corpus layer is current but the synthesis layer is increasingly stale. Living-review trigger conditions exist but are firing rarely. A reviewer asking "what does the wiki currently say about cadmium in rice?" gets a more current answer than they would have a month ago; a reviewer asking "what does the wiki currently *synthesize* about cadmium in rice?" largely gets pending stubs.

**Independence — 3/5.** The architectural firewall is explicit and well-designed: separate domains, two-direction drift checks in Part 2, no HMTc-certified brands in the wiki, no standards arithmetic in `wiki/`. The lint pass catches silent threshold deviation. The 2-point gap from Cochrane: the wiki is operated by the same legal entity that operates the certification program it backstops. Cochrane has formal editorial review boards independent of any commercial entity. The Paleo Foundation's structural separation is a strong substitute but it is a substitute, not the original.

### 3. Improvement vectors

All three target the weakest dimension (corpus completeness, 2/5), since lifting that to 4 raises the overall verdict more than improving any other dimension.

**1. Build a PRISMA-grade search-strategy registry.** Add `data/evidence/search-strategies.jsonl` with one record per database query: `{database, query_string, filters, date_run, n_returned, n_deduplicated, n_screened, n_excluded_with_reasons[], n_full_text_assessed, n_included, operator}`. Extend the OpenAlex puller (`tools/autonomy/openalex-pull.mjs` or equivalent) to emit a record per query rather than only the PDFs. Add PubMed and CENTRAL pullers with the same emission contract. This converts the current high-velocity but undocumented ingest into a systematic search whose corpus boundaries are inspectable.

**2. Generate a PRISMA flow diagram from the search registry plus routing audit.** Add `tools/evidence/build-prisma-flow.mjs` to read `search-strategies.jsonl` and the routing audit, then emit `wiki/methodology/prisma-flow-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` (and a Mermaid diagram) covering identification, deduplication, screening, full-text assessment, inclusion, and exclusion-with-reason. Run it on the prebuild trigger. This produces the single artifact a brand legal team or plaintiff expert will ask for first when they audit corpus completeness.

**3. Add a per-cell saturation tracker driving completeness-by-question.** Extend `tools/autonomy/audit-pending-locks.mjs` (or add `audit-cell-saturation.mjs`) to compute, per (ingredient × metal) cell, the rate at which the most recent k=20 incoming sources introduced novel concentration values outside the existing P5–P95 envelope versus confirmed within-envelope. When the novel-value rate drops below 5% with N≥30 sources, mark the cell `saturation: reached` and emit a `corpus_completeness_score` to the cell's frontmatter. This operationalizes "complete literature" as a per-question measurable rather than a corpus-wide aspirational claim, and gives every synthesis cell an auditable completeness statement attached to it.
