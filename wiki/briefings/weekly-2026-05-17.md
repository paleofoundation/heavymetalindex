## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class — closer on architecture than on corpus.** The provenance discipline, wiki/HMTc independence firewall, and version-controlled deterministic standards math are at or near Cochrane's institutional standard. The corpus is the dispositive weakness: 922 sources and 286/347 BLOCKED pool cells (~82%) mean most of the index can't yet produce thresholds from a literature-saturated base, which is exactly the position a plaintiff's expert would attack.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** A Cochrane heavy-metals review on infant formula alone would typically screen 5,000–15,000 records to include 50–200. This index is 922 sources across 36 metals × 321 products × 237 ingredients, and the pool state (286 BLOCKED, 48 READY, 13 PROVISIONAL) is the honest signal: in most cells the evidence base is not yet saturated. The 97 OpenAlex pulls last week show active corpus-completion work, but no per-cell PRISMA-style search-string protocol exists, and the gap between "what we have" and "what's published" is not currently quantified. Below EFSA's ~4; below the Cochrane baseline.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Per-claim provenance is structurally enforced: every numerical claim must trace to a source page (Part 18 lint failure if not), every source carries a `cite_key`, and the routing audit deterministically maps source → target pages. This matches Cochrane's expectation. Not 5/5 because external reviewers can't yet replay the provenance chain without internal tooling; the chain is auditable in-repo but not externally renderable.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 spells out the P97/P45 mechanism, the 90/30 row-pass intent, the native-basis convention, the pool-admission rule (moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion with WHO/JECFA + EFSA precedent), and the regulatory cap. Rationale tags (`literature-baseline`, `regulatory-alignment`, `precautionary`, `feasibility-driven`) are required and enforced. This is at WHO-monograph level. Not 5/5 because the pseudo-sample expansion code is not externally peer-reviewed and the methodology is not pre-registered on a public protocol registry (PROSPERO equivalent).

**Replicability — 3/5.** The standards math lives in `tools/evidence/` and is deterministic; the pooling engine, basis conversion, and routing audit are all reproducible from inputs. However: (a) extractor decisions are LLM-mediated and the audit trail of *why* a given value was extracted vs. excluded is not yet a first-class artifact per cell; (b) raw PDFs are copyright-bound, so an external reviewer can't rebuild from scratch without independent corpus acquisition; (c) the pool-admission disposition log isn't exposed per (row, metal) cell. Above FDA Closer to Zero (~3); below Cochrane (4–5).

**Currency — 3/5.** Git is the source of truth, commits are dense (568 in the visible window), and ingest velocity is high (751 new source pages last week). But synthesis is the bottleneck: 15 cells written, 8 pages touched, zero upgrades from existing confidence tiers (every synthesis event was `null → {low,medium,high}`, no `low → medium` or higher). The cell-state distribution (286 BLOCKED) confounds two distinct conditions — "evidence insufficient" vs. "evidence sufficient but synthesis stale" — and the index can't currently distinguish them at audit time. Cochrane reviews freeze at publication, so the wiki's *theoretical* currency advantage is real, but the *realized* currency is throttled by synthesis throughput.

**Independence — 5/5.** Architecturally enforced. Part 2 of CLAUDE.md, the separate domains (heavymetalindex.com vs. heavymetaltested.com), the staff Standards Workbench living outside `wiki/`, the brand-firewall rule (no `wiki/brands/`, no certified-brand mentions), and the mandatory rationale tag when a threshold deviates from literature-baseline together make the gap between literature and certification *visible by design*. This is the strongest dimension and exceeds what most regulatory bodies achieve, because their evidence base and their enforcement body are institutionally fused. Matches Cochrane.

### 3. Improvement vectors

The weakest dimension is **corpus completeness (2/5)**, with replicability close behind. All three vectors target the 286 BLOCKED-cell problem from different angles.

**Vector A — Build `tools/autonomy/blocked-cell-unblock-queue.mjs`.** For each BLOCKED (row, metal) cell, emit a targeted OpenAlex + PubMed search-string set derived from the cell's matrix slug, metal symbol, and adjacent regulatory vocabulary. Rank queries by expected yield (matrix specificity × metal toxicological prominence × jurisdictional coverage). Output a fetch queue consumable by the existing manual-fetch workflow. This converts "we don't have enough evidence" into "here is the specific literature we have not yet pulled," which is the move from snapshot to corpus-completion protocol — and produces the PRISMA-equivalent input log that hostile review will demand.

**Vector B — Emit per-cell PRISMA artifacts at `data/workbench/prisma/<row>__<metal>.md`.** Consume the routing audit + pool admission log + extractor decisions to produce, per cell: records identified, records screened, records included in pool, records excluded with reason (off-basis-non-convertible, off-matrix, off-population, off-jurisdiction, methodology-disqualifying). Today the pool-admission rule is "every source unless dispositioned out with a stated reason," but the dispositions aren't surfaced as a per-cell auditable artifact. This is the single most impactful replicability move: it turns the synthesis from "trust the engine" into "audit the inclusion chain end-to-end."

**Vector C — Build `tools/autonomy/synthesis-debt-audit.mjs`.** For each cell with `pool_state: READY` and either no `last_full_resynthesis` or one older than the most recently added contributing source, queue a synthesis pass. Emit a debt-ranked queue (READY cells with most stale state surface first). This decouples synthesis throughput from ingest throughput, makes the "evidence sufficient but synthesis stale" condition visible separately from "evidence insufficient," and lets the autonomy loop work synthesis debt down in parallel with ingest — which is the actual bottleneck behind the 15-cells/week synthesis output against a 922-source corpus.
