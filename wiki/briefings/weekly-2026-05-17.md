## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class** on infrastructure, **not yet Cochrane-class** on output. The provenance architecture (cite-key → raw PDF → routing audit → review-events log), the locked deterministic standards math, and the active currency (577 commits / 754 new sources in 7 days) match or exceed Cochrane on those specific axes; but synthesis throughput is the bottleneck (15 cells written this week against 286 BLOCKED pool cells), and several Cochrane-defining artifacts — pre-registered search protocol, PRISMA flow per question, per-source risk-of-bias scoring, dual-reviewer screening, published reproducibility package — do not yet exist in the repo.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 3/5.** Strength: 925 sources, an active OpenAlex pull (97 last week), a `gap-driven-wishlist.mjs` tool, and a triage manifest. Weakness: there is no per-(metal, ingredient, product-row) **pre-registered search protocol** documenting databases queried, search strings, language coverage, date cutoffs, or inclusion/exclusion criteria. 286 BLOCKED pool cells is the visible signature — the search isn't yet exhaustive against the row inventory it is supposed to cover. Comparable to a mid-quality EFSA opinion (~3.5–4) but below Cochrane (4–5), which mandates documented multi-database searches with reference-chasing.

**Source provenance — 5/5.** Every source page carries cite_key, DOI, raw_path, evidence_tier, jurisdiction, and N. Raw PDFs are immutable. The wiki/source link discipline is enforced by `audit-claim-provenance.mjs` and Part 18 lint ("claims without provenance are a lint failure"). Routing audit makes the source→target fan-out reproducible. This is at-or-above Cochrane; Cochrane reviews routinely fail audit on this exact axis (claims that don't trace cleanly back to extracted data).

**Methodology transparency — 3/5.** Strength: standards math is locked in `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` v2.1 with a changelog, the P97/P45 rationale traces explicitly to the 90/30 row-pass policy choice, and pool admission, basis conversion, and N-capping are all specified. Weakness: there is no **published synthesis protocol** analogous to a Cochrane review's Methods chapter — no documented inclusion/exclusion screening, no dual-screening, no risk-of-bias instrument applied per source (the A/B/C tier is closer to GRADE-of-evidence than to ROBINS-I or Cochrane RoB 2), and no a-priori statistical-heterogeneity plan. Closer to FDA Closer to Zero (~3) than to a WHO monograph (~4–5).

**Replicability — 3/5.** The pooling math is in code (`tools/standards/`, `tools/evidence/`), deterministic, and version-controlled — that's the Cochrane-class part. But an external reviewer (the brand-legal use case) cannot today (a) clone a published replication package, (b) re-run the synthesis on a public corpus snapshot, or (c) reproduce a specific contamination_profile value from a specific commit hash without internal repo access. Source-level extracted values exist as prose in source pages but are not yet uniformly machine-readable per (matrix, metal, statistic) row — the 15-cells-this-week figure is partly a symptom of that. Cochrane publishes the data extraction tables as supplementary material; this wiki does not yet.

**Currency — 5/5.** 754 new source pages, 577 commits, daily autonomy loop, file-watcher-triggered routing rebuild, weekly briefing, gap-driven wishlist. Cochrane updates on a 2-year cadence at best and frequently goes stale between updates. This dimension is genuinely **beyond Cochrane-class** and is the strongest structural advantage the wiki has against hostile-expert challenge ("your cited Cochrane review is from 2021; ours reflects literature through last week").

**Independence — 3/5.** Architectural separation (heavymetalindex.com vs heavymetaltested.com), the Part 2 firewall, the bidirectional drift lint, and the rationale-tag discipline (`literature-baseline` / `regulatory-alignment` / `precautionary` / `feasibility-driven`) are all present and load-bearing. But the Paleo Foundation operates both properties, and a plaintiff's expert will press exactly there: "you wrote the literature review *and* you set the certification limits." The defenses are real but they are policy-and-lint, not structural-independence-of-personnel-and-funding the way Cochrane reviewers are unrelated to the interventions they review. Cochrane: 5. This wiki: 3, defensible but contestable.

### 3. Improvement vectors

Weakest dimension is a near-tie between **corpus completeness** and **replicability**. I'm targeting **replicability** because it compounds: a published reproducibility package strengthens corpus-completeness defense (the search log is public) and independence defense (the math is auditable) simultaneously.

**Vector A — Add a per-source structured extraction table the synthesis loop reads from, not source-page prose.** Extend `tools/autonomy/extract-source-values.mjs` to emit one row per (source, matrix, metal, statistic_type, basis, n, value, censoring, jurisdiction, year) into `data/evidence/extracted_values.jsonl`, with the source-page prose generated *from* that table, not the other way around. Today synthesis re-reads prose; tomorrow it joins on a structured table. This is the single highest-leverage change for both replicability and synthesis throughput — it directly attacks the 286 BLOCKED cells.

**Vector B — Publish a `reproducibility/` directory on every release tag containing (1) a frozen corpus manifest (cite_key, DOI, sha256 of raw PDF), (2) the extracted_values.jsonl snapshot, (3) the standards-workbench output with rationale tags, and (4) the exact tool versions used.** Build step: add `tools/reproducibility/freeze-release.mjs` that runs on `npm run release` and writes `reproducibility/<release-tag>/` checked into git. An external reviewer can then run `node tools/standards/recompute.mjs --from reproducibility/2026-Q2/` and either reproduce or falsify any published HMTc value. This is the artifact that converts "trust us" into "verify it."

**Vector C — Add a pre-registered search protocol per HMTc row.** Build `tools/autonomy/search-protocol.mjs` that, for each locked HMTc row, emits a `data/evidence/search-protocols/<row>.md` containing: databases queried (OpenAlex, PubMed, FDA TDS, EFSA Zenodo), search strings (matrix-and-metal product), date window, inclusion/exclusion criteria, language policy, and a hit-count log. Lint that synthesis cannot promote a cell out of `provisional` until its row has a published protocol older than the most recent ingest into that cell. This directly addresses the corpus-completeness gap and gives the plaintiff's-expert-defense a one-page document per row.
