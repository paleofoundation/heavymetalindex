## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** Methodology transparency, provenance discipline, and replicability are at or above Cochrane's baseline because the pooling math is locked in code, every claim is schema-enforced to trace to a source page, and 514 commits in the last week mean an external reviewer can re-derive any wiki state from git. Corpus completeness and editorial independence are the live drags: 922 source pages against a target literature of roughly 23,000 papers, 318 PDFs sitting unprocessed in `raw/`, and "independence from HMTc" still asserted by architectural firewall rather than by an external editorial board — a hostile reviewer can name both gaps without misrepresenting the artifact.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** 922 ingested sources, plus 318 PDFs in `raw/studies` and `raw/reports` with `no_source_page` status, plus 286 BLOCKED pools waiting on additional admissible evidence. CLAUDE.md's own target is ~23,000 papers, so present coverage is ~4%. EFSA's Pb opinion alone synthesized ~300 primary studies; Cochrane reviews of comparable scope routinely exceed 500. There is no pre-registered PRISMA-style search strategy documenting databases queried, date ranges, exclusion criteria, or de-duplication logic, so "completeness" cannot currently be defended — it can only be described.

**Source provenance — 5/5.** `claim-provenance-summary.json` reports 3,840 / 3,840 claims supported, enforced by the generator pipeline rather than by hand review. Per-claim source pages, `cite_key` round-tripping, `values.jsonl` and `claims.jsonl` schemas, and the routing audit collectively make orphan claims a build-blocking lint failure. This exceeds Cochrane's typical practice, where provenance is reviewer-asserted at PRISMA-flow granularity, not row-by-row machine-verified.

**Methodology transparency — 5/5.** `docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md` declares the five governing principles and the locked P97/P45 math with row-pass calibration rationale; `docs/standards-methodology.md` carries pool-admission rules; eight JSON schemas in `data/evidence/schema/` constrain extraction; the version table at the bottom of the governing principles file documents the v1.0 → v2.1 evolution. Cochrane methods sections are typically prose; this is prose plus code plus changelog. EFSA scientific-opinion baseline is met or exceeded.

**Replicability — 5/5.** Pooling is deterministic and lives in `tools/evidence/`; basis conversion is in `basis-conversion.mjs`; the routing audit is regenerated on file-watcher, pre-commit, and prebuild triggers; 1,089 events in `review_events.jsonl` record synthesis history. An external reviewer with the repo and Node can re-derive any cell. This is the strongest dimension and is genuinely beyond Cochrane, which is reproducible-in-principle but rarely reproducible-by-checkout.

**Currency — 3/5.** Re-synthesis triggers are specified (doubling rule, contradiction trigger, A-tier contradiction arbitration) and `last_full_resynthesis` is tracked per cell, so currency is at least mechanized. But the last-7-day synthesis output is 15 cells written against 750 new source pages ingested — synthesis is running ~50× slower than ingest, so the working corpus is drifting away from the synthesized corpus week over week. 286 BLOCKED pools means most (row, metal) cells are formally stale-by-default. Cochrane reviews are typically refreshed every 2–5 years on a defined cadence; here the cadence exists but is under-resourced.

**Independence — 3/5.** Part 2 of CLAUDE.md is the strongest declared firewall I have seen in a commercial-adjacent reference work, and the heavymetalindex.com / heavymetaltested.com architectural split, plus the rule that the wiki must never reference HMTc-certified brands, gives the assertion teeth. But the same foundation operates both. A plaintiff's expert will note the absence of (a) an external editorial board, (b) declared conflict-of-interest policy for contributors, and (c) a public corrections log distinct from git history. Cochrane scores ~5 here because review groups are formally separated from clinical-product manufacturers; this wiki scores 3 because separation is internal and self-policed.

### 3. Improvement vectors (target: corpus completeness)

**Vector A — Pre-register the search strategy as a build artifact.** Extend `tools/evidence/` with a `search-strategy-register.mjs` that emits `data/evidence/search-strategy.json`: one row per (ingredient × metal) and (product-category × metal) pair containing the OpenAlex / PubMed / EFSA / FDA query strings used, the date-range filters, the inclusion/exclusion logic, and the resolved hit count. Make it a build prerequisite for any new ingredient or product page promoted out of `provisional_scaffold`. This converts corpus completeness from "trust us" to "here is the PRISMA flow diagram, generated nightly." It is the single highest-leverage move against the hostile-expert attack line because it makes the unindexed-literature gap visible and bounded rather than unknown.

**Vector B — Burn down the 318-PDF `no_source_page` backlog under a queued autonomy loop.** Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `process-raw-backlog.mjs` runner that reads `data/evidence/raw_ingest_inventory.csv`, filters to `no_source_page` rows ranked by `by_ingest_priority` (P0 → P1 → P2), and feeds them through `ingest-next-manual-fetch-pdf` in series. At current OpenAlex pull rate (97/week) plus a parallel backlog drain (~40/week sustainable), the corpus reaches 2,000 source pages in 12 weeks and 5,000 in 9 months. Emit a weekly burndown chart into the morning report so the gap is monitored, not estimated.

**Vector C — Raise OpenAlex recall to systematic-review level and log the gap explicitly.** Extend the existing OpenAlex fetcher to (1) issue per-(ingredient × metal) saturating queries instead of the current opportunistic mode, (2) record `expected_hits`, `fetched_hits`, and `gap_hits` per cell, and (3) write any cell where `fetched_hits / expected_hits < 0.7` to a new `data/evidence/corpus-coverage-gaps.csv`. Make this CSV's non-emptiness a `--warn-only` lint check now and a build-blocking check after Phase 1 stabilizes. This is the artifact that turns "we have 922 papers" into "we have 922 of an estimated 4,100 currently in OpenAlex for these query terms, with the gap enumerated cell-by-cell" — which is the answer a Cochrane editor expects, and the answer that survives a deposition.
