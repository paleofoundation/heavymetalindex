## Cochrane comparison

### 1. Verdict

**Approaching Cochrane-class.** The methodology layer (HMTc governing principles, P97/P45 row-pass calibration, pool admission rules grounded in WHO/JECFA/EFSA convention, the wiki/HMTc firewall in Part 2, deterministic routing audit) is genuinely peer-comparable to a Cochrane methods section, and the per-source frontmatter discipline (DOI, evidence tier, raw_path, sample_n, basis) is stronger than most regulatory monographs. What blocks the "Cochrane-class" label is corpus completeness — 934 of an estimated ~23,000 relevant papers (~4%), with no PRISMA-style registered search protocol — and synthesis throughput that lags ingest roughly 50:1, leaving 286 BLOCKED pool cells against only 48 READY.

### 2. Dimensional grading

**Corpus completeness — 2/5.** Cochrane baseline: 4–5 (registered search across MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, grey literature, hand-search; PRISMA flow). EFSA: ~4. This wiki: 934 sources against a target population of tens of thousands; OpenAlex pulls (97 this week) and manual fetch are opportunistic; no per-(ingredient, metal) search registry documenting what was searched, what was screened, what was excluded and why. The corpus is growing fast but is not currently *defensible as comprehensive* for any single cell.

**Source provenance — 4/5.** Cochrane baseline: 4–5. Every source page has a stable cite_key, DOI, raw_path back to the immutable `raw/` markdown, evidence_tier, and structured frontmatter that the routing audit fans out deterministically. The half-point deduction: claim-level provenance (the specific paragraph or table cell a wiki number derives from) is not yet enforced — `audit-claim-provenance.mjs` exists but isn't a build-blocking gate. A plaintiff's expert who asks "where exactly does the 230 ppb figure come from" gets a source page, not a line citation.

**Methodology transparency — 4/5.** Cochrane: 5. WHO monographs: 4–5. The locked standards math (P97/P45, native basis, regulatory-cap rule, rationale tagging vocabulary) is explicit, versioned (v2.1, May 2026), and reproducible from `tools/standards/`. The wiki/HMTc drift check (Part 2) is a methodological commitment most certification programs don't even articulate. Weakness: there is no per-cell pre-registered synthesis protocol analogous to PROSPERO. Synthesis triggers (Part 9: 2nd source, n_studies doubles, 2× disagreement) are documented but novel and not externally validated.

**Replicability — 3/5.** Cochrane: 4–5 (two reviewers, kappa statistics, third-reviewer adjudication). The ingest → routing → pooling → standards path is deterministic code, which is *better* than Cochrane on the arithmetic side. But evidence-tier assignment, contamination_profile values, and synthesis adjudication are made by a single AI pass without an inter-rater reliability check. An external reviewer running the pipeline gets the same percentile arithmetic but cannot verify the upstream tier and inclusion judgments converged.

**Currency — 3/5.** Cochrane: 4 (formal update cycles). The wiki ingested 762 sources in 7 days — exceptional velocity — but synthesis wrote only 15 cells across 8 unique pages, and BLOCKED dominates pool state (286 vs. 48 READY, 13 PROVISIONAL). The corpus is current; the *synthesized view* of the corpus is not. Most ingredient `contamination_profile` sub-blocks remain `pending`, which means the public-facing claim surface lags the evidence base.

**Independence — 3/5.** Cochrane: 5 (independent editorial groups, declared CoI per author). The structural firewall (separate domains, Part 2 drift check, standards math excluded from `wiki/`, brand firewall in Part 12) is well-designed and unusual in this market. But governance sits inside one organization (Paleo Foundation), there is no external editorial board, no published conflict-of-interest disclosures, and no independent annual review of whether the firewall held. A hostile expert will argue "self-policed firewall" — and currently that is structurally accurate.

### 3. Improvement vectors

**Vector A — Pre-registered search protocol per (ingredient, metal) cell (raises Corpus completeness from 2 → 3).** Extend `tools/orchestrator/` to emit, before any ingest for a given cell, a `data/evidence/search-registries/<ingredient>-<metal>.jsonl` containing the OpenAlex/PubMed/EFSA/FDA query strings, date range, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and a PRISMA-style accounting of identified → screened → eligible → included → excluded-with-reason counts. The autonomy loop runs the searches, populates the registry, and only then proceeds to ingest. This converts "we ingested what arrived" into "we registered the search and report yield vs. exclusion." A plaintiff's expert can then attack the search strings, not the existence of a method.

**Vector B — Two-pass synthesis adjudication with disagreement logging (raises Replicability from 3 → 4).** Extend the synthesis trigger in Part 9 so that every cell promoted out of `pending` runs two independent adjudication passes — Claude Code and Codex, per Karen's existing multi-model setup — on the same source set, each emitting a structured verdict (tier per source, typical_ppb, p95_ppb, n_studies). A new `tools/audit-synthesis-irr.mjs` computes simple agreement (Cohen's kappa for tier assignment; ratio bounds for numeric values), writes disagreements to `data/evidence/synthesis-disagreements.jsonl`, and blocks promotion of any cell where the two passes disagree on tier or differ >25% on numeric headlines. Karen reviews the disagreement log, not every cell. This produces the inter-rater reliability evidence Cochrane requires.

**Vector C — BLOCKED-pool resolver workflow (raises Currency from 3 → 4).** The 286 BLOCKED cells are currently a status, not a workflow. Extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `resolve-blocked-pool.mjs` pass that classifies each BLOCKED cell by *why* it is blocked (missing analyte coverage, basis-conversion gap, all-sub-LOD pool, single-source pool, regulatory-cap missing) and routes each class to its remediation: targeted OpenAlex queries for missing-analyte gaps, automatic basis conversion via `tools/evidence/basis-conversion.mjs` for unit mismatches, escalation to manual-fetch for single-source cells with the specific search terms attached, and a regulation-page sweep for missing caps. Emit a weekly `data/evidence/blocked-pool-burndown.md` so the gap between READY (48) and BLOCKED (286) becomes a tracked metric, not an opaque pool-state number.
