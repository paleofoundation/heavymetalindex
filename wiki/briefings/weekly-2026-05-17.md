### 3. Improvement vectors (target the weakest: replicability + corpus completeness)

**(a) Build per-cell PRISMA equivalents via the ingest pipeline.**
Extend the autonomy loop to emit `data/evidence/search-strategy/<ingredient>--<metal>.md` for every (ingredient, metal) pair the synthesis engine touches, containing: OpenAlex query string used, date range, total hits, dedup count, screening exclusions with rationale taxonomy (out-of-matrix, wrong analyte, no quantitative data, non-peer-reviewed, etc.), and final included-source list. This is the artifact that converts "we have 921 sources" into "here is the search strategy by which we identified the relevant subset for cell X" — the document a plaintiff's expert would otherwise force you to produce on the stand. Build step: extend `tools/autonomy/` with a `synthesize-search-strategy.mjs` invoked at every full-resynthesis trigger.

**(b) Unblock the 286 BLOCKED pools via summary-source admission.**
The governing principles §"Pool admission and basis" already authorizes moment-matching pseudo-sample expansion under log-normal/gamma for summary-only sources (mean/median/max/SD/IQR/lb-ub), citing WHO/JECFA TDS methodology. The math is specified but not yet operationalized for the BLOCKED rows. Build step: `tools/evidence/pool-admit-summary.mjs` that detects summary-only source rows, performs moment-matching expansion to N pseudo-samples (weighted by reported N, capped at 2× median per source), logs the conversion per source, and feeds the pooling engine. Target: move >150 of 286 BLOCKED pools to READY in one bulk pass, raising replicability from 2 to ~3.5.

**(c) Convert synthesis from event-triggered to queue-throughput.**
Synthesis is the bottleneck — 750 sources ingested vs 15 cells synthesized weekly. Replace the per-cell trigger logic with a daemon: `tools/autonomy/synthesis-queue-daemon.mjs` reads the routing audit, queues every (ingredient, metal) cell where contributing-source count has grown since `last_reviewed`, processes in priority order (cell cite-count × HMTc category importance × time-since-last-review), and writes ≥100 cells/week to `data/evidence/review_events.jsonl`.
