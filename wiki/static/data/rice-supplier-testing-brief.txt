SELECTED DATED REQUIREMENTS
Units: ug/kg means micrograms per kilogram.
EU consolidation: 19 August 2026. Values are ug/kg; no moisture or cooking conversion. This is not an exhaustive compliance checklist.

EU - Rice - cadmium: 150 ug/kg (0.15 mg/kg). Annex I 3.2.12.3.

EU - Non-parboiled white rice - inorganic arsenic: 150 ug/kg (0.15 mg/kg). Annex I 3.4.1.1.

EU - Parboiled or husked rice; rice flour - inorganic arsenic: 250 ug/kg (0.25 mg/kg). Separate entries: Annex I 3.4.1.2 and 3.4.1.3.

EU - Rice destined for infant and young-child foods - inorganic arsenic: 100 ug/kg (0.10 mg/kg). Ingredient scope; Annex I 3.4.1.5.

EU - Non-alcoholic rice-based drinks - inorganic arsenic: 30 ug/kg (0.030 mg/kg). Drink scope; Annex I 3.4.1.6.

EU - Rice other than husked rice - nickel: 1,500 ug/kg (1.5 mg/kg). Applies from 1 July 2026; Annex I 3.6.11.2.

EU - Husked rice - nickel: 2,000 ug/kg (2.0 mg/kg). Applies from 1 July 2026; Annex I 3.6.11.3.

US - Infant rice cereal - inorganic arsenic: 100 ug/kg (100 ppb). Final nonbinding guidance action level for infant rice cereal; FDA considers other factors in enforcement.

EU default basis: edible food as placed on the market unless specified otherwise. Nickel transition: food lawfully marketed before 1 July 2026 may remain until its minimum-durability/use-by date under Article 10. Justify applicable processing factors under Article 3. The FDA infant-cereal action level is not a universal raw-rice limit.
