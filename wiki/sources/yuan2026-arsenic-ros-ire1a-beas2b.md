# Yuan et al. 2026: ROS/IRE1alpha pathway in arsenic-induced apoptosis and autophagy in BEAS-2B cells

This in vitro mechanistic study investigated the role of the reactive oxygen species (ROS)/IRE1alpha signaling pathway in arsenic trioxide (As2O3)-induced apoptosis and autophagy in BEAS-2B human bronchial epithelial cells. The study aimed to elucidate the cytotoxicity and cellular mechanisms by which inorganic arsenic damages lung cells, with ROS identified as the primary upstream mediator.

## Key numbers

- As2O3 at 0.25-0.75 µM did not significantly alter BEAS-2B cell viability over 24 hours.
- As2O3 at 1.0-2.0 µM significantly reduced cell viability in a dose-dependent manner (CCK-8 assay), p less than 0.01.
- LDH release (necrosis/late apoptosis marker) was significantly elevated at 1.0 and 2.0 µM As2O3.
- Cleaved caspase-3 protein (apoptosis executioner) was upregulated at 2.0 µM As2O3 versus control, p less than 0.01.
- ROS levels were markedly elevated at 1.0 and 2.0 µM As2O3 versus control (fluorimetry and fluorescence microscopy).
- GRP78 (ER chaperone/UPR marker), p-IRE1alpha, and p-eIF2alpha were significantly increased at 0.5 and 2.0 µM As2O3 versus control, p less than 0.01.
- N-acetylcysteine (NAC, antioxidant) pretreatment (5.0 mM, 30 min) suppressed GRP78, p-IRE1alpha, and p-eIF2alpha upregulation and reduced As2O3-induced cleaved caspase-3, confirming ROS as the upstream effector.
- LC3B-II/beta-actin ratio (autophagosome marker) was elevated at 0.5 and 2.0 µM As2O3; NAC administration markedly reduced LC3B-II levels, confirming ROS mediation of autophagy.

## Methods (brief)

BEAS-2B cells (human bronchial epithelial line) exposed to As2O3 at 0.25-2.0 µM for 24 hours. Cell viability: CCK-8 assay. LDH: fluorimetric. Apoptosis nuclear morphology: Hoechst 33342 staining. Western blot: cleaved caspase-3, GRP78, p-IRE1alpha, p-eIF2alpha, LC3B-II (all normalized to beta-actin); n = 3 independent biological replicates. ROS: DCFH-DA dye (fluorimetry and fluorescence microscopy). Autophagy vacuoles: MDC fluorescent marker. NAC pretreatment as antioxidant control.

## Evidence fitness

Supports: mechanistic understanding of how inorganic arsenic damages bronchial epithelial cells through oxidative stress and endoplasmic reticulum stress pathways (ROS-IRE1alpha-apoptosis/autophagy axis). Provides cellular-level evidence relevant to arsenic-induced lung injury. Limitation: in vitro only; As2O3 concentrations used (0.25-2.0 µM) are higher than typical environmental inhalation exposures; BEAS-2B is an immortalized cell line, not primary cells.

## Limitations

In vitro only; no animal or human data; As2O3 concentrations may not reflect environmental or dietary inhalation doses; single cell type; BEAS-2B cells are SV40-transformed, which affects apoptotic thresholds relative to primary bronchial epithelial cells.
