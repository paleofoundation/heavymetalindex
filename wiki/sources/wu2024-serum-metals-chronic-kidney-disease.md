# Wu et al. 2024: Serum metals and chronic kidney disease

This study measured 28 serum metals by ICP-MS in 443 chronic kidney disease patients and 100 people attending health checkups. Serum gallium was mostly below detection in both groups.

## Key numbers

- Serum gallium, median (IQR): 0 (0, 0) in the comparison group and 0 (0, 0.02) in the chronic kidney disease group, P < 0.001 (Table 1). Values below the limit of detection were handled as described in Methods.

## Methods (brief)

Serum metals were measured by ICP-MS (Agilent 7700x); associations were modeled with regression, machine learning and Bayesian kernel machine regression.

## Evidence fitness

Supports that serum gallium is usually below detection in this population. Does not support a kidney effect of gallium.

## Limitations

Medians of zero mean gallium was rarely quantified, so group comparisons for gallium carry little information.
