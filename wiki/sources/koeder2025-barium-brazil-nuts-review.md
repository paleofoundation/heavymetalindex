# Koeder and Keller 2025 : Barium levels in Brazil nuts, a global review of the literature

## Summary

Global literature review of barium concentration data for Brazil nuts, covering 24 eligible publications from 1933 to 2023, with no language or time restrictions. The review reports a literature mean barium level of 1.27 mg/g (95 percent CI: 0.89 to 1.64 mg/g; range 0.03 to 5.89 mg/g; n = 43) and discusses health implications relative to tolerable daily intake (TDI) values.

## Health context

Barium has no known biological function. Chronic high intake may impair kidney function and affect the nervous and cardiovascular systems. The ATSDR suggested gastrointestinal absorption of barium in humans is in the range of 5 to 30 percent of the administered dose. BaSO4 (the form proposed as predominant in some nuts) is considered poorly absorbed, but other barium salts (chloride, carbonate, acetate, nitrate, hydroxide) are more bioavailable. The current WHO guideline value for barium in drinking water is 1.3 mg/L.

## Methods (brief)

Search on PubMed, Web of Science, Chemical Abstracts, and Semantic Scholar. Terms: "Brazil nuts" OR "Bertholletia" AND "barium" OR "minerals." No language or time restriction. Completed January 2023. Twenty-four eligible publications identified (1933 to 2023). Statistical analysis in IBM SPSS Statistics 20 (Palo Alto, CA, USA). Sensitivity analysis excluding one defatted-nut study confirmed results.

## Implications

- Ingredient page: provides the most comprehensive published estimate of barium in Brazil nuts; literature mean 1.27 mg/g (1270 ug/g) with range 30 to 5890 ug/g.
- For regulatory context: consuming 2 Brazil nuts per day may exceed barium TDI for most regions, based on published TDI of 0.2 mg/kg body weight/day.
- Geographic variability is large; Acre state in Brazil and Guyana-origin nuts showed the highest values.
