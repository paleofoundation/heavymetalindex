# Deger and Mujdeci 2026 -- whitening toothpaste effects on bulk-fill resin composite surface roughness, in vitro

## Summary

This in vitro study evaluated the effects of five whitening toothpastes on the surface roughness of two bulk-fill resin composites (GrandioSO x-tra, Filtek Bulk Fill Posterior) and one nanohybrid composite (Clearfil Majesty Esthetic) using simulated toothbrushing at 5 and 30 minutes. The study does not measure heavy-metal contamination; it assesses the mechanical effects of whitening dentifrices on restorative dental materials. This source has no primary metals occurrence data relevant to the [[products/toothpaste]] page's contamination profile. It is catalogued here because it was deposited in the HMI-FILL-0055 toothpaste batch but does not contribute to the metals evidence base.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only. This source contains no heavy-metal concentration data. It evaluates toothpaste-material interaction (surface roughness) rather than toothpaste heavy-metal content. It does not contribute to the metals evidence tables on [[products/toothpaste]].

## Methods (brief)

Five whitening toothpastes (Opalescence Whitening, Colgate Optic White, Rocs Sensation Whitening, Signal White Now, Curaprox Black Is White) and a distilled-water control were applied to 60 disc specimens (10 per group) of three composite resins using an electric toothbrush at 2 N constant force for 5 and 30 minutes. Surface roughness (Ra, micrometres) measured with a contact profilometer. Statistical analysis: two-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc tests (alpha = 0.05).
