# Huang et al. 2020 - False positive monomethyltin in toys

## Overview

This paper investigates false positive detections of monomethyltin (MMT) in organotin migration tests for toys under the EU Toy Safety Directive (Directive 2009/48/EC). The derivatization reagent sodium tetraethylborate can generate a tetraethylmethyltin (TEMT) artefact from inorganic tin in gastric juice, which co-elutes with monomethyltin in GC-MS organotin analysis and produces false positives. The experimental substrate is metallic tin powder and tinplated toy materials, not food. This paper has no food occurrence data and is not relevant to dietary tin exposure.

## Methods

Metallic tin powder (99.99%) and tinplated toy materials treated with artificial gastric juice (0.07 mol/L HCl) following EN 71-3:2013+A3:2018. GC-MS analysis for organotin speciation after derivatization with sodium tetraethylborate.

## Implications

Not applicable; no food data. The artefact mechanism (inorganic tin reacting with NaBEt4 to produce false positive for monomethyltin) is analytically relevant background for understanding organotin speciation methods, but it does not affect total tin food measurements by ICP-MS.
