# Levels of Selected Heavy Metals in Food Packaging Papers and Paperboards Used in India

## Summary

Sood and Sharma measured 14 elements in 10 types of food packaging papers and paperboards bought in India. The label for the measurement is papers and paperboards, not cartons specifically. The samples were digested. This is not a migration test.

## Key numbers

The results text gives ranges in ppm: aluminum 11.7 to 102.7, arsenic not detected to 0.3, chromium 0.02 to 2.1, nickel not detected to 0.19, and lead not detected to 0.38. Table 1 prints pizza box aluminum 102.722±0.264 ppm and arsenic 0.300±0.024 ppm, pastry box chromium 2.174±0.091 ppm, pizza box nickel 0.196±0.004 ppm, and paper plate lead 0.388±0.007 ppm. Methods say 3 samples of each group were purchased. Cadmium, mercury, and tin: Data gap. Arsenic and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking. Permitted concentrations printed in the table are comparison values, not measurements.
