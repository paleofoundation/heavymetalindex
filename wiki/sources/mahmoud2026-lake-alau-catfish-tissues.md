# Tissue-Specific Bioaccumulation of Zinc, Lead, Copper, Cadmium and Manganese in Clarias gariepinus from Lake Alau, Northeastern Nigeria

## Summary

Mahmoud, Susan, and Luka measured zinc, lead, copper, cadmium, and manganese in gill, liver, and muscle of Clarias gariepinus from Lake Alau, Borno State, Nigeria. Results are mg/kg wet weight. Monthly means are n=3. Table captions print January 2019 to June 2020 while the methods and month rows print January 2025 to June 2026.

## Key numbers

Table 3 and Table 4 muscle means, mg/kg wet weight: zinc 0.29±0.04, lead 0.27±0.03, copper 0.35±0.05, cadmium 0.25±0.03, manganese 0.26±0.03. Arsenic, mercury, and chromium: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
