# Borse et al. 2022: A yttrium garnet laser and tooth bleaching

This in vitro dental study tested whether pretreating enamel with a neodymium-doped yttrium aluminum garnet laser changes the whitening effect of 35% hydrogen peroxide on tea-stained extracted teeth. Yttrium appears only as part of the laser crystal; no yttrium exposure was studied.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to yttrium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Teeth were stained, laser-treated or not, bleached and assessed for color change with a spectrophotometer.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: a dental laser study that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for yttrium.

## Limitations

Thirty teeth in vitro; yttrium was not measured.
