# Tabari et al. 2026 — Health risk assessment of potentially toxic elements in canned pinto and fava beans, Gilan Province, Iran

## Overview

This Iranian study measured 10 potentially toxic elements (Pb, Cd, As, Cr, Fe, Cu, Zn, Ni, Mn, Sn) in canned pinto beans and canned fava beans purchased from markets in Gilan Province, Iran, by ICP-OES. All measured tin concentrations were far below Iranian, EU, and Codex regulatory limits. Tin was detected at trace levels consistent with modest tinplate migration into legume matrices. Lead exceeded the Codex permissible limit in several samples; cadmium was also above limits in a subset.

## Key numbers

All concentrations in mg/kg wet weight. LOD for Sn: 0.02 mg/L; LOQ: 0.066 mg/L; MDL in matrix: 0.0151 mg/kg.

**Tin (Sn) concentrations:**

| Matrix | Mean +/- SD (mg/kg) | Range (mg/kg) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|
| Pinto beans (canned) | 0.218 +/- 0.195 | 0.000 to 0.645 |
| Fava beans (canned) | 0.1225 +/- 0.158 | 0.000 to 0.471 |

**Regulatory limits for Sn in canned foods cited:**

| Standard | Limit (mg/kg) |
|----------|---------------|
| INSO (Iran) | 250 |
| EU | 200 |
| Codex Alimentarius | 250 |

All samples well below all three limits.

## Evidence Fitness

Modeled or limited evidence (EF-3). The dataset is a small market survey from one Iranian province. It provides directional occurrence context for Sn in canned legumes, confirming that tin migration into this matrix is minimal relative to regulatory limits. The sample sizes per brand are not stated in the reviewed text. The mean values are suitable as low-exposure reference points for canned legume matrices.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-OES (PerkinElmer Optima 7300 DV). Wet acid digestion with HNO3 and HClO4. Recoveries not reported in the reviewed excerpt. Speciation not performed; all results as total element concentrations. The paper also performed non-carcinogenic (hazard quotient, HQ) and carcinogenic (target carcinogenic risk, TCR) risk calculations for Pb, Cd, As, and Cr; Sn was not included in the risk model because its HQ was negligible.

## Implications

Certification: Tin concentrations in canned pinto and fava beans (mean 0.218 and 0.1225 mg/kg respectively) are very low compared to the 200 mg/kg EU limit, confirming that legume matrices are low-risk for Sn from tinplate migration under normal market conditions.

Courses: Useful illustration of species-and-matrix-specific exposure: legumes show far lower Sn from can migration than acidic products (fruits, tomato paste), consistent with the pH-dependent tin release mechanism.

App: For canned legume matrices, Sn is not a risk driver; Pb and Cd are the analytes requiring attention in this matrix class.
