# Peng et al. 2018 : Methionine-capped gold nanoclusters for cadmium detection in milk powder

## Overview

Peng and colleagues synthesized methionine-capped gold nanoclusters (Au NCs, less than 2 nm) as a fluorescence-enhanced probe for Cd2+ detection. Cadmium displaces methionine from the Au NC surface, quenching fluorescence in a concentration-dependent manner. Gold nanoclusters are the analytical reagent; cadmium is the target analyte. The sensor was validated in spiked skim milk powder and drinking water at concentrations relevant to food safety monitoring.

## Key numbers

LOD: 0.1 µg/L Cd2+. Working range: 0.5 to 3.0 µg/L in spiked skim milk powder. Recoveries in milk powder: 91.2 to 103.8%. LOD 12.25 nM as reported in the paper. Sensor selectivity against Ca, Mg, Fe, and other common ions demonstrated.
