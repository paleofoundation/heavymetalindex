# Dai et al. 2026: Serum elements and vitamin D in kidney tumors

This case-control study measured 14 elements including gallium by ICP-MS in serum of patients with kidney tumors and healthy controls. Serum gallium was slightly higher in patients with renal cell carcinoma than in the other groups.

## Key numbers

- Serum gallium, mean ± SD: renal cell carcinoma 1.13 ± 0.16, benign kidney tumors 1.04 ± 0.18, healthy controls 1.04 ± 0.18 (F = 4.453, P = 0.013) (Table 2). The table footnote unit renders as mg/L in the extracted text and needs checking against the PDF.
- Gallium correlated weakly with eGFR (r = 0.212, P = 0.034) (Results).

## Methods (brief)

Serum elements were measured by ICP-MS with internal standards; vitamin D metabolites by UPLC-MS/MS.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the presence of measurable gallium in human serum and a small group difference. Does not support a causal role for gallium in kidney cancer.

## Limitations

Single-center case-control design; association only. Unit of the gallium values needs verification against the original table.
