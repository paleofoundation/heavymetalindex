# Kawakatsu et al. 2025: Rare sugars from glucose on a boron-doped diamond electrode

This chemistry paper oxidized d-glucose electrochemically on boron-doped diamond electrodes to produce rare sugars such as d-arabinose and d-erythrose. Boron is only the electrode dopant.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to boron exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Constant-current and constant-potential electrolysis; HPLC and LC/MS product analysis.

## Evidence fitness

Boron appears here only as a component of an engineered material. The paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for boron.

## Limitations

Off-topic for boron exposure; no boron measurements in food, water or people. Funded by JSPS KAKENHI; no competing financial interest declared.
