# da Rosa et al. 2019: Arsenic species in retail rice milk by LC-ICP-MS

## Overview

da Rosa and colleagues developed a simple dilute-and-filter LC-ICP-MS method for arsenite, arsenate, DMA and MMA in rice milk and applied it to four retail rice milks, with total arsenic measured by ICP-MS after microwave digestion. Total arsenic ranged from 14.57 to 46.94 µg/L, and all four arsenic species were found across the products. The paper is mainly a methods paper, but its Table 4 results are direct measurements of a plant milk.

## Key numbers

- Wholegrain (brown) rice milk: total arsenic 46.94 ± 1.96 µg/L; As(III) 12.48, As(V) 6.99, DMA 22.57, MMA 1.24 µg/L (Table 4).
- Polished-rice milk I: total arsenic 29.48 ± 1.23 µg/L; DMA 16.64 µg/L (Table 4).
- Polished-rice milk II: total arsenic 15.33 ± 1.10 µg/L; MMA below LOD (Table 4).
- Brown-rice milk with coconut: total arsenic 14.57 ± 0.96 µg/L; DMA and MMA below LOD (Table 4).

## Methods (brief)

For speciation, samples were diluted 3-fold in 9.0 mmol/L ammonium phosphate mobile phase, filtered and separated on an anion-exchange column coupled to ICP-MS. Total arsenic was measured by ICP-MS after nitric acid microwave digestion. LOQs were 0.25 to 0.43 µg/L; spike recoveries 81-116%. Each product was analysed in triplicate. Results are per litre and are kept unconverted.

## Evidence Fitness

Four products, each one unit analysed in triplicate, so the data show the range found rather than a market survey. No certified reference material was used. The abstract calls arsenate the main species, which Table 4 does not support, and the text's claim that brown-rice milks carry more arsenic holds only for the wholegrain product. Inorganic arsenic is not printed as a separate total. Rice milk is a plant milk, so this is direct evidence for the plant-milk page.
