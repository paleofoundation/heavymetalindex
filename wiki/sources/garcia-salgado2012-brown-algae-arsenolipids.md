# García-Salgado et al. 2012 - brown-algae arsenolipids

## Overview

García-Salgado et al. identified and quantified arsenosugar phospholipids and arsenic hydrocarbons in two dried commercial brown macroalgae, Wakame and Hijiki. The paper reports whole-powder total arsenic and lipid-soluble organoarsenic species, not inorganic arsenic. Values below therefore keep whole-powder total arsenic and individual arsenolipids separate.

## Key numbers

The paper reports whole dry-powder total arsenic as `µg As g-1 dry mass`:

| Material | Species | Whole-powder tAs |
|---|---|---:|
| Wakame | *Undaria pinnatifida* | 40 ± 3 µg As g-1 dry mass |
| Hijiki | *Hizikia fusiformis* | 113 ± 5 µg As g-1 dry mass |

The lipid fraction contained `6.7%` of initial total arsenic for Wakame and `1.6%` for Hijiki.

Table 2 quantifies arsenolipids in `µg As kg-1` and as percentage of lipid arsenic. Data are `mean ± s.d.` from three analyses, each involving extraction, clean-up, and HPLC-ICPMS; column recoveries ranged from `~70 to 110%`.

Figure 2 states that As-HC332 and As-HC360 were present in both Wakame and Hijiki, while As-HC388 was present in Wakame but not Hijiki by the confirmatory GC-MS discussion; Table 2 nevertheless reports a small Hijiki As-HC388 HPLC-ICPMS quantified value of `22 ± 2 µg As kg-1`.

For QA/QC, the paper reports NIES No. 9 Sargasso certified total arsenic `115 ± 5 µg As g-1` and measured `116 ± 2 µg As g-1 (n = 3)`.

## Methods (brief)

Total arsenic analyses were performed on dry powders, extracts, or combined silica-column fractions by ICP-MS in helium collision-cell mode after microwave-assisted acid mineralization. Arsenolipids were extracted in triplicate from Wakame and Hijiki with chloroform/methanol, washed with `1% w/v` bicarbonate solution, cleaned on silica, and analyzed by reversed-phase HPLC with simultaneous ICPMS and electrospray MS detection. HPLC used a Zorbax Eclipse XDB-C8 column with an acetic acid/methanol gradient; accurate masses were measured with an LTQ Orbitrap XL in FTMS high-resolution positive-scan mode.

## Implications

This source adds seaweed organoarsenic speciation context for commercial Wakame and Hijiki. It should not be treated as an inorganic-arsenic occurrence paper: the quantified species are arsenosugar phospholipids, arsenic hydrocarbons, and two unidentified arsenolipid peaks. Downstream extraction should keep whole-powder tAs, lipid-fraction arsenic percentage, and named arsenolipid species distinct.
