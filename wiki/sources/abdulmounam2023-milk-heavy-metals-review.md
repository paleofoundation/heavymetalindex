# Abdul Mounam 2023: Review of heavy metal contamination of milk and dairy products

## Overview

A single-author narrative review from the University of Baghdad on sources, toxicity and regulatory limits of heavy metals in milk and dairy products. Tables 2 to 6 compile lead, cadmium, nickel, cobalt and copper levels in milk reported by other studies between 2012 and 2018. The paper reports no measurements of its own.

## Key numbers

- No original concentrations. Table 1 lists sources of each metal; Tables 2 to 6 quote prevalence figures from other papers.

## Methods (brief)

Literature review; search method and inclusion criteria are not described.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only (tier C). Quoted values belong to the primary studies and should be cited from those papers. The printed DOI (10.26505/DJM.24027530504) differs from the manifest DOI (10.26505/djm.v24i2.1008); the record uses the printed DOI. It does not measure half-and-half, skim milk or semi-skimmed milk.
