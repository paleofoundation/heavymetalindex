# Levels and Health Risk Assessment of Heavy Metals in Selected Skin-Whitening Creams Marketed in Lafia Town

## Summary

Abel, Abubakar, and Muhammad measured lead, chromium, cadmium, iron, and copper by flame atomic absorption spectrophotometry in five anonymous skin-whitening creams, coded A to E, from Lafia, Nigeria. Table 1 is mg/kg. Basis: Data gap. These are skin creams, not oral whitening strips, gels, powders, or pens.

## Key numbers

Table 1 means, mg/kg: lead 0.3180 (range 0.00 to 1.240; A, B, and E printed 0.000), chromium 4.0762 (0.338 to 11.760), cadmium 0.0970 (0.046 to 0.137). Nickel, arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
