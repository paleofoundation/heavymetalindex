# Occurrence of Potentially Toxic Metals Detected in Milk and Dairy Products in Türkiye: An Assessment in Terms of Human Exposure and Health Risks

## Summary

This journal article measured lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, chromium, and aluminum by ICP-MS in retail yogurt, kefir, and ayran. The label is yogurt, kefir, ayran. A footnote says only yogurt is µg/kg. Kefir and ayran are µg/L. Tin is not reported.

## Key numbers

Yogurt n=13, µg/kg: aluminum 946±815, chromium 56±41, nickel 72±100, arsenic 42±37, cadmium 0.02±0.07, mercury 0.02±0.03, lead 0.01±0.02. Kefir n=7 and ayran n=8 are µg/L in the same tables. Tin: Data gap. Arsenic, mercury, and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
