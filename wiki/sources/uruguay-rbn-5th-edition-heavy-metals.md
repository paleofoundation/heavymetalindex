The fifth edition of Uruguay's Reglamento Bromatologico Nacional includes general and particular inorganic-contaminant limits for foods, plus food-contact-material provisions. HMI records the food contaminant rows and the fillable ceramic Pb/Cd migration rows that are expressed in mg/kg. Surface-area migration rows in mg/dm2 and soluble-percent colorant rows are gap-logged because they cannot be represented in the ug/kg register without changing basis.
