# Engrola 2023 ,  Arsenite oxidase with antimonite

Crystal structures of arsenite oxidase with arsenic or antimony oxyanions. Sb(III) is oxidized about 6,500 times more slowly than As(III) (PDF p.1).

## Key numbers

Crystal structures of arsenite oxidase with arsenic or antimony oxyanions. Sb(III) is oxidized about 6,500 times more slowly than As(III) (PDF p.1).
