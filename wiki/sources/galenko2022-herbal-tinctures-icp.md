# Inductively coupled plasma-atomic emission spectrometry for the analysis of heavy metals and arsenic in tinctures

## Summary

Galenko, Alyautdin, and Gravel measured elemental impurities by ICP-AES in peony, motherwort, and valerian tinctures sold through a pharmacy network in Russia. The article is in Russian with an English title and English table headers. Three parallel samples and five readings were used. Unit mg/kg, with decimal commas. The authors selected a 25 mL aliquot. Manufacturer names are omitted. Valerian numeric cells are figure bars without printed heights.

## Key numbers

English abstract: concentrations did not exceed 0.722 mg/kg. Conclusion: lead, cadmium, and mercury 0,001 to 0,054 mg/kg. Peony 25 mL: arsenic 0,007±0,002, cadmium 0,001±0,001, chromium 0,001±0,0001, nickel 0,070±0,025, lead below LoD. Motherwort 25 mL: arsenic 0,008±0,003, cadmium 0,001±0,0002, chromium 0,002±0,0006, nickel 0,032±0,006, lead 0,005±0,0017, mercury 0,003±0,001. Peony zinc 0,471 at 20 and 25 mL sits outside the conclusion range 0,001 to 0,421. Aluminum and tin: Data gap. Peony mercury cell: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
