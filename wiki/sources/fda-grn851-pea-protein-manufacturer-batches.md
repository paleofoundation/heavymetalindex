# FDA GRAS Notice 851: Manufacturer heavy-metal results for three pea protein isolate grades

## Overview

This GRAS notice, dated January 2019, lists the manufacturer's lead, arsenic, cadmium and mercury results for three lots each of three pea protein isolate grades. The Index withholds the manufacturer and grade names.

## Key numbers

- Lead: below 0.01 to 0.03 mg/kg across nine lots (Tables 4 to 6).
- Cadmium: 0.04 to 0.08 mg/kg across nine lots (Tables 4 to 6).
- Total arsenic below 0.10 and mercury below 0.01 mg/kg in every lot (Tables 4 to 6).

## Methods (brief)

The tables give the method only as "Ext", an external laboratory, with a specification of below 0.2 mg/kg for each metal.

## Evidence Fitness

These are manufacturer test results submitted to FDA with a GRAS notice, not independent measurements. FDA published the notice but did not verify the values. Nine lots from one manufacturer, analyzed by an unnamed external laboratory and method. Inorganic arsenic was not measured.
