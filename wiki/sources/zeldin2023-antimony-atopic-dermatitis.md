# Zeldin 2023 ,  Antimony inventories and atopic dermatitis models

The printed correlation with pediatric atopic dermatitis visits was 0.84 in Montana, Idaho, Wyoming, and the Dakotas, and -0.014 elsewhere (PDF p.3). In vitro, antimony inhibited ceramide-family lipids in Roseomonas mucosa and induced calcium flux blocked by a TRPA1 antagonist. No antimony concentration is printed for those assays.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the printed coefficients and the billing-code conflict. Does not support a diaper-area cohort or a skin-wipe concentration.

## Limitations
