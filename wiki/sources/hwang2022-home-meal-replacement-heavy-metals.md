# Hwang et al. 2022 - heavy metals in Korean home meal replacement products

Hwang and colleagues measured lead, cadmium, total arsenic, tin, total mercury, and methylmercury in 480 home meal replacement (HMR) products purchased in Korea. The product panel included ready-to-eat, ready-to-heat, ready-to-cook, and ready-to-prepare foods. Some HMR subgroups included infant and toddler foods, but the paper reports occurrence by fat/liquid matrix phase rather than by infant-formula row, so this page routes only to the broad prepared-meals product row.

The source does not provide brand-level values. The occurrence table is reported at matrix-phase level and is therefore compatible with the brand firewall.

## Methods (brief)

The authors purchased HMR products from Korean convenience and grocery stores from January to September 2020. After removing non-edible portions, samples were homogenized and stored at `-18 C`.

For Pb, Cd, As, and Sn, `0.5 g` samples were digested with `7 mL` ultrapure nitric acid and `0.5 mL` hydrogen peroxide in a microwave-assisted acid digestion system, diluted to `25 mL`, filtered through `0.45 um` membranes, and analyzed by ICP-MS. For Me-Hg, `1.0 g` samples were extracted with sodium chloride, hydrochloric acid, toluene, and L-cysteine before DMA analysis. Total Hg was also analyzed by DMA.

## Implications

This is direct occurrence evidence for Korean home meal replacement and prepared-meal products. The study is useful for prepared-food heavy-metal surveillance and for method context where matrices span solid/liquid and fatty/non-fatty foods.

For HMTc use, the paper's product aggregation is broad. It does not provide product-row-specific values for infant formula powder, baby foods, seafood, sauces, breads, rice cakes, meat products, or meal kits. The values should remain broad prepared-meal context unless a later extraction pass can resolve product-row fit from supplementary material or underlying sample metadata. Total arsenic is not inorganic arsenic, and total mercury is not methylmercury except for the explicit Me-Hg rows.
