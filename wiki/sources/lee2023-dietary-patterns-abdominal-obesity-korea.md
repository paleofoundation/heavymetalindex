# Lee et al. 2023 — Dietary patterns and abdominal obesity in Korean adults (KoGES-HEXA)

This paper examines associations between dietary patterns and incident abdominal obesity in 48,037 middle-aged and older Korean adults over an average 4.89-year follow-up using the KoGES-HEXA cohort. Factor analysis identified three dietary patterns — "healthy," "coffee and sweets," and "multi-grain." The healthy pattern (high in vegetables, seaweeds, soy products, and fish) was inversely associated with abdominal obesity; the coffee and sweets pattern was positively associated. There is no heavy metal measurement content in this paper.

## Key numbers

None relevant to heavy metals. Study outcome: 5,878 incident abdominal obesity cases. Hazard ratio for healthy pattern (Q4 vs Q1): 0.86 (95% CI 0.75–0.98) for men, 0.90 (95% CI 0.83–0.99) for women.

## Methods (brief)

Prospective cohort (KoGES-HEXA). Dietary assessment via 106-item validated food-frequency questionnaire. Dietary patterns by factor analysis. Cox proportional hazards models adjusting for covariates. No metal measurement performed.
