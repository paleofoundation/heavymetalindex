# Minor and Toxic Element Content in Edible Mushrooms

## Summary

Fardani measured zinc, copper, lead, and cadmium by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in cultivated Pleurotus osteratus and Auricularia auricula-judae from Mataram, Indonesia. The species spelling osteratus is the paper's. n=3 for each table. Methods started from 2 g wet weight and then dried the sample. The Auricularia zinc and copper text says dry basis.

## Key numbers

Content column, mg/kg, decimal commas as printed: Pleurotus zinc 131,34, copper 4,57, lead below 0,500, cadmium 0,547. Auricularia zinc 74,6, copper 1,17, lead below 0,500, cadmium below 0,150. The abstract prints Pleurotus zinc 131.34 and Auricularia zinc 74.5. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
