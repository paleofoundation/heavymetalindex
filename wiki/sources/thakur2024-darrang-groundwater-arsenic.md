# Thakur et al. 2024 - Darrang groundwater arsenic and health risk

Thakur and colleagues measured arsenic, iron, fluoride, and major hydrogeochemical parameters in shallow groundwater across the Darrang district of Assam and modelled non-carcinogenic and carcinogenic health risk from arsenic exposure. This is lane a3 and a4 pathway and exposure evidence, not bottled-mineral-water occurrence, because the sampled matrix is shallow groundwater used for drinking and agriculture.

## Key numbers

- `347` groundwater samples were collected across `86` grids of `16 km²` each in Darrang district during `2023`.
- Groundwater pH ranged from `6.01` to `8.47` with mean `7.42`.
- Electrical conductivity ranged from `72` to `1029 µS cm−1`, with mean `280.3 µS cm−1`.
- Bicarbonate ranged from `34.0` to `320 mg/L`, with mean `101.3 mg/L`.
- Calcium ranged from `0.63` to `111.38 mg/L`, with mean `9.67 mg/L`.
- Total arsenic ranged from `0.125 µg/L` to `100.19 µg/L`, and `78%` of samples (`n = 274`) exceeded the WHO `10 µg/L` drinking-water limit.
- Iron ranged from `BDL` to `9.341 mg/L`, and `97%` of samples exceeded the `0.3 mg/L` limit cited by the authors.
- Fluoride remained within the WHO `1.5 mg/L` limit in `100%` of samples.
- For arsenic non-cancer risk, child hazard quotient ranged from `0.07` to `42.08`; `98.8%` of child exposures had `HQ > 1`.
- Adult arsenic hazard quotient ranged from `0.05` to `11.52` in females and `0.02` to `13.08` in males; `93.9%` and `95.1%` respectively had `HQ > 1`.
- The paper states carcinogenic risk above `10−4` for arsenic-polluted water in `99.42%` of children and `99.13%` of adults.

## Methods (brief)

Groundwater was collected from shallow tubewells after a `10-minute` pumping period using stratified random grab sampling across all seven Darrang community-development blocks. Filtered samples were acidified to `pH ≤ 2` with nitric acid for Ca, Mg, Fe, and As analysis, while separate raw samples were used for other ions and fluoride. Field measurements included temperature, pH, EC, TDS, turbidity, and ORP. Fluoride was measured with ion-selective electrode, sodium and potassium by flame photometer, and calcium, magnesium, iron, and arsenic by flow-injection hydride-generation AAS (Thermo Fisher iCE3500), calibrated from `1` to `100 µg/L` with standard solution. The paper applies multivariate hydrogeochemical analysis and USEPA-style risk calculations.

## Implications

Certification: Do not use these groundwater values as bottled-water product occurrence or food-occurrence pool values. They are environmental and exposure-context measurements.

Courses: Strong groundwater-pathway case linking alkaline desorption, silicate weathering, and reductive dissolution to arsenic release and then to child health-risk calculations.

App: Context-only support for arsenic groundwater-pathway and exposure-risk framing in the Brahmaputra floodplain.
