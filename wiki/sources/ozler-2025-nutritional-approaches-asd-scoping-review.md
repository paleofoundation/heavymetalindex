# Ozler and Sanlier 2025, Nutritional approaches in autism

Ozler and Sanlier scoped dietary models and supplements used in autism spectrum disorder. The camel milk section states that camel milk raises glutathione peroxidase and superoxide dismutase and may reduce oxidative stress. That section does not report a gut microbiota measurement for camel milk. Elsewhere the review states that the gut microbiota of children with autism differs from that of children without autism, and it discusses probiotics and prebiotics as interventions for gastrointestinal and behavioral symptoms. Those statements are not camel milk results.

The DOI is 10.1007/s13668-025-00655-y. This page records that bibliographic identity and the separation above. It does not copy a metal concentration and it does not set a contamination value.

## Key numbers

No camel milk metal concentration and no camel milk microbiota count are copied here.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports a description of how this review separates camel milk from its probiotic discussion. It does not support a camel milk contamination profile, a certification limit, or a claim that camel milk changed gut microbiota.

## Limitations

Secondary citations inside the review are not re-analyzed here. This page does not re-extract those underlying studies.
