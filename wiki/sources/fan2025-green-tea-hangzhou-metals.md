# Fan et al. 2025 — 15 heavy metals in 120 green tea samples from Hangzhou, China; ICP-MS

Fan et al. measured 15 heavy metals (Al, As, Ba, Cd, Cr, Cu, Hg, Li, Mn, Ni, Pb, Sb, Se, Sn, V) by ICP-MS in 120 green tea samples collected from supermarkets (67), local markets (43), and planting locations (10) in Hangzhou, China. This is a large, well-characterised primary dataset from a major Chinese green tea production zone (West Lake area of Hangzhou is internationally renowned for Longjing/Dragon Well tea). All 120 samples were below Chinese national standard limits for the regulated metals. The comprehensive pollution index (P = 0.094) was well below 0.7, placing the Hangzhou green tea production zone in the "clean and safe" category under the Numero classification system.

## Key numbers
- 120 green tea samples; Hangzhou area, Zhejiang Province, China
- Al: 126.00–975.00 mg/kg, mean 276.50 ± 148.50 mg/kg, median 227.00 mg/kg (highest concentration element)
- As: 0.013–0.17 mg/kg, mean 0.054 ± 0.030 mg/kg, median 0.047 mg/kg
- Ba: 4.01–103.00 mg/kg, mean 21.40 ± 15.92 mg/kg, median 16.75 mg/kg
- Cd: 0.0093–0.17 mg/kg, mean 0.040 ± 0.024 mg/kg, median 0.034 mg/kg
- Cr: 0.044–3.52 mg/kg, mean 0.62 ± 0.55 mg/kg, median 0.42 mg/kg
- Hg: ND–0.017 mg/kg, mean 0.0033 ± 0.0034 mg/kg (69.2% detection rate)
- Mn: 202.00–2010.00 mg/kg, mean 836.00 ± 274.76 mg/kg, median 814.50 mg/kg
- Ni: 2.03–29.90 mg/kg, mean 10.70 ± 4.37 mg/kg, median 10.35 mg/kg
- Pb: 0.051–1.71 mg/kg, mean 0.45 ± 0.30 mg/kg, median 0.37 mg/kg
- Sb: ND–0.22 mg/kg, mean 0.022 ± 0.022 mg/kg (98.3% detection)
- Sn: ND–12.80 mg/kg, mean 0.18 ± 1.22 mg/kg (94.2% detection)
- V: 0.016–0.71 mg/kg, mean 0.11 ± 0.11 mg/kg
- All values on dry weight basis (tea leaves before infusion)
- Hazard Index (HI) for consumers = 0.42 (below 1, indicating acceptable risk under EPA framework)
- Chinese standard limits (NY659-2003/GB2762-2022): As 2.0, Cd 1.0, Cr 5.0, Hg 0.3, Pb 5.0 mg/kg

## Methods (brief)
ICP-MS (Agilent 8900); 15 elements measured; CEM microwave digestion with HNO₃; samples from supermarkets, local markets, and planting sites in Hangzhou area (6 districts/counties); GBW10052 (GSS-30) green tea CRM for accuracy validation; recoveries 86.4–97.5%; LODs 0.0003–0.03 mg/kg.

## Implications
Certification: Establishes a Hangzhou-specific baseline for green tea metal concentrations. Pb (mean 0.45 mg/kg, max 1.71 mg/kg) and Al (mean 276.50 mg/kg) are the metals of highest nominal concentration in tea leaves, though the all-below-regulatory-limits finding indicates the Hangzhou production zone is relatively clean.
Courses: Illustrates the breadth of metals that accumulate in tea leaves (15 elements) and the relatively high Al content in tea (a known Al accumulator plant), alongside the regulatory framework for Chinese tea safety.
App: Tea leaf Pb (mean 0.45 mg/kg dry weight, max 1.71 mg/kg) and Cd (mean 0.040 mg/kg) are the primary regulated metals of concern in tea ingredients. As-infused concentrations are substantially lower due to extraction fraction.
