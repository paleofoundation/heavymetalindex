# Tapia et al. 2026: Toxic residues in meat, a book chapter review

## Overview

This book chapter reviews mycotoxins, clenbuterol, hormones, antibiotics, nitrosamines and heavy metals in beef, pork and poultry. Heavy-metal levels are quoted from studies in Iran, Bangladesh, Colombia and Saudi Arabia.

## Key numbers

- No original concentrations; metal values are quoted from cited studies.

## Methods (brief)

Narrative literature review using PubMed, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar and SciELO.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only (review chapter, tier C). No findings for chicken pot pie, chili con carne, canned pork and beans or pork chops.
