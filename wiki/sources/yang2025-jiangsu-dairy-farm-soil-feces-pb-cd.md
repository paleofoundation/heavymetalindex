# Yang et al. 2025: Lead and cadmium in soil and cow feces at three Jiangsu dairy farms

## Overview

Yang and colleagues mapped lead and cadmium in soil around three dairy farms in Jiangsu and measured both metals in cow feces as a sign of exposure. Lead pollution was low and cadmium pollution moderate in several areas, with soil pH a key factor. No milk was analysed.

## Key numbers

- No food concentrations. Soil results are in Tables 1 to 3 and fecal results in the Results text; neither is a food measurement.

## Methods (brief)

Soil was air-dried, sieved and digested with aqua regia; feces were pooled from six cows per farm. Pollution indices were compared with Jiangsu provincial soil background values.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only for dairy farm exposure pathways. It does not measure milk, cream or any dairy product, so it is not evidence for half-and-half or any other dairy page. Milk values cited in the introduction come from other studies.
