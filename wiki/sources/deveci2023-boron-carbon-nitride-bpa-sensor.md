# Deveci et al. 2023: A boron-functionalized carbon nitride sensor for bisphenol A

This method paper built a molecularly imprinted electrochemical sensor from graphene quantum dots and boron-functionalized graphitic carbon nitride to detect bisphenol A in food samples. Boron is part of the electrode material, not an analyte.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to boron exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Nanocomposite synthesis and characterization; molecularly imprinted electrochemical sensing of bisphenol A.

## Evidence fitness

Boron appears here only as a component of an engineered material. The paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for boron.

## Limitations

Off-topic for boron exposure; no boron measurements in food, water or people. Funded by the Turkish Academy of Sciences; no conflicts declared.
