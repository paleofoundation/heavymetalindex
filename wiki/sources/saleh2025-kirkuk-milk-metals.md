# Analysis of Heavy Metals Levels in Milk and Dairy Products and Assessing the Associated Health Risks

## Summary

Saleh measured lead and cadmium by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in milk collected in Kirkuk and Sulaymaniyah, Iraq, from November 2023 to July 2024. The sample is 100 milks: 20 cow, 20 sheep, 20 goat, and 20 buffalo. The title says milk and dairy products. Only milk concentrations are printed. Basis: Data gap. Unit mg/kg. Arsenic and mercury are named and not measured.

## Key numbers

Table 2, mg/kg, min to max (mean): cadmium cow 0.01 to 0.05 (0.03), sheep 0.02 to 0.06 (0.04), goat 0.02 to 0.06 (0.04), buffalo 0.01 to 0.04 (0.025); lead cow 0.01 to 0.03 (0.02), sheep 0.05 to 0.1 (0.03), goat 0.07 to 0.1 (0.04), buffalo 0.06 to 0.09 (0.075). Sheep and goat lead means sit outside the printed ranges. Location lead means: Laylan 0.470, Altun Kupri 0.090, Sulaymaniyah 0.185. Nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.
