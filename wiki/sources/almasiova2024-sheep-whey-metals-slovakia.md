# Almasiova et al. 2024, metals in sheep whey

This is sheep whey from one farm, not cow whey and not cheese. Table 2 prints mg kg−1. This note writes mg/kg for those numerals. n = 12 whey samples. No recovery percentage is printed. Cadmium was not detected. The cadmium limit of detection is 0.00005 mg/kg. Arsenic is not inorganic. Chromium is not hexavalent. Wet versus dry weight is not stated.

## Key numbers

Whey column, mg/kg: aluminum mean 0.13 ± 0.21, median 0.04; arsenic mean 0.24 ± 0.17, median 0.20; chromium mean 0.09 ± 0.28, median 0.004; nickel mean 0.17 ± 0.22, median 0.09; lead mean 0.09 ± 0.20, median 0.04. Cadmium ND.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-OES. Cheese and milk are separate columns and are not copied.

## Evidence fitness

The whey column supports a sheep-whey note. It does not support cow whey or a cheese value.

## Limitations

One farm. No recovery percentage. Basis (wet or dry) is not stated. Cadmium is not a measured concentration.
