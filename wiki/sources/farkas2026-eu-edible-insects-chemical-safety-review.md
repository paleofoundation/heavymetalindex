# Farkas et al. 2026: Chemical safety of edible insects in the EU, a review

## Overview

This structured narrative review covers heavy metals, mycotoxins, pesticide residues, veterinary drug residues, persistent contaminants and processing contaminants in edible insects, and how the EU novel food framework handles them. It finds that rearing substrates drive contamination and that the EU has no generally applicable insect-specific maximum levels for contaminants.
