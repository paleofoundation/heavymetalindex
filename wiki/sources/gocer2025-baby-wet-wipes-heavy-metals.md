# Göçer 2025 ,  Heavy metals in baby wet wipes

The English abstract in the thesis PDF reports antimony up to 695.4 ppb in wipe solution and 565.46 ppb in some fibres (PDF p.4-5). Brand names are not repeated.

## Key numbers

The methods print an approximate mass of 0.2 g and the addition of 10 mL of water after digestion. They do not print a made-up final volume, so no milligram-per-kilogram conversion is made.

## Methods (brief)

Thesis. Solution and fibre results left in ppb.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the two ppb figures. Does not support a sheet concentration in mg/kg.

## Limitations

The 10 mL addition is not a final volume.
