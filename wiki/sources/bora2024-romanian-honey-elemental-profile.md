# Bora et al. 2024: Elemental profile of Romanian honey, including a gallium screen

This survey measured many elements in honey from Romania by inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry. Gallium was one of the elements the authors report as not detected in any sample. The record below is limited to that gallium result. The paper also reports quantified results for other elements, which are not extracted here.

Numbers were checked against the Europe PMC full text (PMC11048993), retrieved 2026-11-04.

## Key numbers

- The authors state that 61 honey samples were collected in 2018 and 2021 from eight regions of Romania (Methods, section 2.1).
- Gallium was not detected in any honey sample (Results). The table footnote prints the gallium limit of quantification as 0.04 µ/L and states that gallium results are in µg/kg (Table 2 note). The paper does not give the dilution that would turn the solution limit into a honey concentration.
- Digestion used about 0.5 g of honey with 9 mL hydrochloric acid and 3 mL nitric acid, mineralized in a Milestone START D microwave system, and measured on a Thermo iCAP Q ICP-MS (Methods, sections 2.3 and 2.4).

## Methods (brief)

Honey came from local beekeepers, was unpasteurized, and was stored refrigerated in the dark until analysis. Each reported sample was drawn from three hives in the same production lot. The gallium result is a non-detect across the set, not a measured concentration.

## Evidence fitness

Useful as a food measurement that places gallium below the authors' reporting limit in this Romanian honey set. It does not support a typical honey concentration, a dietary intake, a toxicity threshold, or compliance with a gallium limit. Other elements in the same tables are out of scope for this gallium record.

## Limitations

The authors' own sample accounting does not agree with itself. The methods say 61 samples and also designate them H1 to H33. Floral counts are given once as 11 multifloral honeys and later as 15 multifloral and 46 monofloral. Southeast is listed twice, with different county lists. Those conflicts do not change the gallium statement, which is that no sample had a detectable result, but they do limit how far the sampling frame can be quoted. The limit of quantification is printed in µ/L while the gallium column is labeled µg/kg.
