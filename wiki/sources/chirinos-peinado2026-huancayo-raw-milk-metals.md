# Risk Assessment of Lead and Cadmium Exposure Through Raw Milk Consumption from Small-Scale Dairy Systems in the Central Peruvian Andes

## Summary

This journal article measured lead and cadmium in smallholder raw cow milk from Huancayo, Peru. n=45. The overall means are µg/kg. One district sentence prints a cadmium mean in mg/kg while Table 3 headers µg/kg and prints the same digits. Both are kept. District rows are not all copied.

## Key numbers

Lead 11.30±18.94 µg/kg. Cadmium 7.85±18.11 µg/kg. The text says the El Tambo cadmium mean is 33.58 mg/kg. Table 3 is headed µg/kg and prints 33.58. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
