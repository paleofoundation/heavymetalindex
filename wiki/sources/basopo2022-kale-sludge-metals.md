# A comparison of selected heavy metals in soils mixed with domestic and industrial sludges and assessment of effects of the sludge pollutants on oxidative stress markers of the African kale grown using sewage sludge manure

## Summary

Basopo, Ndebele, and Chitsa grew African kale (Brassica oleracea var. acephala) for 60 days on soil mixed with domestic or industrial sewage sludge in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, and measured cadmium, copper, and zinc in leaves by atomic absorption spectrophotometry. This is a field trial on sludge-amended soil, not a market survey. Soil Table 2 is not a kale concentration. Unit mg/kg. Wet or dry basis: Data gap. Plant replicate count: Data gap.
