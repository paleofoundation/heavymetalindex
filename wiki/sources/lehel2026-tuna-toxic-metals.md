# Food safety evaluation of potential human exposure to toxic metals in tuna species

## Summary

This journal article measured arsenic, cadmium, mercury, and lead by ICP-MS in yellowfin and bluefin tuna from the Hungarian market. n=40 in each species group. Wet or dry basis: Data gap. Arsenic and mercury are not speciated in the means copied here.

## Key numbers

Yellowfin averages: arsenic 2.23, cadmium 0.01, mercury 0.22, and lead 0.29 mg/kg. Bluefin averages: arsenic 1.26, cadmium below 0.004, mercury 0.47, and lead 0.28 mg/kg. Nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
