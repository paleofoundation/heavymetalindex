# Hengqin and Macao consumer bodies 2025: Baby diaper comparative test

The Hengqin Guangdong-Macao consumer association and the Macao SAR Consumer Council tested 20 baby diapers and pull-up pants (10 of each; 8 bought in Macao and 12 in mainland China) against GB/T 28004.1-2021. The report states that lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury were not detected in any of the 20 samples. It does not print a concentration, a detection limit, or a per-sample metal result.

## Key numbers

No numeric metal value is printed. The metal finding is qualitative: Pb, As, Cd, and Hg were not detected in all 20 samples. Formaldehyde was not detected. Phthalates were not detected. pH was 6.0 to 6.2. The report says the standard interval for pH is 4.0 to 7.5. Mass deviation was from -4% to +5%, against a stated standard tolerance of ±10%. Mass deviation is a performance result, not a metal result.

Sample codes such as A8, A10, B8, and B10 appear in the report for non-metal observations. Product names are not repeated here.

The primary text of GB/T 28004.1-2021 was not in this corpus, so its numeric metal limits are not quoted from this news report.

## Methods (brief)

The report says samples were tested against GB/T 28004.1-2021. It does not describe digestion, extraction, instrumentation, or a limit of detection for the four metals. Arsenic and mercury species are not stated. A not-detected statement without a printed limit cannot be read as a concentration of zero.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports a 2025 Macao and mainland comparative-test statement that four metals were not detected in 20 diapers and pull-up pants. It does not support a microgram-per-kilogram value, a pass-rate against a named numeric limit, or a brand comparison.

## Limitations

Chinese web print. No per-sample table of metals. No detection limit. Species of arsenic and mercury not stated. The standard's own metal numbers are not in this file.
