# Mercury and lead levels in common soaps from local markets in Mashhad, Iran

## Summary

This journal article measured lead and mercury by graphite-furnace atomic absorption spectrophotometry in soaps from markets in Mashhad, Iran. The label is bathing soaps. Table 1 is µg/g. Category means are copied. Product names are omitted. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis: Data gap.

## Key numbers

Table 1 lead: cosmetic 0.10±0.01, hygiene 0.19±0.04, contraband 0.13±0.04 µg/g. Mercury: 0.02±0.004, 0.08±0.013, and 0.23±0.122 µg/g for those same categories. Cadmium, arsenic, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
