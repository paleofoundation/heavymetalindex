# Finger 2024: Yttrium-90 eye plaque radiotherapy for iris melanoma

This single-center case series describes six patients with small iris or iridociliary melanomas treated with a high-dose-rate yttrium-90 plaque in a single outpatient procedure. Treatment times averaged under ten minutes, and no serious ocular side effects or metastases were seen over about 16 months of follow-up. Yttrium here is a beta-emitting radioisotope used as a radiation source.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to yttrium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Clinical case series with tumor staging, slit-lamp and gonio photography, and high-frequency ultrasound to track tumor size and side effects.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: a radiotherapy case series that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for stable yttrium.

## Limitations

Six patients and short follow-up. The author discloses patents, royalties, stock and executive roles in the company that holds the applicator patent.
