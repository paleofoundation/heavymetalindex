# Toxic elements in traditional kohl-based eye cosmetics in Spanish and German markets

## Summary

This journal article measured toxic elements in 12 kohl-based eye cosmetics (powder, paste, and related presentations) from Spanish and German shops. The abstract reports ppm. Antimony is measured and is not a target metal on this row. Brand names are omitted.

## Key numbers

Abstract: lead ranged between 1.7 and 410,000 ppm in six of the study samples. Two products reached arsenic levels of 9.2 and 12.6 ppm. Cadmium in one product was 20.7 ppm, and two other products were 11.8 and 12.7 ppm. Mercury, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
