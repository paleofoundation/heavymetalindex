# Ahmed et al. 2021 -- Cobalt and lead in 41 cosmetics from Saudi Arabia markets including 9 kajal (kohl) samples

## Overview

This study from King Faisal University (Saudi Arabia) and the Sudan Atomic Energy Commission measured cobalt (Co) and lead (Pb) in 41 cosmetic products of four types purchased from local markets in Saudi Arabia: henna (lawsonia inermis), foundation cream, kajal (kohl), and skin-whitening cream. Products were analyzed by atomic absorption spectroscopy (AAS). Quality control was performed using the IAEA-V-10 hay powder standard reference material, with reported recoveries of 115% for Co and 119% for Pb.

Evidence tier is B: peer-reviewed open-access journal, transparent AAS methods with CRM QC, but only two metals measured (Co and Pb) and no speciation reported; sample selection method and market locations are not described in detail.

## Key numbers

### Kajal (kohl) results, Table 3 of the source

Kajal samples: n=9, purchased in Saudi Arabia. Two brand groups (designated M and N in the study) were analyzed.

| Sample group | Co (micrograms per gram) | Pb (micrograms per gram) |
|---|---|---|
| Brand M | 41.33 plus or minus 2.50 | 3.83 plus or minus 0.66 |
| Brand N | 36.71 plus or minus 4.35 | 10.60 plus or minus 1.24 |
| Combined mean for kajal | 39.02 plus or minus 4.06 | 7.22 plus or minus 3.81 |

The combined Pb mean of 7.22 micrograms per gram falls below the FDA's 10 microgram per gram guideline for cosmetics. However, the brand N mean (10.60 micrograms per gram) is above that threshold.

### Exceedance rates, as stated in the source text

- Lead: 7 of 9 kajal samples (78%) had Pb between 1 and 10 micrograms per gram. 2 of 9 (22%) had Pb above 10 micrograms per gram, exceeding the FDA guideline. No sample in any other product type exceeded 10 micrograms per gram.
- Cobalt: 100% of kajal samples exceeded a proposed limit of 5 micrograms per gram. The source compares these results against a proposed Co limit from Iwegbue et al. (2010) because there is no FDA or WHO cobalt limit for cosmetics at the time of publication.

### Results for all four product types combined

Overall Co concentration range across all 41 samples: 21.14 plus or minus 3.70 to 144.91 plus or minus 2.27 micrograms per gram. Overall Pb concentration range: 0.75 plus or minus 0.00 to 10.60 plus or minus 1.24 micrograms per gram. The highest Pb value in the entire dataset (10.60 micrograms per gram) was in a kajal sample.

## Regulatory context stated by the source

The FDA's permissible range for cosmetics is listed in the source as 10-20 micrograms per gram for Pb in all four product types. The Co limit of 5 micrograms per gram is not an FDA limit but a proposed limit from the peer-reviewed literature. The source does not state a Saudi Arabia-specific regulatory limit for these metals.

## Analytical notes

AAS method used; CRM recovery approximately 115-119% for both analytes. Only Co and Pb were measured; no other metals (As, Cd, Hg) were analyzed. Sample n for kajal was 9 across two brand groups; individual sample counts per brand are not unambiguously reported in the text extraction. Pb concentration units are stated as micrograms per gram (equivalent to ppm or mg/kg).
