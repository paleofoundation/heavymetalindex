# Schmidt et al. 2025 -- Microbial bioindicators of cadmium uptake in cacao genotypes, Trinidad

## Overview

This US-Trinidad study used amplicon and metagenomic sequencing to identify soil microbial bioindicators associated with cadmium (Cd) accumulation in leaf and stem tissue of sixteen field-grown Theobroma cacao genotypes at the International Cocoa Genebank (ICGT) in Trinidad. The two highest cadmium-accumulating genotypes (PA 32 and TRD 94) showed enrichment of four amplicon sequence variants (ASVs) belonging to the genus Haliangium, the family Gemmataceae, and the order Polyporales. ASVs negatively associated with plant Cd were identified as Paenibacillus sp. (beta = -2.21), Candidatus Koribacter (beta = -2.17), and Candidatus Solibacter (beta = -2.03) for prokaryotes, and Eurotiomycetes (beta = -4.58) for fungi. Only two ASVs were associated with both leaf and stem Cd, both belonging to the Ktedonobacterales.

## Key numbers

Leaf Cd content ranged from 0.0023 to 0.0754 mg across the 16 genotypes (n=48 trees). Stem Cd content ranged from 0.00032 to 0.016 mg. The two highest-accumulating genotypes (PA 32 and TRD 94) showed significantly higher leaf and stem Cd than the lowest-accumulating genotypes (SLC 18, GS 10, LX 44). ICP-AES detection limit: 0.1 mg/kg. Soil sampled at 0 to 20 cm depth from drip-zone around each tree; soil Cd measured alongside plant tissue. Microbial analysis: 5,543 Cd-associated gene families, including 68 genes related to binding and transport of divalent heavy metals identified through metagenomic GO term annotation. PERMANOVA analysis: microbial community composition differed significantly by genotype for both prokaryotes and fungi.

Genotype accessions studied: SLC 18, GS 10, LX 44, IMC 103, IMC 14, IMC 58, LCT EEN 368, IMC 33, NA 154, PA 32 (high), CLM 35, TRD 94 (high), GU 351/P (high), GU 255/P (high), POUND 31/C (high). Classification based on field studies in Lewis et al. 2018.

## Methods

Soil collected March 2021 at 0 to 20 cm from drip zone of 3 trees per accession (n=48). Leaf and stem samples collected October 2021 at late interflush-2 stage. Cd in soil and plants measured by ICP-AES (UC Davis). Microbial DNA extracted from 0.75 g soil using DNeasy PowerSoil Pro kit. Amplicon sequencing (V4 region 16S rRNA for prokaryotes, ITS1/ITS2 for fungi) by Novogene. Metagenomic sequencing to 12 GB depth per sample. Statistical analysis: MaAsLin2 for associations between ASVs and plant Cd concentrations; PERMANOVA for community composition differences.

## Implications

The identification of soil microbial taxa as bioindicators of cacao Cd accumulation provides a novel screening approach for identifying high- vs. low-accumulating planting stock prior to field deployment. The finding that taxa in the Ktedonobacterales associate with both leaf and stem Cd simultaneously may indicate organisms with broad roles in Cd mobilization across plant tissues. The negative associations with Paenibacillus and Candidatus Solibacter/Koribacter are consistent with plant-growth-promoting rhizobacteria that may reduce Cd availability at the root-soil interface.
