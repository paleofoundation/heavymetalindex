# Silva et al. 2026: GSTP1 rs1695 and mercury memory performance in Suruí Indigenous people

## Summary

This cross-sectional study enrolled 113 Paiter-Suruí Indigenous adults from the Sete de Setembro Indigenous Land in Rondônia, Brazilian Amazon (fieldwork April to May 2023). Hair mercury ranged from 0.1 to 6.5 micrograms per gram (median 1 microgram per gram; interquartile range 1.43). The GSTP1 rs1695 polymorphism was genotyped from oral mucosa cells using TaqMan PCR. The study addressed three aims: the association between hair mercury and neurological impairment; the association between GSTP1 rs1695 genotype and hair mercury; and whether GSTP1 rs1695 modifies the effect of mercury on neurotoxicity signs. Individuals with impaired memory had significantly higher mercury levels (beta = 4.39) and those with impaired muscle strength also had higher mercury (beta = 1.24). Carriers of the GSTP1 AA genotype showed a 0.46-point reduction in memory score per 1 microgram per gram increase in hair mercury compared to GSTP1 GG carriers (beta = -0.46), indicating that the AA (wild-type) genotype is associated with better cognitive protection at equivalent mercury exposure.

## Evidence Fitness

Modeled or limited evidence. Cross-sectional design with 113 participants from a single Indigenous community; convenience sample. Hair mercury reflects chronic methylmercury exposure from fish consumption and ASGM. The finding that GSTP1 AA genotype modifies the mercury-memory association is clinically relevant but limited by sample size and lack of adjustment for all potential confounders. The study is cited for the GSTP1 rs1695 effect modification on mercury neurotoxicity in this population.

## Methods (brief)

Cross-sectional; Paiter-Suruí adults aged over 12 years from Lapetanha Village and five neighboring villages who underwent neurological evaluation and provided hair and oral mucosa samples. Hair mercury analyzed at LAPESF, Rio de Janeiro State University (method not specified in excerpts; standard occipital collection from scalp). Cognitive assessment using Brief Cognitive Screening Battery (BCSB; cutoff less than 6) and semantic verbal fluency test (S-VFT; cutoff less than 9). Motor and somatosensory assessments by standardized neurological examination protocol. GSTP1 rs1695 genotyped from oral mucosa cells by TaqMan PCR. Limitation: cross-sectional design cannot establish causality; convenience sample; LOD/LOQ for mercury analysis not specified in excerpts.
