# Kohl: a lead-hazardous eye makeup from the Third World to the First World

## Summary

This journal article measured lead and antimony in 22 kohl samples purchased in London, Rabat, and the United States. The PDF printing does not show a DOI line. The identifier used here is the Environmental Health Perspectives volume DOI for volume 94, pages 121 to 123 (1991). Lead is reported in percent. Antimony is not a target metal on this row. Brand names are omitted.

## Key numbers

Of 22 samples, 7 exceeded 50 percent lead, 5 contained more than 20 percent lead, 1 contained 3.31 percent lead, and 9 contained less than 0.6 percent lead. Sample 1, Rabat, is printed at 69.6 percent lead. Cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
