# Sørensen et al. (Danish EPA) 2014. Nanosized anatase titanium dioxide in consumer products

This Danish EPA project mapped nano anatase titanium dioxide in sunscreens and photocatalytic paints and reviewed its toxicity. More than 30 sunscreen brands were on the Danish market, with a trend away from titanium dioxide towards chemical filters among Danish producers. An expert tested seven titanium dioxide sunscreens and found that two used anatase as the primary crystal form and the rest pure rutile; the anatase-based products were more photocatalytic.

## Key numbers

- Seven sunscreens tested for crystal form; two primarily anatase, five pure rutile (PDF pp. 7-8).
- Uncoated anatase pigments retain about 0.3 % niobium pentoxide and 0.3 % phosphorus pentoxide from the ore and 0.2 % alumina added in manufacture; food specifications require at least 99.0 % purity (PDF p. 26, citing IARC 2010 and Kuznesof 2006).

No lead, cadmium, arsenic or mercury values are reported.

## Methods (brief)

Market survey by questionnaire to companies and an expert, and a literature review of toxicity. Crystal-form testing method and product identities are not tabulated.

## Evidence fitness

Relevant to the EU Annex VI condition that nano titanium dioxide be rutile or rutile with up to 5 % anatase. It provides no heavy-metal data.

## Limitations

Seven products, method not reported, Danish market only, no infant products identified.
