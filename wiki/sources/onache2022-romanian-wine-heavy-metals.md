# Onache et al. 2022, metals in Romanian wine, white prose figures only

This paper measured metals in soil and in wine from Romanian wine-growing areas. Soil tables are not wine. Tables 5 to 8 did not yield a usable numeric grid in the text layer, so only white-cultivar figures printed in prose are copied. Red-wine prose is excluded. No recovery percentage or certified reference material result is printed. A blank and one quality-control point are mentioned.

## Key numbers

Wine metals other than iron, manganese, and zinc are expressed in μg/l. European decimal commas are kept as printed.

| White cultivar and place | Metal | Printed figure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feteasca Alba, Iasi | Lead | 37,77 μg/l in the Table 6 discussion, and 37,76 μg/l in the conclusions |
| Chardonnay, Babadag | Lead | 9,98 μg/l |
| Feteasca Alba, Stefanesti | Lead | 4,055 μg/l |
| Chardonnay, printed Cardonnay, Babadag | Nickel | 298,945 μg/l |
| Feteasca Alba, Stefanesti | Nickel | 3,465 μg/l |

No white-wine chromium, arsenic, or mercury figure is printed. The all-wine lead span is not white-only and is not used.

## Methods (brief)

Wine was digested and measured by ICP-MS. Quality assurance is limited to a calibration curve, one quality-control point, and blanks.

## Evidence fitness

The prose figures support a narrow white-wine lead and nickel note. They do not support a full white-wine metal table.

## Limitations

No recovery percentage. Table grids were not recoverable as text. Decimal commas are the printed notation.
