# Plaisen et al. 2018 : Gold nanoparticle anti-aggregation colorimetric assay for bromide in rice

## Overview

Plaisen and colleagues developed a citrate-capped gold nanoparticle (AuNP) anti-aggregation colorimetric assay for the detection of bromide in rice. Bromide prevents Cr3+-induced AuNP aggregation, maintaining the red color and enabling spectrophotometric quantification. Gold is the analytical reagent; bromide is the target analyte. The method was validated in spiked white, brown, and black rice samples.

## Key numbers

LOD: 0.012 mg/kg. LOQ: 0.04 mg/kg. Calibration range: 0.31 to 3.75 mM Br-. Recoveries: 79.9 to 92.2%. RSD: less than 2%.

## Implications for the wiki

This paper contributes to [[metals/gold]] as documentation of AuNP-based sensing platforms for anion detection in rice food matrices. No food occurrence data for gold or any heavy metal are provided.
