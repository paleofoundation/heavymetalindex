# Coppola et al. 2026 : Serum trace metals and dietary patterns in the SPES biomonitoring cohort, Campania, Italy

This 2026 cross-sectional biomonitoring study measured serum concentrations of five trace metals (As, Hg, Cd, Pb, Ni) in 4,137 adults from Campania, Italy, and modelled associations with 15 dietary food groups using a mixed-effects log-linear regression framework with FDR correction. Total arsenic (As-75 by ICP-MS) was measured in serum without speciation; the authors explicitly note that the seafood association likely reflects arsenobetaine from organic arsenic species rather than inorganic arsenic.

## Key numbers

- Serum tAs (completed-data, n=4137): geometric mean 0.988 µg/L; median 1.49 µg/L; P95 11 µg/L
- LOQ for serum As-75: 0.0015 µg/L; detection frequency >93%
- Seafood density (fish + crustaceans/molluscs), per IQR: GMR 1.238 (95% CI 1.147-1.335), 23.8% higher serum tAs; FDR q = 2.38 x 10-6
- Fish sentinel, per IQR: GMR 1.148 (95% CI 1.064-1.238); q = 0.005
- Crustaceans/molluscs, per IQR: GMR 1.140 (95% CI 1.066-1.220); q = 0.002
- Mercury (Hg-202): GM 0.745 µg/L; P95 7.21 µg/L; associated with seafood
- No FDR-significant dietary associations identified for Cd, Pb, or Ni
- Rice and water: no primary-family FDR-significant association with serum tAs

## Methods

NexION 350 ICP-MS (PerkinElmer); 20-channel panel; 500 µL serum diluted 1:10 in nitric acid diluent; Y/Rh/Lu internal standards; Italian EPIC food-frequency questionnaire with 15 food groups; mixed-effects log-linear regression with municipal-cluster random intercepts; FDR control by Benjamini-Hochberg; left-censoring handled with Tobit and interval-censored Gamma models.

## Implications

Provides population-level serum tAs biomarker data for a large Italian adult cohort, with the clearest dietary signal from seafood. Because speciation was not performed, the association most likely reflects arsenobetaine from fish and crustaceans rather than inorganic arsenic. The absence of a rice or water association in this Mediterranean population with relatively low rice consumption is consistent with seafood being the dominant tAs dietary source for this cohort. The seafood-serum tAs association (GMR 1.24 per IQR increase) quantifies the biomonitoring impact of habitual seafood consumption at a population scale.
