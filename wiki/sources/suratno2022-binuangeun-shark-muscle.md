# Heavy Metals Concentration in Muscle Tissue of Threatened Sharks (Rhizoprionodon acutus, Sphyrna lewini, and Squallus hemipinnis) from Binuangeun, Lebak Banten, Indonesia

## Summary

Suratno and colleagues measured metals by ICP-MS in dorsal muscle of three shark species bought at auction in Binuangeun, Lebak, Banten. Table 2 is ug/g wet weight after a moisture conversion whose factor is not printed. The title spells Squallus. The body spells Squalus hemipinnis. The conclusion calls the arsenic result total arsenic. Species of arsenic were not measured on these samples.

## Key numbers

Table 2 averages, ug/g wet weight: R. acutus n=16, arsenic 9.60 (1.57 to 23.49), cadmium 0.004 (0.002 to 0.006), lead 0.060 (0.009 to 0.140), nickel 0.037 (0.001 to 0.139). S. hemipinnis n=6, arsenic 9.57 (3.73 to 13.50), lead 0.036 (0.003 to 0.063), nickel and cadmium below LoD. S. lewini n=8, arsenic 3.93 (3.00 to 5.85), lead 0.006 (0.001 to 0.021), nickel 0.037 (0.011 to 0.062), cadmium below LoD. Prose also prints arsenic 9.60±8.01, 9.57±3.95, and 3.93±1.08. Mercury, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
