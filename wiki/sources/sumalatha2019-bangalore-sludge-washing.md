# Sumalatha et al. 2019 — Removal of Heavy Metals from Industrial Sludge Using Soil Washing Technique

Column washing of Bangalore-area industrial sludge removed substantially more metals with hydrochloric acid plus EDTA than with distilled water. This is a remediation experiment relevant to sludge contamination pathways, with no measurements in food or a demonstrated safe land-application endpoint.

## Methods (brief)

Sludge collected November 2013 and analyzed using USEPA 3050 B. Air-dried, 2 mm-sieved material compacted to 0.98 g/cm³ and 33.5% water content; pore volume 56.55 cm³. Columns compared distilled water against 0.1 N HCl+0.1 N EDTA. Transport parameters fitted to experimental elution curves. Sampling and analytical-replicate counts and numeric detection limits are not provided.

## Implications

Supports laboratory washing and contaminant-transport comparisons. Metal-rich leachate still requires management; the study does not establish agricultural reuse or field-scale safety.

Evidence Fitness: EF-2 for source-reported removal and transport estimates; environmental remediation context, with replication and reporting-basis gaps.
