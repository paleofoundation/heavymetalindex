# Assessment of the Risk of Heavy Metals Contamination in Infant Formulas and Children Food Products Available in Gusau, Zamfara State, Nigeria

## Summary

Rabiu and Abubakar measured metals by MP-AES in products bought in Gusau, Nigeria. Fifty brands were purchased. Twenty are infant formula and thirty are children's foods. Only the infant-formula rows are used. The discussion says intake estimates assumed dry (as sold) undiluted formulas. Tables are ug/g.

## Key numbers

Abstract means for infant formula, ug/g: cadmium 2.500, cobalt 0.859, arsenic 0.796, lead 0.563, chromium 0.484, aluminum 1.641. Table 2 age-group means are also printed (0 to 6 months n=6, 6 to 12 months n=8, 12 to 24 months n=6). Mercury, nickel, and tin: Data gap. Arsenic and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
