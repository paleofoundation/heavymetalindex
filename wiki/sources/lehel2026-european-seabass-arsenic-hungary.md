# Lehel et al. 2026 : Potentially toxic elements in European seabass marketed in Hungary

This 2026 occurrence and consumer risk characterisation study measured total arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury in 40 European seabass (Dicentrarchus labrax) dorsal muscle samples purchased at a Budapest market in March 2023. All results are expressed as mg/kg wet weight. Total arsenic was detected in 92.5% of samples; speciation was not performed and iAs was conservatively estimated at 5% of tAs for risk calculation purposes.

## Key numbers

- tAs: detected in 37/40 samples (92.5%); mean 1.44 ± 0.90 mg/kg ww; LOD 0.50 mg/kg; LOQ 1.67 mg/kg
- Estimated iAs (5% of tAs, conservative): mean 0.071 mg/kg ww; under this assumption 8/40 samples (20%) potentially exceeded EU iAs ML of 0.1 mg/kg (EU Reg. 2025/1891)
- Cd: detected in 14/40 (35%); mean 0.06 ± 0.02 mg/kg ww; 13/40 (32.5%) exceeded EU ML 0.05 mg/kg
- Pb: detected in 11/40 (27.5%); mean 0.25 ± 0.14 mg/kg ww; 6/40 (15%) exceeded EU ML 0.30 mg/kg
- Hg: all results below method LOD (0.50 mg/kg)
- Hazard index at mean EU adult consumption (65.7 g/day, 70 kg): HI = 1.32 (MB), driven by Pb (THQ Pb = 1.056)
- iAs EDI (mean adult): 0.067 µg/kg BW/day; THQ 0.223
- Spike recoveries: As 102%; Cd 98.2%; Hg 105.1%; Pb 96.6%

## Methods

ICP-OES (Perkin Elmer Optima 8300 DV); microwave digestion (CEM MARS6, HNO3 + H2O2, 200 degrees C); Y internal standard (1 mg/L); Au for Hg stabilisation; CRM NIST SRM 1577c (bovine liver). Risk characterisation used LB/MB/UB scenarios for below-LOD handling.

## Implications

Provides total arsenic occurrence data for European seabass from the Hungarian market, with Adriatic Sea provenance. The 92.5% detection rate and mean tAs of 1.44 mg/kg are consistent with known organic arsenic accumulation in marine fish muscle; because speciation was not performed, the actual iAs fraction cannot be determined directly from this study. The 32.5% Cd exceedance rate and the near-threshold Pb values indicate that seabass from this source merits continued monitoring for Cd and Pb beyond arsenic.
