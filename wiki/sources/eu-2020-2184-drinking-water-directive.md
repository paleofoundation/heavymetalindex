# Directive (EU) 2020/2184, antimony parametric value

Directive (EU) 2020/2184 of 16 December 2020 on the quality of water intended for human consumption (recast), OJ L 435, 23 December 2020. Annex I, Part B sets chemical parametric values. Antimony is one of them.

## Key numbers

Annex I, Part B prints Antimony 10 μg/l (PDF p.35, OJ L 435/35). Annex III, Part B, Table 1 prints the uncertainty of measurement for antimony as 40 percent of the parametric value (PDF p.51). The unit on the parametric value is μg/l, as printed. Article 24 sets a transposition deadline of 12 January 2023.

## Methods (brief)

The file is the OJ text as published. No later consolidated edition was opened for this parameter. The basis is a parametric value for water intended for human consumption, not a migration limit and not a product-content limit.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports the parametric value of 10 μg/l and the 40 percent measurement-uncertainty figure. It does not support a diaper limit, a textile limit, or a claim that 10 μg/l is the same number as the U.S. maximum of 0.006 mg/L.

## Limitations

Member-state transposition can add detail this directive does not print. This page does not record national implementing rules.
