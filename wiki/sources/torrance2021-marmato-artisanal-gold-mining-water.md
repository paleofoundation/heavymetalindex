# Torrance et al. 2021: Artisanal gold mining and water quality in Colombia

This study assessed water quality around artisanal gold mining in Marmato, Colombia. Gallium and indium were present at detectable and elevated levels, which the authors attribute to substitution in the zinc sulfide mineral sphalerite.

## Key numbers

- Gallium was present at detectable levels and described as elevated in mining-affected waters; the authors link it to sphalerite and marmatite in the ore (Discussion, p. 11).

## Methods (brief)

Water samples were analyzed for dissolved metals by ICP-MS.

## Evidence fitness

Supports a qualitative statement that zinc sulfide ore processing can release gallium to water. The main text gives no gallium value used on this page.

## Limitations

Gallium is discussed only briefly; quantitative values were not extracted here.
