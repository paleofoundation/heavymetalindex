# The assessment of lead concentration in raw milk collected from some major dairy farms in Iran and evaluation of associated health risk

## Summary

This journal article measured lead by graphite-furnace atomic absorption spectrophotometry in raw cow milk from farm storage tanks in Tehran. n=57. Table 2 and the prose do not print the same high value. Both are kept.

## Key numbers

Table 2 lead 0.012±0.005 ppm. The prose prints a highest lead of 0.19 and also 0.02 mg/mL. Cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
