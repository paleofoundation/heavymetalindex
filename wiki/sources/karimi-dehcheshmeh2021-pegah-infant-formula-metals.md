# Evaluation of Aflatoxin M1 and Heavy Metal in Raw Materials and Infant Formula Produced in Pegah Dairy Plants, IRAN

## Summary

Karimi Dehcheshmeh, Shakerian, and Rahimi measured lead and cadmium by graphite-furnace atomic absorption spectrophotometry in raw materials and finished formula from Pegah Dairy Plants, Shahrekord, Iran, collected in 2018 to 2019. The journal year is 2021. Tables say ppb. Samples were dried and ashed. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis of the ppb values: Data gap. Aflatoxin is not copied.

## Key numbers

Table 4 finished formula, column order mean cadmium, mean lead, ppb, and every row prints n=80: infant formula 4.97±3.08 and 12.57±2.89; immediate 5.56±0.51 and 13.97±4.096; whole 2.88±0.808 and 10.10±1.15; skim 8.01±0.697 and 14.53±1.59. Prose assigns lead and cadmium in the opposite order on several rows, including maltodextrin 4.91 and 18.97 ppb. Both assignments are printed. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
