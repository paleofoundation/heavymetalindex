# Chang and Nakrani 2014. Methylisothiazolinone in baby wipes

Chang and Nakrani report six children with allergic contact dermatitis linked to methylisothiazolinone in baby wipes. The paper is a clinical series about a preservative. It does not report concentrations of lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, or other metals.

## Key numbers

No heavy-metal concentration is reported. The sample is six children. Methylisothiazolinone is the named allergen.

## Methods (brief)

Case series in Pediatrics, 2014.

## Evidence fitness

The paper can document that baby wipes are a preservative-allergy matrix. It cannot support a metal occurrence claim.

## Limitations

No laboratory metal data. Brand names of wipes, if present in the cases, are not repeated.
