# Heavy metals in leathers, artificial leathers, and textiles in the context of quality and safety of use

## Summary

This journal article measured arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, and zinc in acidic-sweat extracts of leathers, artificial leathers, and textiles. Samples were dried to constant mass before extraction. Results are mg/kg of that dried material. These are leathers and textiles, not laundry combination products. Chromium is not speciated. n=9 materials (A1 to A3, B1 to B3, C1 to C3). Cited OEKO-TEX and REACH limits are not results.

## Key numbers

Leather chromium: A1 401.6, A2 461.4, A3 228.6 mg/kg. The abstract says lining and upper leather chromium was above 228.0 mg/kg. Textile C1 lead is 0.003 mg/kg. Mercury, nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
