# Savici et al. 2026: Multi-generational reproductive toxicity of potassium dichromate in rats

## Summary

This three-generation rat study assessed the effects of Cr(VI) on sperm quality and testicular histopathology across F0, F1, and F2 generations. Male and female Wistar rats received potassium dichromate in drinking water at 25, 50, or 75 mg/L continuously across generations; controls received chromium-free water. Cr(VI) produced dose- and generation-dependent impairment of sperm quality and testicular architecture, with the most pronounced effects in the F2 generation at the highest dose. The findings demonstrate cumulative multigenerational reproductive toxicity and are cited on the hexavalent chromium wiki page for the reproductive toxicity section.

Doses: 25 mg/L (E1), 50 mg/L (E2), 75 mg/L (E3) potassium dichromate in drinking water. Sample size: n = 7 males per group per generation. Sperm from vas deferens; motility and concentration by CASA (HTM-IVOS v12.3); morphology from 200 cells per sample stained with Eosin; histology scored by two blinded pathologists.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only. This is an animal study at doses (25 to 75 mg/L potassium dichromate in drinking water) substantially above typical human water exposure levels and designed to characterise dose-response and multigenerational accumulation in an animal model. The study documents Cr(VI) as a reproductive toxicant with cumulative multigenerational effects. It does not translate directly to human dietary exposure estimates but provides mechanistic context for the reproductive toxicity hazard. Findings are cited as toxicological context in the hexavalent chromium wiki page.

## Methods (brief)

Wistar rats (F0: 28 male and 28 female, 220 to 240 g). Cr source: potassium dichromate from Sigma-Aldrich (99% or higher purity), diluted to 25, 50, or 75 mg/L in distilled water. Mating 1:1 (confirmed by vaginal plug). Sperm collected from vas deferens at euthanasia; CASA analysis (HTM-IVOS v12.3) on Leja 4-chamber slides; progressive spermatozoa defined as VAP 50.0 microm/s or greater and STR 80.0% or greater. Morphology from eosin smears, 200 cells per sample. Histology: H.E. staining, 5 microm paraffin sections; testicular injury graded by modified Cosentino system (0 to 4) and Johnsen score (1 to 10) by two blinded pathologists. LOD/LOQ, CRM and recovery are not reported; this is a histological and sperm motility study rather than an analytical measurement study. Ethical approval: Ethical Committee of Banat University of Agricultural Sciences and Veterinary Medicine "King Michael I of Romania" Timisoara (No. 60/23.07.2019); animal handling in compliance with EU Directive 2010/63/EU.
