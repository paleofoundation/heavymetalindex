# Perveen et al. 2025: Alginate-sodium ferrite nanocomposite DSPE for metals in water and juices

## Overview

Pakistani-Turkish analytical chemistry study developing a dispersive solid-phase extraction method using alginate-integrated sodium ferrite nanocomposite (AG/Na2Fe4O7-NC) for trace determination of cadmium, cobalt, copper, manganese, nickel, and lead in water and juice samples by FAAS. Recovery range 97.1-98.7%. Validated against certified reference material (SPS Waste Water Level 2).

## Scope and use

This is a method development paper. It does not report unspiked analyte concentrations in real juice samples; all juice data are spike-recovery experiments. The source is included here as analytical-method context for trace-metal determination in commercial fruit-juice matrices in Turkey. It does not contribute occurrence data to the infant-juice product page. The LOD values (Pb 0.1 ng/mL = 0.1 µg/L) are relevant context for understanding method sensitivity.
