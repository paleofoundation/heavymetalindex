# Uddin et al. 2026: A rice mutant with low grain zinc and cadmium

This Bangladeshi and Japanese study screened brown rice from 8503 mutagenized plants of the indica cultivar BRRI dhan84 by ICP-MS and isolated 38 mutants with altered grain iron, zinc, arsenic or cadmium. One mutant, in the OsHMA2 transporter gene, had lower grain zinc and cadmium and grew normally in a paddy field. The authors note it was sensitive to zinc-deficient conditions.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zinc exposure in its text; grain zinc values appear only in figures (Figures 3 and 5).

## Methods (brief)

Seeds were mutagenized with ethyl methanesulfonate or gamma rays, and brown rice from M2 plants was analyzed by ICP-MS. Candidate mutants were chosen by robust Z-scores, and causal genes were identified by whole genome sequencing and F2 segregation.

## Evidence fitness

The paper supports the point that, in rice, genes that move cadmium into grain also move zinc, so a low-cadmium allele can lower grain zinc. It does not provide market or dietary zinc data.

## Limitations

Zinc values are graphical only. Field results come from one Bangladeshi university site with low-zinc soil.
