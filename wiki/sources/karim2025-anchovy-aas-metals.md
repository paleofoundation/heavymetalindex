# Assessment of Heavy Metal Contents in Anchovies Using Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy

## Summary

This colloquium extended abstract, not a peer-reviewed journal article, reports iron and cadmium by atomic absorption spectroscopy in anchovies from four shops in Tapah, Perak. About 100 g was taken from each shop and dried at 60 C. Fish count: Data gap. The abstract uses ppm. Table 1 prints mg/L and a converted mg/kg column.

## Key numbers

Abstract and Table 1 mg/L: iron 4.22, 5.16, 5.88, and 6.72; cadmium 0.06, 0.15, 0.15, and 0.41. The printed mg/kg conversion column runs from 4033.26 to 6617.43 for iron and from 57.34 to 403.74 for cadmium. Lead and the other target metals: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
