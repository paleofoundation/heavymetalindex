# Muthukumaran et al. 2023: A boron nitride sensor for the drug 5-fluorouracil

This method paper made a polythiophene and hexagonal boron nitride nanocomposite electrode for electrochemical detection of the anticancer drug 5-fluorouracil. Boron nitride is the electrode material, not an analyte.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to boron exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Nanocomposite synthesis; electron microscopy, XRD and FT-IR characterization; voltammetric and amperometric sensing.

## Evidence fitness

Boron appears here only as a component of an engineered material. The paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for boron.

## Limitations

Off-topic for boron exposure; no boron measurements in food, water or people. Supported by SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
