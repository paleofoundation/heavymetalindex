# Mineral and heavy metal by inductively coupled plasma optical emission spectrometer in traditional Turkish yogurts

## Summary

Tarakci and Dag measured minerals by ICP-OES in 15 traditional yogurts from Ordu, Turkey. Analyses were in duplicate. Table 2 is mg/kg. The samples were ashed. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis is not stated.

## Key numbers

Table 2, mg/kg, n=15: chromium minimum 0.013, maximum 0.021, mean 0.017; aluminum 15.61 to 20.83, mean 18.11; nickel 0.036 to 0.059, mean 0.050; lead 0.045 to 0.061, mean 0.055; cadmium 0.0023 to 0.003, mean 0.002. Table 1 copper mean is printed 0,71. Arsenic and mercury: Data gap. Contradictory prose ppm sentences are not used as the table values.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
