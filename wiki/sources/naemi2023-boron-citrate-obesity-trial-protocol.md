# Naemi et al. 2023: Protocol for a boron citrate supplementation trial in obesity

This is a protocol, not a results paper. It describes a planned 12-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 60 adults with obesity, comparing boron citrate capsules containing 10 mg of boron daily with maltodextrin placebo for effects on inflammatory markers, body measures and cardiometabolic factors.

## Key numbers

- Planned dose: boron citrate capsules containing 10 mg of boron once daily for 12 weeks (Methods, Study interventions).
- No outcome data are reported.

## Methods (brief)

Stratified block randomization by age and sex; primary outcomes are CRP, TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-10 and anthropometric indices; diet assessed by 3-day food records. Registered as IRCT20220806055624N1.

## Evidence fitness

Documents a planned supplementation dose and trial design. It does not support efficacy, safety or exposure claims.

## Limitations

No results. The abstract states that no adverse effects of boron have been reported, which is a background claim, not a finding of the trial. Funded by the Endocrine Research Center of Tabriz University of Medical Sciences; no competing interests declared.
