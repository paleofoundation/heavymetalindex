# Auclair et al. 2025 - PGE toxicity in Hydra vulgaris with gene expression profiling

## Overview

This study assessed the toxicity of multiple platinum group elements (Pd, Pt, Rh, Ru, Ir) in Hydra vulgaris over 96 hours at concentrations of 0.3 to 100 ug/L, with gene expression profiling to characterize molecular responses. The work addresses the comparative ecotoxicology of PGEs in a freshwater model organism relevant for environmental risk assessment.

## Key numbers

- Pd concentrations tested: 0.3, 1, 10, 32, and 100 ug/L
- At 10 ug/L: measured Pd concentration was 60% of nominal (sorption/precipitation in test medium)
- At 100 ug/L: Pd recovery after 48h was 87% (Pt recovery: 43%; Rh: 100%)
- Pd molecular weight: 106.4 g/mol (similar to Rh at 102.9 and Ru at 101.1 g/mol)
- Ir and Pt are heavier (192.22 and 195.1 g/mol respectively)
- Gene expression profiling conducted across all PGE treatments

## Methods (brief)

Hydra vulgaris exposed to nominal concentrations of Pd, Pt, Rh, Ru, and Ir for 96 hours. Measured concentrations verified by ICP-MS at 0, 48, and 96 hours. Gene expression profiling by RNA sequencing. Comparative ecotoxicity analysis across all five PGEs tested.

## Implications

This page makes the source discoverable for palladium evidence routing. Provides comparative ecotoxicity data for Pd alongside other PGEs in a freshwater model organism, with gene expression endpoints offering mechanistic insight beyond traditional mortality endpoints.
