# Influence of beech and spruce on potentially toxic elements-related health risk of edible mushrooms growing on unpolluted forest soils

## Summary

This journal article measured cadmium and lead by ETAAS in edible mushrooms from beech and spruce forest in Czechia, dry weight. Methods say 96 samples of eight species. Table 2 prints six species whose n values sum to 94. Both counts are kept.

## Key numbers

Table 2: cadmium in Xerocomellus pruinatus 12.1±8.67 (n=8). Lead in Xerocomellus chrysenteron 3.23±2.93 (n=21). Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
