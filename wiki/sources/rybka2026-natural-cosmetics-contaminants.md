# Rybka et al. 2026 -- Contaminants in 40 natural cosmetics from Poland (Podkarpackie Province, 2023)

## Overview

This study from the University of Rzeszow and the Voivodeship Sanitary and Epidemiology Station in Rzeszow (Poland) measured formaldehyde, mercury, silver(I) nitrate, and microbiological contamination (aerobic mesophilic microorganisms and Staphylococcus aureus) in 40 natural, eco, or reduced-preservative cosmetic products sampled by the State Sanitary Inspectorate in Podkarpackie Province, Poland in 2023. Mercury was measured by atomic absorption spectrometry with amalgamation on a DMA-80 analyzer.

Evidence tier is B: peer-reviewed journal, official regulatory surveillance sampling, transparent AAS method, but only mercury is reported for heavy metals; no lead, arsenic, cadmium, or other metals were measured.

## Key numbers

### Mercury

Mercury concentrations ranged from 0.0000001 plus or minus 0.00000001 percent to 0.0000002 plus or minus 0.00000003 percent by mass (approximately 0.001 to 0.002 mg/kg). All samples were within EU legal limits for mercury in cosmetics. No sample exceeded the EU threshold.

### Formaldehyde and microbiological

Formaldehyde was below the detection limit (less than 0.005%) in all samples intended for skin and hair cleansing. Silver(I) nitrate content in eye products was below 1.57%. The total aerobic mesophilic microorganism count was less than 10 CFU/g in all samples; Staphylococcus aureus was not detected in any sample.

## Context for traditional eye cosmetics

This paper does not test kohl, surma, kajal, or any traditional eye cosmetics. The products are natural and eco-labeled face, body, and hair cosmetics from Poland distributed in 2023 through the standard EU-regulated market. Its relevance to the traditional-eye-cosmetics product page is limited to general context on EU-market cosmetics mercury compliance. The study demonstrates that EU-regulated natural cosmetics in this dataset comply with mercury limits, providing a contrast to the extreme lead levels found in traditional kohl.

## Analytical notes

Only mercury is measured for metals; no lead, arsenic, cadmium, nickel, or other metals are reported. Mercury concentrations are expressed in percent by mass; the values are extremely low (order of 0.001 mg/kg), well within EU limits. Microbiological results all within EU limits. Results apply only to the 40 samples analyzed in this Podkarpackie Province surveillance exercise.
