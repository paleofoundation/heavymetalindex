# Analysis of mercury in skin lightening cream by microwave plasma atomic emission spectroscopy (MP-AES)

## Summary

This journal article measured mercury by MP-AES in skin lightening creams. The metal scope is Hg only. n=16, with 8 samples above the detection limit. The abstract prints the positive range as ng/g. Table 3 headers the same digits as µg/g. Both printings are copied. Brand names are omitted.

## Key numbers

Abstract range 422.61 to 44,960.79 ng/g. Table 3, sample 7, 422.61 µg/g, and sample 12, 44,960.79 µg/g. The discussion also prints 422.61 to 44,960.79 µg/g. Limits of detection and quantification are printed as 15.01 and 50.02 ng/g. Lead, cadmium, arsenic, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
