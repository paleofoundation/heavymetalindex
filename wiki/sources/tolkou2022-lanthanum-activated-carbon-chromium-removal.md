# Tolkou et al. 2022: Lanthanum-modified activated carbon for chromium removal from water

Activated carbon from coconut shells was modified with lanthanum chloride and tested for removing hexavalent chromium from water, with best removal at pH 5 and four hours of contact. About 3% of the lanthanum leached from the material during tests.

## Key numbers

- Lanthanum leaching from the modified carbon about 3% (Section 2, p. 5).

## Methods (brief)

Adsorbent preparation and characterization (BET, FTIR, SEM) with bench-scale adsorption tests in triplicate.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. It indicates lanthanum-modified sorbents can release some lanthanum to water but does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
