# Wang et al. 2025: Soil microbes in selenium mining soils

This study compared soil bacterial and fungal communities across selenium-impacted mining soils in Enshi, China. Soil gallium was highest in control soils and was one of the elements most strongly associated with microbial community composition.

## Key numbers

- Soil gallium, mean ± SD: control 27.76 ± 1.81 mg/kg, low selenium 20.1 ± 2.24 mg/kg, high selenium 10.24 ± 3.59 mg/kg (Table, p. 5).
- Gallium was among the elements significantly correlated with microbial community composition (Mantel test) and among the top predictors of bacterial diversity (random forest) (Results).

## Methods (brief)

Soil chemistry was measured alongside amplicon sequencing of bacterial and fungal communities; associations were tested with Mantel tests and random forest models.

## Evidence fitness

Supports a statistical association between soil gallium and soil microbial communities. Does not support any effect on the human gut microbiome, and correlation does not show cause.

## Limitations

Correlational; gallium co-varied with many other elements.
