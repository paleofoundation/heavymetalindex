# Uchenna and Onyebuchi 2023 - Heavy metals in Ihiala market fruits

This study measured metals in banana, watermelon, apple, and pineapple sold in markets in Ihiala, Anambra State, Nigeria. It provides fresh-fruit context, including a direct pineapple matrix, with arsenic and iron exceeding the comparison limits used by the authors.

## Key numbers

Source units are mg/kg.

- Mean fruit concentrations across sampled fruits: Zn 0.038917; Ni 0.042750; Cu 0.040000; Pb 0.003333; Cr 0.003917; As 0.004833; Fe 1.914750.
- WHO/FAO comparison limits in the source table: Zn 0.300; Ni 0.500; Cu 0.500; Pb 0.100; Cr 0.050; As 0.003; Fe 0.800.
- The source reports Zn, Ni, Cu, Pb, and Cr as within limits and As and Fe as not acceptable against its cited comparison values.
- The study collected 12 fruit samples from three markets plus three unripe banana samples for ripening comparison.

## Methods

The authors used market sampling, questionnaires, and laboratory analysis. Statistical comparisons were made across fruits and markets.

## Implications

The source supports fresh-fruit occurrence context for Nigerian market fruit. The source reports aggregate means rather than a clean pineapple-only table in extractable text, so fruit-specific routing should preserve that limitation.
