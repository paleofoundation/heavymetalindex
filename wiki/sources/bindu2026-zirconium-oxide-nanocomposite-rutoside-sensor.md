# Bindu and Bhat 2026: A zirconium-containing mixed oxide electrode for rutoside

This study made a carbon paste electrode with a creatine-modified zirconium cobalt zinc nickel oxide nanocomposite to detect rutoside, a flavonoid, and tested it on a lemon sample. The analyte is rutoside; zirconium was not measured in any sample.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Combustion synthesis of the nanocomposite, structural characterization, and cyclic, differential pulse and linear sweep voltammetry with impedance spectroscopy.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium in a mixed oxide electrode for sensing another compound; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
