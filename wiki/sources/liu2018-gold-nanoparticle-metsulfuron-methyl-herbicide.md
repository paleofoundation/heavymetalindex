# Liu et al. 2018 : Gold nanoparticle colorimetric sensor for metsulfuron-methyl in agricultural water

## Overview

Liu and colleagues developed a citrate-capped AuNP colorimetric anti-aggregation assay for the detection of metsulfuron-methyl, a widely used sulfonylurea herbicide. In the presence of melamine, metsulfuron-methyl prevents NaCl-induced AuNP aggregation through hydrogen bonding, maintaining the red color. Gold is the analytical probe; metsulfuron-methyl is the target analyte. Validated matrices are agricultural irrigation water, tap water, and well water.

## Key numbers

LOD: 0.05 mg/L. Linear range: 0.1 to 100 mg/L. R2: 0.999. Validated in spiked agricultural irrigation water, tap water, and well water. Best sensitivity at pH above 3.5.

## Implications for the wiki

This paper contributes to [[metals/gold]] as documentation of AuNP colorimetric platforms applied to pesticide/herbicide residue monitoring in agricultural water. No food occurrence data for gold or any heavy metal are provided.
