# Content of selected nutrients and heavy metals in prenatal multivitamins and minerals: an observational study

## Summary

This journal article measured choline, iodine, arsenic, lead, and cadmium in prenatal multivitamin and mineral products. n=47 (32 nonprescription and 15 prescription). Product metal concentrations are in Figure 2 and are not printed as a numeric table in the article text, so they are not copied. Method detection and quantification limits are printed. Brand names are omitted. The iodine and nutrient amounts per serving are not metal concentrations. United States Pharmacopeia daily limits cited in the paper are not product results.

## Key numbers

Method lower detection limits, printed as parts per billion: arsenic 3.58, lead 0.0597, cadmium 0.0934, iodine 0.016. Lower limits of quantification: arsenic 10.75, lead 0.18, cadmium 0.2802, iodine 0.048. Measurable arsenic, lead, and cadmium were found in 7 (14.9 percent), 32 (68.1 percent), and 29 (61.7 percent) of the 47 products. Mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap. Finished-product concentrations: Data gap in the article text.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
