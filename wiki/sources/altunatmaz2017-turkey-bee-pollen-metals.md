# Mineral element and heavy metal (cadmium, lead and arsenic) levels of bee pollen in Turkey

## Summary

Altunatmaz and colleagues measured minerals and heavy metals in 20 bee pollen samples produced in Turkey and 4 imported bee pollen samples sold in Turkey. The paper prints the unit as µg/gpollen.

## Key numbers

The abstract range for pollen produced in Turkey is chromium 0.124 to 1.595, nickel 0.002 to 1.761, cadmium 0.006 to 0.181, lead 0.000 to 0.479, and arsenic 0.006 to 1.035 µg/g. Table 2 means for those 20 samples are chromium 0.793, nickel 0.512, cadmium 0.069, lead 0.193, and arsenic 0.391 µg/g. The imported prose range is lead 0.000 to 0.339, cadmium 0.044 to 0.081, and arsenic 0.008 to 1.054 µg/g, and Table 4 prints those extremes. A combined-sample sentence prints wider ranges, including lead 0.000 to 1.383 µg/g, and no table cell reaches 1.383. Mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Fresh or dry basis: Data gap. Arsenic and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
