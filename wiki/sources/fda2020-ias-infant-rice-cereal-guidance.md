# FDA 2020 - Inorganic arsenic in infant rice cereal guidance

## Overview

This FDA guidance finalizes the action level for inorganic arsenic in rice cereals for infants. It is the guidance document itself, distinct from [[sources/fda2020-iAs-rice-cereal-supporting-doc]], which contains the supporting achievability and risk tables.

## Key numbers

| Parameter | Source value |
| --- | --- |
| Action level | 100 microgram per kilogram (µg/kg), or 100 parts per billion (ppb) |
| Analyte | Inorganic arsenic |
| Matrix | Rice cereals for infants |
| Scope | All types of infant rice cereals, including white-rice, brown-rice, organically grown, and conventionally grown |
| Docket | FDA-2016-D-1099 |
| Guidance date | August 2020 |
| Legal posture | FDA guidance, nonbinding recommendation |

FDA states that it intends to analyze infant rice cereal samples for total arsenic and to speciate samples containing more than 100 µg/kg or 100 ppb total arsenic to determine inorganic arsenic levels. The guidance says FDA considers the 100 µg/kg action level an important source of information for determining whether infant rice cereal is adulterated within the meaning of section 402(a)(1) of the FD&C Act.

The guidance's NHANES/WWEIA intake footnote reports mean per-capita daily dry instant infant-cereal intake during the first year of life as rice 4.8 g/day, oatmeal 2.8 g/day, barley 0.1 g/day, and mixed grains 0.8 g/day for cereals not containing fruit.

## Methods (brief)

This is a nonbinding FDA guidance, not a sampling report. The guidance relies on FDA's arsenic-in-rice risk assessment and supporting document. It states that the method used by FDA for analyzing inorganic arsenic in rice was posted separately on FDA's website.

## Implications

Certification: The source is a U.S. federal policy comparator for iAs in infant rice cereal and does not set HMTc thresholds.

Courses: The source cleanly distinguishes action levels from tolerances or rulemaking limits under 21 CFR 109.4 and 109.6.
