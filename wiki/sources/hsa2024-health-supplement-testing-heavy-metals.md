Singapore HSA testing guidelines specify heavy-metal limits for health supplements, traditional medicines, medicated oils and balms, and medicated plasters. Table 1 lists arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury limits by weight in parts per million. The document also requires quantitative reporting and limits of detection for non-detect results.
