# Ogimoto et al. 2012, aluminium in foods with aluminium additives, including doughnuts

Ogimoto and colleagues measured aluminium in Japanese foods made with aluminium-containing additives. This note uses the two doughnut rows only.

## Key numbers

Table 2 prints Content (mg/g). Doughnut-1 is 0.03 mg/g. Doughnut-2 is ND, and ND is defined as below 0.01 mg/g. The paper attributes the aluminium to baking powder. Lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, chromium, and tin are not reported for doughnuts.

## Methods (brief)

Aluminium was measured in foods that contained aluminium food additives. A recovery table is printed (Table 1). The doughnut unit is mg/g as printed. This page does not convert mg/g to another unit.

## Evidence fitness

The two rows support an aluminium occurrence note for those doughnut samples. They do not support a multi-metal doughnut profile or a certification limit.

## Limitations

One row is below 0.01 mg/g. The table does not print a doughnut sample size next to the two rows. The unit mg/g is the printed unit.
