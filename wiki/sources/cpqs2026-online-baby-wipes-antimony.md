# CPQS 2026 ,  Online baby wet wipes, total antimony

The 2 April 2026 page reports total antimony detected in 20 of 21 baby wet wipes bought online, and not detected in one. The annex table prints a total-antimony row in µg/L.

## Key numbers

In sample order, without brand names, the row is 283.93, 96.71, 15.02, 236.77, 226.48, 107.23, 233.18, 41.78, 181.98, 116.51, 383.77, 157.12, 305.19, not detected, 450.45, 207.8, 107.82, 410.74, 293.38, 149.55, and 355.43 µg/L. Nine cells wrap across two lines. Detected results run from 15.02 to 450.45 µg/L.

## Methods (brief)

Association monitoring report, not a peer-reviewed paper. The unit is the one printed on that row.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the µg/L row and the detection count. Does not support a brand comparison or a conversion to milligrams per kilogram of sheet.

## Limitations

Brand names printed on the source are not repeated.
