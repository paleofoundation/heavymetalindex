Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 sets requirements for CE-marked EU fertilising products. Annex I Part II includes contaminant ceilings for organic, organo-mineral, and inorganic macronutrient fertilisers, liming materials, soil improvers, growing media, and plant biostimulants. Rows are loaded where the regulation states product concentrations in mg/kg dry matter; cadmium phosphate-fertiliser values expressed per kg P2O5 and micronutrient-fertiliser values expressed per kg total micronutrient content are gap-logged instead.
