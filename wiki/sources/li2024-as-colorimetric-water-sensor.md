## Overview

A colorimetric sensor for detection of As3+ (arsenite) in water was developed. The paper focuses on method development for drinking water applications. No food matrix samples are reported. The sensor detects As(III), a specific inorganic arsenic oxidation state, rather than total arsenic or total inorganic arsenic.

## Key numbers
- LOD, linear range, and selectivity data in full paper.
- No food occurrence data.

## Methods (brief)
Colorimetric detection of As(III)/As3+. Speciation-sensitive: specifically detects As(III) rather than total As. Water matrix validation only.

## Implications
Certification: Method development; no food data. As(III) speciation relevant to understanding arsenic chemistry in food matrices.
