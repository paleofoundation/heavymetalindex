# Gawra et al. 2025 — Cadmium and ciprofloxacin in zebrafish larvae

This conference abstract describes a short laboratory exposure study in zebrafish larvae. It reports contrasting biochemical responses to cadmium and ciprofloxacin, while molecular analyses were still underway.

## Key numbers

| Experimental parameter | Reported value | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Larval age | 96 hours | Reference point is not specified in the abstract. |
| Exposure duration | 24 hours | Distinct from larval age. |
| Ciprofloxacin range | 10–50 mg/L | Nominal antibiotic exposure in water. |
| Cadmium range | 0.24–24 µg/L | [Official SETAC abstract record](https://setac.secure-platform.com/vienna2025/solicitations/102006/sessiongallery/94247/application/7982) resolves the book's corrupted “?g/L” unit. |

## Methods (brief)

The listed endpoints include survival, development, swimming behavior and biochemical markers. The authors report no survival or developmental effects at the tested levels. Catalase and cholinesterase activities increased with the antibiotic and decreased with cadmium. Increased zigzag and high-speed swimming is specifically described for antibiotic-exposed larvae.

## Evidence fitness

Supports preliminary, attributed biochemical and behavioral findings in an experimental aquatic model. It does not measure contaminants in food or consumer products, establish a bioaccumulation factor, or demonstrate human health effects. The two compounds' responses remain distinct.

## Limitations

The abstract supplies no sample counts, detailed dose grid, measured exposure verification, effect sizes or exact p-values. The cadmium unit is corrupted in the book; a cached official SETAC abstract record supplies µg/L, with that correction traced separately. A reported absence of survival effects does not establish absence of all toxicity. Transcriptomic and DNA-methylation analyses were not yet reported as completed.
