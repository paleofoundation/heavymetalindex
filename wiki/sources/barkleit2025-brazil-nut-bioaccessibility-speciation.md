# Barkleit et al. 2025 : In vitro bioaccessibility and speciation of trace elements in Brazil nuts

## Summary

ICP-MS and gamma/alpha spectrometry analysis of selenium, strontium, barium, lanthanum, europium, and the radionuclides Ra-226 and Ra-228 in commercially available whole Brazil nuts and defatted Brazil nut flour (BNF). The study applies the unified bioaccessibility method (UBM) developed by the Bioaccessibility Research Group of Europe (BARGE) to simulate oral digestion in three phases (saliva, stomach, gastrointestinal tract, GIT). It is the first study to report bioaccessibility data for Ba, Ra, La, and Eu from Brazil nuts.

## Key numbers

**Total element concentrations (from Table 1, p. 4):**

| Element | Defatted BNF (mean +/- SD, ug/g) | Whole Brazil nut (mean +/- SD, ug/g) | Whole nut literature range (ug/g) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Se | 10.1 +/- 0.8 | 1.91 +/- 0.25 | 1 to 55 |
| Sr | 760 +/- 40 | 230 +/- 13 | 115 to 198 |
| Ba | 8000 +/- 400 | 1720 +/- 90 | 49 to 2476 |
| La | 0.122 +/- 0.008 | 0.0291 +/- 0.002 | 0.0014 (one ref) |
| Eu | 2.49 +/- 0.17 | 0.65 +/- 0.03 | less than 0.0004 (one ref) |

Radionuclide activities (Table 1, p. 4):

| Radionuclide | Defatted BNF (mBq/g) | Whole Brazil nut (mBq/g) | Literature range (mBq/g) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ra-226 | 74.0 +/- 5.3 | 51.6 +/- 3.7 | 17 to 205 |
| Ra-228 | 63.0 +/- 4.6 | 47.7 +/- 3.0 | 18 to 100 |

Defatted BNF concentrations are approximately 3 to 4 times higher than whole nut values, consistent with a lipid content of approximately 60 to 70 percent; elements reside predominantly in the lipid-free fraction.

**Bioaccessibility from defatted BNF (Table 2, p. 5):**

| Element | GIT bioaccessibility (%) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Se | 84 +/- 5 (this work) | Literature comparators: 70 +/- 23; 74; 19 +/- 2 |
| Sr | 52 +/- 5 | Literature: 7.8 +/- 0.8 (one study) |
| Ba | approximately 1.9 (this work) | Approximately 2.2 in prior study |
| La | 27 +/- 13 | No prior data |
| Eu | 27 +/- 6 | No prior data |
| Ra-226 | approximately 1.9 mBq/g at GIT | n/a |

Ba and Ra showed very low bioaccessibility (approximately 2 percent each), attributed to binding to phytate and insoluble complexes. Sr showed intermediate bioaccessibility (52 percent). Se was highly bioaccessible (approximately 85 percent).

## Methods (brief)

Commercially available defatted Brazil nut flour and whole Brazil nuts purchased in Germany (origin not specified). ICP-MS (Agilent) for elemental concentrations; gamma and alpha spectrometry for Ra-226 and Ra-228. UBM oral bioaccessibility protocol (sequential saliva, gastric, GIT digestion steps). NMR spectrometry for Se speciation: predominantly selenomethionine (SeMet) confirmed. No CRM or recovery data reported in the extracted pages; LOD/LOQ not stated in the available pages.

## Evidence fitness notes

Single commercially available sample set (defatted flour and whole nut, one source each). First report of Ba, Ra, La, Eu bioaccessibility from Brazil nuts. Se bioaccessibility values consistent with published literature. Ba and Ra bioaccessibility notably low despite high total concentrations.

## Implications

- Ingredient page: provides direct evidence for Ba and Ra occurrence in whole Brazil nuts (Ba mean 1,720 ug/g wet weight; Ra-226 mean 51.6 mBq/g) and their very low gastrointestinal bioaccessibility.
- Ingredient page: Se bioaccessibility approximately 85 percent, predominantly as SeMet, is the best available estimate for effective dietary selenium delivery from Brazil nuts.
- The Ba whole-nut value (1,720 ug/g = 1.72 mg/g) is consistent with the range reported by Koeder and Keller 2025.
