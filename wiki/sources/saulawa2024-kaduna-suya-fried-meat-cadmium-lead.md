# Saulawa and Jikamshi 2024: Cadmium and lead in street-vended fried meat, Kaduna, Nigeria

## Overview

Saulawa and Jikamshi measured cadmium and lead by AAS in street-vended fried spiced meat (suya) made from five animal species in Kaduna. Both metals were found in all samples, with no significant difference among species or areas.

## Key numbers

- All samples (n = 46): cadmium mean 0.049, range 0.00 to 0.13 ppm; lead mean 0.44, range 0.29 to 0.78 ppm (Table 2).
- Chicken suya: cadmium 0.077, lead 0.50 ppm; cattle suya: cadmium 0.033, lead 0.46 ppm (Table 3).

## Methods (brief)

Samples were oven-dried at 45 °C, ashed at 500 °C, dissolved in nitric and hydrochloric acids and read by flame AAS. No detection limits, reference materials or recoveries are reported.

## Evidence Fitness

Sample counts are inconsistent (50, 46 and 92), the results basis is not stated, and there is no analytical quality control. Fried chicken and fried beef are cooked-meat proxies for chicken pot pie filling and chili con carne; neither dish was measured.
