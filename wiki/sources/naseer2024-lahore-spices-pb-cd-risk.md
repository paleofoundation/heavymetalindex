# Naseer et al. 2024 -- Lead and cadmium in turmeric, black pepper, and white pepper from Lahore markets

## Overview

Naseer et al. measured lead and cadmium in 18 samples of turmeric powder, black pepper, and white pepper collected from six market locations in Lahore, Pakistan, using FAAS (Hitachi Z-5000). The paper assesses EDI and THQ by age-sex group using a consumption survey of 84 household members. No cumin samples were included. The study is relevant to the HMI library as a Pakistan-market spice contamination dataset with EDI methodology documentation, but its value for the cumin ingredient page is contextual only. All cadmium values were within the WHO limit of 0.3 mg/kg; lead was substantially elevated in multiple spice samples, with 66.7% of turmeric and black pepper samples and 83.3% of white pepper samples exceeding the WHO safe limit of 10 mg/kg Pb.

**Key findings:**
- 66.7% of turmeric and black pepper samples had Pb above WHO safe limit of 10 mg/kg
- 83.3% of white pepper samples had Pb above WHO safe limit of 10 mg/kg
- Highest Pb: white pepper from Shadbagh (17.167 mg/kg) and turmeric from Shahdara (15.833 mg/kg)
- All THQ <1 for all age-sex groups and all spices; TTHQ <1, indicating generally acceptable cumulative risk at reported consumption rates

## Methods (brief)

FAAS: Hitachi Z-5000. Digestion: HNO3/HClO4 (2:1) in a homogeneous solution (30 mL); 1 g pre-weighed spice dissolved; digested at 400 degrees C. Triplicate digestion per sample. Consumption survey: 24-hour dietary recall and food frequency questionnaire to 20 families (84 members) in Lahore (May-June 2021). EDI calculated as (C x IR) / BW; RfD for Pb 3.57 x 10^-3 mg/kg/day, Cd 1.00 x 10^-3 mg/kg/day.

## Implications

**Scope:** This paper does not include cumin. It is included in the deposit as a Pakistan-market spice dataset providing regional context for turmeric and pepper contamination in South Asian markets. The very high Pb values (up to 17.2 mg/kg in white pepper, 15.8 mg/kg in turmeric) from Lahore markets are consistent with the documented pattern of elevated spice Pb in Pakistani and South Asian markets generally.

**Species studied:** Turmeric (Curcuma longa), black pepper (Piper nigrum), white pepper (Piper nigrum). No Cuminum cyminum included.
