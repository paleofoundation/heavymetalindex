# Al-Thani et al. 2023 (Oman) — elemental impurities in herbal weight loss supplements

## Summary

Al-Thani et al. screened 18 herbal weight loss supplement products from the Omani market for 15 elements using ICP-OES with microwave-assisted digestion. Four micro-elements (Pb, Co, Ni, Cd) were below the limit of quantification in almost all products. Arsenic, iron, aluminum, and manganese were detectable in all 18 products. At daily intake levels calculated from labelled usage instructions: two products (A6 and A7) provided arsenic above the maximum tolerable daily intake (MTDI); one product (A17) provided manganese at 1,410 µg/g, exceeding the maximum recommended daily dose; and two products (A7, A18) exceeded the MTDI for aluminum. Macro-elements (Na, K, Ca, Mg) were within acceptable daily intake thresholds. The study is the first reported investigation of elemental impurities in weight-loss supplements from the Omani market.

## Key numbers

Elemental concentrations in µg/g (micro-elements) and mg/g (macro-elements). Method: ICP-OES (Optima 8000 Perkin-Elmer, plasma argon 8.0 L/min). LOQ approximately 0.009 mg/L (instrument).

Micro-element range across 18 products (selected elements, µg/g):

| Element | Range across all 18 products | Notable exceedances |
| --- | --- | --- |
| As | 3 to 10 | A6 and A7 exceeded MTDI at labelled dose |
| Mn | 0 to 1,410 (A17) | A17 above maximum recommended daily Mn |
| Al | 0 to 1,318 (A5) | A7 and A18 exceeded MTDI for Al |
| Fe | 1 to 1,297 (A4) | Within acceptable daily intake |
| Pb | not detectable (most) | below LOQ |
| Co | not detectable | below LOQ |
| Ni | not detectable | below LOQ |
| Cd | not detectable | below LOQ |

Macro-elements (mg/g) in selected products:
- Na: 0.26-4.85 mg/g; K: 2.17-18.46 mg/g; Ca: 0.08-11.30 mg/g; Mg: 0.37-10.44 mg/g.
- All macro-elements within acceptable daily intake ranges per labelled dose.

Cumulative daily exposure for arsenic (As, µg/day at 3x daily dose):
- A6: 225 µg/day, A7: 180 µg/day (both exceed MTDI, 147 µg/day for 70 kg adult at 2.1 µg/kg bw/day).
- A17: 120 µg/day As (near MTDI).

## Methods (brief)

18 herbal weight loss products from the Omani market. ICP-OES (Optima 8000 Perkin-Elmer) with axial viewing for micro-elements, radial viewing for macro-elements. Microwave-assisted digestion (ultraWAVE, Milestone) in HNO3/H2O2 (3-step program, 110-240 C). Calibration: multi-element standards (0.1-10.0 mg/L micro, 1.0-200.0 mg/L macro). QC: triplicate injection per QC standard, three-day intermediate precision. Daily exposure calculated from labelled dose (3 doses/day) and 70 kg adult body weight.

## Implications

- Primary multi-element occurrence dataset (n=18, 15 elements) for herbal weight loss supplements from the Omani market; arsenic exceeded MTDI in 2 of 18 products at labelled dose, and manganese was notably elevated in one product (A17: 1,410 µg/g).
- Pb, Co, Ni, Cd below LOQ in almost all products, contrasting with supplement surveys from South/Southeast Asian markets.
- Arsenic and aluminum both warrant monitoring in herbal weight loss products; As from botanical contamination (soil, water), Al from possible botanical or mineral additives.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] and [[products/supplements-botanicals-herbs]]; contributes As, Mn, Al occurrence data to [[metals/arsenic]], [[metals/manganese]], [[metals/aluminium]] for supplement categories.
