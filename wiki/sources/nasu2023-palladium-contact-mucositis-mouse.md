# Nasu et al. 2023 - Murine model of palladium-induced intraoral allergic contact mucositis

## Overview

This study established a novel murine model of palladium-induced intraoral allergic contact mucositis (ACM) and characterized the hypersensitivity mechanism as Type IVb (CD8+ T cell-mediated). Oral mucosa swelling was measured from day 1 through day 7 after challenge, with macroscopic findings of redness, swelling, and lesions documented. The model provides mechanistic insight into how Pd from dental alloys causes intraoral allergic reactions.

## Key numbers

- Oral mucosa swelling measured days 1-7 post-challenge
- Statistical significance reached at specified timepoints (p<0.05 and p<0.01)
- n=10 mice per experimental group (Pd-ACM and ICM groups)
- Macroscopic findings: redness, swelling, and lesions in sensitized mice
- Type IVb (CD8+ T cell-mediated) hypersensitivity established as mechanistic pathway

## Methods (brief)

Murine sensitization and challenge protocol using palladium compounds applied to oral mucosa. Swelling quantified by caliper measurement days 1-7. Histological examination and immunohistochemical staining for T cell subsets. CD4+ and CD8+ T cell depletion experiments to characterize hypersensitivity subtype.

## Implications

This page makes the source discoverable for palladium evidence routing. Establishes the mechanistic basis of Pd-induced intraoral allergy as CD8+ T cell-mediated Type IVb hypersensitivity in a murine model, directly relevant to understanding clinical oral mucositis in patients with Pd-containing dental restorations.
