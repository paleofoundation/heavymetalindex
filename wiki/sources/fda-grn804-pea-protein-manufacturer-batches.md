# FDA GRAS Notice 804: Manufacturer heavy-metal results for pea protein isolate and concentrate

## Overview

This GRAS notice, submitted to FDA in 2018, includes the manufacturer's own lead, arsenic, cadmium and mercury results for two pea protein grades. The Index withholds the manufacturer and product names.

## Key numbers

- Lead: 0.02 and below 0.01 ppm in isolate batches, 0.02 ppm in the isolate blend; 0.04 ppm (batch) and 0.02 ppm (blend) in the concentrate (Tables 10 and 11).
- Cadmium: 0.0096, 0.0081 and 0.001 ppm (isolate); 0.041 and 0.015 ppm (concentrate) (Tables 10 and 11).
- Total arsenic and mercury: below the printed values in every row (Tables 10 and 11).

## Methods (brief)

Lead, arsenic and cadmium were measured by ICP-MS (EPA 3050B/6020, or EPA 3050/6020 with USP 730) and mercury by cold vapor (EPA 7471B), as stated in the table footnotes.

## Evidence Fitness

These are manufacturer test results submitted to FDA with a GRAS notice, not independent measurements. FDA published the notice but did not verify the values. Five results from one manufacturer, two of them blends of batches, is a narrow base. Inorganic arsenic was not measured.
