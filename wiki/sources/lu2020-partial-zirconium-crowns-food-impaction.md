# Lu and Wang 2020: Partial zirconium crowns and food impaction in molars

This Chengdu clinical study compared partial zirconium crowns that preserve adjacent tooth contacts with traditional full crowns. After one year, food impaction occurred on 2.08% of surfaces in the partial-crown group versus 15.62% in the control group. Food impaction here means food trapped between teeth, not a dietary contaminant.

## Key numbers

- Food impaction incidence 2.08% (1 of 48 surfaces) with partial crowns versus 15.62% in the control group; no secondary caries on 80 adjacent surfaces (Abstract).
- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Prospective comparison of two crown preparation methods with 12-month clinical follow-up and chi-square testing.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium dental crowns as a restorative material; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

Small single-center study; zirconium release from crowns was not measured.
