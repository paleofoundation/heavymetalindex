# Korea Consumer Agency 2023: Newborn tape-type diapers

The Korea Consumer Agency quality test of 31 January 2023 (비교공감 제2023-2호) covered 10 newborn tape-type disposable diapers. Safety items under the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety hygiene-product standard were pH, fluorescent whitener, formaldehyde, chlorinated phenols, migration of eight elements (including antimony and arsenic), total lead and cadmium, phthalates, and residual acrylic acid monomer. Every product was reported compliant on those safety items. The report does not print element concentrations.

## Key numbers

No metal concentration is printed. The metal finding is compliance: migration of the eight elements, and total lead and cadmium, met the hygiene-product standard in all 10 products.

Three products were noncompliant on ingredient-name labeling. The agency reported that those labels would be corrected. The product names in that labeling passage are not repeated here.

## Methods (brief)

The report applies the MFDS hygiene-product standard. It does not reprint the numeric migration ceilings or the digestion method in a form that adds concentrations. Acrylic acid monomer is not a metal. Arsenic species and chromium species are not stated beyond the standard's element list as summarized in the report.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports a 2023 Korean compliance statement for 10 newborn tape diapers. It does not support a microgram-per-kilogram value or a ranking of products.

## Limitations

Pass or fail only. Korean web report. Three labeling failures are described without product names because the finding is about labeling, not about a metal concentration that would be misread if the name were dropped.
