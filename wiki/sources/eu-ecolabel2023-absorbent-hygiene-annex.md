# European Commission 2023: EU Ecolabel ceilings for absorbent hygiene products

This annex sets voluntary EU Ecolabel criteria for absorbent hygiene products, including baby diapers, tampons, and sanitary towels. Table 9 is a list of restricted impurities in the final product. The figures are ceilings an applicant must meet. They are not measurements of products on the market, and they are not maximum levels that apply to unlabeled products.

The file is the annex PDF. Its document date in the file properties is 14 September 2023. The annex text itself does not print a Commission Decision number or a decision date. Those are not supplied from outside the file.

## Key numbers

Table 9, criterion 7.3.8, impurities in the final product, as printed (decimal commas in the annex are written here with a point):

| Substance | Restriction |
| --- | --- |
| Formaldehyde | < 16 ppm |
| PCDD, PCDF, and dioxin-like PCB congeners listed in the table | sum TEQ of detected congeners < 2 ng/kg |
| Each listed PAH | < 0.2 mg/kg |
| Sum of listed PAHs | < 1 mg/kg |
| Tributyltin | < 2 ppb |
| Each of monobutyltin, dibutyltin, triphenyltin, dioctyltin, monooctyltin | < 10 ppb |
| Antimony | < 30 mg/kg |
| Cadmium | < 0.1 mg/kg |
| Chromium | < 1 mg/kg |
| Lead | < 0.2 mg/kg |
| Mercury | < 0.02 mg/kg |

Criterion 7.3.4 requires that antimony, arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, and selenium impurities in dyes and inks on exempted printed components stay below Council of Europe Resolution AP (89) 1. The annex does not print those AP (89) 1 numbers.

Nursing pads are named in the technical-test note as using the same recommended tests as baby diapers. There is no nursing-pad product page, so that product is not routed.

## Methods (brief)

Assessment is a signed declaration plus analyses on a representative final product, or on each material in that product, at least once a year. Recommended tests are NWSP 360.1R0 or equivalent for sample preparation, NWSP 360.2R0 or equivalent for extraction, and NWSP 360.3R0 or equivalent for instrumental analysis. The annex does not state whether chromium is hexavalent or total, or whether mercury is methylmercury or total. The organotin rows name the species. The heavy-metal rows name the elements.

## Evidence fitness

These rows support a statement of what the EU Ecolabel requires of an awarded product. They do not support an occurrence distribution, a pass rate, or a legal maximum for products that do not carry the label. Formaldehyde, dioxins, PAHs, phenols, phthalates, and pesticides in the same table are recorded only so the metal rows are not read out of the table they belong to.

## Limitations

Voluntary criteria. Chromium and mercury species are not stated. AP (89) 1 numeric dye ceilings are incorporated by reference and are not in this PDF. The decision number is not printed in the annex. Nursing pads are in scope of the criteria and have no destination page in this ingest.
