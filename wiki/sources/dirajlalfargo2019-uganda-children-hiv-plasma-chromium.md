# Dirajlal-Fargo et al. 2019: Plasma chromium in Ugandan children with and without HIV

This conference abstract reports plasma selenium and chromium in Ugandan children living with HIV, children exposed to HIV but uninfected, and unexposed children. Mean plasma chromium was 1 µg/L, and the authors judged chromium concentrations sufficient.

## Key numbers

- Mean plasma chromium 1 µg/L; one HIV-positive child and six HIV-exposed uninfected children had levels above 1 µg/L (Results, p. S879).
- HIV-positive group n = 57; mean age of all participants 7 years (Results, p. S879).

## Methods (brief)

Cross-sectional study; plasma chromium and inflammation markers; analytical method not stated in the abstract.

## Evidence fitness

Supports only a coarse statement that plasma chromium averaged about 1 µg/L in this Ugandan pediatric cohort. It does not support group comparisons, because statistical results are truncated in the printed abstract.

## Limitations

Conference abstract; analytical method, detection limit and full group sizes not given; p-values missing from the printed text.
