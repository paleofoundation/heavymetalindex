# Amorati et al. 2023: A nanoporous cerium oxide material that slows autoxidation

The authors synthesized a nanoporous cerium oxide material and showed in kinetic experiments that it slows the autoxidation of an organic substrate more effectively than other metal oxide nanoparticles, proposing a radical-quenching mechanism. Food storage is mentioned as a possible application, but no food testing was done.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to cerium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Material synthesis and characterization with oxygen-uptake kinetics in a model autoxidation system.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: materials chemistry that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
