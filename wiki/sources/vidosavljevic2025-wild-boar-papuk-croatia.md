# Vidosavljevic et al. 2025: Heavy metals in wild boar tissues, Papuk Nature Park, Croatia

## Overview

This ICP-MS study measured As, Cd, Hg, Pb, Cr, Cu, Fe, and Zn in muscle, liver, and kidney of 38 wild boars from Papuk Nature Park, Eastern Croatia. The key finding is that cadmium exceeded permitted EU levels in 50% of kidney samples (max 6.64 mg/kg), 20% of liver samples (max 4.60 mg/kg), and 43% of muscle samples (max 0.672 mg/kg). Lead exceeded limits in 63% of liver samples (max 0.463 mg/kg), 51% of kidney (max 11.8 mg/kg), and 65% of muscle (max 9.10 mg/kg). Mercury exceeded limits in 13-27% of liver and kidney samples. Arsenic exceeded limits in 30% of muscle samples.

## Key numbers

All concentrations in mg/kg wet weight by ICP-MS.

| Analyte | Tissue | Exceedance rate | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cd | Kidney | 50% | 6.64 mg/kg |
| Cd | Liver | 20% | 4.60 mg/kg |
| Cd | Muscle | 43% | 0.672 mg/kg |
| Pb | Liver | 63% | 0.463 mg/kg |
| Pb | Kidney | 51% | 11.8 mg/kg |
| Pb | Muscle | 65% | 9.10 mg/kg |
| tHg | Liver | 13% | 0.552 mg/kg |
| tHg | Kidney | 27% | 0.484 mg/kg |
| tHg | Muscle | 15% | 0.103 mg/kg |
| As | Muscle | 30% | 0.286 mg/kg |

Median concentrations of non-toxic elements: muscle (Cr 0.193, Cu 2.94, Fe 44.5, Zn 20.6 mg/kg); kidney (Cr 0.140, Cu 5.32, Fe 110, Zn 23.3 mg/kg); liver (Cr 0.130, Cu 3.71, Fe 278, Zn 36.0 mg/kg).

## Implications

- Wild boar is a non-commercial organ-meat species (game meat context); values in this free-ranging wildlife population in a nature park can be elevated compared to farmed animals.
- The high exceedance rates (Cd in 50% of kidney, Pb in 63% of liver) are consistent with the page's higher_risk_variants note that wild-game organ meats carry elevated contamination due to environmental exposure.
- Note: these are wild game, not livestock; do not pool with farmed-animal occurrence data for contamination_profile updates.
