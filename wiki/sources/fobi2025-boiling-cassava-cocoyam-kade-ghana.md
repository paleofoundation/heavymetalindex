# Fobi et al. 2025 — Effect of Boiling on Heavy Metal and Cyanide Concentrations and Associated Health Risks in Cassava and Cocoyam From a Gold Mining Area in Kade, Ghana

## Overview

Field/farm sampling with a laboratory boiling experiment (raw vs boiled, paired by composite) of Cassava tubers from farms; 50 g cut into 2-cm pieces; raw (control, same preparation without cooking) vs boiled in deionised water 1:3 at 100 °C for 20 min. Peeling is not stated.. Growing area or market: Grown on cassava/cocoyam farms 2 km from a gold mining site in Kade, Kwaebibirem municipality, Eastern Region, Ghana; collected at six points (P1-P6) 50 m apart; not purchased.. Collection period: 27 May 2024 (single sampling date). Method: Cd: 2.01 g desiccated sample, HNO3 reflux, cloud point extraction (detector not stated). As: 1.121 g, HNO3 at 60 °C + HClO4, AAS (Buck VGP 210). Hg: 1.11 g, HCl at 45 °C for 2 h, dithizone complex, UV-Vis at 488 nm.. Basis: Raw cassava: 'mg/kg dry weight' (Table 4 title). Cooked cassava: not stated (Tables 5 and 6). Cd method uses 'desiccated' sample..

## Limitations

Cooked cassava results have no stated basis while raw results are dry weight; the reported boiling reductions may be partly or wholly a moisture/basis artefact. Whether cooked pieces were dried before analysis is not stated. Reduction percentages are internally inconsistent: abstract and conclusion say boiling reduced Hg by 65-80 %, As by 85-95 % and Cd by 60-75 % in both crops, but cassava Table 4 vs Table 5 gives Hg 72-95 % (text: ~70-90 %) and Cd 62-96 % (two points non-detect). No LOD/LOQ for any analyte; cooked non-detects printed as 0.000 cannot be bounded. Arsenic at 10-20 µg/kg by AAS without stated hydride generation and Hg by dithizone UV-Vis spectrophotometry: method sensitivity for these levels is not demonstrated; no CRM or recovery. Exposure arithmetic does not reproduce from the tables: e.g. raw cassava As mean 0.0168 mg/kg x 0.527 kg/day / 60 kg = 0.148 µg/kg bw/day vs Table 7 RCS adult EDI 0.0539; raw Hg mean ~0.368 mg/kg gives ~3.2 µg/kg bw/day vs Table 7 1.1990. Not used for occurrence but HI conclusions are unverifiable. Peeling of cassava before analysis is not stated; material is 'cassava tubers' cut into pieces. The ± is not defined (replicates of a composite assumed); n per point = 1 composite.
