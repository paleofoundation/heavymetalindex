# Adjorlolo-Gasokpoh et al. 2012: mercury in cassava near the Bogoso and Prestea gold mines

## Findings and scope

The study measured total mercury in surface soil and in four cassava tissues at farms near the gold mines at Bogoso and Prestea, Ghana. In peeled cassava flesh, the site means were 122.08 to 167.67 µg/kg (Table 2, PDF p.3). Across all cassava tissues, the means ranged from 66.60 µg/kg (outer peel, Bogoso entrance) to 195.47 µg/kg (cortex, Prestea Ankobra). Surface-soil means ranged from 125.29 to 352.52 µg/kg (abstract, PDF p.1; Table 1, PDF p.3). Mercury was the only element measured.

Means and standard deviations are as printed in Table 2 (PDF p.3), total mercury in µg/kg. Each mean is of four printed replicate values.

The standard deviation for Prestea Ankobra flesh is printed as "24..08". It is shown here as 24.08, which the four printed replicates reproduce. The 0 to 5 cm surface-soil means for the six sites (Table 1, PDF p.3) were 217.96, 230.29, 151.11, 125.29, 316.52 and 352.52 µg/kg.

## Methods and Evidence Fitness

Cassava tubers came from three plants per site. They were washed, rinsed with distilled water and peeled. The peel was separated into periderm (outer skin) and cortex, and the core was retained as flesh. Leaves were taken from the same plants. All tissues were oven-dried at 35 °C to constant weight, ground and sieved through a 120 µm mesh (PDF p.2). A 1 g portion was digested with sulfuric and nitric acid, permanganate and persulfate. Mercury was measured by cold-vapour atomic absorption (Shimadzu 6400AA) with stannous chloride reduction (PDF p.2 to 4). Recovery of spiked standards was 99.3% to 102.0% (PDF p.4). No tissue reference material or detection limit is reported, and the sampling date is not stated.

Evidence Fitness is Modeled or limited evidence. The flesh values are limited occurrence evidence for peeled cassava tuber grown near gold mines. The paper does not state the basis, but because the tissue was dried before weighing, the values are treated here as dry weight. The leaf, peel and soil values are context for pathways and are not edible-tuber occurrence.

## Assessment limits

The methods describe three plants per site and state that analyses were done in triplicate, but the tables print four replicate columns without defining them (PDF p.2 vs p.3). Three printed soil standard deviations do not match their printed replicates. The abstract mentions a mercury speciation exercise, but the paper reports no speciation method or data. All values are total mercury and cannot be read as methylmercury. Without a sampling date, the measurements cannot be placed in time. A regression of concentration against distance uses only four to six site means.
