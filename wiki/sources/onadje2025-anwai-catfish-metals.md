# Bioaccumulation of Cadmium, Copper and Lead in Edible Tissues of Clarias gariepinus and Oreochromis niloticus from Anwai River, Nigeria

## Summary

Onadje and colleagues measured cadmium, copper, and lead by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in flesh, liver, gills, and stomach of Clarias gariepinus and Oreochromis niloticus from the Anwai River, Nigeria. Ten fish of each species were analysed. The catfish rows are the Clarias gariepinus rows. The byline is Onadje.

## Key numbers

Table 1 Clarias gariepinus flesh, mg/kg: cadmium 4.43±0.51, copper 0.09±0.04, lead 2.44±0.11. Liver cadmium is printed 11.0±2.00 in the table and 11.00±2.00 in the prose. The abstract range 0.09 mg/kg to 4.56 mg/kg does not include that liver cadmium. Arsenic and mercury: Data gap.
