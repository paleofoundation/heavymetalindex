# Ramzannezhad et al. 2026: Cerium oxide nanocomposite electrode for vanillin in foods

A nanocomposite electrode containing cerium oxide nanoparticles was developed to measure the flavoring vanillin in foods. Cerium oxide improved the electrode's electrochemical response; cerium itself was not measured in any food.

## Key numbers

- Vanillin detection limit 6 nM and quantification limit 19 nM (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Nanocomposite synthesis and characterization, electrochemical optimization and recovery testing in food samples.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: cerium is an electrode material; the paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims about cerium.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
