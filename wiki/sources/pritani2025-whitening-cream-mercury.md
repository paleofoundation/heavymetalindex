# Analysis of Hydroquinone and Mercury in Whitening Cream Using UV-Vis Spectrophotometry and ICP-OES

## Summary

Pritani, Marini, and Nurhayatina measured mercury by ICP-OES in nine whitening creams, coded A to I, from Kuningan, Indonesia. The byline spells the first author Pritani. Table 4 is mg/L and includes negative values. Basis: Data gap. These are skin-whitening creams.

## Key numbers

Table 4 mercury, mg/L: A -0.5902, B -0.5989, C -0.2188, D -0.0092, E -0.0071, F -0.0069, G -0.5997, H -0.6018, I 138.4844. The abstract says A to H ran from -0.0071 to -0.6018 mg/L and sample I was 138.4844 mg/L. Other target metals: Data gap.
