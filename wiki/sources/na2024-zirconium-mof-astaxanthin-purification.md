# Na et al. 2024: Zirconium metal-organic frameworks for purifying astaxanthin

This study tuned the pores of zirconium-based metal-organic frameworks to adsorb and purify astaxanthin, a carotenoid extracted from the microalga Haematococcus pluvialis. The best material adsorbed 26.21 mg/g of astaxanthin. Zirconium carryover into the purified product was not reported.

## Key numbers

- Astaxanthin adsorption capacity 26.21 mg/g on the best material; extraction efficiency 89.0% ± 2.3% (Abstract).
- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Modulator-induced defect engineering of UiO-67 frameworks, adsorption and selectivity tests, spectroscopy, density functional theory calculations and fixed-bed column separation.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium frameworks as adsorbents for carotenoid purification; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium. Zirconium residues in the purified astaxanthin were not measured.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
