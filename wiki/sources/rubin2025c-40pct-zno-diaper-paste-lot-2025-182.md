# Rubin 2025c. Lead and cadmium in one 40% zinc oxide maximum-strength diaper paste

One retail unit of a 40% zinc oxide maximum-strength diaper rash paste contained 4657.1 ppb lead and 473.1 ppb cadmium as received, by ICP-MS at an independent laboratory. Arsenic and mercury were below the reporting values. This is the highest lead result among the four single-product laboratory reports in this collection.

## Key numbers

Laboratory report: Purity Laboratories, Inc.; case 2508207; received 08/14/2025, tested and completed 08/20/2025. The sample was described to the laboratory as "Max Strength Diaper Rash Paste Lot# 2025-182". The advocate's annotation identifies a 40% zinc oxide skin protectant marketed for moderate to serious diaper rash.

| Analyte | Result (ppb, as received) | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arsenic | < 10.00 | below reporting value; not speciated |
| Cadmium | 473.1 | detected |
| Lead | 4657.1 | detected |
| Mercury | < 5.00 | below reporting value; not speciated |

The article describes the result as "over 4,000 ppb Lead", which matches the report. It compares the result with the Washington State Toxic-Free Cosmetics Act figure of one part per million (1,000 ppb) and says the state agency may decline to act because the product is sold with medical properties.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-MS. No sample mass, digestion, calibration, recovery, detection limit or accreditation is printed. Results apply to the sample as received. Arsenic and mercury are totals.

## Evidence fitness

The report supports one as-received result for this unit. It does not support statements about other lots, the brand, the category, absorbed dose, or legality.

## Limitations

This is a single advocate-selected sample with no quality-control data. The '<' entries are reporting values. The article's regulatory comparison is the author's and draws on an instrument that is not in this collection.
