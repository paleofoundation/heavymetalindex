# Rubin (Lead Safe Mama) 2025. Metals in one baby mineral sunscreen (laboratory case 2505271)

A consumer-advocacy business had one unit of a zinc oxide baby sunscreen lotion, SPF 50 tested by a commercial laboratory for lead, cadmium, mercury and arsenic and published the report. The published headline is: "Testing Coppertone Pure & Simple SPF 50 Zinc Oxide Baby Sunscreen for Lead, Cadmium, Mercury, & Arsenic with Independent (Community-Funded), Third-Party Laboratory Testing". This page reports the result by laboratory case number.

## Key numbers

Laboratory report image, PDF p. 20. Case 2505271-01, received 05/14/2025, tested 05/19/2025, completed 05/21/2025. Units are ppb (µg/kg) in the product as received.

| Analyte | Result (ppb) | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | 1017.5 | ICP-MS |
| Cadmium | 356.1 | ICP-MS |
| Arsenic (total) | < 10.00 | ICP-MS |
| Mercury (total) | < 5.00 | ICP-MS |

The same test appears as row 27 of the 2026 comparison chart with the same values. The chart row for this test prints a zinc content of 24.08%. The internal lot number is printed as 2028-080, while every other report in the series uses 2025-nnn; the values are not affected. Ledger rows MBS-0150 to MBS-0153.

## Methods (brief)

The unit was sent to the laboratory with an anonymised sample description and an internal lot number, and the report was annotated with the product name afterwards. The report states ICP-MS and prints reporting limits through the less-than values. It does not describe the digestion, sample mass, recovery or quality control. The OCR text layer renders the arsenic sign as "=" and garbles the lead line; values here are read from the report image.

## Evidence fitness

The report supports one measured result for each metal in one unit as sold. It is useful as a data point alongside other sources and as a record of the reporting limits used. It does not describe other lots, the brand's current product, or the fraction of metal that crosses infant skin. Arsenic and mercury are totals.

## Limitations

One unit, one laboratory, no replicate and no method detail. Grey literature. The same result is also printed on the comparison chart and must be counted once.
