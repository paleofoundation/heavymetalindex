# Grygoyc and Jablonska-Czapla 2021: Tellurium speciation in soil near a Polish e-waste facility

An IC-ICP-MS method was developed for tellurium speciation (Te(IV) tellurite and Te(VI) tellurate) and applied to soils from an industrial zone in southern Poland. Total tellurium in the WEEE-impacted soils ranged from 0.021 to 0.166 mg/kg dry weight, 2 to 5-fold above the global average background of approximately 0.035 mg/kg. Te(IV) tellurite was the dominant detected species, consistent with the greater toxicity of tellurite relative to tellurate.

## Key numbers

- Soil LOD for Te(VI): 0.002 mg/kg; LOD for Te(IV): 0.004 mg/kg (Table 2 footer, p. 7).
- CRM 73324 total Te: 0.42 mg/kg; Te(VI): 0.004 mg/kg; Te(IV): 0.040 mg/kg (Table 2, p. 7).
- WEEE-area soil total Te range: 0.021 to 0.166 mg/kg (n=14) (Table 2, p. 7 to 8).
- WEEE-area soil Te(VI) range: 0.002 to 0.020 mg/kg; Te(IV) range: 0.005 to 0.017 mg/kg.
- Global average background soil Te (literature): approximately 35 µg/kg (0.035 mg/kg) (p. 2).
- Near-WEEE soils approximately 0.6 to 4.7-fold above background; maximum sample (105 Edel) at 0.166 mg/kg approximately 4.7-fold above background.
- BCR sequential extraction: Te predominantly bound to sulphides, organic matter, and residual silicates (Fraction 3+residual); extraction efficiency 9 to 47% (pp. 8 to 9).

## Methods (brief)

IC-ICP-MS using a Hamilton PRPX100 anion exchange column; aqueous extraction of soil with 0.1 M KH2PO4; total Te also measured by ICP-MS after microwave acid digestion. Sequential extraction used BCR scheme for soil fractionation.

## Evidence fitness

Supports characterization of tellurium concentrations and speciation in soils near a WEEE processing facility, with Te(IV) as the dominant measurable species. The low BCR extraction efficiency (9 to 47%) indicates most tellurium is in stable mineral forms with limited mobility. Does not address food or human exposure.

## Limitations

Small geographic area; industrial contamination zone not generalizable to background agricultural soils; low BCR extraction efficiency makes speciation fractions approximate; total Te determined by separate acid digestion rather than by the speciation method, so speciation fractions do not sum to total.
