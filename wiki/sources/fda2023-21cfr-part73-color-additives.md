The 4-1-2023 edition of 21 CFR Part 73 lists color additives exempt from certification for foods, drugs, cosmetics, and medical devices. Many sections contain direct impurity specifications for arsenic, lead, mercury, cadmium, chromium, nickel, antimony, tin, or aluminum. HMI loads direct not-more-than specifications and gap-logs cross-referenced sections whose inherited specifications are not restated in the local PDF.

## Titanium dioxide and zinc oxide sections, 4-1-25 edition

The 2025 edition of the sections covering titanium dioxide and zinc oxide was checked against government section PDFs from govinfo. These are colour-additive specifications. They govern titanium dioxide and zinc oxide used to colour a product, not their use as sunscreen active ingredients, which falls under the over-the-counter sunscreen monograph.

- §73.575 Titanium dioxide (printed pp. 496-497): synthetically prepared TiO2; lead not more than 10 ppm, arsenic 1 ppm, antimony 2 ppm and mercury 1 ppm; loss on ignition at 800 °C not more than 0.5 %; water-soluble substances 0.3 %; acid-soluble substances 0.5 %; TiO2 not less than 99.0 % after drying. Lead, arsenic and antimony are determined in the solution obtained by boiling 10 g in 50 mL of 0.5 N hydrochloric acid for 15 minutes, so those three limits are on an acid-extractable basis.
- §73.1575 (drugs, printed pp. 509-510) and §73.2575 (cosmetics including the eye area, printed p. 518) adopt the identity and specifications of §73.575(a)(1) and (b). §73.1575 allows silicon dioxide and/or aluminium oxide as dispersing aids up to 2 % total.
- §73.1991 Zinc oxide (drugs, printed pp. 511-512): French-process zinc oxide for externally applied drugs including the eye area; ZnO not less than 99 %; loss on ignition at 800 °C not more than 1 %; cadmium not more than 15 ppm, mercury 1 ppm, arsenic 3 ppm, lead 20 ppm. The source note printed after this section is 42 FR 37537, July 22, 1977.
- §73.2991 Zinc oxide (cosmetics including the eye area, printed p. 520) adopts §73.1991(a)(1) and (b), so the same cadmium, mercury, arsenic and lead limits apply.
