# Heavy metals and associated risks of wild edible mushrooms consumption: transfer factor, carcinogenic risk, and health risk index

## Summary

This journal article measured cadmium, chromium, nickel, and lead by ICP-MS in wild edible mushrooms from Romania, reported on a dried-weight basis as species means. The abstract says 228 mushrooms and section 3.3 says 128 subsamples. Both counts are kept. There is no single overall mean. Arsenic and mercury are not measured as totals for this row.

## Key numbers

Species means are the reported results. An overall mean is not printed. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Chromium is not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
