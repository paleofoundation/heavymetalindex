# Enyoh et al. 2023 — Preliminary characterization and probabilistic risk assessment of microplastics and potentially toxic elements (PTEs) in garri (cassava flake), a common staple food consumed in West Africa

## Overview

Market survey (retail purchase of five garri samples) with xrf elemental screen and microplastic analysis of Garri (fermented, pressed, fry-toasted cassava granules) as sold: four white garri (one unpackaged open-market, three packaged) and one yellow (palm-oil) garri, packaged.. Growing area or market: Purchase markets: S1 open market, Sango Ota (Ogun State, Nigeria); S2 supermarket, Abuja (Nigeria); S3 halal shop, Shin-Ōkubo (Tokyo, Japan); S4 and S5 halal shop, Shinjuku (Tokyo), ordered online. Growing/production origin not stated per sample; Japanese-market garri described as 'mainly imported from West Africa'.. Collection period: not reported. Method: Garri oven-dried at 105 °C for 6 h; 50 g sieved to 500 µm; EDXRF (Horiba XGT-5000), single-point analysis on sample taped to holder, 50 kV, 400 s; calibrated with CRM CJ-2 (simulated Asian mineral dust).. Basis: Dry weight: 'PTEs analysis was conducted directly on the dried garri samples' after oven-drying at 105 °C (p. 3, p. 5). Table 2 unit mg/g..

## Limitations

Reported Cr (up to 0.07 mg/g = 70 mg/kg) and Ni (0.23-1.17 mg/g = 230-1,170 mg/kg) are orders of magnitude above typical food values; unit 'mg/g' is internally confirmed by the authors' Fe/Zn conversions, so either the EDXRF quantification (mineral-dust calibration, single point, taped dried granules) is invalid for this matrix or the unit is wrong throughout. Values should not be used as occurrence estimates without independent confirmation. No LOD for EDXRF; S5 Cr 'ND' cannot be bounded. Text gives Ni range '0.23 to 1.21 mg/g' but Table 2 maximum mean is 1.17 (1.21 is the S2 SD). Table 4 Ni EDIs: adult values (e.g. S1 1.9E-03) do not equal C x 800 g / 70,000 g (1.22E-02) while child values do; S3 adult Ni EDI printed 0.0E+00. Cr EDIs reproduce. Collection/purchase dates not reported. Pb, Cd, As, Hg, Al, Sn and U were not reported (EDXRF element list limited to Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn), so their absence is not evidence of absence. Production/growing origin of the Japan-bought samples not stated beyond 'mainly imported from West Africa'.
