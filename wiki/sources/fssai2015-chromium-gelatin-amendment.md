This Food Safety and Standards Authority of India amendment modifies the 2011 Contaminants, Toxins and Residues regulations under the metal-contaminants section. It inserts a chromium maximum level for gelatin into regulation 2.1.1, clause (2), serial number 9. The limit is stated as 10 parts per million by weight, which is loaded as 10,000 µg/kg. The amendment does not specify hexavalent chromium, so the row is recorded as total chromium (Cr-total).
