# Estimating daily intakes of toxic metals and essential elements from infant formula, gruel, porridge and ready-to-eat baby food

## Summary

This journal article measured toxic metals by ICP-MS in infant formula, gruel, porridge, and ready-to-eat baby food sold in Sweden, in ready-to-eat form. The label is formula rows only. Gruel, porridge, and puree results are not formula results. Arsenic is total arsenic. Mercury is total mercury. Figure 1 states n=8 for infant formula at or under 6 months.

## Key numbers

Median cadmium in infant formulas 0.19 µg/kg (range 0.03 to 0.39 µg/kg). Among infant formulas at or under 6 months, arsenic ranged from 0.26 to 2.3 µg/kg. All infant formulas were above the mercury limit of detection, median 0.41 µg/kg, and the highest mercury in formula was 0.68 µg/kg. Half of the infant formulas up to 6 months (n=4) were below the lead limit of detection of 0.14 µg/kg. Six of eight infant formula samples at or under 6 months had tin below the limit of detection of 0.19 µg/kg, and the tin paragraph prints a highest concentration of 6.8 µg/kg. Nickel, aluminum, and chromium: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
