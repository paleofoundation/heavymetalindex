# Akther et al. 2015: Lead and chromium in canned and non-canned soft drinks, Chittagong

## Overview

Akther and colleagues measured lead and chromium by atomic absorption in 18 non-canned and 12 canned carbonated soft drinks bought in Chittagong. Lead was detected in about half the drinks, at no more than 0.017 mg/L, below the Codex 0.02 mg/L level the authors cite. The printed issue is dated 2015; the manifest listed 2016.

## Key numbers

- Non-canned soft drinks: lead 0.005 to 0.016 mg/L in 8 of 18; highest S-13, 0.016 ± 0.0042 mg/L (Table 1, p. 154).
- Canned soft drinks: lead 0.006 to 0.017 mg/L in 7 of 12; highest S-12, 0.017 ± 0.0005 mg/L (Table 2, p. 155).
- Chromium up to 0.019 mg/L in canned and 0.018 mg/L in non-canned drinks (Tables 1 and 2).

## Methods (brief)

Nitric acid digestion and AAS (Analytik Jena ZEEnit 700P). Values are means ± SEM. Lead calibration standards started at 0.40 ppm, far above the sample results, and no detection limit, recovery or reference material is reported.

## Evidence Fitness

One sample per brand, no LOD and a calibration range that does not cover the reported lead levels, so low values are uncertain. Drink flavors are not given, so this is general carbonated soft drink evidence, a proxy for diet-cola and lemon-lime-soft-drink, which are not linked. The PDF does not print the DOI. Results are per litre.
