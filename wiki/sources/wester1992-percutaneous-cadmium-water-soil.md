# Wester et al. 1992. Cadmium absorption through human skin from water and soil

Ronald Wester and colleagues applied cadmium-109 chloride to human cadaver skin in vitro, from water and from soil, for 16 hours. Columns in this copy are interleaved, so percent-of-dose cells are not transcribed. A sentence that appears to state a drinking-water figure in picograms per litre is treated as a damaged microgram sign and is not recorded as a limit.

## Key numbers

Water was prepared at 116 ppb cadmium. Soil from Yolo County was prepared at 13 ppb. The abstract discusses daily whole-body exposure at 116 ppb with 0.5% absorption, but the systemic-intake clause is broken across columns and is not given a number here.

## Methods (brief)

Human cadaver skin, cadmium-109, 16-hour in vitro exposure, water and soil vehicles.

## Evidence fitness

The applied concentrations are usable as the experimental doses. Absorption percentages from this scan are not.

## Limitations

Columns in this copy are interleaved, so the absorption table is not transcribed.
