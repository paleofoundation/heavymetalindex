This official-gazette source publishes Guyana's Fisheries (Products) Regulations 2023. The regulations were made under the Fisheries Act and published in the Official Gazette, Legal Supplement B, on 29 July 2023. Regulation 3 designates the Veterinary Public Health Unit of the Ministry of Health as the competent authority for enforcement.

The routeable heavy-metal rows are in Schedule Four, Part I, Table 1, "Heavy Metals." The table sets maximum residue limits for lead, cadmium, mercury, and arsenic in fishery and aquaculture products. The source contains both a commodity-specific `ppm` table and a generic `ppb` table; HMI records both as printed and keeps them distinct in the structured evidence notes.

The source does not specify methylmercury for the mercury rows, so HMI records mercury as `tHg`. The arsenic row in the generic `ppb` table does not specify inorganic arsenic, so HMI records it as `tAs`. Schedule Four later gives performance criteria that mention inorganic tin, but Table 1 does not set an inorganic-tin maximum residue limit; that non-routeable tin mention is recorded in the structured evidence record.

Values are recorded in the structured evidence record in `ug/kg`. The `ppm` values are converted as `1 ppm = 1 mg/kg = 1000 ug/kg`; the `ppb` values are converted as `1 ppb = 1 ug/kg`.
