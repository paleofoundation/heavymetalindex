# Prattay et al. 2024: Lead, cadmium, nickel and chromium in candies, chocolates and wrappers, Dhaka

## Overview

Prattay and colleagues bought 39 confectionery brands in Dhaka and measured lead, chromium, nickel and cadmium by AAS in 26 candy brands, 9 chocolate brands and their wrappers, alongside moisture, sulphated ash and bacterial counts. Lead in candies reached 2.40 µg/g and cadmium 0.44 µg/g. Wrappers carried far more lead, up to 2586.75 µg/g.

## Key numbers

- Hard candy brand S10: lead 2.40 ± 0.01 µg/g, nickel 14.78 ± 1.87 µg/g (Table 6, p. 7).
- Toffee brand S8: nickel 48.10 ± 4.20 µg/g (Table 6, p. 7).
- Chocolate brands: lead 0.86 to 1.18 µg/g where detected, cadmium 0.17 to 0.42 µg/g (Table 6, p. 7).
- Chocolate wrapper P30: lead 2586.75 ± 50.39 µg/g (Table 7, p. 8).

## Methods (brief)

Candy and chocolate were ground, oven-dried at 70 °C for 1 h and digested with nitric acid and hydrogen peroxide; wrappers were ashed at 650 °C before digestion. Flame AAS; QA rests on calibration standards (recoveries 101 to 102%). No reference material and no detection limits are reported.

## Evidence Fitness

Each brand is one pooled result of triplicate analyses, with no LOD and no reference material. Brands with no detectable lead show chromium near 11 to 12 µg/g, an odd pattern that may reflect analytical batches. Plain candies and chocolate are proxies for chocolate-nut-candy and fruit-snack-candy, and no biscuit was measured, so those thin pages are not linked. Wrapper values are packaging, not food.
