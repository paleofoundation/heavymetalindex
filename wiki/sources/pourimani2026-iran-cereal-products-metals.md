# Health risk assessment of heavy metals in Iranian cereal products using ICP-AES and a hybrid machine learning framework

## Summary

Pourimani and colleagues measured arsenic, cadmium, mercury, lead, and copper by ICP-AES in 13 packaged cereal products from Arak, Iran. The file is an unedited accepted manuscript, in press, provided before final publication. Table 4 is mg/kg wet weight. A zero means below detection.

## Key numbers

Abstract ranges, mg/kg: arsenic 0-0.834, cadmium 0.0002-0.014, mercury 0-0.502, lead 0-2.0, copper 0-1.203. Table 4 highest printed cadmium is 0.0240 (MF-13) and highest printed lead is 0.2000 (MF-13). Those table maxima do not match the abstract ranges. Sample codes are used. Trademark names are not copied.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
