# Health Risk Associated With Metals and Metalloids in Frequently Used Oil Perfumes Sold at Karu Market, Fct, Nigeria

## Summary

This Research Square preprint reports lead, chromium, cadmium, and arsenic in 10 oil perfumes from Karu market, Nigeria. The label for the measurement is preprint. A published article is identified at doi 10.1007/s44371-025-00270-4. The digits below were read from the preprint printing. Product names are omitted.

## Key numbers

The results text prints ranges of 0.16±0.00 to 0.27±0.00 mg/L for lead, 0.11±0.00 to 0.91±0.00 mg/L for chromium, 0.05±0.0 to 0.10±0.00 mg/L for cadmium, and 0.37±0.00 to 0.75±0.00 mg/L for arsenic. The paper prints the unit as mgL with a superscript minus before 1. The same paragraph prints means of cadmium 0.08±0.01, lead 0.23±0.04, chromium 0.29±0.30, and arsenic 0.62±0.12 without repeating the unit. The conclusion repeats those means. Mercury, nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Limit of detection and limit of quantification: Data gap. Arsenic and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking. Hazard quotients and cancer-risk calculations are not concentrations.
