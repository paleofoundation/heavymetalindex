# Limmer & Seyfferth 2024 — Rice irrigation and the arsenic–cadmium trade-off

## Verified source identity

Environmental Geochemistry and Health, volume 46, article 339. DOI: [10.1007/s10653-024-02116-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10653-024-02116-x). The previous record incorrectly named another journal and linked a different DOI; those fields were corrected on 17 October 2026.

## Findings and scope

The two-year Delaware mesocosm experiment compared six irrigation regimes. More aerobic management increased grain cadmium and reduced arsenic overall, with a weaker response for inorganic arsenic. No tested regime simultaneously minimized both contaminants. This is site-specific evidence, not a universally optimal irrigation prescription.

The design used 18 mesocosms and three replicates per regime. Total elements were measured by ICP-MS; grain arsenic speciation used IC-ICP-MS. See the primary text's Experimental design, Plant sampling and analysis, Results and Discussion sections.

## Assessment limits

Exact effect-size and uncertainty extraction, commercial transfer, costs and joint appraisal of yield and nutritional outcomes remain unfinished. Earlier approximate concentration ranges and an unqualified best-compromise recommendation are not retained as verified findings. The sample count represents repeated mesocosm-year observations, not independent farms.
