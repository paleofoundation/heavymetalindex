Korea MFDS Notice 2024-29 sets standards and specifications for utensils, containers and packages that contact food. The first part of the manifest PDF includes common manufacturing criteria with product-composition limits for lead and antimony in food-contact metals and lead in tin plating. The loaded rows cover those percent-by-weight content restrictions. A separate combined sum cap for lead, cadmium, mercury, and hexavalent chromium and the part 2 test-method/migration PDF are gap-logged rather than split into species-specific register rows.
