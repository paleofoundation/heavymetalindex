# Hossen et al. 2026 - vegetables from Noakhali

Hossen et al. quantified six heavy metals in nine commonly consumed vegetables and nine paired soils from integrated farms in Noakhali, Bangladesh, then calculated intake and risk metrics. This is direct vegetable-occurrence evidence paired with soil context.

## Key numbers

- Sample frame: 9 vegetable samples and 9 paired soil samples from integrated farms in Noakhali.
- The abstract identifies bitter gourd, lady finger, and pumpkin as the vegetables with the greatest heavy-metal accumulation.
- Table 2 reports fresh-weight vegetable concentrations including bitter gourd at Pb 4.78E-03 mg/kg, Cd 0.17 mg/kg, and Cr 3.10 mg/kg; lady finger at Pb 3.03E-05 mg/kg and Cd 0.27 mg/kg; pumpkin at Pb 3.77E-03 mg/kg and Cd 0.21 mg/kg.
- The abstract reports estimated average daily intake of lead and cadmium through vegetable consumption as 2.4128 and 0.3644 mg/person/day, respectively, exceeding FAO/WHO permissible limits.
- The abstract reports cancer-risk values above 10^-4 for lead in bitter gourd, lady finger, and pumpkin; chromium in bitter gourd; and cadmium in bitter gourd and lady finger.
- Table 5 reports TTHQ values above 1 for eggplant (1.9161), bitter gourd (8.4850), lady finger (5.1931), and pumpkin (4.4974).

## Methods (brief)

Vegetable and soil samples were collected in 2024 from nine integrated farms. Samples were oven-dried, wet-digested with nitric acid mixtures, and analyzed in triplicate by atomic absorption spectrophotometry. The study calculated EDI, THQ, TTHQ, and cancer risk using standard toxicological formulas.

## Implications

Certification: This contributes direct occurrence evidence for multiple vegetables, but the dataset mixes product types and should be routed by vegetable family rather than collapsed into one benchmark row.

Courses: Strong example of pairing occurrence data with intake and carcinogenic-risk calculations at source level.

App: Supports vegetable contamination context for Bangladesh-market produce, particularly cadmium, lead, and chromium flags.

## Verification notes

- The paper reports concentrations in fresh weight.
- Some AAS table values for lead are printed in scientific notation at very low levels for several vegetables while the risk tables report high THQ/TTHQ values. If these row-level values are reused in downstream risk synthesis, the original PDF tables should be rechecked carefully for unit consistency.
