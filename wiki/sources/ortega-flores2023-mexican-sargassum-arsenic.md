# Ortega-Flores et al. 2023 - Mexican Sargassum arsenic

Ortega-Flores et al. measured inorganic arsenic in stranded holopelagic *Sargassum* biomass from the Mexican Caribbean over a full seasonal cycle. The same sample set had total arsenic reported previously, and this paper adds iAs by morphotype and season. The values are raw stranded biomass on a dry-weight basis, not washed or processed seaweed food.

## Key numbers

The abstract and conclusion summarize the full dataset as iAs `12.7 to 62.9 mg kg-1 dw`, representing `14.1% to 81.7%` of TotAs. The introduction states that the same samples had previously reported TotAs `9.5 to 255 mg kg-1 dw`.

Section 3.1 reports iAs by morphotype in `mg kg-1 dw`:

| Morphotype | Annual mean iAs | Reported range |
|---|---:|---:|
| *Sargassum fluitans* III | 37.8 mg kg-1 dw | 20.6-71.5 mg kg-1 dw |
| *Sargassum natans* VIII | 37.7 mg kg-1 dw | 12.6-64.7 mg kg-1 dw |
| *Sargassum natans* I | 28.0 mg kg-1 dw | 11.9-47.7 mg kg-1 dw |

Section 3.1 also reports seasonal iAs summaries in `mg kg-1 dw`:

| Season | Mean iAs | Reported range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warm-dry season | 41.0 mg kg-1 dw | 22.0-71.5 mg kg-1 dw |
| Winter season | 33.8 mg kg-1 dw | 15.5-54.2 mg kg-1 dw |
| Rainy season | 31.3 mg kg-1 dw | 11.9-69.3 mg kg-1 dw |

Section 3.2 reports annual mean TotAs of `91.8 mg kg-1 dw` for *S. natans* VIII, `90.7 mg kg-1 dw` for *S. fluitans*, and `68.2 mg kg-1 dw` for *S. natans* I. The highest TotAs concentrations occurred during the rainy season, with August values above `200 mg kg-1 dw` for *S. natans* VIII and *S. fluitans*.

The iAs proportion increased over the study period. Section 3.2 reports iAs:TotAs ranges of `14-32%` for June-September and `56-80%` for March-May. It also states that iAs proportions remained below `40%` in samples with TotAs above `100 mg kg-1 dw`, whereas iAs proportions over `50%` occurred in samples with TotAs below `50 mg kg-1 dw`.

Table 1 summarizes the present study row as stranded Mexican Caribbean *S. fluitans*, *S. natans* I, and *S. natans* VIII with iAs `12.7-62.9 mg kg-1`, TotAs `21.7-255 mg kg-1`, and iAs:TotAs `14-82%`.

## Methods (brief)

Samples were collected monthly from June 2018 to May 2019 at Puerto Morelos, Quintana Roo, Mexico. Raw stranded biomass was collected from ten `0.5 x 0.5 m²` quadrats along a `50 m` transect, classified into the three morphotypes, visually cleaned of sand by hand, oven-dried at `60°C for 24 h`, crushed with an agate mortar, and stored for analysis. TotAs was measured by HG-AAS in the earlier Ortega-Flores et al. 2022 work, with IAEA-392 algae reference-material recovery greater than `90%` and As LoD `0.5 mg kg-1`. For iAs, dried ground biomass was extracted in ultrapure water under sonication for `30 min` at a `1:100` sample/water mass ratio, centrifuged, and analyzed by anodic stripping voltammetry using standard additions of As(V). The iAs method LoD was `0.2 µg L-1`, precision was `~6%`, NMIJ CRM 7405-b Sargassum fusiforme was used for accuracy control, and each extract was analyzed in triplicate.

## Implications

This source is high-value inorganic-arsenic evidence for raw stranded *Sargassum* biomass that may enter seaweed valorisation streams. It should not be treated as a ready-to-eat seaweed food result without preserving the raw stranded-biomass matrix and dry-weight basis. Downstream extraction should keep TotAs and iAs distinct, and should preserve the morphotype and seasonal stratification where row fit permits.
