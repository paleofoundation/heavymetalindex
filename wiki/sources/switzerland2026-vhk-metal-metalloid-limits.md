The Swiss Contaminants Ordinance (VHK, SR 817.022.15), consolidated 25 February 2026, contains Annex metal and metalloid maximum levels for foods. HMI records rows from the metal/metalloid table for inorganic arsenic, total arsenic salt, lead, cadmium, total chromium, nickel, total mercury, and inorganic tin. Copper and zinc rows are present in the source but not loaded because they are outside the prompt species vocabulary.
