# Heavy metal toxins in breakfast cereals: a baseline study using hybrid plasma mass spectrometry

## Summary

Pillay, Stephen, and Xavier measured arsenic, cadmium, mercury, and lead by ICP-MS in eight brands of breakfast cereals bought from retail outlets in the United Arab Emirates. Brand names are not printed. Measurements were in triplicate. The abstract uses ug/L and the results text uses ppb, and the two ranges do not match.

## Key numbers

Abstract, ug/L: arsenic 100-900, cadmium 8-50, mercury 270-370, lead 115-30000. Results, ppb: arsenic above 800 in sample 7 and 100-180 in the others; cadmium about 10-60; mercury about 250-400; lead above 10000 in sample 6 and 30000 in sample 7. Basis: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
