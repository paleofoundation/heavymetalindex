# Jabłońska-Czapla et al. 2024: Technology-critical elements in Polish river sediments

This study measured technology-critical elements, including gallium, in bottom sediments of a Polish river draining a zinc and lead mining area. Gallium reached 25.6 mg/kg, and contamination indices rated sediments as highly contaminated with gallium, but most gallium was locked in the residual mineral fraction.

## Key numbers

- Maximum sediment gallium 25.6 mg/kg (site BP3); average 11.8 mg/kg (Results and Abstract).
- Gallium was mainly in the residual fraction; at most 7% was in fraction F3 of the sequential extraction (Results).
- Cited range for gallium in the Silesia-Cracow area: 1.50 to 177 mg/kg (Results; secondary).

## Methods (brief)

Sediments were digested with nitric acid, hydrogen peroxide and hydrofluoric acid and analyzed by ICP-MS; mobility was assessed by sequential chemical extraction.

## Evidence fitness

Supports measured gallium in river sediment near mining and its low mobility. Does not address food or water intake.

## Limitations

One river system; sediment data do not translate directly to human exposure.
