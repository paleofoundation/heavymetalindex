# Jaiswal et al. 2025: Lithium phytoremediation by Crotalaria juncea and Cenchrus purpureus (out of scope)

## Overview

This paper is out of scope for the hemp ingredient page. The common name "sunn hemp" refers to Crotalaria juncea L., a leguminous plant in the family Fabaceae, which is taxonomically unrelated to Cannabis sativa L. (family Cannabaceae). This paper measures lithium (Li) accumulation and physiological responses in Crotalaria juncea and napier grass (Cenchrus purpureus) under lithium stress. Neither the species nor the analyte (lithium) are relevant to the Cannabis sativa heavy metals evidence base.

This source page is retained as an audit record of the deposit item and to document why it was excluded from the hemp ingredient page.

## Evidence fitness

Not applicable. Wrong species (Crotalaria juncea, not Cannabis sativa). Wrong analyte (lithium, not a heavy metal in the HMT&C analyte panel). No relevance to hemp food safety.
