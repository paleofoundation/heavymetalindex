# Rajaji et al. 2023: Lanthanum sulfide electrode for a herbicide in food and water

A strontium-doped lanthanum sulfide material was made by ultrasound-assisted synthesis and used as an electrode to detect the herbicide mesotrione in food and water samples. Lanthanum is part of the electrode; it was not measured as a contaminant.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to lanthanum exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Sonochemical synthesis, structural characterization and electrochemical detection with recovery tests in real samples.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: lanthanum is an electrode material; does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
