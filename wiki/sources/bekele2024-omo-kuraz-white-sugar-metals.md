# Determination of selected toxic heavy metals and analysis of proximate composition in white sugar manufactured from the Omo Kuraz Sugar Factory in Ethiopia

## Summary

This Research Square preprint reports lead and cadmium in white sugar from one factory, Omo Kuraz, across five annual batches. The label for the measurement is preprint, single factory.

## Key numbers

Average cadmium 0.429±0.031 mg/kg. The results text gives the cadmium range through the batch extremes, including 0.217 and 0.617 mg/kg. Average lead 0.296±0.013 mg/kg. Highest lead is printed as Batch-2023 at 0.508±0.013 mg/kg and lowest as Batch-2019 at 0.054±0.004 mg/kg. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
