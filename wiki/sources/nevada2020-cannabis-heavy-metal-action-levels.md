Nevada Cannabis Compliance Board regulations set heavy-metal action levels for cannabis product categories including usable cannabis, infused pre-rolls, wet cannabis for extraction, extracts, and liquid cannabis products. The recurring table values are arsenic 2 ppm, cadmium 0.82 ppm, lead 1.2 ppm, and mercury 0.4 ppm. Arsenic and mercury are not speciated in the table and are loaded as total species.
