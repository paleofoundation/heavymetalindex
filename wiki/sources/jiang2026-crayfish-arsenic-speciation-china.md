# Jiang et al. 2026 : Arsenic speciation, cooking stability, bioaccessibility, and risk in Chinese crayfish (Procambarus clarkii)

This 2026 paper measured total arsenic (tAs) and six arsenic species in 60 crayfish abdominal muscle samples from the Yangtze River basin, China, and also assessed cooking-induced changes and in vitro bioaccessibility. All values are wet weight. The paper provides one of the first comprehensive speciation datasets for crayfish from a large multi-province survey.

## Key numbers

tAs in raw crayfish (wet weight, n=60):
- Mean 109.3 µg/kg; median 91.1 µg/kg; range 53.6-419.9 µg/kg; P90 160.8 µg/kg

Arsenic species (mean, µg/kg wet weight):
- Arsenobetaine (AsB): mean 44.2 (41.3% of tAs); range 18.9-106.6; P90 68.4
- DMA: mean 22.8 (21.3%); range 11.1-59.2; P90 36.1
- Arsenocholine (AsC): mean 16.6 (15.6%); max 276.7
- As(III): mean 9.0 (8.4%); max 55.6; P90 13.6
- As(V): mean 2.3 (5.4%)
- MMA: mean 3.4 (5.4%); max 25.1; P90 8.8
- iAs (As(III) + As(V)): mean 12.5 (11.6%); range ND-80.7; P90 16.4
- Speciation mass balance: 92.7% of tAs mean

Regulatory context: mean iAs (12.5 µg/kg) is well below EU ML for crustaceans (100 µg/kg) and Chinese national standard (500 µg/kg); maximum iAs detected (80.7 µg/kg) also below both MLs.

Cooking effects (dry-weight basis):
- Frying: no significant change in tAs or iAs
- Steaming: no significant change in tAs or iAs
- Boiling in 2% NaCl: tAs reduced 32.2% and iAs reduced 28.2% (leaching into cooking water, p < 0.05)

Bioaccessibility (in vitro gastrointestinal digestion, intestinal phase):
- tAs: mean 82.0% bioaccessible
- iAs: mean 95.3% bioaccessible (substantially more soluble than tAs overall)

Risk assessment:
- Mean iAs EDI at mean consumption (10.1 g/day, 60 kg BW): THQ < 1 for all scenarios
- High consumption + maximum iAs sample: TCR = 1.19 x 10-3, exceeding the 10-4 concern threshold (using updated EPA OSF 32 (mg/kg/day)-1, 2025)

## Methods

tAs by ICP-MS (Agilent 7700x) after microwave HNO3/H2O2 digestion; arsenic speciation by HPLC-ICP-MS (Hamilton PRP-X100, 250x4.1mm, 10 µm; Agilent 1260 HPLC + ICP-MS); 6 species quantified; CRM DORM-4 (fish protein); recovery 93.1-106.2%; LODs 1.1-2.0 µg/kg per species; in vitro digestion following Brodkorb et al. INFOGEST protocol.

## Implications

Provides the first multi-province tAs and iAs speciation dataset for Procambarus clarkii from China, the world's largest crayfish-producing country. The organic arsenic-dominated speciation profile (AsB 41.3%, DMA 21.3%, AsC 15.6%) means that total arsenic substantially overestimates actual iAs toxicological risk. Boiling in saline water reduces iAs by approximately 28%, relevant to practical risk reduction. The high iAs bioaccessibility (95.3% intestinal) confirms that iAs in crayfish is efficiently absorbed and must be the focus of risk characterisation rather than tAs alone.
