# Jurowski et al. 2022: Total chromium in thyme herbal medicinal liquids from Polish pharmacies

Six oral thyme-herb liquid products from Polish pharmacies were analyzed for total chromium by electrothermal atomic absorption spectrometry. Chromium was found in all six at 2.0 to 6.0 µg/L, and estimated daily intake from labeled use was below 200 ng/day.

## Key numbers

- Total chromium: 2.0 ± 0.3 µg/L (product E) to 6.0 ± 0.4 µg/L (product F); A 2.3 ± 0.2 and C 2.4 ± 0.3 µg/L (Results, p. 2986; Figure 1).
- LOD 1.65 µg/L and LOQ 4.95 µg/L; certified reference material BCR-482 (lichen) measured 0.137 against 0.134 mg/kg certified, recovery 97.81% (p. 2985).
- Estimated chromium per single dose 20.15 to 63.45 ng; per day 40.30 to 181.41 ng (Tables 3 and 4, pp. 2985 to 2986).

## Methods (brief)

ETAAS with a L’vov platform and a dedicated temperature program; tiered assessment comparing concentration, single-dose and daily exposure with the ICH Q3D oral permitted daily exposure.

## Evidence fitness

Supports low total-chromium concentrations in six thyme herbal liquids sold in Polish pharmacies. It does not establish chromium species or wider market prevalence.

## Limitations

Several reported concentrations lie below the stated LOQ of 4.95 µg/L; only one lot per product; the permitted daily exposure is printed both as 10,700 µg/day and 10,700 mg/day.
