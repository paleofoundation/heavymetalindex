# Heavy Metal Concentrations in Dairy Products from Sheep Milk Collected in Two Regions of Southern Italy

## Summary

This journal article measured lead, cadmium, mercury, and chromium by graphite-furnace atomic absorption spectrophotometry in ewe milk. The label is ewe milk, dry basis. n=8. Cheese and ricotta results are not milk concentrations.

## Key numbers

Dry basis, µg/g dry weight: chromium 0.14±0.113, cadmium 0.07±0.019, lead 0.18±0.069, mercury 0.002±0.005. Arsenic, nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Chromium and mercury are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
