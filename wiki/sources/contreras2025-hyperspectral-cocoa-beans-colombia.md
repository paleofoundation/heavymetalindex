# Contreras et al. 2025 -- Hyperspectral dataset of Colombian cocoa beans with cadmium annotation

## Overview

This Colombian-French study presents the CoCoaSpec dataset, a multimodal hyperspectral imaging resource that integrates hyperspectral images of cocoa beans at 350 to 1,000 nm spectral range with physicochemical measurements including cadmium content. The dataset comprises 19 scenes of dried cocoa beans acquired under both open (invasive cut-test) and closed (non-invasive) conditions using four cameras (EOS M50 RGB, Toucan multispectral, Ultris SR5 hyperspectral, Specim IQ push-broom hyperspectral). Cadmium was measured by microwave digestion followed by atomic absorption spectrometry following UNE-EN 15550:2018 and EU Regulation No. 488/2014. The dataset supports development of AI-driven non-invasive quality assessment and cadmium detection models for cocoa.

## Key numbers

Cadmium measured in four batch subsets of cocoa beans from El Carmen de Chucurri, Santander, Colombia. The study reports cadmium content as one of the physicochemical annotations integrated with hyperspectral signatures. Specific cadmium values by batch are available in the dataset supplementary materials; the study design used batches with fermentation times of 96, 144, 192, and 264 hours. Polyphenol concentration quantified by UV-Vis spectrophotometry (ABTS and DPPH assays). Moisture by gravimetric analysis.

Fermentation classification following Colombian standard NTC 1252:2021: well-fermented (brown interior, structured), partially fermented (intermediate brown-violet coloration), poorly fermented (purple-pink interior). The Colombian standard requires at least 87 of 100 beans to be well or partially fermented, and moisture below 10% for high-quality batch classification.

## Methods

Cocoa beans from ASOCAPAYARI farm (Association of Farmers Neighbors of the Serrania de Los Yarigues National Natural Park), El Carmen de Chucurri, Santander, Colombia. Four batches (900 g each) of CNN-51, ICS-95, and TCS-01 varieties received monthly from January to December 2024. Each batch split into training (70%) and testing (30%) subsets. Optical laboratory: four cameras covering 350 to 1,000 nm. Cut test via Swiss guillotine. Cadmium measured by microwave digestion and atomic absorption spectrometry per UNE-EN 15550:2018. Dataset deposited at Zenodo.

## Implications

The CoCoaSpec dataset enables development of non-invasive machine-learning models for cadmium prediction in Colombian cocoa beans from hyperspectral signatures, eliminating the need for destructive chemical analysis in routine quality control. The integration of cadmium data with fermentation status and polyphenol content across four batches provides a basis for understanding how post-harvest processing conditions affect cadmium detectable signatures.
