# Ibarra 2010, lead in imported candies, thesis, not used as a hard-candy concentration

This is a thesis, not a peer-reviewed article. Hard-candy lead means in the thesis depend on how non-detects are entered (substitution at 0, 0.005, or 0.01 μg/g, or a detectable-only rule). The detectable count disagrees between an appendix (18) and Table 9 (19). No recovery percentage is printed. A substituted zero is not a measured concentration, so this note does not publish a hard-candy lead mean from the thesis.
