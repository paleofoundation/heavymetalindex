# Nowar et al. 2024: Systematic review of heavy metals in Bangladeshi vegetables

## Overview

Nowar and colleagues searched the literature for cross-sectional studies of heavy metals in Bangladeshi vegetables, compiled fresh-weight means, and estimated daily intakes, hazard quotients and cancer risks. It is a systematic review with no new measurements, so it serves as context only.

## Key numbers

- Compiled mean ranges across vegetables: arsenic 0.02 to 0.45, lead "Not detectable (ND)-9.45", cadmium 0.00 to 0.51 mg/kg fresh weight (Abstract).
- Hazard quotients above 1 for arsenic and lead in some vegetables, including cabbage, string bean, bottle gourd and green papaya (Abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Database and manual searches, screening in Rayyan by two independent reviewers, inclusion of English-language peer-reviewed cross-sectional studies reporting fresh-weight data, then pooled means and US EPA risk formulas.

## Evidence Fitness

Review-level context: the compiled values are quotes of other papers and are not recorded as findings. Primary studies it cites should be ingested from their own texts. It does not measure a mixed-vegetable product, so it is not linked to vegetables-mixed.
