# EFSA CONTAM Panel 2026 - Fish consumption frequency contributing to methylmercury exposure

## Summary

This EFSA Scientific Opinion assesses the frequency of consumption of different fish, crustacean, and mollusc species in EU member states and their contribution to methylmercury (MeHg) dietary exposure. The Opinion also examines consumer awareness of national advisories for reducing MeHg exposure through fish consumption. This is a regulatory evidence synthesis document from the EFSA CONTAM Panel and constitutes the primary EU regulatory evidence base for understanding which seafood species and consumption patterns drive methylmercury intake in European populations.
