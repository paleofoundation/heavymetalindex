# Makoś-Chełstowska et al. 2021. A review of toxic compounds in disposable baby diapers

Patrycja Makoś-Chełstowska, Aleksandra Kurowska-Susdorf, and Justyna Płotka-Wasylka review analytical methods and health concerns for disposable baby diapers. The copy is a journal pre-proof. Concentrations of phthalates, organotins, formaldehyde, and metals that the review cites from other papers are not copied onto this page. A cited number is not this review's measurement.

## Key numbers

The review states that, at the time of writing, most countries have no legal regulations defining a maximum permissible concentration of individual xenobiotics in diapers. No metal limit adopted by the authors as their own instrument is printed as a number here.

## Methods (brief)

Literature review of monitoring methods. Not a new laboratory survey.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports the regulatory-gap statement above. It does not add occurrence numbers.

## Limitations

Pre-proof. Cited studies would need their own source pages before their numbers are used.
