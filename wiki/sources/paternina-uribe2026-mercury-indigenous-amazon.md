# Paternina-Uribe et al. 2026 - Mercury exposure in Colombian Amazon indigenous communities

## Summary

Paternina-Uribe et al. assessed mercury exposure and its determinants among indigenous communities in the Colombian Amazon. This study documents high mercury burden in populations whose diet relies heavily on locally caught fish and who live near artisanal gold mining (ASGM) operations. ASGM is a primary anthropogenic mercury source; the Minamata Convention targets this sector. The study provides biomonitoring evidence of mercury exposure in a vulnerable subsistence-fishing population with high dietary fish intake, illustrating the highest end of the fish-mercury exposure spectrum.
