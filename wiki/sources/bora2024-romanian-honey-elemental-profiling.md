# Bora et al. 2024: Elemental profile of Romanian honey

This survey measured 30 elements in 61 honey samples from eight regions of Romania by ICP-MS. Gallium was among the elements analyzed but was not detected in any sample.

## Key numbers

- Gallium (isotope 70Ga) was analyzed but not detected in any of the 61 honey samples (Table notes, pp. 10 to 11).
- Limit of quantification for gallium: 0.04 µg/L in the measured solution (Table notes, p. 11).

## Methods (brief)

Honey samples were digested and analyzed by ICP-MS for 30 elements, with results compared across region, honey type and year.

## Evidence fitness

Supports a non-detect result for gallium in one food, honey, at a stated quantification limit. Does not support any dietary intake estimate.

## Limitations

One food from one country. The limit of quantification is stated per liter of measured solution, not per kilogram of honey.
