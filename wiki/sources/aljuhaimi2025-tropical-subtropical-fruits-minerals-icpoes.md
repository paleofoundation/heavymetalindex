# AlJuhaimi et al. 2025 — Minerals and toxic elements in 15 tropical and subtropical fruits, Konya Turkey

This study measured macro- and microelements and toxic elements in 15 fruits purchased at retail in Konya, Turkey in 2023. Samples were dried at 70 °C for 24 hours and ground before microwave-assisted acid digestion (HNO3/H2O2) and ICP-OES analysis (Agilent-5110). All reported values are on a dry-weight basis. Cape gooseberry (listed in Table 1 as "Cape Gooseberry / Peruvian groundcherry," scientific name given as *Peruvian groundcherry* rather than *Physalis peruviana*, though the common and commercial synonymy is unambiguous) is one of the 15 fruits. This source is the sole peer-reviewed occurrence dataset for cape gooseberry in the current HMI corpus.

## Key numbers

All values are mean ± standard deviation on a dry-weight basis (µg/g dry weight = mg/kg dry weight = 1,000 µg/kg dry weight). Cape gooseberry moisture was 80.55 ± 1.21% (Table 2), enabling approximate wet-weight conversion by multiplying dry-weight values by (1 − 0.8055) = 0.195.

The units for LOD/LOQ in the methods section are stated as mg/kg. At face value, the reported cape gooseberry lead value of 0.399 mg/kg dry weight falls below the stated lead LOD of 5.277 mg/kg. Similarly, the reported nickel value of 0.038 mg/kg falls below the stated nickel LOD of 1.578 mg/kg. The paper does not address these apparent sub-LOD detections. It is possible the LOD/LOQ table reflects solution-phase limits before back-calculation to the sample matrix, or that the instrument limits were stated in a different concentration unit. Neither the methods section nor the supplementary material resolves this discrepancy. As a result, the lead and nickel values for cape gooseberry in this study are treated as potentially sub-LOQ and given low weight in the contamination profile.

## Methods (brief)

Retail fruits from Konya, Turkey; purchased 2023. Drying at 70 °C for 24 h; grinding; microwave digestion at 210 °C, 200 PSI; 0.2 g sample in 5 mL concentrated HNO3 + 2 mL H2O2 (30%), diluted to 20 mL. ICP-OES (Agilent-5110). Three replicates per fruit. Statistics by ANOVA (JMP). No certified reference material (CRM) recovery is reported. No LOD/LOQ validation against matrix blanks is described. Speciation for arsenic (inorganic vs. organic) was not performed; total arsenic only. Mercury was not measured in this study.

## Evidence fitness

This source supports dry-weight occurrence data for total arsenic, cadmium, total chromium, cobalt, and molybdenum in cape gooseberry. The total arsenic value (3.05 µg/g dry) is above the instrument LOQ, and cadmium (0.032 µg/g) is above its LOQ. These are usable single-study estimates for a matrix with no other published occurrence data. The lead and nickel values appear to fall below or near the instrument LOD as stated in the methods and should not be used as quantified concentration estimates without verification; they are flagged [VERIFY table check] in the key numbers above. This source does not support fresh-weight estimates without explicit moisture-based conversion using the reported 80.55% moisture. It does not support inorganic arsenic or methylmercury speciation. No CRM recovery is reported, introducing unknown method accuracy.

## Limitations

No certified reference material or recovery data are reported, leaving method accuracy unverified. The discrepancy between reported lead and nickel values and the stated instrument LOD/LOQ is unresolved and limits confidence in those specific analytes. Sample size is n=1 lot per fruit type (triplicate analysis of one composite), precluding statistical characterization of between-lot or between-region variability. Geographic provenance of the cape gooseberry at retail is not reported. All values are dry weight; fresh-weight comparison requires moisture conversion. Mercury, inorganic arsenic, and methylmercury are not measured.
