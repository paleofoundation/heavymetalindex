# Analysis of heavy metal characteristics and health risks of edible mushrooms in the mid-western region of China

## Summary

This journal article measured arsenic, cadmium, chromium, mercury, and lead by ICP-MS in market cultivated edible mushrooms from 10 cities in Shaanxi, China. n=244. Auricularia auricula and Tremella fuciformis are dry; the other species are fresh. Table 4 means are mg/kg. Arsenic and mercury are totals. Chromium is not speciated.

## Key numbers

Table 4 means: lead 0.0624±0.0088 mg/kg (ND to 1.6637), cadmium 0.3215±0.0322 mg/kg, total arsenic 0.1728±0.0985 mg/kg, total mercury 0.0148±0.0041 mg/kg, chromium 0.1165±0.0114 mg/kg. Exceedance counts in the text and the table disagree (lead 2 versus 4 of 244; arsenic 12 versus 20 of 244). Both counts are kept. Nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
