# Chen and Zhong 2024: A lanthanum amino acid chelate for copper-stressed rice

The authors made a lanthanum chelate with amino acids from soybean protein hydrolysate and tested it on rice seedlings exposed to copper. At 20 mg/L the chelate outperformed inorganic lanthanum salts in promoting growth and reduced copper in rice tissues.

## Key numbers

- Maximum chelation rate 94.95% (abstract).
- A 20 mg/L chelate treatment reduced copper in seedling tissues and outperformed inorganic lanthanum salts (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Chelate synthesis and characterization by UV-vis, FTIR and electron microscopy; hydroponic seedling tests with copper stress.

## Evidence fitness

Shows that new lanthanum agrochemicals are being developed for crops. It reports no lanthanum residue data and does not support exposure statements.

## Limitations

Seedling hydroponics; no lanthanum analysis of plant tissue reported in the abstract.
