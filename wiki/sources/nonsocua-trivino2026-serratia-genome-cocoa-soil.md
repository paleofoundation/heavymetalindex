# Nonsocua-Trivino and Brandao 2026 -- Draft genome of Serratia surfactantfaciens strain 5b, cadmium-precipitating bacterium from cocoa soil

## Overview

This Colombian genome announcement presents the draft genome sequence of Serratia surfactantfaciens strain 5b, a cadmium-precipitating bacterium isolated from cadmium-bearing cocoa farm soil in El Carmen de Chucuri, Santander, Colombia. Strain 5b was previously characterized for its ability to immobilize cadmium via microbially induced carbonate precipitation (MICP) and to produce bioactive secondary metabolites including prodigiosin and the lipopeptide biosurfactant serrawettin W2. In aqueous assays, strain 5b grew in the presence of up to 2 mM Cd(II) and removed more than 99% of 0.15 mM Cd(II) within 96 to 144 hours by MICP. The draft genome comprises 4.99 Mbp across 28 contigs (N50 554,169 bp, L50 3), with 59% GC content and 99.82% assembly completeness (CheckM).

## Key numbers

Genome assembly: 4.99 Mbp, 28 contigs, N50 554,169 bp, L50 3, GC content 59%, 99.82% completeness (CheckM v1.2.3), contamination 0.8%. Annotation (NCBI PGAP v6.10): 4,781 genes, including 4,639 protein-coding sequences, 16 non-coding RNAs, 81 tRNA genes, 12 rRNA genes, 32 pseudogenes. No plasmid-related contigs detected (ABRicate/PlasmidFinder v2.1.6). Average nucleotide identity (ANI) to S. surfactantfaciens reference genomes: 99.29%, confirming taxonomic assignment.

Cadmium removal capacity (from prior characterization, cited in this paper): greater than 99% removal of 0.15 mM Cd(II) within 96 to 144 hours in aqueous medium by MICP; growth in up to 2 mM Cd(II); inhibited at 4 mM Cd(II). GenBank accession for whole-genome shotgun project: JBPSCT000000000.1; BioProject PRJNA1291823; BioSample SAMN49979229.

## Methods

DNA extraction: Wizard Genomic DNA Purification Kit from bacterial culture grown 24 h in tryptic soy broth at 30 degrees C. Quality assessed by NanoDrop 200c. Sequencing: Illumina MiSeq paired-end 2 times 150 bp (Illumina DNA Prep kit, MiSeq v.2 reagent kit). Read processing: FastQC v1.12.0, Trimmomatic v.0.36 (sliding window 3:20, HEADCROP 15). Assembly: SPAdes v4.0.0 (k-mer values 39 to 99, contigs above 500 bp retained). Completeness and contamination: CheckM v1.2.3. Annotation: NCBI PGAP v6.10. Plasmid detection: ABRicate v1.0.1 / PlasmidFinder v2.1.6.

## Implications

The genome resource for S. surfactantfaciens strain 5b supports mechanistic studies of cadmium precipitation and heavy-metal resistance, providing sequence data for the MICP pathway genes and antimicrobial secondary metabolite biosynthesis clusters. The 99.82% assembly completeness with low contamination (0.8%) makes this a reliable reference genome for future comparative genomics of Serratia strains in heavy-metal-contaminated cacao environments. The strain was isolated from Santander, Colombia, one of the regions with documented high soil cadmium concentrations in Latin American cacao-growing areas.
