The Australian Drinking Water Guidelines are NHMRC-endorsed national guidance for drinking-water quality management in Australia. Version 4.0 was updated in June 2025 and includes health-based guideline values for antimony, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, nickel, and uranium, plus an aesthetic guideline for acid-soluble aluminium. Chromium is recorded as `Cr-VI` because the fact sheet states the guideline for hexavalent chromium, with total chromium used as a trigger for separate speciation analysis. Uranium is documented on the regulation page but gap-logged rather than loaded because `U` is outside the current `regulatory_limits.csv` metal-species vocabulary. Controlled-vocabulary values from this source are captured in `data/evidence/regulatory_limits.csv` and documented at [[regulations/australia-nhmrc-adwg-6-v4-drinking-water]].
