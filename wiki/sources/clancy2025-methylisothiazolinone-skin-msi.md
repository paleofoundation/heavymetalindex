# Clancy 2025. Mass spectrometry imaging of methylisothiazolinone in skin

This paper maps the organic preservative 2-methyl-4-isothiazolin-3-one in ex vivo human skin by time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry. It reports no metal concentrations. numbers is empty.

## Key numbers

This source does not report a metal concentration or a metal limit.


## Methods (brief)

MI (95 percent) was diluted to 0.0175 M, stated as equivalent to 0.2 percent w/v diagnostic patch testing, and 20 uL was applied to 11 mm skin biopsies. Donors were two women, both 42 years, abdominal skin, Fitzpatrick type 2.

Detection and reporting: not a metal assay; background ion at m/z 116.0 discussed but no metal LOD

Basis: applied solution on ex vivo skin
