# Analysis of migrated toxic elements from branded and non branded children toys especially teethers from Ranchi, Jharkhand

## Summary

This Research Square preprint reports a migration test of toxic elements from children's toys, especially teethers, from Ranchi. The label is teethers, migration test, and the record is a preprint. The table covers 12 items. The conclusion says 26 toys. Both counts are kept. Shape words are used instead of product names. A 120 mg/kg figure in the discussion is a calculation, not a measured result.

## Key numbers

Table lead highest 3.89 mg/kg. The discussion says 10.50 mg/kg, which is not in the table. Chromium in one white teether 10.11 mg/kg. Mercury in one dog-shaped teether 11.58 mg/kg. Arsenic is printed 0.00 on the table rows. Aluminum, nickel, and tin: Data gap. Chromium and mercury are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
