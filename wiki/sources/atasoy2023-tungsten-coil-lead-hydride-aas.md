# Atasoy 2023: A tungsten-coil trap for measuring lead by atomic absorption

This analytical chemistry paper develops a method that traps gaseous lead hydride on a heated platinum-coated tungsten coil before atomic absorption measurement. The analyte is lead; tungsten is only the trap material. The method was checked with certified reference materials and applied to drinking water and fish.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to tungsten exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Hydride generation with in situ preconcentration on an externally heated tungsten coil, compared with graphite furnace atomic absorption; accuracy checked with NIST SRM 1640a and DOLT-5.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure to tungsten: the paper measures lead and does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for tungsten.

## Limitations

No tungsten was measured in any sample.
