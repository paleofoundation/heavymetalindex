# Bora et al. 2020, wine metals and storage, white cadmium prose only

Bora and colleagues measured metals in micro-vinification wines stored at 5 C and 25 C. Red cultivars are excluded. Recovery was 84.6% to 99.8%. Cadmium limit of detection is 0.0202 µg/L and lead is 0.0003 µg/L.

## Key numbers

The results text names Feteasca Alba cadmium as 0.09 ± 0.01 μg/L. The same sentence also names Merlot at 0.09 ± 0.01 μg/L. Merlot is red and is not used as a white value. A later table block repeats a copper, iron, and manganese header while its first-column figures include 0.09 ± 0.01 on a Feteasca Alba 2015 semi-dry row and 12.50 ± 1.50 in the third column of that row. Because the header and the prose do not agree, this note does not assign the storage-temperature grid. Data gap: white lead by storage temperature is not copied from that mislabeled block. Cells printed UDL are under the limit of detection and are not zeros.

## Methods (brief)

0.2 mL wine was microwave digested and measured by ICP-MS. Each cell is a mean of n = 3.

## Evidence fitness

The named Feteasca Alba cadmium figure supports a white-cultivar cadmium note. The storage grid is not used.

## Limitations

The text-layer header of the cadmium block is not reliable. Red rows are excluded. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin were not measured.
