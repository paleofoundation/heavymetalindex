# Aquino Hancco et al. 2026 - mercury, cadmium, and lead in Lima canned tuna and fish

## Overview

Aquino Hancco and colleagues report mercury, cadmium, and lead measurements in canned tuna and fresh fish sold at the Central Market of Lima, Peru, during 2023. The paper reports summary tables rather than full sample-level results. It also contains draft-like "figure suggestions" text inside the Results section; this page therefore treats the source as lower-tier market-surveillance evidence until independently audited.

The paper does not report methylmercury speciation. Mercury values are total mercury.

## Methods (brief)

The authors collected canned tuna (*Thunnus* spp.) and fresh fish species, including mackerel (*Scomber japonicus*) and hake (*Merluccius gayi*), from Central Market of Lima vendors between April and August 2023. Each sample was obtained in triplicate. Samples were transported at `4 C`, homogenized, acid-digested, and analyzed by AAS after blank and standard checks.

Descriptive statistics were calculated as mean +/- standard deviation. ANOVA was used to compare metal concentrations among species, with `p < 0.05` used for significance.

## Implications

This source contributes Peru-market occurrence context for canned tuna and fresh marine fish. The clearest product signal is a canned-tuna lead outlier (`3.113 mg/kg`) and cadmium values up to `0.269 mg/kg`; total mercury in canned tuna was reported below the source's `0.5 mg/kg` comparator.

The paper's summary-table format limits downstream extraction: cadmium and lead in canned tuna are reported as selected-set ranges and examples rather than full sample-level tables. The source does not support methylmercury-specific occurrence values.
