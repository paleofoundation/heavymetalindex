# Turner 2018 ,  Hazardous elements in second-hand plastic toys

EN 71-3 migration was run on second-hand plastic toys. Migratable antimony exceeded 100 µg/g in at least one component (PDF p.1-2).

## Key numbers

The exceedances of EU limits that the paper names are for cadmium or lead, not for antimony. Brand names are not repeated.

## Methods (brief)

EN 71-3 on second-hand plastic toys.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the antimony migration statement and the statement that the named legal exceedances are not for antimony.

## Limitations

Not a total-content result and not a certification comparison.
