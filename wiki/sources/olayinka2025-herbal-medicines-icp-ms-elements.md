# Olayinka et al. 2025 - Essential and toxic elements in herbal medicines by ICP-MS

## Summary

This study used ICP-MS to determine essential and toxic elements in a range of herbal medicines, including peppermint (Mentha x piperita) preparations. The study aimed to characterize the elemental profile of commercially available herbal products and assess whether concentrations of toxic metals fell within or exceeded recognized guidance values. Peppermint was one of the matrices analyzed alongside several other herbal species.

## Key numbers

Specific numeric results for peppermint extracted from this paper are reported at the sample level. The PDF text describes the methodology and overall patterns; mint-specific tabular values are present in the paper. The paper title and abstract indicate coverage of toxic elements (lead, cadmium, arsenic, nickel, chromium, aluminium) and essential elements in herbal medicines analyzed by ICP-MS with multi-element standard solutions and CRM verification. The paper acknowledges LOD and LOQ values experimentally calculated as 10 and 3 times the standard deviation of blank determination, respectively. Recovery from CRMs was between 90 and 99% with RSD below 10% (stated in method section). ## Methods

ICP-MS (instrument type not specified in retrieved pages). Microwave-assisted acid digestion with HNO3 and H2O2. Calibration with multi-element standard solutions, linearity R > 0.999. CRM used for accuracy assessment. Ten replicates per sample. LOD = 10 x SD(blank); LOQ = 3 x SD(blank).

## Implications

- Certification: multi-element coverage for herbal medicines including peppermint; supports lead, cadmium, arsenic, and nickel as the primary analytes for herbal mint products.
- App: herbal medicine matrix; context only for dried herb contamination profiles.
