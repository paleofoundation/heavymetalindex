# GB 2762-2025 — Food contaminant maximum levels

## Identity and edition

This is the Chinese original of GB 2762-2025. The cover gives 2 September 2025 as its issue date and 2 September 2026 as its implementation date. The foreword replaces GB 2762-2022 and amendment 1. The National Health Commission's Announcement No. 6 of 2025 lists the standard among the national food safety standards it released.

## Verified honey provision

Section 4.1 and Table 1 express lead limits as Pb in mg/kg. The honey row (蜂蜜), in the other-food category on printed page 4, gives 0.5 mg/kg. Section 3.4 states the general edible-portion basis. The equivalent concentration is 500 µg/kg; this unit conversion changes neither the food scope nor the measurement basis.

## Evidence Fitness and limitations

The primary instrument supports its exact regulatory provisions and edition dates. It is not an occurrence survey and provides no measured honey concentrations or evidence of health effects. The honey lead entry does not establish a limit for another analyte or another food. [[regulations/china-gb-2762-2025-contaminants]] records this selected provision; historical citations to the [[regulations/china-gb-2762-2022-contaminants|2022 edition]] retain their original temporal scope.
