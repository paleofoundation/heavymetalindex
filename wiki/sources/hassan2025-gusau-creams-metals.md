# Heavy Metals Content in Selected Cream Marketed in Gusau Metropolis, Zamfara State, Nigeria, and Associated Health Risk

## Summary

Hassan, Hussaini, and Rafia measured zinc, nickel, and lead in five beauty creams bought in Gusau, Nigeria, each in triplicate. Codes A to E are used. Product names are omitted. The paper calls the matrix beauty cream. Basis: Data gap.

## Key numbers

Table 2, mg/kg: A zinc 2.225±0.90, nickel 6.410±4.290, lead 6.750±4.650; B 3.696±1.678, 6.550±3.290, 6.170±2.180; C 4.480±1.850, 6.130±1.740, 3.140±2.740; D 5.740±4.460, 14.50±19.30, 2.833±1.457; E 4.260±1.900, 14.90±18.40, 34.80±48.80.
