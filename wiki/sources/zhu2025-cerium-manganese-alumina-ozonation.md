# Zhu et al. 2025: Cerium and manganese oxide catalyst for removing humic acid from water

Activated alumina coated with cerium and manganese oxides was used with ozone and hydrogen peroxide to remove humic acid, a precursor of disinfection byproducts. The catalyst removed 95.5% of humic acid under optimal conditions and stayed effective over 12 reuse cycles.

## Key numbers

- Humic acid removal 95.5% under optimal conditions; above 90% after 12 cycles (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Catalyst preparation by impregnation, characterization by SEM, EDS and XPS, and bench-scale ozonation and adsorption kinetics.
