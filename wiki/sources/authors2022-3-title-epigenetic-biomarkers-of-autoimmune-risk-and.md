- 91 The organic heavy metal methylmercury (MeHg) is an environmental pollutant of global public health concern1-3. MeHg is
- 92 formed by microbial methylation of inorganic mercury that deposits in waterways after atmospheric emission from coal-fired
- 93 power plants and burning of gold-mercury amalgams during artisanal gold mining3,4. After conversion to the organic form,
- 94 MeHg bioconcentrates and biomagnifies in the aquatic food web and humans are exposed via consumption of contaminated
- 95 fish and seafood4-6. Clinically apparent MeHg neurotoxicity, including gross motor impairment, was first documented after
