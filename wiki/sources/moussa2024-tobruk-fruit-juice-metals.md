# Moussa 2024: Metals in commercial fruit juices, Tobruk, Libya

## Overview

Moussa measured ten elements by flame AAS in eight commercial fruit juice brands bought in Tobruk, Libya. Lead was found in one juice; cadmium, cobalt, chromium and mercury were not detected in any, though no detection limits are given.

## Key numbers

- Lead 0.046 mg/L in one of eight juices, the other seven not detected (Table 1; Figure 1).
- Cadmium and mercury not detected in all eight juices (Table 1).
- Nickel 0.032 to 0.345 mg/L in five juices; iron up to 51.360 mg/L in one juice (Table 1).

## Methods (brief)

Juices were filtered and read directly by flame AAS against standards. No digestion, detection limits, blanks or reference materials are described.

## Evidence Fitness

Eight single samples with no QA or detection limits; flame AAS cannot support the mercury non-detects at drinking-water limits. Peer review is unverified. For sports-drink-bases this is a proxy: it measures finished fruit juices, not sports drink bases.
