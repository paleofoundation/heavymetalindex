Chile Decreto 977 is the Reglamento Sanitario de los Alimentos. Article 125 sets a numeric arsenic impurity cap for metals in contact with foods and raw materials, while Article 160 sets maximum levels for inorganic arsenic, cadmium, and tin in listed food categories. The loaded rows preserve the source units and convert mg/kg, mg/L, and percent-by-weight values to ug/kg for the register. A combined impurity cap for several metals considered together is gap-logged because it cannot be assigned to a single HMI metal species.
