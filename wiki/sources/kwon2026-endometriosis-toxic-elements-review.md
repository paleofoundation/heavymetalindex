# Kwon et al. 2026 - Toxic elements and endometriosis etiology review

## Summary

Kwon et al. reviewed the epidemiological and mechanistic evidence linking dietary and environmental exposure to toxic elements (lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic) with endometriosis development. The review synthesizes studies examining blood and urine biomarkers in endometriosis cases vs controls and discusses potential mechanisms including endocrine disruption and oxidative stress. This paper is relevant to the mercury-total page as a biomonitoring and exposure context source for a female reproductive health outcome endpoint. The specific mercury findings require full-text review for extraction.
