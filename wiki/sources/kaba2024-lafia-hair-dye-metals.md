# Analysis of some heavy metal concentrations in selected human hair dye products and their effects on human female users in Lafia Metropolis, Nasarawa State

## Summary

This journal article measured metals by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in five coded hair dye products from Lafia, Nigeria, and also measured metals in hair. The dye codes are CCF, WO, OO, DBH, and DBC. The dye results are mg/L. Hair biomonitoring results are not dye concentrations.

## Key numbers

Prose for the dye products: lead is higher in WO at 1.273±0.012 mg/L and lower in DBH at 0.436±0.013 mg/L. Cadmium is higher in DBC at 1.737±0.082 mg/L and lower in OO at 0.836±0.039 mg/L. Arsenic is higher in WO at 0.339±0.095 mg/L and lower in DBH at 0.076±0.091 mg/L. Wrapped table digits are not joined. Nickel, aluminum, chromium, mercury, and tin in the dyes: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
