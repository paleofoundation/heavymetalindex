This public-comment draft standard covers compound pet feed, pet additive premix feed, and other pet feed. The inorganic-contaminants table lists cadmium, chromium, mercury, lead, total arsenic, and inorganic arsenic limits. Because the document is marked as a public-comment draft, all rows are loaded with draft status and must not be treated as implemented limits.
