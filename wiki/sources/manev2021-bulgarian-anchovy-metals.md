# Heavy metal levels in anchovy (Engraulis encrasicolus L.) from Bulgarian Black Sea coast

## Summary

Manev, Neshovska, and Kirov measured arsenic, lead, cadmium, mercury, manganese, zinc, and aluminum in whole-body anchovy (Engraulis encrasicolus) from the Varna and Burgas regions of the Bulgarian Black Sea. Table 1 is mg/kg wet weight. Sample count: Data gap. DOI: Data gap.

## Key numbers

Table 1, mg/kg wet weight: Varna arsenic 0.82±0.08, lead 0.055±0.006, cadmium below 0.05, mercury 0.077±0.008, aluminum below 10.0; Burgas arsenic 0.49±0.05, lead below 0.05, cadmium below 0.05, mercury below 0.05, aluminum below 10.0. Nickel, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
