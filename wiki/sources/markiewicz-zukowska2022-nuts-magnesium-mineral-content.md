# Markiewicz-Zukowska et al. 2022 : Magnesium content in 10 nut types (Poland)

## Summary

Atomic absorption spectrometry (AAS) analysis of magnesium, calcium, potassium, selenium, and zinc in 10 types of commercially available nuts (n = 120 samples; 12 replicates per type) purchased from Polish retail markets. The study reports per-type medians and ranges and evaluates nuts as dietary sources of these minerals against reference values. All 10 nut types qualify as magnesium sources under EU labeling criteria.

## Key numbers

Magnesium content in mg/kg (median; range from Table 1, p. 5):

| Nut type | Median Mg (mg/kg) | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brazil nuts | 10,509 | 8,221 to 20,474 |
| Cashews | 7,281 | 3,699 to 9,394 |
| Pine nuts | 5,346 | 2,448 to 8,050 |
| Almonds | 5,359 | 4,196 to 7,647 |
| Peanuts | 4,049 | 2,612 to 6,282 |
| Hazelnuts | 2,571 | 2,106 to 6,358 |
| Walnuts | 2,772 | 1,115 to 5,375 |
| Pecans | 1,993 | 77 to 5,973 |
| Macadamia | 2,044 | 660 to 3,882 |
| Pistachios | 1,463 | 214 to 2,884 |

- Brazil nuts had the highest magnesium content overall; pistachios had the lowest (p. 4)
- LOD/LOQ for Mg reported in abstract as determined by standard AAS method with palladium/magnesium nitrate matrix modifier for selenium; Mg LOD = "of Ca, Mg" listed in p. 3 methods as standard reference materials (exact LOD not extracted)
- All 10 tested nut types qualify as dietary magnesium sources in this study population

## Evidence Fitness

Reconstructable dataset. n = 120 (12 per nut type); single-country retail market (Poland, 2022). The wide intratype range (e.g. pecans 77 to 5,973 mg/kg) reflects genuine variability across cultivars and origin. Data cannot be treated as globally representative but establish plausible concentration ranges for temperate-market nut supplies.

## Methods (brief)

Atomic absorption spectrometry (AAS). Samples purchased from Polish retail. Twelve replicates per nut type. Matrix modifier (palladium + magnesium nitrate hexahydrate) used for selenium only; standard flame AAS for Mg. Potassium by flame atomic emission spectrometry. Statistical comparisons by Tukey test; chemometric classification by principal component analysis (PCA) and hierarchical cluster analysis (HCA).

## Implications

- Courses: illustrates that nuts as a category span nearly a 10-fold range in magnesium content, with Brazil nuts at the high end and pistachios at the low end; useful for dietary adequacy examples.
- App: no contamination_profile implication; magnesium is a nutritional analyte here, not a hazard analyte.
- Microbiome: no direct connection.
