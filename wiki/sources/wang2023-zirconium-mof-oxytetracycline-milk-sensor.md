# Wang et al. 2023: A zirconium metal-organic framework fluorescent sensor for oxytetracycline in milk

This study built a fluorescent sensor from an amino-functionalized zirconium metal-organic framework coated with a molecularly imprinted polymer and used it to detect the antibiotic oxytetracycline in milk. The analyte is oxytetracycline; zirconium was not measured in milk.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Microwave-assisted framework synthesis, imprinting, fluorescence quenching measurements and spiked milk recovery tests.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium as a sensing material for an antibiotic residue; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
