# Colloidal silver 2025 (Romania) — toxicological risk from colloidal silver dietary supplements

## Summary

This study characterized six colloidal silver dietary supplement products from the Romanian market across a wide concentration range (15-1000 mg/L). All products were from the same manufacturer. Silver nanoparticles were characterized as 0.5-5 nm (all products) with an additional 3-15 nm fraction in the highest-concentration product (1000 mg/L). At maximum recommended usage frequencies, all six products exceeded the EPA oral reference dose for silver (0.005 mg/kg/day), with the 1000 mg/L product yielding daily silver intakes up to 12 mg/day -- more than 34x the EPA reference dose for a 70 kg adult. The findings indicate that all products on the market from this manufacturer present a potential toxicological risk when used as directed, with argyria (irreversible skin discoloration from silver deposition) as the primary chronic exposure concern.

## Key numbers

Silver concentrations as labeled/measured; particle characterization by TEM and DLS. EPA oral RfD for silver: 0.005 mg/kg/day (approximately 0.35 mg/day for 70 kg adult).

## Methods (brief)

Six colloidal silver supplements from Romanian market. Silver nanoparticle characterization by TEM (transmission electron microscopy) and DLS (dynamic light scattering). Daily intake estimated from product volume per dose, dose frequency, and silver concentration. Comparison against EPA chronic oral RfD (0.005 mg/kg/day). Risk narrative based on documented argyria from colloidal silver consumption.

## Implications

- All six colloidal silver products from the Romanian market exceeded the EPA oral RfD for silver when used as directed; the 1000 mg/L product up to 12 mg Ag/day provides context for potential argyria risk.
- No EU maximum level for silver in food supplements creates a regulatory gap; labelling compliance and voluntary usage limits are the only current consumer-protective mechanisms.
- Nanoparticle characterization (0.5-5 nm) is relevant to bioavailability: smaller AgNPs have higher surface area and potentially greater systemic absorption.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] and [[products/supplements-oral-liquids-droppers]]; provides silver exposure data not covered in standard Pb/Cd/As/Hg supplement surveillance.
