# Shaikh and Samant 2025: Cadmium and lead in ready-to-eat noodles, pasta, oats and soup, India

## Overview

Shaikh and Samant screened nine ready-to-eat products for zinc, cadmium, lead and copper by sample DC voltammetry, preparing each product two ways: a warm dilute hydrochloric acid "digestive" extraction and dry ashing. Each product is a single sample, and many results are below an unstated detection limit. The authors conclude the products are safe, although several printed values are high.

## Key numbers

- Ashing (total): Soup B lead 6.6 mg/kg; Noodles C cadmium 1.3 and lead 2.8 mg/kg; Soup A all four metals "BDL" (Table 4).
- Digestive extraction: Noodles B lead 7.2 mg/kg; Noodles C cadmium 4.0 mg/kg; Soup A cadmium 0.4 and lead 1.2 mg/kg (Table 3).

## Methods (brief)

Five grams were soaked in 0.1 N hydrochloric acid and boiled, or ten grams were ashed at 800 to 900 °C and dissolved in hydrochloric acid; metals were measured on a static mercury drop electrode by standard addition. No detection limits, reference materials or recoveries are reported.

## Evidence Fitness

Very small (one sample per brand), no quality control, and the two preparations disagree in both directions, so values are low-confidence screening data. The soups are of unstated type, so they are a proxy for broccoli-cheese-soup, chicken-noodle-soup, cream-of-potato-soup and vegetable-beef-soup; instant noodles are a proxy for ramen-soup, oats for cereal and pasta (whole-grain status not stated) for whole-wheat-pasta.
