# Ma et al. 2026: Serum trace elements and type 2 diabetes in Ningxia, China

A propensity score-matched case-control study measured 18 serum trace elements in 637 T2DM cases and 637 matched controls from Ningxia. Serum tellurium was 0.05 µg/L (median) in both groups, with no statistically significant difference between cases and controls. The logistic regression association with T2DM was inconsistent across model specifications: significant only in unadjusted analysis and in a fully adjusted quartile trend, but not in continuous analysis adjusted for key confounders.

## Key numbers

- Serum Te, controls (n=637): median 0.05 (IQR 0.03 to 0.09) µg/L (Table 2, p. 6).
- Serum Te, T2DM cases (n=637): median 0.05 (IQR 0.03 to 0.08) µg/L (Table 2, p. 6); p = 0.4430 (not significant).
- Model 1 (unadjusted) continuous OR: 1.133 (95% CI 1.019 to 1.261), p = 0.0260.
- Model 2 (adjusted for age, sex, smoking, alcohol, BMI, hypertension) continuous OR: 1.102 (95% CI 0.969 to 1.254), p = 0.1326 (not significant).
- Model 3 (additionally adjusted for education and physical activity) continuous OR: 1.170 (95% CI 0.969 to 1.385), p = 0.0283; Q4 quartile OR 1.660 (1.090 to 2.528), p-trend = 0.0283.
- RCS P-overall for Te: 0.1551; P-nonlinear: 0.8971 (no significant dose-response curve).
- WQS Te weight: 0.130 (third-highest contributor after Mo = 0.286 and V = 0.201 in the negative-direction model).

## Methods (brief)

ICP-MS (Agilent 7800 quadrupole); acid microwave digestion (CEM MARS 6, 130 degrees C, 155 min); 18 serum elements measured; below-LOD values replaced with LOD/2; propensity score 1:1 matching; multivariable logistic regression with three models; WQS, RCS, LASSO, and BKMR multimetal analyses.

## Evidence fitness

Provides absolute serum tellurium concentrations for a large matched Chinese adult sample (median 0.05 µg/L); values are substantially lower than those from the Norwegian biomonitoring study (0.08 to 0.55 µg/L serum) and far lower than the PCOS study from Saudi Arabia (1.32 to 12.33 µg/L). The T2DM association is not supported by the continuous adjusted analysis or the RCS dose-response. Does not establish causality.

## Limitations

Cross-sectional baseline with no longitudinal follow-up; Ningxia industrial region; inconsistent association across models limits interpretability; no information on dietary tellurium sources; LOD for Te not separately tabulated.
