# Khabir et al. 2021. Epidermal zinc after zinc oxide nanoparticle sunscreen on human skin

Using zinc oxide nanoparticles labelled with the rare isotope 67Zn on excised human skin, the authors found the particles stayed in the stratum corneum while dissolved zinc reached the viable epidermis. Exogenous zinc in the viable epidermis was 1.0 ± 0.3 µg/mL, below endogenous epidermal zinc (4.3 ± 0.7 µg/mL) and below the 21 to 31 µg/mL that was cytotoxic to cultured keratinocytes.

## Key numbers

Table 1 (PDF p. 8), mean ± 95 % CI, LA-ICP-MS:

| Layer | 67Zn (µg/g) | Exogenous Zn (µg/g) | Endogenous Zn (µg/g) | Exogenous Zn (µg/mL) | Endogenous Zn (µg/mL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stratum corneum | 235 ± 49 | 360 ± 70 | 39 ± 5 | 120 ± 23 | 13 ± 2 |
| Viable epidermis | 2.0 ± 0.5 | 3.1 ± 0.8 | 13 ± 2 | 1.0 ± 0.3 | 4.3 ± 0.7 |
| Dermis | 0.9 ± 0.3 | 1.4 ± 0.5 | 16 ± 7 | 0.5 ± 0.2 | 5 ± 2 |

HaCaT IC50 26 ± 5 µg Zn/mL. Most tracer was recovered in the surface wash (630 ± 110 µg/g). Solution ICP-MS of tape-stripped viable epidermis gave 4.5 ± 4.0 µg/g, which the authors attribute to incomplete stratum corneum removal. Endogenous zinc in homogenised untreated skin was 15 ± 4 µg/g by ICP-MS and 11 ± 8 µg/g by LA-ICP-MS (PDF p. 8). Micrograms per gram are dry tissue; micrograms per millilitre were converted using about 70 % epidermal water (Supplement S3, not in this collection).

## Methods (brief)

67ZnO-PEG nanoparticles at 20 % wt/wt in caprylic/capric triglyceride applied for 48 h at 35 °C; multiphoton microscopy with second-harmonic generation and zinc-selective fluorescent probes; LA-ICP-MS maps of skin cross-sections; solution ICP-MS. Corresponding authors: m.roberts@uq.edu.au and andrei.zvyagin@mq.edu.au.

## Evidence fitness

Supports layer-specific zinc concentrations and the conclusion that zinc permeates as ions on intact adult skin at levels below keratinocyte cytotoxicity. It does not address infant or damaged skin, in vivo clearance, or metal impurities.

## Limitations

Ex vivo non-viable skin, three donors, oil vehicle. The discussion prints 2.1 ± 0.5 µg/g where Table 1 gives 2.0 ± 0.5 µg/g for tracer zinc in the viable epidermis; the table value is used here.
