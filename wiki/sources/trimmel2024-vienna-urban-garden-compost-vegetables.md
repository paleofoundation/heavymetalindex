# Trimmel et al. 2024: Elements in urban garden soils and vegetables

This study analyzed soils and vegetables from Vienna urban gardens for 22 elements including gallium. Washing lettuce significantly lowered gallium and several other surface-bound elements, and gallium in tomato fruit parts was below the quantification limit.

## Key numbers

- Gallium mass fractions in tomato fruit parts were below the quantification limit (Results, tomato section).
- Washing lettuce leaves significantly reduced gallium along with Be, Al, V, Ni and Tl (Abstract; Figure 3).

## Methods (brief)

Washed and unwashed vegetable tissues were digested and analyzed by ICP-MS; lead isotope ratios were used to trace sources.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the statement that gallium on leafy vegetables is partly surface-bound and reduced by washing. Does not give intake estimates.

## Limitations

Numeric gallium values are in supplementary tables not reviewed here. Results describe a few Vienna gardens.
