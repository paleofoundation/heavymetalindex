# Vu et al. 2024: Cerium nanogel sensor for choline in milk and infant formula

A cerium-containing nanogel with enzyme-like activity was combined with choline oxidase to build a color-change test for hydrogen peroxide and choline, validated with choline spiked into commercial milk and infant formula. Cerium is part of the sensor, not the analyte.

## Key numbers

- Choline linear range 4 to 400 µM (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Polymer nanogel synthesis by atom transfer radical polymerization, enzyme entrapment and colorimetric assay with recovery testing in spiked foods.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: cerium is a sensor component; the paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims about cerium.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
