# Seoul Research Institute of Public Health and Environment 2026: Children's diapers and cotton swabs

The Additives Testing Team tested children's diapers and cotton swabs on sale in Korea against the MFDS hygiene-product standard and the 2024 strengthened common safety standard for children's products. All 50 samples were reported compliant. The report prints no individual concentration and no recovery percentage.

## Key numbers

No metal concentration is printed.

Children's diapers: 23 samples (17 domestic, 6 imported). The inner lining, which contacts skin, and the waterproof layer were tested separately. Tests were migration of eight hazardous elements (the report names antimony, barium, and arsenic among them) and content of lead and cadmium.

Cotton swabs: 27 samples (4 domestic, 23 imported), shaft and cotton tip tested for the same migration and content items. All 27 were reported compliant. There is no cotton-swab product page, so that result stays on this source page as an explicit gap and is not routed to a product.

All 50 samples met the applicable standard.

Microwave digestion and dry ashing were compared for recovery before the survey. The report does not print the recovery percentages.

## Methods (brief)

The page states that a suitable pretreatment was chosen after comparing microwave and dry-ashing recoveries, and that diapers were split into inner lining and waterproof layer. It does not name the instrument, a detection limit, or whether arsenic is inorganic or total. Lead and cadmium content are the elements named for the content test. The web page was modified on 23 April 2026. The file header shows a print date of 25 September 2026.

## Evidence fitness

The diaper count supports a 2026 Seoul compliance statement for 23 children's diapers, without concentrations. The cotton-swab count is the same kind of statement and is unrouted.

## Limitations

Pass or fail only. Eight-element list is incomplete in the prose (antimony, barium, arsenic, and others). Species of arsenic is not stated. No cotton-swab destination page.
