# Wen 2022 — Mercury Contamination of Water and the Removal of Mercury from Water

Wen reviews historical mercury releases from combustion, mining and natural exchange, then discusses adsorption and carbon-dot-modified chitosan fabrics. The review is useful for discovery and pathway context but contains chemical and numerical errors that preclude treating its broad safety or efficacy statements as verified findings.

## Key numbers

Secondary estimates include Guizhou anthropogenic emissions 22.6–55.5 source-reported tons/year around 2000, reported as 6.3–10.3% ofChina’s total; Guizhou coal emissions<5 source-reported tons in 1981 andapproximately 26 source-reported tons in 2006; CaliforniaHg production 220 million pounds over 1850–1981. These are review quotations, not current inventories.

Reported coal-emission fractions Hg(0)56%, Hg(II)33%, particulateHg 13% sum 102%. The review describes five fabric reuse cycles without tabulated quantitative removal capacity.

## Methods (brief)

Narrative review of published emission inventories and adsorption studies. No systematic search protocol, new analytical method or independent environmental sampling. Distinguishes historical geographies and time spans; source references remain required for primary verification.

## Implications

Supports mercury source-pathway and removal-material discovery. Claims that mercury compounds are generally harmless or that only elemental mercury is toxic are rejected; the paper itself contradicts them in its introduction. No current emissions trend or safe discharge concentration is inferred.

Evidence Fitness: EF-3 for qualified historical/pathway discovery; EF-X for the erroneous chemical generalizations and unvalidated quantitative efficacy claims.
