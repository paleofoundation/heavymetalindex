# Molaee Aghaee 2014 ,  Storage time and temperature for antimony from PET

Bottled water stored at several temperatures. Not identified as baby bottles.

## Key numbers

Results remained below 6 ppb at room temperature, outdoors, and 40 °C, and were above 6 ppb at 65 °C and 80 °C for longer storage (PDF p.1-2). The 6 ppb figure is the maximum contaminant level the paper uses.

## Methods (brief)

Storage experiment.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the below-or-above 6 ppb statements. Does not support a baby-bottle concentration.

## Limitations

Not a wipe or textile study.
