# Assessment of chemical, heavy metal, and microbiological contamination of Indonesian seaweed-based food products

## Summary

Suroto and colleagues measured arsenic, cadmium, mercury, lead, and tin by ICP-MS in 20 Indonesian seaweed-based food products. Table 2 is mg/kg. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis: Data gap. Product type and place are printed. Brand names are not printed.

## Key numbers

Abstract detection: lead 50%, arsenic 35%, cadmium 25%, mercury 5%. Highest printed cells include seaweed powder (Makasar) arsenic 7.96, cadmium 0.07, mercury 0.01, lead 1.58 mg/kg; seaweed salt arsenic 5.21 mg/kg with lead printed nd; dried spinossum lead 0.48 mg/kg. Tin is ND in every row. Nickel, aluminum, and chromium: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
