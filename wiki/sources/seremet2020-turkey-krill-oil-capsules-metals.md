# Assessment of heavy metal levels and fatty acid compositions of some krill oil capsules marketed in Turkey

## Summary

Seremet Kurklu, Basibuyuk, and Kamarli Altun measured mercury, cadmium, and lead in five krill oil capsules marketed in Turkey. Product names are omitted. The samples are coded 1 through 5.

## Key numbers

The abstract prints mercury below 0.2 µg/kg, cadmium below 3 µg/kg, and lead below 3 µg/kg in all samples. Table 3, headed as the amount in the samples solution, prints the same ceilings for each of the five codes. Arsenic, chromium, nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Mercury is not reported as methylmercury.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking. Fatty acid amounts are not metal concentrations.
