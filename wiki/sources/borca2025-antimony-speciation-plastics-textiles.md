# Borca 2025 ,  Antimony speciation in plastics and textiles

Portable X-ray fluorescence totals and L3-edge spectroscopy. The methods text says five PET bottles. The table prints six. This page follows the table.

## Key numbers

Six PET bottles: 351, 331, 361, 322, 370, and 370 mg/kg (PDF p.4). Antimony in those bottles was organically bound, consistent with glycolate or acetate, and a significant amount of antimony trioxide was excluded. Three polyester textiles: 286, 471, and 452 mg/kg. One textile spectrum resembled senarmontite. Electrical objects were much higher, with bromine in the thousands of mg/kg on three of them.

## Methods (brief)

pXRF and X-ray absorption spectroscopy. Not a migration test into water.

## Evidence fitness

Supports speciation in the measured consumer plastics and textiles. Does not support calling the bottles baby bottles or the textiles infant sleepwear.

## Limitations

The paper cites Biver but does not say the textiles are the same objects.
