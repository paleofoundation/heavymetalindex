# Distribution of metals and metalloids in dried seaweeds and health risk to population in southeastern China

## Summary

This journal article measured metals in dried retail seaweeds from Zhejiang, China. The set is 295 samples (142 red and 153 brown). Combined means are mg/kg. Arsenic is total arsenic. Chromium is not speciated. Mercury is not speciated.

## Key numbers

Combined means: aluminum 553.8±626.4 mg/kg, with red and brown means both near 597 mg/kg, so the combined aluminum mean conflicts with the group means and both printings are kept. Total arsenic 22.61±14.0 mg/kg. Cadmium 1.077±1.309 mg/kg. Chromium 2.498±4.189 mg/kg. Mercury is printed 0.0370±0054 mg/kg and is copied as printed. Nickel 1.338±1.240 mg/kg. Lead 0.595±0.562 mg/kg. A brown aluminum cell is printed with a plus sign rather than a plus-minus sign. Inorganic arsenic and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
