# Kowalczyk 2022 ,  Antimony and other elements in teas

An EFSA Journal EU-FORA report. It restates the WHO tolerable daily intake and is not the 2004 food-contact opinion.

## Key numbers

The highest antimony intake evaluated from tea was 0.03 percent of the TDI of 6 µg/kg body weight (PDF p.6 and p.11). The PDF renders the microgram glyph poorly. No tea concentration in micrograms per kilogram is printed in the main text opened here.

## Methods (brief)

Dietary exposure report for tea.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the 0.03 percent statement. Does not support an infant-formula concentration or a food-contact specific migration limit.

## Limitations

Not an infant study.
