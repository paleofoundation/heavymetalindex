# Ochu et al. 2012, metals in imported candies, lollipop paragraph only

Sample B is lollipops. Figure 2 has no numerals in the text layer, so bar heights are not read. No recovery statement was located. Abstract ranges cover all candy types and are not assigned to lollipops.

## Key numbers

The results paragraph prints Sample B nickel 5.0 to 5.2 mg/g. Chromium was not detected in Sample B. A range 40.1 to 42.5 mg/g in the same sentence has no metal name and is not assigned. The unit mg/g is extreme beside the paper's own literature comparators and is left as printed.

## Methods (brief)

Pellets were measured by energy dispersive spectrometry. The lollipop numbers used here are the results paragraph, not the figure.

## Evidence fitness

The nickel range supports a lollipop note. Chromium not detected is not a concentration. The unnamed 40.1 to 42.5 mg/g range is not used.

## Limitations

No quality-assurance statement. Figure 2 cannot be read as numbers. The unit is mg/g as printed.
