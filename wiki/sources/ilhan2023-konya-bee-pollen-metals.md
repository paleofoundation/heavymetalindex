# PCA and LDA Assessment of the Heavy Metal Contamination in Honey Bees, Bee Pollen and Honey Produced in Urban Areas of Türkiye

## Summary

İlhan, Bayır, and Aygün measured cadmium, chromium, copper, iron, manganese, nickel, lead, and zinc in honey bees, dried bee pollen, and honey from 20 colonies in four urban areas of Konya. This record uses the bee pollen column. Bee and honey values are not pollen concentrations.

## Key numbers

Table 1 prints bee pollen means ± Sx in mg/kg: cadmium 0.0105±0.0005, chromium 0.075±0.0029, nickel 0.353±0.0144, and lead 0.146±0.0079. A discussion sentence prints bee pollen chromium as 0.75 mg/kg. Pollen was dried in a dark environment. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Chromium is not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
