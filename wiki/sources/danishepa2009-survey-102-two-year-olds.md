# Tønning et al. 2009: Danish EPA survey of products used by two-year-olds, diaper tin screen

Danish EPA Survey No. 102 screened five disposable paper diapers, coded 9-1 through 9-5, among many other products used by two-year-olds. The metal result for diapers is a tin screen. No tin was detected above the detection threshold in filling or in the elastic bands near the legs, and organotin species were not measured.

## Key numbers

Section 6.9.5.4 states: "No tin was detected above the detection threshold (0.02-0.03 µg/g) in the diaper's filling material, nor in the elastic bands near the legs." The survey did not publish a tin concentration for any of the five diapers.

Table 6.67 prints the detection threshold as "0.03 µg/g for filling material and02 µg/g for elastic rim." The results sentence prints 0.02-0.03 µg/g. The broken "and02" token is recorded as printed and is not rewritten as 0.02. The results sentence is the threshold cited with the non-detect finding.

Formaldehyde, which is not a metal, is in Table 6.74 in µg/g, with detection below 2. Outer lining with print and inner lining: product 9-1 = 4, 9-4 = 4, 9-5 = 2, and 9-2 and 9-3 below 2. Filling-material formaldehyde analysis was not finished. Rosin was not detected above 2 µg/g in filling or waistband.

## Methods (brief)

Table 6.67: 2.5 g of filling and of elastic rim, extracted in artificial sweat at 40 °C with 0.14 M nitric acid (100 mL for padding, 50 mL for elastic). ICP-MS on ions 118 and 120, internal standard rhodium. Organotin speciation was not performed because the tin screen was negative. Sample codes are 9-1 to 9-5. Packaging text that would identify a product is not repeated here.

## Evidence fitness

The diaper chapter supports a censored tin result for five Danish paper diapers: not detected at the stated threshold, on an artificial-sweat migration basis. It does not support an organotin concentration, a detected tin value, or any other metal. The rest of Survey No. 102 covers other product categories and is not extracted on this page.

## Limitations

The methods-table threshold for the elastic rim is a broken numeral ("and02"). The non-detect statement uses the range 0.02-0.03 µg/g. Five diapers, one market, and a screen rather than a quantified result. Tin is total tin by ICP-MS ions 118 and 120, not a named organotin species.
