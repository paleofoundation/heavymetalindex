# Otim et al. 2019, sachet alcohol in Uganda, no element-specific vodka number

The paper measured 20 metals in sachet-packaged spirits, including vodka products. Table 4 in the text layer is a sum across metals, not a concentration of one metal. The per-metal table did not survive text extraction as element-specific cells. No element-specific vodka number is copied.

## Key numbers

Data gap: no lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, chromium, or aluminum concentration is available for the sachet vodka rows from the text layer.

## Methods (brief)

Sachet spirits from Acoli, Uganda. The products are sachet-packaged, not bottled spirits.

## Evidence fitness

The paper documents sachet vodka in the sample list. It does not support an element-specific vodka concentration on this page.

## Limitations

A summed metal index is not a single-metal concentration.
