# Li et al. 2025 -- NHANES urinary tungsten and pain-depression comorbidity

## Summary

This study used NHANES 2009-2010 data (n=1,202) to examine associations between urinary concentrations of heavy metals and pain-depression comorbidity (PDC), defined as concurrent chronic pain and depression. The urinary tungsten median was 0.0866 ng/mL. In fully adjusted logistic regression, a one-unit increase in log-transformed urinary tungsten was associated with an odds ratio of 1.158 (95% CI: 1.017-1.317, p=0.026) for PDC. Restricted cubic spline analysis confirmed a nonlinear positive relationship (p for nonlinearity = 0.036). In Bayesian kernel machine regression mixture analysis, tungsten had the highest posterior inclusion probability (PIP = 0.91) among all metals examined.

## Key numbers

- Urinary tungsten median: 0.0866 ng/mL (NHANES 2009-2010 spot urine)
- PDC prevalence in sample: 12.4% (n=149/1,202)
- Fully adjusted OR for PDC per log-unit urinary tungsten: 1.158 (95% CI: 1.017-1.317, p=0.026)
- RCS p-value for nonlinearity: 0.036
- BKMR posterior inclusion probability (PIP) for tungsten: 0.91 (highest among metals analyzed)
- Positive dose-response with PDC severity score confirmed by BKMR
- Sample: n=1,202 US adults; NHANES 2009-2010

## Evidence Fitness

Modeled or limited evidence. Cross-sectional design; spot urine tungsten as a biomarker of recent exposure; temporal precedence between exposure and outcome cannot be established. The BKMR PIP of 0.91 provides stronger evidence for association than single-metal models in a mixture context. Residual confounding from lifestyle, medication use, and socioeconomic factors is likely. The nonlinear relationship and high PIP are hypothesis-generating.

## Methods (brief)

Urinary metals by ICP-MS in NHANES 2009-2010 spot urine. Creatinine adjustment applied (reported as creatinine-standardized concentrations). Logistic regression with stepwise full adjustment; restricted cubic splines for dose-response shape; BKMR for mixture analysis with ten metals simultaneously. LOD/LOQ for tungsten not stated in this paper.

## Implications

- Certification: observational association data only; no food concentration or food-chain data.
- Courses: illustrates novel health outcome framing (PDC) and the BKMR approach for ranking metals in mixtures; tungsten PIP prominence notable for a less-studied metal.
- App: no ingredient contamination profile data.
- Microbiome: not addressed in this paper.
