# Potential Human Health Risks Assessment through Determination of Heavy Metal Contents in Regularly Consumed Yogurts in Bangladesh

## Summary

Hasan, Satter, and Das measured metals by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in sour, sweet, and homemade yogurt from Bangladesh. Each category has 100 samples. Table 1 is mean ± SD in mg/Kg. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis is not stated.

## Key numbers

Table 1 sour yogurt: lead 0.011±0.006, cadmium 0.031±0.005, chromium 0.445±0.267, arsenic 0.029±0.024 mg/Kg. Sweet: lead 0.01±0.005, cadmium 0.03±0.007, chromium 0.46±0.265, arsenic 0.035±0.026. Homemade: lead 0.011±0.005, cadmium 0.02±0.005, chromium 0.251±0.174, arsenic 0.025±0.021. One prose sentence swaps the sour and sweet lead order. Mercury, nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
