# Ozarkar et al. 2025 — Comparative Analysis of Bioremediation and Phytoremediation in Mitigation of Heavy Metals.

This experiment compares bacterial cultures and aquatic plants under nickel and cobalt exposure. Its charts and narrative describe remediation potential, but missing residual concentrations, uncertainty and method details prevent a reproducible quantitative removal estimate.

## Key numbers

Nominal nickel and cobalt treatments: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 ppm. PlantAAScharts compare day 1 withday 20. Bacterial colorimetry is plotted across 0–4 hours forE. coli and 0–2 hours forS. aureus. No exact residual or percentage-removal data are tabulated, so nominal dose is retained separately from outcome.

## Methods (brief)

Cultures sourced from a Kalyan college; aquatic plants collected from the Besol River, Nalambi Gaon, Ambernath, Maharashtra. PreparedNi/Costock dilutions and nutrient broth; plant endpointAAS and bacterial colorimetry. The paper supplies no calibration curve for converting bacterial optical response to dissolved metal, no sampling denominator, and no statistical uncertainty.

## Implications

Supports bounded remediation-method context. Colorimetric bacterial growth cannot by itself establish metal removal; the claim of superior plant performance is not a validated paired efficacy estimate. Pure bacterial isolates do not measure a host microbiome.

Evidence Fitness: EF-3 for experimental design and qualitative observations; EF-X for quantitative efficacy claims until residual-concentration and control data are available.
