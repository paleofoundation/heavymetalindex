The Taiwan Council of Agriculture draft import-inspection rule lists heavy-metal inspection limits for feed-use calcium phosphate products. The table gives lead 50 ppm, arsenic 12 ppm, cadmium 10 ppm, and mercury 0.5 ppm for defluorinated calcium phosphate, calcium phosphates for feed use, and dicalcium phosphate. Because the local document is a draft, rows are loaded with draft status.
