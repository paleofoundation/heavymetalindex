# Mercury concentration in shark meat from traditional markets of Gyeongsangbuk-do, South Korea

## Summary

This journal article measured total mercury in shark meat from traditional markets in Gyeongsangbuk-do, South Korea. n=105. Wet or dry basis: Data gap. Methylmercury was not measured.

## Key numbers

Total mercury mean 2.29±1.77 µg/g, range 0.06 to 8.93, geometric mean 1.44 µg/g. Lead, cadmium, arsenic, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
