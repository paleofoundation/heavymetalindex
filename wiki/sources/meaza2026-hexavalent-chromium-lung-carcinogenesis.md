# Meaza et al. 2026: Hexavalent chromium DNA double-strand breaks and repair inhibition in guinea pig lungs

This mechanistic study translated the established mechanism of hexavalent chromium Cr(VI)-induced lung carcinogenesis (DNA double-strand breaks via gamma-H2AX, inhibition of homologous recombination repair via RAD51) to a guinea pig animal model. Guinea pigs were chosen because, unlike mice and rats, they lack endogenous ascorbic acid (vitamin C) synthesis and have a dichotomous bronchiole branching pattern similar to humans, making them more physiologically relevant for Cr(VI) inhalation carcinogenesis research.

## Key numbers

Acute exposure (oropharyngeal aspiration, 24 h):
- Saline, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8 mg/kg zinc chromate led to 948.8, 1282.5, 1212.7, and 1092.6 total gamma-H2AX foci (DNA double-strand breaks) in 100 bronchiolar epithelial cells respectively; lowest dose (0.2 mg/kg) induced statistically significant increase versus control in bronchioles.
- Alveolar gamma-H2AX foci: 271.7, 366.6, 329.4, and 306.9 total at the same doses; not statistically significant.
- RAD51 foci (DNA repair marker) in bronchioles: 177.1, 369.9, 379.2, and 259.9 for saline and three doses; low and middle doses significantly increased RAD51 in bronchioles (not in alveoli).

Subchronic exposure (90-day, all three zinc chromate doses):
- All three doses significantly induced gamma-H2AX foci (DNA double-strand breaks) in bronchioles but not alveoli (1163.4, 1139.7, 1191.1 versus saline 800.5 total gamma-H2AX foci per 100 bronchiolar cells).
- Subchronic exposure inhibited RAD51 foci in bronchioles (DNA repair inhibition), consistent with the established carcinogenic mechanism of Cr(VI).
- Males had overall higher DNA double-strand break levels than females in both bronchioles and alveoli.

## Methods (brief)

Formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded guinea pig lung tissue; gamma-H2AX (DNA double-strand break marker) and RAD51 (homologous recombination repair marker) quantified by confocal microscopy using immunofluorescence (cytokeratin used to identify epithelial cells); 100 cells counted per region per animal; gamma-H2AX/RAD51 ratio calculated as repair index. Statistical analysis: n = 7 males and 7 females per group; p or q values reported; q values used for FDR-corrected multiple comparisons.

## Evidence fitness

Supports: establishing that Cr(VI) induces DNA double-strand breaks and inhibits DNA repair specifically in bronchiolar epithelial cells (the site of Cr(VI)-induced tumors in humans), and that this mechanism is reproduced in a human-relevant guinea pig model. This paper is specifically about hexavalent chromium carcinogenesis, not arsenic. It is contextual evidence for inhalation pathway metal carcinogenesis in lungs.

## Limitations

Animal model only (guinea pigs); oropharyngeal aspiration route differs from occupational inhalation; Cr(VI) as zinc chromate does not capture all Cr(VI) compound speciation; no long-term tumor endpoint measured; sex differences noted but not fully explained.
