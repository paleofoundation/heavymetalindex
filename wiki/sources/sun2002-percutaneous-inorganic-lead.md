# Sun et al. 2002. Percutaneous absorption of inorganic lead compounds

Chee-Ching Sun and colleagues measured lead on the skin of 10 lead-battery workers by skin stripping and measured urinary lead in rats after skin application of lead sulfate, lead oxide, lead powder, and lead stearate. This copy does not preserve the results tables, so no absorption percentage is entered.

## Key numbers

No results-table cell was clean enough to record. The experiment used four compounds and 10 workers.

## Methods (brief)

Skin stripping of workers and epicutaneous dosing of rats.
