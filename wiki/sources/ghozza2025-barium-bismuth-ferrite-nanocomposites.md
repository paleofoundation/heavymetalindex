# Ghozza et al. 2025: Barium-doped bismuth ferrite nanoparticles for dye degradation

This materials paper synthesizes barium-doped bismuth ferrite nanoparticles by solution combustion with different fuels and reports their electrical, magnetic and dye-degrading properties. It contains no exposure or toxicity information for bismuth.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to bismuth exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Solution-combustion synthesis, X-ray diffraction with Rietveld refinement, electron microscopy, conductivity and magnetization measurements, and electrocatalytic degradation of Congo red dye (abstract).

## Evidence fitness

This is a materials or analytical chemistry paper in which bismuth is a component of an engineered material. It does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for bismuth, and no bismuth was measured in food, water, feed, soil or human or animal tissue.

## Limitations

Not relevant to food or health.
