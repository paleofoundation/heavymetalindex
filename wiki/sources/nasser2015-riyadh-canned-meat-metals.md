# Molecular identification of isolated fungi, microbial and heavy metal contamination of canned meat products sold in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

## Summary

This journal article measured iron, zinc, copper, cadmium, lead, and nickel in 13 canned meat products from Riyadh, and also identified fungi. Fungi are not metal concentrations. Samples were dried at 70 C for 24 h before ashing. Table 5 is mg/kg. Whether those mg/kg values are the dried mass or the original wet mass: Data gap. Cited regulatory maxima in the discussion are not results.

## Key numbers

Text: lead 0.27 to 1.1 mg/kg, highest named as sample 2 at 1.1 mg/kg; Table 5 prints sample 2 lead as 1.12 mg/kg and sample 4 lead as Not detected. Text: cadmium 0.16 to 0.62 mg/kg, highest sample 5 at 0.62 mg/kg, and sample 3 undetected; Table 5 prints sample 11 cadmium as 0.14 mg/kg. Nickel is Not detected in samples 1 to 10 and is 0.28, 0.77, and 0.21 mg/kg in samples 11, 12, and 13. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
