# Zhou et al. 2024: A terbium and zirconium framework probe for malachite green in fish

This study made a ratiometric fluorescent probe from a zirconium-based metal-organic framework functionalized with terbium and used it, including with a smartphone readout, to detect the dye malachite green in fish. The analyte is malachite green; zirconium was not measured in fish.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Hydrothermal synthesis of the probe, fluorescence resonance energy transfer measurements, smartphone color analysis and spiked fish recovery tests.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium as a sensing material for a dye residue; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
