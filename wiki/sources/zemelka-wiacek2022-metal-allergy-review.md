# Zemelka-Wiacek 2022 - Review: metal allergy mechanisms, biomarkers, and implant hypersensitivity

## Overview

This review covers the state-of-the-art understanding of metal allergy mechanisms, biomarkers, and hypersensitivity reactions to implants including dental alloys and orthopedic implants. Metal allergens covered include nickel, chromium, cobalt, and palladium. Environmental exposure routes discussed include jewelry, everyday metal items, mobile phones, leather, metal-rich foods, and implants (stents, anchors). Palladium is discussed in the context of dental alloys and cross-reactivity with nickel.
