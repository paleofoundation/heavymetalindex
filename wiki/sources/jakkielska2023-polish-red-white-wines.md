# Jakkielska et al. 2023, Polish red and white wines, printed white mercury only

Jakkielska and colleagues measured metals in 31 white wines and 22 red wines. The white group includes an apple ice wine (sample 3-B, made from apples). Figure 1 averages and ranges for aluminum, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, nickel, and lead are chart-only. Those bar heights are not copied. Recoveries were 96% to 104%.

## Key numbers

One white-group mean is printed in prose: mercury 0.075 µg/L. The paper prints µg·L−1. Mercury is total by ICP-MS, not methylmercury. The abstract span 0.075 to 86403 µg/L is a cross-element span of means, not a result for one metal. Data gap: aluminum, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, nickel, and lead have no printed white-wine number in the main text.

## Methods (brief)

Wine was diluted ten times and measured by ICP-MS. Numeric detection limits are in a supplement that is not in this file.

## Evidence fitness

The mercury mean supports a white-group mercury note that includes the apple ice wine. It does not support the other metals from Figure 1.

## Limitations

Most target metals are chart-only. The white group is not only grape wine.
