# Telalović et al. 2011: Aluminum and zirconium mesoporous catalysts

This chemistry study made mesoporous silica materials containing both aluminum and zirconium and showed that they catalyze the cyclization of citronellal better than materials with either metal alone. It has no connection to food, water or human exposure.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Surfactant-free one-pot synthesis, characterization by X-ray diffraction, electron microscopy, nitrogen physisorption, ammonia desorption, NMR, XPS and infrared spectroscopy, and catalytic tests.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium in heterogeneous catalysts; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
