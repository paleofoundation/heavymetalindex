# ATSDR 2019 ,  Toxicological profile for antimony

The October 2019 profile states minimal risk levels for oral and inhalation exposure and adopts ICRP gastrointestinal absorption reference values. It does not print a percent absorption for intact infant skin.

## Key numbers

Table 1-1 (PDF p.21): acute oral MRL 1 mg Sb/kg/day from a NOAEL of 99 mg Sb/kg/day and an uncertainty factor of 100; intermediate oral MRL 0.0006 mg Sb/kg/day, with the table printing a NOAEL of 0.064 mg Sb/kg/day; acute and intermediate inhalation 0.001 mg Sb/m3; chronic inhalation 0.0003 mg Sb/m3. A footnote on PDF p.58 prints the intermediate oral NOAEL as 0.06 mg Sb/kg/day. No chronic oral MRL. ICRP 1981 reference values cited in the profile: 10 percent gastrointestinal absorption for tartrate and 1 percent for other forms. Animal tartrate and trichloride estimates are described as about 2 to 7 percent.

## Methods (brief)

Agency toxicological profile and MRL worksheets. The profile states that EPA had not classified the carcinogenicity of antimony as of this edition.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the MRLs and the ICRP absorption values as reference figures. Does not support a chronic oral MRL, an IRIS reference dose, or a dermal fraction for a wipe.

## Limitations

Table 1-1 and the later footnote do not print the same intermediate NOAEL. The metal page uses 0.06 mg Sb/kg/day and records 0.064 as the table cell.
