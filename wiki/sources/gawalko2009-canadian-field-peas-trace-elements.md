# Gawalko et al. 2009: Trace elements in Canadian field peas

## Overview

The Canadian Grain Commission reported a baseline for trace elements in Canadian field pea seed. The journal article is closed. Unpaywall reported no open-access copy on 2026-11-03, the mounted library path was not present in this checkout, and no PDF was found under raw/. The PubMed abstract (PMID 19680975) is the text that was read.

## Abstract figures, not a table extraction

The abstract states a mean total cadmium of 0.023 mg/kg. It states that mean arsenic and lead were below a method quantification limit of 0.050 mg/kg, and that mean total mercury was below a quantification level of 0.002 mg/kg. It states that the measured values were below the Codex maximum residue levels the authors cite for those elements, and that mean selenium was 0.331 mg/kg. Sample size, range, crop years and growing region inside Canada are not in the abstract, so they are not repeated here and these means are not occurrence rows.

## What this cannot support

The measurements are whole field pea seed. They are not a protein isolate, a concentrate, or a retail powder. They do not show whether protein separation raises or lowers a metal relative to the seed. Selenium is an essential element in the abstract and is not treated here as a toxic-metal result.
