Colorado MED rules set elemental impurity limits for regulated marijuana products by route of administration. The extractable table gives lower ppm values for inhaled products and higher ppm values for topical or transdermal products. Arsenic and mercury are not speciated in the table, so they are loaded as total arsenic and total mercury.
