# Saez et al. 2026 -- Blood metals in Valencia Region working adults

## Summary

Cross-sectional human biomonitoring study (BIOMOVAL project) measuring whole-blood concentrations of 19 metals in 541 working adults in the Valencian Community, Spain, 2021. Antimony was detected in 99% of samples. The study reports descriptive statistics, risk assessment against biomonitoring equivalents, and regression models for exposure predictors. For Sb, windows-open time and agricultural activity were the statistically significant exposure correlates.

## Key numbers

**Whole blood Sb (microg/L), N = 541 working adults, Valencia Region, Spain, 2021 (Table 3):**

| Statistic | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Detection frequency | 99% |
| LoQ | 1.08 microg/L |
| Minimum | less than LoQ |
| P25 | 1.76 microg/L |
| Median | 2.36 microg/L |
| Arithmetic mean | 2.34 microg/L |
| Geometric mean | 2.18 microg/L |
| P75 | 2.75 microg/L |
| P95 | 3.36 microg/L |
| Maximum | 19.7 microg/L |
| SD | 1.09 microg/L |

Analytical method: ICP-MS, samples diluted 1:20 (v/v), 0.5 mL blood to 10 mL final volume with HNO3.

No health-based guidance value (HBM-I or HBM-II) is available for blood Sb; a hazard quotient was not calculated for Sb.

Statistically significant predictors of blood Sb in regression: time spent with windows open (positive, p < 0.05) and agricultural activity (positive, p < 0.05).

## Evidence Fitness

Reconstructable dataset for European adult working-population blood Sb reference ranges. Detection frequency near complete at this LoQ. The study does not attribute sources to specific foods or products.

## Methods (brief)

Whole blood collected from 541 working adults, Valencian Community, Spain, 2021. Questionnaire on diet, lifestyle, occupation. ICP-MS for all analytes except Hg (CV-AFS). Statistical analysis: descriptive statistics, Spearman correlations, forward stepwise linear regression on log10-transformed concentrations. Approval: CEIm of Hospital Universitario Dr. Peset.

## Implications

- Certification: European adult background blood Sb median of 2.36 microg/L provides a reference range for interpreting biomonitoring studies and establishing clinical significance of elevated values.
- Courses: illustrates that near-complete blood Sb detection is achievable at LoQ of 1.08 microg/L in an occupational/general population.
- App: not directly applicable to food contamination profiles.
- Microbiome: not addressed.
