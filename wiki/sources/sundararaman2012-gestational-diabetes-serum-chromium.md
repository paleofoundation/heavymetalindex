# Sundararaman et al. 2012: Serum chromium in gestational diabetes

Pregnant women with gestational diabetes in Chennai had lower serum chromium than matched pregnant controls. Differences were not significant when groups were split by parity.

## Key numbers

- Serum chromium: gestational diabetes 1.59 ± 0.02 ng/mL (range 0.16 to 4.0), n = 30; controls 4.58 ± 0.62 ng/mL (range 0.82 to 5.33), n = 60; P < 0.001 (Table 1, p. S71).

## Methods (brief)

Case-control comparison; fasting serum chromium by inductively coupled plasma emission spectrometry.

## Evidence fitness

Supports an observed association between lower serum chromium and gestational diabetes in one Indian city. It does not establish causation or benefit from supplementation.

## Limitations

Small sample; dispersion statistic not identified (the narrow value for cases, against a wide range, suggests a standard error); no detection limit or reference material reported.
