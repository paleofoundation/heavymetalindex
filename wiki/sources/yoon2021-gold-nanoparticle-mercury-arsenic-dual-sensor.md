# Yoon et al. 2021 : Gold nanoparticle dual colorimetric probe for mercury and arsenic

## Overview

Yoon and colleagues developed d-penicillamine-functionalized gold nanoparticles (AuNPs) as a dual colorimetric probe for the simultaneous detection of Hg2+ and As3+ in water. The two analytes trigger distinct aggregation mechanisms: Hg2+ coordinates with d-penicillamine via soft-metal thiol interaction, while As3+ forms a different binding geometry, producing distinguishable color responses. Gold is the analytical probe; mercury and arsenic are the target analytes. Validated in spiked drinking water.

## Key numbers

LOD Hg2+: 0.5 nM (approximately 0.1 ppb). LOD As3+: 0.7 nM (approximately 0.05 ppb). Recoveries in water: Hg2+ 95.4 to 103.5%; As3+ 96.8 to 104.1%.
