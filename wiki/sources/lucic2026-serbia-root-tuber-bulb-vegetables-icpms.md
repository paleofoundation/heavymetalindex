# Lučić et al. 2026: Toxic elements in root, tuber and bulb vegetables from Belgrade markets

Eight vegetables bought at four Belgrade markets were measured for twelve elements by ICP-MS on a fresh-weight basis. Lead stayed at or below 0.040 mg/kg in every sample, under the EU limit of 0.10 mg/kg. Cadmium was below the EU limits except in celeriac, where the highest sample reached 0.248 mg/kg against a 0.15 mg/kg limit. Modelled hazard indices stayed below 1.

- Means: celery root had the highest mean Cd (0.134 mg/kg); garlic the highest mean Pb (0.0225 mg/kg), then celery root (0.0210 mg/kg) (p. 8).
- EU Regulation 2023/915 comparison by the authors: all Pb (limit 0.10) and Ni below limits; Cd below limits except celeriac, maximum 0.248 mg/kg, about 1.65 times the 0.15 mg/kg limit (p. 13).
- Risk: deterministic HI 7.96 × 10⁻⁴ to 2.16 × 10⁻¹ (adults) and up to 3.84 × 10⁻¹ (children); Monte Carlo P90 HI below 1 in both groups; P90 total cancer risk above 10⁻⁴ only for adults in the aggregate scenario, and this depends on an assumed cadmium slope factor (Abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Microwave digestion and ICP-MS for Hg, Pb, Mn, Cr, Ni, Cu, Zn, Co, Fe, Al, Cd and As. LODs 0.03 µg/kg (Cd) to 21.5 µg/kg (Al); LOQs 0.09 to 65.2 µg/kg. CRMs NIST SRM 1547 peach leaves and LGC AF-038, recoveries 90% to 109.3%; RSD 2.1% to 13.9% (p. 7). Values below LOD were set to the LOD for risk calculations. Below-LOD counts: Hg 15, As 12, Pb 6, Cd 5 of 32 (p. 7).

Limitations: four composites per vegetable from one city in one month. The CRMs are plant and feed materials, not vegetables. The paper frames all eight vegetables as "underground" matrices (bulbs, tubers, or roots); leek is classified by the authors as a bulb despite its partial above-ground growth, so none of the eight fall within the above-ground non-root vegetable scope.

## Implications

Certification: Good-quality fresh-weight market data for root, tuber and bulb vegetables, with celeriac cadmium the one regulatory exceedance. For the non-root vegetables page it is a close-matrix comparator, not direct evidence.

Courses: A worked example of deterministic versus Monte Carlo dietary risk.

App: Supports low lead estimates and a celeriac-specific cadmium flag.

Microbiome: Not applicable.
