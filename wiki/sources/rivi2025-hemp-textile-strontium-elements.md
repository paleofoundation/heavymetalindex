# Rivi et al. 2025: Elemental composition and strontium isotope ratio in Italian hemp for textiles

## Overview

This study characterized the elemental composition and strontium isotopic ratio of Italian industrial hemp stalks and fiber material for geographic traceability purposes. While the focus is on textile-grade hemp fiber rather than food applications, the study documents heavy metal levels (Hg, Pb, Cd) in hemp material and confirms they were below international textile safety thresholds.

## Key numbers

Measured by AAS, ICP-MS, and MC-ICP-MS after microwave-assisted acid digestion; CRM recoveries 95.7-102.1%.

Heavy metal concentrations (hemp stalks and fibers, Italian origin):
- Hg, Pb, Cd: all below international textile safety thresholds (specific numeric values for Pb and Cd not extracted from PDF summary; source states these elements were "well below" limits)
- Sr: 0.7085 to 0.7105 (87Sr/86Sr isotopic ratio, used as geographic marker)

The analytical protocol included Principal Component Analysis (PCA) indicating partial clustering by geographical origin, distinguishing northern from southern Italian samples, primarily driven by Sr, Ca, Fe, and other trace metals rather than by Pb or Cd.

## Methods (brief)

AAS (Hg, Pb, Cd), ICP-MS (trace elements), MC-ICP-MS (87Sr/86Sr); microwave-assisted acid digestion; CRM recovery 95.7-102.1%; Italian hemp stalks and fibers; multiple Italian regions.

## Evidence fitness

This study applies to hemp fiber/stalk for textile use, not to hemp seed for food. The confirmation that Italian hemp fiber had Hg, Pb, and Cd below textile safety limits provides useful geographic context (Italy is a significant European hemp producer). The numeric values for Pb and Cd in stalks are not extracted in this summary because stalk values are not applicable to hemp seed food safety assessments. The elemental profiling and geographic traceability methodology is relevant to understanding why regional variability in hemp metal levels occurs.

## Limitations

Textile-grade hemp stalks and fibers only - does not represent edible hemp seed. No numeric values for Pb or Cd extracted into food-relevant concentration ranges. Geographic traceability focus rather than food safety focus.
