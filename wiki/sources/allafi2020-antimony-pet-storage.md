# Allafi 2020 ,  Temperature and storage time for antimony from PET

Commercial bottled water. Not identified as baby bottles.

## Key numbers

No significant change after three months at 25 °C. After heating to 50 °C, 8.530 ppb at 24 hours and 16.8 ppb at 7 days (PDF p.1).

## Methods (brief)

Storage and heating of commercial bottles.

## Evidence fitness

Supports those water concentrations. Does not support a baby-bottle result.

## Limitations

Units are the ppb values the paper prints.
