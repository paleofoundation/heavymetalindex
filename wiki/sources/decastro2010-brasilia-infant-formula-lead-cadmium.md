# Toxic metals (Pb and Cd) and their respective antagonists (Ca and Zn) in infant formulas and milk marketed in Brasilia, Brazil

## Summary

This journal article measured lead and cadmium by graphite-furnace atomic absorption spectrophotometry in infant formulas and in milk powder and fluid milk sold in Brasilia. Only powdered rows marked infant formula are formula results. Abstract medians that mix milk powder and fluid milk are not formula-only results. Many formula cells are dashes.

## Key numbers

One powdered infant-formula lead example is L1 at 3.7084±0.5819 mg/kg. Other formula lead cells include detections and dashes. Cadmium formula rows are dashes. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
