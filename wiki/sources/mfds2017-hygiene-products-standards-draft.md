# MFDS 2017: Draft hygiene-product standards for disposable diapers

Public Notice No. 2017-421, dated 22 November 2017, is a Ministry of Food and Drug Safety draft of standards and specifications for hygiene products. A WTO TBT stamp on the file is dated 28 November 2017. Section 13 covers disposable diapers for adults and children, including liners and hygiene mats. The numbers below are draft ceilings. They are not measurements, and this file is not the current consolidated notice.

## Key numbers

Children's diapers and children's mats, inner lining and waterproof layer. Migration, in mg/kg. The footnote limits the migration rows to products for children under 36 months, and to synthetic resin and paper:

| Element | Migration ceiling (mg/kg) |
| --- | ---: |
| Sb | 60 |
| As | 25 |
| Ba | 1,000 |
| Cd | 75 |
| Cr | 60 |
| Pb | 90 |
| Hg | 60 |
| Se | 500 |

Total content, inner lining and waterproof layer, in mg/kg: total lead 300 or below, total cadmium 75 or below. This content row in the diaper section is intact in the text layer.

Also printed for diapers, and not metals: formaldehyde 75 mg/L, and 20 mg/L for diapers for children under 36 months; pentachlorophenol and tetrachlorophenol 0.5 mg/kg; azo dyes 30 mg/kg; the sum of DEHP, DBP, and BBP 0.1% on synthetic-resin tape.

Section 12, children's cotton swabs, prints the same migration ceilings on lines that can be read. The text layer concatenates the content row ("총총카드뮴(Cd) 납(Pb) 300 이하" followed by "75 이하"). Word positions on page 30 separate the rows: at the same vertical position, 총 납(Pb) sits with 300, and on the next line 총 카드뮴(Cd) sits with 75. The content ceilings are total lead 300 mg/kg and total cadmium 75 mg/kg, the same pair as the intact diaper content row. The concatenated text-layer string is an extraction break, not a second pair of limits. There is no cotton-swab product page. The swab figures are noted here only as the same numbers appearing in section 12, not as a routed product. The document is a draft notice. It has no personal corresponding author and no correction record.

## Methods (brief)

The draft points migration testing to section 5 item 23 and total lead and cadmium to section 5 item 24. It does not, in the diaper table itself, state whether arsenic is inorganic or total, whether mercury is methylmercury or total, or whether chromium is hexavalent or total. The content rows are explicitly total lead and total cadmium.

## Evidence fitness

The diaper table supports a record of what this 2017 draft would have required. It does not support occurrence, and it does not support a statement that these numbers are the law in force in 2026. Later Korean test reports in this corpus cite the hygiene-product standard as applied at the time of those tests. They are separate pages.

## Limitations

Draft instrument. Species other than total lead and total cadmium are not stated. The cotton-swab content pair is assigned from word positions on page 30, total lead 300 mg/kg and total cadmium 75 mg/kg. Cotton swabs have no product page.
