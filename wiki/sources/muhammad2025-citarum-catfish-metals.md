# Bioaccumulation of Heavy Metals in Catfish (Hemibagrus nemurus) at the Downstream of Citarum River, Indonesia

## Summary

Muhammad and Syamsudin measured mercury, lead, and cadmium in Hemibagrus nemurus muscle from five stations on the downstream Citarum River, West Java, over 14 days in 2022. Fish were taken with stocking nets and fishing rods. Muscle was oven-dried. The printed fish units are not one label: Table 1 prints mg.L-1 and Figure 5 prints mg/kg-1.

## Key numbers

Table 1 fish averages, printed under Fish [mg.L-1], use decimal commas: mercury 1,26, lead 0,31, cadmium 0,01. Figure 5 station averages, mg/kg-1, are mercury 4.890, 0.590, 0.173, 0.214, 0.465 and lead 0.203, 0.178, 0.209, 0.325, 0.640 and cadmium 0.030, 0.011, 0.004, 0.009, 0.008. The discussion ranges, mg.kg-1, are mercury 0.173 to 4.897, lead 0.178 to 0.640, and cadmium 0.004 to 0.030. Fish count: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
