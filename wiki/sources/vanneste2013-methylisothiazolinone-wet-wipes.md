# Vanneste et al. 2013. Methylisothiazolinone in household wet wipes

Vanneste and colleagues report allergic contact dermatitis from methylisothiazolinone, including household wet wipes. The note is about a preservative. It does not report heavy-metal concentrations. The matrix is household wipes, not baby wipes.

## Key numbers

No heavy-metal concentration is reported.

## Methods (brief)

Contact-point clinical report. HPLC is mentioned in the broader literature on these preservatives. This page does not treat an unrecovered chromatogram as a metal result.

## Evidence fitness

The report can show that household wet wipes are a methylisothiazolinone source. It cannot support a metal profile.

## Limitations

Short contact point. Not a metal survey. The journal flags this section as editor-reviewed rather than fully peer-reviewed.
