# Vitamin B12 supplement 2025 (Poland) — heavy metals in vitamin B12 dietary supplements

## Summary

A study from Poland measured cadmium, lead, and mercury in 10 vitamin B12 dietary supplement products by HR-CS GFAAS and CV-AAS. Cadmium concentrations ranged 0.14-0.45 mg/kg; lead ranged 0.14-0.43 mg/kg; mercury was below the LOD in all 10 products. All hazard quotients (HQ) were below 1, indicating no non-carcinogenic risk from cadmium, lead, or mercury at the labelled daily doses. The study also evaluated vitamin B12 content (methylcobalamin and cyanocobalamin), finding that declared versus measured B12 concentrations did not always match.

## Key numbers

Values in mg/kg product weight by HR-CS GFAAS (Cd, Pb) and CV-AAS (Hg). LOD/LOQ: Cd 0.05/0.17 µg/L, Hg 0.01/0.03 µg/L, Pb 1.0/3.3 µg/L.

| Metal | Range (mg/kg) | EU ML (mg/kg) | Exceedances | HQ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cd | 0.14 to 0.45 | 1.0 | 0 of 10 | <1 all |
| Pb | 0.14 to 0.43 | 3.0 | 0 of 10 | <1 all |
| Hg | <LOD | 0.1 | 0 of 10 | <1 all |

- All HQ values below 1 for all three metals across all 10 products.
- Mercury below limit of detection (0.01 µg/L instrument LOD) in all products.
- Calibration: 0.5-20 µg/L (Cd), 5-60 µg/L (Pb), 0.1-5.0 µg/L (Hg).

## Methods (brief)

10 vitamin B12 dietary supplement products from the Polish market. Cd and Pb by HR-CS GFAAS after acid digestion; Hg by CV-AAS. Calibration with certified standards; matrix modifiers used for GFAAS. Hazard quotient calculated as EDI divided by reference dose. B12 (methylcobalamin and cyanocobalamin) also assayed spectrophotometrically and by HPLC.

## Implications

- Small primary occurrence dataset (n=10) for Cd, Pb, Hg in vitamin B12 dietary supplements on the Polish market; all within EU limits, all HQ below 1.
- Cd range 0.14-0.45 mg/kg and Pb range 0.14-0.43 mg/kg are consistent with low-to-moderate contamination typical of solid-form European supplement products.
- Mercury below LOD confirms low methylmercury risk in this product category from the Polish market.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] and [[products/supplements-vitamin-mineral]]; contributes Cd, Pb, Hg occurrence to [[metals/cadmium]], [[metals/lead]], [[metals/mercury]].
