# Speciation of Lead, Iron and Cadmium in Selected Brands of Canned Sardine Fish Sold in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria

## Summary

Ekwere, Verla, Verla, and Horsfall measured lead, iron, and cadmium by flame atomic absorption spectrophotometry in canned sardine fish meal and drained oil from Port Harcourt supermarkets. Fish meal was dried before digestion. The discussion says the work is on a dry-weight basis. Nine fish-meal samples and nine oil samples were analysed. Brand names are omitted here.

## Key numbers

Abstract pooled means, mg/kg: fish meal lead 13.12±3.81, iron 19.22±4.83, cadmium 2.50±0.48; oil lead 14.37±4.34, iron 15.54±2.45, cadmium 1.65±0.4. Table 5 brand means are different and are not copied. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
