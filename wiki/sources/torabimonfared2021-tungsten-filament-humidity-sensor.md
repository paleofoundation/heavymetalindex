# Torabi-Monfared et al. 2021: A tungsten lamp filament used as a humidity sensor

The authors built an optical relative humidity sensor from the thermionic emission of a fluorescent lamp tungsten filament, reading the emitted light with a camera. Sensing relies on reaction of water with the filament to form hydrated tungsten oxide. The paper concerns instrumentation, not tungsten exposure.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to tungsten exposure.

## Methods (brief)

The filament emission was imaged under controlled humidity, and the surface chemistry was examined by infrared spectroscopy and X-ray diffraction.
