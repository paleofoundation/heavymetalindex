# Lead and Cadmium Bioaccumulation in Fresh Cow's Milk in an Intermediate Area of the Central Andes of Peru and Risk to Human Health

## Summary

This journal article measured lead and cadmium by flame atomic absorption spectrophotometry in raw cow milk from the Mantaro Valley, Peru. n=40. Samples were dried before ashing. Whether the reported values are the dried mass or the original milk: Data gap.

## Key numbers

Lead 15±2.6 µg/kg. Cadmium in the abstract is 505±123; the table standard deviation is printed 123.2. Both are kept. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
