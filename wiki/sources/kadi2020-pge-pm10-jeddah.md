# Kadi et al. 2020 - PGE concentrations in PM10 from Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

## Overview

This study measured platinum group element concentrations in PM10 particles from three sites in Jeddah (urban, suburban, and industrial areas) using ICP-MS. Palladium concentrations varied substantially by site and day of week, with higher concentrations at the site with greatest traffic density. The work contributes to global monitoring data on catalytic converter-derived Pd in ambient air.

## Key numbers

- Pd concentration at site S3 (highest traffic): 19.6 +/- 24.9 to 195.3 +/- 200.4 pg/m3
- Concentrations varied by day of week (working days vs weekends)
- Te and Hg co-precipitation methods used for PGE isolation
- ICP-MS calibration curves from 0 to 7000 ng/L
- Comparison made with published German data for PM10, PM2.5, and PM1 fractions

## Methods (brief)

PM10 high-volume sampler deployed at three sites in Jeddah. Filters digested and PGEs isolated by co-precipitation with tellurium and mercury. ICP-MS quantification. Sampling conducted across working days and weekends to assess traffic-related signal. Comparison with published European reference concentrations.

## Implications

This page makes the source discoverable for palladium evidence routing. Provides PM10 Pd concentrations from a Middle Eastern urban environment, adding geographic diversity to the global dataset on catalytic converter Pd emissions and inhalation exposure estimates.
