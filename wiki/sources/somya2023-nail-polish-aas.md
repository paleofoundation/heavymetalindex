# Determination of heavy metals (Ni, Cd, Co, Cr, Pb) using atomic absorption spectrophotometry (AAS) in nail polish samples

## Summary

Somya, Anita, and Jaiswal measured nickel, cadmium, cobalt, chromium, and lead by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in 20 nail polish samples from a local market in India (10 premium and 10 local). The samples are not identified as children's nail polish. The table unit is ppm. Basis: Data gap. Several table digits wrap across lines, so only the complete prose figures are copied.

## Key numbers

Prose: lead 0.40597, identified as the highest concentration among the samples. Chromium level was 0.00 ppm. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
