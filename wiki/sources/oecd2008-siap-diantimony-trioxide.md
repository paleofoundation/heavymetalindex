# OECD 2008, SIDS Initial Assessment Profile for diantimony trioxide

The SIAM 27 profile for diantimony trioxide, CAS 1309-64-4, states oral and dermal absorption percentages and acute toxicity figures. The dermal percentage is a summary of an unpublished in vitro skin study. That primary report was not opened.

## Key numbers

PDF p.1: oral absorption 0.3 percent at 100 mg/kg body weight and 0.05 percent at 1,000 mg/kg body weight. The profile states that 1 percent is assumed at relevant human exposure levels. Dermal absorption is called negligible on the basis of an in vitro human-skin study that showed 0.26 percent absorption. PDF p.2: 4-hour nose-only LC50 greater than 5.20 mg/L; oral LD50 in excess of 20,000 mg/kg body weight; dermal LD50 higher than 8,300 mg/kg body weight.

## Methods (brief)

SIDS initial assessment profile. Not a consumer-product survey. The Roper and Stupart report summarized here was not in the corpus.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the percentages and acute figures as the profile prints them. Does not support using 0.26 percent as absorption of antimony from a wipe, a textile, or infant skin.

## Limitations

The profile prints one dermal percentage. OEHHA 2016, opened separately, also prints 0.14 percent at a higher surface load. Those are summaries of the same unpublished study, not a new measurement.
