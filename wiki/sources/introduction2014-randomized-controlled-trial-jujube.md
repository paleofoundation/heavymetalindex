## Study Design and Population

A randomized controlled trial conducted in Isfahan, Iran, comparing jujube fruit supplementation to control in lactating mothers. Sample comprised 40 lactating mothers (20 intervention, 20 control). Intervention group received jujube fruit for 8 weeks; control group received standard dietary advice only.

## Metals Measured and Baseline Concentrations

Analysis included three metals measured in human milk samples:

| Metal | Baseline Mean | SD | Unit |
|-------|---------------|-----|------|
| Pb (Lead) | 29.49 | 16.66 | µg/L |
| Cd (Cadmium) | 4.65 | 3.51 | µg/L |
| As (Arsenic) | 1.23 | 0.63 | µg/L |

## Analytical Methods

Quantile regression analysis was applied to evaluate differences between intervention and control groups across different percentiles of the metal concentration distributions.

## Key Findings

**Intervention Group Changes (relative to control, baseline to 8-week endpoint):**

- **Lead (Pb):** Significant reduction at the 90th percentile: −2.54 µg/L (95% CI reported)
- **Cadmium (Cd):** Significant reduction at the 75th percentile: −0.19 µg/L (95% CI reported)
- **Arsenic (As):** No significant change across measured percentiles

## Population Context

- All participants were lactating mothers in Isfahan, Iran
- Study duration: 8 weeks
- Intervention: dietary supplementation with jujube fruit
- Outcome: changes in metal concentrations in human milk
