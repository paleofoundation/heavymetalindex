This source is the Laws of Trinidad and Tobago consolidation of the Food and Drugs Act, Chapter 30:01, including the Food and Drugs Regulations and the Fish and Fishery Products Regulations. The PDF is marked "Updated to December 31st 2007."

HMI records four routeable metal-limit surfaces from the source. Food and Drugs Regulations Division 2 limits arsenic and lead in colours for use in or upon food. Division 6 lists arsenic and lead levels for named foods and a residual "food not named" category under poisonous-substances provisions. Division 19 sets naturally occurring contaminant limits in food-grade salt for arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury. Fish and Fishery Products Regulations regulation 62(18) and Second Schedule Table 1 set accepted levels for lead and methylmercury in fish and fishery products.

Values are recorded in the structured evidence record in `ug/kg`, converted from source `parts per million` or `ppm` values by multiplying by 1000. The source's arsenic rows are recorded as total arsenic (`tAs`) because the law does not specify inorganic arsenic. The salt mercury row is recorded as total mercury (`tHg`). The fish schedule explicitly says methyl mercury, so those rows are recorded as `MeHg`.
