# Lin et al. 2022: Gutzeit-XRF method for inorganic arsenic in apple juice

## Overview

US academic study (University of Massachusetts Amherst) developing a greener Gutzeit method combined with X-ray fluorescence spectroscopy (XRF) for inorganic arsenic detection, substituting silver nitrate for toxic mercury bromide as the arsenic capture reagent. A commercial Gerber apple juice was used as a model matrix.

## Key numbers

Silver nitrate method LOD: 1.02 µg/L iAs; LOQ: 3.40 µg/L iAs. Recovery from apple juice matrix: 85-99%. This is notably below the FDA action level of 10 µg/L (10 ppb) for inorganic arsenic in apple juice.

## Scope and use

This is a method development paper. The apple juice is a model matrix, not a surveyed product. The source is included here as analytical-method context for inorganic arsenic detection in fruit juice and to document that the FDA 10 µg/L action level for inorganic arsenic in apple juice was the reference standard for method performance assessment. It does not contribute occurrence data to the infant-juice product page.
