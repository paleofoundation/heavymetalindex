# Jasinska-Balwierz et al. 2025 (Poland) — heavy metals in adaptogenic herbal dietary supplements

## Summary

Jasinska-Balwierz et al. measured lead, cadmium, nickel, copper, and zinc in 11 adaptogenic herbal dietary supplements from the Polish market using ICP-OES. The findings are notable for significant lead and nickel exceedances: 5 of 11 products exceeded the maximum permissible level (MPL) for lead, with Schisandra supplement recording the highest concentration at 10.048 mg/kg -- 235% above the MPL. Nickel exceeded limits in a majority of samples. Cadmium was below MPL in all tested products. The study evaluates exposure via target hazard quotient and incremental lifetime cancer risk and concludes that some adaptogenic supplements pose an unacceptable metal contamination risk.

## Key numbers

Values in mg/kg dry weight by ICP-OES. LOD (mg/L): Pb 0.0130, Cd 0.0028, Ni 0.0043, Cu 0.0045, Zn 0.0033. LOQ approximately 3-fold LOD. MPL for Pb referenced as 3.0 mg/kg (EU Commission Regulation for food supplements).

- Lead in Schisandra supplement 10.048 mg/kg = 3.35x the EU ML of 3.0 mg/kg for food supplements.
- 5/11 products (45%) exceeded the EU lead ML.
- Nickel exceedances evaluated against toxicological reference values in absence of an EU ML.

## Methods (brief)

11 adaptogenic herbal dietary supplement products from the Polish market. ICP-OES after microwave acid digestion (HNO3/H2O2). Calibration with certified multi-element standards. LOD/LOQ calculated from blank measurements. Exposure assessed via EDI, THQ (target hazard quotient), and ILCR (incremental lifetime cancer risk) assuming labelled daily dose and standard body weight.

## Implications

- Primary occurrence dataset (n=11) for Pb, Cd, Ni, Cu, Zn in adaptogenic herbal supplements; 45% exceeded EU lead ML, with Schisandra supplement at 10.048 mg/kg -- the highest reported Pb concentration in this evidence unit's supplement cohort.
- Nickel exceedances highlight the gap in EU regulation (no ML for Ni in food supplements), making risk characterization dependent on toxicological reference values.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] and [[products/supplements-botanicals-herbs]]; contributes high-concentration Pb signal to [[metals/lead]] and [[metals/nickel]] supplement records.
- Schisandra and other adaptogens are higher-risk botanical matrices for lead given soil-accumulation properties.
