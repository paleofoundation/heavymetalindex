# Tan et al. 2020: A zirconium metal-organic framework for fluoride removal from water

This study made a zirconium-based metal-organic framework (MOF-801) and tested it for removing fluoride from water. It removed up to 92.3% of fluoride with a maximum capacity of 19.42 mg/g and kept about 79% removal after four regeneration cycles. Zirconium release into treated water was not reported.

## Key numbers

- Maximum fluoride removal 92.3%; Langmuir capacity 19.42 mg/g; about 79% removal after the fourth cycle (Abstract).
- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Solvothermal synthesis, PXRD, FESEM, XPS and BET characterization, and adsorption tests varying contact time, dose and fluoride concentration, with kinetic, isotherm and regeneration studies.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium as a metal-organic framework adsorbent for fluoride removal; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium. The paper did not report zirconium concentrations in treated water.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
