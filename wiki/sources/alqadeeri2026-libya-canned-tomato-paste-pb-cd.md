# Al-Qadeeri et al. 2026: Lead and cadmium in canned tomato paste, Derna, Libya

## Overview

Al-Qadeeri and Abdullah measured lead and cadmium by AAS in 12 canned tomato paste products bought in Derna, Libya, after nitric acid digestion. Table 2 reports each product's mean and SD in mg/L of the digest solution, not per kilogram of paste. The authors compare these values with limits they also quote in mg/L.

## Key numbers

- Lead: "0.007±0.000" mg/L (STB 04) to "0.094±0.002" mg/L (STB 09) of digest (Table 2, p. 54).
- Cadmium: "0.001±0.002" mg/L (STB 11) to "0.090±0.002" mg/L (STB 08) of digest (Table 2).
- Three products (STB 05, 08, 09) are above the 0.05 mg/L cadmium limit the authors cite (Discussion, p. 55).

## Methods (brief)

Half a gram of paste was heated with 25 mL water and 5 mL concentrated nitric acid until clear, filtered and diluted to 50 mL, then read by AAS. No detection limits, reference materials, recoveries or replicate counts are reported.

## Evidence Fitness

The values are concentrations in the digest solution (0.5 g in 50 mL), so they are not mg/kg of paste and must not be compared with mg/kg rows or food limits; the extraction keeps them per litre. There is no QA, the replicate count is not stated, and several cadmium SDs exceed their means. Peer review is unverified.
