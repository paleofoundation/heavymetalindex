# Huang et al. 2024: Zirconium-loaded tannin foam for fluoride and lead in tea garden soil

This study made a bayberry tannin foam loaded with zirconium and tested it as an adsorbent for fluoride and lead in water and as an amendment in acidic tea garden soil. It shifted fluoride and lead toward less reactive soil fractions. Zirconium itself was not measured in soil, water or tea plants.

## Key numbers

- Adsorption capacities from water: 5.02 mg/g for lead and 4.55 mg/g for fluoride (Abstract).
- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Material synthesis from tannin foam and zirconium sulfate, adsorption kinetics and isotherms in water, and a 60-day soil incubation with sequential extraction of fluoride and lead fractions.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium as an adsorbent to immobilize fluoride and lead; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium. The paper did not report whether zirconium was released into water or soil.

## Limitations

Laboratory and pot-scale work; no measurement of zirconium release from the material or of zirconium, fluoride or lead in tea leaves.
