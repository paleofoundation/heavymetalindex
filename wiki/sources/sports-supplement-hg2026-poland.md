# Sports supplement Hg 2026 (Poland) — mercury in sports food supplements

## Summary

This study measured total mercury in 50 sports food supplement products from the Polish market using the AMA-254 amalgamation atomic absorption spectrometer. All 50 products were below the EU maximum level of 0.1 mg/kg (100 µg/kg) for mercury in food supplements. The median mercury content was 9.008 µg/kg, with considerable variation across product categories. The highest individual value was 51.694 µg/kg, still approximately half the EU limit. A screening exposure assessment was performed against EFSA tolerable weekly intakes.

## Key numbers

Total mercury by AMA-254 amalgamation AAS. LOD: 0.003 ng Hg.

| Statistic | Value (µg/kg) |
| --- | --- |
| Median | 9.008 |
| IQR | 5.287 to 21.938 |
| Range | 0.395 to 51.694 |
| EU ML | 100 (= 0.1 mg/kg) |
| Exceedances | 0 of 50 |

- EFSA TWI references used: inorganic Hg 4 µg/kg bw/week; methylmercury 1.3 µg/kg bw/week (both expressed as Hg).
- Mercury speciation (inorganic vs. methyl) not performed; all values are total Hg.

## Methods (brief)

50 sports food supplement products from the Polish market. Total Hg by AMA-254 amalgamation AAS: approximately 0.02 g solid sample placed in nickel cuvettes; direct thermal decomposition, amalgamation on gold, and AAS detection. LOD 0.003 ng Hg. Results compared against EU Commission Regulation ML (0.1 mg/kg) and EFSA TWIs. Exposure assessment assumed labelled daily dose and body weight of 70 kg.

## Implications

- Primary occurrence dataset (n=50) for total mercury in sports food supplements on the Polish market; all 50 products within EU limits (max 51.694 µg/kg versus EU ML 100 µg/kg).
- Median (9.008 µg/kg) and range (0.395-51.694 µg/kg) establish a baseline distribution for sports supplement Hg in the EU market.
- Mercury speciation absent; inorganic and methylmercury contributions to total Hg unknown.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]]; contributes to [[metals/mercury]] supplement occurrence record.
