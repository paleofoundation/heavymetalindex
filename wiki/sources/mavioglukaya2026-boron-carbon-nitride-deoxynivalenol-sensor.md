# Mavioğlu Kaya et al. 2026: A boron carbon nitride sensor for the mycotoxin deoxynivalenol

This method paper built a molecularly imprinted surface plasmon resonance sensor on sulfur-doped boron graphitic carbon nitride to detect the mycotoxin deoxynivalenol in drinking water and orange juice. Boron is part of the sensor material, not an analyte.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to boron exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Nanocomposite synthesis by thermal polycondensation and microwave treatment; SPR sensing with recovery tests.

## Evidence fitness

Boron appears here only as a component of an engineered material. The paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for boron.

## Limitations

Off-topic for boron exposure; no boron measurements in food, water or people. No external funding; no conflicts declared.
