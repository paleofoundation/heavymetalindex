# Liu et al. 2018: Cerium-doped titanium dioxide spray to break down an insecticide on bok choy

Field-grown bok choy treated with the insecticide dimethoate was sprayed with a cerium-doped nano titanium dioxide hydrosol to speed photocatalytic breakdown of the residue. The treatment raised reactive oxygen species in the leaves and accelerated dimethoate degradation; cerium residues on the vegetable were not measured.

## Key numbers

- Cerium doping of the titanium dioxide was 0.6% in molar ratio (Materials, p. 3).
- No cerium measurements in bok choy are reported.

## Methods (brief)

Field plots with dimethoate application followed by hydrosol spray; residues and breakdown products measured by UPLC-MS.

## Evidence fitness

Shows a route by which a cerium-containing material can be sprayed directly onto a leafy vegetable. Because cerium on or in the leaves was not measured, it cannot support any residue or exposure statement.

## Limitations

No cerium analysis; one crop and location.
