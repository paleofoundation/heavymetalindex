# Deng 2021 ,  Review of Sb-oxidizing bacteria

Review. A cited disruption of aioA decreased Sb(III) oxidation by 25 percent in one strain (PDF p.6). The review states that the genes required for antimony methylation are still unknown (PDF p.8-9).

## Key numbers

Review. A cited disruption of aioA decreased Sb(III) oxidation by 25 percent in one strain (PDF p.6). The review states that the genes required for antimony methylation are still unknown (PDF p.8-9).

## Methods (brief)

No new experiment. Methylated antimony is described as reported from fungi, archaea, and bacteria.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the review's own statements. Does not support a named methylation gene in an infant gut.

## Limitations

Later papers, including Thomas 2011, narrow the methylation statement without closing it.
