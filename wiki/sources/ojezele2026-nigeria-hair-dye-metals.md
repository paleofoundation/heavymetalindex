# Heavy metal concentrations and inter-metal relationships in commercially available hair dyes of different colours: a chemometric evaluation

## Summary

This journal article measured chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, cadmium, nickel, and iron in 21 commercially available hair dyes in seven colour categories, three samples per colour. Results are mg/kg of hair dye product. Brand names are not copied. Chromium is not speciated.

## Key numbers

Abstract: chromium 0.230±0.245 mg/kg in green dyes to 2.53±0.12 mg/kg in blue dyes. Colour-mean table, in the printed column order black, brown, red, green, white, purple, blue: lead white 2.56±0.26 mg/kg; nickel blue 4.93±0.18 mg/kg; cadmium brown 0.111±0.097 mg/kg. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
