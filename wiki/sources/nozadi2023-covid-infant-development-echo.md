# Nozadi et al. 2023 — COVID-19 pandemic hardships and infant/toddler development in the ECHO program

This paper examines effects of pandemic-related financial and social hardships on developmental outcomes (communication, socioemotional) in 684 children under 4 years from 8 ECHO cohorts, using the Ages and Stages Questionnaire (ASQ). It found that boys had higher odds of negative changes in communication and personal-social skills during the pandemic, but pandemic hardships per se did not significantly explain within-child developmental changes. There is no heavy metal measurement content in this paper.
