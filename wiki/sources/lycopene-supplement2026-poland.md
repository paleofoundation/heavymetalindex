# Lycopene-supplement 2026 (Poland) — preliminary quality and safety evaluation of lycopene dietary supplements

## Summary

A preliminary quality and safety study measured cadmium, lead, nickel, chromium, arsenic, and mercury in three lycopene-based dietary supplement products from the Polish market. All six metals were within EU maximum levels for food supplements. Mercury was below the limit of quantification across all three products. The study also evaluated active compound content and microbiological purity, finding the chemical contaminant burden to be low in this small sample.

## Key numbers

Values in mg/kg dry weight unless noted. EU regulatory reference: Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/915 (Pb 3.0 mg/kg, Cd 1.0 mg/kg, Hg 0.1 mg/kg for food supplements).

| Metal | Max observed (mg/kg) | EU ML (mg/kg) | Exceedances |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pb | 0.153 | 3.0 | 0 of 3 |
| Cd | 0.095 | 1.0 | 0 of 3 |
| Hg | <LOQ | 0.1 | 0 of 3 |
| Ni | measured | n/a | 0 of 3 |
| Cr | measured | n/a | 0 of 3 |
| As (total) | measured | n/a | 0 of 3 |

- LOQ: 0.001 mg/kg for Hg; 0.005 mg/kg (sub-method 1 analytes); 0.01 mg/kg (sub-method 2 analytes).
- n=3; small sample limits generalization to the broader Polish lycopene supplement market.

## Methods (brief)

Three lycopene dietary supplement products sampled from the Polish market. Metals measured by validated analytical methods with LOQ established per analyte. Results compared against EU Commission Regulation maximum levels for food supplements. Active compound lycopene content and microbiological purity also assessed (not summarized here as outside metal scope).

## Implications

- Small primary occurrence dataset (n=3) for lead, cadmium, mercury, nickel, chromium, and total arsenic in lycopene dietary supplements on the Polish market.
- All six metals within EU regulatory limits, with mercury below LOQ in all products.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] as a finished-supplement occurrence entry; contributes to [[metals/cadmium]], [[metals/lead]], and [[metals/arsenic]] records for the supplement category.
- n=3 is insufficient to characterize the distribution; findings are directional, not benchmark-ready.
