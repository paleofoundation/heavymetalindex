# Comparative analysis of heavy metal content of unregulated facial whitening creams and branded facial whitening creams

## Summary

This journal article measured metals by ICP-OES in facial whitening creams purchased in Oman. The abstract says a total of twenty-two samples were purchased. The methods sentence says twenty-two unregulated facial creams were purchased. Both sentences are kept. The label is face whitening creams. Results are ppm. Brand names are omitted. Chromium is not speciated. Arsenic is not speciated.

## Key numbers

Printed maxima: arsenic 1.74 ppm (Al Batinah South), cadmium 2.02 ppm (Al Wusta), chromium 8.04 ppm (Dhofar), lead 29.85 ppm (Al Buraimi). A results sentence also prints lead at 25.36 ppm in one sample with copper 6.52 ppm and chromium 2.65 ppm. Mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
