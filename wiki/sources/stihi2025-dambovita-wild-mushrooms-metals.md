# Assessment of the impact of metals in wild edible mushrooms from Dambovita County, Romania, on human health

## Summary

This journal article measured metals in wild edible mushrooms from Dambovita County, Romania. The abstract prints nickel, chromium, lead, and cadmium in mg/kg. Fresh or dry basis for that abstract sentence: Data gap.

## Key numbers

Abstract: nickel 0.033 to 4.507 mg/kg, chromium 0.003 to 0.760 mg/kg, lead maximum 0.886 mg/kg, cadmium maximum 0.850 mg/kg. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Chromium is not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
