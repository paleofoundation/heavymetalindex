# Mexico 2025 (TXRF) — heavy metals in Mexican dietary supplements

## Summary

This study used total X-ray fluorescence spectrometry (TXRF) to measure lead, arsenic, and chromium in 45 dietary supplement products from the Mexican market. Mean concentrations were: Pb 1.99 mg/kg, As 2.39 mg/kg, Cr 26.88 mg/kg. Approximately 80% of supplements had low lead estimated daily intakes (EDIs). However, cumulative carcinogenic risk (CCR) calculations placed some products in the intolerable range, and a subset exceeded USP Chapter 2232 elemental impurity limits. The findings highlight quality control gaps in an unregulated market segment.

## Key numbers

Values in mg/kg product weight unless noted. TXRF method with LODs: Pb 0.05 µg/L, As 0.03 µg/L, Cr 0.08 µg/L (instrumental).

| Metal | Mean (mg/kg) | n above USP limit | CCR finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pb | 1.99 | subset exceeded | n/a |
| As (total) | 2.39 | subset exceeded | intolerable range for some |
| Cr | 26.88 | subset exceeded | intolerable range for some |

- 80% of supplements had low lead EDIs (below EFSA TDI of 0.00006 mg/kg bw/day).
- CCR calculations placed some products in intolerable range for arsenic and chromium.
- EFSA CONTAM chromium TDI cited in comparison.
- Arsenic reported as total As; no inorganic speciation performed.

## Methods (brief)

45 dietary supplement products from the Mexican market. TXRF (total X-ray fluorescence spectrometry) with Pb/Cr/As measured simultaneously. Calibration with certified multi-element standards (1000 µg/mL Pb, Cr, As stock solutions diluted to working standards). LODs recalculated from linear regression; LOQs calculated accordingly. EDI computed as product concentration multiplied by daily dose. CCR estimated for arsenic and chromium.

## Implications

- Primary occurrence dataset (n=45) for lead, total arsenic, and chromium in dietary supplements from the Mexican market; demonstrates that mean Pb (1.99 mg/kg) and total As (2.39 mg/kg) can be substantial in unregulated supplement categories.
- CCR in intolerable range for some products indicates meaningful cancer risk signal from arsenic and chromium at Mexican market exposures.
- TXRF method covers Pb, As, Cr simultaneously but does not speciate arsenic; inorganic arsenic fraction unknown.
- Routes to [[products/dietary-supplements]] and [[products/supplements-botanicals-herbs]]; contributes to [[metals/lead]], [[metals/arsenic]], and [[metals/chromium]] supplement occurrence records.
