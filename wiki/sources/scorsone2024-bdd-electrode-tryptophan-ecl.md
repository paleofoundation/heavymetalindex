# Scorsone et al. 2024: Tryptophan detection on boron-doped diamond electrodes

This method paper used electrochemiluminescence on boron-doped diamond electrodes to detect L-tryptophan, with a detection limit of 0.4 nM for tryptophan in buffer. Boron is only the electrode dopant.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to boron exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Electrochemiluminescence with in situ hydrogen peroxide generation at boron-doped diamond electrodes; selectivity tests against indolic compounds.

## Evidence fitness

Boron appears here only as a component of an engineered material. The paper does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for boron.

## Limitations

Off-topic for boron exposure; no boron measurements in food, water or people. No external funding; no conflicts declared.
