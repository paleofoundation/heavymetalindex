# Emurotu et al. 2024: Heavy metals in livestock offal and muscle, Felele Abattoir, Lokoja, Nigeria

## Overview

This study determined concentrations of aluminium (Al), cadmium (Cd), chromium (Cr), nickel (Ni), and lead (Pb) in kidney, liver, and muscle tissues of cattle, goats, and rams slaughtered at the main abattoir in Lokoja, northcentral Nigeria. Samples were collected over six weeks; animals were open-grazing, with access to contaminated land along roads, dump sites, and mining areas. Analysis was by ICP-OES. All internal organs had Ni concentrations above the regulatory threshold. Cadmium in muscle exceeded the maximum permissible limit (MPL) of 0.05 mg/kg. The combined hazard index was greater than 1 for all animal-offal combinations, driven predominantly by cadmium.

## Key numbers

**Concentration ranges across kidney, liver, and muscle of all three species (mg/kg dry weight)**

The paper reports concentration ranges in its supplementary Table S2:

| Analyte | Range across all tissue types | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Al | not detected to 42.4 | Above MPL (1.0 mg/kg) in all internal organs |
| Cd | 0.34-0.95 | Muscle above MPL (0.05 mg/kg) |
| Cr | not detected to 1.68 | Below MPL (1.0 mg/kg) except cow kidney |
| Ni | 0.42-9.22 | Above regulatory threshold in all internal organs |
| Pb | not detected to 1.49 | Above WHO MPL in muscle and liver |

LOD values (mg/kg): Al 0.00197; Cd 0.00011; Co 0.00074; Cr 0.00125; Ni 0.00072; Pb 0.00240. Standard addition method used for QA. The hazard quotient sequence was Cd > Ni > Cr > Pb > Al. The percentage contribution of Cd to the total Hazard Index was highest. Authors note that cadmium tissue hazard quotient (THQ) exceeded 1, indicating potential health risks.

## Methods (brief)

Approximately 100 g samples collected at Felele abattoir, transported on ice. Dried at ambient temperature, ground in porcelain mortar, sieved (less than 2 mm). Digested in 65% HNO3 and 70% HClO4 (3:2) overnight then heated in water bath at 70 degrees C for 3 h. Analysis by ICP-OES. Standard addition procedure for accuracy validation; CRM analysis not explicitly stated for tissue samples.

## Implications

- The concentration ranges are on a dry-weight basis; the paper does not report wet-weight or moisture content, so values cannot be directly compared to wet-weight occurrence data or EU limits without conversion.
- Context note: open-grazing animals in an urban-industrial catchment (Lokoja is situated at the Niger-Benue confluence with proximity to industrial and road emissions); values likely exceed what would be found in controlled or regulated slaughter systems.
- Cadmium in offal from this abattoir exceeded limits; supports the page's discussion of feed exposure, environmental contamination, and regional variation as drivers.
