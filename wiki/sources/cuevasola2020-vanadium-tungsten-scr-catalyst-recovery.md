# Cueva Sola et al. 2020: Solvent extraction to recover tungsten and vanadium from spent catalysts

This hydrometallurgy paper recovers vanadium and tungsten from leach liquors of spent catalysts used for nitrogen oxide control, using a quaternary ammonium extractant. The feed contained about 7 g/L tungsten and 0.7 g/L vanadium. The work concerns metal recycling, not food or human exposure.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to tungsten exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Solvent extraction and stripping tests varying pH, extractant level, diluent, chloride, temperature and stripping reagents, with McCabe-Thiele analysis of stage requirements.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: an industrial recovery paper that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for tungsten.

## Limitations

Process chemistry only; no environmental or biological measurements.
