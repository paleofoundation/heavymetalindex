# Zheng et al. 2019 : Gold nanoparticle-amplified microcantilever biosensor for foodborne bacteria

## Overview

Zheng and colleagues developed a microcantilever array biosensor using gold nanoparticles as mass-loading signal amplifiers for the simultaneous culture-free detection of six foodborne bacterial pathogens in milk. Gold nanoparticles provide the analytical mass amplification; they are not measured as food contaminants. The multiplex capability (six pathogens in a single measurement) and sub-10 cell/mL sensitivity without PCR amplification make this a demonstration of AuNP utility in food microbiological safety screening.

## Key numbers

LOD: 1 to 9 cells/mL depending on species. Working range: 3 to 1.2x10^5 cells/mL. Detection time: under 1 hour. Validated in spiked pasteurized milk.

## Implications for the wiki

This paper contributes to [[metals/gold]] as documentation of AuNP-based sensing platforms applied to microbiological food safety. No food occurrence data for gold or any heavy metal are provided.
