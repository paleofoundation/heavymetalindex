# Market vegetable metal measurements in Pakistan

## Study scope and provenance

This primary study reports market vegetables from peri-urban Rawalpindi and Multan. The acquisition filename mentions collard greens, but the paper itself supplies a turnip-root row. Filename wording is not used to establish food identity. The original held PDF hash and DOI identify the artifact; the PDF is not redistributed. Only the exact turnip row authorized for the ingredient draft is extracted here. Broader vegetable results remain outside this bounded extraction.

## Turnip findings

Table 1 identifies Rawalpindi turnip and reports means ± standard deviations on a dry-weight basis: cadmium 1.3256 ± 0.087 mg/kg, total chromium 1.634 ± 0.149 mg/kg, lead 4.266 ± 0.862 mg/kg and nickel 5.286 ± 0.282 mg/kg. The same row also reports manganese 9.642 ± 1.478 mg/kg, iron 37.512 ± 3.390 mg/kg and zinc 25.831 ± 5.165 mg/kg. These are study summaries, not individual sample results or a general concentration profile.

## Methods and limitations

The methods describe drying vegetables, grinding the material, acid digestion and atomic absorption spectrometry. Table 1 labels the root-vegetable group n=36, without resolving the turnip-specific denominator. The collection dates and usable turnip-specific reporting limits are unresolved. No dry-to-fresh conversion, pooling with other markets or independent treatment effect is inferred.

## Evidence Fitness

The source supports the bounded turnip row and its original units and dispersion. It does not establish radish or collard-greens occurrence, a current market distribution, current legal maxima, a reduction factor or food safety. Other crops, metals and regions in the paper require separate extraction and routing before an ingest-complete claim can be made.
