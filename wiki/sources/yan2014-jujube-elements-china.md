# Yan et al. 2014, elements in dried jujube from four Chinese locations

Yan and colleagues measured elements in four dried jujube fruits. Table 4 unit is μg/g. This note writes µg/g for those numerals. Cadmium was ND at all four locations. ND is not a measured zero. Recovery on the Xinjiang Hetian sample is printed in the method table (aluminum 104.0%, lead 92.0%).

## Key numbers

Table 4, µg/g.

| Origin | Aluminum | Lead | Cadmium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Xinjiang Hetian | 32.0 ± 1.3 | 3.1 ± 0.2 | ND |
| Hebei | 4.8 ± 0.2 | 3.0 ± 0.2 | ND |
| Xinzheng | 50.6 ± 2.4 | 4.2 ± 0.5 | ND |
| Huixian | 15.9 ± 0.6 | 3.6 ± 0.3 | ND |

The discussion prints lead from 3.0 to 4.2 µg/g, which matches the table. Origins are not pooled.

## Methods (brief)

Dried fruit was digested and measured by ICP. The method reports n = 6 for the precision check.

## Evidence fitness

The four rows support a dried-jujube note for aluminum and lead. Cadmium ND is not a concentration.

## Limitations

Dried fruit, not fresh. Four locations only. Cadmium is not detected.
