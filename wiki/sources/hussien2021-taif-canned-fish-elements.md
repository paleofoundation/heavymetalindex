# Investigating microplastics and potentially toxic elements contamination in canned tuna, salmon, and sardine fishes from Taif markets, KSA

## Summary

This journal article measured microplastics and potentially toxic elements in canned tuna, salmon, and sardine from Taif markets. The sardine rows are the non-predatory fish results. Tuna and salmon rows are not copied onto the non-predatory page. The sardine aluminum figure is ppm. Cadmium, chromium, lead, and nickel are printed absent for the sardine rows. Wet or dry basis, and whether salt or oil was weighed with the tissue: Data gap. n=2 sardine groups, with triplicates pooled. An Indonesia aluminum cell is not aligned in the table text and is not copied.

## Key numbers

Sardine, Morocco: aluminum 18.5701±0.05 ppm. Cadmium, chromium, lead, and nickel for those sardine rows are printed absent. Mercury and tin: Data gap. A tuna Italy arsenic value of 0.4087 is not a sardine result.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
