# Human exposure assessment to potentially toxic elements (PTEs) from tofu consumption

## Summary

The published article is Environmental Science and Pollution Research, 2021, DOI 10.1007/s11356-021-13076-5. The digits below were read from the Research Square preprint printing of the same work (DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-171049/v1). Table 3 is mg/kg wet weight. n=130. Chromium is named in the aim and has no tofu column in the table copied here.

## Key numbers

Aluminum in flavoured tofu 6.71±5.65 mg/kg wet weight. Nickel in smoked tofu 0.38 mg/kg wet weight. The Europe lead table prints 0.03±0.01 and the results text prints 0.003. The text says the lowest nickel is 0.18 in flavoured tofu; the table prints flavoured nickel 0.31±0.12 and homemade nickel 0.18±0.15. Both printings are kept. Chromium, arsenic, mercury, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
