# Mondal et al. 2018 : Gold nanoparticle-aptamer colorimetric biosensor for staphylococcal enterotoxin B

## Overview

Mondal and colleagues developed a label-free colorimetric biosensor using gold nanoparticles (13 nm, citrate-capped) conjugated to an anti-SEB DNA aptamer for the detection of staphylococcal enterotoxin B (SEB) in food. SEB binding to the aptamer displaces the citrate salt and induces AuNP aggregation with a red-to-blue color shift. Gold nanoparticles are the analytical signal element; SEB is the target analyte. Validated matrices include commercial whole milk, apple juice, and pomegranate juice.

## Key numbers

Visual LOD: 50 ng/mL SEB. Spectrometric LOD: 0.5 ng/mL. Detection time: a few minutes. Recoveries: 88 to 96% across food matrices. Spectrometric result at 520 nm (dispersed AuNPs) versus 620 nm (aggregated AuNPs).

## Implications for the wiki

This paper contributes to [[metals/gold]] as documentation of AuNP-aptamer colorimetric platforms applied to foodborne toxin detection in multiple food matrices including dairy and juices. No food occurrence data for gold or any heavy metal are provided.
