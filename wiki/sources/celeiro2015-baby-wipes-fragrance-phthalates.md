# Celeiro et al. 2015. Fragrance allergens, musks, phthalates, and preservatives in baby wipes

Maria Celeiro and colleagues developed a pressurized-liquid-extraction GC-MS method for 65 fragrance allergens, musks, phthalates, and preservatives in baby wipes. The DOI is 10.1016/j.chroma.2015.01.049. No elemental metal concentration is reported.

## Key numbers

No metal result.

## Methods (brief)

PLE-GC-MS of organic target lists in baby wipes.

## Evidence fitness

The paper supports an organic-contaminant method for wipes. It does not support a metal occurrence claim.

## Limitations

Out of scope for metal totals except as a documented non-metal source on the wipes page.
