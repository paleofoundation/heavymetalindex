# Sola et al. 2024 - Dietary exposure to arsenic and toxic elements, Catalonia review

## Summary

Sola et al. reviewed studies on dietary exposure to arsenic and other toxic elements (including mercury) conducted in Catalonia, Spain, in the 21st century. The review synthesizes findings from total diet studies, food basket analyses, and biomonitoring. This paper is relevant to the mercury-total page as a European dietary exposure context source, documenting the contribution of different food groups to mercury intake in Spain.
