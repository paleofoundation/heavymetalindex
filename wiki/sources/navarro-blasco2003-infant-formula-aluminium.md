# Navarro-Blasco 2003, aluminium in infant formulae, author manuscript

This file is an author manuscript. Aluminium was measured after reconstitution according to the manufacturer instructions. The unit is µg l-1, written here as µg/L. It is not a powder concentration in mg/kg. Recovery was 96% to 104%. Detection limit 3.3 µg/L. Soya formula is a separate row and is not used for cow's-milk formula.

## Key numbers

Table 3, cow's-milk-based types, µg/L.

| Type | n | Mean | Median ± SD | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preterm | 7 | 449 | 421 ± 137 | 317 to 726 |
| Starter, non-adapted | 4 | 237 | 231 ± 128 | 118 to 368 |
| Starter, adapted | 16 | 252 | 196 ± 152 | 68 to 573 |
| Follow-up | 19 | 292 | 272 ± 189 | 66 to 788 |

Specialised rows (without lactose, hypoallergenic, inborn errors) and the soya row are not copied onto the cow's-milk page. The type-row counts in the full Table 3 sum to 82, while the methods also print powder n = 61 and ready-to-use n = 14. Both are printed.

## Methods (brief)

Formula was reconstituted and aluminium was measured by atomic absorption. The detection limit is wet weight as printed for the formula matrix.

## Evidence fitness

The four cow's-milk rows support a reconstituted-formula aluminium note. They do not support a dry-powder mg/kg value or a soya value.

## Limitations

Author manuscript. Specialised formulae are not treated as ordinary cow's-milk formula. Sample-count statements disagree.
