# Khan et al. 2026: Metals in biscuits and cookies by ICP-MS, Quetta, Pakistan

## Overview

Khan and colleagues analysed eight biscuit and cookie samples from Quetta by ICP-MS for aluminum, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, nickel, lead, titanium and zinc. Lead and cadmium were low. Most arsenic results are printed as negative numbers, meaning below the blank.

## Key numbers

- Lead 0.0214 to 0.0596 ppm across eight samples (Table 01, p. 11).
- Cadmium 0.0005 to 0.0064 ppm, with one sample printed as 0.0000 (Table 01).
- Arsenic printed as -0.0070 to -0.0003 ppm in seven samples and 0.0019 ppm in one (Table 01).

## Methods (brief)

Samples were briefly oven-dried, ground, digested with nitric, perchloric and sulfuric acid and analysed by ICP-MS (NexION 300Q) against calibration standards with blanks. No detection limits or reference materials are reported. The results table is an image and was read from the rendered page.
