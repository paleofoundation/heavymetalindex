# Rubin 2026. Comparison chart of metals in eleven diaper creams

A consumer-advocacy business charted its laboratory results for eleven diaper creams, one unit each, in ascending order of lead. Lead ran from 136 to 4,657 ppb, cadmium from 14.1 to 510 ppb, and mercury was below 5 ppb in every product. Arsenic was below 10 ppb in ten products and 52 ppb in one. The chart rounds to whole numbers. Only four of the eleven values have a laboratory report in this collection.

## Key numbers

The chart image (last page of the file) is dated 17 January 2026 and states that all measurements are in parts per billion, rounded to the nearest whole number. The product names are printed on the chart. This page uses the chart's column numbers. The zinc oxide percentages come from the article's product list.

Items 4, 5, 10 and 11 match the laboratory reports in [[sources/rubin2025d-13pct-zno-diaper-cream-lot-2025-218]] (1244.1 and 45.26 ppb), [[sources/rubin2025b-40pct-zno-diaper-paste-lot-2025-169]] (1401.0 and 509.5 ppb), [[sources/rubin2025a-40pct-zno-diaper-ointment-lot-2025-142]] (3303.8 and 55.50 ppb) and [[sources/rubin2025c-40pct-zno-diaper-paste-lot-2025-182]] (4657.1 and 473.1 ppb). Each chart value is the report value rounded to a whole number. The reports for items 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are linked from the article but were not in the collection.

The article states that "the contamination levels for the Zinc Oxide products vary pretty wildly (nor correlated to the percentage of Zinc)". A warning symbol marks items 4 to 11, which the legend says would exceed the Washington State Toxic-Free Cosmetics Act lead limit "IF the Toxic-Free Cosmetics Act (TFCA) standards were applied to diaper ointments".

## Methods (brief)

Each product was one unit analysed by ICP-MS at the laboratory named in the four linked reports. The chart gives no methods beyond that. Results are finished product as sold. Arsenic and mercury are totals. Mercury and arsenic non-detects are printed with the reporting values (<5 ppb and <10 ppb).

## Evidence fitness

Items 4, 5, 10 and 11 are supported by the reports they match. The other seven values are secondary chart figures. Here they are leads, not verified results. The article does not say whether items 2, 3, 7, 8 and 9 contain zinc oxide or at what percentage, so lead per unit of zinc oxide cannot be computed for them. No product without zinc oxide (petrolatum, dimethicone or lanolin based) appears on the chart, so it cannot test whether zinc oxide is the carrier of the lead.

## Limitations

The chart states whole-number rounding but prints cadmium 14.1 for item 2. A legend colour refers to a "2021 proposed action level" without naming the instrument. The page's dates are internally inconsistent (post header 18 January 2026, body 17 January 2026, and a funding-statistics line dated 17 January 2025). These inconsistencies do not affect the four values checked against reports. The selection of products reflects community nomination and funding, not sampling.
