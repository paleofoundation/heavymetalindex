# D'Anza et al. 2024 -- Colatura di Alici: heavy metals, PAHs, and histamine through fermentation

## Summary

D'Anza and colleagues monitored cadmium, lead, total mercury, four PAHs, and histamine across the full 390-day production cycle of Colatura di Alici di Cetara, an Italian PDO fermented anchovy sauce. Cd and Pb were below the respective limits of quantification (LOQ 0.005 and 0.010 mg/kg) in the raw salt and in all processed matrices. Hg was detectable in raw anchovies (0.072 mg/kg) and in anchovy muscle at 30 days but was not detectable in the final colatura (LOQ 0.010 mg/kg). PAHs were not detected at any processing stage. All values were well below EU Regulation 2023/915 maxima for the raw fish matrix. This is a single-batch process-characterization study; it is not a market survey of commercial colatura or fish sauce.

## Key numbers

Heavy metals measured by AAS electrothermal atomization (Cd, Pb; Perkin Elmer PinAAcle 900T) and direct mercury analyzer (Hg; Milestone DMA 80 Evo). LOQs: Cd 0.005 mg/kg, Pb 0.010 mg/kg, Hg 0.010 mg/kg. All samples run in duplicate; results corrected for mean recovery. Accredited per UNI EN ISO/IEC 17025:2018.

PAHs (benzo[a]pyrene, benzo[a]anthracene, benzo[b]fluoranthene, chrysene; HPLC-FLD; LOQ 0.2 µg/kg each): not detected at T0, T1, or T8 (final colatura) in either production protocol.

Histamine (UHPLC-DAD; LOQ 0.20 mg/kg): peak values in final colatura were 51.0 +/- 1.7 mg/kg (N1) and 49.7 +/- 2.1 mg/kg (N2), both well below the EU 400 mg/kg maximum for fermented fish sauces (Regulation 1019/2013).

EU regulatory limits cited (Regulation 2023/915): Pb 0.30 mg/kg (fish muscle), Cd 0.25 mg/kg (Engraulis anchovies), Hg 0.30 mg/kg (Engraulis anchovies). The authors note that Annex I does not set specific maxima for processed fish sauces; Article 3 concentration-change provisions apply to the final product.

## Evidence Fitness

Modeled or limited evidence. This is a process-characterization study of one production batch from a single geographic source, not a market survey. The heavy-metal results for the final colatura are all below LOQ, so they establish an upper bound rather than a measured central tendency. The study design is rigorous (accredited laboratory, duplicate measurements, recovery correction) but the single-batch scope limits generalizability across commercial colatura products or other fish sauce types.

## Methods (brief)

**Cd and Pb:** AAS electrothermal atomization, Perkin Elmer PinAAcle 900T, Zeeman effect background correction; acid digestion (0.75 g sample, 70% HNO3 + 30% H2O2, microwave, final volume 50 mL); matrix modifiers (NH4)H2PO4 and Mg(NO3)2; absorption wavelengths 247.6 nm (Pb) and 228.8 nm (Cd); argon inert gas; five-point calibration (R2 > 0.99); duplicates; spiked-sample recovery; z scores not exceeding |2| in proficiency testing.

**Hg:** Direct mercury analyzer, Milestone DMA 80 Evo; ~0.300 g sample; sublimation, gold amalgam adsorption, thermal desorption, detection at 253.65 nm; duplicates; LOQ 0.010 mg/kg.

**PAHs:** HPLC-FLD (Waters Alliance); saponification with 2 M ethanolic KOH; liquid extraction with cyclohexane; SPE C18 cleanup; five-point calibration; LOQ 0.2 µg/kg each.

Anchovies (Engraulis encrasicolus L., 9-12 cm) were caught by lampara fishing off the Amalfi-Positano coast in October 2020. Processing began immediately post-catch; average seasoning temperature 12.8 degrees C, relative humidity 76%. Fermentation vessels were traditional wooden terzigni.

Speciation note: only total Hg was measured; methylmercury fraction was not determined.

## Implications

**Certification:** The final colatura data show Cd, Pb, and Hg below LOQ, which is consistent with the EU regulatory picture but does not establish a population distribution for certification limit-setting. The raw-anchovy Hg value (0.072 mg/kg) is well below EU limits (0.30 mg/kg) for Engraulis. This paper is process-characterization evidence, not an occurrence dataset.

**Courses:** Useful illustration of how heavy metal levels change (or do not change) across a long fermentation process; the disappearance of Hg from raw fish to finished sauce supports discussion of potential volatilization or redistribution during fermentation.

**App:** No change to contamination_profile blocks; single-batch data with all final-product values below LOQ do not update central-tendency estimates.

**Microbiome:** No metal-microbiome data in this paper.
