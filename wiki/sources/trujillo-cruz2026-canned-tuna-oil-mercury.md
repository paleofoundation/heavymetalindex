# Total mercury exposure through canned tuna in oil sold in Quito, Ecuador

## Summary

This journal article measured total mercury in canned tuna in oil sold in Quito. The label is canned tuna, Hg only. The tissue basis is wet. Codes A, B, and C are used. n=20 each.

## Key numbers

Total mercury, mg/kg wet: code A 0.22±0.10, code B 0.63±0.10, code C 0.36±0.15. Lead, cadmium, arsenic, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap. Mercury is total mercury, not methylmercury.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
