# Morshdy et al. 2023 - canned meat and chicken metals in Egypt

## Overview

Morshdy and colleagues measured lead, cadmium, total arsenic, total mercury, aluminium, and tin in canned beef, canned corned beef, and canned chicken luncheon sold in Sharkia Governorate, Egypt. The study reports product-category concentration summaries on a `mg/kg wet weight (PPM)` basis for `n = 20` samples per category. It also calculates EDI, HQ, and HI values for Egyptian adult consumption assumptions; those exposure calculations are source-side context and are kept separate from the occurrence table.

## Key numbers

- Article identity: Food Research `7 (1)`, pages `151 - 157`, DOI `10.26656/fr.2017.7(1).710`; received `9 October 2021`, accepted `11 February 2022`, available online `3 February 2023`.
- Sample frame: `60` samples total, collected from grocery stores and hypermarkets in Sharkia Governorate, Egypt from `April` to `October 2020`; `20` samples each of canned beef, canned corned beef, and canned chicken luncheon.
- Detection limits (`µg/g`): Pb `0.01`, Cd `0.005`, As `0.02`, Hg `0.01`, Sn `0.02`, and Al `0.10`. The source says detected levels were expressed as `µg/g wet weight (µg/g WW)` while Table 1 reports `mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`.
- Table 1, canned beef (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): Pb positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.06`, maximum `0.58`, mean ± SE `0.35±0.04`; Cd positive `17 (85%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.10`, mean `0.073±0.006`; tAs positive `13 (65%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.42`, mean `0.24±0.01`.
- Table 1, canned beef (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): tHg positive `15 (75%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.09`, mean `0.075±0.003`; Al positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.13`, maximum `8.21`, mean `3.92±0.53`; Sn positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.9`, maximum `11.62`, mean `4.55±0.53`.
- Table 1, canned corned beef (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): Pb positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.08`, maximum `0.61`, mean `0.39±0.04`; Cd positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.03`, maximum `0.12`, mean `0.081±0.007`; tAs positive `17(85%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.48`, mean `0.28±0.02`.
- Table 1, canned corned beef (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): tHg positive `16 (80%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.11`, mean `0.086±0.003`; Al positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `0.15`, maximum `7.95`, mean `4.02±0.49`; Sn positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `1.18`, maximum `12.36`, mean `5.05±0.62`.
- Table 1, canned chicken luncheon (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): Pb positive `18 (90%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.45`, mean `0.21±0.02`; Cd positive `16 (80%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.11`, mean `0.071±0.007`; tAs positive `15 (75%)`, minimum `ND`, maximum `0.44`, mean `0.27±0.02`.
- Table 1, canned chicken luncheon (`mg/kg wet weight (PPM)`, `n = 20`): tHg positive `13 (65%)`, minimum `1.19`, maximum `11.62`, mean `4.51±0.55`; Al positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `2.5`, maximum `12.36`, mean `5.05±0.62`; Sn positive `20 (100%)`, minimum `1.14`, maximum `10.50`, mean `4.59±0.49`.
- Table 1 acceptability counts by the source's comparison limits: Pb `4 (20%)`, `3 (15%)`, and `7 (35%)`; Cd `8 (40%)`, `5 (25%)`, and `9 (45%)`; tAs `6 (30%)`, `5 (25%)`, and `9 (45%)`; tHg `5 (25%)`, `4(20%)`, and `7 (35%)`; Al `3 (15%)`, `2 (10%)`, and `4 (20%)`; Sn `20 (100%)` for all three product groups.
- Table 2 EDI/HQ/HI: canned beef Pb EDI `0.273`, HQ `0.068`; Cd `0.057`, `0.057`; tAs `0.195`, `0.648`; tHg `0.059`, `0.118`; Al `3.060`, `0.0031`; Sn `3.520`, `0.0058`; HI `0.900`.
- Table 2 EDI/HQ/HI: canned corned beef Pb EDI `0.311`, HQ `0.078`; Cd `0.063`, `0.063`; tAs `0.220`, `0.735`; tHg `0.067`, `0.135`; Al `3.144`, `0.0032`; Sn `3.940`, `0.0065`; HI `1.021`.
- Table 2 EDI/HQ/HI: canned chicken luncheon Pb EDI `0.148`, HQ `0.037`; Cd `0.049`, `0.049`; tAs `0.187`, `0.622`; tHg `0.047`, `0.093`; Al `2.574`, `0.0026`; Sn `3.130`, `0.0052`; HI `0.809`. Table 2 defines EDI as `µg/kg/day`.

## Methods (brief)

The authors collected `20` samples each of canned beef, canned corned beef, and canned chicken luncheon from Sharkia Governorate retail outlets and stored them at room temperature until extraction. One gram of each sample was digested with `5 mL` digestion solution containing `3 mL` nitric acid `65%` and `2` perchloric acids `70%`, held at room temperature for `12 hrs`, heated at `70 oC` for `3 hrs` with swirling every `30 mins`, diluted with `20 mL DDW`, filtered, and held for measurement. Pb, Cd, Al, and Sn were measured by graphite furnace atomic absorption spectroscopy; As and Hg were measured by hydride generation/cold vapour atomic absorption spectroscopy. DORM-3 fish protein reference material was used for quality assurance.

## Implications

Certification (HMTc): This is direct occurrence evidence for canned/processed beef and chicken products in the Egyptian retail market on the source's `mg/kg wet weight (PPM)` basis. Arsenic is total arsenic and mercury is total mercury; the page does not treat them as inorganic arsenic or methylmercury.

Courses: The source is useful for teaching product-category aggregation without brand attribution. It also shows why table/prose consistency checks matter: the PDF's Table 1 and narrative paragraphs disagree for some canned chicken luncheon Hg/Al values.

App: The study can support canned meat and poultry context cards for Egypt, including the source's product-level means, ranges, detection counts, and HI values.

- PDF text was extracted with `pdftotext -layout` to `/tmp/mfk_june8_584.txt`; Table 1 was also visually checked from a rendered PDF page image because the text layer was difficult to read. The article identity block, sample collection, preparation, analytical procedures, quality assurance, Table 1, Table 2, and conclusion were checked against this page.
- DOI `10.26656/fr.2017.7(1).710`, title text, raw handle `MFK_risks-assessment-of-toxic-metals-in-canned-meat`, and candidate cite-key `morshdy2023-canned-meat-chicken-metals` were searched before creation; no existing source page was found.
- Units and basis are copied from the source. Table 1 values are reported as `mg/kg wet weight (PPM)` and Table 2 EDI values as `µg/kg/day`; no conversion was performed.
- Speciation: arsenic is total arsenic (`tAs`), mercury is total mercury (`tHg`), and tin is total tin (`Sn`) as reported by element. The study does not measure inorganic arsenic, methylmercury, or organotin species.
- Source-internal inconsistency: Table 1 prints canned chicken luncheon tHg as minimum `1.19`, maximum `11.62`, mean `4.51±0.55`, and Al as minimum `2.5`, maximum `12.36`, mean `5.05±0.62`. The surrounding prose instead says Hg was `ND` to `0.08` with mean `0.068±0.003` and Al was `0.13` to `8.29` with mean `3.54±0.54`. This page preserves the visually checked Table 1 values and flags the inconsistency.
- Brand firewall: no product brand names are reported. Values are summarized only at product-category level.
- Frontmatter slugs were checked against `docs/gpt-collaboration/taxonomy-snapshot.md`. The taxonomy has no exact canned-beef, corned-beef, or chicken-luncheon product slug, so products route through `processed-meats`, `beef-product`, and `poultry-product`; exact product forms remain matrix descriptors and source text.
