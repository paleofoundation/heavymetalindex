# Thomas 2011 ,  Methanoarchaea and metalloid methylation

Methanosarcina mazei methylated antimony, and purified methyltransferase MtaA with cob(I)alamin methylated antimony in vitro among several metalloids.

## Key numbers

Methanosarcina mazei methylated antimony, and purified methyltransferase MtaA with cob(I)alamin methylated antimony in vitro among several metalloids.

## Methods (brief)

Enzyme and archaeal culture work.

## Evidence fitness

Supports in vitro methylation by MtaA. Does not support a dedicated antimony methylase in an infant gut.

## Limitations

Not a consumer-product study.
