# Edibility of cultivated green seaweed Ulva intestinalis from Monkhali Beach, Cox's Bazar coast of Bangladesh: bio-toxicity and heavy metal contents

## Summary

This journal article measured metals in cultivated Ulva intestinalis. n=3. The label is cultivated Ulva. The abstract lists six numbers against five element names. The table assigns 0.27 to zinc and 0.13 to lead. Methods also state mg/100 g dry weight. Both unit statements are kept and are not converted.

## Key numbers

Table, dry weight, mg/kg: chromium 0.10±0.02, nickel 0.44±0.03, lead 0.13±0.07, cadmium 0.00±0.00, arsenic 0.00±0.00. Mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Arsenic and chromium are not speciated.
