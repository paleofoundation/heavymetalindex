# Ishii et al. 2015. Phthalates in paper diapers

Satoko Ishii and colleagues estimate transdermal exposure of newborns to seven phthalates in paper diapers. Two authors list a diaper manufacturer affiliation. No elemental metal concentration was extracted. Product names are not ranked here.

## Key numbers

No metal result.

## Methods (brief)

Migration and exposure estimate for phthalates.

## Evidence fitness

The paper is context for diaper chemical exposure. It does not support a metal total.

## Limitations

Industry coauthors. Organic analytes only on this page.
