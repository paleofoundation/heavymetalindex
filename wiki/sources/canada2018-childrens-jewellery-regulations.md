This Government of Canada consolidation covers children's jewellery under the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act. It sets total lead and cadmium content requirements for children's jewellery, with the cadmium row applying when the jewellery item is small enough to fit wholly inside the small-parts cylinder under the specified force. Both limits are routeable as statutory ug/kg values.
