# FDA GRAS Notice 788: Manufacturer laboratory report for a pea protein batch

## Overview

This GRAS notice, submitted in 2018, includes a third-party laboratory report on one pea protein batch commissioned by the manufacturer. The Index withholds the manufacturer and product names.

## Key numbers

- Cadmium: 0.042 mg/kg (method detection limit 0.005) (Appendix II).
- Lead not detected (limit 0.05 mg/kg); total arsenic and mercury not detected (limit 0.01 mg/kg) (Appendix II).

## Methods (brief)

The laboratory used Chinese national standard methods GB 5009.12 (lead), 5009.11 (arsenic), 5009.17 (mercury) and 5009.15 (cadmium).

## Evidence Fitness

These are manufacturer test results submitted to FDA with a GRAS notice, not independent measurements. FDA published the notice but did not verify the values. A single batch. The notice also contains five scanned certificates of analysis that are not legible enough to transcribe. Inorganic arsenic was not measured.
