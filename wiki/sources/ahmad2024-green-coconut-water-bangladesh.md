# Ahmad et al. 2024, metals in green coconut water, Bangladesh

Ahmad and colleagues measured metals in green coconut water and in surface seawater. Only Table 2, green coconut water, is used. The table header is mg/L. The methods also say results are expressed as ppm. Both wordings are printed. Chromium is not speciated. Recovery was 88% to 99%.

## Key numbers

Table 2 printed Average row, mg/L: lead 0.008 ± 0.002, chromium 0.007 ± 0.005, nickel 0.003 ± 0.0005, cadmium 0.0015 ± 0.0002. District means are also printed and are not pooled again here. The authors' safe-limit row is a comparator, not a measurement.

## Methods (brief)

Coconut water was analyzed by atomic absorption methods described in the paper. Six districts. Seawater is a separate table.

## Evidence fitness

The Average row supports a green coconut water note. It does not support seawater as coconut water.

## Limitations

ppm and mg/L are both printed for the same table. Chromium is not hexavalent. Arsenic, mercury, aluminum, and tin are not in Table 2.
