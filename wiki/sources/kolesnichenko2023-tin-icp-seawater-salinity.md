# Kolesnichenko et al. 2023 - ICP-OES and ICP-MS methods for total tin in saline waters

## Overview

This analytical chemistry paper develops and compares ICP-OES and ICP-MS procedures using hydride generation (HG) for determination of total tin in Azov Sea and Black Sea waters, addressing matrix effects from high salinity. It is an environmental water chemistry methods paper, not a food occurrence study. The principal finding is that hydride generation substantially lowers detection limits and mitigates salt-matrix suppression relative to direct nebulization.

This source is off-topic for dietary exposure to tin from food. It is included in the evidence deposit for methodological context regarding speciation measurement.

## Key numbers

**Total Sn in environmental waters (method validation matrices):**

| Matrix | Method | Concentration |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Azov Sea water | HG-ICP-OES | 0.17 +/- 0.03 µg/L |
| Black Sea water | HG-ICP-OES | 0.24 +/- 0.05 µg/L |
| Azov Sea water | HG-ICP-MS | 0.19 +/- 0.02 µg/L |
| Black Sea water | HG-ICP-MS | 0.22 +/- 0.04 µg/L |

**Detection limits by method:**

| Method | LOD (µg/L) | LOQ (µg/L) |
|--------|------------|------------|
| HG-ICP-OES | 0.016 | 0.05 |
| HG-ICP-MS | 0.009 | 0.03 |
| Direct ICP-OES | 0.08 | 0.25 |
| Direct ICP-MS | 0.025 | 0.08 |

Russian Federation maximum permissible concentration for Sn in drinking water: 0.1 mg/L (100 µg/L); measured sea-water levels (0.17 to 0.24 µg/L) are approximately 500-fold below this.

## Evidence Fitness

Context only (EF-X). This paper provides no food occurrence data. Its relevance to this wiki is limited to the methodological point that hydride generation substantially lowers detection limits for total Sn in complex matrices relative to direct nebulization, and that speciation of inorganic vs organotin requires separate methods (covered in a planned companion paper by the same authors). This context is relevant to the testing methods section of the tin wiki page.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-OES (Optima 7300 DV, PerkinElmer) and ICP-MS with on-line HG using NaBH4 in HCl. Comparison of on-line HG vs direct nebulization; certified reference material (river water SLRS-6, NRC Canada). No food digestion protocols.

## Implications

Testing methods: HG-ICP-OES and HG-ICP-MS achieve substantially lower detection limits for Sn in complex aqueous matrices than direct nebulization, relevant for detecting trace Sn in drinking water and beverages. The ~3x LOD improvement from HG vs direct nebulization informs method selection for low-Sn matrices such as fresh foods and beverages.
