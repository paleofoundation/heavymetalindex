# Meng et al. 2024: Lanthanum-doped barium titanate breaks down an antibiotic in water

Barium titanate doped with lanthanum was used with ultrasound to break down the antibiotic sulfadiazine in water. The doped material degraded 89.06% of the antibiotic in 60 minutes and outperformed undoped material by about 15% on average.

## Key numbers

- Degradation 89.06% in 60 minutes; rate constant 0.0378 per minute (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Sol-gel-hydrothermal synthesis and piezocatalytic tests with ultrasound across concentration, dose, pH and background ion conditions.

## Limitations

Laboratory material or biomedical study; no environmental, food or human exposure measurements.
