# Zhu et al. 2018: Removing strontium interference when measuring rubidium by ICP-MS

This analytical chemistry paper developed an isotope-dilution tandem ICP-MS method for rubidium in which fluoromethane reaction gas removes the overlap of strontium-87 on rubidium-87. The method gave rubidium results consistent with certified values in food and environmental reference materials without prior chemical separation of strontium.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to strontium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Isotope dilution ICP-QMS/QMS with an octopole reaction cell; fluoromethane converts strontium to strontium fluoride ions while rubidium is largely unreactive; validated on multiple certified reference materials.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: it concerns strontium only as a spectral interference in rubidium analysis. It supports a method note for laboratories but no exposure or toxicity claim.

## Limitations

One of three authors is employed by the ICP-MS instrument manufacturer whose instrument was used; this is relevant to method promotion, not to strontium evidence.
