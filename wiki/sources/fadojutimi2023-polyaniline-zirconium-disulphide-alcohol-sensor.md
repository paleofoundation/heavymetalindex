# Fadojutimi et al. 2023: A polyaniline and zirconium disulphide alcohol vapor sensor

This study made a room-temperature gas sensor from polyaniline and exfoliated zirconium disulphide and tested it on methanol, ethanol and isopropanol vapors. It is a materials and sensor paper with no zirconium exposure data.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Liquid exfoliation of zirconium disulphide, chemical polymerization of polyaniline, composite preparation by sonication, and vapor response testing.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium disulphide in a gas sensor; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
