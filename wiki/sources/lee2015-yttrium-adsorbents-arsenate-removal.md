# Lee et al. 2015: Yttrium-based adsorbents for removing arsenate from water

The authors prepared basic yttrium carbonate, titanium-loaded yttrium carbonate and yttrium hydroxide and tested how well they remove arsenate from water. The titanium-loaded material had the highest capacity. Yttrium is the adsorbent, not a measured contaminant.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to yttrium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Co-precipitation synthesis, surface characterization and isotherm, kinetic, pH and competing-anion tests.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: an arsenic treatment materials paper that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for yttrium.

## Limitations

Yttrium release from the adsorbents into treated water was not reported. The work was funded by, and includes authors from, an electronics company research institute.
