# Mackiewicz et al. 2026: Multielement analysis of superfood seeds including hemp, Poland

## Overview

This study measured macro- and microelements and toxic elements in six types of superfood seeds and grains (amaranth, chia, flax, hemp, pumpkin, quinoa) purchased from Polish retail using ICP-OES. The hemp seed group (n = 5) allows direct comparison against other superfood seeds for toxic element content. All concentrations are reported as mg/L of prepared digest solution, which corresponds to mg/kg dry weight of the product given the stated digestion and dilution protocol.

The abstract states: "The presence of potentially toxic elements, including Al, Pb, and Cd, was also detected. Cadmium accumulation was of particular concern in flax seeds, where all samples exceeded the limit of quantification and approached permissible levels." The hemp seed group did not trigger the same concern as flax. PCA showed hemp and pumpkin seeds clustered together, distinct from chia and flax.

Note: hemp seed group toxic element values cannot be precisely extracted from this summary due to multi-column PDF table layout. The paper should be consulted directly for hemp-specific min/max/median values from Table 1.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-OES; microwave-assisted acid digestion; CRM not specified in text reviewed; certified and uncertified commercial samples from Polish retail; LOQ as stated per element; n = 5 hemp seed samples.

## Evidence fitness

This paper provides direct food-grade commercial hemp seed data with toxic element screening by ICP-OES from Polish retail samples (n = 5). The finding that hemp seeds did not raise the same cadmium concern as flax seeds is a useful comparative signal. The small n limits statistical power and the Polish retail scope may not represent global hemp seed supply. The PDF table layout prevented clean numeric extraction of hemp-specific values in this summary; source page should be verified against primary PDF for precise numbers.

## Limitations

n = 5 hemp seed samples. Table values could not be fully extracted by pdftotext due to multi-column layout. Polish retail only. ICP-OES method is suitable for elements at mg/kg level but less sensitive than ICP-MS for trace Cd and Pb at low ppb levels.
