# Accumulation of Heavy Metals by Wild Mushrooms in Ibadan, Nigeria

## Summary

This journal article measured lead, cadmium, and copper by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in wild mushrooms from Ibadan, Nigeria, on a dry-weight basis. The full species table is not restated. Copper is not a target metal.

## Key numbers

Dry weight: lead 40.00±3.56 to 76.00±9.78; cadmium 67.92±5.89 to 92.45±12.34. A copper pair printed as 56.00 and 56.60 is not a target metal and is not copied as a lead or cadmium result. Arsenic, mercury, nickel, aluminum, chromium, and tin: Data gap.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
