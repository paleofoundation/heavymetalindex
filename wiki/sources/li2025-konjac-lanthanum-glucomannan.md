# Li et al. 2025: Lanthanum and glucomannan accumulation in konjac

Konjac plants were treated with lanthanum chloride during early corm expansion, and corm glucomannan content and gene expression were analyzed. Lanthanum at 20 to 80 mg/L increased glucomannan, and transcriptome analysis identified carbohydrate and hormone-related genes linked to the effect. Lanthanum in the corms, the edible part, was not measured.

## Key numbers

- Lanthanum at 20 to 80 mg/L significantly increased konjac glucomannan compared with controls (abstract).
- 21,047 differentially expressed genes were identified (abstract).

## Methods (brief)

Single lanthanum chloride treatment, corm sampling at 7, 14 and 60 days, RNA sequencing and qRT-PCR validation with three biological replicates.

## Evidence fitness

Supports statements that lanthanum is explored as a yield or quality input for a food crop. It does not quantify lanthanum residues in konjac.

## Limitations

No lanthanum residue data; mechanism inferred from expression correlations.
