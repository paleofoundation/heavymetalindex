# Li et al. 2018 : Gold nanoparticle lateral flow strip for polymyxin B in milk

## Overview

Li and colleagues developed a competitive immunochromatographic lateral flow test strip using gold nanoparticle-labeled monoclonal antibodies for the detection of polymyxin B (PMB) residues in milk and animal feed. Gold nanoparticles are the signal label; polymyxin B is the target analyte. PMB is a last-resort lipopeptide antibiotic with no established maximum residue limit (MRL) in food-producing animals in major jurisdictions, used as a feed additive in some countries. This rapid strip test enables on-site residue screening in 15 minutes without laboratory equipment.

## Key numbers

Visual LOD in milk: 25 ng/mL. Cutoff limit in milk: 100 ng/mL. Visual LOD in animal feed: 500 µg/kg. Cutoff limit in feed: 1000 µg/kg. Test time: 15 minutes.
