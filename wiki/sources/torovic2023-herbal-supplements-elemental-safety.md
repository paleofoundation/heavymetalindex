# Torovic et al. 2023: Elemental profiling of herbal food supplements, Serbia

Fifty-two liquid herbal food supplements from Serbian retail were analyzed for 25 elements. Tellurium was detected in 28.8% of samples, with a maximum concentration of 10.0 µg/kg dry weight. No regulatory Permitted Daily Exposure exists for tellurium, and the paper did not compute a hazard quotient for the element.

## Key numbers

- Te detected in 15/52 samples (28.8%); mean plus or minus SD: 1.2 plus or minus 2.3 µg/kg dry weight; maximum 10.0 µg/kg dw (Table 1, p. 7).
- In all paediatric-labeled supplements (35 samples): 8/35 detected (22.9%), mean 1.0 plus or minus 2.4 µg/kg, maximum 10.0 µg/kg dw.
- In paediatric-only supplements (6 samples): 2/6 detected (33.3%), mean 2.0 plus or minus 3.5 µg/kg, maximum 8.7 µg/kg dw.
- In adult-only supplements (17 samples): 7/17 detected (41.2%), mean 1.6 plus or minus 2.3 µg/kg, maximum 7.3 µg/kg dw.
- Non-detected results substituted with the LOQ (upper bound approach); LOQ for Te not separately stated in the text (p. 5).

## Methods (brief)

ICP-MS (Agilent 7700x); acid microwave digestion (0.5 g sample with 3 mL 67 to 69% HNO3 and 0.5 mL 30% H2O2; Ethos Easy SK-15; 1400 W, 210 degrees C, 40 bar, 20 min). Hazard Index calculations performed for a subset of elements with established ICH Permitted Daily Exposure values; tellurium was not included in the HI calculations because no PDE exists.

## Evidence fitness

Supports quantification of tellurium occurrence in liquid herbal food supplements purchased in Serbia. The detection frequency (~29%) and concentration range (up to 10 µg/kg dw) are relevant for supplement safety assessment. Does not support dietary intake estimation (no consumption data) or risk characterization (no reference dose for tellurium).

## Limitations

Serbian retail products only; no information on product botanical species; LOQ for Te not separately tabulated; non-detected values handled by LOQ substitution (upper-bound approach), which inflates mean estimates; no consumer-exposure information.
