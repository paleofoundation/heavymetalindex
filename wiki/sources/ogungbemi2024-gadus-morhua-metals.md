# Evaluation of Heavy Metals in Gadus morhua

## Summary

Ogungbemi, Adedokun, and Onyeka-Ubaka measured metals by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in dried Gadus morhua stockfish from Lagos markets. Five samples were collected from each of nine markets. Table 2 prints eight rows, SP1 to SP8. The table unit is mg/kg.

## Key numbers

Table 2 Mean row, mg/kg: cadmium 0.110, chromium 0.217, lead 0.716, nickel 0.574, arsenic 0.284. The abstract lists arsenic then nickel for 0.574 and 0.284 mg/kg. The table assigns 0.574 to nickel and 0.284 to arsenic. Mercury, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Arsenic is not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
