# Zaimbashi et al. 2026: A zirconium metal-organic framework electrode for vanillin and folic acid

This study modified screen-printed graphite electrodes with a zirconium metal-organic framework (UiO-66) to detect vanillin and folic acid in tablets, coffee milk and urine. The analytes are vanillin and folic acid; zirconium was not measured in any sample.

## Key numbers

- This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to zirconium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Solvothermal framework synthesis, electrode modification, cyclic and differential pulse voltammetry, and recovery tests in real samples.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure. The paper uses zirconium as an electrode-modifying material for sensing other compounds; it does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for zirconium.

## Limitations

No zirconium was measured in food, water, soil or human samples, so nothing in the paper describes exposure.
