# Fischer, Menné, and Johansen 2005. Occluded nickel elicitation thresholds

Louise Arup Fischer, Torkil Menné, and Jeanne Duus Johansen review occluded nickel dose-response studies and fit a summary threshold. Eight studies were selected on statistical grounds. The DOI is the record that matches this title.

## Key numbers

The authors' analysis gives 0.44 mg Ni/cm2 as the dose to which 5% of a sensitized population react, and 1.04 mg Ni/cm2 as the dose to which 10% react, under occluded exposure. A comparison with open application is printed, but the dose multiplier in that sentence is not cleanly readable and is not recorded.

## Methods (brief)

Statistical summary of published occluded patch studies. Not a new survey of products.

## Evidence fitness

The two doses are the review's own summary statistics. Individual study doses are not re-listed.

## Limitations

Occluded exposure only. The open-application comparison is omitted because the dose multiplier in that sentence is not readable in this copy.
