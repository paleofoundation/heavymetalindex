# Estimated dietary intake of trace metals from swordfish consumption: a human health problem

## Summary

This journal article measured mercury, lead, cadmium, nickel, and chromium by atomic absorption spectrophotometry in Mediterranean swordfish. n=30. Wet weight, µg/g. Arsenic is not reported.

## Key numbers

Mercury 0.77±0.48, cadmium 0.16±0.05, lead 0.11±0.07, nickel 0.52±0.33, chromium 0.12±0.05 µg/g wet weight. Aluminum and tin: Data gap. Mercury and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
