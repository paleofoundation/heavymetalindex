# Kosemund et al. 2009. Safety evaluation of superabsorbent baby diapers

Kosemund and colleagues review the safety assessment used for superabsorbent disposable baby diapers. The article is a manufacturer safety overview published in 2009 (the journal notes online publication in 2008). The text discusses exposure scenarios, a threshold of toxicological concern, and the superabsorbent polymer. It does not report a concentration of lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, chromium, tin, or antimony.

## Key numbers

No metal concentration is printed. Words that match "lead" in the text are the verb, not the element. No metal row was entered in the evidence register.

## Methods (brief)

The paper describes a safety-assessment framework for diaper materials and for foreseeable misuse, including ingestion of granules. It is not an analytical metals method.

## Evidence fitness

The article can document that this safety overview did not supply metal occurrence data. It cannot support a metal level in a diaper, a wipe, or a cream.

## Limitations

Absence of a metal result in a safety narrative is not evidence that metals were absent. Employer names are not repeated here.
