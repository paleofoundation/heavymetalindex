# Genchi et al. 2026 - Heavy metal toxicity mechanisms and clinical management review

## Summary

Genchi et al. provide a comprehensive review of the sources, mechanisms, diagnostics, and evidence-based management of mercury, lead, cadmium, and arsenic toxicity. For mercury, the review covers elemental mercury vapor, inorganic mercury, and methylmercury with separate mechanistic discussions. This paper serves as a toxicology reference source for the mercury-total page, providing mechanistic context for why mercury speciation matters in health interpretation and what distinguishes the toxicity profiles of different mercury forms.
