# Udiba et al. 2020 - Senna obtusifolia lead remediation in Zamfara

Udiba and colleagues evaluated whether wild Senna obtusifolia can phytoextract lead from contaminated soils in Dareta Village. This is in-scope lane a2 remediation evidence with direct plant-tissue and soil lead measurements.

## Key numbers

The paper reports mean soil Pb concentrations across the five plots from `130.68 +/- 5.2` to `396.86 +/- 5.48 mg/kg`.

Leaf Pb concentrations ranged from `173.39 +/- 13.73` to `294.28 +/- 4.38 mg/kg`, while root concentrations ranged from `61.33 +/- 17.86` to `91.64 +/- 2.87 mg/kg`.

Average translocation factor, bioaccumulation factor, and bioconcentration factor are reported as `3.65 +/- 0.66`, `1.01 +/- 0.23`, and `0.29 +/- 0.10`, respectively.

## Methods (brief)

Soil and plant tissues were collected from established plots in Dareta Village and analyzed for lead by atomic absorption spectrophotometry after wet digestion. The paper uses translocation, bioaccumulation, and bioconcentration factors to assess phytoextraction potential.
