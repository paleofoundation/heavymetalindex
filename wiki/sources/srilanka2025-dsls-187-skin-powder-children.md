This public-comment Draft Sri Lanka Standard specifies requirements for skin powder for children. The draft introduces talc-base and starch-base product types and Table 1 sets maximum limits for lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury. The methods section references SLS ISO 21392 for lead, arsenic, and cadmium and SLS ISO 23674 for mercury. Because this is a draft standard, the structured rows are marked as draft guidance rather than implemented law.
