# Schulze et al. 2023: Chemical-genomic profiling of indium, aluminium, and gallium toxicity in yeast

This chemical-genomic study used the S. cerevisiae homozygous diploid deletion collection (~4308 strains) to identify genes required for growth in the presence of aluminium (Al), gallium (Ga), and indium (In) at acidic pH (4.3).

## Key numbers

- Nominal metal concentrations tested: 2.4 mM Al2(SO4)3, 5.0 mM GaCl3, and 2.6 mM InCl3 (Methods; higher concentration for each metal selected for the main screen).
- Indium chloride (InCl3) purity: 98% (Sigma-Aldrich) (p.2).
- All three metals precipitate in yeast culture medium at pH 4.3 as metal-phosphate species; dissolved concentrations are substantially lower than nominal (p.3 to 4).
- Resistant mutants identified: 171 (Al), 133 (Ga), 248 (In) based on single-gene knockout growth (p.4 to 5).
- Shared gene sets: 26% overlap (Al and Ga), 25% overlap (Ga and In), 34% overlap (Al and In); 45 genes protected against all three metals (p.5).
- Indium-specific protective functions: chorismate metabolic processes (p.5).
- Shared protective functions include vesicle-mediated transport, protein folding, and phospholipid metabolism (p.5).

## Methods (brief)

Chemical-genomic screen of S. cerevisiae deletion collection using Scan-o-Matic high-throughput colony imaging. Validation by single-gene deletion assays. GO enrichment and STRING protein-protein interaction network analyses. Metal speciation modeled with JESS software.

## Evidence fitness

Mechanistic study establishing cellular defense pathways against indium at the gene level in a model eukaryote. Human orthologues of identified yeast genes have known roles in disease processes, suggesting similar protective mechanisms may exist in human cells. Provides mechanistic context for why indium is toxic despite low bioavailability at neutral pH. No dietary relevance.

## Limitations

Yeast model; human translation is speculative. Metal speciation in cell culture is complex and nominal vs. dissolved concentrations differ substantially. Acidic pH condition (4.3) selected to increase metal solubility is not physiological.
