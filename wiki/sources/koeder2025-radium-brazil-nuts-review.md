# Koeder and Keller 2025 : Radium levels in Brazil nuts, a review of the literature

## Summary

Systematic literature review of published radium (Ra-226 and Ra-228) activity data for Brazil nuts, with no language or time restriction. Twenty-one samples from 16 publications identified, plus original German unpublished data from 21 branded nuts. Conversion factors applied to estimate effective radiation dose from daily Brazil nut consumption.

## Key numbers

**Ra-226 (from Table 1, p. 3 and text, p. 3-4):**

- Original German data (n = 21 nuts, various brands of Bolivian origin): mean Ra-226 = 40 mBq/g (range 29 to 70 mBq/g)
- Literature excluding German data: mean Ra-226 = 49 mBq/g (range 17 to 205 mBq/g); median 34 mBq/g; n = 23 values
- Including German data: mean Ra-226 = 49 mBq/g; median 34 mBq/g; n = 24
- Studies from USA showed lower mean: 27 mBq/g (range 17 to 34 mBq/g; median 30 mBq/g; n = 7)
- Studies from Brazil: mean 50 mBq/g (range 24 to 105 mBq/g)

**Ra-228 (from text p. 4):**

- Literature mean: 68 mBq/g (range 12 to 235 mBq/g); n = 17
- Including German data: mean 43 mBq/g; median 43 mBq/g; n = 24
- German data: mean Ra-228 = 43 mBq/g

**Effective dose estimates from daily consumption of 1 Brazil nut (4 to 5 g) (Table 2, p. 5):**

| Source region of nuts | Effective dose (uSv/year) |
| --- | --- |
| Brazil | 117 to 146 |
| Germany | 60 to 75 |
| Europe | 57 to 72 |
| USA | 35 to 44 |
| All studies | 88 to 110 |

For comparison, Koeder and Keller estimate that long-term consumption of 1 nut per day results in approximately 100 uSv/year effective dose, consistent with Barkleit et al. 2025 bioaccessibility-adjusted estimate. The maximum permissible dose for the general public from additional sources is 1,000 uSv/year.

## Geographic and biological drivers

High barium levels in soil and efficient barium transport to Brazil nut tree fruits are proposed as the primary driver of high radium levels. Radium and barium share geochemical behavior (both alkaline earth metals). Nuts from Bolivian vs. Brazilian origin show different activity levels, and Amazon-region nuts (Amazonas state, Brazil) showed the highest reported value at 105 mBq/g Ra-226.

## Methods (brief)

Literature search on PubMed, Web of Science, Chemical Abstracts, and Semantic Scholar with no language or time restriction, completed 31 January 2024. Search terms: "Brazil nut" OR "Bertholletia" AND "radium" OR "OR radioactivity." Screening by two reviewers; 16 publications identified reporting Ra-226 or Ra-228. Original German data added as a 17th entry. Sensitivity analyses used publication-level means excluding subgroup-level means.

## Implications

- Ingredient page: provides the most comprehensive published estimate of Ra-226 and Ra-228 activity in Brazil nuts; mean Ra-226 approximately 49 mBq/g and mean Ra-228 approximately 43 to 68 mBq/g depending on dataset.
- Radiation dose from 1 nut/day is estimated at approximately 88 to 110 uSv/year from all studies combined, well below the 1,000 uSv/year general public limit but non-negligible at higher consumption.
- Pairs with Barkleit et al. 2025 bioaccessibility data, which showed approximately 2 percent Ra bioaccessibility from defatted BNF.
