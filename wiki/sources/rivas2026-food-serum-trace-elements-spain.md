# Rivas et al. 2026 : Food group intake and serum trace elements in 465 Galician adults, Spain

This 2026 cross-sectional biomonitoring study measured 14 trace elements in serum from 465 healthy Galician adults and modelled associations with 13 dietary food groups from a validated semi-quantitative FFQ. For arsenic, seafood was the dominant predictor (p = 0.000 across seafood consumption tertiles). The study also found positive associations of serum arsenic with fruit and wine consumption and inverse associations with grains and dairy.

## Key numbers

Serum tAs (all participants, n=465):
- Overall median 1.825 µg/L (IQR: 0.946-3.593)
- Seafood consumption tertile association: p = 0.000 (highest tertile associated with highest serum As)
- Grains: p = 0.019 (inverse association with serum As)
- Nuts: p = 0.023 (positive association)

Random Forest model: seafood was a top-3 predictor for 13/14 elements including arsenic; for arsenic specifically, seafood was the dominant predictor.

Other toxic elements in serum (median values):
- Pb: not extracted from table; Cd and Hg also measured
- 14 total trace elements including Co, Cu, Fe, I, Mn, Mo, Se, Zn (essential) and Cd, Cr, Hg, Ni, Pb (toxic)

## Methods

ICP-MS (Agilent 7700); fasting venous blood in trace-element-free serum tubes; triplicate analysis; Kruskal-Wallis test; Random Forest classification models; logistic regression for selenium as binary outcome; LODs from 3 SD of ten replicate blank measurements.

## Implications

Provides Spanish population-level serum tAs data with seafood as the principal dietary predictor, consistent with the well-established organic arsenic (arsenobetaine) seafood association. The inverse association with grain intake is relevant to the arsenic-total page because it suggests that in a Mediterranean population with low rice consumption, grains do not contribute meaningfully to serum arsenic. The study supports the biomonitoring context section of the arsenic-total page and complements the Italian SPES cohort (Coppola 2026) with a Galician dataset.
