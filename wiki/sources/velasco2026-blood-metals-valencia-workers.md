# Velasco et al. 2026 - Blood metal levels in Valencia Region adult workers

## Summary

Velasco et al. measured blood concentrations of multiple metals (including total mercury, lead, cadmium, and arsenic) in an adult working population in Valencia Region, Spain. This is a biomonitoring study assessing current exposure levels and conducting health risk assessment. It provides population-level blood mercury reference data for a European adult working population, which contextualizes food-exposure estimates and regulatory reference values.
