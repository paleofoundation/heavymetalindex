# Nayak et al. 2015: Salivary chromium during fixed orthodontic treatment

Saliva from 30 orthodontic patients in Bengaluru was analyzed by ICP-MS before treatment, after initial alignment and after 10 to 12 months. Salivary chromium rose after alignment and remained above baseline at 10 to 12 months.

## Key numbers

- Salivary chromium (ppb, mean ± SD, n = 30): pretreatment 69.15 ± 60.48; after aligning wires 102.68 ± 68.12; after 10 to 12 months 87.67 ± 63.47 (Table 2, p. 16).

## Methods (brief)

Unstimulated saliva (5 mL) collected at three time points and analyzed by ICP-MS; repeated-measures comparison by ANOVA.

## Evidence fitness

Supports a statement that fixed orthodontic appliances are a measurable intraoral source of chromium in saliva. It does not convert salivary levels into swallowed dose or health risk.

## Limitations

Small single-center sample; no detection limit or reference material reported; large between-person variation; no control group without appliances.
