# Saeedi et al. 2025: SiO2@ZIF-8@Fe3O4 dispersive solid-phase extraction for Cd, Zn, Cu in fruit juices

## Overview

Iranian analytical chemistry study developing a dispersive solid-phase extraction method using core-shell SiO2@ZIF-8@Fe3O4 nanoparticles for simultaneous determination of cadmium, zinc, and copper in fruit juices by flame atomic absorption spectrometry (FAAS). Seven commercial fruit juices from Azerbaijani producers were used for real-sample validation.

## Key numbers from real samples (Table 4)

Unspiked "found" concentrations in commercial fruit juices (µg/kg, wet-weight basis; FAAS detection; LODs: Cd 0.4 µg/L, Cu 0.3 µg/L, Zn 0.2 µg/L):

| Sample | Cd (µg/L) | Cu (µg/L) | Zn (µg/L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cherry juice | ND | 15.1 | 48.2 |
| Mango juice | 6.2 | 54.5 | ND |
| Pineapple juice | 6.5 | 54.0 | ND |
| Grapefruit juice | 9.2 | 96.5 | ND |
| Pomegranate juice | ND | 47.8 | ND |
| Kiwi juice | 4.2 | 22.1 | ND |
| Apple juice | ND | 47.5 | ND |

ND = not determined or below detection limit. Values are from the "found" column in Table 4.

## Scope and use

This is a method development paper. The seven real-sample results are reported as method-validation samples, not as a population survey. The cadmium results (up to 9.2 µg/L in grapefruit juice) from commercially available Iranian fruit juices are included here as method-validation sample data, not as market-representative occurrence data. These values are cited as method-validation samples on the infant-juice product page.
