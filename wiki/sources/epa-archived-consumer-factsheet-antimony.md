# EPA archived consumer fact sheet on antimony

This archived EPA consumer fact sheet restates the drinking-water goal and the enforceable maximum for antimony in parts per billion. The document is on the EPA archive site. Its long-term health paragraph is an unfilled template. That paragraph is not quoted on this page.

## Key numbers

The text prints that the MCLG for antimony has been set at 6 parts per billion (ppb), and that the MCL has also been set at 6 ppb (PDF p.2). A summary box prints MCLG: 6 ppb and MCL: 6 ppb (PDF p.3). Monitoring language says the supplier was to determine whether antimony is present above 6 ppb. Approved removal methods printed here are coagulation and filtration, and reverse osmosis. The regulation is described as having become effective in 1994.

The short-term health sentence is filled and names nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea above the MCL for relatively short periods. The long-term health paragraph stops in an unfilled template. It is not transcribed.

## Methods (brief)

The file is the archived consumer fact sheet PDF. It points readers to the National Primary Drinking Water Regulations. It is not the CFR text.

## Evidence fitness

The page supports the archived sheet's own statement of 6 ppb for both the goal and the maximum, which matches 0.006 mg/L in the CFR sections opened with it. It does not support quotation of the unfilled long-term health paragraph. It does not support a diaper limit or a textile limit.

## Limitations

The sheet is archived and was not updated to the 2025 CFR edition. Release totals and production figures printed on the sheet are not drinking-water limits and are not adopted here as occurrence data.
