# Lin et al. 2022: Plasma metals in mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer's disease

This exploratory study measured trace metals in the plasma of 40 adults with normal cognition, amnestic mild cognitive impairment or Alzheimer's disease. Plasma gallium did not differ significantly between groups.

## Key numbers

- Plasma gallium, median (IQR): controls 0.071 (0.047, 0.095) µg/L; amnestic MCI 0.059 (0.024, 0.071) µg/L; Alzheimer's disease 0.036 (0.035, 0.036) µg/L; no group difference was significant (Table 2).
- Gallium was not significantly associated with annual change in MMSE score (Table 3).

## Methods (brief)

Plasma metals were measured by ICP-MS; participants were followed for cognitive change.

## Evidence fitness

Supports a reference range for gallium in human plasma from a small sample. Does not support any link between gallium and cognition.

## Limitations

Very small exploratory sample from one center.
