# Bankoğlu Yola et al. 2025: A yttrium oxide composite sensor for glyphosate in water

The authors built a molecularly imprinted electrochemical sensor from a palladium, yttrium oxide and boron nitride composite to detect the herbicide glyphosate in drinking water. Yttrium oxide is part of the electrode; the analyte is glyphosate.

## Key numbers

This paper reports no numerical findings relevant to yttrium exposure.

## Methods (brief)

Composite synthesis by sonication and borohydride reduction, electropolymerization of an imprinted polypyrrole film, and voltammetric calibration and recovery tests.

## Evidence fitness

Off-topic for dietary exposure: a herbicide sensor paper that does not support dietary exposure or toxicity claims for yttrium.

## Limitations

No yttrium measurements in water or food.
