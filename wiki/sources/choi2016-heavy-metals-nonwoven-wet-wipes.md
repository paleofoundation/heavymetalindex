# Choi 2016 ,  Heavy metals in wet-wipe nonwovens

The methods print an approximate sample mass and an exact dilution volume. Table 5 and Table 6 print antimony in the digest, in µg/L. The authors do not print milligrams per kilogram.

## Key numbers

Approximately 0.2 g was digested in 7 mL of 70 percent nitric acid and 1 mL of 50 percent hydrofluoric acid, then diluted with 25 mL of ASTM type 1 water. Table 5 (p.6) prints 12 of 24 wet-wipe nonwovens from 187.86 ± 5.24 to 19,558.35 ± 3,537.3 µg/L. The results prose writes the high end as 19,558 ± 3,537.30 µg/L. Table 6 (p.7) prints combined fractions including 8,935.52 ± 55.22 µg/L, while the abstract states 8,936 ± 55.22 µg/L. An approximate sheet conversion used on the metal page is mg/kg ≈ (µg/L × 0.025 L / 0.0002 kg) / 1000. That conversion is not in the paper.

## Methods (brief)

ICP-MS of acid digests. LOQ for antimony is printed as 0.03 µg/L. Sample codes are not repeated here.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the µg/L digest results and the statement that the mass is approximate. Does not support treating the derived mg/kg figures as author-reported.

## Limitations

Brand names and sample codes are omitted. The abstract and Table 6 do not print the same mask-pack pair.
