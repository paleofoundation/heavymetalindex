# MERCOSUR Project Resolution 07/23 status record

## Evidence Fitness

The official SGT No. 3 meeting record is fit to establish the project number, title, intended repeal relationship, and consultation status as of August 2025. It is not an adopted regional regulation and the public meeting record is not used to load proposed numerical values.

## Verified status

Project Resolution 07/23 is titled as a MERCOSUR technical regulation on maximum limits for metallic and metalloid contaminants in food and would repeal Resolutions GMC 12/11 and 18/21 if adopted. The 2025 record states that Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, and Uruguay remained in internal consultation.

## Routing

The project is routed to [[regulations/mercosur-project-07-23-metal-contaminants]] as non-binding consultation context. Existing binding rows under [[regulations/mercosur-gmc-res-12-2011-inorganic-contaminants]] remain active unless and until a final instrument changes their status.
