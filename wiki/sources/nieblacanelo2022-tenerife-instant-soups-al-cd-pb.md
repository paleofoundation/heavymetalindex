# Niebla-Canelo et al. 2022: Aluminum, cadmium and lead in instant soups, Canary Islands

## Overview

Niebla-Canelo and colleagues measured aluminum, cadmium and lead by ICP-OES in 130 dehydrated instant soups (poultry, meat and vegetable types) bought in Tenerife, and estimated intakes from a 20 g dry portion. Vegetable soups had the highest levels of all three metals. Concentrations refer to the dry product as sold, not the reconstituted soup.

## Key numbers

All concentrations are mg/kg dry weight of the dehydrated powder as sold; n=130 (61 poultry, 17 meat, 52 vegetable). Values from Table 3.

Aluminum (Al), mg/kg:
- Vegetable: mean 32.28 (SD 19.26), max 64.69, min 0.70, median 35.83
- Poultry: mean 23.53 (SD 15.21), max 61.79, min 5.11, median 18.45
- Meat: mean 14.93 (SD 10.79), max 53.20, min 5.28, median 13.10

Cadmium (Cd), mg/kg:
- Vegetable: mean 0.027 (SD 0.016), max 0.069, min 0.0087, median 0.024
- Poultry: mean 0.019 (SD 0.009), max 0.045, min 0.015, median 0.016
- Meat: mean 0.019 (SD 0.0069), max 0.030, min 0.010, median 0.018

Lead (Pb), mg/kg:
- Vegetable: mean 0.12 (SD 0.13), max 0.85, min 0.017, median 0.086
- Poultry: mean 0.094 (SD 0.16), max 0.86, min 0.015, median 0.045
- Meat: mean 0.058 (SD 0.069), max 0.32, min 0.025, median 0.043

Statistically significant differences (Kruskal-Wallis plus Mann-Whitney, p less than 0.05) for Al: all three pair comparisons significant. For Pb: all three pair comparisons significant. For Cd: vegetable vs poultry (p=0.0179), vegetable vs meat (p=0.0167); meat vs poultry not significant (p=0.5959) (Table 3 and Section 3.1).

Dietary intake estimates from Tables 6 to 8, using 20 g dry portion:
- Al EDI: poultry 0.47 mg/day, meat 0.30 mg/day, vegetable 0.65 mg/day; contribution to EFSA TWI (1 mg/kg BW/week): vegetable soups 14.3% to 19.6% for children 3 to 10 years (23.1 kg BW), 4.3% to 6.4% for adults; vegetable soups in children 10 to 14 years reached 10.4% of TWI (43.4 kg BW).
- Cd EDI: poultry 0.38 µg/day, meat 0.39 µg/day, vegetable 0.54 µg/day; highest contribution to EFSA TWI (2.5 µg/kg BW/week): vegetable soups 6.59% for children 3 to 10 years; always below 10% for all soup types and all age groups.
- Pb EDI: poultry 1.9 µg/day, meat 1.2 µg/day, vegetable 2.4 µg/day; against EFSA BMDL nephrotoxic (0.63 µg/kg BW/week): vegetable soups 16.28% for children 3 to 10 years; against EFSA BMDL cardiovascular (1.5 µg/kg BW/week): vegetable soups 6.84% for children 3 to 10 years.

Recovery: Al 109.3% (CRM: SRM 1515 Apple Leaves), Cd 92.3% (CRM: SRM 1573a Tomato Leaves), Pb 94.8% (CRM: SRM 1515 Apple Leaves) (Table 4).
Instrumental detection limits (mg/L): Al 0.005, Cd 0.0007, Pb 0.0009 (Table 2).
Instrumental quantification limits (mg/L): Al 0.015, Cd 0.002, Pb 0.003 (Table 2).

## Methods (brief)

Samples were oven-dried, ashed at 450 °C, dissolved in dilute nitric acid and read by ICP-OES. Accuracy was checked with SRM 1515 apple leaves and SRM 1573a tomato leaves (recoveries 92.3 to 109.3%); detection limits are instrumental, in mg/L.

## Evidence fitness

A large retail sample (130 soups) with reference material checks and full summary statistics per soup type, but no collection dates and no per-brand data. Values are for the dry powder; a serving is diluted in about 250 mL of water. The soups are instant powders by broad type (poultry, meat, vegetable), so for the specific thin soups this is a proxy for broccoli-cheese-soup, chicken-noodle-soup, cream-of-potato-soup, ramen-soup, tomato-soup, vegetable-beef-soup and brown-gravy, and those pages are not linked.

## Limitations

Data cover only dehydrated instant soup powder as sold; canned soups, ready-to-drink carton broths, bouillon cubes, and reconstituted soups are not represented. The sample is from a single retail setting in Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain, limiting generalizability to other markets or production regions. Collection dates are not reported. Only aluminum, cadmium, and lead were measured; inorganic arsenic, total mercury, total arsenic, chromium, nickel, and tin were not assayed. Dietary intake estimates used the dry product only and excluded aluminum, cadmium, and lead contributed by the cooking water. The sample size for meat-type soups (n=17) is small relative to poultry (n=61) and vegetable (n=52) types. Per-brand data are not reported.
