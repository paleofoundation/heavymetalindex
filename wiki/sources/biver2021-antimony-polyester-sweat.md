# Biver 2021 ,  Antimony release from polyester into artificial sweat

Six polyester textiles bought in the European Union. Totals and ISO 105-E04 extracts are both printed. The abstract summary does not cover every extract.

## Key numbers

XRF totals 125, 185, 286, 357, 452, and 471 µg/g (PDF p.2-3). The abstract rounds the top to about 470 µg/g. Three 24-hour extractions at 37 °C printed 2.572 ± 0.031, 0.904 ± 0.121, 0.480 ± 0.126, 2.477 ± 0.093, 0.402 ± 0.128, and 0.689 ± 0.154 µg/g. The abstract's 0.1 to 1 µg/g summary does not cover the two extracts above 2 µg/g. The paper cites an EU Ecolabel fibre limit of 260 µg/g and an extractable clothing limit below 30 µg/g. Those instruments were not opened.

## Methods (brief)

ISO 105-E04 artificial sweat. Release into a simulant, not absorption.

## Evidence fitness

Supports the table of totals and extracts. Does not support reading the extract as a dermal dose or adopting the cited label limits as opened instruments.

## Limitations

Retailer and product names are not repeated. The articles are not identified as infant sleepwear.
