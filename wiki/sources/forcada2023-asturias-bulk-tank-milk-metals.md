# Impact of Potentially Toxic Compounds in Cow Milk: How Industrial Activities Affect Animal Primary Productions

## Summary

This journal article measured lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, and chromium by ICP-MS in farm bulk tank cow milk in Asturias, Spain. The label is farm bulk tank milk near industry. n=16 farms. Values used here are wet weight, µg/kg. The near group is farms under 5 km (n=10).

## Key numbers

Near-group maxima: chromium 74.1, arsenic 16.01 (the abstract prints 16.1), cadmium 0.12, mercury 0.28, lead 2.7 µg/kg. Nickel, aluminum, and tin: Data gap. Arsenic, mercury, and chromium are not speciated.

## What this record does not support

These figures are the printed study results. They are not a population estimate, not a certification limit, and not a brand ranking.
