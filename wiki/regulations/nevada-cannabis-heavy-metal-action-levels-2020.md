# Nevada cannabis heavy-metal action levels

## Scope
The limits apply across Nevada cannabis product categories that require heavy metal screening in the regulation tables.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Arsenic | Cannabis products requiring heavy-metal screening | <2 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Cadmium | Cannabis products requiring heavy-metal screening | <0.82 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Lead | Cannabis products requiring heavy-metal screening | <1.2 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Mercury | Cannabis products requiring heavy-metal screening | <0.4 | ppm | Product as tested |

## How tested
The limits appear in product-category testing tables under independent testing laboratory requirements.

## Enforcement posture
Lots or production runs that fail required quality-assurance testing are subject to disposal, remediation, retesting, or other regulatory handling under the Nevada cannabis rules.

## History of changes
The regulations were adopted July 21, 2020 and effective November 5, 2020.

## How it compares
Nevada values match the concentration equivalents of the Washington 5 g daily-dose table, but are stated directly in ppm.
