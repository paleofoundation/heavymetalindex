# Hong Kong Mercury Control Ordinance - mercury in cosmetics

## Scope
The loaded rows cover cosmetics under Schedule 3 Part 1 of the Mercury Control Ordinance, including skin-lightening soap and cream. The schedule treats cosmetics as regulated mercury-added products unless the product falls within the unintentional trace exception or the eye-area mercury-preservative exception.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | Cosmetic products with unintentional trace mercury | not exceeding 1 | ppm | mercury content |
| Mercury | Eye-area cosmetic products containing mercury as preservative where no effective and safe substitute is available | not exceeding 70 | ppm | mercury content |

## How tested
The ordinance states mercury-content thresholds for the Schedule 3 product descriptions. It does not print a dedicated cosmetic analytical method in the schedule text.

## Enforcement posture
The ordinance prohibits export, import, manufacture, and supply of regulated mercury-added products except as provided by permit or exemption. A cosmetic outside the stated exceptions is treated as a regulated mercury-added product.

## History of changes
Cap. 640 commenced on 1 December 2021. The source is the verified copy last updated 27 March 2026; the cosmetics schedule item was replaced by L.N. 114 of 2025.

## How it compares
These rows are mercury-only product controls under a Minamata implementation ordinance. They should not be expanded to lead, arsenic, or cadmium cosmetics limits.
