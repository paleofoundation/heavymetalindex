# EU Regulation 2019/1009 fertilising-product heavy-metal limits

## Scope
The loaded rows cover CE-marked EU fertilising products in PFC 1(A), PFC 1(B), PFC 1(C)(I), PFC 2, PFC 3(A), PFC 3(B), PFC 4, and PFC 6 where Annex I gives contaminant limits in mg/kg dry matter.

## How tested
Annex I sets product-function-category contaminant ceilings. Conformity is handled through the regulation's conformity-assessment framework; the rows preserve the Annex I species and dry-matter basis.

## Enforcement posture
The regulation is binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all EU Member States for EU fertilising products made available with CE marking.

## History of changes
Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 was adopted on 5 June 2019. The local copy is a consolidated EUR-Lex text marked 30 December 2025.

## How it compares
Most loaded categories use the same 2 mg/kg dry-matter Cr VI, 1 mg/kg dry-matter Hg, 120 mg/kg dry-matter Pb, and 40 mg/kg dry-matter arsenic ceiling, while cadmium and nickel vary by product function category. Organic and organo-mineral categories specify inorganic arsenic; inorganic macronutrient and liming categories state arsenic without inorganic speciation and are loaded as total arsenic.
