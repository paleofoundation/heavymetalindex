# EPA 2001 methylmercury fish-tissue criterion

## Coverage

The EPA document describes a fish-tissue criterion. It is kept distinct from a drinking-water concentration, an FDA action level and a dietary intake reference.

This page indexes source records. A dedicated synthesis remains pending.
