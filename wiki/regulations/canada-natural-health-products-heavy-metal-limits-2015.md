# Health Canada natural health product heavy-metal quality limits

## Scope
The guide applies to natural health products, including oral and topical products. The loaded register rows cover the topical concentration table.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Arsenic | Topical natural health products | 3 | ppm | Finished topical product |
| Cadmium | Topical natural health products | 3 | ppm | Finished topical product |
| Lead | Topical natural health products | 10 | ppm | Finished topical product |
| Total mercury | Topical natural health products | 1 | ppm | Finished topical product |
| Antimony | Topical natural health products | 5 | ppm | Finished topical product |

For oral products, the guide states daily exposure limits rather than concentration limits: total arsenic <10.0 µg/day or inorganic arsenic <2.1 µg/day, cadmium <6.0 µg/day, lead <10.0 µg/day, total mercury <20.0 µg/day, and methyl mercury <2.0 µg/day.

## How tested
The guide allows elemental impurities to be tested individually or as total heavy metals expressed as lead, and refers to pharmacopoeial or internationally accepted methods for individual elements.

## Enforcement posture
Health Canada describes the guide as a guidance document for product quality. The topical ppm rows are loaded as guidance impurity limits.

## History of changes
The extracted guide is dated May 1, 2015, Version 3.1.

## How it compares
The topical Pb and Hg concentration limits are close to Singapore and ASEAN health-supplement values; Health Canada also adds antimony and dose-based oral limits.
