# FSANZ Application A1329 (F2026L01125) - cadmium in molluscs and Blacklip Rock oysters

## Overview

F2026L01125 is a variation to the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code made under section 92 of the Food Standards Australia New Zealand Act 1991. It arises from Application A1329 and amends the cadmium provisions of [[regulations/au-nz-food-standards-code-schedule-19-contaminants|Schedule 19 - Maximum levels of contaminants and natural toxicants]] as they apply to Blacklip Rock oysters (Saccostrea spathulata) farmed in the Northern Territory. The variation was signed by the delegate of the Board of Food Standards Australia New Zealand on 20 August 2026 and registered on the Federal Register of Legislation on 28 August 2026 (Authorised Version F2026L01125). It is published in the Commonwealth of Australia Gazette No. FSC 192 on 1 September 2026, which is the gazettal date for the purposes of the variation's commencement clause.

## Scope

The variation changes only the cadmium row of Schedule 19 (section S19-4) for molluscs. It does two things: it narrows the general molluscs cadmium row by adding an exclusion, and it inserts a commodity-specific cadmium maximum level for the excluded commodity. It does not change any other analyte, matrix, or maximum level in Schedule 19.

## Exact limit and units

Schedule 19 maximum levels are stated in mg/kg on the portion of the food ordinarily consumed.

| Provision | Contaminant | Food / matrix | Maximum level |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| Item [1] (amends S19-4) | Cadmium | Molluscs, excluding Blacklip Rock oysters (Saccostrea spathulata) farmed in the Northern Territory; dredge/bluff oysters; and queen scallops | 2 |
| Item [2] (inserts S19-4 row) | Cadmium | Blacklip Rock oysters (Saccostrea spathulata) farmed in the Northern Territory | 3 |

Before this variation, the general molluscs cadmium row excluded only dredge/bluff oysters and queen scallops. Item [1] adds Northern Territory-farmed Blacklip Rock oysters to that list of exclusions. Item [2] inserts, below the entry for "Amaranth, grain", a new cadmium maximum level of 3 mg/kg for Northern Territory-farmed Blacklip Rock oysters.

## Commencement

The variation's own commencement table sets two different dates.

- Clauses 1 to 3, and Item [1] of the Schedule (the exclusion from the general molluscs cadmium row), commence on the day of gazettal, 1 September 2026.
- Item [2] of the Schedule (the commodity-specific 3 mg/kg cadmium maximum level for Northern Territory-farmed Blacklip Rock oysters) commences on the day after the end of the period of 24 months beginning on the day of gazettal, which is 2 September 2028.

Between 1 September 2026 and 2 September 2028, Northern Territory-farmed Blacklip Rock oysters are excluded from the general molluscs cadmium row while the commodity-specific 3 mg/kg maximum level is not yet in force. This is the structure of the instrument as made and is recorded here as fact, not interpretation.
