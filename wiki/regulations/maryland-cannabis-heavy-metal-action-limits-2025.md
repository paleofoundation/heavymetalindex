# Maryland cannabis heavy-metal action limits

## Scope
The limits apply to Maryland cannabis compliance testing for usable cannabis, cannabis products except edible products, and edible cannabis products.

## How tested
The technical authority requires independent testing laboratories to test for the listed heavy metals and includes LOQ concentrations. It cites ICH Q3D and USP Chapter <232> as bases for permitted daily exposure.

## Enforcement posture
The document states it has force and effect of law under COMAR 14.17.06.02. Products failing for heavy metals may be remediated under the technical authority.

## History of changes
Revision 6.0 is effective February 2025.

## How it compares
Maryland uses route-specific action limits and includes chromium, unlike several cannabis regimes that list only arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury.
