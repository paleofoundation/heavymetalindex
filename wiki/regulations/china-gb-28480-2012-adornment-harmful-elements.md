# China GB 28480-2012 - harmful elements in adornments

## Scope
GB 28480-2012 covers adornments of various materials, excluding gems. It defines children's jewellery as jewellery for children aged 14 years and under.

## How tested
Nickel release is measured under GB/T 19719 and GB/T 28485 as applicable. Hexavalent chromium is measured under GB/T 28019, and other harmful elements are measured under GB/T 28020 or GB/T 28021.

## Enforcement posture
The standard is a mandatory national standard for covered adornments in China. Products that do not meet the applicable total-content, soluble-element, and nickel-release requirements are not compliant with the standard.

## History of changes
GB 28480-2012 was issued as a Chinese national standard for harmful-element limits in adornments. The local source is the 2012 edition.

## How it compares
The children's-jewellery soluble-element table aligns closely with legacy toy-migration values for Sb, As, Cd, Cr, Pb, and Hg, while the total-content row adds separate limits for metal adornment materials. The nickel release provisions are important for compliance but use area/time units rather than mass fraction.
