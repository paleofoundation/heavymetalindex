# Mexico NOM-252-SSA1-2011 - bioavailable metals in toys and school articles

## Scope
NOM-252 applies to toys for children up to 12 years old and preschool school articles in Mexico. The full soluble-element table applies to toys for children under three years and preschool school articles that may be sucked, licked, or swallowed.

## How tested
Appendix A extracts soluble elements under conditions simulating materials remaining four hours in the alimentary tract after ingestion. Determination may use ICP-MS, ICP optical emission, flame AAS, hydride generation, or graphite furnace techniques as applicable.

## Enforcement posture
The NOM is mandatory for manufacturers, importers, and marketers of covered products in Mexico. Compliance is assessed against the stated bioavailability limits and test methods.

## History of changes
The final NOM was published in the Diario Oficial de la Federación on May 15, 2012, replacing the prior NOM-015/1-SCFI/SSA-1994 framework for metal bioavailability in coated toys and school articles.

## How it compares
The general-material values match common international toy-migration values for several elements. The preschool/modeling-clay/finger-paint row is stricter for cadmium, chromium, and mercury while retaining the same lead value.
