# FDA CPG 540.600 - Methylmercury in fish and aquatic animals

FDA Compliance Policy Guide Sec. 540.600 gives the agency's regulatory-action guidance for methylmercury in fish, shellfish, crustaceans, and other aquatic animals, whether fresh, frozen, or processed.

## Action Level

| Matrix | Analyte | Action level | Basis |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Fish, shellfish, crustaceans, and other aquatic animals | Methylmercury expressed as mercury | 1.0 ppm | Edible portion only |

The CPG states that legal-action recommendations should be based on the exact edible portion used for analysis. The broader FDA action-level guidance also states that action levels are limits at or above which FDA may take legal action to remove products from the market; they are not permissible contamination targets when contamination is avoidable.

## How this page interacts with HMT&C

This page records FDA's enforcement-relevant methylmercury action level for fishery products. It does not set an HMT&C certification threshold. Source pages may cite it when a paper compares measured fish mercury values against the FDA 1 ppm action level, while keeping the distinction clear between total mercury (tHg) measurements and methylmercury (MeHg) enforcement language.
