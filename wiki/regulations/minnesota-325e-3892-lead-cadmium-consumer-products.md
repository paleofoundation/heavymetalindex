# Minnesota 325E.3892 - lead and cadmium in covered consumer products

## Scope

Minnesota section 325E.3892 applies to covered products or product components including jewelry, toys, cosmetics and personal care products, games, school supplies, pots and pans, cups and food containers, craft supplies, art supplies, costumes, keys, clothing, footwear, headwear, and accessories.

## Exact limit and units

| Species | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Structured value |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Pb | Covered products, subject to statutory exceptions | 0.009 | percent by total weight | 90,000 ug/kg |
| Cd | Covered products, subject to statutory exceptions | 0.0075 | percent by total weight | 75,000 ug/kg |
| Pb | Keys/key chains/key rings after July 1, 2028 under the statutory exception | 1.5 | percent by total weight | 15,000,000 ug/kg |

## How tested

The statute requires covered persons to furnish relevant compliance information on request. It does not specify a particular analytical method for lead or cadmium.

## Enforcement posture

The commissioners of the Pollution Control Agency, commerce, and health may coordinate enforcement. The Pollution Control Agency and commerce may enforce with the attorney general, and the attorney general may enforce under section 8.31.

## History of changes

The current text lists History: 2023 c 60 art 3 s 24; 1Sp2025 c 4 art 7 s 27,28. The 2025 current text adds or clarifies exceptions compared with the 2023 source text.

## How it compares

The general 90 ppm Pb and 75 ppm Cd thresholds are broader than many product-specific limits because they apply across multiple consumer-product categories. The key exception is much higher and should not be applied to other covered products.
