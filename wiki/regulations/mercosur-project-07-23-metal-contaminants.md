# MERCOSUR Project Resolution 07/23 - metal contaminants in food

## Status

Project Resolution 07/23 remained in internal consultation among Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, and Uruguay in the official August 2025 SGT No. 3 record. It is not treated as binding law.

## Intended relationship

The project is framed as a replacement for MERCOSUR Resolutions [[regulations/mercosur-gmc-res-12-2011-inorganic-contaminants|GMC 12/11]] and 18/21. That intended repeal has not been applied to HMI's active register because the project has not been verified as adopted.

## Numerical treatment

No proposed value is loaded into `regulatory_limits.csv`, and no proposal can bind a compiled standard. The existing MERCOSUR contaminant rows remain separately traceable until an adopted instrument establishes a legal transition.
