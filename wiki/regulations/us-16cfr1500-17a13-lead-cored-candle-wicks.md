# US 16 CFR 1500.17(a)(13) - lead-cored candle wicks

## Scope
The regulation applies to metal-cored candlewicks containing lead and to candles that use those wicks. It is a consumer-product ban under the Federal Hazardous Substances Act regulations.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Metal-cored candlewicks and candles with such wicks | 0.06 | percent | lead by weight of the metal core |

## How tested
The provision is expressed as percent lead by weight of the metal in the wick. The excerpt does not prescribe a specific analytical method in the transcribed paragraph.

## Enforcement posture
Candlewicks above the limit and candles using them are banned hazardous substances. The CPSC may enforce the ban through ordinary FHSA/CPSC authorities, including import controls and corrective actions.

## History of changes
The CFR text in the local PDF is the 2024 compilation. The cited regulatory finding explains that a ban on wicks containing more than 0.06 percent lead by weight of the metal was selected as the least burdensome requirement adequate to reduce risk.

## How it compares
The 0.06 percent value equals 600 ppm or 600 mg/kg in the metal core, which is much higher than food or cosmetic impurity limits but applies to a durable consumer-product component.
