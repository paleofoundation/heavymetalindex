# China GB 14930.1-2022 detergent arsenic limits

## Scope
The standard covers detergents used directly for food cleaning and detergents for tableware and food-contact tools, equipment, packaging, and containers. Type A covers detergents directly used for food cleaning; Type B covers dishware and other food-contact cleaning uses.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| tAs | Type A detergent directly used for food cleaning | 3.0 | mg/kg | product as placed on the market |
| tAs | Type B detergent for tableware and food-contact articles | 5.0 | mg/kg | product as placed on the market |
| Heavy metals (as Pb) | Both types | 1.0 | mg/kg | grouped heavy metals calculated as Pb, product as placed on the market |

The total-arsenic limits are species-specific. The final row is a grouped ceiling on the sum of heavy metals calculated as lead, not a species-specific lead limit; because lead, cadmium, and mercury are members of that summed group, each is individually bounded at or below 1.0 mg/kg as a conservative consequence of the grouped ceiling.

## How tested
The table lists GB/T 30797 as the test method for total arsenic and GB/T 30799 for the grouped heavy-metals-as-lead determination. The grouped heavy-metals line is documented here as a grouped ceiling rather than being loaded as a species-specific lead value, because the source states combined heavy metals calculated as Pb.

## Enforcement posture
The document is a Chinese national food safety standard for detergent products in the stated scope.

## History of changes
The local copy identifies GB 14930.1-2022 as the 2022 national food safety standard for detergents.

## How it compares
The Type A arsenic ceiling is lower than the Type B ceiling because Type A products directly contact food during cleaning.
