# Israel IMC-GMP cannabis heavy-metal limits

## Scope
The limits apply to cannabis inflorescences after drying and to cannabis products under the IMC-GMP quality procedure.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Cd | Cannabis inflorescences after drying and cannabis products | 0.5 | ppm | product after drying/product as applicable |
| Pb | Cannabis inflorescences after drying and cannabis products | 5.0 | ppm | product after drying/product as applicable |
| tHg | Cannabis inflorescences after drying and cannabis products | 0.1 | ppm | product after drying/product as applicable |
| tAs | Cannabis inflorescences after drying and cannabis products | 2.5 | ppm | product after drying/product as applicable |

## How tested
The extracted table states maximum metal levels but does not name the analytical method in the visible excerpt.

## Enforcement posture
The procedure sets quality requirements for medical cannabis production under the Israel Ministry of Health Medical Cannabis Unit.

## History of changes
The procedure number is 152, dated July 2016; the local copy identifies update 5 in March 2024.

## How it compares
The Israel action levels are less stringent for lead than several US state cannabis tables but more stringent for mercury than the Nevada 0.4 ppm action level.
