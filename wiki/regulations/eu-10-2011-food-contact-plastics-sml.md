# EU Regulation 10/2011 — specific migration limits for metals (plastic food-contact, Annex II)

## Scope

Commission Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 governs plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with food. Annex II sets specific migration limits (SML) for metals that may migrate from the plastic into the food it holds. The measured quantity is migration into food (or a food simulant), expressed in mg/kg of food — a different basis from toy-material migration (mg/kg of material) and from total-content limits (mg/kg of article). For infant food-contact articles — bottles, nipples, sippy cups, milk-storage bags, feeding accessories — this is the governing instrument.

## Metals with a positive specific migration limit (Annex II point 1)

Values in mg/kg food, current after Regulation (EU) 2016/1416 (aluminium, zinc) and Regulation (EU) 2020/1245 (lanthanides, not-detectable regime).

| Metal | SML (mg/kg food) |
| --- | ---: |
| Aluminium | 1 |
| Antimony | 0.04 |
| Barium | 1 |
| Cobalt | 0.05 |
| Copper | 5 |
| Iron | 48 |
| Lithium | 0.6 |
| Manganese | 0.6 |
| Nickel | 0.02 |
| Zinc | 5 |
| Lanthanides (Eu, Gd, La, Tb — sum) | 0.05 |

## Metals with no positive SML — "not detectable" regime (Annex II point 2)

Lead, cadmium, mercury, and arsenic are not authorised substances and have no positive migration limit; their migration must be not detectable, at the detection limits the Regulation states. This is the strictest possible ceiling — below the limit of detection.

| Metal | Treatment | Detection limit (mg/kg food) |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Lead | Not detectable | 0.01 |
| Cadmium | Not detectable | 0.002 |
| Mercury | Not detectable | 0.01 |
| Arsenic | Not detectable | 0.01 |
| Chromium | Not detectable | 0.01 (relaxed to 3.6 for total chromium where documented Cr(VI) exclusion applies) |

## Limit structure and related controls

This Regulation sets its heavy-metal controls as a not-detectable ceiling at the stated detection limit for lead, cadmium, mercury, and total arsenic, and as a positive specific migration limit for nickel and aluminium (0.02 and 1 mg/kg food); hexavalent chromium is bounded by the not-detectable total-chromium limit. Total-content limits from children's-product law (US CPSIA, Washington Children's Safe Product Act) apply in addition to migration limits, as a separate ceiling in a different basis. Stainless-steel drinkware components are additionally controlled for nickel release under the former EU Nickel Directive 94/27/EC (now REACH Annex XVII entry 27).
