# EU Directive 84/500/EEC: ceramic food-contact articles

## Coverage

The instrument concerns migration of lead and cadmium from ceramic food-contact articles. Migration testing and total metal content are distinct measurements.

This page indexes source records. A dedicated synthesis remains pending.
