# EU REACH Regulation 2020/2081 - tattoo and permanent make-up metal limits

## Scope
Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/2081 amends REACH Annex XVII to restrict substances in mixtures for use for tattooing purposes and permanent make-up. Appendix 13 to entry 75 states specific concentration limits by weight for selected metals and metalloids.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Mercury | 0.00005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Nickel | 0.0005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Antimony | 0.00005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Arsenic | 0.00005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Cadmium | 0.00005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Chromium VI | 0.00005 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |
| Lead | 0.00007 | percent by weight | mixture for tattooing or permanent make-up |

Organometallic tin appears in Appendix 13 at 0.00005 percent by weight, but it is not loaded as elemental tin because the row regulates an organometallic-tin compound class rather than a source-stated tin species concentration.

## How tested
The Appendix states concentration limits by weight for substances in tattooing and permanent make-up mixtures. The regulation also requires labels and batch-reference information for covered mixtures, but the numeric metal entries themselves are concentration limits rather than exposure limits.

## Enforcement posture
The restriction is a statutory REACH Annex XVII restriction. Mixtures for tattooing or permanent make-up that exceed the applicable concentration limits may not be placed on the market for, or used for, those purposes after the applicable transition dates.

## History of changes
Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/2081 was adopted on 14 December 2020 and published in the Official Journal on 15 December 2020. Most entry 75 restrictions applied from 4 January 2022, with separate transitional treatment for specified pigments outside the heavy-metal rows loaded here.

## How it compares
The REACH tattoo-ink values are lower than broad cosmetics impurity allowances because they are specific concentration limits for substances in tattooing and permanent make-up mixtures. Nickel has a higher numeric ceiling than the other loaded metal rows, while lead is set at 0.00007 percent by weight.
