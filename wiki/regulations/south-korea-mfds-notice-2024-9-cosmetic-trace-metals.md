# South Korea MFDS Notice 2024-9 - cosmetic trace-metal limits

## Scope
MFDS Notice No. 2024-9 applies to cosmetics manufactured, imported, or distributed in Korea. Article 6 sets safety-management standards for marketed cosmetics, including trace-detection allowances when listed substances were not intentionally added and complete technical removal is impossible.

## Exact limit and units

| Cadmium | Cosmetic products | 5 | micrograms/g | unintentional trace presence in marketed cosmetic product |

## How tested
Article 6 points testing to attached Table 4, while allowing other scientifically and reasonably valid in-house standards where recognized. The extracted methods appendix includes procedures for lead, nickel, arsenic, mercury, antimony, and cadmium.

## Enforcement posture
These are statutory Korean cosmetic safety standards under the Cosmetics Act. The clause applies when the substances were not intentionally added and their unintentional origin and technical irreducibility are objectively confirmed.

## History of changes
The notice history lists amendments through MFDS Notice No. 2024-9, dated 7 February 2024. The 2024 PDF supersedes the earlier 2023-17 version for this ingest pass.

## How it compares
Korea splits lead and nickel by cosmetic product type, unlike the simpler ASEAN-style finished-cosmetic tables. Arsenic, mercury, antimony, and cadmium are stated as single all-cosmetic trace limits.
