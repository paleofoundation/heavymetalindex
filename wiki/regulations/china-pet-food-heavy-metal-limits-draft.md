# China draft pet food heavy-metal limits

## Scope
The draft applies to compound pet feed, pet additive premix feed, and other pet feed. The draft states that listed limits are calculated on an 88% dry-matter basis unless otherwise specified.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Cd | Compound pet feed, pet additive premix feed, and other pet feed | 2 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| Cr-total | Compound pet feed, pet additive premix feed, and other pet feed | 5 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| tHg | Compound pet feed, pet additive premix feed, and other pet feed | 0.3 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| Pb | Compound pet feed | 5 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| Pb | Pet additive premix feed and other pet feed | 10 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| tAs | Pet feed containing aquatic animals/products or algae/products | 10 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| iAs | Pet feed containing aquatic animals/products or algae/products | 2 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| tAs | Non-aquatic/non-algae compound pet feed | 2 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |
| tAs | Non-aquatic/non-algae pet additive premix feed and other pet feed | 4 | mg/kg | 88% dry matter basis |

## How tested
The draft table names GB/T 13082 for cadmium, GB/T 13088 for chromium, GB/T 13081 for mercury, GB/T 13080 for lead, GB/T 13079 for total arsenic, and GB 5009.11 for inorganic arsenic.

## Enforcement posture
The local copy is explicitly marked as a public-comment draft. It is retained as draft guidance, not as an implemented legal ceiling.

## History of changes
The draft was prepared under GB/T 1.1-2020 drafting rules and has placeholder publication and implementation dates in the local copy.

## How it compares
The draft distinguishes total arsenic and inorganic arsenic for aquatic/algae-containing pet feed but only total arsenic for non-aquatic/non-algae pet feed categories.
