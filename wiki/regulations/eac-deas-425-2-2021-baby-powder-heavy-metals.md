# EAC DEAS 425-2:2021 - baby powder heavy-metal limits

## Scope
DEAS 425-2:2021 covers baby powder under the EAC skin-powder standard series. The PDF was attached to WTO TBT notifications for EAC partner states and is labelled as a Draft East African Standard.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Baby powder | 10 | mg/kg max | finished product |
| Arsenic | Baby powder | 2 | mg/kg max | finished product |
| Mercury | Baby powder | 2 | mg/kg max | finished product |

The table also states that total heavy metals as lead, mercury, and arsenic in combination in finished baby powder must not exceed 10 mg/kg; that combined cap is gap-logged rather than loaded as a species row.

## How tested
The heavy-metal table references EAS 847-16 for determination of lead, mercury, and arsenic content. The draft also includes microbiological and talc/asbestos testing provisions.

## Enforcement posture
Because the source is a draft standard and WTO notification attachment, the rows are recorded as draft guidance rather than implemented maximum levels.

## History of changes
The displayed draft is the third edition of DEAS 425-2, dated 2021.

## How it compares
The lead and arsenic limits match the EAC adult body/face powder draft, while the baby-powder mercury value is 2 mg/kg rather than 1 mg/kg.
