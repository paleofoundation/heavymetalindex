# China GB 2762-2025 - Maximum levels of contaminants in food

## Overview

GB 2762-2025 is China's National Food Safety Standard for maximum levels of contaminants in foods. The standard title page records publication on 2 September 2025 and entry into force on 2 September 2026. It replaces [[regulations/china-gb-2762-2022-contaminants|GB 2762-2022]] together with Amendment No. 1 to that standard. It was issued by the National Health Commission and the State Administration for Market Regulation. As of 14 September 2026 it is the active Chinese framework for food-contaminant maximum levels.

## Scope

GB 2762-2025 is an omnibus food-contaminant standard. It sets food-specific maximum levels for lead, cadmium, mercury (total mercury and methylmercury), arsenic (total arsenic and inorganic arsenic), tin, nickel, chromium, and the non-metal contaminants nitrite, nitrate, benzo[a]pyrene, N-nitrosodimethylamine, polychlorinated biphenyls, and 3-chloro-1,2-propanediol. Maximum levels are stated in mg/kg on the ordinarily consumed part of the food unless the standard provides otherwise.

## Key changes from GB 2762-2022

The standard's foreword lists the substantive changes relative to GB 2762-2022 and its Amendment No. 1. The metal-scope changes are as follows.

- The application principles were revised. In GB 2762-2022, application principle 3.5 named dried meat products alongside dried aquatic products and dried edible fungi in the rule that derives a dried product's contaminant limit from the corresponding fresh-food limit combined with the product's dehydration or concentration factor. GB 2762-2025 removes dried meat products from that rule; the standard now applies the general meat-products (肉制品) category maximum levels to dried meat products directly, and the conversion rule retains only dried aquatic products and dried edible fungi.
- Consequent to that principle change, the maximum-level treatment for lead, cadmium, arsenic, and chromium in dried meat products was modified. The general meat and meat-product category values themselves are unchanged from GB 2762-2022 (see the metal tables): lead in meat products 0.3 mg/kg, cadmium in meat and meat products 0.1 mg/kg, total arsenic in meat and meat products 0.5 mg/kg, and total chromium in meat and meat products 1.0 mg/kg.

The foreword also records revisions to the nitrite limit for packaged drinking water (natural mineral water excepted), a new nitrite limit for edible bird's nest, a revised presentation of Table 10 indicators, and a revised Appendix A. Those are non-metal or presentational changes and are outside the heavy-metal scope of this page.

## Selected limits cited by source pages

This page is the current-framework routing anchor for China food-contaminant maximum levels. Source pages should quote only the exact GB 2762-2025 values that their source or official comparator uses, with matrix, basis, and analyte species (total versus inorganic arsenic, total versus methylmercury) preserved. Liquid infant formula limits are converted from the solid-product value at an 8:1 ratio per the standard's own notes.

## How this page interacts with HMT&C

This page records the active Chinese regulatory framework for food-contaminant maximum levels. It does not set or justify HMT&C certification thresholds. Standards work may cite it as a jurisdictional comparator when a China-market product category is in scope.
