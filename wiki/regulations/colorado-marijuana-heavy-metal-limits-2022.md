# Colorado marijuana heavy-metal limits

## Scope
The limits apply to regulated marijuana products, including inhaled, topical/transdermal, oral, rectal, vaginal, and audited products as described in the table.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Lead | Inhaled product or audited product administered by metered-dose nasal spray | <0.5 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Arsenic | Inhaled product or audited product administered by metered-dose nasal spray | <0.2 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Cadmium | Inhaled product or audited product administered by metered-dose nasal spray | <0.2 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Mercury | Inhaled product or audited product administered by metered-dose nasal spray | <0.1 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Lead | Topical and/or transdermal product | <10 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Arsenic | Topical and/or transdermal product | <3 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Cadmium | Topical and/or transdermal product | <3 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Mercury | Topical and/or transdermal product | <1 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Lead | Oral consumption or audited product with rectal or vaginal administration | <1 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Arsenic | Oral consumption or audited product with rectal or vaginal administration | <1.5 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Cadmium | Oral consumption or audited product with rectal or vaginal administration | <0.5 | ppm | Product as tested |
| Mercury | Oral consumption or audited product with rectal or vaginal administration | <1.5 | ppm | Product as tested |

## How tested
The table sits in the required contaminant testing rules for regulated marijuana. Products are tested as finished marijuana products or audited products according to the rule text.

## Enforcement posture
The limits are part of Colorado marijuana rules and failure above the acceptable limits is a regulatory compliance issue.

## History of changes
The file is the 2022 Colorado MED rules extract for heavy metals.

## How it compares
Colorado uses route-specific limits. The inhalation values are stricter than Nevada and Washington-style cannabis action levels for the same metals.
