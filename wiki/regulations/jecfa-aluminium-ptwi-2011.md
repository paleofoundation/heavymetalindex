# JECFA aluminum evaluation: 74th meeting (2011)

## Coverage

This is a pointer to the aluminum evaluation in WHO Technical Report Series 966, section 3.1.1. The 74th meeting took place in 2011. It is kept separate from the earlier 67th-meeting evaluation; no numerical intake crosswalk is compiled here.
