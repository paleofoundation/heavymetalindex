# EU 2025/1891 - inorganic arsenic in fish and other seafood

## Scope

Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/1891 amends Regulation (EU) 2023/915 by setting maximum levels for inorganic arsenic in fish and other seafood. The new inorganic-arsenic rows apply to Annex I points 3.4.5 to 3.4.8 for fish muscle meat, crustaceans, bivalve molluscs, and cephalopods. The source defines inorganic arsenic for these rows as the sum of As(III) and As(V).

The regulation also restates the total-arsenic row for salt at Annex I point 3.4.9. That salt row is recorded separately as `tAs`; it must not be treated as an inorganic-arsenic value.

## Exact limit and units

The values below are transcribed from the Annex to Regulation (EU) 2025/1891. The source uses decimal commas in `mg/kg`; the structured evidence register converts each value to `ug/kg`.

| Annex point | Species | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 3.4.5.1 | iAs | Fish species other than those listed under 3.4.5.2 | 0.10 | mg/kg | wet weight; muscle meat, or whole fish if eaten whole |
| 3.4.5.2 | iAs | Anglerfish, monkfish, giant stargazer, flatfishes, haddock, herring, ray, and shark | 0.50 | mg/kg | wet weight; muscle meat, or whole fish if eaten whole |
| 3.4.6.1 | iAs | Crabs and crab-like crustaceans, prawns, and shrimps | 0.10 | mg/kg | wet weight; appendage muscle for crabs and crab-like crustaceans |
| 3.4.6.2 | iAs | Crustaceans other than 3.4.6.1 and 3.4.6.3 | 0.20 | mg/kg | wet weight; muscle meat from appendages and abdomen, cephalothorax excluded |
| 3.4.6.3 | iAs | Langoustine and rock lobster | 1.5 | mg/kg | wet weight; muscle meat from appendages and abdomen, cephalothorax excluded |
| 3.4.7.1 | iAs | Scallops | 0.10 | mg/kg | wet weight; for Pecten maximus, adductor muscle and gonad only |
| 3.4.7.2 | iAs | Bivalve molluscs other than scallops | 0.50 | mg/kg | wet weight |
| 3.4.8 | iAs | Cephalopods | 0.050 | mg/kg | wet weight; animal without viscera |
| 3.4.9 | tAs | Salt | 0.50 | mg/kg | product as placed on the market |

The seafood limits apply from 8 October 2025. Fish and seafood lawfully placed on the market before that date may remain on the market until their date of minimum durability or use-by date.

## How tested

Regulation (EU) 2025/1891 sets the legal maximum levels and species definitions but does not create a new analytical method on this page. Compliance interpretation should use Regulation (EU) 2023/915, applicable EU sampling and analysis rules for contaminants, and a method capable of measuring inorganic arsenic as the sum of As(III) and As(V) for the seafood rows.

## Enforcement posture

These are binding EU maximum levels. Covered fish and seafood above the applicable inorganic-arsenic maximum level may not be placed on the EU market after the transition rule has run its course. Salt is covered by the restated total-arsenic maximum level, not by the seafood iAs rows.

## History of changes

Regulation (EU) 2025/1891 was adopted on 17 September 2025 and published in the Official Journal on 18 September 2025. It entered into force on the twentieth day following publication, 8 October 2025, and amended Regulation (EU) 2023/915 by adding seafood inorganic-arsenic rows in Annex I section 3.4.

## How it compares

The 2025 amendment is narrower than the full EU contaminants framework: it targets inorganic arsenic in fish and other seafood and should not be generalized to total arsenic occurrence data. It is most comparable to seafood-specific contaminant limits in Codex or national food-contaminant rules, but comparisons must preserve arsenic species, edible tissue, and wet-weight basis.

For seafood occurrence studies, total arsenic (`tAs`) is not a substitute for these `iAs` limits unless the source measured inorganic arsenic directly or supplies a legally accepted conversion basis for the specific comparison.
