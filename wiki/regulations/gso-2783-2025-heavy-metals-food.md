# GSO 2783:2025 - heavy metals in food

## Scope

The official GSO catalog describes GSO 2783:2025 as a technical regulation for maximum residue limits for heavy metals in food. Its public metadata names arsenic, inorganic arsenic, mercury, cadmium, lead, and tin.

## Legal status

The GSO catalog record is active and was published on 29 October 2025. That verifies the regional standard's existence and publication, but it does not by itself prove a single binding date across every GCC member state. National adoption, transition, and enforcement must be checked in the implementing jurisdiction before the standard is used as a market-access deadline.
