# US EPA 40 CFR Part 503 biosolids metal limits

## Scope
The loaded rows cover land-application pollutant concentration limits in 40 CFR 503.13 Table 1 and Table 3 for sewage sludge and derived bulk material. Loading-rate tables are not loaded because their units are kg/ha rather than product concentration.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| tAs | Table 1 ceiling concentration | 75 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Cd | Table 1 ceiling concentration | 85 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Pb | Table 1 ceiling concentration | 840 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| tHg | Table 1 ceiling concentration | 57 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Ni | Table 1 ceiling concentration | 420 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| tAs | Table 3 pollutant concentration | 41 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Cd | Table 3 pollutant concentration | 39 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Pb | Table 3 pollutant concentration | 300 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| tHg | Table 3 pollutant concentration | 17 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |
| Ni | Table 3 pollutant concentration | 420 | mg/kg | dry weight basis |

## How tested
Part 503 references inorganic-pollutant test methods elsewhere in the rule and states the pollutant tables on a dry-weight basis.

## Enforcement posture
No person may apply sewage sludge to land except in accordance with Part 503. The regulation imposes ceiling concentration, pollutant concentration, and loading-rate conditions depending on the land-application scenario.

## History of changes
Part 503 was promulgated in 1993, with later amendments. The local copy is the 7-1-2025 edition of 40 CFR Chapter I.

## How it compares
Table 3 pollutant concentrations are lower than Table 1 ceiling concentrations for arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury; nickel is 420 mg/kg in both tables.
