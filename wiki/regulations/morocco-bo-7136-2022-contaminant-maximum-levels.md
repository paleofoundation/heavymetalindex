# Morocco Joint Order 2410-22 - food contaminant metal limits

## Scope
Joint Order No. 2410-22 modifies and completes Joint Order No. 1643-16 by replacing the contaminant annex for primary products and food products. Annex III covers metal trace elements in foods, including infant formula, foods for infants and young children, fishery products, grains, vegetables, fruits, oils and fats, supplements, salt, canned foods, and rice products.

## How tested
The metal annex states maximum levels by contaminant and food category in mg/kg. This PDF excerpt does not provide an analytical-method section for the metal rows.

## Enforcement posture
The order replaces the annex to the 2016 Moroccan contaminant-limit order and was published in the official bulletin. The annex sets maximum authorized contaminant limits for covered primary products and food products.

## History of changes
Joint Order No. 2410-22 was signed in Rabat on 14 September 2022 and published in Bulletin Officiel No. 7136 on 20 October 2022. Article 1 abrogates and replaces the annex to Joint Order No. 1643-16 of 30 May 2016.

## How it compares
The Moroccan schedule follows the same broad contaminant-limit structure used by many food authorities: separate infant-formula and baby-food rows, species-specific arsenic rows for inorganic and total arsenic, fish tiers for mercury, and inorganic-tin limits for canned foods. Comparisons should preserve the metal species, product scope, and stated unit for each row.
