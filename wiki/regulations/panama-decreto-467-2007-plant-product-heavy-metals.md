# Panama Decreto Ejecutivo 467/2007 - plant-product heavy metals

## Scope

The heavy-metals table applies to fruits, vegetables, grains, legumes, leguminous vegetables, vegetable oils, and related plant products handled and marketed in Panama. Nitrate rows are outside the HMI metal register.

## Exact limit and units

The table expresses limits as ppm. HMI converts ppm to `ug/kg` by multiplying by 1000.

| Species | Matrix | Limit ppm | Structured value |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pb | Fruits | 0.01 | 10 ug/kg |
| Pb | Small fruits, berries, and grapes | 0.2 | 200 ug/kg |
| Pb | Vegetables including peeled potatoes, except brassicas, leafy vegetables, mushrooms, hops, and herbs | 0.1 | 100 ug/kg |
| Pb | Brassicas except cabbage | 0.3 | 300 ug/kg |
| Pb | Leaf vegetables except spinach | 0.3 | 300 ug/kg |
| Pb | Cereal grains | 0.2 | 200 ug/kg |
| Pb | Dry legumes | 0.2 | 200 ug/kg |
| Pb | Leguminous vegetables | 0.2 | 200 ug/kg |
| Pb | Vegetable oils except cocoa butter | 0.1 | 100 ug/kg |
| Cd | Cereals, legumes, and leguminous vegetables | 0.01 | 10 ug/kg |

## How tested

The table is part of a decree administered by the Ministerio de Salud. The excerpt does not name a heavy-metal analytical method.

## Enforcement posture

Article Second states the decree is mandatory for natural and legal persons engaged in handling and marketing fruits and vegetables in Panama. Article Third assigns oversight to health authorities, and Article Fourth provides sanctions for noncompliance.

## History of changes

The decree was given in Panama City on 7 November 2007 and published in Gaceta Oficial Digital No. 25932 on 4 December 2007.

## How it compares

The Panama plant-product lead rows are narrower than broad food-contaminant tables because they focus on produce and grains; the cadmium entry is a single grouped row for cereals, legumes, and leguminous vegetables.
