# Taiwan draft feed-import phosphate heavy-metal limits

## Scope
The draft table covers feed-use defluorinated calcium phosphate, feed-use calcium phosphates, and dicalcium phosphate import inspection items.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Pb | Feed-use calcium phosphate import-inspection products | 50 | ppm | as imported |
| tAs | Feed-use calcium phosphate import-inspection products | 12 | ppm | as imported |
| Cd | Feed-use calcium phosphate import-inspection products | 10 | ppm | as imported |
| tHg | Feed-use calcium phosphate import-inspection products | 0.5 | ppm | as imported |

## How tested
The visible table lists the heavy-metal inspection items and limits but does not provide detailed analytical methods in the excerpt.

## Enforcement posture
The document is marked as a draft import-inspection regulation; rows are retained as draft guidance and not as implemented legal limits.

## History of changes
The local copy is from the Executive Yuan Gazette, volume 23, issue 76, dated 27 April 2017.

## How it compares
The Taiwan draft is narrow: it applies to feed-use phosphate import products rather than all compound feeds.
