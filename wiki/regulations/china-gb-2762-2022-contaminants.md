# China GB 2762-2022 - Maximum levels of contaminants in food (superseded)

## Overview

GB 2762-2022 was China's National Food Safety Standard for maximum levels of contaminants in foods from 30 June 2023 until 2 September 2026. The National Health Commission and State Administration for Market Regulation announced it in 2022 as part of Announcement No. 3 of 2022. USDA FAS reports that it entered into force on 30 June 2023 and updated GB 2762-2017. It was replaced by [[regulations/china-gb-2762-2025-contaminants|GB 2762-2025]], which entered into force on 2 September 2026. This page is retained as a historical comparator for literature that cites GB 2762-2022.

## Scope

GB 2762-2022 is an omnibus food-contaminant standard. It covers food-specific maximum levels for contaminants such as lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic, tin, nickel, chromium, nitrite, nitrate, benzo[a]pyrene, N-dimethylnitrosamine, polychlorinated biphenyls, and 3-chloro-1,2-propanediol.

This page records the active Chinese regulatory framework for food-contaminant maximum levels. It does not set or justify HMT&C certification thresholds. Standards work may cite it as a jurisdictional comparator when a China-market product category is in scope.
