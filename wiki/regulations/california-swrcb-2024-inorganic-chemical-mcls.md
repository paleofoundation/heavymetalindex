# California SWRCB 2024 - inorganic chemical MCLs including Cr(VI)

## Scope

The amended section 64431 table applies primary inorganic-chemical maximum contaminant levels to California public water systems.

## Exact limit and units

| Species | Chemical row | MCL mg/L | Structured value |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Al | Aluminum | 1.0 | 1,000 ug/kg-equivalent |
| Sb | Antimony | 0.006 | 6 ug/kg-equivalent |
| tAs | Arsenic | 0.010 | 10 ug/kg-equivalent |
| Cd | Cadmium | 0.005 | 5 ug/kg-equivalent |
| Cr-VI | Chromium (hexavalent) | 0.010 | 10 ug/kg-equivalent |
| Cr-total | Chromium (total) | 0.05 | 50 ug/kg-equivalent |
| tHg | Mercury | 0.002 | 2 ug/kg-equivalent |
| Ni | Nickel | 0.1 | 100 ug/kg-equivalent |

## How tested

Section 64432 requires public water systems to monitor for the MCLs in Table 64431-A. The excerpt does not list analytical method codes in the table itself.

## Enforcement posture

Public water systems must comply with the primary MCLs in Table 64431-A and monitor under the cited sections. These are enforceable drinking-water standards.

## History of changes

The regulation text is marked SWRCB-DDW-21-003, Hexavalent Chromium MCL, February 2024. The companion State Water Board resolution adopted a 10 ppb hexavalent chromium MCL.

## How it compares

The table records both total chromium and hexavalent chromium as separate rows, preserving species. Arsenic and mercury are not narrowed beyond the element names in the table and are recorded as total arsenic and total mercury.
