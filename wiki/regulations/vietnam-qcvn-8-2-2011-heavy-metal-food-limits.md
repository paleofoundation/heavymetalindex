# Vietnam QCVN 8-2:2011/BYT - heavy-metal limits in food

## Scope

QCVN 8-2:2011/BYT applies to importers, producers, and sellers of food products at risk of heavy-metal contamination, and to relevant organizations and individuals.

## Exact limit and units

The technical-requirements tables set maximum limits in mg/kg or mg/l for arsenic, cadmium, lead, mercury, methylmercury, and tin. The structured register converts each mg/kg or mg/l value to ug/kg-equivalent by multiplying by 1000 and preserves the food category in each row.

## How tested

The regulation lists test-method references including Vietnamese standards and AOAC methods for arsenic, cadmium, lead, mercury, methylmercury, and tin. It allows other test methods with equivalent accuracy.

## Enforcement posture

The management section requires relevant entities to ensure that foods do not exceed the heavy-metal limits and supports recall/destruction or other treatment where contamination exceeds allowable limits.

## History of changes

QCVN 8-2:2011/BYT was issued with Circular No. 02/2011/TT-BYT. The local English PDF is treated as a translation companion to the Vietnamese text, which is gap-logged as duplicate-language coverage for this ingest.

## How it compares

Vietnam uses a broad commodity schedule similar to Codex-style food contaminant tables. Arsenic is not identified as inorganic in the maximum-limit table, so HMI records those rows as total arsenic; methylmercury receives separate fish rows.
