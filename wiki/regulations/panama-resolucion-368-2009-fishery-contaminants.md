# Panama Resolucion 368:2009 - fishery product contaminants

## Scope

Resolucion No. 368 applies to fish, fish subproducts, and other products derived from fishing and aquaculture in Panama. The resolution authorizes the Departamento de Proteccion de Alimentos of the Ministerio de Salud to inspect and sample shrimp farms, fish farms, processing plants, vessels, markets, collection centers, transport, and similar operations.

HMI records the Article Sixth metals table for cadmium, lead, and mercury in fishery products. Antibiotics, dyes, steroids, pesticides, additives, histamine, dioxins, PCBs, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, organoleptic checks, and parasite controls are outside the regulatory metal-limit register for this ingest.

## Exact limit and units

Article Sixth states the maximum contents in `mg/kg` or `ppm`. The structured evidence register converts each value to `ug/kg` by multiplying by 1000. The source table does not state a wet-weight or dry-weight qualifier.

The source says mercury rather than methylmercury, so HMI records these mercury rows as `tHg`.

## How tested

Article First authorizes inspection and sampling by the Departamento de Proteccion de Alimentos. Article Second establishes laboratory testing to control maximum permitted residues of chemical substances, including heavy metals, in fish, fish subproducts, and other fishery or aquaculture products. Article Seventh requires fishery farms and processing establishments to conduct laboratory tests for the substances cited in the resolution in ISO 17025-accredited laboratories and under the Ministerio de Salud's national toxic-residue monitoring plan.

The metals table does not name a metal-specific analytical method. The histamine section names HPLC for histamine, but that method statement is not generalized to the metal rows.

## Enforcement posture

The resolution is mandatory for natural and legal persons engaged in production, processing, and transport of fishery products. Article Fifth authorizes sanitary and legal actions when results show prohibited substances, chemical substances, or other findings that may pose consumer-health risk. Article Eighth assigns the Departamento de Proteccion de Alimentos responsibility for strict compliance.

## History of changes

Resolucion No. 368 was dated 15 May 2009 and promulgated in Gaceta Oficial Digital No. 26307 on 19 June 2009. Article Ninth repeals Resolucion No. 092 of 10 February 2005. Article Tenth states that the resolution takes effect from promulgation in the official gazette.

## How it compares

This is a fishery-specific contaminant schedule, broadly comparable to seafood contaminant limits in Costa Rica, Guyana, Mexico, EU, and Codex frameworks. Comparisons should preserve the product grouping and the source's unspeciated mercury basis.

The Panama cadmium table includes a crustacean row in addition to fish rows. The lead and mercury sections use separate product groupings and should not be inferred from the cadmium grouping.
