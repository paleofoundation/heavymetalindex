# Canada CFIA fertilizer and growing-media heavy-metal standards

## Scope
The loaded rows cover CFIA's fixed compliance-verification concentrations for growing media without directions for use. The same memorandum also describes application-rate formulas for fertilizers and supplements; those formula-based values are not fixed product ceilings and are gap-logged rather than loaded.

## Exact limit and units
| Metal | Matrix | Limit | Unit | Basis |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| tAs | Growing media without directions for use | 7.5 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |
| Cd | Growing media without directions for use | 2 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |
| Cr-total | Growing media without directions for use | 105 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |
| tHg | Growing media without directions for use | 0.5 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |
| Ni | Growing media without directions for use | 18 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |
| Pb | Growing media without directions for use | 50 | mg/kg | wet weight (as is) growing media |

## How tested
The memorandum requires proponents to provide analyses for the listed metals and states that the standards apply to total metal content, not extractable metal content.

## Enforcement posture
The standards are used by CFIA to evaluate products regulated under the Fertilizers Act where metal concerns have been raised. For growing media without use directions, CFIA applies a standard 45-year application-rate assumption to verify compliance.

## History of changes
The local copy is a 2024 CFIA trade memorandum. It states that the fixed growing-media values are used where no label application rate is available.

## How it compares
The CFIA wet-weight growing-media values are distinct from the CCME dry-weight comparison columns printed beside them and from the formula-based fertilizer examples elsewhere in the memorandum.
