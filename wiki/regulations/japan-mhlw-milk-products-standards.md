# Japan MHLW milk and milk-product standards

## Overview

This page records the local English translation of Japan's Ministerial Ordinance on Milk and Milk Products ingested as [[sources/mhlw2007-milk-products-standards]]. It is retained as a source-snapshot page because current-law status was not independently verified during this ingest.

## Scope

The ordinance covers definitions, compositional standards, manufacturing and storage conditions, testing methods, and container/package specifications for milk, milk products, and foods using milk as a principal ingredient.

## Exact limit and units

The local PDF gives compositional requirements for formulated milk powder, including milk solids content minimum 50.0% and moisture not more than 5.0%. Heavy-metal provisions found during ingest relate to container/package tests, including heavy-metals tests using 4% acetic acid elution and lead-standard color comparison. The source page does not extract a finished-food heavy-metal maximum level for infant formula.

## How tested

The ordinance includes detailed test procedures. Heavy-metals testing for relevant container/package sections uses 4% acetic acid elution and lead-standard color comparison; arsenic testing references the general Arsenic Test Method.

## Enforcement posture

This page is regulatory context only. Current legal use should verify against current Japanese MHLW text before application.

## History of changes

The source PDF is an English translation generated in 2007 of an ordinance originally dated 1951-12-27.

## How it compares

Comparison to Codex/EU/FDA/FSANZ formula contaminant limits is not performed here because the extracted heavy-metal provisions are packaging/material specifications rather than finished-food MLs.
