# Codex CXS 193-1995 - Tin Maximum Levels For Canned Foods

## Overview

Codex CXS 193-1995, the General Standard for Contaminants and Toxins in Food and Feed, sets international maximum levels for total tin in canned foods. The tin rows are relevant to HMI whenever a source page reports canned-food Sn migration or compares canned-food results against Codex.

## Selected Limits

| Commodity / product | Maximum level | Unit | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Canned foods other than beverages | 250 | mg/kg | The ML does not apply to non-tinplate canned cooked cured chopped meat, cooked cured ham, cooked cured pork shoulder, corned beef, and luncheon meat. |
| Canned beverages | 150 | mg/kg | Relevant Codex commodity standards include CXS 247-2005. |
| Cooked cured chopped meat in containers other than tinplate containers | 50 | mg/kg | Product-specific exception row. |
| Cooked cured ham in containers other than tinplate containers | 50 | mg/kg | Product-specific exception row. |
| Cooked cured pork shoulder in containers other than tinplate containers | 50 | mg/kg | Product-specific exception row. |
| Corned beef in containers other than tinplate containers | 50 | mg/kg | Product-specific exception row. |
| Luncheon meat in containers other than tinplate containers | 50 | mg/kg | Product-specific exception row. |

## How This Page Interacts With HMT&C

This is an external Codex comparator, not an HMT&C certification threshold. It is most useful for canned-food pages where the underlying source reports total Sn in mg/kg and where can lining, tinplate exposure, corrosion, storage time, or product acidity can explain variance.
