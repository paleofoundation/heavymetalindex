# Sorghum

Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) is a drought-tolerant cereal grain consumed as a staple across the Sahel, Horn of Africa, and parts of South Asia. The heavy-metals profile of sorghum is shaped by soil Cd uptake in semi-arid production zones, by Pb exposure on smallholder farms near roadways and mining-influenced soils in West and East Africa, and by occasional Cr findings from industrial-fertilizer use.
