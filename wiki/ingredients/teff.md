# Teff

Teff (Eragrostis tef) is a tiny-grained cereal endemic to the Ethiopian highlands and a staple food for an estimated 70 million people, primarily as the fermented flatbread injera. Teff is highly nutrient-dense and increasingly traded internationally as a gluten-free grain, which expands its heavy-metals significance beyond the Ethiopian domestic context. Teff's heavy-metals profile is shaped by Ethiopian soil composition, by Fe-rich fertilizer history (which can confound trace-element analysis), and by post-harvest processing in traditional injera production.
