# Plantain

Plantain (Musa paradisiaca) is a starchy cooking banana that is a primary calorie source across West and Central Africa (especially Cameroon, Uganda, and DRC), parts of the Caribbean, and northern South America. Plantain differs from dessert banana in being typically cooked before consumption and in having a longer growth cycle that increases soil-contact heavy-metals exposure. Production in mining-influenced regions of Latin America and in industrially-impacted parts of West Africa is the dominant Pb and Cd exposure context. This page is a routing-destination stub.
