## Reported measurements

Singhato and colleagues, Table 6, mg/kg fresh weight. Pacific white shrimp: moisture 76.1 ± 0.0, arsenic 1.7 ± 0.0, cadmium 0.1 ± 0.0, mercury not detected, lead 0.1 ± 0.1. Banana prawn: arsenic 1.8 ± 0.3, cadmium 0.1, mercury below the limit of quantification, lead 0.1 ± 0.1. Giant tiger prawn: arsenic 1.5 ± 0.2, cadmium 0.1, mercury not detected, lead 0.1 ± 0.1. The arsenic column is the paper's arsenic column, not inorganic arsenic. The not-detected footnote gives a limit of detection of 0.001 mg/kg. [[sources/singhato2025-thailand-seafood-heavy-metals]]

Luvonga and colleagues report wild-caught shrimp total arsenic of 1.025×10^4 ± 370 ng/g as received, also stated as 10.25 ± 0.37 ug/g, with inorganic arsenic below 0.005. Aquacultured shrimp total arsenic was 133 ± 11 ng/g, with inorganic arsenic below 0.005. Arsenobetaine was 92 percent of total arsenic in the wild-caught shrimp and 8 percent in the aquacultured shrimp. [[sources/luvonga2021-seafood-arsenic-speciation]]

Park and colleagues, a Korean seafood speciation table, report shrimp samples 1 to 6 with arsenocholine from 16.9 to 69.0 mg/kg. Arsenic(III) was not quantified. Arsenic(V) was not detected to 0.10 mg/kg. Arsenobetaine ranged from 0.18 to 0.94 mg/kg. Arsenocholine, arsenobetaine, and the methylated species are not inorganic arsenic. [[sources/park2005-korean-seafood-arsenic-speciation]]

Onyegeme-Okerenta and West report Penaeus monodon (shrimp) in mg/kg dry weight. Cadmium at Buguma was 3.62 mg/kg. Total chromium at Krakrama was 9.1 mg/kg. Lead at Buguma was 9.15 mg/kg. Total arsenic at Bonny was 0.01 mg/kg, the lowest total arsenic in that note. The authors cite an FAO/WHO cadmium figure of 0.5 mg/kg and an EU shellfish cadmium figure of 0.05 mg/kg, and an EU shellfish chromium figure of 0.5 mg/kg. Those are the authors' comparators. Inorganic arsenic was not measured. The note says inorganic arsenic was assumed as 3 percent of total arsenic for an intake calculation, which is not a measured inorganic arsenic concentration. [[sources/onyegeme-okerenta2023-shellfish-nigeria]]
