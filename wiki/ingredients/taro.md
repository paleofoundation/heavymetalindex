# Taro

Taro (Colocasia esculenta) is a starchy corm crop with deep cultural significance and dietary importance across Pacific Island nations, West Africa, parts of Southeast Asia, and southern Japan. Like cassava and yam, taro develops in sustained soil contact, exposing the corm to soil-solution heavy-metals uptake and surface-adhered contamination. Volcanic-soil production zones (Hawaii, parts of West Africa, parts of Southeast Asia) carry distinct trace-element baselines from continental-soil zones. This page is a routing-destination stub.
