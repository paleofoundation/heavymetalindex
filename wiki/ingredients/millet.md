# Millet

Millet refers to a family of small-grain cereals — pearl millet (Pennisetum glaucum), finger millet (Eleusine coracana, also known as ragi), foxtail millet, and proso millet — that together feed an estimated 500 million people across the Sahel, India, and parts of Southeast Asia. Millets are staple cereals in semi-arid zones where rice and wheat agriculture is marginal, and their heavy-metals profile is dominated by soil-uptake Cd and Pb in regions with industrial-fertilizer history.
