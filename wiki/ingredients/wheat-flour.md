## Reported measurements

The 2022 note reports nine Ethiopian wheat flour samples by flame atomic absorption spectrometry. Nickel was 0.154 to 0.854 mg/kg. Cadmium was 0.0411 to 0.216 mg/kg. The note says wet weight is assumed and the basis is not explicitly stated. Lead was not measured as a concentration (below the limit of detection of 0.043 mg/kg in all nine samples). The implications text says cadmium is below the authors' European Union comparator of 0.2 mg/kg for cereals but approaching or exceeding it, beside the range that reaches 0.216 mg/kg. This page repeats both and does not choose one. The note says a comparison was also made against FAO/WHO and Ethiopian Food, Medicine and Health Care Administration and Control Authority limits, and it does not print those limit numbers. Hazard quotients and the hazard index are risk indices, not flour concentrations, and are not repeated here. Iron, manganese, copper, and zinc are named in the analyte list, and the key numbers do not print their concentrations, so those concentrations are not repeated here. ## Wheat flour, cadmium, and occupational nickel

The flour note on this page reports nickel and cadmium in nine Ethiopian wheat flours, with lead below a stated detection limit. The gut studies below are rat cadmium chloride, mouse drinking-water cadmium, an occupational nickel cohort, and a five-day rat gavage that included nickel chloride and a hexavalent chromium salt. Milling is not a gut model. The chromium arm of the gavage is not a flour result, and this section does not treat it as total chromium in flour.

[[sources/liu2023-cadmium-microbiota-metabolome-rats|Liu et al. 2023]], per WikiBiome source summary, DOI 10.3389/fvets.2023.1219729, exposed Sprague-Dawley rats to cadmium chloride for 30 days. The note states lower intestinal ZO-1, higher TNF-alpha and IL-6 in intestine and blood, and Muribaculaceae in blood that was absent in the unexposed group. The note does not print the dose or the number of rats. The direction is cadmium toward gut and blood microbiota and barrier markers.

[[sources/zhang-2015-cadmium-hepatic-energy-gut-microbiome|Zhang et al. 2015]], per WikiBiome source summary, DOI 10.1021/acs.chemrestox.5b00237, gave mice 10 mg/L cadmium in drinking water for 10 weeks. Firmicutes and gamma-proteobacteria decreased in feces after 4 weeks, and Firmicutes decreased in cecum contents after 10 weeks. The direction is cadmium toward gut microbiota, alongside hepatic energy-metabolism changes in the same animals. The summary does not name a food.

[[sources/yang2023-nickel-gut-microbiome-uric-acid|Yang et al. 2023]] compared 109 occupationally nickel-exposed workers with matched controls. Serum nickel in the exposed group was 5.70 ± 3.21 µg/L, serum uric acid was 355.95 ± 67.87 µmol/L, and uric-acid-lowering bacteria, including Lactobacillus and related taxa, were lower. The direction is occupational nickel toward gut microbiota and then toward a host metabolite. Serum nickel is not a concentration in this ingredient.

[[sources/richardson2018-toxic-metals-rat-gut-microbiota|Richardson et al. 2018]], per WikiBiome source summary, DOI 10.1038/s41598-018-24931-w, gavaged Sprague-Dawley rats for 5 days with sodium arsenite, cadmium chloride, sodium dichromate, cobalt chloride, or nickel chloride, 5 rats per cohort. The note does not print the milligram doses. Observed OTUs and Shannon diversity were lower for arsenic, chromium, and nickel (p < 0.05). Differential genera numbered 37 for nickel, 17 for arsenic, 5 for cadmium, and 10 for chromium. Sodium dichromate is a hexavalent chromium salt, so the chromium arm is not a total-chromium result. The direction is short oral exposure toward the rat gut microbiota.

### What remains uncertain

No study cited here fed wheat flour. Liu and colleagues do not print the cadmium dose. Serum nickel is an occupational biomarker. The rat note does not print gavage doses, and its chromium salt is hexavalent, so it does not interpret a total-chromium cell. Lead on this page is a non-detect in the flour note and is not given a separate gut finding here.
