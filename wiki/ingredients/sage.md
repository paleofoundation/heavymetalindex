# Sage

## Overview

Sage is represented in the UAE retail-herb survey by Dghaim and colleagues. That study reports sage-specific measurements on a dry-weight basis; it does not establish a worldwide typical concentration. [[sources/dghaim2015-heavy-metals-uae-herbs]]

The population contamination profile remains pending. See [[ingredients/index]] for the ingredient inventory.
