## Tofu from Spain

Paz and colleagues measured aluminum, nickel, lead, and cadmium in tofu. The published article is Environmental Science and Pollution Research, 2021 (DOI 10.1007/s11356-021-13076-5). The digits below were read from the preprint printing of that article. Table 3 is mg/kg wet weight. n=130. Chromium is named in the aim and is not in the tofu columns copied here. [[sources/paz2021-tofu-potentially-toxic-elements]]

| Study and location | Design | Metal | Finding | Units and basis | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paz 2021, Spain | tofu; flavoured | aluminum | 6.71±5.65 mg/kg | mg/kg wet weight | 130 |
| Paz 2021, Spain | tofu; smoked | nickel | 0.38 mg/kg; text also says the lowest nickel is 0.18 in flavoured tofu, while the table prints flavoured 0.31±0.12 and homemade 0.18±0.15 | mg/kg wet weight | 130 |
| Paz 2021, Spain | tofu; Europe group | lead | table 0.03±0.01 mg/kg; results text 0.003 | mg/kg wet weight for the table | 130 |

Cadmium is in the same table. Arsenic, mercury, chromium, and tin: Data gap.
