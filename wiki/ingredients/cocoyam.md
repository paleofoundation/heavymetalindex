> Provisional scaffold. This page was created on 2026-10-22 so that ingested cocoyam (Colocasia esculenta) measurements have a routing destination. The contamination profile has not been populated, and the routed values have not been synthesized.

## Literature scope

Two studies from Ghanaian mining areas report lead, cadmium, total arsenic and total mercury in raw and cooked cocoyam [[sources/adjei-mensah2021-ghana-mining-towns-home-processing-cassava-plantain|1]] [[sources/fobi2025-kade-cassava-cocoyam-boiling-mercury-arsenic-cadmium|2]]. Their values are held in the source records and the evidence register until a synthesis pass covers this ingredient.

## What remains uncertain

Every cocoyam value in the current evidence comes from farms near Ghanaian mines and on mixed or unstated bases, so nothing on this page describes cocoyam in general. The contamination profile is pending until a synthesis pass reviews the routed evidence.
