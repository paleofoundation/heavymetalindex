## Bee pollen

İlhan, Bayır, and Aygün measured dried bee pollen from 20 colonies in four urban areas of Konya. Table 1 is mg/kg, printed as mean ± Sx. A discussion sentence prints bee pollen chromium as 0.75 mg/kg, which is not the table mean. Bee and honey columns are not copied. [[sources/ilhan2023-konya-bee-pollen-metals]]

Altunatmaz and colleagues measured 20 bee pollen samples produced in Turkey and 4 imported samples. The paper prints the unit as micrograms per gram of pollen. The abstract ranges for the Turkey samples match the Table 2 extremes. A combined-sample sentence prints lead up to 1.383 µg/g, and no table cell reaches that figure. [[sources/altunatmaz2017-turkey-bee-pollen-metals]]

Sattler and colleagues measured five dehydrated bee pollen samples from Rio Grande do Sul. Samples were dried at 42 C. Table 3 is mg/100 g. Chromium is in Table 2, also mg/100 g. A prose sentence gives chromium as 0.2 to 4.2 mg and attaches mg/100 g to the molybdenum clause. [[sources/sattler2016-bee-pollen-metals]]
