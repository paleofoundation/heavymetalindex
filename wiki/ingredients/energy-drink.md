## Retail energy drinks, Nigeria

Babayo and colleagues measured cobalt, chromium, cadmium, arsenic, nickel, and lead in 30 retail energy-drink samples in Nigeria (23 liquids and 7 powders). Liquids were measured by atomic absorption spectrophotometry and powders by X-ray fluorescence. The discussion states the results as mg/L for both forms. Sample codes are not brand names. Wet, dry, or as-sold basis beyond that mg/L header: Data gap. [[sources/babayo2026-heavy-metals-energy-drinks-nigeria]]

| Study and location | Design | Metal | Finding | Units and basis | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | 23 liquids and 7 powders | cobalt | 0.0017 to 0.0835 mg/L; EJ 0.0835, PR 0.0826, KR 0.0534 | mg/L as printed | 30 |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | same | chromium | MP 0.4159, KR 0.3764, ES 0.2501 mg/L | mg/L | 30 |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | same | cadmium | MP 0.0566, SD 0.0282, OR 0.0198, BH 0.0182 mg/L | mg/L | 30 |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | same | arsenic | EJ 0.0451, KR 0.0316, ES 0.0063, MP 0.0056 mg/L; not speciated | mg/L | 30 |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | same | nickel | 0.0015 (AR) to 0.1360 (PH) mg/L | mg/L | 30 |
| Babayo 2026, Nigeria retail | same | lead | EJ 0.2092, KR 0.1754, SY 0.1393, PE 0.1225, AL 0.0832, 3H 0.0825 mg/L | mg/L | 30 |
