       Abstract
       This is the frozen, version-pinned Heavy Metal Tested & Certified standard for baby cereals / grain
       products, dry (rice-based). Each per-analyte certification limit is the strictest heavy-metal maximum
       level set by any government in the world for this product class, expressed in the product's native basis
       (as_sold) and derived by lookup against sovereign law under HMTc Method v2.0 (government-limit
       default, 8:1 basis, read-across). The per-jurisdiction derivation below is the complete "show your
       work". This snapshot is immutable: any material change to a limit mints a new version with its own
       timestamped snapshot.

3    |    Method and provenance

HMTc Method v2.0 (government-limit default, 8:1 basis, read-across). The default standard for any (product,
analyte) is the strictest maximum level set by a credible government regulator, converted to the row's native
basis. Where the law itself splits a limit by formulation, the standard adopts the law's own split and cites it. When
both a species and its parent total carry numeric claims, the published species ceiling is min(species government
or read-across floor, parent-total limit), disclosed as a cap (iAs ≤ tAs, MeHg ≤ tHg, Cr-VI ≤ total Cr). A species
claim is never published above its parent. Read-across limits are disclosed as read-across and never presented
as direct governance.

The occurrence and toxicology corpus that contextualises these limits is maintained independently as the Heavy
Metal Index literature baseline (heavymetalindex.com). This certification standard cites that baseline one way;
it does not use the baseline's contents as the basis for any government-limit value stated here.
