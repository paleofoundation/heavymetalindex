## Scientific status

All 108 required questions have a scoped answer or explicit unfinished disposition. Zero are labelled completed question-level reviews. This is intentional: a source check or a useful operational inference does not satisfy the contract's full search, appraisal and review requirements.

The pilot is a purposive reuse of existing research, not a systematic search or a claim to Cochrane equivalence. FDA survey rows are recalculated. Existing primary-reconciled FDA TDS extraction is reused. Selected public original texts were inspected for the formula survey, rice interventions and dated EU provisions. EFSA's existing extraction is used as historical authority context and corroborated against its official overview. The mounted raw-PDF library was unavailable; TDS and the full EFSA opinion were not freshly re-extracted.

The review record distinguishes evidence verification, inference, scope limits, source families, appraisal still required, and AI authorship. No independent human review or outcome certainty grade is claimed. No event search is represented as complete. EU examples are explicitly from the 1 July 2025 consolidation, not a statement that all current amendments or worldwide instruments were checked.

A source correction was necessary: Limmer & Seyfferth 2024 is in *Environmental Geochemistry and Health*, DOI `10.1007/s10653-024-02116-x`. Its former journal/access URL and unqualified irrigation recommendation were inconsistent with the primary article. The original source record is also preserved. Other pages citing this source remain outside this pilot and need reassessment before broader release.

## Generator ownership

`data/evidence/syntheses/pilot-*-v1.json` contains the authored scoped answers. The compiler produces:

1. The three existing Markdown pages, with exact contract section order and stable question anchors.
2. The three page-question records in `data/evidence/questions/`.
3. Source-specific observations recalculated from the existing FDA registers.

Intervention records preserve comparator, species, matrix, scale, uncertainty, applicability and explicit unknown costs/mass balance. Tables are generated from these records. Scientific source content and input datasets are pinned; changes fail the build until dependent answers are reassessed. Cosmetic source-history changes do not produce a false scientific update.

Seventeen legacy page writers defer to the registered compiler owner for these three pages. This replaces their output with a functioning answer-driven compiler; it does not merely suppress generation. Other pages keep their existing path. Unknown contract adoption fails closed. Both ends of `prebuild` check the pilot for drift. The public presentation transformer also preserves v1 heading names/order, and the product status component recognizes partial review.

This is deliberately a frozen pilot, not a general-purpose library migration engine. Source-index expansion, new evidence, completed appraisals and subsequent versions need an explicit compiler/data update. Legacy completeness dashboards and lab comparator exports are not converted into question-level evidence assessments by this work.
