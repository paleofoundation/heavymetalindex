# Rice: evidence-informed scope and gap plan

Revision 2 · 18 October 2026. Revised following the user's request to develop the plan from substantially more of the collected evidence. This is an internal planning document. It expands the scientific scope before further article or library rollout. It does not designate the Rice page, source summaries or this exploratory map as a completed systematic review.


This revision develops the existing 24-theme/97-record map into a more concrete findings-led plan, adds organoarsenic transformations, integrated field programmes and exposure attribution, and names further source-accuracy holds. It preserves the linked local preview as the presentation baseline. The additional records are drawn from the existing collection, with exact canonical links; their inclusion here is a planning disposition, not acceptance into a quantitative synthesis.

## Decision and present evidence base

Develop Rice as an integrated critical reference spanning material identity, occurrence, mechanisms, interventions, exposure and operational decisions. The current nine-reference page remains a useful presentation pilot but is too narrow to determine the scientific content of the finished reference. Its question structure, prose treatment and evidence drawers can be retained; their current brevity is not a publication specification.

The work below uses a fresh inventory of the local source corpus, canonical source summaries and existing structured evidence. It develops themes from a much wider set of records and checks selected originals to expose planning requirements. It does not assume that every linked record is eligible, every stored summary is accurate, or the local corpus exhausts the literature.

## Examples of the deeper comparisons the manuscript should make

These examples demonstrate the expanded scientific content. Primary checks support the specific scope stated above; the cultivar and ash examples remain findings reported in stored summaries pending full-text verification. None is an outcome-level certainty assessment.

**Occurrence and comparability.** The FDA pilot describes cooked-food composites. Shi's global study describes 2,270 polished-market-rice samples from 32 countries, including 1,167 reused from earlier publications. It explicitly describes non-stratified sampling and half-detection-limit substitution. A useful synthesis must explain the differences in material, selection, period and censoring before comparing distributions. Adding both datasets to one undifferentiated rice range would erase the context that makes them useful. [Shi source record](../../wiki/sources/shi2020-rice-grain-cadmium-global-supply-chain.md)

**A missing species-specific evidence stream.** Brombach's exploratory study analyzed 87 European commercial rice products, including nine baby-rice products. Its reported methylmercury range was 0.11–6.45 µg/kg, whereas total mercury ranged from 0.53–11.1 µg/kg. These are different endpoints. A total-mercury-only FDA presentation cannot explain the species measurements, product subgroup uncertainty or differences from modeled grain-mercury estimates. [Brombach source record](../../wiki/sources/brombach2017-methylmercury-european-commercial-rice.md)

**Controls and competing outcomes.** Seyfferth reports lower grain As/Hg species and methane under drier management, alongside a 50–97% increase in grain Cd in the driest conditions. This broadens the required outcome set beyond the current selected irrigation example. The manuscript should compare designs, baselines and settings, including the separate Limmer experiment, and examine yield and micronutrients before developing a management implication. The result is not a universal irrigation optimum. [Seyfferth](../../wiki/sources/seyfferth2025-rice-water-management-as-hg-cd.md); [Limmer](../../wiki/sources/limmer2024-arsenic-cadmium-rice-irrigation.md)

**Different processing interventions.** Pogoson's laboratory sequence used two successive 12-hour chemical soaks followed by cooking in experiments on ten rice types. The reported reductions were 79% for Cd, 81% for inorganic As and 66% for DMA. This is a different process from excess-water cooking or percolation. An informative comparison must retain conditions, residual-food basis, uncertainty, nutrient/yield effects and implementation evidence rather than rank isolated percentages. [Pogoson](../../wiki/sources/pogoson2020-rice-cooking-cadmium-arsenic.md); [Carey](../../wiki/sources/carey2015-rice-arsenic-percolating-cooking.md)

**Genetics, growing conditions and evidence units.** The stored Liu preprint describes grain measurements across 208 cultivars, while the Inoue transporter work includes yeast-system experiments. Those records suggest an important cultivar and metal-transport chapter, but 208 cultivars are not 208 independent studies, and a transporter signal is not a commercial grain-performance result. Publication version, field transfer, essential-element uptake and other contaminants need explicit assessment. [Liu](../../wiki/sources/liu2022-south-china-rice-cd-as.md); [Inoue](../../wiki/sources/inoue-2024-rice-nramp5-cadmium.md)

**Useful null and multi-outcome results.** The stored Gupta field-trial summary describes approximately 20% greater yield with ash application and no statistically detected treatment effect on grain iAs, DMA or Cd. That finding adds context even though it is not a large reported contaminant reduction. Its uncertainty and design need appraisal before interpreting the null result. [Gupta](../../wiki/sources/gupta2023-rice-ash-bangladesh-arsenic-cadmium.md)

**Exposure and health evidence.** Rothenberg's cohort enrolled 391 children and analyzed 190 at 36 months, using maternal hair total mercury as the prenatal exposure indicator. The manuscript needs to examine attrition, exposure attribution, confounding and outcome estimates, rather than infer a health effect from a rice concentration table. Biomarkers, modeled intake, in-vitro digestion and child-development outcomes belong in distinct but connected evidence streams. [Rothenberg source record](../../wiki/sources/rothenberg2021-maternal-methylmercury-rice-child-neurodevelopment-china.md)

## Execution sequence

1. **Freeze the exploratory scope and reconcile records.** Preserve this inventory, source fingerprints, the original pilot baseline and known corrections. Produce exact study/material/outcome units and identify secondary reports, duplicate versions and reused samples. Correct clearly verified source errors through the existing canonical source and evidence workflow; leave an auditable correction trace.
2. **Specify the synthesis questions and protocol.** Use the 36 IDs to organize a smaller set of coherent synthesis questions with material, species, setting, comparator, population/route and outcome criteria. Specify eligibility, grouping, appraisal and synthesis before the formal review. This mapping is exploratory and retrospective; record it as such rather than presenting it as a prospectively registered protocol. Log later amendments and their reasons.
3. **Screen the complete candidate set, then search beyond it.** Give each of the 319 local candidates an auditable question-specific disposition: potentially/directly eligible, indirect/contextual, inapplicable, duplicate/report family or access-blocked. Retrieve originals and supplements automatically where available. Use existing reviews and primary references for citation chasing; execute reproducible bibliographic and agency searches suited to each question, with dates, syntax and language/access limits. A local-corpus inventory does not establish search completeness.
4. **Extract and reconcile the full eligible findings.** Reuse existing structured values after checking source locator, matrix, species, basis, statistic, unit, censoring and sample identity. Resolve CSV/JSONL/table disagreements. Capture null and adverse findings, replication, uncertainty, funding/COI and linked outcomes. Summary-only evidence remains usable within its limitations; do not require raw samples or a percentile to admit it.
5. **Appraise and synthesize by question.** Use occurrence/sampling/analytical appraisal for surveys, design-appropriate bias assessment for interventions, and environmental-health methods for exposure/outcome evidence. Compare compatible settings and show incompatibility transparently. Quantitative pooling needs a justified estimand, grouping, overlap resolution and sensitivity plan; otherwise use structured comparative synthesis. Do not average unlike matrices, species, markets or bases, or count a review and its component data twice.
6. **Write a scientifically substantial Rice manuscript.** Build cross-study explanations of convergence, disagreement and plausible effect modifiers, with scoped conclusions and uncertainty. Derive supplier, brand, retailer/legal and consumer implications from the same assessed evidence. Keep relevant derivative and finished-product detail in their existing destinations with parent-page synthesis. Build the evidence tables, methods and complete reference ledger together.
7. **Challenge the result before scaling.** Check whether material conclusions still hold when a prominent study is removed, when overlap is resolved, when non-detect treatment changes or when a different market is considered. Examine null/adverse results and plausible competing explanations. Compare coverage against the relevant authority reviews and included review bibliographies; document missing themes and their disposition. Arrange the declared scientific/methodological review without relabelling AI extraction as independent human review.
8. **Verify publication and updates.** Reconcile the manuscript with question/synthesis records, evidence rows, references and downloads; preserve the short sidebar, prose and accessible drawers. Tests establish arithmetic and implementation behavior. Scientific review establishes support and interpretation. Extend to other families only after the evidence-informed Rice example demonstrates the intended depth, with its remaining limitations explicit.

Routine retrieval, extraction, routing and consistency adjudication are agent work. User involvement is reserved for genuinely unavailable access, policy conflicts, consequential interpretation exceptions and the independent review/sign-off that is actually being claimed. Do not turn normal evidence sorting into a manual task for the user.

## Acceptance criteria

A satisfactory next manuscript accounts for the whole potentially relevant local set and the documented external search. It has no arbitrary reference quota or top-cited-source cap. It does not need to cite irrelevant records merely to raise its count. Every excluded or context-only candidate has a reason; inaccessible potentially eligible material remains visible in the assessment record.

Every material conclusion shows which exact findings support it, the main conflicting or null findings, the limits of applicability and the basis for certainty. The narrative explains why studies differ and what can still be concluded. Unsupported universal ranges, single-study recommendations, prestige labels and generic unfinished-review boilerplate cannot stand in for that work.

A reader should be able to answer: What was measured? In which rice material and setting? What agrees or disagrees across the evidence? What can change the result? What does an intervention accomplish and what can it worsen? Which actions can be justified for the reader's actual use case? What remains uncertain, and what specific evidence would resolve it?

Neither this exploratory map nor a successful implementation test establishes a completed systematic review, a current supplier ranking, a universal safe rice, or superiority to every other reference in existence. The aspiration is converted into inspectable coverage, critical synthesis, useful decisions and documented scientific review.

## Methods used to shape this plan

These intervention-review principles require adaptation to food occurrence and analytical questions.

Citing these methods does not mean HMI has already executed them or received endorsement.
