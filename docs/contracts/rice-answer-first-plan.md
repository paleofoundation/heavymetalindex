The approach follows Google's [contextual linking guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable), [Article structured-data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) and [AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). These support ordinary reader access and accurate interpretation, with no special AI schema or promised ranking outcome.
