### Comparator machine (owner instruction, 22 October 2026)

Explicit owner instruction: the comparison column must actually work on every ingredient page, the way it works on Rice.

1. **Every page builds Rice-grade references.** For each analyte × material × basis scope with extracted results, the ingredient's adapter recovers subgroup units and supplies reference definitions to `applyHmiBaselines`, following `hmi-baseline.md` exactly: subgroup medians, equal family weight, bounds rather than imputation, sensitivity and leave-one-out, frozen membership.
2. **References use every eligible unit.** A reference admits all compatible units from every ledger family in its scope. Leaving out a family or unit requires a recorded scientific reason. Hand-picking a subset, or using one study when the ledger holds more compatible families, fails.
3. **Single-family references only when that's all there is.** A reference built from one family is allowed only when the completed ledger shows it is the only compatible family for that scope. The reference's limitations must say so.
4. **Every row tries every comparison mode.** For each row, attempt in order:
   - the HMI rating
   - within-study direct contrasts (form, origin, year)
   - bounded classification

   Record the result of each attempt.
5. **A row may stay `unavailable` only for a terminal reason code:**
   - `no_numeric_value_or_bound`
   - `basis_incompatible_with_all_references`
   - `species_or_material_without_compatible_reference_after_full_ledger`
   - `bounds_span_categories`
   - `reference_below_display_minimum_after_full_ledger`

   Free-text "not yet admitted", "has not been assessed" or "pending" reasons block release.
6. **No coverage quota.** Every unavailable row needs a terminal reason, so honest gaps are allowed and lazy gaps are not.

## Lower-contamination sourcing comparisons

Owner clarification, 22 October 2026: ingredient pages must assess which regions, cultivars, product types or preparations appear lower in each decision-relevant analyte. Reporting contamination without examining supported alternatives leaves a sourcing question unanswered. Assess this explicitly for the contaminants driving the page's practical conclusions; do not require a separate public FAQ for every analyte or manufacture an overall cleanest-region winner.

When comparable observations support it, name the lowest observed group and useful alternatives, specifying the analyte/species, statistic, concentration, unit/basis, sampling period and comparison set. Give independent sample/lot counts and variability where available; unknowns stay explicit. Prefer within-study comparisons, while allowing compatible cross-study synthesis with a documented applicability rationale. Descriptive differences need not be statistically significant to be reported as descriptive, but must not be called established differences or consistent advantages without adequate support. Do not suppress a useful scoped finding merely because a universal ranking is impossible.

Separate three conclusions: **lowest observed in the stated comparison**, **tentatively lower with limited or inconsistent evidence**, and **supported sourcing preference**. A sample minimum, low group mean or single survey does not by itself establish that a whole region, cultivar or product type is reliably lower. Distinguish growing origin from purchase/collection market. Match material, preparation, species, units/basis, statistic and censoring; justify any conversions or harmonization. Non-detects are bounds, not measured zeroes, and different reporting limits can prevent ordering groups. Consider sample-frame differences, overlap, confounding and repeated publications of the same study before generalizing. Do not rank dry fruit against prepared/canned contents merely by converting mass units.

If a defensible comparison is unavailable, answer the sourcing question directly with the specific reason and the evidence needed to resolve it, such as origin-linked measurements of comparable finished preparations. Incomplete extraction or inaccessible sources remain unresolved work, not evidence that no lower-contamination option exists. Explain relevant other-analyte or nutrition/processing trade-offs before suggesting a preference: the group lowest in one metal need not be lowest in another. Lower concentration alone does not establish safety, compliance or lower exposure regardless of serving size.

Put a useful comparison and its main implication in Variation or Procurement, with citations to the measurements. If it materially changes the page's main advice, summarize it in the overview or Quick Read. A reader question such as “Which regions or types have the lowest measured nickel?” can introduce the detail as a supporting question, or as a general FAQ when it spans sections. Do not hide a decision-changing answer exclusively in FAQs or duplicate the full answer across sections. Retain the comparison, source locators, applicability and ranking limits in canonical synthesis records.

Useful observed differences are stated directly with their comparison scope; unsupported regional winners and generic “cannot rank” boilerplate fail this check.
- Paragraphs add distinct understanding and make a connected argument. The overview carries the general answer; questions or a table add useful detail without duplicating it. There is no filler added to match Rice's length.
- At matching desktop and phone widths beside Rice, the explanation has adequate substance and readable paragraphs. Where paired with questions, prose remains the main reading experience rather than a token introduction above a question wall. Assess content and rendered balance together, without measuring a fixed ratio.

A component-only pass, a citation-rich topic preview, a methods-only checklist, repeated boilerplate or a main conclusion available only inside a drawer or accordion fails this check. If source access or reconciliation prevents a supported explanation, record the unresolved work rather than inventing prose or declaring acceptance. Do not use this editorial check to imply completed scientific review.

Evidence integrity, scientific assessment, presentation acceptance and deployment are separate states. A useful evidence draft can fail presentation acceptance; a visually matching page can still have unfinished scientific assessment. Neither state alone authorizes deployment.

## Table cells carry only row-specific content (owner instruction, 22 October 2026)

Explicit owner instruction: a table cell shows only what is specific to that row. Information that is the same in every row of a table (for example a basis such as "Honey as sold", a unit, a period or a method shared by all rows) is redundant in the cells; state it once in that column's information icon. The comparison cell holds only the comparison button(s) and the information icon: no reference label (such as "vs scoped HMI baseline"), reference value, note or other text beside the buttons. The named comparator, reference value and method go inside the information icon and the evidence drawer. This applies to occurrence and mitigation explorers and every other ingredient table, and supersedes the earlier instruction to show the named comparator visibly in the cell. The presentation gate fails a page whose comparison cells contain anything besides the buttons and information icon, or whose occurrence rows all repeat the same row note.

## Thin pages ledger (owner instruction, 22 October 2026)

Explicit owner instruction: when the evidence in the HMI library is too thin for a full ingredient page, the page is recorded in a dedicated ledger of thin pages that evidence must be pulled for. Every ingredient build assesses this after its complete evidence ledger and writes `thin-page.json` in its own audit directory (status `adequate`, `thin` or `blocked`); `node tools/contracts/build-thin-pages-ledger.mjs` rolls these into `data/evidence/contracts/thin-pages-ledger.json`, whose `fetch_queue` is the single evidence-acquisition queue.

A page is **thin** when, after the complete ledger, any of these holds: fewer than three independent occurrence families for the ingredient (`few_occurrence_families`); a decision-relevant analyte has neither an eligible HMI reference (at least four units) nor a within-study comparison (`decision_analyte_without_comparison`); no lower-contamination comparison is possible for a decision-relevant analyte (`no_lower_contamination_comparison`); or eligible evidence exists but is access-blocked (`access_blocked_evidence`). A page is **blocked** when a required page input is missing, such as hero artwork or an unavailable primary text it cannot do without (`missing_required_input`).

Each thin or blocked record names concrete fetch targets: what to obtain, why it would change the page, where to look (agency monitoring programme, total diet study, dataset, publisher record or literature search terms) and a priority. A thin page may still publish when every gate passes, because it states its limits honestly; a blocked page may not publish until its missing input arrives. Thin status never authorizes padding, borrowed values from another ingredient, or invented comparisons.
