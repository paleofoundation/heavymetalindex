## Pre-submission hygiene (do before Day 1)

These changes amplify every subsequent request. Order matters.

1. **Add `noindex: true` frontmatter to all `wiki/batch-reports/*.md`, all unflagged `wiki/lint/*.md` (14 of 15), and `wiki/log.md`.** These are internal artifacts per CLAUDE.md Parts 8/17/18. Currently publishing and almost certainly dragging on the "Not indexed" count. After the change, resubmit `sitemap.xml` in GSC. Net effect: ~37 thin pages drop out of the index, sharpening the authority signal on the remainder.
2. **Resolve `/ingredients/carrot` vs `/ingredients/carrots`.** Pick canonical (probably `/carrot` singular to match `/cocoa`, `/rice`, `/oat`), add a 301 redirect from the other in `vercel.json` rewrites, then delete the redirected file.
3. **Resolve `/products/category-5-beverages` vs `/products/category-5-beverages-master`.** Likely the `-master` page is the canonical parent and the un-suffixed page is a stub or stale; verify, delete the loser, redirect.
4. **Verify same-slug cross-type pairs render distinctly.** `/ingredients/fish-containing-baby-foods` and `/products/fish-containing-baby-foods` are different by design (ingredient profile vs product category) but Google may flag near-duplicates if body content is too similar. Check; differentiate body content if needed.
5. **Confirm the metal hub-plus-speciation pages all self-canonical.** `metals/arsenic` + `metals/arsenic-inorganic` + `metals/arsenic-total` are intentionally three distinct pages per CLAUDE.md Part 14. Each needs `<link rel="canonical">` pointing to itself, and the hub page should clearly disambiguate to the two children. Same for mercury (3 pages), tin (3 pages), chromium (2 pages).
6. **Re-submit `sitemap.xml` in GSC after the above are merged.** Sitemap resubmission is free and triggers re-crawl prioritization.

## Sitewide amplifiers

Ranked by impact on the indexing problem.

1. **Noindex hygiene (highest impact, low effort).** Listed above. Eliminates ~37 thin pages from the sitemap. Probably explains a meaningful share of the 270 "Not indexed" count. Do this before Day 1.
2. **Add JSON-LD structured data to every wiki page.** Most-impactful authority signal you haven't pulled. Three schema types:
   - `Organization` on `/`, anchored to Paleo Foundation (Cyprus operator per CLAUDE.md Part 1).
   - `Article` or `TechArticle` on every metal/ingredient/product/regulation page, with `author`, `datePublished`, `dateModified` derived from frontmatter `updated` and git history.
   - `Dataset` on every `corpus/by-metal/*` and `corpus/by-product/*` page — Google's `Dataset` schema is specifically built for evidence aggregations and is heavily weighted for citation contexts.
   This is a Quartz template change (one file, applied site-wide). High leverage.
3. **Internal-link audit on hub pages.** Per Part 5b the routing layer maps sources↔products↔ingredients↔metals, but rendered pages may not surface all those edges as crawlable HTML links. Verify each hub page (`metals/index.md`, `ingredients/index.md`, etc.) renders an inline link to every leaf page. Currently 854 sitemap URLs but only 280 known to Google — Google may be hitting a discovery ceiling because internal links aren't fanning out.
4. **Resubmit sitemap in GSC after noindex hygiene.** Sitemap caching headers are set in `vercel.json` so the file refreshes on rebuild; GSC needs a manual nudge to re-fetch.
5. **Edge-level access control for `/workbench/` and `/staff`.** Per Part 19 these are noindexed but the meta-robots layer is the only barrier. Defense-in-depth: add Vercel middleware or password gating. Doesn't directly affect indexing but closes the only remaining leak path.
6. **Source-page discoverability.** The 451 source pages should not be manually submitted, but they should be reachable in ≤2 clicks from a hub page. Audit the metal-page "Sources" section rendering — if it's collapsing into a single citation link rather than fanning out to all contributing source pages, Google won't discover them.
7. **Date-prominent rendering.** Brand legal teams and journalists check "when was this updated?" Make sure `updated:` frontmatter renders visibly on each page (Quartz default does this; verify it survives custom templates).
8. **Robots.txt explicit Disallow for `/workbench/`, `/staff`.** Currently blanket `Allow: /` relying on per-page `noindex`. Belt-and-suspenders.
9. **Submission timing.** Manual requests work better when the page has changed recently. Batch submissions on the same days as content-update generator runs (per Part 7 standing initiatives in OPERATING.md).
10. **Track submission outcomes in a spreadsheet.** GSC's manual-request UI doesn't preserve history well. Maintain a simple CSV with URL, date-submitted, date-indexed (verify weekly), days-to-index. After a month this becomes evidence for what content types Google prioritizes for this domain — informs the next round.

## What would change the plan

If the "2 reasons" breakdown in GSC turns out to be something other than "Discovered / Crawled — currently not indexed":

- **"Duplicate without user-selected canonical"** (in any meaningful volume): treat as a canonical-tag issue first; the duplicate pairs called out above are the likely culprits. Fix and re-submit `sitemap.xml`; only then do manual requests.
- **"Alternate page with proper canonical tag"**: Google is choosing a different page as canonical. Audit which canonical Google is picking — likely the hub page is being chosen over the speciation child (or vice versa). May need to revisit Part 14 page structure or strengthen the speciation child's content.
- **"Soft 404"**: thin content. The stub pages flagged above are the likely cause. Populate or noindex.
- **"Excluded by 'noindex' tag"** in unexpected volume: check whether the `draft: true` filter or some frontmatter default is over-flagging pages.
- **"Page with redirect"**: 301 chains. Audit `vercel.json` rewrites.
