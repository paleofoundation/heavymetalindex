# Regulation layout evidence scope

Regulation pages use the shared five-asset placeholder system and PR #248 hero
geometry. The sidebar preserves the record’s existing jurisdiction, authority,
instrument status and effective date, separate from References document counts.

A regulation may supply up to three complete paragraphs from its own overview,
scope, testing, enforcement, limits or limitations only when its primary
`source_url` is linked in the body or an existing `source_refs` pin resolves to
an eligible source record. Pending records cannot supply facts. Qualifiers stay
attached, long paragraphs stay in the body, and no legal limit is generated or
recalculated. Tables and article text remain unchanged. Where provenance or
compact evidence is missing, the template supplies fewer facts without filler.
