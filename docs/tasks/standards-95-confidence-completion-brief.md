# What "95% confidence against v2 methodology" means here

Confidence is DEFINITIONAL for sovereign-ML, read-across, bounded, and baseline cells (governance v2.7, Rule #1): the number is the law, or a disclosed derivation from it. Lens agreement is a necessary condition for `confirmed`, never an estimate of 0.95; the manifest is never described as a statistical probability. The sentence the manifest must support under oath: "Every published limit is the number in a named sovereign instrument in force on the verification date, a disclosed protective derivation from such an instrument, a species bounded by its parent total, or a named-method LOQ baseline; each was re-derived blind from the register of record (commit X) and from the instrument text, and the agreement is machine-checked against a hash of the published cell." Lookups are worded "strictest among the instruments in the register of record as of <register commit>, jurisdiction set disclosed", never "strictest in the world".

The unit of verification is (compiled_dir, slug, variant, analyte).

Lens 0, deterministic, no model, every cell every run: audit C-checks via `--all --block`/`--dir=` against the CERT dirs; the hardened readiness cellState; panel-coherence (species ≤ total); the D-set: D1 explicit provenance tag from the canonical vocabulary; D2 register `basis` of every value-determining row (binding_id, source_id, components[].source_id) satisfies the record's `basis_guard`; D3 `subsumed` parent is firm; D4 baseline-screen contract (numeric LOQ + resolvable method); D5 read-across disclosure token present; D6 brand/firewall regex over all string fields; D7 FULL_PANEL in every variant; D8 recordless pages via the pages.json join; D9 a derive-strictest recomputation (min of same-analyte, same-basis-family, non-gated rows after roundDisplay, deduped by instrument) equals the published value; D10 in-force/supersession check on every cited instrument; the three hashes below. A cell failing Lens 0 never reaches a model.

M1, REGISTER-ROW VERIFICATION at ROW grain (~408 distinct value-determining rows, verified once, reused by every citing cell): two blind Fable 5.1 agents (effort high, fresh contexts, given only the row and the primary instrument text) each return derived_value, jurisdiction, scope, basis, in-force status, successor; disagreement or any cannot-verify → one xhigh deep-read; no majority vote over cannot-verify. Persist in `data/evidence/regulatory_limits_verification.jsonl` keyed by `regulatory_limit_id` + `row_sha256` over the row's columns. Primary text = the register `url` or `raw/`; a `wiki/regulations/*` page is Claude-written and is a pointer, not evidence. Rows with no primary text are `cannot-verify` until the page/url is backfilled from the primary document (regulatory fetch pathway); never lower the bar. Tier-1 cells (Pb, Cd, tAs, iAs, tHg, MeHg) require corroborated primary; Tier-2 may confirm on page-only with a flag. Primary-source verification runs once per INSTRUMENT (~151 distinct regulation pages, ~63 value-determining), not per cell.

M2, APPLICABILITY at RECORD grain (349 records), batched by `evidence_key = sha256(analyte + sorted anchor ids + basis_class + conversion)` so identical evidence is adjudicated once: two agents blind to the record's own label/substantiation/rationale/candidates, answering per cell: does the row's `product_scope` cover this product form on this basis; is the read-across the nearest same-basis sovereign instrument; child-protective check for cats 1, 2, 9, 10, 15, 16, 20, 21 (at least as protective as the strictest child-food/child-contact analog); baseline method fit. Agents return structured evidence (derived_value, derived_anchor_id, same_basis_candidates, blind_value BEFORE seeing the published value), never a bare verdict; the lint recomputes min(candidates) and refuses `confirmed` when it disagrees or when any stricter guard-passing non-gated row is missing from the list.

Directional rules (D2/C6 refinements): a species instrument may anchor a total cell ONLY as a disclosed protective read-across (species ≤ total by identity) carrying `species_direction: species-as-total-ceiling` and audit exception code C6d; a total instrument may never numerically anchor a species cell. Cross-basis anchoring is allowed only as a disclosed read-across in the protective direction with a `basis_bridge` field (e.g. a drinking-water or migration limit used as a content ceiling), forbidden in the loosening direction, and forbidden outright for release-basis records (Cat-9/21/23); a toy-migration row on a cosmetic/textile content cell is a D2 refute, not a declarable class. Each such cluster gets ONE decision record (`confidence/_decisions.jsonl`) fanned to its members (the 82 live total-Cr cells on the NHMRC Cr-VI row are one cluster), never 82 adjudications.
