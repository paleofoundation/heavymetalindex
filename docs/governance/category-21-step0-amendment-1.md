| 22 | Children's metal jewelry | `childrens-metal-jewelry` |

## Why it earns a row

Cheap children's metal jewelry is a leading Pb and Cd recall category (CPSC enforcement history;
cadmium substitution for lead in imported costume jewelry). It currently has only a provisional
scaffold (`childrens-jewelry`). It fits Cat 21's mouthing + handling exposure pathway and CPSIA/ASTM
jurisdiction without distorting scope.

## Scaffold resolved and cross-link

- `childrens-jewelry` → **row 22** (`childrens-metal-jewelry`).
- The existing `piercing-post-assemblies` page is the adult nickel-contact sibling. It stays where it
  is as a specialized nickel-contact node and **cross-links** to row 22; it does not merge.

## Boundary rules

- Children's metal jewelry marketed for ages 0-5 with a primary mouthing exposure may also implicate
  Category 9 (Infant Contact Products); route by marketed age and primary exposure, defaulting here for
  general children's costume jewelry.
- Adult body-piercing posts and adult jewelry route to the piercing-post / adult nodes, not here.

## Drift direction

Additive; scaffold only; no thresholds. Gives a leading Pb/Cd recall category a defensible home.
