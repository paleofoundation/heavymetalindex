## 2026-09-02, standards confidence pass (session 1)

1. **Precautionary provenance** (`OI-20260902-precautionary-provenance-tag`). The rationale tag `precautionary` (Part 19: a value the program sets below the strictest applicable regulatory ceiling, with documented rationale) enters the compiled cell vocabulary as `type: precautionary`. The compiler first resolves the honest Method v2.0 floor for the cell: a sovereign row, a disclosed read-across, or the parent total for a species. The subcategory's `precautionary[analyte] = { value, rationale }` then overlays the program number and keeps the floor on the cell as `sovereign_floor`. A precautionary value that is not below its floor is refused and the floor stands. This is how a ratcheting program keeps a protective number when the instrument that carried it is superseded (Israel MoH 2016) or when a wrong-basis anchor is corrected, without loosening a live limit and without citing a dead instrument. The gap between law and program is stated on the cell, never hidden (Part 2).
2. **Residual rows bind** (`OI-20260902-catch-all-rows-bind-program-wide`). A sovereign general-food residual row (Singapore Food Regulations "other food", Thailand MoPH 414/2020 "food other than the listed categories", Uruguay RBN "foods in general") is the law for every food the instrument does not list elsewhere and is therefore a direct maximum level in the government-limit lookup. A program number tighter than that floor is kept as precautionary.
3. **Group rows stay at the strictest member.** Where a published subcategory groups species or forms that the law governs at different levels (marine predatory fish, canned seafood with tuna, freshwater fish with tilapia, shellfish with bivalves, seaweed with wakame), the group value is the strictest member's level and the split is disclosed on the cell; no taxonomy change.
