# External-reviewer commissioning brief

## Why this document exists

The Heavy Metal Index is positioned as a Cochrane-level reference on heavy metals in food, consumer products, and the supply chain. Cochrane systematic reviews achieve their authority not from internal effort but from transparent external review against published methodology. The Index's current defensibility argument (CLAUDE.md Part 1) rests on the assertion that the wiki is independently auditable; to convert that assertion from claim to fact, the wiki commissions three independent reviewers from distinct stakeholder positions and publishes their findings alongside its own response.

This document is the brief Karen sends to each reviewer. It specifies what they are paid to do, what they receive access to, what they produce, and how the Index commits to publishing their findings.

## The three reviewer slots

The three slots are chosen to cover the three distinct hostile-reader positions the wiki must withstand. A reviewer in one slot is not a substitute for a reviewer in another; their pressures and reading habits are different.

**Slot 1 — Toxicology academic.** A practicing toxicologist with a publication record in heavy-metal toxicology, exposure assessment, or environmental health. The reviewer reads for scientific accuracy: dose-response framing, speciation handling, mechanism-of-action claims, vulnerable-population framing, the appropriateness of cited evidence for the claims made. Anticipated time commitment: 3 days of review. Anticipated honorarium: $3,000-5,000 depending on academic standing.

**Slot 2 — Brand regulatory-affairs veteran.** A senior regulatory affairs or quality-assurance professional with 10+ years of experience at a consumer-products company in food, infant nutrition, dietary supplements, cosmetics, or food-contact materials. The reviewer reads for operational usefulness: can a brand QA team actually use this wiki to make sourcing, formulation, or testing decisions, and does the wiki frame the regulatory landscape accurately enough that the reviewer would trust it in their own work. Anticipated time commitment: 2 days of review. Anticipated honorarium: $2,000-4,000.

**Slot 3 — Plaintiff-side class-action attorney.** An attorney with documented experience in consumer-products class actions involving heavy metals (baby food lead/cadmium/arsenic, infant formula contamination, cocoa cadmium, spice lead adulteration, cosmetics heavy metals). The reviewer reads for hostile-reader vulnerabilities: where the wiki could be cherry-picked, mischaracterized in court, or used to support a complaint against a certified brand. The plaintiff-side perspective is the one the wiki specifically needs to survive (per CLAUDE.md Part 1 strategic frame); the reviewer is paid to attack, not defend. Anticipated time commitment: 2 days of review. Anticipated honorarium: $3,000-6,000.

## What each reviewer receives

- Public read access to heavymetalindex.com.
- Methodology reference: [/methodology](https://heavymetalindex.com/methodology), [/coverage](https://heavymetalindex.com/coverage), [/audits](https://heavymetalindex.com/audits).
- The locked HMTc Comprehensive Testing Category Taxonomy v2.0 (March 30, 2026), provided as a reference document.
- This brief, naming what the reviewer is expected to produce.
- A direct contact line to Karen Pendergrass for clarification on methodology, scope, or specific pages flagged during review.
- A 60-day clock from contract signing to deliverable.

## What each reviewer produces

A written report of 5-15 pages structured as:

1. **Approach.** What the reviewer read, in what order, and with what reading frame. Reviewer chooses the sample.
2. **Findings.** Discrete numbered findings, each one categorized as:
    - **Major:** the finding undermines a defensibility claim the wiki makes about itself. The wiki must respond before the finding can be considered closed.
    - **Substantive:** the finding identifies a real defect that should be addressed but does not undermine the wiki's overall posture. The wiki commits to either fixing it or stating a rationale for not fixing.
    - **Minor:** the finding identifies an improvement opportunity. The wiki acknowledges and either fixes it or files it as a backlog item.
3. **Specific page-level findings.** For each page the reviewer audited in detail, a per-page audit. The reviewer may use the structure of their choice; suggested format is page URL, finding, severity, recommended action.
4. **Recommendations.** Up to ten ranked recommendations for the wiki's next-quarter work, in the reviewer's professional judgment.
5. **Conflict of interest disclosure.** The reviewer states any direct or indirect financial relationships with Paleo Foundation, HMT&C, brands that have been or might seek HMT&C certification, brands involved in heavy-metal class actions, or competing certification bodies. Industry consulting work elsewhere does not disqualify a reviewer; non-disclosure does.

## How the Index commits to publishing

Each reviewer report is published verbatim at heavymetalindex.com/external-reviews/<reviewer-slot>-<date> within 30 days of receipt. The wiki publishes its response alongside the report, finding-by-finding. The response either:

- States that the finding is accepted and links to the corrective commit/PR/page edit; or
- States that the finding is rejected and explains why; or
- States that the finding is accepted in principle and committed to a published backlog item with an expected delivery quarter.

The reviewer may request that financial or COI-sensitive content be redacted from the public version. Such redactions are noted in the published report with the section heading retained.

## What the reviewer is explicitly NOT asked to do

- Validate every numerical claim. The trace-every-claim audit is run automatically by `tools/audit-claim-provenance.mjs`; the reviewer's role is to spot-check the audit, not replicate it across 3,500+ claims.
- Settle methodological debates in heavy-metal toxicology. The reviewer's role is to flag where the wiki's choices are defensible or not, not to determine the "correct" answer on contested issues.
- Apply HMTc certification thresholds. Those are explicitly out of scope for the wiki's public surface (per CLAUDE.md Part 19, Part 2 firewall); reviewers should flag any rendered page that violates the firewall, but should not propose threshold values.

## What the reviewer IS explicitly asked to address

- **For the toxicology academic:** Whether the wiki's framing of vulnerable populations, dose-response, and speciation matches current scientific consensus. Whether the cited sources support the claims that cite them. Where the wiki is too cautious or not cautious enough on health endpoints.
- **For the brand regulatory-affairs veteran:** Whether a brand QA team could use this wiki to make a real sourcing or formulation decision. Whether the regulatory crosswalks are accurate and complete enough. Whether the mitigation pages are operationally actionable. Where the wiki is missing categories or rows that a brand RA reader would expect.
- **For the plaintiff-side class-action attorney:** Where the wiki could be cherry-picked for a complaint against a certified brand. Where the firewall between the wiki and HMTc certification is leaky in ways that could be exploited. Where regulatory limits are misframed in ways that overstate or understate certification claims. Which pages, if shown in court, would help or hurt the wiki's defensibility.

## Acknowledging the limits of what this review can establish

External peer review of three reviewers does not establish the wiki as Cochrane-equivalent on its own. Cochrane review depends on a sustained methodology, an established editorial board, and a multi-decade publication record. The Heavy Metal Index is in pre-launch; this external review is a calibration step, not a certification of equivalence. The review establishes that the wiki has been read by three independent professional readers with distinct stakeholder positions and has published their findings and responses. That is what converts the wiki from "self-published reference" to "peer-reviewed reference" in the sense that matters for brand legal and regulatory affairs use.

## Acceptance criteria for this brief itself

A reviewer reading this brief should be able to answer: what am I paid to do, what do I produce, how long does it take, how will my work be published, and what happens if my findings undermine the wiki's defensibility claim. If a reviewer cannot answer all five from this brief, the brief is incomplete and should be revised before sending.
