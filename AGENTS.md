### Ingredient evidence completeness (owner instruction, 22 October 2026)

Explicit owner instruction: every ingredient page is built from **all** of its eligible evidence, not a selection. "Partial synthesis", "illustrative sources", "targeted repair" and "explicit unknowns" are not release states for evidence that is in the library and readable. 1. **Evidence ledger.** Each ingredient keeps one ledger, `data/evidence/contracts/<ingredient>/evidence-ledger.json`, in the shared schema. Its universe is:
   - every `wiki/sources` record tagged to the ingredient or any of its aliases
   - every record whose title or body names the ingredient or its aliases
   - every row for the ingredient inside a multi-food dataset (total diet studies, monitoring programmes, national surveys)
2. **One terminal disposition per item, decided on content.** Allowed values:
   - `occurrence_extracted`
   - `mitigation_extracted`
   - `exposure_or_regulatory_used`
   - `context_only`
   - `out_of_scope`
   - `duplicate_of:<id>`
   - `access_blocked`

3. **`access_blocked` must be earned.** It requires a recorded retrieval attempt in the HMI Library (mounted, not only the backup), the raw folders and the publisher or open-access copy, plus a dated retrieval request. A missing file is access work to do, not an exemption.
4. **Complete extraction.** Every `occurrence_extracted` item contributes every reportable result for the ingredient, all of them rather than an example. That means each material, form, origin or market group, year, analyte and statistic, recorded under the occurrence-record rules already in force (species, basis, N, censoring, locator).
5. **Every result is in the table.** Every extracted occurrence result appears in the findings table as a row. Rows may be grouped or filtered but never silently dropped. The rendered row IDs must equal the ledger's extracted result IDs.

### Comparator machine (owner instruction, 22 October 2026)

Explicit owner instruction: the comparison column must actually work on every ingredient page, the way it works on Rice.

1. **Every page builds Rice-grade references.** For each analyte × material × basis scope with extracted results, the ingredient's adapter recovers subgroup units and supplies reference definitions to `applyHmiBaselines`, following `hmi-baseline.md` exactly: subgroup medians, equal family weight, bounds rather than imputation, sensitivity and leave-one-out, frozen membership.
2. **References use every eligible unit.** A reference admits all compatible units from every ledger family in its scope. Leaving out a family or unit requires a recorded scientific reason. Hand-picking a subset, or using one study when the ledger holds more compatible families, fails.
3. **Single-family references only when that's all there is.** A reference built from one family is allowed only when the completed ledger shows it is the only compatible family for that scope. The reference's limitations must say so.
4. **Every row tries every comparison mode.** For each row, attempt in order:
   - the HMI rating
   - within-study direct contrasts (form, origin, year)
   - bounded classification

   Record the result of each attempt.
5. **A row may stay `unavailable` only for a terminal reason code:**
   - `no_numeric_value_or_bound`
   - `basis_incompatible_with_all_references`
   - `species_or_material_without_compatible_reference_after_full_ledger`
   - `bounds_span_categories`
   - `reference_below_display_minimum_after_full_ledger`
   - `value_fails_source_integrity_check` (owner amendment, 22 Oct 2026): the value is shown but held because it cannot be reconciled with its own paper's method or figures; the specific conflict is recorded in the ledger and evidence drawer.

   Free-text "not yet admitted", "has not been assessed" or "pending" reasons block release.
6. **No coverage quota.** Every unavailable row needs a terminal reason, so honest gaps are allowed and lazy gaps are not.

Descriptive pools must remain jurisdiction- and market-aware. Record product row, formulation, basis, species, geography/market, time period, method quality, censoring, and inclusion/exclusion criteria. Stratify materially different markets; cross-market inclusion requires an auditable rationale. Unit or basis conversions must be explicit and logged. Source-reported descriptive percentiles are literature facts, carry no compliance weight, and are not certification thresholds.

Use the maintained pooling engine (`tools/evidence/build-hmtc-aggregate-distributions.mjs`) for any authorized descriptive pooling; do not write ad-hoc per-cell math. Its output lives in `data/evidence/hmtc_aggregate_distributions.csv` and `data/workbench/standards/`. Historical standards derivations remain staff-side and must never be promoted as live limits.

## Infant Formula Row Fit

For formula sources, "infant formula" alone is broad until powder/liquid and soy/non-soy status are resolved. But sources that explicitly describe the matrix as cow-milk, milk-based, powdered milk, milk formula, follow-up milk, or dietetic milk should route to the non-soy formula row unless the source states soy, plant protein, amino-acid-only, or another non-dairy formulation.

Keep specialty or dietetic milk formulas flagged as specialty/context when appropriate. Preserve group means, ranges, and maxima as summary evidence; any modelled distribution must be disclosed as modelled rather than presented as observed sample values. This row-fit rule does not weaken the species rule: total chromium still cannot substitute for Cr(VI), total arsenic for inorganic arsenic, or total mercury for methylmercury.

## Dataset Ingests

For datasets with many foods or ingredients, each routeable row or grouped summary must be mapped to destination wiki pages or recorded in a routing gap.

A dataset with 300 ingredient findings should produce 300 destination routes, updates, or explicit audit records, not merely one source page.

## Do Not

Do not silently drop source data because a destination page is missing.
Do not require individual studies to report any specific percentile.
Do not exclude summary-only sources merely for lacking sample-level values or percentiles. Preserve their statistics; any descriptive moment-matching expansion must be documented and must not set a live limit.
Do not end a commit with a bare `git commit` or `git commit -a`. Declare the files and commit by pathspec — `git commit -- <files>` or `tools/commit-scoped.sh -m "msg" -- <files>` — or FRANK's `git add -A` rides along into your commit. See OPERATING.md Part 5 ("Scoped commits").
Do not treat a source-reported or pooled occurrence percentile as a certification limit. Follow the LIVE government-limit method lock, including primary-text verification beyond the register and explicit native-basis conversion.
Do not silently pool samples across jurisdictions, markets, time periods, bases, product forms, or analyte species. If the benchmark target is jurisdiction-specific, document why each non-target-market source is included, excluded, or retained as context.
Do not write standards arithmetic (P-value computations, pooling math, sensitivity bands, candidate values) into `wiki/`. That content lives in `data/workbench/standards/` and on the local staff Standards Workbench tool. The wiki is the public literature evidence layer only.
Do not mix total arsenic with inorganic arsenic.
Do not mix total mercury with methylmercury.
Do not mix wet, dry, as-sold, and reconstituted bases without explicit conversion logged in the contribution log.
Do not promote pending-tier evidence into public claims.
