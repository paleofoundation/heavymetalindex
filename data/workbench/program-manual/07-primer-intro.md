# Team Primer

This is the short version. It is written for people joining or supporting the Heavy Metal Tested and Certified program who do not yet know how it works, and it aims to get everyone onto the same page in about fifteen minutes of reading.

It is deliberately brief. It tells you what the program is, walks the full path a brand travels from first inquiry to a certified mark, and puts the analytes, statuses, numbers, and terms you will hear most often on a single page. It is not the rulebook. Every rule here is stated in summary; the binding detail, the decision rules, the testing schedule, the enforcement and appeals machinery, and how the limits themselves are set, lives in the full Program Manual, which this primer points you to at the end.

This primer is for internal use. It contains no brand-identifying information and none should be added to it. Read it first; keep the full manual on hand for when you need the exact rule.
