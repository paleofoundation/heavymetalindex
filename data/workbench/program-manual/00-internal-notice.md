# Internal Use

This edition of the Heavy Metal Tested and Certified Program Manual is prepared for internal use by the Institute of Contaminant Standards and its operating personnel. It is the complete edition. It carries Part 6, Program Operations, which describes the entity structure, the wiki and certification firewall, the standards pipeline, staffing, and the internal governance workflow. Part 6 is not part of the public edition.

Nothing in this manual is brand-identifying. The program's firewall against brand-level data (described in Part 6.2) applies to this document as it applies to every other public and internal surface: no certificate holder, laboratory result, or applicant is named here, and none should be added. Subject to that single constraint, this edition may be shared freely within the organization and with counsel, advisers, and prospective governance members under the confidentiality terms of their engagement.

A public edition of this manual is generated from the same source with Part 6 and this notice removed. When a reader outside the organization asks for "the Program Manual," the public edition is the correct document to send. The two editions never diverge on a fact; the public edition is a strict subset.

The authoritative operating documents that this manual reproduces in summarized form, the Program Governance Policy and the Lot Testing Schedule, are under separate version control. Where this manual and a controlled operating document disagree, the controlled document governs for operational purposes, and the Program Operator issues a Technical Clarification to resolve the discrepancy.
