# Where to Go for More

This primer covers the shape of the program. When you need the exact rule, the full Program Manual is the reference, and it is organized so you can go straight to what you need.

- **How a product gets certified, in binding detail.** Program Manual Part 2: the principles, the analyte panel and tiers, the three-lot baseline, the five statuses, public-facing and private certification, the readiness and corrective-action tracks, the mark rules, and the decision rules.
- **The testing and surveillance schedule.** Part 4: risk tiers, how often each status is tested, the reflex triggers, speciation, sampling, and chain of custody.
- **How the limits themselves are set.** Part 3.1: the government-maximum default and the disclosed occurrence calibration beneath it, the publication gates, and the rationale tags.
- **Why the program holds up under challenge.** Part 5: the methodology defense, the program-level adversarial read, the recognition landscape, and the governance structure.
- **How the program is organized and run.** The internal edition of the manual carries Part 6, Program Operations: the entity structure, the firewall, the standards pipeline, the roles, and the governance workflow.

If you read one thing after this primer, read Part 2. It is the operational constitution, and it answers almost every "what happens if" question a brand or a colleague will ask you.
