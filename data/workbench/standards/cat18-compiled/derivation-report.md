# HMTc Category 18 (Pet Supplements) — standard derivation report
Method: HMTc Method v2.0 — government-limit default. Every cell is the strictest applicable sovereign
maximum for this exact product form in its native basis, a disclosed read-across where the exact cell
is unregulated, a substantiated not-material determination, or a control. No cell is occurrence-derived.
Why the anchor family is child-protective rather than adult-supplement. The nearest same-form sovereign analog for a pet supplement is a human food supplement, and several governments do set maxima for those. HMTc does not use them, for the same reason Category 16 does not: they are an adult-intake framework, they run one to three orders of magnitude above the child-food ceilings, and a companion animal is a small-bodied consumer receiving a concentrated dose. A four-kilogram cat given a one-gram daily supplement scoop, or a five-kilogram dog given a soft chew, sits in a body-weight-normalised intake range comparable to an infant on formula and far above a seventy-kilogram adult. That is the disclosed rationale for which analog family HMTc adopts; it is not a calculation and it does not select any number. Every number still comes from a named sovereign instrument in the register, and the instrument, the limb of the instrument, and the floors HMTc declined are all named on the cell.

Why the numbers differ from HMTc Category 16 in three cells, and why two earlier divergences are now closed. Category 16 anchors its numeric cells on the liquid or ready-to-eat limb of form-split instruments and applies those values to as-sold solid supplements. Category 18 cannot do that: the register classifies those limbs as_consumed, the 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula, and Method v2.0 step 4 requires preserving the product-form split the government rule itself writes. Two of the earlier divergences are now closed, and closed by finding a stricter as-sold instrument rather than by abandoning the basis rule. Cadmium and total mercury are anchored on Israel's food-for-special-dietary-purpose limb (il_fcs2016_cd_15_food_for_special_dietary_purpose_5 at 5 ug/kg, il_fcs2016_thg_16_food_for_special_dietary_purpose_4 at 4 ug/kg), which is not form-split and which matches Category 16's published values exactly; lead was already equal at 10 ug/kg and is now anchored on the same limb (il_fcs2016_pb_16_food_for_special_dietary_purpose_10). Three divergences remain, every one of them in the lax direction, and each is named here and again on its own cell. Inorganic arsenic: Category 16 reads eu2023_ias_formula_liquid_10 across at 10 ug/kg; Category 18 takes the powder limb eu2023_ias_formula_powder_20 at 20 ug/kg. Nickel: Category 16 reads eu2024_ni_formula_liquid_100 across at 100 ug/kg; Category 18 takes eu2024_ni_formula_powder_250 at 250 ug/kg. Aluminium: Category 16 reads aunz_sch19_al_formula_nonsoy_500 across at 500 ug/kg, describing it there as the only government aluminium maximum level for any food in any jurisdiction, while Category 18 declines that row because the register classifies it as_consumed ('portion ordinarily consumed') and no permitted conversion makes it comparable, and publishes a control with no ceiling at all — so a pet supplement certified here receives no numeric aluminium ceiling where a human supplement certified under Category 16 receives 500 ug/kg. In all three, the Category 18 basis reasoning is the more rigorous of the two and the Category 16 cell is the side performing an undisclosed as-consumed to as-sold read; the reconciliation is logged against Category 16 and is not performed from inside this config. The list is exhaustive and checkable cell by cell against the compiled records, and it names the direction of each difference rather than only its existence.

Why Israel's food-for-special-dietary-purpose limb anchors lead, cadmium and total mercury. Israel's FCS-2016 metal-contaminant table carries three limbs that could reach a purpose-formulated product: the infant and toddler formula limbs, the 'food for special dietary purpose' limb (Pb row 16 at 10, Cd row 15 at 5, tHg row 16 at 4), and a separate 'dietary supplements' limb (Pb row 17 at 3000, Cd row 16 at 1000, tHg row 15 at 100). The supplement limb is declined for the reason Category 16 declines every adult-supplement floor. That leaves the formula limbs and the special-dietary-purpose limb, both inside this category's declared analog family, and Method v2.0 step 3 then requires the strictest applicable of them, which is the special-dietary-purpose limb in all three cells. The determination is stated rather than assumed: HMTc is not applying Israel's own classification of a supplement, which would be the 3000/1000/100 limb, but Israel's strictest formulated-food ceiling, to a product Israel does not regulate at all. The full argument, with every declined limb named, sits on each of the three cells.

The two applicability filters, in order. The first is mechanical: only register rows the register itself classifies basis_class=as_sold may anchor a cell, which blocks every reconstituted and feed-basis row without any judgment being exercised. The second is declared and uniform: the anchor must come from the adopted analog family, which is finished foods intended for a small consumer. The second filter is needed because basis_class records whether a limit is written on the product as sold, not how much water the commodity contains, so a fresh-vegetable or fluid-milk ceiling can pass the mechanical filter and still be looser than it looks against an as-sold dry concentrate. The widened stricter-unselected audit scan is pointed at exactly those commodity families so that every such row surfaces in the derivation report with its disposition attached, rather than being quietly out of scope. The audit scope is now identical on all seven subcategories, so the scan is the same scan on every row and a disposition written on one row is true of that row rather than of the category in the abstract. It surfaces exactly one entry across the whole category: il_fcs2016_ias_1_milk_10, the Israeli fluid-milk inorganic-arsenic ceiling, on the inorganic-arsenic cell of all seven subcategories. It is not adopted. Every other row the earlier version of this category surfaced — hk_cap132v_thg_vegetables-other-than-edible-fungi_10, hk_cap132v_thg_milk_10, hk_cap132v_thg_secondary-milk-products_10, il_fcs2016_thg_1_milk_10 and il_fcs2016_cd_1_milk_5 — sits at or above the values now published for cadmium and total mercury and is therefore no longer stricter than any binding. A separate sweep is run outside this compiler with no scope regex, no infant exclusion and no reliance on basis_class tagging, counting a row as as-sold if its basis_class is as_sold or if its free-text basis carries the register's as-sold wording; it returns four further rows stricter than a binding in this category, and all four are packaged drinking-water ceilings: il_fcs2016_cd_17_bottled_drinking_water_and_mineral_water_3, il_fcs2016_tas_16_1_bottled_drinking_water_and_mineral_water_10, il_fcs2016_ias_16_1_bottled_drinking_water_and_mineral_water_6 and il_fcs2016_thg_17_bottled_drinking_water_and_mineral_water_1. Water is a different matrix consumed in litres per day rather than grams per dose, this category does not read a drinking-water ceiling across to a dosed concentrate, and each is named on its own cell. That is the whole set. The claim is deliberately stated in a form that can be falsified by re-running the sweep, rather than in a form that silently depends on a register tagging field being complete — the earlier version of this sentence rested on exactly that dependency and was wrong because of it.

Why all seven rows carry the same numbers. The adopted anchor family is a finished-child-food ceiling, which is defined by the consumer and the basis rather than by the ingredient, so it does not vary between a vitamin powder and a botanical blend. Category 16 publishes the same uniform shape for the same reason. The rows differ where the evidence actually differs: in the methylmercury posture (every-lot speciation where marine or freshwater material is routine, reflex speciation otherwise), in the hexavalent-chromium posture (every-lot speciation trigger where mineral, clay or geologic carriers are declared, and a lead-and-chromium co-elevation adulteration trigger on the botanical row), in the total-chromium rationale (chromium is a declared nutrient in the vitamin and mineral row and a contaminant everywhere else), and in the tin escalation rule. Occurrence history is recorded in the scope notes because it drives sampling posture; it never moves a ceiling.
Native basis family: as sold — the supplement as packaged and dosed (dry weight for powders, chews, tablets and capsules; neat liquid for oils and drops). No reconstitution, dilution, or as-fed conversion is performed anywhere in this category..
Credible-regulator set: EU, UK, CODEX, international, US, AU-NZ, CA, HK, IN, IL.
Credible-set note: The default credible-regulator set is widened by two national food-safety authorities, each because it is the only sovereign that regulates a cell the default set leaves empty, and in each case the addition makes the standard stricter rather than more convenient. India (FSSAI, Food Safety and Standards Regulations, Metal Contaminants) supplies the only as-sold child-food total-arsenic maximum and the only as-sold non-canned child-food tin maximum; no EU, UK, Codex, US, Canadian or FSANZ instrument sets either. Israel (Ministry of Health, Food Control Services, FCS-2016) supplies the only as-sold child-food total-mercury maximum in any jurisdiction — the EU, UK, Codex, US, Canadian and FSANZ child-food instruments set no mercury maximum at all — and its 'food for special dietary purpose' limb additionally supplies the strictest as-sold sovereign lead, cadmium and total-mercury ceilings on the register for a purpose-formulated finished food, which is why Israel now anchors three of this category's seven numeric cells rather than one. Each of those three adoptions moves the published value down or holds it level; none makes the standard more convenient. Both are binding national maximum levels enforced by a sovereign food authority, both are already relied on by the live HMTc human-supplement standard (Category 16), and Israel's FCS-2016 is already relied on by the live Category 25 standard. Widening the set also widens this compiler's stricter-unselected audit scan to Indian and Israeli rows, so the addition increases audit coverage as well as strictness.
