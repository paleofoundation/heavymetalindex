## Discrepancies and notes on the old rows

- cn_pb_liquid-ready-to-feed-infant-formula_10: the 10 ug/kg value is not printed in the 2025 table; it is derived from the 0.08 mg/kg solid-product limit via footnote c (8:1). Footnote c is present unchanged in GB 2762-2025 (PDF p.6), so the derived value stands. Successor china_gb2762_2025_pb_infant_formula_liquid_8to1_10 is flagged derived.
- cn_pb_complementary-supplementary-foods-for-in_200: the old row carried basis_class as_consumed and basis "as consumed" while its own note said "as placed on market; no reconstitution basis stated". The table states no reconstitution basis, so the successor is as_sold / product as placed on market.
- china_gb2762_crtotal_grains_1000: the old scope ("grains and grain products including cereals and milled grain products") spans two Table 7 lines (谷物 and 谷物碾磨加工品, both 1.0 mg/kg). The successor id is assigned to the cereals line; china_gb2762_2025_crtotal_milled_cereal_products_1000 continues the milled half.
- china_gb2762_cd_beans_legumes_200 and china_gb2762_crtotal_beans_legumes_1000: the old scopes said "beans and bean products (legumes, pulses)"; the Table 2 and Table 7 lines are 豆类 only (dried pulses and their flours per Annex A). Bean products carry no cadmium or chromium limit in this standard. Values unchanged; scope descriptions corrected on the successors.
- china_gb2762_cr_milk_powder_2000 and china_gb2762_cr_liquid_milk_300 used metal_species "Cr" while cn_crtotal_milk-powder-and-modified-milk-powder-gen_2000 used "Cr-total" for the same Table 7 line. Successors use "Cr-total" (register-dominant token, 44 rows vs 6). Downstream configs that reference the old ids by source_id (tools/standards/cat1-config.json, cat17-config.json, cat30-config.json) still resolve but now point at superseded rows and should be re-pointed at the successors.
- china_gb2762_pb_spices_1500: the old scope rendered 花椒 as "peppercorn"; it is Sichuan pepper (Zanthoxylum). Corrected on the successor.
- Three old-row pairs map to one 2025 line each and received one successor: (cn_pb_formulas-for-special-medical-purposes-fs_150, cn_pb_fsmp-1-10yr_150); (china_gb2762_tas_milk_powder_500, cn_tas_milk-powder-and-modified-milk-powder-gen_500); (china_gb2762_cr_milk_powder_2000, cn_crtotal_milk-powder-and-modified-milk-powder-gen_2000).
- No official source URL for the 2025 PDF is recorded in raw/regulatory/_manifest-reg-all.csv, so the url column of every 2025 row is blank and the raw path is cited in notes.
- Old rows point at regulation pages regulations/cn-infant-food-contaminants and regulations/cn-gb2762-2022 as well as regulations/china-gb-2762-2022-contaminants; only the last exists as a wiki page. Not changed here (out of scope).
