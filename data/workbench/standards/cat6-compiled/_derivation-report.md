Edible dried seaweed/kelp food products (nori, wakame, kombu, dulse) sold as dried, on a DRY-WEIGHT basis (mg/kg DM) — these numbers must never be numerically compared against the wet-weight animal-seafood ppb. Cadmium (500) and total mercury (100) bind the French CSHPF edible-seaweed maxima (confirmed tightenings from 2000 and 2500). Lead 5000 and total arsenic 5000 bind the Israel dried-seaweed lines; inorganic arsenic 1000 binds FSANZ. Nickel binds EU 2024/1987 seaweed 30000; tin binds the French CSHPF seaweed 5000. MeHg and Cr-VI are not-material (speciation-bounded); aluminium and total chromium are screened (no on-scope sovereign ML). Seaweed used as a dietary supplement (>=80% dried seaweed) routes to the supplements category, which carries the separate 3000 cadmium supplement line.
