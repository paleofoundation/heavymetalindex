# Defensible standards status — iteration 1 (2026-06-16)

Honest accounting of what can be defended today vs what is held. Generated from
`standards-recovery-worklist.csv` + `standards-outlier-adjudication.csv` after the
engine corrections, the seaweed/fish recoveries, and the Cat 1 recovery iteration 1.

## The four buckets (49 locked material cells)

- **FIRM — 23 cells.** Pool has ≥3 distinct sources, sensitivity acceptable, no outlier
  flag. These are defensible standards now (still living — more in-house evidence is
  recoverable for every cell, which can only sharpen them).
- **THIN-provisional — 12 cells.** 1–2 sources; emitted as honestly-graded provisional
  standards (HMTc regulates the under-studied; the value ratchets as data accumulates).
- **PROVISIONAL-PENDING — 8 cells, HELD.** Outlier-tail flagged (a single high source may
  dominate, or the matrix is genuinely bimodal). Render "adjudication pending", never a
  firm value, until reviewed: chocolate/Pb, fish-freshwater/Pb, olive-oil/Ni, olive-oil/tAs,
  shellfish/Cd, spices/Cd, spices/Pb, vinegar/Pb. Plus infant-formula/tAs + /tHg (demir2023
  Turkish-market elevation). spices Pb/Cd especially (p97/p50 = 8212×/4866×) likely need a
  clean/dirty split (turmeric lead-adulteration is a real, separable population).
- **BLOCKED/insufficient — 6 cells, UNTOUCHED.** Cereals Pb/Cd/iAs/tAs (no category3 summary
  exists; needs PDF extraction) and fish-freshwater/Hg. Per policy: do not touch what cannot
  be built defensibly.

## What this means

Cat 6 Seafood and the specialty categories carry mostly FIRM + THIN-provisional cells with a
handful held PENDING — they are publishable as **iteration 1** standards with honest grading,
NOT as final firm thresholds. The Cat 1 flagship still rests on incomplete pools (recovery in
progress) and stays on its existing published edition until recovered + adjudicated.

## The campaign (what completes this)

1. Recover the in-house stranded evidence per the worklist (every cell has more available;
