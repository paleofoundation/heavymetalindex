# HMTc Category 4 (Fruits, Vegetables, and Produce) — standard derivation report
Method: HMTc Method v2.0 — government-limit default. Every cell is the strictest applicable sovereign
maximum for this exact product form in its native basis, a disclosed read-across where the exact cell
is unregulated, a substantiated not-material determination, or a control. No cell is occurrence-derived.
CELL RESOLUTION RULE, applied uniformly across all eight rows. (R1) Where a credible sovereign government writes a maximum level for this product form in this row's native basis, the cell is the strictest such value and is tagged regulatory-alignment. (R2) Where the exact cell is unregulated but a credible sovereign writes a maximum level for the SAME commodity in an adjacent form or basis, the cell is a disclosed read-across from that named row, taken at face value only where face value is the conservative (stricter) direction. (R3) Where no sovereign instrument reaches the commodity at all, the cell is a CONTROL: testing is mandatory, an escalation rule is published, and no number is invented. Aluminium is a control in all eight rows because no jurisdiction regulates aluminium as a produce contaminant. Hexavalent chromium is a substantiated not-material determination wherever a total-chromium parent is published for the row and a control wherever it is not; it is never a published number. Methylmercury is a CONTROL in all eight rows, bounded by identity below each row's published total-mercury ceiling (methylmercury cannot exceed total mercury), and it is never a published number. No sovereign government writes a methylmercury maximum level for any terrestrial plant food, but the routed corpus contains quantified methylmercury in edible plant and fungal tissue (wang2021-houttuynia-mercury-china in leaf and root tissue of an edible leafy vegetable; stasinos2014-heavy-metals-tubers-review in root and tuber tissue; asensio2023-madrid-mushrooms-mercury), so the species is not absent from produce and no not-material determination is supportable anywhere in this category. Control is the fail-safe treatment and is applied uniformly: a fresh commodity and its dried, manufactured and pickled derivatives are treated alike, so no row is granted a weaker determination than the fresh row it derives from. TAXONOMY LUMPING IS DISCLOSED, NOT SILENTLY RESOLVED: several sovereign instruments split classes that this taxonomy carries as a single row (fruiting versus bulb versus brassica versus legume vegetables inside non-root-vegetables; temperate versus tropical roots and tubers inside root-tuber-vegetables; table olives versus pickled cucumbers versus fermented brassicas inside pickled-fermented-vegetables; pome, stone, citrus and berry fruit inside fresh-fruit). Because one published HMTc value must be met by every product in the row, each such row binds the strictest value applicable to any member, which is materially stricter than law for the looser members. Every collapsed split is named in that row's scope_note, and the recommended taxonomy splits are recorded as outstanding items rather than resolved by picking a looser residual-class value, which would let HMTc certify a lot that is unlawful in a market of sale.
Native basis family: dietary as-sold; fresh and frozen produce and drained pickled product on a wet-weight edible-portion basis, dried fruit and manufactured fruit snacks on a dry or concentrated as-offered basis that is never conflated with the fresh wet-weight maximum levels.
Credible-regulator set: EU, UK, CODEX, international, US, AU-NZ, CA, HK, CH, MERCOSUR, PY, IL.
Credible-set note: The default credible-regulator set is widened by four members for this category, each of which contributes a comprehensive per-commodity produce contaminant schedule rather than a single opportune entry, and each of which is already treated as credible by the live Category 27, 28 and 29 plant-commodity compilers. Switzerland (VHK 817.022.15, 218 register rows) is an EU-aligned metal and metalloid annex whose per-class produce values mirror EU Reg. 2023/915 and EU Reg. 2024/1987 and which supplies the per-class cadmium and nickel rows that are absent from the register's EU coverage. MERCOSUR (GMC Res. 12/2011, 159 rows) is a supranational bloc instrument with a complete produce schedule for lead, cadmium and total arsenic on a product-as-offered-to-consumer basis, including the register's only dried-fruit maximum levels and its only total-arsenic table-olive row; Paraguay (Decreto 1335/2019, 159 rows) is its national enactment at identical values and is admitted so the audit scan sees the national instrument as well as the bloc resolution. MERCOSUR is not, however, the register's only table-olive source: Hong Kong Cap. 132V supplies the table-olive lead row (400 ug/kg) and Switzerland VHK 817.022.15 the table-olive cadmium row (20 ug/kg), and this config names both as anchors in the pickled-fermented-vegetables row; further table-olive rows exist from GSO, Thailand, Colombia and India and sit outside the admitted set. Israel (MOH FCS-2016, 166 rows) supplies the only inorganic-arsenic maximum level any credible government writes for fruits and vegetables and the only total-mercury and inorganic-arsenic rows for dried fruit. Every anchor in this config remains inside this set, and the audit scan runs across all twelve members. Regulators present in the register but NOT admitted here (China, Thailand, India, Vietnam, Singapore, GSO, Colombia, Panama, Uruguay, Trinidad and Tobago, Philippines, Morocco, Fiji) are not a judgment about their credibility; admitting them is a separate governance decision, and China GB 2762 in particular has no produce rows in the register at all (recorded as a register gap).
