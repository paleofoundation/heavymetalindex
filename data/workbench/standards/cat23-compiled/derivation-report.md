# HMTc Category 23 (Food-Contact Consumer Goods and Kitchenware) — standard derivation report
Method: HMTc Method v2.0 — government-limit default. Every cell is the strictest applicable sovereign
maximum for this exact product form in its native basis, a disclosed read-across where the exact cell
is unregulated, a substantiated not-material determination, or a control. No cell is occurrence-derived.
TWO CATEGORY RULES govern every row. (1) THE MATERIAL-FAMILY RULE: a row's published ceiling is anchored only where a sovereign instrument governs element migration into food from that row's PRINCIPAL food-contact material family. Polymer surfaces anchor EU 10/2011 Annex II directly, or by disclosed read-across where the instrument expressly excludes the polymer (silicone under Art. 2(2)(c), ion-exchange resin under Art. 2(2)(a)) or does not reach it (cured liquid-applied coatings, paper and board, wood). Metal, alloy, ceramic-glaze, vitreous-enamel and glass surfaces have no sovereign migration ceiling in the register, so those rows are controls with mandatory testing and no invented number. (2) THE COMPONENT RULE: every food-contact COMPONENT of an article is held to the regime of its own material family, and where a component's family has no sovereign ceiling that component is under control. A row's published cells state the ceiling for its principal food-contact material; the component plan names the other components, the regime each falls under, and which component is expected to bind. A result from a non-binding component does not represent the article: a can body wall does not represent its seam, a vacuum bottle wall does not represent its base-plug solder, a plain tumbler does not represent a decorated one, a blender jar does not represent its blade assembly, and a cold reservoir does not represent a post-descaling draw.
Native basis family: Specific element migration INTO FOOD OR A FOOD SIMULANT, µg/kg of food (Reg (EU) 10/2011 Annex II convention). Bases are never conflated in this category. A migration ceiling is never published as a content ceiling and a content, food or water ceiling is never published as a migration ceiling. The three foreign bases that surround these rows appear only as disclosed companions: FOOD basis (canned-food tin maximum levels, on the contents not the article), MATERIAL-CONTENT basis (Japan Notice 370 and Korea MFDS 2024-29 percent-by-weight lead in food-contact metals, solder and tin plating; CPSIA total lead in substrate; EU REACH Annex XVII cadmium; RoHS homogeneous materials), and WATER basis (drinking-water parametric values, on the water as delivered not the device). The TOY article-extraction basis (EU 2009/48 Annex II Part III Categories I to III, ASTM F963 soluble limits, per kg of MATERIAL into 0.07 M HCl) is barred from every row by basis_guard..
Credible-regulator set: EU, UK, CODEX, international, US, AU-NZ, CA, HK.
