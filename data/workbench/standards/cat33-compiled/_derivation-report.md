## chocolate — Cocoa and chocolate products (cocoa powder, dark, milk, chips)

Native basis: product as placed on the market (as-sold solid). Covers cocoa powder, dark chocolate, milk chocolate and chocolate chips. Cocoa sold as a beverage/drink mix routes to Cat 5 Beverages; cocoa butter (a fat) routes to Cat 30 Culinary Oils & Fats. A confection with a Supplement Facts panel routes to Cat 16. Products marketed for ages 0-5 route to Cat 1. Cadmium is a strictest-sovereign-per-form split (EU 100 for low-cocoa milk chocolate; MERCOSUR/Paraguay 200 for <40% cocoa and 300 for >=40% cocoa, which govern dark chocolate and cocoa powder below the looser EU 800/600); nickel is the EU 2024/1987 cocoa-solids split; lead and total arsenic are the MERCOSUR/Paraguay cocoa-content split. Certification applies the form matching the product's declared cocoa content.

## confectionery-candy — Candy and confectionery (hard candy, caramels, imported/ethnic sweets)

Native basis: confection as placed on the market (as-sold solid). Covers hard candy, caramels, and imported/ethnic sweets (tamarind, chili, and similar). The dominant certifiable hazard is lead (adulteration + packaging + imported ethnic sweets), which binds the FDA 2006 candy guidance (0.1 ppm) with MERCOSUR/Paraguay concurrence at 100; total arsenic is likewise a real candy limit. Cocoa-containing confections route to the chocolate row. A confection with a Supplement Facts panel (e.g., supplement gummies) routes to Cat 16.

## gummies-jellies — Gummies and jelly sweets

Native basis: confection as placed on the market (as-sold solid). Covers gummies (gelatin/pectin soft candies) and jelly sweets (fruit jellies, Turkish delight). Gummies are soft candies, so the lead and total-arsenic cells bind the candy limits (100 ug/kg); fruit-jelly-sweet limits (fruit/vegetable sweets, jams/jellies) are looser and noted in the audit. Supplement gummies with a Supplement Facts panel route to Cat 16.
