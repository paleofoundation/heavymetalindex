# HMTc Category 11 (Meat, Poultry, and Eggs) — standard derivation report
Method: HMTc Method v2.0 — government-limit default. Every cell is the strictest applicable sovereign
maximum for this exact product form in its native basis, a disclosed read-across where the exact cell
is unregulated, a substantiated not-material determination, or a control. No cell is occurrence-derived.
Category-specific method notes. (1) MUSCLE MEAT vs OFFAL. Every credible instrument writes separate and materially looser maximum levels for edible offal, because lead and cadmium concentrate in liver and kidney. Every row here is scoped to muscle meat and attached edible fat; offal of the same species is a separately-tested sub-lot assessed against that species' offal maximum levels, and no offal value anchors a muscle-meat row. The taxonomy boundary rule places offal inside the animal's own subcategory, which is honoured by sub-lot testing rather than by publishing an offal-derived ceiling. (2) THE CATEGORY 1 BOUNDARY. Products marketed for ages 0-5 route to Category 1 (mixed meals), so the infant and baby-food action levels and maximum levels that would otherwise be the strictest values in the register are excluded by applicability from every row here, and are excluded explicitly in each row's audit_exclude_scope. (3) GAME AND LEAD AMMUNITION. Fragmenting lead projectiles are a documented, mechanical lead pathway in wild-harvested game. That evidence drives mandatory radiographic fragment screening before grinding and priority surveillance on the game row; it does not and cannot move the number, which remains a disclosed read-across from the domestic-mammal muscle-meat maximum level. (4) BASIS TREATMENT ON THE THREE COMPOSITE ROWS, DISCLOSED IN THE DIRECTION IT CUTS. On the processed-meat, canned-meat and analogue rows the component maximum level is applied to the finished product as sold, with no invented conversion factor. This config does NOT claim that treatment is conservative, because it is not: a formulation that is part water, brine, starch or rusk can carry a higher concentration in its meat or protein fraction than the component maximum level itself allows. The gap is closed by a second, independent obligation rather than by a conversion factor. Certification of those three rows requires documented compliance of the raw material or source commodity against the same named component maximum levels, in their own basis, IN ADDITION to finished-product compliance against the published ceiling; neither test substitutes for the other. The eggs row runs the opposite way and is treated accordingly: dried and powdered egg is concentrated relative to whole egg, so applying the whole-egg maximum level to the powder as sold is the STRICTER treatment, and it is applied without being reversed. (5) REGISTER GAPS ARE DISCLOSED, NOT FILLED. The EU 2023/915 and Codex CXS 193 lead rows for meat and offal, the EU and Codex cadmium offal rows, and the FSANZ cadmium meat and offal rows are absent from this register. They are recorded as register gaps for ingestion through the verified regulatory-fetch pathway and are NOT written into this config from memory. No absent row is assigned a remembered value anywhere in this config. Because the method selects the strictest applicable value, ingesting a missing row can only lower a published cell or leave it unchanged, never raise it; every affected cell must be re-derived when the missing rows land.
Native basis family: dietary as-sold, edible portion, wet weight (bone, shell and inedible fat excluded); no reconstitution, drained-weight or dry-to-wet conversion is performed anywhere in this category — the 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.
Credible-regulator set: EU, UK, CODEX, international, US, AU-NZ, CA, HK.
Provenance tags: regulatory-alignment | read-across | not-material | control | ratchet.

The exact cell is therefore unregulated and the nearest applicable sovereign cell is the domestic-mammal muscle-meat lead maximum level, adopted by disclosed read-across at 0.1 mg/kg. **Audit / substantiation notes**
  - **Pb substantiation:** Hong Kong Cap. 132V Part 2 Schedule is the only credible sovereign instrument on this register that sets a lead maximum level for eggs, and it does so twice: poultry eggs 0.2 mg/kg and lime-preserved eggs 0.2 mg/kg. Both are named so that the preserved-egg form is covered explicitly rather than by silence. The EU sets no egg lead maximum level and Codex CXS 193's egg rows are absent from this register; neither omission licenses borrowing the stricter 100 ug/kg muscle-meat value, because meat is not an applicable scope for an egg and Method v2.0 selects the strictest APPLICABLE sovereign value, not the strictest value in the instrument. That this ceiling sits above the muscle-meat ceiling is a fact about the sovereign instruments and is disclosed rather than harmonised away.
  - **Cd substantiation:** No credible sovereign government sets a cadmium maximum level for eggs: Hong Kong Cap. 132V regulates poultry eggs for lead and mercury but not cadmium, and the EU, Codex, FSANZ, UK, US and Health Canada set no egg cadmium value at all. The exact cell is therefore unregulated. The nearest applicable sovereign cell is the cadmium maximum level for meat of poultry in the SAME instrument and for the SAME producing animal (0.05 mg/kg), adopted by disclosed read-across. The looser poultry-liver (0.5 mg/kg) and poultry-kidney (1.0 mg/kg) cadmium values were considered and rejected: an egg is not offal, and cadmium's affinity for renal and hepatic tissue is exactly why those rows are looser. Choosing the stricter muscle-meat analogue is what the method requires.
  - **tAs substantiation:** No credible sovereign government sets a total-arsenic maximum level for eggs. Hong Kong Cap. 132V regulates meat of poultry at 0.5 mg/kg total arsenic, the nearest applicable sovereign cell for a product of the same producing animal, adopted by disclosed read-across. The organoarsenical feed-additive pathway that motivates the poultry inorganic-arsenic control applies equally to laying flocks, so the same reflex speciation obligation is carried into this row's iAs cell.
  - **tHg substantiation:** Hong Kong Cap. 132V Part 2, mercury expressed as total mercury, poultry eggs, 0.05 mg/kg = 50 ug/kg. This is a direct sovereign maximum level for the exact cell, not a read-across.
  - **Cr substantiation:** No credible sovereign government sets a total-chromium maximum level for eggs. The nearest applicable sovereign cell is meat of poultry (Hong Kong Cap. 132V, 1 mg/kg), adopted by disclosed read-across and published as a screen for the reasons given in the Cr-VI cell.

**Audit / substantiation notes**
  - **Pb substantiation:** A formulated processed-meat product is not a separately named commodity in any credible sovereign contaminant instrument, so the exact cell is unregulated and the ceiling is a disclosed read-across from the maximum levels governing its principal components — the muscle meat of the species it is formulated from. **Audit / substantiation notes**
  - **Pb substantiation:** No credible sovereign government sets a lead maximum level for a meat analogue, so the exact cell is unregulated and the ceiling reads across from the maximum levels governing the principal protein components. Hong Kong Cap. 132V sets 0.1 mg/kg for pulses and 0.1 mg/kg for legume vegetables, the commodity classes from which pea and soy protein are drawn; FSANZ Schedule 19 sets 0.2 mg/kg for cereals, the class from which wheat gluten is drawn. The strictest applicable component value binds at 100 ug/kg, so a wheat-gluten-based analogue is held to the pulse ceiling rather than the looser cereal one. BASIS TREATMENT, DISCLOSED IN THE DIRECTION IT ACTUALLY CUTS: the component maximum levels are written on dry commodities as placed on the market and are applied to the hydrated finished product as sold, with no dry-to-wet conversion, because Method v2.0 forbids inventing a basis conversion. That is the LOOSER of the two available treatments, not a conservative one: a hydrated extrudate is mostly water, so a protein ingredient can exceed its own commodity maximum level and still leave the finished product inside this ceiling. The programme states that openly and closes the gap with a second, independent requirement rather than with a conversion factor: every certified lot must carry supplier documentation of the source commodity for each protein ingredient, tested against the named component maximum levels in the commodity own basis, in addition to finished-product compliance against this ceiling. The ingredient-basis treatment is the natural candidate for the first Standards Ratchet review on this row once eligible certified-lot data exist.
  - **Cd substantiation:** EU Regulation (EU) 2023/915 Annex I sets cadmium maximum levels for each of this row's principal protein commodities: pulses 0.040 mg/kg, soy beans (with peanuts) 0.20 mg/kg, and wheat gluten (with rice, quinoa and wheat bran) 0.15 mg/kg. The strictest applicable component value binds at 40 ug/kg. This is the tightest cell in Category 11 and it is deliberately not relaxed toward the soy row: the method takes the strictest applicable component maximum level, and a soy-based analogue is not entitled to the looser oilseed ceiling merely because soy is the protein it happens to use.
  - **tAs substantiation:** No credible sovereign government sets a total-arsenic maximum level for pulses, peas, soy beans or a meat analogue. Rice is deliberately excluded from the component set and from the audit scope: a rice-containing formulation is governed by the far more consequential sovereign rice INORGANIC-arsenic maximum levels, which are handled as the companion instrument in the iAs cell rather than folded into a total-arsenic average here.
