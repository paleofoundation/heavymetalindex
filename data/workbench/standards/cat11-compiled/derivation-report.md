# HMTc Category 11 (Meat, Poultry, and Eggs) — standard derivation report
Method: HMTc Method v2.0 — government-limit default. Every cell is the strictest applicable sovereign
maximum for this exact product form in its native basis, a disclosed read-across where the exact cell
is unregulated, a substantiated not-material determination, or a control. No cell is occurrence-derived.
Category-specific method notes. (1) MUSCLE MEAT vs OFFAL. Every credible instrument writes separate and materially looser maximum levels for edible offal, because lead and cadmium concentrate in liver and kidney. Every row here is scoped to muscle meat and attached edible fat; offal of the same species is a separately-tested sub-lot assessed against that species' offal maximum levels, and no offal value anchors a muscle-meat row. The taxonomy boundary rule places offal inside the animal's own subcategory, which is honoured by sub-lot testing rather than by publishing an offal-derived ceiling. (2) THE CATEGORY 1 BOUNDARY. Products marketed for ages 0-5 route to Category 1 (mixed meals), so the infant and baby-food action levels and maximum levels that would otherwise be the strictest values in the register are excluded by applicability from every row here, and are excluded explicitly in each row's audit_exclude_scope. (3) GAME AND LEAD AMMUNITION. Fragmenting lead projectiles are a documented, mechanical lead pathway in wild-harvested game. That evidence drives mandatory radiographic fragment screening before grinding and priority surveillance on the game row; it does not and cannot move the number, which remains a disclosed read-across from the domestic-mammal muscle-meat maximum level. (4) BASIS TREATMENT ON THE THREE COMPOSITE ROWS, DISCLOSED IN THE DIRECTION IT CUTS. On the processed-meat, canned-meat and analogue rows the component maximum level is applied to the finished product as sold, with no invented conversion factor. This config does NOT claim that treatment is conservative, because it is not: a formulation that is part water, brine, starch or rusk can carry a higher concentration in its meat or protein fraction than the component maximum level itself allows. The gap is closed by a second, independent obligation rather than by a conversion factor. Certification of those three rows requires documented compliance of the raw material or source commodity against the same named component maximum levels, in their own basis, IN ADDITION to finished-product compliance against the published ceiling; neither test substitutes for the other. The eggs row runs the opposite way and is treated accordingly: dried and powdered egg is concentrated relative to whole egg, so applying the whole-egg maximum level to the powder as sold is the STRICTER treatment, and it is applied without being reversed. (5) REGISTER GAPS ARE DISCLOSED, NOT FILLED. The EU 2023/915 and Codex CXS 193 lead rows for meat and offal, the EU and Codex cadmium offal rows, and the FSANZ cadmium meat and offal rows are absent from this register. They are recorded as register gaps for ingestion through the verified regulatory-fetch pathway and are NOT written into this config from memory. No absent row is assigned a remembered value anywhere in this config. Because the method selects the strictest applicable value, ingesting a missing row can only lower a published cell or leave it unchanged, never raise it; every affected cell must be re-derived when the missing rows land.
Native basis family: dietary as-sold, edible portion, wet weight (bone, shell and inedible fat excluded); no reconstitution, drained-weight or dry-to-wet conversion is performed anywhere in this category — the 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.
Credible-regulator set: EU, UK, CODEX, international, US, AU-NZ, CA, HK.

**Audit / substantiation notes**
  - **Pb substantiation:** No credible sovereign government names venison, bison, wild boar, rabbit or hare in a lead maximum level: the credible instruments enumerate cattle, sheep, goat, pig and poultry. The exact cell is therefore unregulated and the nearest applicable sovereign cell is the domestic-mammal muscle-meat lead maximum level, adopted by disclosed read-across at 0.1 mg/kg. Two independent credible instruments carry that same value (Hong Kong Cap. 132V meat of cattle, pig, goat and sheep; FSANZ Schedule 19 meat of cattle, sheep, pig and poultry), which is why no looser figure is defensible. Wild cervids and lagomorphs can carry higher renal and hepatic cadmium than farmed stock, which is a surveillance and offal-routing consideration; it does not justify adopting the looser liver or kidney maximum level as this row's muscle-meat ceiling.

Applying an additive purity specification to the finished food would conflate an ingredient specification with a food contaminant maximum level and would set a finished-product ceiling from a rule that never addressed the finished product. The register records the row's basis as 'color additive ingredient specification', which this row's basis guard rejects by construction, so the id could not be named as an anchor even if it were scope-applicable; it surfaces in the audit scan only because the scan deliberately runs without the basis guard so that near-miss rows are seen and dispositioned rather than silently filtered. BASIS DIRECTION FOR THE FAT COMPONENT, DISCLOSED: the treatment cuts the OPPOSITE way for the fat fraction than it does for the protein commodities, and the programme states both rather than only the flattering one. A dry protein commodity is CONCENTRATED relative to the hydrated finished product, so applying its maximum level to the finished product is the LOOSER treatment (disclosed above). Added fat is DILUTED in the finished product, typically 10-20% of mass, so applying the edible-fats-and-oils maximum level to the whole finished product is the STRICTER treatment: the finished patty must meet a ceiling written for neat oil. The programme does not choose between these directions to suit a preferred number. Method v2.0 takes the strictest applicable component value in the row's native basis and invents no basis conversion in either direction, and the consequence here is simply that this row's total-arsenic ceiling is set by its fat fraction while its cadmium and nickel ceilings are set by its protein fraction. Certification accordingly requires source-commodity documentation for the added-fat ingredient tested in its own basis, exactly as it does for each protein ingredient.
