# Outreach Sequences

Short, honest, action-anchored. Every email ends in one concrete, low-friction step. None makes a safety claim, normalizes contamination, or offers legal advice. Personalize the brackets; keep them under ~150 words.

---

Because the 2027 retailer cycles are already being set, the brands in the strongest position next year are the ones starting now. Want the 30-day slot? — [signature]

If you suspect your results aren't clean, that's the case *for* moving, not against — the Confidential Remediation Track enrolls you privately, before any public exposure, and documents improvement from day one. Happy to walk your team through how the confidentiality works. — [signature]
