# GB 2762-2025 vs our GB 2762-2022-derived rows — infant/child diff

**Verdict: no infant or child metal limit changes. China's 2026-09-02 supersession
is a non-event for the infant/child standards.**

Extracted 2026-08-04 from the authoritative text
(`raw/regulatory/cn/china-gb-2762-2025-effective-2026-09-02.pdf`, 21pp, published
2025-09-02, effective 2026-09-02) and compared cell-by-cell against the 17 `CN`
rows in `data/evidence/regulatory_limits.csv`.

**Chromium — the analyte for which China is our only source — is unchanged at
2.0 mg/kg for milk powder.** The `Cr-total: 2000` powder pick stands.

**Nickel:** GB 2762-2025 sets exactly one nickel limit in the entire standard —
hydrogenated vegetable oils and products containing hydrogenated fats, 1.0 mg/kg.
There is no nickel limit for infant formula or dairy, so China contributes nothing
to the Ni pick (which comes from the EU).

The published change summary for GB 2762-2025 (Pb/Cd/As/Cr in meat jerky; nitrite
in packaged drinking water) is consistent with this: none of it touches infant or
child categories.

## The 8:1 conversion — earlier concern retracted

GB 2762 footnote c states 液态婴幼儿配方食品根据 8∶1 的比例折算其限量 (liquid
infant formula limits convert at 8:1). An earlier note in this session flagged
this as conflicting with the repo's 1:7 reconstitution factor. **That flag was
wrong.** The live row already derives liquid RTF as 0.08 ÷ 8 = 0.01 mg/kg and
cites "Table 1 (Lead), footnote c". China's own ratio was applied correctly; the
1:7 factor is used elsewhere and does not govern these rows.

## Extraction method (and why the obvious approaches fail)

The PDF has a real embedded text layer — it is not a scan — but neither
`pdftotext -raw` nor `-layout` recovers the tables:

- every digit is emitted as its own glyph run, so 250 arrives as `2 5 0` and 0.08
  as `0. 0 8`;
- the halves of a single value can land on **different output lines** (`08(以固态
  产品计)` with its `0.` on the next line), which no line-oriented parse can fix.

The working method is coordinate-based: `pdftotext -bbox-layout`, group words by
vertical centre to rebuild the true visual row, sort by x within the row, and
re-join numeric fragments separated by sub-6pt gaps while treating >12pt gaps as
column breaks. Script: `tools/regulatory/extract-gb2762.mjs`.

**Trap found during extraction:** an initial pass mis-assigned Table 8
(亚硝酸盐/硝酸盐 — nitrite and nitrate) to the chromium section, because the
section-boundary regex only matched single-character metal names and skipped
`4.8亚硝酸盐`. That produced plausible-looking "chromium" values of 2.0 and 100
for infant formula. They are nitrite (以NaNO2计) and nitrate (以NaNO3计). All
`4.N` headings must bound the sections, not just the metal ones.
