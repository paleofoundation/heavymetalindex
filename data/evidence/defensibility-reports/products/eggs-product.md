## How to read this

- Sections follow the page's own H2 structure.
- Each claim shows the claim value, the section it appeared in, the cited sources in that section, and — when the cited source page itself has audit rows — the retrieval date, models used, agreement state, and span id from each row.

A page-level sources block alone is not credited here — it would inherit every claim onto every listed source and defeat the report's purpose.
- This v1 verifies that a citation exists. It does not yet verify that the cited source contains the claimed value — that is the v2 PDF-side check.
