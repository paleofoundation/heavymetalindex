## Species and basis

Arsenic and chromium in the occurrence papers in this slice are totals unless a row says otherwise. Rovira uses a Cr(VI) reference dose on total chromium for a risk model. That model is not a chromium(VI) measurement. Salamone doses Sb(III). Migration studies (Chapa-Martínez, Westerhoff, Tukur, Chu) are not total-content measurements of the water as sold except where the paper says as purchased.

Pereira 2020 does not state dry versus wet weight for the baby foods.
