## Baby wipes, September 2026

- [[sources/cpqs2026-online-baby-wipes-quality-monitoring]] - China Association for Quality Promotion of Consumer Products Safety, 2 April 2026, online baby wipes. Annex antimony is a per-liter image read.
- [[sources/gocer2025-baby-wet-wipes-solution-fiber-packaging]] - 2025 Istanbul thesis. Ten baby wipes. Solution, fiber, and packaging in instrument ppb. Not milligrams per kilogram.
- [[sources/shin2004-disposable-diaper-heavy-metals]] - Scanned 2004 diaper paper. Not a wipe measurement. Table 1 quoted only where the prose and the image agree.
- [[sources/choi2016-wet-wipes-nonwoven-heavy-metals]] - Wet-wipe sheet digests reported in micrograms per liter. A labeled milligram-per-kilogram reconstruction is shown and is not the printed result.
- [[sources/biver2021-antimony-release-polyester-artificial-sweat]] - Antimony release from polyester textiles into artificial sweat. Not a baby-wipe measurement.
- [[sources/stone2012-wa-qapp-parabens-metals-childrens-cosmetics]] - Ecology Publication 12-07-021. Method plan, not wipe results.
- [[sources/stone2012-wa-qapp-phthalates-metals-packaging]] - Ecology Publication 12-07-022. Method plan for packaging.

## Diaper creams, September 2026

Finished-product measurements of zinc oxide diaper creams are consumer-commissioned single-unit reports (grey literature). The pathway studies measure zinc, not lead or cadmium. The regulatory texts set no finished-product metal limit for over-the-counter skin-protectant drugs.

- [[sources/rubin2025a-40pct-zno-diaper-ointment-lot-2025-142]] - One 40% zinc oxide ointment, ICP-MS as received. Lead 3303.8 ppb, cadmium 55.50 ppb.
- [[sources/rubin2025b-40pct-zno-diaper-paste-lot-2025-169]] - One 40% zinc oxide paste. Lead 1401.0 ppb, cadmium 509.5 ppb.
- [[sources/rubin2025c-40pct-zno-diaper-paste-lot-2025-182]] - One 40% zinc oxide paste. Lead 4657.1 ppb, cadmium 473.1 ppb.
- [[sources/rubin2025d-13pct-zno-diaper-cream-lot-2025-218]] - One 13% zinc oxide cream. Lead 1244.1 ppb, cadmium 45.26 ppb.
- [[sources/rubin2026-diaper-cream-comparison-chart-11-products]] - Eleven-product chart, rounded. Four values match the reports above; seven are chart-only.
- [[sources/agren1991-zno-vehicles-zinc-absorption-skin-wounds]] - Zinc from zinc oxide dressings through intact human skin and rat wounds.
- [[sources/khabir2021-epidermal-zinc-zno-nanoparticle-sunscreen]] - Zinc-67 tracer in excised human skin after a zinc oxide nanoparticle formulation.
- [[sources/pal2016-uvb-zno-nanoparticle-penetration-hairless-mouse]] - UVB and zinc oxide nanoparticle uptake in mouse skin. Numbers only in a supplement not on file.
- [[sources/yasugi2026-hyperzincemia-zno-ointment-preterm-infant]] - Case report: hyperzincemia in a preterm infant with zinc oxide ointment on eroded diaper dermatitis.
- [[sources/sccs2012-zinc-oxide-nano-opinion-1489-12]] - EU opinion on nano zinc oxide. No impurity data; dermal absorption of zinc.
- [[sources/ich2022-q3d-r2-elemental-impurities]] - ICH Q3D(R2), cutaneous permitted daily exposures for drug products.
- [[sources/fda2025-21cfr347-skin-protectant-otc]] - US OTC skin protectant monograph, zinc oxide 1 to 25 percent.
- [[sources/fda1990-55fr25204-diaper-rash-tentative-final-monograph]] - US 1990 proposed diaper-rash rule, zinc oxide above 25 to 40 percent in ointment.
- [[sources/fda2003-68fr33362-skin-protectant-final-monograph]] - US 2003 final skin protectant monograph.
- [[sources/healthcanada2024-nnhpd-diaper-rash-products-monograph]] - Health Canada Diaper Rash Products Monograph, 2024.
- [[sources/edqm2026-pheur-0252-zinc-oxide-knowledge-database]] - European Pharmacopoeia zinc oxide monograph record. Thallium 5 ppm added; lead and cadmium limits not obtained.

Same tests as five chart rows.

- [[sources/dccf2021-infant-child-sunscreens-korea]] - Korean consumer federation, 17 infant and child sunscreens, compliance outcome only.
- [[sources/rodriguez-romero2019-sunscreen-metals-coastal-seawater]] - One adult SPF 50 nano titanium dioxide spray and its release to seawater.
- [[sources/tovar-sanchez2013-sunscreen-emerging-pollutants-coastal]] - Titanium, zinc, aluminium and iron in seven adult sunscreens; non-detects without a detection limit.
- [[sources/sccs2012-zinc-oxide-nano-opinion-1489-12]], [[sources/sccs2014-zinc-oxide-nano-addendum-1518-13]], [[sources/sccs2014-titanium-dioxide-nano-opinion-1516-13]], [[sources/sccs2024-titanium-dioxide-scientific-advice-1661-23]] - EU committee opinions on the mineral UV filters and cosmetic titanium dioxide impurity profiles.

- [[sources/danishepa2014-nano-anatase-tio2-consumer-products]], [[sources/danishepa2015-dermal-absorption-nano-tio2-zno-sunscreen]] - Danish EPA crystal-form mapping and skin-model penetration study.
- [[sources/fda2019-sunscreen-proposed-rule-84fr6204]], [[sources/healthcanada2026-primary-sunscreen-monograph]] - US proposed GRASE rule and Canadian sunscreen monograph.
- [[sources/khabir2021-epidermal-zinc-zno-nanoparticle-sunscreen]], [[sources/mohammed2019-zno-nanoparticle-sunscreen-repeated-application]], [[sources/gulson2016-nanoparticles-sunscreens-ichmet]], [[sources/crosera2015-tio2-nanoparticle-skin-penetration-hacat]], [[sources/pal2016-uvb-zno-nanoparticle-dermal-penetration-mouse]] - Zinc and titanium skin penetration in adult, ex vivo and animal skin.

## Baby lotions, oils, and powders, September 2026

Measurements and government texts for baby lotions, baby oils, and baby powders. Case reports of lead-oxide folk powders and an edible-oil survey are context, not commercial baby product measurements.

- [[sources/yaghi2026-baby-powder-benghazi-libya]] - Sixteen baby talcum powders, Benghazi, 2024. Six metals by flame AAS, mg/kg as sold. Several table and prose conflicts are flagged.
- [[sources/omenka2016-personal-care-products-ibadan]] - Thirty-five personal care products, Ibadan. Two baby powders and four baby petroleum jellies. One baby powder lead result corroborated across three laboratories.
- [[sources/szcc2020-infant-powders-shenzhen-consumer-council]] - News report of a 2020 Shenzhen Consumer Council test of 20 infant powders. Maxima and counts only.
- [[sources/cnpharm2019-nmpa-notice-childrens-powder-lead]] - News report of a 2019 Chinese national non-compliance notice. Two children's powders with lead above 10 mg/kg on re-test.
- [[sources/leadsafemama2025-baby-lotion-single-lot]] - One baby lotion lot reported non-detect for four metals. Summary graphic only.
- [[sources/rosnan2017-talcum-powder-cu-pb-ni-aas]] - Undergraduate report excerpt. Eight talcum powders, digest-solution mg/L, not convertible to mg/kg.
- [[sources/duan2023-infant-lead-poisoning-topical-powder]] - Case report. A lead-containing traditional powder used by a breastfeeding mother. Not a baby product measurement.
- [[sources/karwowski2016-folk-diaper-powder-infant-lead]] - Abstract only. A folk diaper powder with 62% lead by weight.
- [[sources/islam2024-edible-fats-oils-bangladesh]] - Edible oils including mustard oil. Context only; no skin-application data.
- [[sources/cfr2025-21cfr73-1550-talc-color-additive]] - US federal lead and arsenic specification for talc used to colour drugs, measured in an acid extract.
- [[sources/jecfa2003-talc-ins553iii-specification]] - FAO/WHO food-additive specification for talc. Lead 2 mg/kg.
- [[sources/healthcanada2024-diaper-rash-products-monograph]] - Canadian diaper rash monograph. Classifies powder, oil, and ointment ingredients. No metal values.
